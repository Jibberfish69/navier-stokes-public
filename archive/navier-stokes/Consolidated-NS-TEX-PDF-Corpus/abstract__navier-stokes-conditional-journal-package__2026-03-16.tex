\begin{abstract}
This paper presents a conditional classical-closure theorem for the three-dimensional incompressible Navier-Stokes equations on \([0,T)\times\mathbb{R}^3\) with smooth divergence-free finite-energy data. The claim is limited and explicit: if one proves on the classical equation itself a quantified scale barrier against singular cascade transfer, nonlinear compactness strong enough to pass the convective term, and gradient control forced by the same dynamics, then the singularity pathway isolated by this framework does not occur on \([0,T)\). This manuscript does not claim an unconditional proof of global regularity and does not claim that the Clay Millennium problem is solved.
\end{abstract}
