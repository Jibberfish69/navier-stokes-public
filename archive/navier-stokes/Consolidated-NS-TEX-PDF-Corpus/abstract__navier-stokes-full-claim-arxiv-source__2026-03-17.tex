\begin{abstract}
This paper proves classical global regularity for the three-dimensional incompressible Navier-Stokes equations on \([0,T)\times\mathbb{R}^3\) for the stated smooth divergence-free Euclidean target class. The proof closes one explicit route on the classical equation itself: a quantified scale barrier suppresses the singularity-feeding high-frequency cascade, nonlinear compactness closes the convective term on one fixed classical approximation surface, and a same-surface Euclidean gradient-transfer inequality prevents derivative-scale blow-up. Every theorem-critical step is carried on the original equation with no essential dependence on modified dynamics or geometric surrogate structure.
\end{abstract}
