\section{Bridge II: Nonlinear Compactness}

The second bridge upgrades compactness to the level required for nonlinear closure.

\begin{proposition}[Bridge II obligation]
To support Theorem 2.1, one must establish compactness and defect control sufficient to pass the convective term on the classical equation without importing regularized structure.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Explanation of role]
Weak compactness alone is generally insufficient for nonlinear passage. The argument requires an explicit convergence mode and defect exclusion at the precise regularity level used in the theorem.
\end{proof}
