\section{Bridge III: Gradient Control}

The third bridge prevents derivative-scale re-entry of singular behavior.

\begin{proposition}[Bridge III obligation]
To support Theorem 2.1, one must prove gradient control from the same classical dynamics used elsewhere in the argument, including explicit continuation logic.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Explanation of role]
Even after cascade and compactness control, derivative-scale failure can remain unless a classical gradient mechanism closes the route. If this bridge is absent, the theorem cannot be promoted beyond the conditional level.
\end{proof}
