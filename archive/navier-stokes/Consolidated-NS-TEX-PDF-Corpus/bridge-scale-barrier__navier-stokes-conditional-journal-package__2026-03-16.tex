\section{Bridge I: Scale Barrier}

The first bridge isolates the cascade-transfer mechanism that can feed gradient blow-up despite coarse boundedness information.

\begin{proposition}[Bridge I obligation]
To support Theorem 2.1, one must exhibit a classical scale barrier that is explicit in hypotheses, localization, and transfer quantity, and that does not rely on modified-equation damping.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}[Explanation of role]
Energy/enstrophy control alone does not exclude concentration at dangerous scales. The bridge obligation identifies and blocks that channel. Without this bridge on the classical equation, the theorem remains a conditional program rather than a closed proof.
\end{proof}
