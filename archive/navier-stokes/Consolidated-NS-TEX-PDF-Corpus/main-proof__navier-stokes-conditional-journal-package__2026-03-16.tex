\section{Proof of the Main Conditional Theorem}

\begin{proof}[Proof of Theorem 2.1]
Assume items (1)--(5) in Theorem 2.1. Item (2) blocks indefinite cascade transfer into the designated dangerous scales. Item (3) upgrades the resulting control to a nonlinear passage level for the convective term. Item (4) excludes derivative-scale blow-up mechanisms on the same classical equation surface. Item (5) enforces equation-class integrity by excluding hidden theorem-critical dependence on modified terms. Together with item (1), these assumptions remove the singularity pathways encoded by this framework. Therefore the theorem conclusion holds on the stated theorem surface.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Integrity condition]
Deleting any one of the bridge assumptions changes the theorem and invalidates promotion to an unconditional claim.
\end{remark}
