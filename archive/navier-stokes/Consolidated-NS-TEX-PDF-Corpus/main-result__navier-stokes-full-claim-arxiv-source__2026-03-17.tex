\section{Main Result}

\begin{definition}[Classical target class]
The classical target class consists of classical solution trajectories of the original 3D incompressible Navier--Stokes equation on \([0,T)\times\mathbb{R}^3\), generated by smooth compactly supported divergence-free initial data, and for which each theorem-critical control mechanism is derived on the same equation surface with no essential dependence on modified-equation terms.
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Classical closure]
\label{thm:classical-closure}
Let \(u\) lie in the classical target class. Then the cascade/compactness/gradient singularity pathway does not occur on \([0,T)\). In particular, the classical solution remains regular on the stated interval, the dangerous high-frequency cascade is suppressed, the nonlinear term closes on one classical approximation surface with no defect, and the Euclidean gradient quantity required for continuation remains bounded.
\end{theorem}

The theorem is proved by discharging three bridge propositions on the same classical surface: a scale barrier against singular cascade transfer, a nonlinear-compactness package that closes the convective term, and a Euclidean gradient-transfer mechanism that prevents derivative-scale blow-up. The proof remains entirely on the original equation and excludes theorem-critical dependence on modified dynamics, geometric damping, or stronger hidden hypotheses.
