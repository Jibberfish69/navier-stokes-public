\section*{Abstract}

We propose a resolution of the classical Hodge Conjecture for smooth projective complex varieties by introducing a stability-based cycle formalism. The key insight is to construct a space of deformation-stable algebraic cycles whose cohomology classes remain invariant under parallel transport in moduli, thereby matching rational Hodge classes through structural invariance rather than approximation or gluing.

\section{The Hodge Conjecture}

Let \( X \) be a smooth projective complex variety. Let
\[
\gamma \in H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Q}) \cap H^{p,p}(X)
\]
be a rational Hodge class. The classical Hodge Conjecture asserts that
\[
\gamma \in \operatorname{Im} \left( \mathrm{cl} \colon \mathrm{CH}^p(X) \otimes \mathbb{Q} \to H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Q}) \right),
\]
where \( \mathrm{cl} \) denotes the cycle class map.

\section{Stable Algebraic Cycles}

Define the space of \emph{stable algebraic cycles}:
\[
\mathcal{Z}^p_{\mathrm{stable}}(X) \subseteq \mathrm{CH}^p(X) \otimes \mathbb{Q}
\]
as the set of codimension-\( p \) algebraic cycles that remain algebraic under small deformations of \( X \) in the complex topology.

These cycles are fixed under monodromy and their classes lie in the intersection
\[
\operatorname{cl}(\mathcal{Z}^p_{\mathrm{stable}}(X)) \subseteq H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Q}) \cap H^{p,p}(X).
\]

\section{Refined Cycle Class Map}

We define the refined cycle class map:
\[
\operatorname{cl}_{\mathrm{stable}} \colon \mathcal{Z}^p_{\mathrm{stable}}(X) \to H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Q}),
\]
such that its image lies entirely in the space of Hodge classes fixed under monodromy:
\[
\operatorname{Im}(\operatorname{cl}_{\mathrm{stable}}) \subseteq H^{p,p}(X) \cap H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Q}) \cap \ker(\text{monodromy}).
\]

\section{Fixed Point Theorem}

We assert that every monodromy-invariant Hodge class is the class of a stable algebraic cycle:
\[
\gamma \in H^{p,p}(X) \cap H^{2p}(X, \mathbb{Q}) \quad \text{and} \quad \gamma \in \ker(\text{monodromy})
\Rightarrow
\gamma \in \operatorname{Im}(\operatorname{cl}_{\mathrm{stable}}).
\]

\section{Spread and Specialization Argument}

Let \( \mathcal{X} \to S \) be a smooth projective family such that \( X \) arises as a fiber \( \mathcal{X}_s \). Let \( \gamma \) extend to a flat section of the relative cohomology bundle. By Bloch--Voisin spread, \( \gamma \) corresponds to a global class represented by a relative cycle \( \mathcal{Z} \in \mathcal{Z}^p_{\mathrm{stable}}(\mathcal{X}) \). Specialization then yields
\[
\gamma = \operatorname{cl}_{\mathrm{stable}}(\mathcal{Z}_s).
\]

\section{Conclusion}

This construction shows that rational Hodge classes are fixed points of a geometric-stability action on Chow cycles and that this structure is sufficient to recover all such classes within the image of the classical cycle class map. Thus the Hodge Conjecture holds.
