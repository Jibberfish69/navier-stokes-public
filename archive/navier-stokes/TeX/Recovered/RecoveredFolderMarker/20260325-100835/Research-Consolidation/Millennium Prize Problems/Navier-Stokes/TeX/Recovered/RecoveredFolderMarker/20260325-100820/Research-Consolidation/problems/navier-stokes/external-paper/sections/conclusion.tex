\section{Conclusion}

This manuscript reconstructs a source-grounded route toward a classical no-blow-up theorem on the stated three-dimensional Navier--Stokes target class. Its route is organized around one explicit singularity mechanism: dangerous-scale cascade, failure of nonlinear closure, and derivative-scale blow-up. The route is carried by four named bridges: scale barrier, monotone functional \(Q(t)\), nonlinear compactness, and curvature-based regularity with feedback into the scale barrier.

Just as importantly, the route is kept on the original problem rather than
silently replaced by a nearby modified system. The realized classical carrier,
exact pullback, and compactness layer remove ambiguity about the comparison
model used by the secondary projected-flow track. Bridge IV is now stated and
proved in Euclidean form in the export package; what remains on the primary
source-grounded route is the corresponding closed-loop warrant on the declared
theorem surface.
