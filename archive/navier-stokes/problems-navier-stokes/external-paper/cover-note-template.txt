A Source-Grounded Four-Bridge Route for 3D Navier-Stokes

Dear Editor,

Please find attached our manuscript, "A Source-Grounded Four-Bridge Route for 3D Navier-Stokes."
The paper presents a source-grounded conditional route theorem for the classical three-dimensional incompressible Navier-Stokes equations, together with an explicit classical realization, compactness layer, and pullback package on the original equation.

The manuscript is intentionally classical in scope. It keeps every theorem-critical step on the Euclidean Navier-Stokes equation itself and excludes theorem-critical dependence on modified dynamics, geometric surrogate structure, or stronger hidden hypotheses.

The submission package contains:
- a journal-neutral TeX source tree,
- a bibliography positioned against the standard Navier-Stokes regularity literature,
- a compiled PDF for referee review.

Sincerely,
