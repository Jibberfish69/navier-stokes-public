maximal smooth interval. That control does not stop the velocity from changing more
sharply across shorter distances. Viscosity is the part of the equation we expect to stop that
sharpening. It acts alongside the three other pieces of the same motion:

The regularity hope is that viscous smoothing always keeps pace with the sharpening produced

route to sharpen the flow.
