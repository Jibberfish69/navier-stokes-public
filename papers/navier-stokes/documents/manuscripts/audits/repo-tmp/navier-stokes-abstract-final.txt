    If the corresponding whole-terminal bound holds for every finite rectangle, their
agreement on overlaps carries the differentiated flow to one H ∞ terminal state. Local
existence launches a smooth solution from that state, and uniqueness identifies it as the
continuation of the same history beyond T∗ . At that endpoint the lowest rectangle gives
a finite critical-height roof, which stops an infinite ascent through height doublings. The
higher rectangles make the accumulated Lipschitz action finite, keeping distinct material
labels apart, and their local Sobolev control sends the scale-invariant content required
by a singular point to zero. This manuscript does not derive D∗ < ∞ from (u0 , ν), so
its datum-to-global conclusion through the lowest rectangle remains conditional at that
step.
