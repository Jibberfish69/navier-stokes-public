29    The Support Boundary
Many strong estimates do real support work outside the class faces. A strong estimate enters the
class proof by naming the requirement it protects. A source-control estimate, no-pulse estimate,
signed-current estimate, or comparison estimate may be essential to a route. It still has to say which
class requirement it protects.

Proposition 8 (Support enters through a face). Let S be a source, readout, comparison, or supplier
statement. The statement becomes a class-proof conclusion by landing a selected same-solution object
in PackQ , PartN,Q , FieldN,r,Q , membership readout, terminal packet capture, or no genuine finite
exit. Until that landing is supplied, S serves the class proof as support.

Proof. The class proof has only three witness requirements and three closing hinges. A supplier
estimate can help prove one of them, and a readout estimate can explain the continuation consequence
after the requirements survive. A comparison estimate can show why a neighbouring theorem world
behaves differently. None of those roles is itself membership or exit. The statement enters the proof
only when its exact target is a class requirement or closing hinge.

Corollary 1 (Forward-positive estimates keep their value). Demoting a forward-positive estimate
from class authority preserves its value. It assigns the estimate a proof job:

  support Pack,   support Part,    support Field,        support readout,   or support no genuine exit.

Proof. The estimate remains available exactly where it has mathematical force. The class proof
only prevents the estimate from pretending to be the class law by itself. Once the estimate names a
requirement or hinge, it can be used in the proof of that requirement or hinge.


30    Comparison And Transfer Boundary
The Navier–Stokes class proof is a same-solution Navier–Stokes proof. Euler comparison can be
illuminating because it shows what disappears when viscosity and parabolic participation are absent.
It becomes proof material here by landing an exact Navier–Stokes packet in the same witness tree.

Proposition 9 (Comparison transfer role). Let C be a comparison branch. It enters the present
Navier–Stokes class proof only through a theorem of the form

           C =⇒ selected Navier–Stokes packet Q has a Pack, Part, or Field decision.

That theorem is the point where comparison pressure becomes Navier–Stokes proof material.

Proof. The working class object OworkNS is built from the original Navier–Stokes datum, pressure,
viscosity, and same-fluid participation. Comparison branches may share shapes with Navier–Stokes
branches, but the class proof needs the exact same-solution packet. The transfer theorem supplies
that exact packet. Without it, the comparison branch has no class face inside Owork
                                                                                NS .


31    Whole-Space R3 CM Exterior-Source Branch
The periodic proof and the whole-space proof use the same CM discipline, but they do not have
the same compactness boundary. On T3 , the transported whole-torus material atlas has no spatial
escape. On R3 , a finite terminal claim can also try to hide mass or derivatives in the far field.
