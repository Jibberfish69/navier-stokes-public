11. Conclusion: the continuation estimate in action

The preceding steps have already produced the smooth endpoint and the same-history restart. The same
construction also answers the practical continuation question: can a fixed Sobolev norm ∥u(t)∥H s blow up as
t ↑ T∗ ?

Take the (1, 3)-rectangle R1,3 [u0 ; T∗ ] from the compatible intersection. Its zeroth adjacent row contains

                             u = V0 ∈ L2 (0, T∗ ; H 4 ),                ut = V1 ∈ L2 (0, T∗ ; H 2 ).

This is the time–space relation supplied by the equation. At this level the viscous term makes the spatial
shift explicit:
                               ut = ν∆u − P∇· (u ⊗ u),     ∆ : H 4 −→ H 2 .
The adjacent trace theorem now takes the middle height:

                            u ∈ L2t Hx4 ,            ut ∈ L2t Hx2       =⇒      u ∈ C([0, T∗ ]; H 3 ).

Consequently,
                                                B3 := sup ∥u(t)∥H 3 < ∞.
                                                          0≤t≤T∗

Since H 3 (R3 ) ↪→ W 1,∞ (R3 ), this finite projection of the intersection gives the continuation quantity
                                                ∫︂ T∗
                                         A :=           ∥∇u(t)∥L∞ dt ≤ CT∗ B3 < ∞.
                                                 0



Now use that derived estimate on the actual nonlinear evolution. For every integer s ≥ 3, the standard
Sobolev energy inequality gives

                            1 d
                                 ∥u(t)∥2H s + ν∥∇u(t)∥2H s ≤ Cs ∥∇u(t)∥L∞ ∥u(t)∥2H s .
                            2 dt

                                                        (︃    ∫︂ t              )︃
                      ∥u(t)∥2H s ≤ ∥u0 ∥2H s exp          2Cs      ∥∇u(τ )∥L∞ dτ ≤ ∥u0 ∥2H s e2Cs A .

                                              sup ∥u(t)∥H s < ∞                 (s ≥ 3).
                                            0≤t<T∗


For s = 3, this is exactly the norm used by the restart theorem in Step 8. The terminal datum remains
regular and the common lifespan δ0 stays positive, so the solution restarts past T∗ . In one line,
                                    ∫︂ T∗
     I[u0 ] −→ R1,3 [u0 ; T∗ ] −→           ∥∇u∥L∞ dt < ∞ −→ sup ∥u(t)∥H s < ∞ −→ continuation past T∗ .
