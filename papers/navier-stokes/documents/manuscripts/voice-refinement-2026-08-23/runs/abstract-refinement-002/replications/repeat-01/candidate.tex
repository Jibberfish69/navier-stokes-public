We prove global smoothness for the unforced three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes equations on \(\R^3\) for every viscosity \(\nu>0\) and every divergence-free Schwartz initial velocity \(u_0\).
Let \([0,T_*)\) be the maximal classical lifespan of the velocity generated by \(u_0\), and suppose that \(T_*<\infty\).
For the fixed lowest adjacent rectangle \((N,M)=(0,1)\), the whole-terminal estimate gives
\[
u\in L^2(0,T_*;H^2),
\qquad
u_t\in L^2(0,T_*;L^2).
\]
The adjacent trace identity then places this velocity in \(C([0,T_*];H^1)\), and the embedding \(H^1(\R^3)\hookrightarrow L^3(\R^3)\) yields
\[
\sup_{0\leq t\leq T_*}\|u(t)\|_{L^3(\R^3)}<\infty.
\]
This collides immediately with the Escauriaza--Seregin--\v{S}ver\'ak finite-endpoint criterion, which requires
\[
T_*<\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sup_{0<t<T_*}\|u(t)\|_{L^3(\R^3)}=\infty.
\]
Thus a finite singular endpoint cannot occur, and \(T_*=\infty\).
