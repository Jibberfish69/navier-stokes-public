We prove global smoothness for the unforced three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes equations on \(\R^3\) for every viscosity \(\nu>0\) and every divergence-free Schwartz initial velocity \(u_0\).
Let \([0,T_*)\) be the maximal classical lifespan of the solution generated by \(u_0\), and suppose that \(T_*<\infty\).
The lowest whole-terminal adjacent rows, obtained by fixing \((N,M)=(0,1)\), give
\[
u\in L^2(0,T_*;H^2),
\qquad
u_t\in L^2(0,T_*;L^2).
\]
The adjacent trace identity then yields
\[
u\in C([0,T_*];H^1),
\]
and the embedding \(H^1(\R^3)\hookrightarrow L^3(\R^3)\) gives
\[
\sup_{0\leq t\leq T_*}\|u(t)\|_{L^3(\R^3)}<\infty.
\]
This bound continues the same \(u_0\)-generated velocity through \(T_*\) by the endpoint theorem of Escauriaza, Seregin, and \v{S}ver\'ak, while a finite singular endpoint for that velocity on \((0,T_*)\) requires its \(L^3\) norm to escape every bound.
The two conclusions cannot hold on the same terminal interval, so \(T_*\) is infinite and the solution remains smooth for all \(t\geq0\).
