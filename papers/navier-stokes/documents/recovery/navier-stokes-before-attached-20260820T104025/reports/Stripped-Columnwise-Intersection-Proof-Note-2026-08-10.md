# One Fluid, Four Columnwise Intersections, and Terminal Restart

This note consolidates the constructive mathematics from the supplied collective note.
Repeated passages, status declarations, retrospective commentary, and alternative proof routes have been removed.
Every hypothesis used by a displayed implication is written at the point of use.

## 1. One datum and one pressure-complete history

Let

\[
u_0\in H^\infty(\mathbb R^3)\cap L^2(\mathbb R^3),
\qquad \nabla\cdot u_0=0,
\qquad \nu>0.
\]

Let \((u,p)\) be the maximal classical Navier--Stokes history generated by this datum on \([0,T_*)\):

\[
\partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p=\nu\Delta u,
\qquad
\nabla\cdot u=0,
\qquad
u(0)=u_0.
\tag{1.1}
\]

Taking divergence gives

\[
-\Delta p=\partial_i\partial_j(u_i u_j).
\tag{1.2}
\]

With \(R_i=\partial_i(-\Delta)^{-1/2}\), fix the whole-space pressure normalization

\[
p=R_iR_j(u_i u_j).
\tag{1.3}
\]

Pressure is a simultaneous response of the same velocity history; it introduces no independent datum.

## 2. The temporal binomial jet

Set

\[
V_n:=\partial_t^n u,
\qquad
P_n:=\partial_t^n p.
\tag{2.1}
\]

The temporal Leibniz rule gives

\[
\partial_t^n\bigl((u\cdot\nabla)u\bigr)
=
\sum_{a=0}^n\binom na(V_a\cdot\nabla)V_{n-a}.
\tag{2.2}
\]

The pressure and velocity rows satisfy

\[
P_n
=
R_iR_j
\sum_{a=0}^n\binom na V_{a,i}V_{n-a,j},
\tag{2.3}
\]

\[
V_{n+1}
=
\nu\Delta V_n
-
\sum_{a=0}^n\binom na(V_a\cdot\nabla)V_{n-a}
-\nabla P_n.
\tag{2.4}
\]

At \(t=0\), equations (2.3)--(2.4) recursively determine the complete initial temporal jet from \(u_0\).
Since differentiation, Sobolev multiplication, and the Riesz transforms preserve \(H^\infty\),

\[
V_n(0),P_n(0)\in H^\infty
\qquad(n\ge 0).
\tag{2.5}
\]

## 3. The pressure-explicit mixed jet

For a spatial multi-index \(\alpha\), define

\[
J_{n,\alpha}
:=
\partial_t^n\partial_x^\alpha u,
\qquad
\Pi_{n,\alpha}
:=
\partial_t^n\partial_x^\alpha p.
\tag{3.1}
\]

Applying \(\partial_x^\alpha\) to (2.4) gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
J_{n+1,\alpha}
&+
\sum_{a=0}^n
\sum_{\beta\le\alpha}
\binom na\binom\alpha\beta
(J_{a,\beta}\cdot\nabla)
J_{n-a,\alpha-\beta}
\\
&\qquad
+\nabla\Pi_{n,\alpha}
=
\nu\Delta J_{n,\alpha}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{3.2}
\]

The matching pressure row is

\[
\Pi_{n,\alpha}
=
R_iR_j
\sum_{a=0}^n
\sum_{\beta\le\alpha}
\binom na\binom\alpha\beta
J_{a,\beta,i}
J_{n-a,\alpha-\beta,j}.
\tag{3.3}
\]

For each fixed temporal order \(n\), the row

\[
\mathcal J_n
=
\{J_{n,\alpha}:\alpha\}
=
\{V_n,\nabla V_n,\nabla^2V_n,\ldots\}
\tag{3.4}
\]

contains the complete spatial derivative column of \(V_n\).
The temporal and spatial indices are independent coordinates of the same history.

## 4. Critical height and its completed pressure--transport rows

Let

\[
\Lambda=(-\Delta)^{1/2},
\qquad
E_s(t):=\frac12\|\Lambda^s u(t)\|_2^2.
\tag{4.1}
\]

Define the complete pressure--transport production at level \(s\) by

\[
\mathcal P_s
:=
-\operatorname{Re}
\left\langle
\Lambda^su,
\Lambda^s\bigl((u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p\bigr)
\right\rangle.
\tag{4.2}
\]

Pairing (1.1) with \(\Lambda^{2s}u\) gives

\[
E_s'=-2\nu E_{s+1}+\mathcal P_s.
\tag{4.3}
\]

The critical height is

\[
H:=E_{1/2}=\frac12\|\Lambda^{1/2}u\|_2^2.
\tag{4.4}
\]

The first three height rows are

\[
H'=-2\nu E_{3/2}+\mathcal P_{1/2},
\tag{4.5}
\]

\[
H''=4\nu^2E_{5/2}-2\nu\mathcal P_{3/2}+\mathcal P_{1/2}',
\tag{4.6}
\]

\[
H'''=-8\nu^3E_{7/2}
+4\nu^2\mathcal P_{5/2}
-2\nu\mathcal P_{3/2}'
+\mathcal P_{1/2}''.
\tag{4.7}
\]

Induction gives

\[
H^{(n)}
=
(-2\nu)^nE_{n+1/2}
+
\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
(-2\nu)^j
\partial_t^{\,n-1-j}\mathcal P_{j+1/2}.
\tag{4.8}
\]

Define the completed row

\[
R_n
:=
\frac{
H^{(n)}
-
\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
(-2\nu)^j
\partial_t^{\,n-1-j}\mathcal P_{j+1/2}
}{(-2\nu)^n}.
\tag{4.9}
\]

Then

\[
R_n=E_{n+1/2}.
\tag{4.10}
\]

The velocity-time rows contract to the height-time rows:

\[
H^{(n)}
=
\frac12
\sum_{a=0}^n
\binom na
\operatorname{Re}
\left\langle
\Lambda^{1/2}V_a,
\Lambda^{1/2}V_{n-a}
\right\rangle.
\tag{4.11}
\]

The exact diagonal is

| Temporal velocity row | Completed height row | Spatial energy |
|---|---|---|
| \(V_0=u\) | \(R_0=H\) | \(E_{1/2}\) |
| \(V_1=u_t\) | \(R_1=(H'-\mathcal P_{1/2})/(-2\nu)\) | \(E_{3/2}\) |
| \(V_2=u_{tt}\) | \(R_2=(H''-\mathcal P_{1/2}'+2\nu\mathcal P_{3/2})/(4\nu^2)\) | \(E_{5/2}\) |
| \(V_n=\partial_t^nu\) | \(R_n\) | \(E_{n+1/2}\) |

## 5. Four distinct columnwise intersections

### 5.1 The spatial Sobolev column

Define

\[
\mathcal S_\infty
:=
\bigcap_{n=0}^\infty H_x^{n+1/2}(\mathbb R^3).
\tag{5.1}
\]

For any spatial derivative order \(k\), choose \(n\) so that

\[
n+\frac12>k+\frac32.
\]

The three-dimensional Sobolev embedding gives

\[
H_x^{n+1/2}\hookrightarrow C_b^k.
\tag{5.2}
\]

Since \(k\) is arbitrary,

\[
\mathcal S_\infty\hookrightarrow C_x^\infty.
\tag{5.3}
\]

No individual Sobolev row is assigned all derivative orders; the complete compatible intersection is.

### 5.2 The critical-height temporal column

For a finite time interval \(I\), define

\[
\mathcal H_\infty(I)
:=
\bigcap_{r=0}^\infty H_t^r(I).
\tag{5.4}
\]

For each temporal derivative order \(k\), choose \(r>k+1/2\).
One-dimensional Sobolev embedding gives

\[
H_t^r(I)\hookrightarrow C_t^k(I),
\]

and hence

\[
\mathcal H_\infty(I)\hookrightarrow C_t^\infty(I).
\tag{5.5}
\]

At the same time, the completed rows (4.9)--(4.10) identify the spatial energies carried by this temporal column.
If every completed row is finite, and the kinetic \(L^2\) coordinate is retained to control low frequency, then

\[
\{R_n<\infty\text{ for every }n\}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
u\in\bigcap_{n\ge0}H_x^{n+1/2}
=
\mathcal S_\infty.
\tag{5.6}
\]

The complete height-column intersection reads both temporal smoothness of \(H\) and spatial smoothness of \(u\).

### 5.3 The velocity-time column

For a fixed spatial order \(m\), define

\[
\mathcal V_\infty(I;H_x^m)
:=
\bigcap_{r=0}^\infty H_t^r(I;H_x^m).
\tag{5.7}
\]

Vector-valued Sobolev embedding gives

\[
\mathcal V_\infty(I;H_x^m)
\hookrightarrow
C_t^\infty(I;H_x^m).
\tag{5.8}
\]

Intersecting in the spatial index as well gives

\[
\mathcal V_{\infty,\infty}(I)
:=
\bigcap_{r=0}^\infty
\bigcap_{m=0}^\infty
H_t^r(I;H_x^m).
\tag{5.9}
\]

Given arbitrary temporal and spatial derivative orders \(a\) and \(|\alpha|\), choose

\[
r>a+\frac12,
\qquad
m>|\alpha|+\frac32.
\]

Successive time and spatial Sobolev embeddings give

\[
\partial_t^a\partial_x^\alpha u\in C^0(I\times\mathbb R^3).
\]

Thus

\[
\mathcal V_{\infty,\infty}(I)
\hookrightarrow
C_{t,x}^\infty(I\times\mathbb R^3).
\tag{5.10}
\]

### 5.4 The complete pressure-explicit mixed NS column

For finite \(N,M\), retain every \(J_{n,\alpha}\) and \(\Pi_{n,\alpha}\) with

\[
0\le n\le N,
\qquad
|\alpha|\le M,
\]

together with every differentiated row (3.2)--(3.3) among those coordinates.

The all-row intersection is

\[
\mathcal N_\infty(I)
:=
\bigcap_{N=0}^\infty
\bigcap_{M=0}^\infty
\{\text{compatible pressure-explicit mixed rows through }(N,M)\}.
\tag{5.11}
\]

Every finite mixed derivative occurs in some finite block, and common coordinates in two blocks are the same derivatives of the same history.
Applying the preceding mixed Sobolev embedding coordinatewise gives

\[
\mathcal N_\infty(I)
\hookrightarrow
C_{t,x}^\infty(I\times\mathbb R^3).
\tag{5.12}
\]

The four intersections are nested simultaneous readings of one fluid history.

## 6. Finite rectangles and projective compatibility

For \(T<T_*\), let \(I_T=(0,T)\), and define

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathfrak R_{N,M}(u;T)
:={}&
\sum_{n=0}^N
\|V_n\|_{L^2(I_T;H^{M+1})}^2
\\
&+
\sum_{n=0}^N
\|V_{n+1}\|_{L^2(I_T;H^{M-1})}^2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{6.1}
\]

The corresponding pressure-complete record is

\[
\mathcal R_{N,M}[u_0;T]
:=
\bigl(
\{V_n|_{I_T}\}_{n=0}^{N+1},
\{P_n|_{I_T}\}_{n=0}^{N}
\bigr),
\tag{6.2}
\]

with the recurrences (2.3)--(2.4) retained.

For each \(T<T_*\), classical persistence gives

\[
\mathfrak R_{N,M}(u;T)<\infty
\qquad(N,M<\infty).
\tag{6.3}
\]

If \(N'\le N\), \(M'\le M\), and \(T'\le T\), restriction of the larger record gives the smaller record:

\[
\rho^{N,M,T}_{N',M',T'}
\mathcal R_{N,M}[u_0;T]
=
\mathcal R_{N',M'}[u_0;T'].
\tag{6.4}
\]

The whole-terminal finite-column statement has the orderwise form

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{0<T<T_*}
\mathfrak R_{N,M}(u;T)
\le
C_{N,M}(u_0,\nu,T_*)
<\infty
}
\qquad(N,M<\infty).
\tag{6.5}
\]

Each finite pair \((N,M)\) carries its own constant.
No constant uniform over all column depths is part of (6.5).

When (6.5) holds for every finite \((N,M)\), monotone convergence gives the endpoint-spanning rows

\[
V_n\in L^2(0,T_*;H^{m+1}),
\qquad
V_{n+1}\in L^2(0,T_*;H^{m-1})
\tag{6.6}
\]

for every finite \((n,m)\).
Compatibility (6.4) then gives the projective family

\[
\mathcal I[u_0]
:=
\varprojlim_{N,M}
\mathcal R_{N,M}[u_0;T_*].
\tag{6.7}
\]

## 7. The adjacent trace inside each column

Fix \(n,m\), and assume the adjacent pair (6.6).
With \(A=(1-\Delta)^{1/2}\), Fourier truncation gives

\[
\frac{d}{dt}\|V_n(t)\|_{H^m}^2
=
2\operatorname{Re}
\bigl(
A^{m-1}V_{n+1},
A^{m+1}V_n
\bigr)_{L^2}.
\tag{7.1}
\]

Hence

\[
\begin{aligned}
\left|
\|V_n(t)\|_{H^m}^2
-
\|V_n(r)\|_{H^m}^2
\right|
\le{}&
2
\|V_{n+1}\|_{L^2(r,t;H^{m-1})}
\\
&\times
\|V_n\|_{L^2(r,t;H^{m+1})}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{7.2}
\]

Thus

\[
V_n\in C([0,T_*];H^m).
\tag{7.3}
\]

For the critical spatial row, the finite block containing

\[
u\in L^2(0,T_*;H^{5/2}),
\qquad
u_t\in L^2(0,T_*;H^{1/2})
\tag{7.4}
\]

gives

\[
E_{3/2}(t)-E_{3/2}(r)
=
\int_r^t
\operatorname{Re}
\bigl(
\Lambda^{1/2}u_t,
\Lambda^{5/2}u
\bigr)_2
\,ds.
\tag{7.5}
\]

Consequently,

\[
\sup_{t<T_*}E_{3/2}(t)
\le
E_{3/2}(0)
+
\|u_t\|_{L_t^2H_x^{1/2}}
\|u\|_{L_t^2H_x^{5/2}}.
\tag{7.6}
\]

Since \(T_*<\infty\),

\[
E_{3/2}\in L^\infty(0,T_*)\cap L^1(0,T_*).
\tag{7.7}
\]

The stronger coordinates

\[
u,u_t\in L^2(0,T_*;H^1)
\tag{7.8}
\]

give

\[
H'
=
\operatorname{Re}
\bigl(
\Lambda^{1/2}u_t,
\Lambda^{1/2}u
\bigr)
\in L^1(0,T_*).
\tag{7.9}
\]

Combining (4.5), (7.7), and (7.9),

\[
\mathcal P_{1/2}
=
H'+2\nu E_{3/2}
\in L^1(0,T_*).
\tag{7.10}
\]

## 8. The base spatial row and the common endpoint

The zeroth temporal row of (6.6), intersected over every spatial order, gives

\[
u\in
\mathscr S_0(0,T_*)
:=
\bigcap_{m=0}^\infty
L^2(0,T_*;H^m).
\tag{8.1}
\]

For every integer \(m\ge0\), Sobolev multiplication and boundedness of the Leray projector give

\[
\|\mathbb P\nabla\cdot(u\otimes u)\|_{H^m}
\le
C_m\|u\|_{H^{m+2}}^2.
\tag{8.2}
\]

Thus

\[
\int_0^{T_*}
\|\mathbb P\nabla\cdot(u\otimes u)\|_{H^m}
\,dt
\le
C_m
\|u\|_{L_t^2H_x^{m+2}}^2
<\infty.
\tag{8.3}
\]

Also,

\[
\int_0^{T_*}\|\Delta u\|_{H^m}\,dt
\le
\sqrt{T_*}
\|u\|_{L_t^2H_x^{m+2}}
<\infty.
\tag{8.4}
\]

The original projected equation gives

\[
u_t
=
\nu\Delta u
-
\mathbb P\nabla\cdot(u\otimes u)
\in
L^1(0,T_*;H^m)
\tag{8.5}
\]

for every \(m\).
Since \(u\in L^1(0,T_*;H^m)\),

\[
u\in W^{1,1}(0,T_*;H^m)
\hookrightarrow
C([0,T_*];H^m).
\tag{8.6}
\]

The endpoint representatives agree under the embeddings \(H^{m+1}\hookrightarrow H^m\), because they are limits of the same velocity history.
Hence

\[
u_*:=u(T_*)
\in
\bigcap_{m=0}^\infty H^m
=
H^\infty.
\tag{8.7}
\]

## 9. The pressure-complete endpoint jet

Let

\[
v_n:=V_n(T_*).
\]

Starting with \(v_0=u_*\), define

\[
P_{n,*}
=
R_iR_j
\sum_{a=0}^n
\binom na
v_{a,i}v_{n-a,j},
\tag{9.1}
\]

\[
v_{n+1}
=
\nu\Delta v_n
-
\sum_{a=0}^n
\binom na
(v_a\cdot\nabla)v_{n-a}
-\nabla P_{n,*}.
\tag{9.2}
\]

Closure of \(H^\infty\) under differentiation and multiplication, together with boundedness of the Riesz transforms, gives

\[
v_n,P_{n,*}\in H^\infty
\qquad(n\ge0).
\tag{9.3}
\]

Equations (2.3)--(2.4), (7.3), and (9.1)--(9.2) identify this recursively generated jet with the terminal trace of the actual pressure-complete history:

\[
v_n
=
\lim_{t\uparrow T_*}\partial_t^n u(t),
\qquad
P_{n,*}
=
\lim_{t\uparrow T_*}\partial_t^n p(t).
\tag{9.4}
\]

Every mixed derivative reaches the endpoint, and

\[
u,p\in C^\infty([0,T_*]\times\mathbb R^3).
\tag{9.5}
\]

## 10. Restart

The endpoint datum \(u_*\in H^\infty\cap L^2\) is divergence-free.
Classical local existence produces a smooth Navier--Stokes solution beginning from \(u_*\) on \([T_*,T_*+\delta)\).
Its initial temporal jet is generated by the same pressure-explicit recurrences (9.1)--(9.2), so it agrees with the terminal jet (9.4).
Classical uniqueness identifies the left and right histories as one continuation.

If \(T_*<\infty\) were maximal, this continuation would be impossible.
The columnwise terminal estimate (6.5), together with the derived intersections above, yields

\[
T_*=\infty,
\qquad
u,p\in C^\infty([0,\infty)\times\mathbb R^3).
\tag{10.1}
\]

## 11. Consolidated dependency chain

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
u_0
&\longrightarrow
\text{one pressure-complete NS history}
\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{temporal binomial jet and mixed spatial columns}
\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{completed critical-height/VPI rows}
\\
&\longrightarrow
\{\text{finite compatible rectangles with (6.5)}\}_{N,M}
\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{four columnwise intersections}
\\
&\longrightarrow
u(T_*)\in H^\infty
\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{actual pressure-complete endpoint jet}
\\
&\longrightarrow
\text{same-history restart}
\\
&\longrightarrow
T_*=\infty.
\end{aligned}
}
\]
