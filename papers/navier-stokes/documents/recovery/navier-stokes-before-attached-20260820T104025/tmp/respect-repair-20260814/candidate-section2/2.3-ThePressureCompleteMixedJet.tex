\subsection{The Pressure-Complete Mixed Jet}\label{sub:ThePressureCompleteMixedJet}

The temporal tower from \cref{sub:SingularityAndTheDerivativeTower} is already present in the original Navier--Stokes evolution.
For the one solution fixed above,
\[
  \partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p
  =
  \nu\Delta u,
  \qquad
  \nabla\cdot u=0.
\]
Solving this equation for the first Eulerian response gives
\[
  V_1
  =
  \nu\Delta V_0-(V_0\cdot\nabla)V_0-\nabla Q_0,
\]
where \(V_0=u\) and \(Q_0=p\).
The new time row is assembled from two spatial derivatives of velocity, one spatial derivative of pressure, and the nonlinear interaction of velocity with its own gradient.
Time has entered, but it has entered through space.

Differentiate the equation once in time.
The next response obeys
\[
  V_2
  +(V_1\cdot\nabla)V_0
  +(V_0\cdot\nabla)V_1
  +\nabla Q_1
  =
  \nu\Delta V_1.
\]
One further differentiation splits the transport term three ways:
\begin{align*}
  V_3
  &+(V_2\cdot\nabla)V_0
  +2(V_1\cdot\nabla)V_1
  +(V_0\cdot\nabla)V_2
  \\
  &+\nabla Q_2
  =
  \nu\Delta V_2.
\end{align*}
The binomial pattern is forced by repeated differentiation of the same nonlinear product.
With
\[
  V_n:=\partial_t^n u,
  \qquad
  Q_n:=\partial_t^n p,
\]
the equation at temporal depth \(n\) is
\[
  V_{n+1}
  +
  \sum_{a=0}^{n}
  \binom{n}{a}
  (V_a\cdot\nabla)V_{n-a}
  +\nabla Q_n
  =
  \nu\Delta V_n,
  \qquad
  \nabla\cdot V_n=0.
\]

Pressure cannot be removed from this response by calling it a constraint.
Taking the divergence determines the same temporal pressure row from the same velocity rows:
\[
  -\Delta Q_n
  =
  \partial_i\partial_j
  \sum_{a=0}^{n}
  \binom{n}{a}
  (V_a)_i(V_{n-a})_j.
\]
Rapid decay fixes the additive normalization, so the Riesz-transform form is
\[
  Q_n
  =
  R_iR_j
  \sum_{a=0}^{n}
  \binom{n}{a}
  (V_a)_i(V_{n-a})_j.
\]
Pressure is slaved to velocity, but it is slaved at every row rather than absent from the rows.

The temporal recurrence still hides the spatial distribution inside each product.
Differentiate one transport factor in the coordinate direction \(x_\ell\):
\[
  \partial_\ell\bigl((V_a\cdot\nabla)V_{n-a}\bigr)
  =
  ((\partial_\ell V_a)\cdot\nabla)V_{n-a}
  +(V_a\cdot\nabla)\partial_\ell V_{n-a}.
\]
At higher spatial order, every derivative must choose between the two factors.
For \(\alpha\in\Nzero^3\), retain the velocity coordinate from the tower and attach the pressure coordinate at the same depth:
\[
  J_{n,\alpha}
  :=
  \partial_t^n\partial_x^\alpha u,
  \qquad
  \Pi_{n,\alpha}
  :=
  \partial_t^n\partial_x^\alpha p,
\]
and for \(\beta\le\alpha\), set
\[
  \binom{\alpha}{\beta}
  :=
  \prod_{k=1}^{3}\binom{\alpha_k}{\beta_k}.
\]
The simultaneous temporal and spatial product rules give
\[
  \partial_t^n\partial_x^\alpha\bigl((u\cdot\nabla)u\bigr)
  =
  \sum_{a=0}^{n}
  \sum_{\beta\le\alpha}
  \binom{n}{a}
  \binom{\alpha}{\beta}
  (J_{a,\beta}\cdot\nabla)
  J_{n-a,\alpha-\beta}.
\]
Substitution into the equation exposes the full mixed recurrence:
\begin{align*}
  J_{n+1,\alpha}
  &+
  \sum_{a=0}^{n}
  \sum_{\beta\le\alpha}
  \binom{n}{a}
  \binom{\alpha}{\beta}
  (J_{a,\beta}\cdot\nabla)
  J_{n-a,\alpha-\beta}
  \\
  &+\nabla\Pi_{n,\alpha}
  =
  \nu\Delta J_{n,\alpha},
  \qquad
  \nabla\cdot J_{n,\alpha}=0.
\end{align*}
Its pressure coordinates satisfy
\[
  -\Delta\Pi_{n,\alpha}
  =
  \partial_i\partial_j
  \sum_{a=0}^{n}
  \sum_{\beta\le\alpha}
  \binom{n}{a}
  \binom{\alpha}{\beta}
  (J_{a,\beta})_i
  (J_{n-a,\alpha-\beta})_j.
\]

This is the mixed jet of the original pressure-complete solution.
The temporal rows, spatial columns, nonlinear splits, divergence relations, and pressure rows are coordinates of one object generated by the original equations.
Nothing later generates a second jet or selects derivatives from outside this one.
A finite rectangle will only cut out finitely many of these coordinates after the equation has produced them.

The escalation now has a precise geometry: climbing one temporal row repeatedly opens the spatial column already coupled to it.
Critical height is the first scalar at which that column can be read without losing the scale singled out by the endpoint threat.
