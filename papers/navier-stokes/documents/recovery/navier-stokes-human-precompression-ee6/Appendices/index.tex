\section{Local Construction and Classical Estimates}\label{app:LocalConstructionAndClassicalEstimates}

A maximal history is not merely a chain of short solutions; its last time must
be tied to a norm that the equation can test.  The local construction makes that
link, and the classical estimates show why finite kinetic energy alone cannot
keep the history smooth.

\section{Derivative and Finite-Record Calculus}\label{app:DerivativeAndFiniteRecordCalculus}\label{sec:FromCriticalHeightToCompatibleFiniteViews}

Strict classicality gives every derivative before \(T_*\), but it does not tell
us which finite part of that information can survive uniformly as the cutoff
approaches the endpoint.  The calculus here turns the derivative family into
finite records so the terminal estimate has a precise object to keep bounded.
