\section{The Regularity Problem}\label{sec:TheRegularityProblem}

The question begins with an ordinary promise and one extraordinary doubt.  We
are allowed to start the fluid as smoothly as we like.  We are not allowed to
assume that this smoothness survives the motion it creates.  Before following
that motion toward a stretching vortex, we fix exactly what was put into the
equation, what the equation is being asked to return, and where the known
energy control stops short of that return.
