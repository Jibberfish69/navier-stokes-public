\section{The Intersection Inside the Tower}\label{sec:TheIntersectionInsideTheTower}

Section~\ref{sec:CriticalHeightAndDerivativeTower} did not discover one
privileged Sobolev estimate.  It discovered two directions inside the equation.
Temporal order moves through the responses \(V_0,V_1,V_2,\ldots\); spatial
order moves through the Sobolev column carried by each response.  We now make
that relation exact, identify the finite pieces that possess time traces, and
assemble those pieces without changing their shared coordinates.
