\section{Critical Height and the Derivative Tower}\label{sec:CriticalHeightAndDerivativeTower}

The energy balance left one precise freedom.  It controls the amount of
velocity and the time accumulated by one spatial derivative, but it does not
stop the same velocity change from being pressed through successively smaller
distances.  In three dimensions the equation contains a mechanism capable of
attempting that compression: a vortex may be stretched along its axis while
incompressibility contracts its cross-section.  We follow the same vortex
through that motion.  Its narrowing forces us to compare stretching with
viscosity, the comparison exposes the scale the energy balance cannot see,
and a finite terminal time then forces the fluid to cross its changing states
with less and less time left.  Velocity, acceleration, jerk, and the rest of
the derivative Tower enter because the proposed singular history must produce
those changes; they are not machinery added from outside the equation.
