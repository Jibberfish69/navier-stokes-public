\subsection{Restarting the Same History}\label{sub:RestartingTheSameHistory}

Use the endpoint itself as the restart datum.  The local theorem gives a
right-hand solution on one interval for all finite Sobolev heights.  Its jet and
the left trace jet both begin from \(u_*\) and obey the same normalized
recurrence; the matching jets make the join smooth, and uniqueness makes the
two pieces one history.

\begin{lemma}[Smooth local restart and uniqueness]\label[lemma]{lem:SmoothLocalRestart}
Let \(t_0\geq0\) and \(a\in\Hinfty_\sigma(\R^3)\).  There are
\(\delta>0\) and a unique smooth normalized pair \((w,q)\) on
\([t_0,t_0+\delta]\times\R^3\), with \(w(t_0)=a\) and
\[
  q=R_iR_j(w_iw_j).
\]
All finite spatial Sobolev orders persist on this same interval.  The temporal
jet at \(t_0\) is generated by
\eqref{eq:SectionThreeEndpointPressureJet}--\eqref{eq:SectionThreeEndpointVelocityJet}.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
Translate time and apply Proposition~\ref{prop:LocalMaximalHistory} to \(a\).
Its lifespan depends only on \(\nu\) and \(\norm{a}_{H^3}\), while persistence
carries every finite height of \(a\) on that interval.  The normalized pair is
unique.  Differentiating at the initial face and using the Riesz pressure formula
gives the endpoint recurrences.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Global continuation from whole-terminal rectangles]\label{thm:CompatibleIntersectionClosure}
Let \(u_0\) and all its spatial derivatives be rapidly decaying, with
\(\nabla\cdot u_0=0\), and fix \(\nu>0\).  Let \((u,p)\) be its maximal
normalized classical history.  If a hypothetical finite branch satisfies
\Cref{ass:BodyWholeTerminalRectangleCondition}, then \(T_*=\infty\) and
\((u,p)\) is smooth on every finite interval.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Suppose instead that \(T_*<\infty\).  The hypothesis supplies every terminal
rectangle with compatible overlaps.  Their intersection produces the common
endpoint \(u_*\in\Hinfty_\sigma\) and its actual normalized jet.  Restart the
local theorem at \(T_*\) from \(u_*\), obtaining \((w,q)\) on
\([T_*,T_*+\delta]\), and set
\[
  (\widetilde u,\widetilde p)(t)
  :=
  \begin{cases}
    (u,p)(t),&0\leq t<T_*,\\
    (w,q)(t),&T_*\leq t\leq T_*+\delta.
  \end{cases}
\]
The two velocities meet at \(u_*\).  Every one-sided pressure and velocity
derivative also agrees because both jets are generated from that datum by the
same normalized recurrences.  The piecewise pair is smooth across \(T_*\) and
solves the same fixed-viscosity equation beyond it.  Strong uniqueness makes it
the continuation of the original datum-generated history, contradicting
maximality.  Hence
\[
  T_*=\infty.
\]
On each finite interval, every finite temporal and spatial coordinate remains in
the compatible intersection.  Sobolev embedding then gives
\[
  u,p\in C^\infty([0,\infty)\times\R^3).
\]
\end{proof}
