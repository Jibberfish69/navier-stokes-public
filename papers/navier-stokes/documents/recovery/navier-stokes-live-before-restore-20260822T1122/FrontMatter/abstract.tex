Let \(u\) be the maximal classical solution of the three-dimensional
incompressible Navier--Stokes equations on \(\R^3\), generated by a
divergence-free Schwartz datum \(u_0\) at viscosity \(\nu>0\).  The regularity
problem asks whether its maximal time \(T_*\) can be finite.  The threat is
visible in a stretching vortex: it can narrow while kinetic energy remains
finite.  Scaling leaves the critical \(\dot H^{1/2}\) height unchanged, and
viscosity assigns a width \(r\) the shorter clock \(r^2/\nu\), so shrinking
clocks can fit before a finite time.  Yet the fluid must cross every change.
Its temporal responses come from the same equation, whose viscosity couples
that Tower to higher spatial derivatives.

On each strict subinterval, viscosity genuinely gains one spatial derivative
in every finite response.  At a proposed last time, that estimate must instead
survive every approaching cutoff.  The lowest payment is
\[
  D_*:=\int_0^{T_*}\norm{\Lambda^{3/2}u(t)}_2^2\,dt<\infty.
\]
This is equivalent to \(u\in L^2(0,T_*;H^2)\),
\(u_t\in L^2(0,T_*;L^2)\), and to a bounded upper prefix of the signed
critical production.  Each form gives
\(u\in C([0,T_*];H^1)\subset L^\infty(0,T_*;L^3)\); the endpoint
\(L^3\) criterion then excludes a finite \(T_*\).

Can kinetic energy make that payment?  With
\(Z=\norm{\Lambda u}_2^2\) and \(Y=\norm{\Lambda^2u}_2^2\), the direct
enstrophy route stops at
\[
  Z'+\nu Y\leq C\nu^{-3}Z^3,
\]
while energy controls only \(\int Z\).  Viscosity absorbs small critical data,
and energy carries any history surviving beyond a datum-dependent horizon into
that regime.  An earlier large-data singularity remains open.  Energy pays
every fixed low-frequency range, so any failure of \(D_*\) is ultraviolet.

The same endpoint-uniform payment at every finite order produces one
\(H^\infty\) terminal state and a same-history restart, returning conditionally
to the vortex and local singularity tests.  The manuscript does not derive
\(D_*<\infty\) from arbitrary
\((u_0,\nu)\).  That ultraviolet estimate remains the missing step between the
datum and global regularity.
