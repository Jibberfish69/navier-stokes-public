\section{The Regularity Problem}\label{sec:TheRegularityProblem}

The question here is whether the three-dimensional Navier--Stokes equation can
take perfectly smooth initial data and produce a first singular time.

We start by fixing the data, the equation, and the exact global conclusion.
The initial field is not allowed to carry roughness into the problem, and a
solution on every shorter interval is not yet a solution through its alleged
last time.  Once those two points are explicit, the regularity problem becomes
concrete: can the same velocity reach \(T_*\) with every derivative still
finite, or can the equation force one of those derivatives to escape while
kinetic energy remains bounded?
