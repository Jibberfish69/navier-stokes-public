# Forced global-smoothness derivation order

Every movement belongs to the single history generated by the prescribed
triple \((u_0,\nu,f)\). The intersection and critical-height rectangle
relations are independent constructions from the same mixed jet. They meet
when the intersection is projected onto a finite rectangle and the complete
rectangle relations are integrated.

The intersection and the rectangle identities do not prove one another. The
datum-generated whole-terminal theorem supplies the coordinates on \((0,T_*)\); the
critical-height and viscous construction supplies the complete equations
relating those coordinates. For each fixed finite \((N,M)\), their composition
gives the rectangle estimate. The proof uses the bound for the selected finite
rectangle and requires no constant uniform in all derivative orders.

## Consequences

Finite rectangles also supply the matrix formulations, material-trajectory
bounds, classical space–time regularity norms, and the higher-order
quantitative catalogue developed in the later sections. The explicit
small-critical estimates are retained as a separate quantitative regime
inside the general continuation framework.
