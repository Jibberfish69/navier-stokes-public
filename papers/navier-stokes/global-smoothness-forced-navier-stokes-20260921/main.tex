\begin{abstract}
Fix a smooth divergence-free finite-energy datum \(u_0\), a viscosity
\(\nu>0\), and a prescribed
\(f\in C^\infty([0,\infty);\mathcal S(\R^3;\R^3))\).  The resulting forced
three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes evolution is carried as one
pressure-normalized history.  For every finite pair of temporal and spatial
orders, the datum generates a compatible whole-terminal rectangle containing
the corresponding adjacent rows of its velocity--pressure--force mixed jet.
Each rectangle keeps every selected derivative norm finite through the chosen
finite endpoint, and restriction compatibility assembles the rectangles into
the complete space--time intersection.  Independently, the critical-height
construction and the positive-sign viscous tether supply the relationship
identities on the same coordinates.  Pairing, squaring, and integrating the
complete forced rows keeps viscosity, the full binomial transport term,
normalized pressure, and forcing inside the estimates.  Since every finite
mixed order lies in one of these rectangles, adjacent rows produce one
\(H^\infty\) endpoint, the forced recurrences determine its
complete pressure-normalized jet, and local theory restarts the original
history with the same prescribed absolute-time force.  No finite maximal time
can occur, and the forced solution is globally smooth and unique.
\end{abstract}
