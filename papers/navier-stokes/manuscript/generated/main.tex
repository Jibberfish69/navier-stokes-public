\begin{abstract}
We prove global existence and smoothness for the unforced three-dimensional
incompressible Navier--Stokes equations on \(\R^3\), for every positive
viscosity and every divergence-free Schwartz initial velocity.  Let \((u,p)\)
be the pressure-normalized maximal classical solution on \([0,T_*)\).  The
main estimate sends the datum to a compatible family of finite mixed
rectangles spanning the whole interval \((0,T_*)\): each chosen temporal row
is controlled in two adjacent spatial Sobolev spaces, and the differentiated
pressure law retains every term generated by transport.  If \(T_*\) were
finite, the adjacent controls would give an endpoint trace at every finite
order.  Compatibility identifies those traces as one \(H^\infty\) velocity
and pressure jet, while the limiting momentum and pressure recurrences make
that jet the initial jet of a local restart.  Uniqueness joins the restart to
the original history, contradicting maximality.  The proof also keeps the
size of its conclusion.  Once global smoothness is known, a
residual-certificate construction takes \(u_0\), \(\nu\), a finite horizon,
and a requested Sobolev order and produces a finite checkable majorant for
the solution.  For effectively represented data and computable \(\nu\) and
horizon, a fixed dovetailed search terminates with such a verified numerical
majorant.  Substitution into the rectangle construction gives explicit finite
recursions for all mixed velocity and pressure norms, critical-height mass and
frequency tails, endpoint moduli, and restart lifespan, with every datum order
and horizon dependence displayed.  Replacing the base producer while retaining
the mixed-row, pressure, endpoint, and restart construction gives quantitative
global theorems for three-dimensional axisymmetric flow without swirl,
critical hyperdissipation, small critical-data viscous--resistive MHD, and the
periodic Navier--Stokes--Voigt equation; the last application also yields an
explicit radius of time analyticity.

\end{abstract}
