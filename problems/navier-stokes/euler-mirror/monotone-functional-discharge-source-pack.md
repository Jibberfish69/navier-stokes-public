## Target Obligation

- `bridge-control-functional`

## Exact Bridge Claim

The parent route names a monotone functional `Q(t)` as one of the four
principles. After deleting viscosity, the exact surviving same-surface law is
the Euler energy conservation identity:

```math
Q_E^{base}(t) := \|u(t)\|_{L^2_x}^2,
\qquad
\frac{d}{dt}Q_E^{base}(t) = 0.
```

This preserves the *bridge slot* but not the full parent `Q(t)=\|u\|_{L^2}^2 +
\|\nabla u\|_{L^2}^2` monotone package.

## Exact Consequence

The Euler mirror can say honestly:

1. the control-functional slot still exists;
2. `L^2` conservation is the exact surviving baseline law;
3. any stronger named control object must be rewritten on Euler terms rather
   than inherited from Navier-Stokes.

## Honest Boundary

The following do **not** survive automatically after removing `\nu \Delta u`:

1. `H^1`-level monotonicity;
2. dissipative Gronwall control of the parent `Q(t)`;
3. direct compactness or continuation closure from the parent proof.
