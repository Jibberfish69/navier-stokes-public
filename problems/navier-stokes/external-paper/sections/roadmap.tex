\section{Proof Roadmap}

This roadmap records the conditional periodic continuation route for the three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes equation on \(\mathbb T^3\).  It is not a closed proof until the signed critical-height sign-persistence / peak-height theorem supplies the open Gold \(L^1\) source input.  The route has four PDE steps.

First, a terminal same-fluid tail either admits a positive-scale family of scale-critical good parabolic cylinders or loses every uniform positive good scale.  The good-cylinder alternative gives a finite cover after a standard covering selection.

Second, on each good moving cylinder, a Caffarelli--Kohn--Nirenberg type smallness condition gives finite-depth local regularity.  Testing the affine-subtracted equation against a transported cutoff gives a local affine-excess estimate.  A common scheduler and the finite cover globalize these local estimates into a terminal transported-tower bound.

Third, any first finite terminal endpoint must be one of four primitive failures: participation failure, packing failure, averaged tower-amplitude escape, or loss of terminal good scale.  After the open coupled-storage theorem is supplied, the terminal package eliminates all four alternatives.

Fourth, the averaged endpoint package yields a finite readout cover.  On that cover, averaged field and tower bounds recover the pointwise endpoint package.  The pointwise package gives a uniform \(H^s(\mathbb T^3)\) bound for some \(s>5/2\), and the standard periodic local theory extends the solution past any alleged finite endpoint.

The conditional route is stated on \(\mathbb T^3\).  A whole-space result on \(\mathbb R^3\) is a separate export problem.
