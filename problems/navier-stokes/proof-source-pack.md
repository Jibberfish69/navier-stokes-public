## Authority boundary

This pack is Navier--Stokes-only. The monograph, Yang--Mills lane, and Hodge
Conjecture lane supply no theorem, constant, operator, coercive estimate,
physical mechanism, or completion claim to this proof program. Black-hole and
spacetime-model analogies are excluded.

The governing sources are the direct Navier--Stokes corpus and lane-local
theorem files that prove a named bridge for the classical incompressible
equations.
