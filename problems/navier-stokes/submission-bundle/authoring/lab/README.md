# Lab

The lab is the non-authoritative workbench for source material and conversion
attempts.

It has three active shelves:

- `transcripts/`: raw and semi-processed conversation bundles.
- `experiments/`: rendered attempts to turn source material into reader-facing
  proof explanation or educational examples.
- `example-math-papers/`: external PDF models for exposition, scale, and proof
  structure.

Lab files can be mined for math, wording, pacing, and explanation style. They
do not become manuscript text because they exist here.
