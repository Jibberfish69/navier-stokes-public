# Example Math Papers

This folder is a local reading shelf for external model papers. These are not NS proof samples and not PDF templates. They are outside `templates/` on purpose: the job is to study readable mathematical exposition and formal proof-paper structure before turning those lessons into our own PDF-writing machinery.

The downloaded third-party PDFs are intentionally ignored by git. Keep the index tracked; keep the article files local unless there is an explicit reason to vendor external PDFs into the repository.

## Downloaded PDFs

| File | Paper | Local source | Why it is here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `2016-wright-rational-billiards-to-moduli-spaces.pdf` | Alex Wright, "From rational billiards to dynamics on moduli spaces" | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.08290 | Broad expository route from simple examples to a deep modern theorem. |
| `2016-cohn-conceptual-breakthrough-sphere-packing.pdf` | Henry Cohn, "A conceptual breakthrough in sphere packing" | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.01685 | Clear explanation of why the proof idea matters before the technical engine takes over. |
| `2017-wilkinson-lyapunov-exponents.pdf` | Amie Wilkinson, "What are Lyapunov exponents, and why are they interesting?" | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.02843 | Concept-first exposition for a hard analytic/dynamical object. |
| `2017-ellenberg-gijswijt-cap-sets.pdf` | Jordan Ellenberg and Dion Gijswijt, "On large subsets of F_q^n with no three-term arithmetic progression" | Annals: https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2017/185-1/p08 | Compact proof-paper model with a central mechanism a reader can hold. |
| `2017-viazovska-sphere-packing-dimension-8.pdf` | Maryna Viazovska, "The sphere packing problem in dimension 8" | Annals: https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2017/185-3/p07 | Dense formal proof-paper discipline, useful as a Clay-level formatting and proof-payment reference. |
| `2019-margalit-mathematics-of-joan-birman.pdf` | Dan Margalit, "The Mathematics of Joan Birman" | Extracted from March 2019 Notices full issue mirror: http://cm2vivi2002.free.fr/notices-ams-201903.pdf | Model for a readable intellectual arc rather than a result ledger. |
| `2020-piccirillo-conway-knot-not-slice.pdf` | Lisa Piccirillo, "The Conway knot is not slice" | Annals: https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2020/191-2/p05 | Short, direct, theorem-targeted proof paper. |
| `2022-hom-getting-a-handle-on-the-conway-knot.pdf` | Jennifer Hom, "Getting a handle on the Conway knot" | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.09171 | Model for explaining a famous proof route and its obstacles cleanly. |
| `2023-park-threshold-phenomena-random-discrete-structures.pdf` | Jinyoung Park, "Threshold phenomena for random discrete structures" | arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13823 | Gentle onboarding through examples before abstraction. |
