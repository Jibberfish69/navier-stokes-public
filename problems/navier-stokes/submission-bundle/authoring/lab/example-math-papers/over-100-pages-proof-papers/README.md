# Over 100 Pages Proof Papers

This local shelf holds long proof-heavy papers.

Use these PDFs to study how a major proof pays obligations over many sections:
front-door statement, route explanation, lemma dependency, proof pacing,
appendix discipline, and reader trust across a large artifact.

This is not a Navier-Stokes proof-source shelf. Promote only extracted writing
or structure lessons into `../../../writing-samples/` or `../../../templates/`.
