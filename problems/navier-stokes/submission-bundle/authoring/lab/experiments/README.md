# Experiments

This shelf holds conversion attempts.

An experiment takes source material from transcripts, notes, or model papers
and tries to make something readable: a proof excerpt, educational example,
worked derivation, or style trial.

Experiments are not templates. They are allowed to be provisional, partial, or
bad. Their job is to show what can be rescued, what should be rewritten, and
what should never be promoted.

When an experiment works as prose, extract a curated passage into
`../../writing-samples/`. When its form is reusable, abstract that form into
`../../templates/`. When its math is correct and needed, rewrite it into the
manuscript source.
