\section{Selected-Packet Euler Boundary Theorem}

This appendix records the only Euler material used by the Navier--Stokes
submission.  Euler enters as a boundary reading of the same selected terminal
packet after the Navier--Stokes class test has already named the packet and its
first Part or Field face.  Its proof role is comparison after the
selected packet has already been typed.

\begin{definition}[Base Euler boundary reading]
For a selected terminal packet \(Q\), write
\[
  \Member_E(Q):=\mathrm{EVol}_Q(u)\wedge \mathrm{EMom}_Q(u,p).
\]
Here \(\mathrm{EVol}_Q\) means incompressible flux-volume readability on
\(Q\), and \(\mathrm{EMom}_Q\) means weak inviscid momentum-pressure
readability on the same packet.  This base Euler reading consists of that
volume-momentum pair; fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes participation and
positive-scale Navier--Stokes Field coherence are stronger predicates.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Clean selected-packet Euler boundary]
A selected Navier--Stokes terminal packet \(Q\) is in the clean Euler boundary
case at depth \(N\) when:
\begin{enumerate}
\item \(Q\) is the exact packet already selected by the Navier--Stokes terminal
      claim.
\item \(Q\) retains enough carrier information to give the flux-volume
      reading \(\mathrm{EVol}_Q\).
\item \(\mathrm{EVol}_Q(u)\wedge\mathrm{EMom}_Q(u,p)\) holds on \(Q\).
\item The Navier--Stokes failure under discussion is exactly loss of
      fixed-viscosity participation or loss of positive-scale one-field
      coherence.  Reynolds stress, source measure, pressure-source residue,
      concentration residue, oscillation defect, and compactness gap payments
      have all been settled on the same packet.
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}[Clean Part face has an Euler boundary reading]
Assume \(Q\) is in the clean Euler boundary case and the first
Navier--Stokes face lost after Pack is
\[
  \Pack_Q\wedge\neg\Part_{N,Q}.
\]
Then the selected packet gives the Navier--Stokes class-exit conclusion
\[
  \Exit(Q;\Owork)
\]
through the Part face, and the same packet has the base Euler reading
\[
  \Member_E(Q).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The Navier--Stokes conclusion is the Pack-out-of-CM audited CM conclusion on the selected
record.  Pack has survived, and \(\neg\Part_{N,Q}\) says that the packet no
longer carries the same differentiated pressure-viscosity-incompressibility
participation law through depth \(N\).  Since that participation law is one
requirement in \(\Member(Q;\Owork)\), the first failed face yields
\(\Exit(Q;\Owork)\).

The Euler conclusion is separate and weaker.  The clean boundary hypotheses
state that the same selected packet still has the incompressible volume reading
and weak inviscid momentum-pressure reading.  That conjunction is exactly
\(\Member_E(Q)\).  This records the clean Part-side case where a
Navier--Stokes exit also has a base Euler boundary reading.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Clean Field face has an Euler boundary reading]
Assume \(Q\) is in the clean Euler boundary case, \(\Pack_Q\) and
\(\Part_{N,Q}\) survive for the branch split, and the first remaining
Navier--Stokes face lost is
\[
  \Pack_Q\wedge\Part_{N,Q}\wedge\forall r>0\,\neg\Field_{N,r,Q}.
\]
Then the selected packet gives \(\Exit(Q;\Owork)\) through the Field face,
and the same packet has the base Euler reading \(\Member_E(Q)\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
After Pack and Part survive, the remaining Navier--Stokes membership
requirement is a positive-scale coherent Field record.  The hypothesis
\(\forall r>0\,\neg\Field_{N,r,Q}\) removes every positive-scale
Navier--Stokes Field record on \(Q\).  That is the first failed face of
\(\Member(Q;\Owork)\), so it yields \(\Exit(Q;\Owork)\).

The base Euler reading requires the flux-volume and inviscid momentum-pressure
readings already included in the clean boundary hypotheses.  Hence the same
packet may leave the fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes class through Field while
still having the base Euler boundary reading.
\end{proof}
