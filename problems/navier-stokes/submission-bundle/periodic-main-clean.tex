\section{Periodic Navier--Stokes Global Smoothness}

\begin{theorem}[Periodic global smoothness]
Let $\nu>0$ and let
\[
  u_0\in C^\infty(\mathbb T^3;\mathbb R^3),
  \qquad \nabla\cdot u_0=0,
  \qquad \int_{\mathbb T^3}u_0\,dx=0.
\]
Then the unforced incompressible Navier--Stokes system
\[
  \partial_tu+u\cdot\nabla u+\nabla p=\nu\Delta u,
  \qquad \nabla\cdot u=0,
  \qquad u(0)=u_0,
\]
on $\mathbb T^3$ has a unique global smooth solution
\[
  u\in C^\infty([0,\infty)\times\mathbb T^3).
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Let $(u,p)$ be the maximal smooth solution on $[0,T_*)$ supplied by the classical local theory. Suppose $T_*<\infty$.

The terminal-tail uniformization theorem applies to the original periodic solution. It gives a finite retained terminal readout cover, pointwise field coherence, and a finite-depth transported-center tower on the terminal approach tail. This theorem is proved in the appendices from the scale-critical good-cylinder decomposition, local epsilon regularity, the averaged ACT estimate, the averaged endpoint package, and the terminal pointwise readout layer.

The pointwise endpoint matrix is exhaustive: a first finite endpoint must enter one of the four endpoint faces
\[
  Dead,\qquad packing\text{-}detached,\qquad tower\text{-}blown,\qquad Jump.
\]
The endpoint package removes all four rows. Participation persistence removes the dead row, pack control removes the packing-detached row, the terminal tower readout removes the tower-blown row, and field coherence removes the jump row. Cross entries reduce to those rows. Hence a first finite endpoint has no admissible endpoint face.

Equivalently, the terminal tower remains bounded to finite depth $N$ on the terminal cover. Choose $s>5/2$ and $N>s+2$. Finite-cover Sobolev patching gives
\[
  \sup_{t<T_*}\|u(t)\|_{H^s(\mathbb T^3)}<\infty.
\]
Periodic Navier--Stokes local theory at this fixed $H^s$ level gives a lifespan depending only on $\nu$, $s$, and the displayed bound. Restarting the same solution at times $t_j\uparrow T_*$ therefore extends the smooth solution beyond $T_*$, contradicting maximality.

Therefore $T_*=\infty$. Smoothness on all of $[0,\infty)$ follows from the smooth local theory and bootstrapping.
\end{proof}
