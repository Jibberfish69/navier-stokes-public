# Reader-Facing Abstract, Introduction, And Review Checklist

## Abstract

We prove smoothness by the Silver participation-complement contrapositive on one
original fluid history. Start with smooth divergence-free data, fixed positive
viscosity, and the no-reset Navier--Stokes evolution. Full VPI participation means
that this same history retains transport, simultaneous nonlocal pressure response,
viscosity, incompressibility, material ancestry, and coherent field participation.
The exhaustive departure analysis gives

```text
not Smooth(Q) => not Member(Q) => not VPIParticipation(Q).
```

Therefore, directly contrapositively,

```text
VPIParticipation(Q) => Member(Q) => Smooth(Q).
```

Dead, Blown, Jump, Part, Field, Euler, and any other limiting-PDE label records only
how a nonsmooth departure presents the same loss of full participation. Pack is
outside CM and may appear only as Field-window evidence. Euler is selected only
when the original NS history reaches a limit in which effective viscosity and all
Reynolds, forcing, pressure, incompressibility, and ancestry defects vanish.

Gold remains an independent, nonblocking forward-positive research lane. The
reader-facing PDF/export audit is separate from theorem acceptance and was not run
as part of this surface repair.

## Introduction

The proof does not begin with nonsmooth original data, work backward from Euler, or
seek a positive theorem saying that no terminal exit can occur. It keeps the one
original smooth-data, fixed-positive-viscosity Navier--Stokes fluid inside its full
VPI law for as long as the history exists. Every possible first departure from
smoothness is then reduced to loss of class membership and loss of that full
participation law. Contraposition closes smoothness in one step.

Part and Field language localizes a realized negative-side failure; it does not
create a second proof obligation. Distributional fixed-viscosity solvability is a
weaker equation record and is not, by itself, full VPI participation. Positive
estimates, packet ledgers, no-exit attempts, and Gold supplier calculations remain
historical or parallel support and are not premises of Silver.
