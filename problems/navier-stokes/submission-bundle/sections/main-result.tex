\section{Main Result}

The main theorem is a same-solution terminal classification theorem after admission.  It keeps the annular boundary visible because the stronger gold annular supplier remains unpaid.  The specific residual annular case is sorted as follows: gold pass, exact terminal time-face pulse obstruction, no-admission boundary or Pack boundary on the governing record, readout-complete admitted Member branch, or readout-complete admitted Part/Field exit.

\begin{theorem}[Same-solution terminal classification with annular boundary]
Let \(u_0\) be smooth and divergence-free, and let \((u,p)\) be the maximal classical Navier--Stokes solution generated by \(u_0\).  Suppose a finite terminal claim selects a terminal record \(Q\) for that same branch.  If \(Q\) is admitted as a same-solution terminal witness, then either
\[
  \Member(Q;\Owork)
\]
or the first failed CM witness face gives
\[
  \neg\Pack_Q,
  \qquad
  \Pack_Q\wedge\neg\Part_{N,Q},
  \qquad
  \Pack_Q\wedge\Part_{N,Q}\wedge\forall r>0\,\neg\Field_{N,r,Q}.
\]
In the failed-face cases,
\[
  \Exit(Q;\Owork):=\neg\Member(Q;\Owork).
\]
One admitted same-solution terminal record cannot be both a member-continuation record and the same finite nonsmooth terminal witness.
\end{theorem}

Without that tie, the paper has only a comparison object or a diagnostic pattern, not the terminal record being classified.
