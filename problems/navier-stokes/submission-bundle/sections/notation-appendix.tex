\section{Notation and Internal Dependency Labels}

The paper uses a small number of project names.  Each name is tied to a proof role.

\begin{description}
\item[\(\Owork\).] The one no-reset Navier--Stokes history issuing from the original smooth datum at fixed positive viscosity.  Its transport, simultaneous nonlocal pressure response, viscosity, incompressibility, material ancestry, and coherent field participation remain one object.
\item[\(\operatorname{VPIParticipation}(Q)\).] Full participation of that same history through the record \(Q\); this is stronger than merely satisfying a distributional Navier--Stokes equation.
\item[\(\Member(Q;\Owork)\).] The class-membership predicate for \(Q\) in that original fully participating history.
\item[\(\Exit(Q;\Owork)\).] The class-exit conclusion.  It means
\[
  \Exit(Q;\Owork):=\neg\Member(Q;\Owork).
\]
\item[\(\Pack_Q\).] Field-window evidence outside CM.  It is neither a CM face nor a premise of the Silver implication.
\item[\(\Part_{N,Q}\).] A negative-side presentation of lost participation on a tested local or derivative record.
\item[\(\Field_{N,r,Q}\).] A negative-side presentation of lost coherent field participation at scale \(r\).
\item[Dead, Blown, Jump.] Mechanism labels for negative-side participation loss; they do not name additional proof branches.
\item[Conditional other-PDE identification.] A limiting failure of the original Navier--Stokes history may be identified with another PDE only when every defect needed for that identification vanishes.  Euler requires zero effective viscosity and no Reynolds, forcing, pressure, incompressibility, or ancestry defect.
\item[Gold route.] The independent forward-positive research route.  Its open estimates neither prove nor block Silver.
\item[Silver route.] The exhaustive implication
\[
  \neg\operatorname{Smooth}(Q)
  \Longrightarrow \neg\Member(Q;\Owork)
  \Longrightarrow \neg\operatorname{VPIParticipation}(Q),
\]
and its direct contrapositive
\[
  \operatorname{VPIParticipation}(Q)
  \Longrightarrow \Member(Q;\Owork)
  \Longrightarrow \operatorname{Smooth}(Q).
\]
\end{description}

Part, Field, Dead, Blown, Jump, and a conditionally identified other PDE classify the single negative side.  Pack, packets, annuli, source reserves, and positive-forward estimates are evidence or independent Gold material; none supplies a positive no-exit theorem or a fully participating nonsmooth exception to Silver.
