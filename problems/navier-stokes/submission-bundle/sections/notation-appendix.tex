\section{Notation and Internal Dependency Labels}

The proof is written in standard PDE language.  The labels below identify project-internal dependency names.

\begin{description}
\item[\(Field_{avg}\).] Terminal positive-scale same-fluid good-cylinder cover.
\item[\(Jump_{avg}\).] Terminal loss of uniform positive good-cylinder scale.
\item[AACT.] Local affine-excess energy estimate on a same-fluid moving cylinder.
\item[DTC.] Transported-center control of velocity and forcing rungs.
\item[\(End_{NS,avg}\).] Averaged endpoint exclusion package.
\item[\(End_{NS}\).] Pointwise endpoint exclusion package.
\item[READ.END.] Terminal averaged-to-pointwise readout on the finite readout cover.
\item[Source branch.] The final proof uses \(FFSRC.A\Rightarrow FCI.5f\).
\end{description}

All theorem statements in the manuscript are periodic statements on \(\mathbb T^3\).  Whole-space export to \(\mathbb R^3\) is a separate theorem.
