\section{Notation and Internal Dependency Labels}

The proof uses the following project-internal names.

\begin{description}
\item[\(\Owork\).] The working same-solution Navier--Stokes class object.
\item[\(\Member(Q;\Owork)\).] The downstream class-membership predicate; a member
branch reads out to smooth continuation.
\item[\(\Exit(Q;\Owork)\).] The class-exit conclusion, defined by
\(\Exit(Q;\Owork):=\neg\Member(Q;\Owork)\).
\item[\(\Pack_Q\).] The Pack witness face: positive same-fluid carrier / packing
requirement.
\item[\(\Part_{N,Q}\).] The Part witness face: pressure-viscosity participation
requirement.
\item[\(\Field_{N,r,Q}\).] The Field witness face: positive-scale one-field
coherence requirement.
\item[CM-test entry.] Admission of an alleged finite Clay terminal witness to the
same-fluid terminal packet tree.
\item[Finite failure type exhaustion.] The theorem that every admitted finite
terminal failure produces loss of Pack, loss of Part, or loss of every positive
Field scale.
\item[CM contrapositive embedding.] The theorem that the derived face failure
supports \(\Exit(Q;\Owork)\).
\end{description}

Legacy periodic, whole-space, Euler-mirror, source-reserve, and positive
packet-survival surfaces are provenance or support unless a current authority
surface promotes them into Pack, Part, Field, membership readout, terminal CM
entry, finite failure type exhaustion, or contrapositive embedding.
