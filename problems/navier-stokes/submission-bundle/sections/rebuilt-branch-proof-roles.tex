\section{How Branch Families Pay the CM Proof}
\label{sec:branch-proof-roles}

Silver starts with the one smooth-data, fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes history.
Forward branches may illustrate how a departure appears, but they neither
start nor end the proof.  The common question is whether the same object still
has full VPI participation.

The answer is recorded through the same five outcomes used in the main proof:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{member readout} &
  \Part_{N,Q}\wedge\Field_{N,r,Q}
  \text{ plus the continuation norm,}\\[1mm]
\text{carrier-window support} & \text{same-solution admission support outside the exit conclusion},\\[1mm]
\text{Part failure} & \neg\Part_{N,Q},\\[1mm]
\text{Field failure} &
  \forall r>0\,\neg\Field_{N,r,Q},\\[1mm]
\text{context} &
  \text{a real estimate whose selected record or face payment is still unwritten.}
\end{array}
\]
Part and Field are two presentations of the same conclusion:
\(\neg\operatorname{VPIParticipation}(Q)\).  Pack remains carrier-window
evidence outside CM.  Forward calculations are examples or support until they
are tied to this same history.

control, but that open estimate does not block Silver.
