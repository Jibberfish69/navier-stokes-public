\section{Branch-Family CM Proof Roles}
\label{sec:branch-status-ledger}

The rebuilt manuscript is organized by branch family. Each branch enters the
proof only through a selected terminal packet, a continuation readout, or a first
Pack/Part/Field face failure. The table records that proof role so the branch
material cannot read as a separate open theorem list.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{L{0.18\textwidth} L{0.22\textwidth} L{0.22\textwidth} L{0.22\textwidth}}
\hline
\textbf{Branch} & \textbf{Positive-forward aim} & \textbf{Survivor or residue} & \textbf{CM-exit role}\\
\hline
Four-body diagnostic & Close tail, source, exact-object capture, and recovery in one circuit & Circular payment debt among the four bodies & Terminal-witness classification support\\
Energy/enstrophy/source & Lift energy into derivative control & Vortex-stretching source reserve & Part or Field face after terminal admission\\
Scale barrier/tail & Make high-frequency transfer summable & Lower-prefix memory and upper-tail leakage & Pack, Part, or Field face according to the selected witness\\
Compactness/exact object & Extract a same-solution terminal object & Detached or under-controlled terminal limit & Same-witness admission test\\
Recovery/surface fidelity & Convert terminal control into continuation & Recovery available on the wrong surface or wrong object & Continuation readout if same-solution; otherwise face failure\\
Lower-prefix/upper-tail & Collapse split survivor families & Split terminal family & Separately tested Pack/Part/Field witnesses\\
Kernelized lifted band & Use scale separation to gain decay & Half-derivative lower-shell remainder & Demoted positive-route support unless selected as a witness\\
Shell gain/half derivative & Use active-window smallness to pay lower strain & Active-window coefficient gap & Field-side pressure after Pack and Part survive\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The ledger has two functions. First, it prevents branch material from being
promoted before it lands on the CM-exit grammar. Second, it keeps the manuscript
from reading like a raw archive. Each branch must finish with a clear status:
continuation readout, Pack failure, Part failure, Field failure, support, or scope
boundary.

Later branch families are read by the same rule: each one is either a
continuation readout, a Pack failure, a Part failure, a Field failure, or
demoted positive-route support.
