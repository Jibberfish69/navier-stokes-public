\section{Proof Roadmap}

The proof follows the forward-positive Navier--Stokes program until a real
same-solution obstruction appears, then changes the question. Instead of forcing
one favorite positive estimate to control every obstruction, it asks whether the
nonsmoothness-capable fail branch can remain a legal branch of the same CM class.

First, each obstruction is split into a pass branch and a fail branch.

Second, the pass branch is represented by a lawful \(\Member(Q;\Owork)\) witness
and reads out to smooth continuation.

Third, the fail branch is admitted to the same-fluid terminal CM tree and
exhausted by the Pack/Part/Field witness faces:
\[
  \neg\Pack_Q,\qquad
  \neg\Part_{N,Q},\qquad
  \forall r>0\,\neg\Field_{N,r,Q}.
\]

Fourth, the derived face failure is embedded as
\[
  \Exit(Q;\Owork):=\neg\Member(Q;\Owork).
\]
The no-third-branch law then excludes an in-class terminal nonsmooth branch.

Source-reserve, signed-pair, no-free-sink, no-pulse, periodic, whole-space, and
comparison surfaces are support or context unless they land in this CM witness
chain or satisfy one reopening test.
