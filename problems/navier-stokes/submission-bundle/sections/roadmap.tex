\section{Proof Roadmap}

The proof begins by fixing the object under test.  A finite terminal claim must select the terminal record of the same Navier--Stokes branch: same datum, same equation, same pressure law, same viscosity, same incompressibility constraint, and same preterminal smooth solution.  After that admission, the paper asks whether the selected record is a member record or an exit record.

The first route is gold.  The energy identity controls kinetic energy and integrated enstrophy.  The enstrophy identity exposes the nonlinear source term
\[
  \int (u\cdot\nabla)u\cdot\Delta u\,dx.
\]
The calculation in the opening section gives the local inequality \(Y'(t)\le C_\nu Y(t)^3\).  That inequality gives local control and leaves finite-time growth possible.  The annular and funnel work sharpens this into the residual source-refill question on shrinking positive-radius windows near a proposed endpoint.

The second route is silver.  If a residual terminal obstruction survives the forward accounting, the paper tests the selected record \(Q\).  Carrier-window support admits the record to the same-solution CM test.  A retained admitted record must then carry
\[
  \Part_{N,Q},\qquad
  \Field_{N,r,Q}
\]
to the participation-law membership readout.  A record that never reaches admission remains carrier-window support outside the exit conclusion.  An admitted failed record is exhausted by the Part and Field faces:
\[
  \neg\Part_{N,Q},\qquad
  \Part_{N,Q}\wedge\forall r>0\,\neg\Field_{N,r,Q}.
\]
The first failed Part or Field face yields \(\Exit(Q;\Owork):=\neg\Member(Q;\Owork)\).

The annular endpoint witness is the marked boundary of the present manuscript.  The radius-zero point is too small to carry the singularity claim by itself; the claim is carried by fields on shrinking positive-radius annuli.  Gold may close the boundary by proving scale-uniform annular control.  Silver may close it by admitting the surviving annular residue as the same-solution terminal record and landing Part or Field.  Until one of those payments is written, the paper keeps the boundary visible.

Two downstream families are held for later sections.  The lower-carrier interval-integrability work belongs to the later receiver/source analysis, where a moving collar has to be shown to carry enough integrability for the retained window.  The strong-defect bridge work belongs to the later geometric-defect analysis, where a surviving defect has to be connected to a lawful Part or Field role.  In the opening these families serve only as reminders that the annular boundary is not the whole proof topology.  They do not change the theorem stated above, and they do not convert the open gold annular supplier into a completed positive proof.

The remaining sections follow this order.  They define the CM membership object, state the Part/Field witness laws, prove the class-law implication from Part/Field failure to exit, and then read the branch material as proof payment, context, obstruction, obsolete material, or open continuation.  Each paragraph identifies the object under test and the proof role it pays.
