
```math

2^k \cdot 2^{2k}\|\Delta_k u^{(n)}\|_{L^2_x}^2.
```

The current Body `1` surface already controls the `2^{2k}` factor through
`L_t^2 H_x^1`. What is missing is the extra `2^k`.

## Sharp Reduced Target

The missing theorem content is therefore the exact `k`-side payment left by the
failed integration route.  The lifted-band estimate must pay the extra square
factor

```math
2^k
```

in

```math
2^{3k}\|\Delta_k u^{(n)}\|_{L^2_x}^2
=
2^k\,2^{2k}\|\Delta_k u^{(n)}\|_{L^2_x}^2
```

while keeping the already-proved band gap `2^{-(j-k)}`.  One way to express the
same target is to improve the lifted-band coefficient from

```math
2^{3k/2}\|\Delta_k u^{(n)}\|_{L^2_x}
```

```math

```

with the coupled integration still carrying the band gap.

The correction is that this gain is not needed as a standalone shellwise theorem
of the form `\int 2^{3k}\|\Delta_k u\|_2^2`. What is actually sufficient is the
coupled version recorded in `coupled-lifted-band-gain-target.md`: the lower
shell `k` must gain enough only when paired with the upper-tail dissipation
convolution generated by the kernelized lifted-band packet.

## Why This Is The Right Refinement

This target is not arbitrary. It is forced by the exact shape of the first
failed integration route.

- The off-diagonal decay `2^{-(j-k)}` already captures the fact that the
  lifted band is not a flat resonance law.
- The obstruction is now concentrated on the lower shell `k`.
- So the natural next theorem is not "more `j-k` decay" by itself, but
  additional lower-shell summability.

In that sense, the lifted band is already lossy in the middle; what remains is
to quantify enough leakage on the `k` side to turn that lossy structure into an
integrated theorem.
