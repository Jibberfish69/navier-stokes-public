# Coupled Lifted-Band Gain Target

## Purpose

This note records the corrected sufficient theorem shape for the open lifted
band. A standalone half-derivative shell gain of the form

```math
\int_0^T 2^{3k}\|\Delta_k u(t)\|_{L^2_x}^2\,dt
\le
C_*2^{-2\delta k}
```

is an insufficient shellwise target.  It does **not** close the route by itself.
The route needs the coupled estimate against the upper-tail carrier displayed
below.

The reason is that after swapping the kernelized lifted-band sums, the lower
shell `k` is paired with an **upper-tail dissipation convolution**, not with
`D_k` on the same shell.

## Exact Swapped Carrier

From the scale-side kernelized lifted-band reduction, the natural swapped
carrier is

```math
\sum_{k>N+M}
2^{3k/2}\|\Delta_k u(t)\|_{L^2_x}\,
\mathcal D_k^\uparrow(t),
```

where

```math
\mathcal D_k^\uparrow(t)
:=
\sum_{j>k+4}
2^{-\sigma(j-k)}D_j(t).
```

Here `D_j(t)=2^{4j}\|\Delta_j u(t)\|_{L^2_x}^2` is the high-shell dissipation
weight and `2^{-\sigma(j-k)}` is the already-real spectral-gap kernel.

## Why Bare HG Is Not Sufficient

The tempting shellwise theorem

```math
\int_0^T 2^{3k}\|\Delta_k u(t)\|_{L^2_x}^2\,dt
\le
C_*2^{-2\delta k}
```

does not by itself close the proof for two reasons:

1. the swapped upper-tail convolution `\mathcal D_k^\uparrow` is not controlled
   by `D_k`;
2. even replacing `\mathcal D_k^\uparrow` by `D_k` would still leave a cubic
   same-shell product rather than the required quadratic control.

So the actual missing theorem must be **coupled**: it has to control the lower
shell gain only when tested against the upper-tail dissipation carrier that
arises from the packet algebra.

## Correct Sufficient Target

The sufficient theorem shape is:

```math
\int_0^T
\sum_{k>N+M}
2^{3k/2}\|\Delta_k u(t)\|_{L^2_x}\,
\mathcal D_k^\uparrow(t)\,dt
\le
C_*2^{-2\delta N}.
```

Equivalently, one may formulate it with a dominating carrier `a_k(t)`:

```math
2^{3k/2}\|\Delta_k u(t)\|_{L^2_x}\le a_k(t),
```

and require

```math
\int_0^T
\sum_{k>N+M}
a_k(t)\,\mathcal D_k^\uparrow(t)\,dt
\le
C_*2^{-2\delta N}.
```

This is the natural sufficient theorem because, after swapping the sums in the
kernelized lifted-band carrier, it is exactly the object that must be bounded.

The route can be sharpened one step further. The new note
`upper-tail-kernelized-dissipation-theorem.md` shows that this paired carrier
theorem itself follows from a cleaner upper-tail square estimate:

```math
\int_0^T
\sum_{k>N+M}
2^k\big(\mathcal D_k^\uparrow(t)\big)^2\,dt
\le
C_*2^{-2\delta N}.
```

So the coupled theorem here is now best read as an intermediate reduction,
rather than the final theorem target.

## Relation To MSI

This coupled gain target is still upstream of `(MSI)`, but it is no longer the
over-strong standalone shellwise HG.

The sharp current chain is:

```math
\text{(UTKD)}
\Longrightarrow
\text{paired carrier theorem}
\Longrightarrow
\text{(MSI)}
\Longrightarrow
\text{lifted high-side remainder closure}.
```

So the missing theorem content is not "control `2^{3k}\|\Delta_k u\|^2` by
itself." It is "control the lower-shell coefficient when it is paired with the
upper-tail dissipation convolution generated by the lifted-band packet."

## Honest Boundary

This note records a corrected sufficient target. It does not prove it.

What is settled:

- the lifted band already carries a genuine `2^{-(j-k)}` gap kernel;
- the residual loss really does sit on the lower shell `k`.

What remains open:

- the upper-tail weighted square estimate `(UTKD)` strong enough to imply the
  paired carrier theorem and inherit the dyadic barrier scale
  `C_*2^{-2\delta N}`.
