# B2 m=±2 Gap Kill Mode

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9. Cross-wall identification attempted first:
W3/W7/W8/W9 are not identified with the B2 strict-atom \(m=\pm2\) sign
computation, because B2's \(m=\pm2\) frame-rotation denominator vanishes at the
strict atom while the surviving walls still require row admission/payment,
graph passage, selected-linear collection, and material-time path/action facts.

Physical sentence: at

\[
S=a\,\mathrm{diag}(1,1,-2),
\]

the two stretching eigenvalues are equal. In-plane spin-2 orientation is
therefore not a selected eigenframe direction at the atom. An \(m=\pm2\)
off-diagonal forcing cannot be read as a finite signed frame-rotation response
until the stretch plane is split; that split is a deviation/payment/route-out
issue, not a free atom certificate.

## Source Check

The B2 specification says the turning sign lives in the \(m\ne0\) sectors, with
\(m=\pm1\) tilting the compression axis and \(m=\pm2\) splitting the degenerate
stretch plane. It also pre-names the kill mode: the degenerate stretch-plane
\(m=\pm2\) channel has a vanishing atom gap, so the computation must use a
deviation-lifted gap or restrict to the \(m=\pm1\) channel where the gap is
\(3a\).

The frame-rotation source gives the exact gap law for in-plane spin:

\[
\frac{D\varphi}{Dt}
=-\,\frac{(\Omega^2)_{12}+H_{12}}{\lambda_1-\lambda_2}.
\]

At the strict atom \(\lambda_1=\lambda_2=a\), so the denominator is zero. The
same source records the degenerate leg: when \(\lambda_1\to\lambda_2\), the gap
closes and the spin-2 phase becomes undefined. That is a boundary stratum, not a
finite rotation-sign mechanism.

The transverse-restoring source also blocks a local rescue. It proves that a
local or finite-jet pressure-Hessian sign theorem is impossible; the correct
replacement is a retained same-field no-free-wrong-sign-borrowing theorem. Thus
the \(m=\pm2\) shortcut cannot bypass the missing retained-row/payment/path
bridges by asserting a local sign.

The aligned-row source supplies the atom-class aligned/drop package and exact
bill arithmetic. That package lives in the aligned \(m=0\) sector and reduces
earlier row content into W3; it does not define the off-frame \(m=\pm2\) phase at
the degenerate atom.

## Legal B2 Remainder

The next legal B2 computation has two possible forms.

1. Work in the \(m=\pm1\) compression-axis tilt channel, where the strict atom
   has exact gap \(3a\).
2. Work in \(m=\pm2\) only after adding a deviation-lifted gap theorem and
   charging that deviation in the existing bill currency, then bridge the result
   to the surviving walls.

Either outcome may still pay later: a proved restoring sign with bridges could
support the one-obligation program, and an adverse or sign-indefinite result
would be a serious refutation of the proposed mechanism. This file only kills
the immediate strict-atom \(m=\pm2\) shortcut.
