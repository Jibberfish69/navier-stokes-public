# B2 Reflection-Helicity Ontology Gate

## Exact Reflection Test

Let
\[
S=a\,\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2),
\qquad
R=\operatorname{diag}(-1,1,1).
\]
Then
\[
R S R^T=S.
\]
The strict atom readout
\[
w(n)=n\cdot S n
\]
is unchanged by \(n\mapsto R^T n\). Thus the \(m=0\) shedding/drop sector and
the scalar service data of the strict atom are reflection-even.

For the legal B2 compression-axis tilt generator,
\[
\phi_{13}(n)=n_1n_3,
\qquad
j_{13}=c\nabla_{S^2}\phi_{13},
\]
reflection gives
\[
\phi_{13}(R^T n)=-\phi_{13}(n),
\qquad
j_{13}\mapsto -j_{13}.
\]
The previous packet computed
\[

=

(e_1\otimes e_3+e_3\otimes e_1).
\]
Under the same reflection, \(H\mapsto RHR^T\), so
\[

\]
The legal gap in the \(13\) channel remains \(3a\). The orientation reverses
while the strict atom and its scalar service/drop data remain the same.

The same argument applies to the \(23\) tilt after reflecting \(n_2\).

## Helicity Is the Only Named Symmetry Breaker, and It Is Not Installed

For a reflected velocity field
\[
u_R(x)=R\,u(R^T x),
\]
vorticity transforms as
\[
\omega_R(x)=\det(R)\,R\,\omega(R^T x)=-R\,\omega(R^T x).
\]
Therefore the helicity
\[
\mathcal H(u)=\int u\cdot\omega\,dx
\]
changes sign:
\[
\mathcal H(u_R)=-\mathcal H(u).
\]
Helicity is therefore the right kind of object to break the reflection
symmetry. The checked census says exactly that the Waleffe / Manley-Rowe
helicity gap is a parked handedness object, that it has not been derived from
Navier-Stokes on the participating terminal object, and that helicity is
sign-indefinite with a possible borderline zero gap.

So the helicity/chirality idea is not a deletion certificate. It names the
missing exact datum:
\[
\text{a retained nonzero handedness row whose sign fixes }c
\text{ relative to the restoring tilt sense}.
\]
That row is not W3, W7, W8, or W9 as currently stated, and it is not admitted
as a new wall because no deletion is paid.
