Cross-wall identification attempted first: the strongest Cycle 24 reading is
that W3's rung-uniformity wall reduces to a data-dependent
\(\Gamma\)-budget lemma, with all later W7/W8/W9 obligations handled after a
finite-\(Re_\Gamma\) race. This fails as a deletion certificate. The proposed
budget is a new uniformity target, not a proved identity, admission clause,
orthogonality, or symmetry deleting W3.

The useful paid-looking part is actually a refutation of an earlier proposed
certificate. Cycle 24 correctly kills the Cycle 19 statement that
\(\Gamma\) is monotone along an amplification pedigree: merging or gathering
same-signed tubes can increase the circulation carried by the next pedigree
object even though it cannot raise the vorticity peak by itself. Therefore the
old reduction "W3 uniformity = \(\Gamma\)-monotonicity" is not a certificate.

The replacement \(\Gamma\)-budget lemma is not yet exact. In Navier-Stokes,
circulation around material loops is not Kelvin-conserved; viscosity contributes
a circulation-diffusion term. Vorticity is divergence-free, but that alone does
not prove that every pedigree plane has a finite, same-signed gatherable
reservoir fixed by the original data. A certificate must specify:

- the exact pedigree surface or tube family whose circulation is measured;
- signed versus unsigned flux, and how folding/cancellation is counted;
- the admissible original-data norm or measure defining
  \(\Gamma_{\rm gather}(u_0)\);
- a proof that viscous reconnection and pressure/strain transport redistribute
  but do not create the signed reservoir in that coordinate;
- a finite bound for every smooth datum in the class under test.

Without those clauses, \(Re_{\max}(u_0)=\Gamma_{\rm gather}(u_0)/\nu\) is only a
name for the missing W3 uniformity datum. It does not prove that the killer
constants are uniform along every retained tower, nor that the finite-\(Re\)
race is W8-selected original-data bill currency.

The per-finite-\(Re\) race is the right shape for a viscous theorem only as a
target. A gap that degenerates as \(Re_\Gamma\to\infty\) is compatible with the
Euler parity/tie intuition, but the loop still needs the actual finite-\(Re\)
inequality, the data-dependent bound, and the carriage of that bound through W7
critical graph closure and W9 compact material-time action. None of those is
installed by Cycle 24.
