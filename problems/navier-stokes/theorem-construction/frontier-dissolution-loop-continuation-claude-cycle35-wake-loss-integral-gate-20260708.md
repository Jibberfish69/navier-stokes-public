## Refutation Attempt Against the Certificate

The proposed lower bound does not yet certify W8, because
\(\nu\int|\nabla\omega|^2\) is palinstrophy dissipation in the vorticity
balance, not an original-data finite currency by itself in 3D. It becomes a
paying currency only after the proof identifies the reusable service stock and
shows that this integral reduces that stock or forces fresh import from the
finite signed reservoir. Otherwise the same episode may dissipate vorticity
while stretching or importing enough vorticity to keep the selected service
capacity unchanged.

The fixed ring geometry is not enough for W3 uniformity. The required constant
is not the loss/service ratio of the displayed pair alone. It is the infimum of
loss per delivered service over all retained ways to obtain the same service:
tilt, profile shaping, core-width variation, non-axisymmetric deformation,
nested partners, roll-up, and weak limits. The engine/sink proximity strongly
suggests a positive infimum, but the certificate is the variational inequality,
not the example.

The lower bound does not yet certify W7. A sequence of conveyor episodes could
approach the critical geometry through thinner tails, chattering exchanges,
moving selectors, or profile weak limits. The proof needs lower
semicontinuity of the wake-loss numerator and upper/continuous control of the
service denominator on the selected event graph. Without that graph, \(c_*>0\)
may be lost exactly at the closure point.

The lower bound does not yet certify W9. Zero-surplus requires that a closed
recycling loop cannot convert service, wake loss, re-import, and re-contraction
into a lossless cycle. A positive local dissipation integral is insufficient
unless the same current proves that the dissipated part is orthogonal to
service-producing strain and cannot be restored as an internal flux without
being charged again.

The circulation-ledger version still needs the exact signed surface theorem.
"Opposite-signed cross-diffusion" must be tied to a chosen circulation-bearing
surface or tube family, with a lower bound on actual loss of signed
\(\Gamma\)-service. Core self-diffusion broadens or lowers peak vorticity, but
does not automatically destroy circulation service unless the service variable
has been defined so that broadening is terminal or freshly priced.
