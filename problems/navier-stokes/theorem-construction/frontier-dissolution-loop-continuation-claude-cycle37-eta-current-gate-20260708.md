## Refutation Attempt Against the Full Certificate

The well-frame is not a free axisymmetric coordinate system. A local record
frame selected by the strain/vorticity geometry rotates, bends, and may have
defects. In that frame, \(\omega_\phi\) is a component of a vector field, not a
globally conserved scalar. Frame rotation and curvature create component-mixing
terms, and those terms are exactly where the non-axisymmetric conveyor can hide.
The certificate must write the frame-covariant \(\eta\)-equation and show every
extra term is either delivery-destroying or wake-priced.

The max principle does not automatically survive swirl. For axisymmetric
Navier-Stokes, the protected max-principle quantity is the swirl variable
\(r u_\theta\), while \(\omega_\theta/r\) gains source terms when swirl is
present. Since the record/ring machinery has nonzero swirl, the no-swirl
Ladyzhenskaya fact is a subcase exclusion, not the active certificate.

The zero-set loss description is promising but incomplete. Cross-diffusion of a
sign-changing scalar component can be represented through level sets only after
one specifies the scalar, the sign measure, the coarea/defect terms, and the
weak-limit closure. Vector circulation loss cannot be replaced by an
\(\omega_\phi\) zero-set integral until the chosen signed circulation surface is
proved equivalent to that scalar row.

The full 3D residue is correctly narrowed to a trade-off, but the inequality is
not written yet. The needed certificate has the form: every term that amplifies
\(\eta\) by tilting the stock away from the no-swirl max-principle sector
reduces the same stock's Biot-Savart delivery to \(d_1u_1\), adds oblique
expulsion/wake loss, or enters the finite signed-reservoir bill. This must be a
single inequality inside the \(\eta\)-current, not a verbal projection
