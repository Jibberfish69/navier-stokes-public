## Refutation Attempt Against the Extinction Certificate

The gradient identity is not yet exact. For a transported-diffused scalar
\(\Gamma_s\), the axial derivative satisfies schematically
\[
D_t(\partial_z\Gamma_s)
=-(\partial_z u)\cdot\nabla\Gamma_s
 + \nu\,\partial_z L_\rho\Gamma_s
 + [\partial_z,L_\rho]\Gamma_s,
\]
not only \(-\alpha\,\partial_z\Gamma_s+\nu(\cdots)\). The desired damping is
the special subcase where \((\partial_z u)\cdot\nabla\Gamma_s\) reduces to
\(\alpha\,\partial_z\Gamma_s\) with the correct sign and the radial/frame
components are harmless. That reduction must be proved in the selected
well-frame where the pump actually feeds the record.

The two \(\alpha\)'s are not automatically the same. The record stretching rate
is measured at the record core and in the selected record direction. The pump
may be supported off-core, across a finite ring tube, or in a rotating local
frame. The certificate must prove that any pump strong enough to feed
core-scale rings is localized where the same axial strain produces the claimed
gradient extinction.

The geometry factor is active. Expanding the source gives terms like
\[
\frac{2\Gamma_s\,\partial_z\Gamma_s}{\rho^4}
-\frac{4\Gamma_s^2\,\partial_z\rho}{\rho^5}.
\]
Even if \(\partial_z\Gamma_s\) decays along material time, the shrinking
\(\rho^{-4}\) weight and off-core geometry can amplify the pump unless the
\(\eta\)-current proves that this amplification is exactly balanced by
delivery loss, wake loss, or stranded replenishment.

The max principle for \(\Gamma_s\) controls amplitude, not the needed gradient
or weighted gradient by itself. Gradient growth in advective-diffusive flows is
possible through the velocity-gradient term, so the proof needs a weighted
gradient inequality, not just the scalar amplitude cap.

The tau-exponential versus tau-linear comparison is a valid target only after
the source term has been integrated in the same material clock as the demand.
At present, replenishment by imported \(\Gamma_s\)-variation, off-core support,
and the moving well-frame are still carried by the unproved stranding and W7
closure clauses.
