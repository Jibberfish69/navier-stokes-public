## Cross-Wall Identification Attempt

The attempted identification is the strongest version of Claude's Cycle-13
claim: W9 is not an independent compact material-time path/action wall because
BKM supplies the infinite material clock, the scalar Riccati supplies the deep
pressure well, W7 supplies the slaved pressure row, and W3+W8 supply the bounds
needed to make the well-frame hull compact. Thus W9 would reduce to W3+W7+W8.

That identification fails. BKM gives a necessary blowup clock and a consumer
criterion, not a compact certificate action. The proposed well selector also
mixes two sign-opposite pressure objects: the pressure minimum supplied by the
cyclostrophic/well picture has positive Hessian, while the accepted alignment
Riccati needs negative pressure curvature along the transported vorticity
direction to sustain growth.

The Cycle-11 \(P_2\)-isotropy observation is accepted as an exact orthogonality
certificate:
\[
  \int_{S^2} P_2(\hat y\cdot e)\,d\Omega(\hat y)=0
\]
for an isotropic shell measure. Therefore an isotropic background cannot
contribute to the \(m=0\) pressure-shell depth moment. This sharpens the
selected anisotropic carrier target, but it does not prove that every relevant
anisotropic contribution is already W8-paid, W7-closed, or W9-carried.

The Cycle-13 material-clock observation is accepted only as a necessary blowup

divergent terminal vorticity clock; along a differentiability point of a chosen
record envelope, the stretching rate is a natural clock variable. This does not
construct W9's compact action, future germ, terminal-window room, zero-surplus
closed graph, or defect routing.

## Refutation Certificate

The sign of the well-selector certificate is wrong as stated. The accepted
scalar spine uses \(H=\nabla^2p\) and gives, at perfect alignment,
\[
  D_t\alpha=-\alpha^2-e\cdot He+\nu R_\alpha .
\]
To keep the aligned branch from lapsing at the square rate, one needs
\[
  e\cdot He\le -\alpha^2+\nu R_\alpha .
\]
That is negative curvature of the pressure along the transported direction.

Cycle 12 instead constructs \(x_w(t)\) as a pressure-well critical point with
positive transverse Hessian from cyclostrophic balance and an axial pressure
minimum from \(p_{\rm axis}\approx -C\Gamma|\omega|\). A nondegenerate pressure
minimum has positive Hessian in the selected directions. Its Morse gap is
therefore a localization gap for a pressure minimum, not the negative-curvature
subsidy in the Riccati. With the signs as written, a positive Hessian at the
well contributes \(-e\cdot He<0\) and helps the aim lapse; it does not sustain
the aligned blowup branch.

This is an attackable exact certificate: either the selector is a pressure
minimum, in which case it has the wrong sign for the Riccati subsidy, or the
selector is changed to a critical point of \(-p\) or to a negative-curvature
pressure ridge, in which case the cyclostrophic positive-Hessian and axial-
minimum arguments no longer construct it. In neither case is W9 deleted by
W3+W7+W8.

There are two independent non-sign obstructions as well. First, the checked
route-state still types BKM, Prodi-Serrin, and critical criteria as
consumer-only: they spend terminal control but do not supply it from original
data. Second, W7's slaved-pressure currency is not W7 graph closure; under
critical limits the pressure source can carry a Reynolds/nonlinear graph defect
that still needs vanishing, compatibility, legal carriage, or typed route-out.
A pointwise pressure-well path is more delicate than that graph closure, not a
free substitute for it.

## Result

The well-selector idea is a useful strict reduction target after sign repair:
prove a sign-consistent pressure-critical selector theorem for the same field,
then prove that its path hull has continuous shifts, terminal-window room,
zero-surplus closure, W7 graph stability, and W8 carrier domination. Current
sources do not prove those facts, and Cycle 13 explicitly leaves the
Burgers-profile \(P_2\) integral and thick-core \(m=0\) dispersion computations
as constants-level rigor still to be done.
