## Cross-Wall Identification Attempt

The attempted cross-wall identification is the corrected version of the
well-selector proposal: W9 would reduce to W3+W7+W8 because the subsidized
branch carries a cyclostrophic pressure saddle, the saddle supplies the path
selector/action, and the neck-conversion mechanism turns W9's recurrence clause
into a per-rung price controlled by W8.

That identification fails as a deletion. The saddle proposal supplies a useful
target, but it does not supply the two certificates W9 requires: a critical
point equation \(\nabla p=0\) with a continuous selected branch, and a
zero-surplus/terminal-window theorem proving that infinitely many capped rungs
cannot be afforded by the admitted W8 carrier. A curvature sign along a
trajectory is not yet a selector, and a per-rung price that is not summed is not
yet a W9 terminal-window closure.

## Accepted Cycle 14 Reduction

Cycle 14's straight-filament self-neutrality is accepted as an exact symmetry
certificate. For a straight \(z\)-uniform filament, the pressure is a function
of \(\rho\) alone, so
\[
  \partial_z^2p=0
\]
on the axis. Equivalently the axial \(P_2\) kernel contributions cancel under
the \(z\)-uniform integral. Thus a straight uniform filament supplies no axial
pressure subsidy to itself. This correctly refutes the earlier crude
near-field self-supply claim.

This is a strict reduction, not a deletion. The flanker/sharpness squeeze is a
physical form of W8's selected payment problem: a sharp record is underpaid by
weaker flankers, while a flat record is paid but non-growing. Cycle 14 itself
does not give the threshold constant or prove the resulting bill is a finite
order-locked original-data payment.

## Cycle 15 Gate

Cycle 15 fixes the sign error in the pressure-minimum selector by replacing the
well with a pressure saddle: transverse positive curvature from cyclostrophic
balance and axial negative curvature from the subsidy row. That repair is
accepted as a target form, but it still does not delete W9.

The first missing certificate is criticality. The accepted aligned Riccati row
requires
\[
  e\cdot\nabla^2p\,e\le -\alpha^2+\nu R_\alpha .
\]
This is a Hessian inequality along the transported vorticity direction. It does
not imply
\[
  \nabla p(x_s(t),t)=0
\]
at any nearby point, and the implicit function theorem for a saddle branch
starts from \(\nabla p=0\) plus an invertible Hessian. Cyclostrophic transverse
balance can identify a transverse axis in an axisymmetric/core model, and the
subsidy row gives negative axial curvature along a direction, but the current
certificate does not prove a full three-dimensional critical point, uniqueness
or typed multiplicity, or continuous branch selection through exchanges.

The second missing certificate is terminal closure. Cycle 15 explicitly says
the rung-price summability verdict is not claimed. Therefore the neck-
conversion mechanism is a sharper recurrence model, not a W9 deletion. It
states that blowup would require infinitely many capped leapfrogging rungs
funded by fresh supplier pairs; it does not prove that W8's order-locked
carrier cannot fund them, that W3's service row stays uniformly admitted, that
W7's nonlinear graph defects vanish or route out, or that W9's zero-surplus
closed graph has terminal-window room.

The corrected saddle selector is therefore a strict reduction target:
construct a sign-consistent pressure-saddle critical point and path hull from
the same field, then prove the capped-rung price is summable or routes every
failure to an existing typed face. Current sources do not do that.
