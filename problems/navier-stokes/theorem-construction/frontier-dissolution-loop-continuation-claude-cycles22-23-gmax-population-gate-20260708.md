where \(g_{\max}\) is the optimal non-overlapping \(P_2\)-export constant from

admissible profile class is not yet the full retained field class, and no checked

Cycle 22 is accepted as a useful narrowing but not as population closure. It
correctly refutes a dissipation-magnitude cap for the near field:
supercritical near-field supply is not excluded by energy alone. It also gives a
good bookkeeping pressure: any near-record vorticity lobe that pays the running
maximizer carries its own local pressure/source bill. That is bill inheritance
as a target, not a completed closed-system theorem.

The proposed closed population still has an unclosed class: finite-energy
strain-dominated exporters and non-axisymmetric anisotropic \(Q\)-distributions
generated by nearby vorticity. Cycle 14 excludes the infinite-energy uniform
Burgers ambient strain, not every finite-energy strain shadow or finite-window
strain relay. The peak-production theorem says only vorticity sup growth is
made by stretching at the running maximizer; it does not prove every pressure
source that can export \(P_2\) supply is either a near-record vorticity core or
zero.

Cycle 23's \(Q\)-neutrality identity is exact in its stated axisymmetric swirl
class. For a complete swirl profile with \(v(0)=v(\infty)=0\),
\[
  \int_0^\infty \left(-2vv'/\rho\right)2\pi\rho\,d\rho
  = -2\pi [v^2]_0^\infty=0.
\]
This is a genuine orthogonality/neutrality certificate for complete
axisymmetric swirl cross-sections. It does not extend by itself to arbitrary
three-dimensional retained lobes, bent/tilted tubes, strain-vorticity mixtures,
finite-window cutoffs, or weak/profile limits. In those cases the missing work
is exactly W7 graph/defect closure plus W8/W9 legal carriage of the selected
profile.

The shadow theorem is also not yet exact. In whole-space finite-energy
incompressible flow, strain is determined nonlocally by vorticity through the
same velocity field, so a vorticity-poor strain region is not an independent
free force. But the claimed dominance of direct lobes over all strain relays
needs a sharp comparison theorem: the source-to-strain decay, the quadratic
suppression of \(Q\), the second \(r^{-3}\) export, and the non-overlap window
must be proved uniformly for the retained profile class. The Burgers annulus
exponent is model evidence, not the general certificate.

Therefore \(g_{\max}\) is not admitted as a new wall. It has not deleted two
existing names, and it has not produced a terminal certificate. If the exact
maximization later proves \(g_{\max}<1/6\) on the full retained class and that
verdict is carried through W3/W7/W8/W9, then it may become a deletion
certificate. If \(g_{\max}\ge1/6\), it instead exhibits a candidate geometry to
attack by closure, classical self-similar exclusion, or payment/action failure.
