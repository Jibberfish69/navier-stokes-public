Physical sentence: a distant coil can matter only if the same velocity field
can keep that coil dynamically stationed near the shrinking record; the new

kinematic theorem to prove on the full retained field class.

Cycle 27 contributes two useful zero-law reductions. The whole-space vorticity
monopole cancellation
\[
  \int_{\mathbb R^3}\omega_i\,dx
  =
  \int_{\mathbb R^3}\nabla\cdot(x_i\omega)\,dx
  =
  0
\]
is exact for sufficiently decaying complete vorticity structures with no
boundary contribution. It is a real sibling of the accepted \(Q\)-neutrality
calculation. It is not yet a retained-field certificate for finite windows,
cutoffs, weak/profile limits, boundary-bearing tubes, or selected shell pieces.

The axial-stretch zero law is also a useful symmetry reduction: an exactly
\(z\)-invariant structure has no axial variation, so the dangerous axial strain
must come from turns, ends, kinks, or nonuniform defects. That demotes straight
columns/sheets as suppliers, but it does not by itself price or route every
turn/end/kink in the retained geometry class.

Cycle 28 correctly concedes that the \(\Gamma\)-budget alone does not kill a
single snaking tube: one circulation can thread many shells, so counting
structures is not an exact budget certificate. That is a paid-looking
refutation of the previous pincer presentation, but it does not delete a wall.

The proposed replacement is the stranding theorem. In its intended form, a coil
at shell distance \(d_k\) must migrate inward at record speed
\(\sim d_k\Omega_n\) per rung time, while an in-class fixed-circulation swirl
can move at only \(\sim \Gamma/d_k\), giving a required/available ratio
\[
  {d_k\Omega_n\over \Gamma/d_k}
  \sim
  \left({d_k\over \delta_n}\right)^2
  =
  4^k .
\]
This is the right attack surface, but it is not yet a certificate. A proof must
specify the moving center/frame, the shell family, the fixed-\(\Gamma\) velocity
cap, how data-scale ambient compression is subtracted or routed, why
stretched-in-place regrowth cannot replace inward migration, and how
pre-positioned coils are treated in the same spacetime current rather than
called frozen by assertion.

The unified race verdict is therefore candidate arithmetic only. The proposed
numbers, such as pressure plus strain supply \(\sim 0.026\Omega^2\) against
demand \(1/6\,\Omega^2\), depend on three unproved items named by Cycle 28
itself: the variational suprema for both aligned and misaligned faces, the
rigorous available-speed cap, and absorption of the \(O(1)\)-shell gather cost.
Those are exactly theorem clauses, not already paid certificates.

No new wall is admitted for "stranding." As a name, it would not be
net-negative yet: it has not deleted at least two of W3, W7, W8, and W9. Its
proper status is a strict reduction target inside the same field-current
program:
\[
  \text{universal variational supply bound}
  +
  \text{stranding speed cap}
  +
  \text{gather-cost absorption}
  \Longrightarrow
  \text{finite-\(Re_\Gamma\) unified race win}.
\]
