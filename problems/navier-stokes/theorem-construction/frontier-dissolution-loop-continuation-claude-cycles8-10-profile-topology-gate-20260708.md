## Cross-Wall Identification Attempt

claim that the fixed-field/profile view makes W9 the same object as W3+W7+W8.
In that reading, W3 supplies persistent row bounds, W7 supplies graph passage,
W8 supplies selected payer control, and the crest-frame profile hull supplies
the missing W9 material-time compact action.

and material-time path content. W3, W7, and W8 do not by themselves provide
those choices, and the fixed-field recoordination does not remove the nonlinear
closure defect that W7 was carrying.

The field-view prompt is therefore accepted only as a strict recoordination
reduction: packet identity is not sacred, and fixed-cover flux cancellation can
restore ownership at the whole-field level. It pays only after the selected
record is realized as a common spacetime current/profile object with signed
local balance, nonlinear graph closure, selected payer domination, continuous
time-shift action, terminal-window room, and typed boundary/singular failures.

## Cycle 8 Gate

The odd-kernel vanishing lemma is accepted under its stated symmetry
hypotheses. If the axis coordinate is \(y_1\), the transverse coordinate is
\(y_\perp\), and the relevant \(m=\pm1\) pressure-tilt kernel is
\[
  K(y)=\frac{y_1y_\perp}{|y|^5},
\]
then a fore-aft even pressure source \(Q(y_1,y_\perp)=Q(-y_1,y_\perp)\) gives
zero \(m=\pm1\) moment by oddness. That is an exact symmetry certificate for
the symmetric model branch.

It does not delete a wall. Evenness of \(|\omega|^2\) does not by itself prove
evenness of \(Q=|S|^2-\tfrac12|\omega|^2\); the strain part must carry the same
parity or be routed out. The support theorem still depends on unproved L1
standing-crest exclusion, L2 wake sign at the relevant \(Q\)-field, and the
gap-normalized transfer from well tilt to frame rotation. The old pursuit-lag
thread is also not an active frontier wall unless the owner installs it.

## Cycle 9 Gate

The scalar pulse tail computation is accepted as a conditional model
calculation. For a one-dimensional positive pulse
\[
  E(t,x_1)=e^{\gamma t}f(x_1-\tilde c t),
\]
with far-field equation
\[
  \nu f''+\tilde c f' +(g_\infty-\gamma)f=0,
\]

\[
  \mu_+ - \mu_-=\frac{\tilde c}{\nu},
\]

simple objection that growth alone must build the leading face.

The deletion claim still fails. The computed scalar \(E\) is not the pressure

the strain contribution is controlled in the same neighborhood. The assertion
that the equatorial annulus channel tilts the same way is still a sign theorem,
not a consequence of the scalar tail exponents alone. The mature-trajectory

near field - is precisely the surviving W9-style history/path burden, not a
certificate deleting it.

flux cancellation is ownership support, not wall deletion.
