# Refutation of the Recoordination Candidate

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9. Cross-wall identification attempted first:
Claude's candidate tries to identify the four-wall frontier with one
"proved per-shell simultaneous mechanism read in the fluid's currency, plus the
CM admission grammar." That identification fails because the proposed
restatement does not preserve the theorem-bearing content of the four walls.

Physical sentence: the fluid is one field, but a proof still has to show that
the readings of that field survive selection, rescaling, weak limits, material
time, and compactification. Same-fluid ontology forbids false source/payment
splits; it does not itself prove every row is admitted, tight, closed, or
future-determining.

## Scope of the Ontology Gates

The non-separable grammar rule kills a false formalization where one slaved row
is priced as though it were externally purchased by another channel. The
T-parity rule kills a T-even magnitude when it is offered as the sign lever for
\(c_0\). Neither rule says:

\[
\text{same-fluid row exists pointwise}
\Longrightarrow
\text{row passes all retained compactness/path/admission tests}.
\]

That missing implication is exactly what the remaining walls ask for.

## Per-Wall Refutation

W8 is not refuted by saying that collection from original data is a separate
ledger. The WLF source explicitly makes the child response a selected delayed
readout of the same parent impulse, and requires the detector to be
parent-weighted before clipping. The selected-linear measure is not a second
fluid source; it is the original-history finiteness statement that prevents a
later selected heat-lag readout from recounting parent work at unbounded critical
price. Claude's restatement as "proved per-shell bill plus reading" drops the
open clause: construction of the finite parent-weighted selected first-admission
pulse measure on the order-locked full-packet carrier, with same-packet overlap
and selected-overrun payment.

W7 is not refuted by saying that the fluid does not take limits. The proof uses
rescaled retained windows from the same history, and the nonlinear pressure
source must pass through critical compactness or expose the defect as paid,
owned, or routed. The source's obstruction

\[
\nabla U_n\rightharpoonup\nabla U,\qquad
Q[U_n]\rightharpoonup Q[U]+\mu_Q
\]

is a legitimate proof event for readings of one field. Per-instant row
attachment does not prove nonlinear graph closedness. Claude's restatement loses
the exact clause that \(Q[U_n]\), the pressure row, material-frame rows, and the
positive VPI record must pass, be paid/carried, parent-owned, Field-routed, or
typed as nonparticipation.

W9 is not refuted by calling a uniform path modulus T-even. A modulus is not
being used as the \(c_0\) sign lever. It is a compactness/admission condition
for the certified material-time path hull. The shift-hull note proves action
only after compact certified paths, terminal-window room, continuous fixed
shifts, and closed zero-surplus certificate rows have been built. BV identities
for already-billed coordinates do not construct that path topology or the
future-determining path/action coordinate.

W3 is not refuted by saying rung-uniform distribution demands a scale
preference. Critical covariance is exactly why fixed-rung local payment is not
enough: the selected row can follow the moving rung unless the tower measure is
tight/uniform or the escape is routed out. The Door-1 source proves a
fixed-window same-field \(H^1\)/enstrophy-viscous mechanism after the row is
admitted. It does not prove finite rung-uniform retained strict tower payment or
typed route-out for moving-rung concentration. Locality at one rung does not
delete the tower distribution clause.

## B2 Does Not Rescue the Candidate

The B2 setup is a lawful computation target, but it does not supply the claimed
one surviving object. The specification itself names kill modes. At the strict
atom \(S=a\,\mathrm{diag}(1,1,-2)\), the \(m=\pm2\) stretch-plane gap vanishes,
so that channel needs a charged deviation-lifted gap; the immediate legal
channel is \(m=\pm1\) with gap \(3a\). A future B2 sign result would also still
need bridges to W3, W7, W8, and W9.

## Conclusion

Claude's candidate correctly pressures the language. It does not prove a
two-are-one identity, an empty-by-admission clause, a refutation of an installed
wall, or a reduction to one surviving written object. It deletes analytic
admission and compactness requirements by calling them presentation errors.
That is not a payable certificate.
