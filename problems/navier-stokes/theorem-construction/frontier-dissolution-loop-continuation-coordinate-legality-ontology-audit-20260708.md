# Coordinate-Legality Ontology Audit

W3 and W8 are not one "payment" wall. W3 asks whether the same local
strain-production identity admits a retained \(H^1\) row tightly enough to pass
cubic service; W8 asks whether original data supply a finite selected-linear

Physical sentence: the fluid has one velocity-pressure history. The dangerous
presentation error is to turn a row of that history into a detachable ledger,

question is therefore not whether the words sound physical, but whether each
coordinate is a lawful reading of the same record and whether its failure is
paid, routed, or closed.

same-field viscous/enstrophy response. Door 1 gives the local identity:
\[

\(H^1\)-level viscous response in the same strain-production budget.

That removes the false ontology in which W3 purchases service from an external
account. It does not delete W3. The surviving W3 content is retained
row-admission, finiteness, and rung-uniform tightness for the \(H^1\) row, or a
typed route-out for moving-rung concentration.
