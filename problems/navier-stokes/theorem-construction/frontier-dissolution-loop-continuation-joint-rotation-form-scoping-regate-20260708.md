## Scoping Correction

The prior quadratic regate was too strong in one phrase. It said the
transverse-active restoring sign was accepted as a quadratic normal form. The
lawful statement is narrower:

1. At the irrotational strict atom \(u=Sx\), \(\Omega=0\) and \(\Delta S=0\), so
   the pressure-Hessian square is the whole off-diagonal rotation form.
2. On vorticity-carrying retained records, \((\Omega^2)_{ij}\) is generally
   alive. For example, in a local incompressible first jet with
   \(\omega=(\alpha,0,\beta)\),
   \[
     (\Omega^2)_{13}={1\over4}\alpha\beta,
   \]
   so the vorticity-square row is not structure-zero.
3. Therefore the mechanism-domain theorem is not pressure-block positivity
   alone. It is a same-field domination or accounting comparison on the
   strain-dominated \(Q>0\) branch:
   \[
     \hbox{transverse slaved pressure response}
     \quad\hbox{dominates, cancels, pays, or routes out}\quad
     \hbox{the vorticity-square off-diagonal row}.
   \]

This is the correct attack surface. It is sharper than the killed linear
orientation framing, and it also blocks the overstrong "rigidity already proved"
reading.
