# Ontological presentation assumption gate

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9.

Cross-wall identification attempted first: the four remaining walls are not
identified with the single phrase "faithful same-fluid retained record." That
phrase is a necessary ontology constraint, but it is not a proof object unless
it supplies admission, graph closure, weighted bill finiteness, and path-action
compactness.

Physical sentence: the retained packet, pressure source, pressure row, cubic
service, selected bill, and material-time path are readings of one
fixed-viscosity incompressible Navier-Stokes history. The proof can still fail
because a same-field reading can be inadmissible, lose nonlinear mass in a
critical limit, cost an infinite selected price before readout, or fail to be a
future-determining compact path after closure.

## 1. The presentation error

The suspect assumption is:
\[
\text{same-fluid retained history}
\Longrightarrow
\text{all record rows survive as one closed proof object}.
\tag{OPG.1}
\]

This implication is not licensed by the checked sources. Same-fluid ontology
only forbids separable fictions: pressure is not a free bank, the child readout
is not a second source, and material time is not an external clock. It does not
prove the functional-analytic properties needed after selection and
compactness.

The corrected ontology is one record with four proof-interface clauses:

1. W3 asks for retained admission/tightness of the localized \(H^1\) strain row
   strong enough to keep cubic service in \(L^3\).
2. W7 asks for nonlinear pressure/VPI graph passage or typed route-out through
   the critical retained limit.
3. W8 asks for finite selected-linear original-data bill currency before the
   later positive readout.
4. W9 asks for a compact future-determining material-time certificate path hull
   with continuous shifts and closed zero-surplus rows.

These are not four fluids. They are also not automatically one theorem.

## 2. Why W3 is not presentation-only

The W3 source isolates the cubic service gap:
\[
\|S_n\|_{L^3}^3\sim1,
\qquad
\|S_n\|_{L^2}^2\to0 .
\tag{OPG.2}
\]
Thus the ontology "same strain field" does not supply the row. The missing
fact is retained localized \(H^1\) admission/finiteness and rung-uniform
distribution, or a typed route-out for the moving-rung concentration.

## 3. Why W7 is not presentation-only

The W7 source isolates the nonlinear graph gap:
\[
\nabla U_n\rightharpoonup\nabla U,
\qquad
Q[U_n]\rightharpoonup Q[U]+\mu_Q .
\tag{OPG.3}
\]
The defect \(\mu_Q\) is not a second pressure source. It is a possible loss in
the proof reading of one pressure-constrained record. To delete W7, the source
must prove strong graph passage, paid/owned carriage, Field/nonparticipation,
or Pack/Part/Field route-out.

## 4. Why W8 is not presentation-only

The W8 source isolates the weighted selected-price gap:
\[
\sum_\ell \|P_{\mathbb R e_\ell}j_P\|^2<\infty,
\qquad
\sum_\ell \omega_\ell|\Delta_\ell|=\infty .
\tag{OPG.4}
\]
Same-parent origin, raw Bessel support, local first admission, and no-recount
do not imply finite selected-linear critical price. The missing fact is the
order-locked full-packet selected carrier with paid overlap and selected
overrun, or an equivalent original-data matrix with strict feedback.

## 5. Why W9 is not presentation-only

The W9 source proves a shift action only after certified paths
\[
Z_j:I_j\to X
\tag{OPG.5}
\]
have a compact shifted hull, terminal-window room, continuous fixed shifts, and
closed zero-surplus certificate rows. Pointwise row compatibility does not
define a single-valued future map on the compactified state.

The abstract obstruction is exact: if two certified representatives have the
same retained state \(x\) at one time but different future germs, then
\(\Phi_h(x)\) is not well-defined. Adding the germ to W7 would simply insert
W9's surviving content into W7 by definition.

## 6. Gate verdict

The assumptions that built the remaining problem presentation are indeed
suspect, but the suspect move is the collapse from physical identity to proof
closure. The current sources do not make that collapse.

No new wall is admitted. "Faithful same-fluid retained record closure" would
only be a lawful new name if it deleted at least two existing walls. At present
it is just the unproved conjunction of W3, W7, W8, and W9.

Partner refutation target: produce one checked source theorem showing that
same-fluid retained-record ontology already gives W3 row admission, W7 nonlinear
graph passage or typed route-out, W8 finite selected-linear bill finiteness, and
W9 compact material-time path/action, or show that one existing wall contains
the other three without adding them by definition.
