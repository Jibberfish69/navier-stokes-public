it owns. A sign choice for \(H_{13}\) or \(H_{23}\), or a scalar helicity row
used as an orienter, is a linear pairing problem. The transverse-active
pressure-Hessian contribution above is quadratic and reflection-even after the
in-plane gauge is quotiented. The Row 5 certificates remain valid only against
scalar or linear orientation shortcuts; they do not attack the quadratic
restoring normal form.

There is one attackable caveat in the square certificate. Before quotienting,
the same algebra gives
\[
  -2\sum_{i<j}(\lambda_i+\lambda_j)\widehat H_{ij}^2

\(\widehat H_{12}\) is in-plane gauge because \(\lambda_1=\lambda_2\). A
partner refutation should attack exactly that quotient step, or else accept the
transverse-active sign and move to the accounting theorem.
