# Row 5 zero-helicity is not tilt-neutral by ontology

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9.

Cross-wall identification attempted first: the Row 5 zero-helicity stratum is
not identified with W9 path/action, W7 typed route-out, or the B2 tilt-neutral
class. Zero scalar helicity is one readout value; it is not the same object as
reflection symmetry, retained graph passage, selected original-data payment, or
compact material-time action.

Physical sentence: a same-fluid front can have zero local handedness because
the packet is viewed in its co-moving frame, or because velocity is orthogonal
to vorticity, while its vorticity axis still tilts the strain eigenframe. Zero
local helicity is weaker than mirror symmetry.

## Exact local jet certificate

At a point \(x_0\), take a Galilean/co-moving frame with
\[
  u(x_0)=0.
\]
Choose an incompressible first jet
\[
  \nabla u(x_0)=S+\Omega,
  \qquad
  S=a\,\operatorname{diag}(1,1,-2),
\]
and choose vorticity
\[
  \omega(x_0)=(\alpha,0,\beta),
  \qquad
  \alpha\beta\ne0.
\]
This is a lawful divergence-free local jet because \(\operatorname{tr}S=0\)
and \(\Omega\) is skew. With the convention
\[
  \Omega v={1\over2}\,\omega\times v,
\]
one has
\[
  \Omega^2={1\over4}\left(\omega\otimes\omega-|\omega|^2I\right),
\]
hence
\[
  (\Omega^2)_{13}={1\over4}\alpha\beta\ne0.
\]
But the local helicity density at the same point is
\[
  h(x_0)=u(x_0)\cdot\omega(x_0)=0.
\]

The legal B2 \(m=\pm1\) frame law already isolated the compression-axis tilt
numerator as
\[
  (\Omega^2)_{13}+H_{13}
\]
over the gap \(3a\). The displayed jet shows that \(h=0\) imposes no local
algebraic vanishing of the \((\Omega^2)_{13}\) part of that numerator. Thus
zero helicity cannot be used as a tilt-neutral certificate unless the retained
record also proves a stronger symmetry or charges/routes every remaining tilt
drive.

## Ontology correction

The Row 5 object should not be presented as:
\[
  h=0 \quad\Rightarrow\quad \text{tilt-neutral retained front}.
\]
The source-correct form is:
\[
  \text{reflection-symmetric retained record}
  \quad\Rightarrow\quad
  \text{zero net reflection-odd tilt drive},
\]
or else a separate theorem must prove that every retained \(h=0\) front is
reflection-symmetric in the variables that enter the B2 frame law.

This matters because local helicity density is not itself a complete
orientation record. It sees \(u\cdot\omega\); the B2 tilt law sees the strain
frame, vorticity-axis square, and pressure Hessian row. Collapsing those
readouts is exactly the suspect presentation move.

## Frontier effect

This refutes only the shortcut from zero helicity to automatic tilt neutrality.
It does not refute a future stronger Row 5 theorem with retained symmetry,
nonzero/zero-stratum typing, and a bridge from chirality to the legal tilt
coefficient.

The surviving exact fact needed from Row 5 is now sharper:
\[
  \text{retained chirality/symmetry row}
  \;+\;
  \text{zero-stratum tilt-drive cancellation or route-out}
  \;+\;
  \operatorname{sgn}(h_{\rm front})\mapsto\operatorname{sgn}(c).
\]
No checked source supplies that conjunction, and admitting it as a new name
would not be net-negative because it deletes no existing wall.
