# Second-Order Ontology Audit of the Surviving Frontier

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9. Cross-wall identification attempted first:
the four survivors are not one paid wall yet. The strongest common reading is a
single faithful retained-record map from original same-fluid history to a
compact material-time certificate, but no checked source proves that map or shows
that it deletes at least two existing walls.

Physical sentence: there is one fixed-viscosity incompressible Navier-Stokes
history. A packet, pressure row, service row, selected bill, material-time path,
and compact certificate are readings of that history. A surviving wall is
lawful only when it states which reading fails to pass, which bill owns that
failure, or which Pack/Part/Field route receives it.

## Audit of the Four Surviving Presentations

W3 was presented as service persistence from payment. The Door-1 source proves
the correct same-field identity: the localized \(H^1\)/enstrophy-viscous row is
the viscous response in the same strain-production budget that contains the
Vieillefosse cubic service. The suspect assumption is the word "payment" as
though it were a separable currency. Ontologically, W3 is not a separate service
event. It is a row-admission clause for the same retained record:

\[
\text{localized same-field }H^1\text{ row}
\quad\leadsto\quad
\text{finite rung-uniform strict tower payment or typed route-out}.
\]

The source proves the conditional fixed-window mechanism, not the tower-uniform
admission law.

W7 was presented as retained graph closedness. This is the least artificial
survivor, because it is exactly where same-fluid ontology meets critical
compactness. The pressure source is constitutive:

\[
-\Delta p=Q[u].
\]

The suspect assumption is that the graph row can be isolated from the rest of
the retained-history certification. A graph limit of the pressure source,
pressure row, material-frame rows, material-time rows, and positive VPI service
is really one retained-record passage problem. The source still allows

\[
\nabla U_n\rightharpoonup\nabla U,\qquad
Q[U_n]\rightharpoonup Q[U]+\mu_Q,
\]

so W7 remains genuine unless \(\mu_Q\) is graph-closed, paid/carried,
parent-owned, Field-routed, or typed as nonparticipation.

W8 was presented as selected-linear original-data payment. This is the most
presentation-sensitive survivor. The WLF/ODP source says the child readout is a
selected delayed reading of the same parent impulse, and the detector must be
parent-weighted before clipping. The suspect assumption is that the
selected-linear bill is a primitive lower physical event. It is not. It is the
pre-readout currency needed to prevent a future selected heat-lag read from
recounting parent work at unbounded critical price. The wall survives only
because the source still lacks the original-history construction of the
order-locked full-packet carrier with same-packet overlap and selected-overrun
payment.

W9 was presented as material-time certificate flow invariance. The abstract
shift-hull lemma proves the action after a compact certified path hull and
closed zero-surplus certificate graph already exist. The suspect assumption is
that action is an independent topological problem. It is not. W9 is the
path-level version of retained-record faithfulness: the path state must carry
same datum, fixed viscosity, pressure law/Reynolds defect, material ancestry,
finite cover, differentiated tower data, service modulus, and zero-surplus payer
closure. It remains open because W7-style pointwise graph passage does not by
itself provide a compact material/label modulus, terminal-window room, or
continuous shift action.

## Audit of the B2 Unifier Presentation

The B2 "one obligation" is a lawful local physical target, but it is not the
common root of the four walls until two extra facts are proved.

First, the channel must be legal. The cycle-11--13 specification already names a
kill mode: at the strict atom \(S=a\,\mathrm{diag}(1,1,-2)\), the stretch-plane
\(m=\pm2\) eigenvalue gap vanishes. The immediate frame-rotation computation is
therefore lawful only in the \(m=\pm1\) compression-axis tilt channel with gap
\(3a\), or in an \(m=\pm2\) channel whose deviation-lifted gap is itself charged.

Second, even a restored off-frame sign does not automatically supply the record
map. It would still need exact bridges saying how the signed mechanism admits
W3's row, constructs W8's original-data bill currency, closes or routes W7's
pressure/VPI graph, and produces W9's compact material-time path/action data.

Thus B2 is a computation inside the faithful retained-record map, not a
deletion of that map.

## Corrected Common Shape

The mature ontology has two layers.

Layer 1 is faithful retained-record passage:

\[
\text{one original same-fluid history}
\Longrightarrow
\text{same retained record rows close, pay, or route out}.
\]

This is where W3, W7, and W8 live: service-row admission, pressure/VPI graph
passage, and selected-linear original-data bill currency are clauses of one
retained-record passage problem.

Layer 2 is material-time compactification of that record:

\[
\text{faithful retained record paths}
\Longrightarrow
\text{compact shift hull with closed certificate graph and terminal room}.
\]

This is W9. It is not a separate fluid, but it is stronger than a pointwise graph
statement because it asks for path topology, label/modulus control, and action on
the compactified closure.

The common-root name "faithful retained-record material-time map" is not
admitted as a new wall. It has not deleted two existing walls. It is only the
audited shape that future deletion certificates must attack.

W7, W8, and W9.

Absent one of those, the ontology audit corrects the presentation but pays no
deletion.
