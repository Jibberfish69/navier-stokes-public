# Source-Ontology Assumption Audit

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9. Cross-wall identification attempted first:
the four-wall frontier is not identified with W7 alone, with W9 alone, with
Claude's recoordination candidate, or with B2. The common physical object is
one fixed-viscosity incompressible Navier-Stokes history, but the remaining
walls ask different proof-interface questions about that history: row admission,
nonlinear graph passage, selected-linear original-data finiteness, and compact
material-time path/action.

Physical sentence: the fluid does not split into four events. A retained
packet, pressure source, pressure row, service row, selected bill, and
material-time certificate are readings of one same field. The proof can still
fail when a reading is not admitted into the retained class, is lost in a
critical limit, is too expensive after selected readout, or does not define a
compact future-determining path.

## Assumption 1: same-fluid ontology makes admission automatic

This assumption is false. Same-fluid ontology says a retained record may not
silently amputate its pressure, frame, material-time, or service rows. It does
not prove those rows survive critical compactness.

The W7 source states the exact obstruction:
\[
\nabla U_n\rightharpoonup\nabla U,
\qquad
Q[U_n]\rightharpoonup Q[U]+\mu_Q .
\]
This is not a second fluid and not a separable pressure bank. It is a possible
loss in the proof reading of one retained record. To delete W7, the defect
\(\mu_Q\) must be graph-closed, paid/carried, parent-owned, Field-routed, or
typed as nonparticipation. The source does not supply that implication.

## Assumption 2: same-time pressure row kills W8's selected-linear bill

This assumption is false as a proof step. The complete participation tangent is
simultaneous at each time, and pressure is its slaved nonlocal elliptic row
to the current velocity-gradient field. W8 is not a claim that a child event
creates a second source later. It is the finiteness of the selected-linear
parent-weighted first-admission bill before the later positive readout can
reprice the same parent impulse.

The WLF source already installs same-parent pairing, no-recount, raw Bessel
admission, and local first-admission support. It still gives the half-tail
attack:
\[
\sum_\ell \|P_{\mathbb R e_\ell}j_P\|^2<\infty,
\qquad
\sum_\ell \omega_\ell|\Delta_\ell|=\infty .
\]
The half-tail is a formal implication test, not a Navier-Stokes history. It
still proves that raw same-parent square support and no-recount do not imply
finite selected-linear critical price. To delete W8, one must prove the
order-locked full-packet selected carrier with paid overlap and selected
overrun from original data, or route its failure out.

## Assumption 3: scale covariance makes W3 rung-uniformity illicit

This assumption is false. Scale covariance forbids a fixed preferred scale. It
does not prove that a selected row cannot follow the moving critical rung.

The W3 source says the service is cubic, so \(L^2\) compactness alone can lose
it:
\[
\|S_n\|_{L^3}^3\sim1,
\qquad
\|S_n\|_{L^2}^2\to0 .
\]
If the same-field bill admits the localized \(H^1\) strain row as retained
strict payment, strong \(L^3\) persistence follows. The missing theorem is the
admission, finiteness, and rung-uniform distribution of that row, or a typed
route-out for moving-rung concentration. Calling the local row "same-field"
does not supply the retained tower measure.

## Assumption 4: actual material histories flow, so W9 is administrative

This assumption is false. Actual smooth histories can be shifted in material
time. W9 is about the compactified certificate family after selection and
closure.

The W9 source proves only the abstract shift-hull lemma: once certified paths
\(Z_j:I_j\to X\) have a compact path hull, terminal-window room, continuous
fixed shifts, and a closed zero-surplus certificate graph, material-time
invariance follows. It does not construct that compact path object for
Navier-Stokes. The path state has to carry pressure law, Reynolds defect,
material ancestry, cover, tower rows, service modulus, and payer data in one
future-determining topology. Pointwise row attachment does not define the
action on closure.

## Assumption 5: B2 is the survivor because it is the physical sign

This assumption is unproved and currently too strong. B2 is a lawful physical
subproblem: decide the off-frame restoring sign for a retained record in a
legal channel. It is not yet the terminal object deleting W3, W7, W8, and W9.

The strict-atom \(m=\pm2\) channel has already been killed as an immediate
certificate because the stretch-plane eigenvalue gap vanishes at
\[
S=a\,\mathrm{diag}(1,1,-2).
\]
The legal immediate channel is \(m=\pm1\) with gap \(3a\), or a
deviation-lifted \(m=\pm2\) theorem with its own charge. Even if the legal B2
sign is proved, a deletion certificate still has to state how that sign proves
W3 row admission, W8 selected-linear original-data payment, W7 graph passage or
typed route-out, and W9 compact path/action.

## Audit Verdict

The remaining frontier was over-presented as four separate fluid obstacles.
That presentation is ontologically wrong. The corrected reading is:

1. W3 is the retained admission/tightness of the same-field \(H^1\) service row.
2. W7 is nonlinear pressure/VPI graph passage or typed route-out through a
   critical retained limit.
3. W8 is finite selected-linear original-data bill currency before positive
   readout.
4. W9 is compact path-germ/action for the certified material-time closure.

These are four proof-interface clauses of one same-fluid record, not four
fluid events. The stronger dissolution claim fails because the current sources
do not prove that the one-fluid ontology automatically supplies those clauses.
The hidden hard assumption behind the remaining arguments is:
\[
\text{faithful same-fluid record}
\Longrightarrow
\text{admitted rows + graph closure + selected bill finiteness + path action}.
\]
That implication is the unproved content. It is not admitted here as a new wall
because it has not deleted two existing names.

## Refutation Target for the Partner Lane

To refute this certificate alone, produce one exact source-backed theorem or
calculation showing one of the following:

1. same-fluid retained-record ontology already implies W3 row admission,
   W7 graph passage, W8 selected-linear bill finiteness, and W9 path action;
2. W7's retained graph/admission clause already contains W3, W8, and W9
   without adding them by definition;
3. W9's compact material-time certificate already contains W3, W7, and W8
   without adding them by definition;
4. a legal B2 sign theorem supplies explicit bridges to W3, W7, W8, and W9.

Absent one of those exact certificates, the ontology audit corrects the
presentation and pays no deletion.
