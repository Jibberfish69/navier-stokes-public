# W3 is not deleted by W7 plus W8 plus W9

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9. Cross-wall identification attempted first:
W3 is not identified with W7+W8+W9, because W7 passes or routes retained graph
rows through a critical limit, W8 supplies selected-linear original-data payment,
and W9 supplies compact material-time path/action only after certificate rows
are carried. W3 asks for the Door-1 local strain \(H^1\) response to be admitted
as a finite, uniform, retained same-field payment row in the strict tower before
zero payment can force selected cubic service persistence.

## Physical sentence

The same Navier-Stokes history can carry a pressure/VPI graph, have an
original-data payment ledger, and admit a compact material-time reading of
already-carried certificates, while still not proving that the localized
enstrophy-viscous response

```math
d\mu_{\rm D1}
```

is distributed as a rung-uniform strict retained tower payment row. W3 is
precisely that admission/finiteness/uniformity point for the Door-1 service row.

## Source check

The Door-1 source is conditional. It proves that Claude's \(a=r\) bubble beats
an \(L^2\)/Korn-only bill but not the same-field \(H^1\) row from the local
strain-production budget. Its condition is explicit: if that row is admitted as
actual retained strict payment, zero payment gives strong \(L^3\) persistence of
the non-affine strain defect. It also says this is not \(c_0\) closure and that
row admission/finiteness plus Door-2 endpoint burdens remain.

The global strict-bill source has the same condition. It defines

```math
d\mu_{\rm strict}
=
d\mu_{\rm bad}
+d\mu_{\rm D1}
+d\mu_{\rm chain}
+d\mu_{\rm end}
+d\mu_{\rm D2a}
+d\mu_{\rm D2b},
```

where \(d\mu_{\rm D1}\) is the localized enstrophy-viscous \(H^1\) response from
the same strain-production budget as the cubic service. But its completion truth
says it proves only the no-loss/no-double-count accounting after the local
payment theorems have already been admitted in the same tower currency.

W7 supplies retained graph passage or typed route-out for pressure source,
pressure row, material-frame rows, material-time rows, and positive VPI record.
Its own source says the missing theorem is graph closedness of the retained VPI
record through critical compactness, not the Door-1 admission of
\(d\mu_{\rm D1}\) as a finite/uniform tower payment row.

W8 supplies selected-linear WLF.60 / ODP.91 original-data payment on the
order-locked full-packet selected carrier. Its source proves no WLF.60 estimate
and keeps parent-weighted pulse-measure finiteness as the remaining theorem; it
does not identify \(d\mu_{\rm D1}\) as collected selected-linear bill.

W9 supplies the abstract shift-hull implication after compact certified paths
and closed certificate rows exist. Its source explicitly says the compact hull
and closed graph are not free and that Navier-Stokes must still carry pressure
law/Reynolds rows, material ancestry, finite cover, tower rows, service modulus,
zero-payer rows, and terminal-window room. W9 is a reader/action theorem for
already-carried rows; it is not an admission theorem for \(d\mu_{\rm D1}\) into
the strict bill.

## Exact failed implication

The full-coalition deletion would need:

```math
\text{W7 retained graph passage/route-out}
+\text{W8 selected-linear original-data payment}
+\text{W9 compact material-time path/action}
\Longrightarrow
d\mu_{\rm D1}\le d\mu_{\rm strict}
\text{ as a finite, rung-uniform retained tower row.}
```

No checked source proves that implication. The prior fixed-window dissipation
gate already isolates why ordinary finite row mass is not enough: a row measure
can follow the moving selected rung without giving rung-uniform retained tower
distribution or typed route-out. W9 does not repair that by itself, because its
path topology starts after the relevant rows have been put into the certificate
state.

## Attack surface for Claude

To refute this certificate, attack the certificate alone. Cite a checked source
where W7+W8+W9 imply the exact Door-1 admission fact: the localized strain
\(H^1\)/enstrophy-viscous row \(d\mu_{\rm D1}\) is admitted, finite, and
rung-uniform as retained same-field strict tower payment, or where concentration
of that row along the moving selected rung is typed as paid/owned/route-out.

Without that exact fact, W3 remains live.
