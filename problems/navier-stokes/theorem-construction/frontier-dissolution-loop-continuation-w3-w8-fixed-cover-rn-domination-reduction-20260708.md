# W3-W8 Fixed-Cover Radon-Nikodym Domination Reduction

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9.

Cross-wall failure sentence: fixed-cover ownership does not identify W3 with
W8, because fixed-cover cancellation removes spatial handoff loss, while W3 is
the Door-1 localized \(H^1\)/enstrophy-viscous row and W8 is the
selected-linear original-data bill on the order-locked full-packet carrier.

Physical sentence: the fixed cover keeps one global owner for the same velocity
field, but the W3 danger can still move through scale/rung coordinates; to
spend that Door-1 row as W8 payment, the selected Door-1 measure must be
dominated by the W8 selected-linear bill or its singular part must route out.

## Source Check

The Door-1 source identifies the W3 row as the same-field localized strain
response
\[
  d\mu_{D1}
  =
  \nu\,\phi^2|\nabla S|^2\,dx\,dt
  + d\mu_{\rm collar}
  + d\mu_{\rm owner},
\]
read from the local strain-production budget. It proves only the conditional
mechanism: once this row is admitted as retained strict payment, localized
Hodge-Korn and Sobolev give strong \(L^3\) persistence of the selected cubic
service.

The global strict-bill accounting source confirms the same status. It lists
\(d\mu_{D1}\) as one summand of \(d\mu_{\rm strict}\), but its completion truth
says the no-loss/no-double-count argument starts only after the local payment
rows have already been admitted into the same tower currency. It does not
construct W8's selected-linear original-data collection measure.

The W8 sources give the relevant local form of the missing admission. The
first-ratio row is the parent-weighted selected first-admission pulse measure
on the same order-locked full-packet selected carrier. The half-tail
\[
  \nu_\ell={2^{-\ell}\over \ell+1},
  \qquad
  \sum_\ell\nu_\ell<\infty,
  \qquad
  \sum_\ell 2^\ell\nu_\ell=\infty
\]
shows that raw ownership, raw square/Bessel support, finite menu, no-recount,
and quadratic viscous thickness are not W8 bill currency. The current W8 local
Radon-Nikodym shape is the same-parent route-measure requirement: the pushed
inverse clock must be absolutely continuous with respect to the parent active
measure, have bounded density, and route singular clock mass to paid
first-entry, terminal no-residue, legal, stop, or CM before it is counted.

Fixed-cover flux cancellation adds a real ownership bridge but not this
domination. If a selected record density has a balance
\[
  \partial_tR+\nabla\cdot J_R
  =
  G_R-B_R+\operatorname{Route}_R,
\]
then a fixed partition \(\{\chi_\alpha\}\) cancels the internal spatial handoff:
\[
  \sum_\alpha\int J_R\cdot\nabla\chi_\alpha=0.
\]
That identity keeps packet exit globally owned. It does not say the selected
Door-1 row is absolutely continuous with respect to the W8 first-ratio bill in
the scale/rung coordinate.

## Exact Reduction Target

The precise theorem that would make W3 a W8 consumer is:
\[
  d\mu_{D1}^{\rm sel}
  =
  f\,d\mu_{W8}
  +
  d\mu^\perp,
  \qquad
  \|f\|_{L^\infty(d\mu_{W8})}\le C,
\]
on the order-locked fixed-cover/rung carrier, with every singular part
\(d\mu^\perp\) assigned to a paid first-entry, terminal no-residue, legal, stop,
or CM route-out.

Equivalently, for the retained tower cells \(E_{\ell,\alpha}\),
\[
  \sum_{\ell,\alpha}\omega_\ell\,
  \mu_{D1}^{\rm sel}(E_{\ell,\alpha})
  \le
  C\,\mu_{W8}(P)
  +
  \operatorname{Route}(P)
\]
with the W3/W8 selected tower weights. Proving this would delete W3 into W8:
the Door-1 \(H^1\) row would be admitted as W8 collection currency, and
fixed-cover cancellation would prevent spatial handoff loss.

Current sources do not prove this. A finite-measure model shows exactly why the
fixed-cover identity is insufficient: take a fixed region \(\alpha_0\) and let
\(\mu_n=\delta_{(\alpha_0,n)}\) in the region/rung coordinate. Spatial ownership
is perfect and there is no inter-region leakage, but the row still follows the
moving selected rung. That is the W3 tower-uniformity obstruction, not a W7/W9
ownership problem.

## Gate Verdict

No wall is deleted. The fixed-cover frame sharpens the W3-W8 interface:
regional ownership is no longer the issue; the issue is Radon-Nikodym
domination of the selected Door-1 row by W8's original-data first-ratio bill,
or typed route-out of the singular part.

This is not a new admitted wall. It is the exact noncircular deletion criterion
inside the existing W3/W8 pair.
