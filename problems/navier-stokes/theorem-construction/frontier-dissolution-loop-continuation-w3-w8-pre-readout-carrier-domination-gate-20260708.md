# W3-W8 Pre-Readout Carrier Domination Gate

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9.

Cross-wall identification attempted first: W3 does not identify with W8 merely
because the Door-1 \(H^1\)/enstrophy row and the W8 first-ratio row have the
same critical scale; W3 is read after retained strict-tower packet selection,
while W8 is a parent-owned selected-linear bill on an order-locked carrier
chosen before positive readout.

Physical sentence: the same velocity field can pay a real local enstrophy bill
at the retained Door-1 packet, but W8 only counts that bill as original-data
payment if the parent carrier and active weight were already announced before
the selected positive tail identified the packet.

## Source Check

The Door-1 source identifies the W3 row as the same-field localized strain
response
\[
  \nu\int\phi^2|\nabla S|^2
\]
from the local strain-production identity. It also marks the result as
conditional: the row has to be admitted as actual retained strict payment before
zero payment gives strong \(L^3\) persistence of the selected cubic service.

The global strict-bill accounting source keeps that condition. Its strict bill
measure includes \(d\mu_{D1}\), but the file proves no-loss/no-double-count only
after the local payment rows are admitted in the same tower currency.

The W8 sources impose a different time order. WLF.60 / ODP.91 is the
parent-weighted selected first-admission pulse measure on the order-locked
full-packet selected carrier. The WLF/ODP affine-transition note says the row
must be built before child positive clipping; the first-ratio bill note says
the active weight is fixed from the stopped parent history before the scalar
positive selected tail is read.

This order is not cosmetic. The BFE routing table states that a weight chosen
from the future selected tail is not an order-locked carrier and routes to
selector/recount/legal instead of W8 payment. It also states that choosing the
primitive channel after seeing the positive tail routes to rotation/reset/
overrun, legal, stop, or typed exit.

## Failed Deletion Attempt

The tempting deletion proof is:
\[
  \hbox{Door-1 same-field }H^1\hbox{ bill at scale }\ell
  \sim
  {\nu\over \ell}
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \hbox{W8 selected-linear first-ratio payment}.
\]
That argument only compares scale size after the selected Door-1 packet is
known. It does not supply the W8 parent carrier before readout, and it does not
prove that the selected Door-1 row is the pushforward of an original-history
first-admission pulse.

If the W8 carrier is chosen by looking at where Door-1 service concentrates,
the move is exactly the source-forbidden future-tail choice. It may reveal a
real W3 payment row, but it is not W8 currency unless an antecedent parent
carrier map exists or the failure routes out through the typed clauses.

## Exact Reduction Target

The noncircular theorem that would delete W3 into W8 is a pre-readout carrier
domination statement. There must be a parent-history map \(\pi_{\rm pre}\),
fixed before selected positive readout, such that
\[
  (\pi_{\rm pre})_\# d\mu_{D1}^{\rm sel}
  =
  f\,d\mu_{W8}
  +
  d\mu^\perp,
  \qquad
  \|f\|_{L^\infty(d\mu_{W8})}\le C,
\]
and every non-announced or singular part \(d\mu^\perp\) is typed by the BFE.20
clauses as selector/recount/legal, rotation/reset/overrun, stop, service/
custody, Pack/Part/Field, or terminally admitted unpaid Bill.

Equivalently: every selected Door-1 \(H^1\)/enstrophy-viscous row must have been
announced by the same order-locked W8 parent first-ratio carrier before positive
readout, with bounded density in the W8 selected-linear bill, or its failure
must leave the retained branch legally.

Current sources do not prove that theorem. They prove the Door-1 row is the
right same-field bill once admitted, and they prove the W8 order-locked carrier
requirement. They do not prove that every selected Door-1 row is already
carried by that pre-readout W8 parent coordinate.

## Gate Verdict

No wall is deleted. The ontology correction is exact: same-fluid row reality is
weaker than pre-readout W8 bill admissibility. W3 is a W8 consumer only after
the pre-readout carrier map / Radon-Nikodym domination / typed singular route-out
theorem is proved.

No new wall is admitted. This is the sharper W3-W8 deletion criterion inside the
existing frontier.
