## Ontology audit

The presentation error to kill is the idea that the four surviving walls are
four independent fluid events. They are not. They are four proof-interface
obligations on one retained same-fluid Navier-Stokes history.

That correction does not delete W7. In the same record, pressure is slaved to
the current velocity gradient:

```math
-\Delta p=Q[u],
\qquad
Q[u]=\operatorname{tr}((\nabla u)^2).
```

The retained row-passage source therefore represents the tower by one packet

```math
\mathcal R_n=(U_n,P_n,Q_n,\mathcal F_n,\mathcal M_n,\mathcal S_n),
\qquad
Q_n=Q[U_n].
```

The suspect assumption in the coalition argument is that once service is
preserved, the selected-linear payer is finite, and the material-time reading is
lawful, the nonlinear pressure-source row must have passed with them. That is
not a consequence of same-fluid ontology. Same-fluid ontology says the row
cannot be silently dropped; it does not say weak critical compactness is
multiplicative.

The exact W7 obstruction remains:

```math
\nabla U_n \rightharpoonup \nabla U,
\qquad
Q[U_n]\rightharpoonup Q[U]+\mu_Q.
```

Here \(\mu_Q\) is not a second fluid and not an optional bookkeeping variable.
It is the same record reporting lost nonlinear pressure-source material. A paid
proof must either close the graph, pay and carry the defect, assign it to a
parent/source relay, or route it to Field/nonparticipation.

## Why the coalition does not force W7

W3 gives service persistence only after the local strain \(H^1\) response is
admitted as retained same-field payment. Its own source leaves row
admission/finiteness, Door 2 endpoint payment, and global tower accounting
outside Door 1. It does not prove strong graph closedness of
\(\nabla U\mapsto Q[U]\) for arbitrary retained tower sequences.

W8 gives the selected-linear WLF.60 / ODP.91 original-data payment target. It
can pay a row only after that row has been typed as the selected-linear bill
currency on the order-locked full-packet carrier. It does not by itself identify
the pressure-source defect \(\mu_Q\) as collected W8 bill.

W9 proves an abstract shift-hull implication: after compact certified
material-time paths and a closed zero-surplus certificate graph are supplied,
fixed shifts preserve the hull. Its source explicitly says Navier-Stokes still
has to supply pressure-law/Reynolds closure, material ancestry, finite cover,
differentiated tower, service modulus, zero-payer rows, and terminal-window
room. W9 is not a producer of the W7 graph passage; it is a lawful reader once
the required rows are already carried.

Thus W3+W8+W9 leave exactly the W7 question open: does every retained
same-record critical limit either pass

```math
Q[U_n]\to Q[U],\quad
P_n\to P,\quad
\mathcal F_n\to\mathcal F,\quad
\mathcal M_n\to\mathcal M,\quad
\liminf\mathcal S_n>0,
```

or expose a paid/owned/typed route-out for the dropped row?

No checked source proves that implication.
