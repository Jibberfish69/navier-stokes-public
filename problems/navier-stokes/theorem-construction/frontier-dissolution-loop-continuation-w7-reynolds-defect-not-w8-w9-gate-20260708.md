# W7 Reynolds Defect Is Not W8 Plus W9

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9.

Cross-wall identification attempted first: W7's Reynolds-defect residue does
not identify with W8+W9. W8 is selected-linear original-data first-ratio
payment before positive readout, and W9 is compact material-time
path/zero-surplus closure after certificate rows are carried; neither source
constructs legal carriage or payment for every Reynolds defect produced by W7
critical graph failure.

Physical sentence: unresolved high-frequency self-interaction of the same
field can leave positive Reynolds ballast energy; that energy is real same-field
content, but it still has to land on cover, ancestry, tower, payer, and
terminal rows before it is either consumed or routed out.

## 1. The candidate

After the field-current gate, W7 is narrowed to this exact residue:
\[
  R=0
  \quad\text{or}\quad
  \mathbb P\operatorname{div}R=0
  \text{ with pressure-service compatibility}
  \quad\text{or}\quad
  R\text{ legally carried/routed.}
\]

The tempting deletion says: nonzero \(R\) is either paid by W8 or legally
carried by W9, so W7 reduces to W8+W9.

That would be a deletion if the checked sources proved the universal
implication
\[
  \text{Reynolds defect in W7 closure}
  \Longrightarrow
  \text{W8 collected bill or W9 closed carried row/route-out}.
\]
They do not.

## 2. What the ballast sources do prove

For an actual positive-semidefinite Reynolds covariance defect,
\[
  u_j\otimes u_j\rightharpoonup u\otimes u+R,
  \qquad R\ge0,
\]
the trace is unresolved kinetic energy:
\[
  \mu_R={1\over2}\operatorname{tr}R.
\]
Pressure gauge can absorb \(\operatorname{div}R=\nabla q\) in the velocity
equation, but it cannot erase \(\mu_R\).

The PSD covariance branch has no vanishing-energy horn. If
\[
  R={\tau\over3}I+A,\qquad \operatorname{tr}A=0,\qquad R\ge0,
\]
then
\[
  |A|_F\le 2\sqrt{2/3}\,\mu_R.
\]
Thus vanishing normalized trace energy forces vanishing normalized anisotropic
covariance. If the trace has a positive terminal subtail and the WKB witness is
admitted at frequency \(\lambda_j\to\infty\), the Field consumer gives
\[
  \|\nabla^m u_j^{\rm WKB}\|_2^2
  \gtrsim
  \mu_R\lambda_j^{2m}.
\]

The ballast must also land on the legal certificate rows. The cover row says
positive ballast either lifts to the finite/readout-compatible cover, or it is
Pack-side cover failure. The ancestry row says its material energy current
must have
\[
  A_R=J_R-u\rho_R
\]
vanishing or legally carried; singular current relative to \(\rho_R\) is
material-history failure. The tower row says derivative graph defects and
differentiated nonlinear flux defects vanish or are legally carried, or the
branch is same-law tower failure.

These are real reductions. They do not install W8 or W9 deletion.

## 3. Why W8 does not collect the defect by default

The ballast energy \(\mu_R\) is a positive same-field energy ledger. W8 is a
different currency: WLF.60 / ODP.91 selected-linear original-data payment on an
order-locked full-packet carrier before positive readout.

No checked source proves that every Reynolds ballast measure is announced on
that W8 carrier with bounded Radon-Nikodym density, or that every singular part
routes through W8's selected-linear first-admission bill. The existing W8
coalition gate already refutes the broader shortcut: retained graph closure and
certificate path closure do not construct selected-linear original-data
payment. The Reynolds ballast sources sharpen this only to a new necessary
landing problem; they do not change the W8 currency.

## 4. Why W9 does not carry the defect by default

W9 acts after the compact certificate path hull, terminal-window room, and
zero-surplus closed graph are already supplied. A positive Reynolds ballast row
is not automatically a zero-surplus row. It must be carried through cover,
ancestry, tower, pressure-service, payer, and terminal-window coordinates, or
else route out.

The ballast ancestry/tower source explicitly says it proves no transport-defect
vanishing theorem, no tower-defect vanishing theorem, and no legal carriage
theorem for nonzero defects. Therefore W9 does not delete W7's Reynolds-defect
residue. W9 can read a legally carried defect in a common path topology only
after W7 or another source has supplied the legal carriage.

## 5. Signed/null-stress branch

The Beltrami/null-stress follow-up also stops short of deletion. A compact
Leray-null aggregate has zero trace-free affine moment, and selected positive
patch service creates a selector force with an \(H^{-1}\) floor. Carrying that
force routes it to acceleration/frame, viscosity, nonlinear stress, or defect
currency; PSD defect carriage pays trace ballast, and admitted terminal ballast
is Field-visible. But the source still says it proves no uniform conversion of
all currencies into one confinement constant, no bath construction/exclusion,
and no confinement closure.

Thus the signed/null-stress branch is typed and narrowed, not deleted into
W8+W9.

## 6. Verdict

No new wall is admitted. The exact post-field-view W7 deletion criterion is:
\[
\begin{gathered}
\text{every Reynolds defect from retained graph failure}\\
\Longrightarrow
\text{vanishes, is pressure-service compatible, is W8-collected,}\\
\text{is W9-legally carried in a closed zero-surplus path, or routes out}.
\end{gathered}
\]

Current sources prove several branch reductions inside this criterion. They do
not prove the universal implication. W7 remains open.

Field, stop, legal, selector, or pressure-service route-out. Otherwise accept no
deletion.
