## Countermodel

The route-measure shortcut sees the same half-tail obstruction. Take fresh
orthogonal parent directions with
\[
  \omega_\ell=2^\ell,\qquad
  \Delta_\ell={2^{-\ell}\over \ell+1}.
\]

The raw/square route information is finite:
\[
  \sum_{\ell\ge0}\omega_\ell|\Delta_\ell|^2
  =
  \sum_{\ell\ge0}{2^{-\ell}\over(\ell+1)^2}
  <\infty .
\]

The selected first-ratio service diverges:
\[
  \sum_{\ell\ge0}\omega_\ell|\Delta_\ell|

\]

Every pulse may be same-parent and first-admitted, and the square route measure
may be Carleson, while the selected-linear bill remains infinite. A Hardy or
Carleson statement that controls only the square route measure therefore has
not proved W8.
