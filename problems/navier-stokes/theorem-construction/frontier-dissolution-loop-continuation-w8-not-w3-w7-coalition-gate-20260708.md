# W8 Gate Against W3 + W7

reduce to W3+W7, because W3 is payment-to-cubic-service persistence after a
strict row is admitted and W7 is retained graph/admission passage through a
critical limit, while W8 is the original-data selected-linear first-admission
payment on the order-locked full-packet carrier before positive readout.

Physical sentence: the same parent packet may keep its cubic service from
silently disappearing and may keep its retained pressure/VPI graph rows lawful,
while still lacking the parent-weighted selected first-admission pulse measure
that prices the fresh positive heat-lag read before the selected child readout.

## Source Check

The W3 gate proves only a conditional Door-1 fact:

```math
\text{admitted retained local strain }H^1\text{ payment}
\Longrightarrow
\text{strong }L^3\text{ persistence of selected cubic service}.
```

It does not construct an original-data selected first-ratio bill. It acts after
the relevant same-field payment row has been admitted.

The W7 gate proves only the retained-record custody fact to be supplied:
pressure source, pressure row, material-frame rows, material-time rows, and the
positive VPI record must pass through the critical graph limit, or the lost row
must be paid, parent/source-owned, or routed out. It keeps record rows lawful;
it does not make the selected-linear pulse measure finite.

The W8 sources state a different producer. WLF.60 / ODP.91 is the finite
parent-weighted selected first-admission pulse measure on the order-locked
full-packet carrier, with same-packet order-lock overlap and selected-overrun
tail payment. The source explicitly says that record-admission and
no-detachment topology preserve the same signed-height edge in compactness;
they do not pay the early branch. The remaining producer is the selected-linear
route-measure / persistent-affine-quotient original-data payment before
positive readout.

The attackable separation is the same half-tail obstruction, now read against
the W3+W7 coalition:

```math
\nu_\ell={2^{-\ell}\over \ell+1},
\qquad
\sum_\ell \nu_\ell < \infty,
\qquad
\sum_\ell 2^\ell \nu_\ell=\infty .
```

This is a formal implication witness, not an actual Navier-Stokes history. It
shows the row-level source gap: raw or square support can be finite, retained
rows can be closed, and service-persistence can be trivial or already admitted,
while the selected-linear first-ratio bill still diverges. To defeat the
certificate, the adversary must supply the exact law that converts W3
service-persistence plus W7 retained graph/admission into WLF.60 / ODP.91
selected-linear bill currency from original history.

producer exists; they do not construct it.

selected-linear WLF.60 / ODP.91 original-data payment on the actual
order-locked full-packet carrier, equivalently the stopped affine-transition /
