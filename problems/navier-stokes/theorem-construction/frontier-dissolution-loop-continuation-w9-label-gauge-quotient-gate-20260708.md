# W9 label-gauge quotient gate

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9.

Cross-wall identification attempted first: W9 is not identified with W7 by
quotienting material labels as gauge. W7 asks for static retained graph
passage/admission through a critical limit. W9 asks for a compact
material-time path topology whose label motions either have a uniform modulus
or are paid/legally routed.

Physical sentence: recentering, rescaling, and chart names are coordinates, but
the selected service can still move through material center, envelope, time,
scale, bank, weight, rung, or profile selector in a way that changes which
same-fluid record is being read and which bill is being charged.

## Gauge shortcut

The tempting deletion is:
\[
\text{moving W9 labels are gauge}
\Longrightarrow
\text{quotient them out}
\Longrightarrow
\text{W9 reduces to W7 pointwise graph passage.}
\tag{LGQ.1}
\]

This is not source-backed. The W9 source can quotient only legal material-time
shifts and legally carried defects. It cannot erase a coordinate that changes
the active service readout, payer address, carrier cover, pressure-law
certificate, material ancestry, or tower certificate.

## Source check

The WKB service-modulus source defines the missing row as a uniform modulus for
the active pressure-service readouts \(F_j\) on the admitted compact WKB
phase-label space. Failure means the service readout resolves shrinking label
distinctions:
\[
  d_X(x_j,y_j)\to0,
  \qquad
  |F_j(x_j)-F_j(y_j)|\ge\varepsilon_0 .
\tag{LGQ.2}
\]

The source then names the only places those distinctions can live: material
path or center, envelope/collar/gauge, time window or refresh label, relative
scale or rung, bank coordinate, weight/population assignment, and
profile-level selector topology. It explicitly says failure of the modulus is
moving service functional escape, not admitted retained service.

The legal-label compactness source gives the same warning at the certificate
level. Dropping a certificate coordinate can make legal prelimit labels converge
to an illegal limit while the observable label stays stable. The first lost
certificate is Pack-side cover failure, Part-side pressure-law/ancestry/tower
failure, or Field-side consumer material after legal tower admission. That is
not a gauge quotient.

Pressure gauge is also already typed. Reynolds/covariance ballast cannot be
erased as gauge unless pressure service remains compatible and the positive
trace energy is carried through cover, ancestry, and tower certificates or
routed out.

## Gate result

A gauge quotient can delete only a coordinate proved to be pure representation.
The checked W9 label coordinates are not pure representation in that sense.
They are the coordinates on which the active service functional, payer row,
same-solution certificate, and material-time shift action are read.

The lawful quotient statement is therefore conditional:
\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{every moving label is either harmless gauge, paid variation,}\\
&\text{or typed Pack/Part/Field/stop/legal/selector route-out}
\Longrightarrow
\text{no W9 label-modulus residue.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{LGQ.3}
\]

No checked source proves the premise. The current sources price bank/weight
variation in some filed ledgers and type certificate loss, but they do not
prove that all material, center, envelope, time, scale, bank, weight, rung, and
profile-selector motion is harmless gauge or paid/routed for arbitrary retained
Navier-Stokes windows.
