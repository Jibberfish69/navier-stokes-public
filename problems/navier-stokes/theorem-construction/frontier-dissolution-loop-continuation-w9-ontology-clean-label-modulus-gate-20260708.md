# W9 ontology-clean label-modulus gate

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9. Cross-wall identification attempted first:
the ontology audit makes W7 and W9 look like one root, because both police
faithful retained-record reading through a limit. The identification fails as
a paid move because W7 controls pointwise retained graph passage through a
critical compactness limit, while W9 controls a path-valued material-time
reading with a common topology, fixed-shift action, terminal-window room, and
uniform material/label modulus.

The earlier \(X_{\rm rows}\times S^1\) certificate is too loose
ontologically. A free \(S^1\) direction not read from the same velocity history
cannot be treated as a Navier-Stokes obstruction. It is only a formal warning
that row values alone do not define a path action.

The lawful version of the same obstruction is already in the W9 sources. The
material-time shift-hull note says the service row needs a uniform
material/label modulus, and that failure of the modulus is moving selector,
center, envelope, time, scale, bank, weight, rung, or profile-admission escape.
The WKB service-modulus note states the same fork: after domain, label, rung,
bank, and angular coordinates are fixed, Arzela-Ascoli passes the active
service readout only from a uniform modulus; failure is moving service
functional escape in one of the admitted label coordinates.

Thus the repaired certificate is not an arbitrary extra metric direction. It
is the absence of a common modulus for admitted certificate rows. A family may
have all pointwise W7 graph rows legal and the W3/W8 visible service/payer rows
fixed, while the selected material label, center, time window, scale, bank,
weight, rung, or profile-admission coordinate moves too fast to produce a
compact \(C_{\rm loc}\) material-time shift hull. W7 can say each snapshot is a
lawful retained graph reading; it does not supply the path regularity that lets
those snapshots form one compact material-time action.

Attackable certificate: replace the free \(S^1\) in the old model by an
admitted W9 label coordinate \(\lambda\) from the compact WKB phase-label
space. Let the pressure/source/material rows be pointwise legal and let the
service/payer rows remain fixed, but let the active label readout move as
\(\lambda_n(t)=nt\) modulo the compact label coordinate. Then the W7-visible
graph rows can be legal at each time and the W3/W8 rows can remain fixed, while
the family lacks the uniform material/label modulus needed for W9 compact
shift-hull/action. To refute the certificate, an adversary must cite a checked
source where W3+W7+W8 supply that modulus or where every such moving label is
already paid or routed out.

This is a narrowing of W9, not a deletion. The remaining W9 sentence is now:
construct a compact material-time certificate-path topology whose metric is
generated by lawful retained rows and whose admitted label motions have a
uniform modulus, or prove that failure of that modulus is paid or routed out.
