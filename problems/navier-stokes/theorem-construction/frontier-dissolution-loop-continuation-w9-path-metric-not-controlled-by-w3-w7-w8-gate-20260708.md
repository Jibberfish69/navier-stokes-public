# W9 path metric is not controlled by W3+W7+W8

Frontier opened: W3, W7, W8, W9.

Cross-wall failure sentence: W9 is not identified with W3+W7+W8, because W3
controls the admitted service-persistence row, W7 controls retained graph
passage through a critical limit, and W8 controls selected-linear payment,
while W9 requires a common compact certificate-path topology whose metric is
stable under fixed material-time shifts.

The W9 source assumes certified paths \(Z_j:I_j\to X\), compactness of their
shifted hull in \(C_{\rm loc}\) or an equivalent weak certificate-path topology,
and continuity of fixed shifts. The B3 audit states the same row as an
uninstalled material-time action on the lifted certified profile family,
preserving datum, pressure law, ancestry, cover, tower, selector/label
tightness, service readout, and zero-payer clauses.

W3, W7, and W8 do not define that path metric. W3 supplies the conditional
Door-1 service-persistence coordinate once the localized H1/enstrophy row is
admitted as strict retained tower payment. W7 supplies critical graph passage
or typed route-out for the retained pressure/material-frame/VPI rows. W8
supplies the selected-linear WLF.60 / ODP.91 payment coordinate. None of these
checked sources says that the W9 certificate metric is generated by those rows,
or that every uncharged metric direction routes out.

Attackable certificate: let the abstract certificate state space be
\[
  X=X_{\rm rows}\times S^1.
\]
Let every W3, W7, and W8 row depend only on \(x_0\in X_{\rm rows}\), with zero
payer and closed pointwise retained rows, and set
\[
  Z_n(t)=(x_0,e^{int}).
\]
Then the W3 service row is constant, the W7 retained graph row is pointwise
closed, and the W8 selected-linear payer can be zero. But the \(S^1\) coordinate
is part of the W9 path metric, and the family has no compact \(C_{\rm loc}\)
shift hull or continuous material-time action without a uniform modulus,
canonical path-germ coordinate, or typed route-out for that uncharged motion.

This is not a new wall. It is the current attack surface of W9 in exact form:
prove that the W9 certificate-path metric is coercively generated by the W3
service row, W7 retained graph rows, W8 payer/legal rows, and their admitted
label/modulus charges; or prove that any remaining uncharged path direction is
a legal stop/selector/Pack/Part/Field route-out. Without that exact fact,
W3+W7+W8 do not delete W9.
