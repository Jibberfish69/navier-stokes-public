# Cycle 11 addendum: zero-surplus graph is not consumed by W7

Claude candidate: the second W9 input, `ZeroSurplusCertificateClosedGraph.A`,
is a consumer of W7 because the certificate graph is the retained record row
graph read as a legal document. If this held, W9 would narrow to
`CertifiedMaterialTimeShiftHullCompactness.A`.

## Gate

The candidate fails as a deletion or narrowing certificate.

W7 supplies retained VPI record graph passage/admission or typed route-out:
pressure source, pressure row, material-frame rows, material-time rows, and
positive retained VPI service must pass the critical limit, or the lost row is
paid, parent/source-owned, Field/nonparticipating, or routed through the
Pack/Part/Field face.

`ZeroSurplusCertificateClosedGraph.A` has row overlap with W7, but it carries
certificate-specific clauses that W7 does not state:

1. every same-packet surplus payer is represented as a nonnegative finite
   measure or lower-semicontinuous nonnegative defect coordinate;
2. the zero condition is imposed on the carried surplus defect
   `[payer bill_i - admissible marginal production_i]`, not merely on an
   observed projected readout;
3. terminal-window no-escape keeps the payer domain compact;
4. the certificate topology closes the payer/lower-semicontinuity coordinates
   together with the pressure-law, ancestry, cover, tower, and service rows.

W7 can carry the retained record rows through a critical compactness limit, but
it does not prove payer-measure lift, payer lower semicontinuity, or
terminal-window no-escape. Those are exactly the remaining source rows in the
zero-surplus closed graph note.

## Verdict

The candidate "W9's zero-surplus closed graph is consumed by W7" is refuted.
There is real overlap in the pressure/service/row graph clauses, but overlap is
not a paid deletion. W9 still carries `ZeroSurplusCertificateClosedGraph.A` plus
`CertifiedMaterialTimeShiftHullCompactness.A`.

DELETED: none.
CERTIFICATE: W7 does not consume `ZeroSurplusCertificateClosedGraph.A` because W7 lacks same-packet surplus-payer measure lift, lower semicontinuity, carried nonnegative surplus-defect coordinates, and terminal-window no-escape.
