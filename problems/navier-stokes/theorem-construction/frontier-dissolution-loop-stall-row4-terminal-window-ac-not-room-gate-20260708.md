## Gate result

Row 4 is accepted only as an actual-history observation: a positive smooth record-work integral cannot occupy an interval of literally zero material time on that one history.

It is refuted as a W9 deletion. Uniform terminal-window room would require an adversary-attackable theorem saying that the retained family has a common compact material-time window and a positive interior-room margin for the shifts under test. Fixed-history absolute continuity does not give that uniform margin; positive work may concentrate into intervals whose lengths tend to zero across a retained sequence. The temporal-thin source then says the concentration becomes an atom only after payer admission on a compact window, while absence of that compact window remains the terminal-window/no-escape row.
