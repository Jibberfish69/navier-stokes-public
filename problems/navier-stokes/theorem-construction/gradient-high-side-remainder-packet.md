# Gradient High-Side Remainder Packet

## Object

`gradient_high_side_remainder_packet`

## Definition

Let `u = u(t,x)` be a classical Euclidean solution and fix `N`. After extracting the strict low-mode low-high and high-low paraproduct pieces with `S_{N-4}u`, define the gradient-side high packet by

```math
\mathfrak{H}^{grad}_{N}[u](t)
:=
\sum_{j\ge N}2^{2j}|\mathcal{C}_j(t)|,
```

where `\mathcal{C}_j` bundles the lifted high-side remainder together with the
genuine high-high commutator term after the threshold-local spill collar has
been separated.

After the threshold-local spill collar is removed, the lifted remainder is the
part of the dyadic enstrophy identity coming from

```math
(S_{j-4}-S_{N+M})u,
```

namely the terms in which the transport coefficient remains below `j` but no
longer lies in the fixed threshold collar.

The gradient bridge is discharged once one proves

```math
\mathfrak{H}^{grad}_{N}[u](t)
\le
\eta\nu D_N(t) + C_*2^{-2\delta N}
```

for almost every `t`, on the same classical surface and with constants independent of the approximation index.

## Honest Boundary

This object is now a reduction surface. After the threshold-local spill collar
is isolated, the remaining live gradient-side burden splits into:

- the lifted high-side remainder created by `(S_{j-4}-S_{N+M})u`;
- the genuine high-high commutator packet tracked in
  `gradient-genuine-high-high-packet.md`.

The genuine high-high component is reduced to a cubic tail. The lifted
high-side remainder remains a separate open packet.

## Why The Lifted Remainder Stays Open

The lifted remainder is still a high-low / low-high paraproduct remainder, not
a genuine near-diagonal high-high packet. Its dyadic coefficient may lie on any
shell

```math
N+M < k < j-4,
```

so the output shell `j` can survive even when `k` is far below `j`. Standard
Littlewood-Paley support geometry therefore does **not** force this packet into
the finite near-diagonal collar used for `\mathcal{C}^{HH}_j`.

That is why the genuine high-high reduction and cubic-tail absorption do not by
themselves settle the full repaired high-side packet on the gradient side.

In particular, the lifted remainder is **not** the packet with one shell far
above the output in the sense `k\ge j+M`. The repaired split comes from

```math
(S_{j-4}-S_{N+M})u,
```

so the live packet geometry is "intermediate transport shell below `j` but
above the fixed collar", namely `N+M<k<j-4`.

## Exact Frontier Question

The live gradient-side frontier is now:

1. prove a theorem-grade bound for the threshold-local spill collar;
2. prove a theorem-grade bound for the lifted high-side remainder;
3. propagate the already explicit genuine high-high reduction and cubic-tail
   absorption through the proposition-level Route B packet.

The cleanest unresolved question is whether the lifted remainder still reduces
to the admissible proposition-level coefficient surface
`\widetilde\Lambda_N^\sharp(t)E_N(t)` plus a harmless fraction of `D_N(t)`, or
whether the packet forces a genuinely new theorem not presently available on
the Euclidean route.

That theorem should be understood first as a statement about the coupled
lifted-band commutator functional

```math
\mathcal J_N^{lift}(t)
:=
\sum_{j\ge N}
2^{2j}
\left\langle
[\Delta_j,(S_{j-4}-S_{N+M})u(t)\cdot\nabla]\Delta_j u(t),
\Delta_j u(t)
\right\rangle,
```

not as a theorem about independently controlled shell coefficients.

The first rigorous coupled reduction is now recorded in
`lifted-band-coarse-grained-stress-strain-flux-inequality.md`: the commutator
functional above is bounded by one mesoscopic stress-strain flux functional
`\Pi_N^{lift}(t)` built from coarse strain in the lifted band and an `H^1`-level
band stress density. So the theorem-facing burden is now a barrier-scale bound
for that coupled flux object, not a theorem about shellwise carriers.

The note `lifted-band-descent-coboundary-l-flux-theorem.md` sharpens that
abstractly: `(L-Flux)` would follow once the lifted mesoscopic strain channel
is exact in the combined space-time-scale direction modulo barrier tail, and
the resulting scale/time boundary terms together with the dual pairings against
`\tau_\ell^{H^1}` satisfy the same barrier remainder bound.

The new note `signed-lifted-band-scale-descent-identity.md` records the
correct proof order for that claim: the exactness theorem has to be proved at
the signed continuous-scale lifted commutator density, where the bulk lifted
interaction is a logarithmic scale derivative and the derivative of the drift
falls only on the threshold-local collar. The positive coupled flux theorem is
downstream of that signed telescoping step, not upstream.

The later `SDS^\sharp`, `UTKD^\sharp`, paired-carrier, and other weighted-tail
reformulations remain searchable side attempts after flattening that coupled
object into positive shellwise carriers, but they are not the theorem-primary
frontier here. The live four-body burden is still the direct proposition-level
one: control the lifted remainder on the exact geometry `N+M<k<j-4`, then
thread the already-local genuine high-high packet through Proposition `4.1`,
Proposition `6.1^\sharp`, the explicit `4 -> 1` return arrow, and `C3`.

## Diagnostic Naive Bound

For the lifted gradient-side low-high contribution, one immediately obtains the
diagnostic estimate

```math
2^{2j}\big|\mathcal{C}^{lift}_j(t)\big|
\le C_{\mathrm{lift}}
\Big(\sum_{N+M<k<j-4}2^{3k/2}\|\Delta_k u(t)\|_{L^2_x}\Big)
2^{4j}\|\Delta_j u(t)\|_{L^2_x}^2.
```

Again the coefficient sum ranges with `j`, so it is not controlled by the
fixed-cutoff low-mode quantity `\mathcal{L}_N(t)`. A naive Cauchy-Schwarz
estimate then produces only a supercritical bound

```math
\sum_{j\ge N}2^{2j}\big|\mathcal{C}^{lift}_j(t)\big|
\le C_{\mathrm{naive}}
2^{-N/2} D_N(t)^{3/2},
```

which is not the desired theorem-grade form
`\mathcal{L}_N(t)E_N(t) + \varepsilon \nu D_N(t)`.

So the lifted remainder is a real mathematical frontier, not just a missing
line-by-line writeup.

## Why The Pointwise Tail-Smallness Shortcut Fails

A tempting shortcut is to estimate the lifted remainder by

```math
\|u_{\ge N+M}(t)\|_{L^2_x}\,E_N(t)^{1/2}D_N(t)^{1/2}
```

and then invoke the scale barrier to declare `\|u_{\ge N+M}(t)\|_{L^2_x}` small.
That is not theorem-grade on the current surface.

The available scale barrier is the integrated tail estimate

```math
\int_0^T \|P_{\ge N}u(t)\|_{L^2_x}^2\,dt
\le
C_*2^{-2\delta N},
```

not a pointwise-in-time smallness theorem for `\|u_{\ge N+M}(t)\|_{L^2_x}`.
So the step

```math
\|u_{\ge N+M}(t)\|_{L^2_x}\ll 1
\quad\text{for each }t
```

is unavailable unless one proves a new pointwise tail theorem. Without that
extra input, the lifted remainder does not collapse to

```math
\varepsilon \nu D_N(t)+C_*2^{-2\delta N}
```

by the present scale-barrier machinery alone.

## Why The Integrated-Time Shortcut Is Still Not A Theorem

Another tempting repair is to try to prove only the integrated statement

```math
\int_0^T |\mathfrak H_N^{grad,lift}[u](t)|\,dt
\le
\varepsilon \nu \int_0^T D_N(t)\,dt + C_*2^{-2\delta N}.
```

On the present surface, that is still not theorem-grade.

There are two separate issues.

First, the exact lifted packet geometry above involves the shells
`N+M<k<j-4`, not a generic factor `u_{\ge N+M}` with one frequency far above
the output. So any integrated proof has to start from that exact support
description and show how the coefficient sum over `N+M<k<j-4` is converted into
an admissible time-integrable factor.

Second, the usual integrated shortcut becomes circular if it argues through the
candidate reduced estimate

```math
\int_0^T |\mathfrak H_N^{grad,lift}[u](t)|\,dt
\le C_{\mathrm{red}}
\int_0^T \|u_{\ge N+M}(t)\|_{L^2_x}\,E_N(t)^{1/2}D_N(t)^{1/2}\,dt
```

and then bounds the resulting term by using an already-established
`\sup_{t<T}E_N(t)`. That `E_N` control is itself the conclusion of the upgraded
Proposition `6.1`, so it cannot be imported into the lifted-remainder lemma
without reopening the bridge circularly.

Therefore the lifted high-side remainder remains open not only at the pointwise
packet level, but also at the integrated theorem-interface level presently
supported by the lane.

Moreover, the candidate reduced form

```math
\|u_{\ge N+M}(t)\|_{L^2_x}\,E_N(t)^{1/2}D_N(t)^{1/2}
```

is not yet itself derived from the exact shell algebra currently recorded in
this lane. The present shell estimate still carries the `j`-dependent
coefficient sum over `N+M<k<j-4`, and no theorem-grade collapse of that sum to
the displayed `L^2` high-tail factor has yet been written.
