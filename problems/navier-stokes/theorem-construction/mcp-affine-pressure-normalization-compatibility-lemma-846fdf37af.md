# Affine/Pressure Normalization Compatibility Lemma

Status: conditional local compatibility theorem.

## Purpose

This note fixes the pressure normalization used by `SCF_base` so that it is compatible with both:

```math
SCF_{base}\le\varepsilon_m\Longrightarrow ATD_m^\varepsilon
```

and the later pointwise pressure readout in

```math
DTC.Read.Press.local.
```

## Statement

On a bounded-distortion same-fluid moving cylinder, write the moving-frame variables in normalized coordinates. The pressure used in `SCF_base` is

```math
p^{norm}=p-c(t)-p^{frame}_{aff}(x,t),
```

where `c(t)` is an arbitrary time-only pressure gauge, `\nabla_x c(t)=0`, and `p^{frame}_{aff}` is the affine potential generated by the chosen translating/accelerating frame. This is exactly the pressure term appearing in the transformed local energy inequality.

Then:

```math
SCF_{base}(Q_r^\Phi)
```

is a CKN-compatible scale-critical quantity, and the later fixed-cover local Poisson readout recovers the original pressure derivatives used in

```math
K_q=-\nabla^{q+1}p+\nu\Delta U_q.
```

## Proof

The time-dependent gauge `c(t)` leaves `\nabla p`, the Navier-Stokes equations, and the local energy inequality unchanged because it has no spatial gradient. The CKN pressure slot is the gauge-invariant quantity obtained after choosing that time-only function.

The moving same-fluid frame introduces inertial affine terms in the transformed equation. These terms are represented by an affine pressure potential `p^{frame}_{aff}` whose gradient is exactly the frame acceleration contribution. After this transformation, the normalized velocity and pressure solve the Navier-Stokes system in the normalized frame with bounded lower-order coefficients controlled by the chart distortion constants.

The `SCF_base` pressure slot is the affine-frame pressure contribution

```math
P_r^{aff}
:=
r^{-2}\iint_{Q_r^\Phi}|p-c(t)-p^{frame}_{aff}|^{3/2}.
```

After the bounded-distortion pullback this is comparable, with constants depending only on the frame bounds, to the normalized unit-cylinder CKN pressure term

```math
\iint_{Q_1}|\tilde p-\tilde p^{norm}_{1}|^{3/2}.
```

For endpoint readout, return to the original pressure `p`. On each fixed readout cover element, use

```math
p=p_i^{loc}+h_i,
\qquad
-\Delta p_i^{loc}=\partial_a\partial_b(\chi_i u_a u_b).
```

The CKN-normalized pressure was used only to obtain local regularity through `ATD_m^\varepsilon`. Once local derivative bounds are available, the fixed-cover Poisson split reads the original pressure derivatives. The allowed gauge `c(t)` has zero spatial derivatives, and the frame-affine term is a known smooth affine potential whose derivatives are controlled by the bounded frame coefficients. Thus all derivatives entering `K_q` are recovered by the `DTC.Read.Press.local` estimate together with the frame-ledger terms already present in the moving-coordinate packet.

## Boundary

This lemma uses gauge and frame normalization only. It excludes amplitude-rescaling readout normalizations of selected coherent modes. The obstruction is the recorded fact that exact readout preservation and fixed-shell amplitude damping are incompatible without an independent amplitude bound.

Therefore the route uses:

```math
\text{CKN pressure gauge + moving-frame affine potential}
```

and routes all pointwise pressure derivatives through the later fixed-cover Poisson readout.
