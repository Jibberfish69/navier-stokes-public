# BirthPackingMapHardObstruction.A

Status: hard mathematical obstruction for the present installed inputs.

The proposed growth-birth ledger would close the route if every one-way material-record growth atom admitted an injective charge into a finite native reserve from the original smooth packet.  That required map is

```math
\iota:\Gamma_N\to\mathcal R_N(u_0),
\qquad
\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma_N}\omega_\gamma
\le C\sum_{\rho\in\mathcal R_N(u_0)}\rho+R_N^{legal}.
```

The current installed inputs do not prove this map.  Smooth initial data gives finite fixed-depth entrance records and finite initial frequency tails, but the present surfaces do not turn those entrance quantities into a finite native reserve assignment for every later nonlinear pressure/RHS, transported-collar, or annular stress-work superheat birth.

The failed candidate storages are also not usable.  Negative material-record storage is bounded below only after the record is already bounded.  Future action assumes the desired clock.  Raw initial frequency tails pay entrance and linear heat pieces but do not pay later nonlinear pressure/collar/stress births.  Placing those births inside legal residuals would move the live clock into the residual.

Therefore the solution shape is the birth-injection ledger, but the actual theorem needed is the native birth-charge packing theorem above.  It is not discharged here.
