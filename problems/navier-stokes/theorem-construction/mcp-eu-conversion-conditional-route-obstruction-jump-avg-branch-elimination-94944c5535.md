# E_u conversion: conditional route and obstruction

## Target

Close the E_u escape channel in the Jumpavg no-escape route.

## Conditional positive route

The moving-frame local energy inequality is available on pack-stabilized same-fluid cylinders. Its frame terms are controlled by affine-relative velocity, pressure components, and scale-normalized frame ledgers.

Under BKM or an equivalent high-regularity terminal ledger, the needed velocity-gradient and local-pressure moduli are available. In that conditional setting, terminal E_u threshold packets cannot persist as uncontrolled moving packets.

Thus:

```math
BKM/high regularity => SCFBase.Modulus => no E_u escape channel.
```

## Original-data obstruction

The live route needs original-data discharge. The local energy inequality supplies balances, but it does not prove:

```math
E_u terminal threshold => D_u/P terminal mass
```

on the same terminal schedule.

A Poincare conversion requires a zero-mode-free or affine-relative E_u
normalization strong enough to remove local mean or affine energy. That theorem
is not installed as an unconditional component-selection rule.

Adding E_u to the charge also remains unavailable, because no additive finite carrier for repeated scale-critical E_u terminal packets is installed.

## Verdict

The E_u channel is conditionally controlled under BKM/high regularity and remains open on the original-data route.

## Required theorem

A future discharge needs either:

```math
E_u terminal threshold => D_u/P terminal mass,
```

or an additive E_u carrier with bounded-overlap upper summability.
