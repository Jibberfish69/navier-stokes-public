## 1. One-row normalization

Let a smooth finite-energy Euler history on R^3 satisfy

\[
\partial_tu+\mathbb P(u\cdot\nabla u)=0,
\qquad \nabla\cdot u=0,
\]

and fix a finite continuation-grade height \(m>5/2\) (for concreteness \(m=3\)). Put

\[
N_0=\|u_0\|_2=\|u(t)\|_2,
\qquad
N_m(t)=\|\Lambda^m u(t)\|_2,
\qquad
K_m(t)=\left(\frac{N_m(t)}{N_0}\right)^{1/m}.
\]

Set \(L=K_m^{-1}\), \(c=L^{3/2}/N_0\),

\[
U(y,\tau)=c(t)u(L(t)y,t),
\qquad
\frac{d\tau}{dt}=\frac1{cL}=N_0K_m^{5/2}.
\]

Then

\[
\|U\|_2=1,
\qquad
\|\Lambda^mU\|_2=1.
\]

With

\[
D=y\cdot\nabla+\frac32,
\qquad
b=(\log K_m)_\tau,
\]

the normalized equation is exactly

\[
\boxed{U_\tau+\mathbb P(U\cdot\nabla U)+bDU=0.}
\tag{EHD.1}
\]

Since \(\langle \Lambda^mDU,\Lambda^mU\rangle=m\|\Lambda^mU\|_2^2=m\), the fixed \(\dot H^m\) norm gives

\[
\boxed{
b(U)=-\frac1m\left\langle\Lambda^m\mathbb P(U\cdot\nabla U),\Lambda^mU\right\rangle.}
\tag{EHD.2}
\]

Thus all radial Sobolev growth of the physical row is represented by the single pressure-complete dilation scalar \(b\).

## 2. Exact physical clock

Let

\[
B(\tau)=\int_0^\tau b(s)\,ds
=\log\frac{K_m(\tau)}{K_m(0)}.
\]

Then

\[
K_m(\tau)=K_m(0)e^{B(\tau)}
\]

and the physical clock is

\[
\boxed{
t(\tau)-t(0)=\frac1{N_0K_m(0)^{5/2}}
\int_0^\tau e^{-\frac52B(s)}\,ds.}
\tag{EHD.3}
\]

Therefore a finite physical terminal time driven by this row requires

\[
\boxed{B(\tau)\to+\infty,
\qquad
\int_0^\infty e^{-\frac52B(s)}\,ds<\infty.}
\tag{EHD.4}
\]

Conversely, divergence of the integral in (EHD.3) excludes a finite terminal face at height \(m\).

## 3. Exact normalized dilation response

Define

\[
\mathcal N(U)=\mathbb P(U\cdot\nabla U)
\]

and regard \(b=\mathcal R_m(U)\), where

\[
\mathcal R_m(U)=
-\frac{\langle\Lambda^m\mathcal N(U),\Lambda^mU\rangle}
{m\|\Lambda^mU\|_2^2}.
\]

Under the L2-preserving scaling generated by \(D\), \(\mathcal R_m\) has homogeneity \(5/2\):

\[
D\mathcal R_m(U)[DU]=\frac52\mathcal R_m(U).
\]

Differentiating along (EHD.1) gives the exact Riccati-shape law

\[
\boxed{
b_\tau+\frac52b^2=\mathcal Q_m[U],
\qquad
\mathcal Q_m[U]:=-D\mathcal R_m(U)[\mathcal N(U)].}
\tag{EHD.5}
\]

The universal \(-\frac52b^2\) term is the automatic scale turn-down. All possible sustained positive dilation is therefore carried by the pressure-complete quartic shape forcing \(\mathcal Q_m[U]\).

## 4. Pressure-complete same-depth geometry

\[
D_tA+A^2+\nabla^2p=0,

\[

## 5. The failed unconditional closure

To close global regularity from (EHD.3), it would suffice to prove, for example,

\[
B(\tau)\le\frac25\log(1+\tau)+C(u_0),
\tag{EHD.9}
\]

or directly

\[
\int_0^\infty e^{-\frac52B(s)}ds=\infty.
\tag{EHD.10}
\]

Equations (EHD.5)--(EHD.8) do not presently imply either statement. The exact obstruction is a noncompact multiscale shape escape on the normalized manifold.

Indeed, let \(\phi_r(x)=r^{3/2}\phi(rx)\) be divergence-free L2-normalized packets with separated Fourier supports, and choose low and high scales \(\varepsilon\downarrow0\), \(R\uparrow\infty\). For coefficients chosen so that

\[
U_{\varepsilon,R}
=\sqrt{1-\delta^2}\,\phi_\varepsilon+\delta\phi_R,
\qquad
\|U_{\varepsilon,R}\|_2=1,
\qquad
\|\Lambda^mU_{\varepsilon,R}\|_2=1,
\]

one has

\[
\delta^2
=\frac{1-\varepsilon^{2m}}
{R^{2m}-\varepsilon^{2m}}
\sim R^{-2m}.
\tag{EHD.11}
\]

Thus conserved L2 mass may live at arbitrarily low frequency while a vanishing L2 fraction at arbitrarily high frequency carries the fixed Hm norm. The two normalization constraints do not compactify the shape. For separated packets, the self-interaction contribution of the high packet to the normalized m-row current scales like

\[
\delta^3R^{2m+5/2}\sim R^{5/2-m},
\tag{EHD.12}
\]

which tends to zero for every \(m>5/2\); the low packet current also tends to zero, and cross-interactions can be made small by separation. Hence pressure completeness, energy/helicity tangency, and the fixed normalized row do not supply a coercive lower or upper sign preventing escape toward this multiscale boundary.

This is a structural countertest to an algebraic closure from scaling freedom alone. It is not an Euler blowup solution.

## 6. Exact surviving theorem target

A finite-time smooth whole-space Euler singularity, expressed in the one-row normalization, must therefore use a genuinely dynamical version of the multiscale escape above: cumulative positive dilation must be generated while the normalized shape continually reorganizes across scales so that no stationary or compact pressure-complete profile carries the entire event.

The remaining theorem is the history-level statement

\[
\boxed{
\text{Every smooth normalized Euler orbit satisfying (EHD.1)--(EHD.2) obeys (EHD.10).}}
\tag{EHD.13}
\]

Equivalently, one may prove a pressure-complete no-self-birth/no-recount estimate on the quartic forcing in (EHD.5) strong enough to prevent super-logarithmic cumulative dilation.
