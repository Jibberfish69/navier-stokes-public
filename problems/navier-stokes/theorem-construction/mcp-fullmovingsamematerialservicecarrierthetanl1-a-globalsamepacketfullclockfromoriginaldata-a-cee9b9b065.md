# Full moving same-material service carrier Theta_N L1 attempt

Status: failed unconditional discharge; exact finite-total estimate isolated.

## Target

Prove, for the original smooth Navier-Stokes material history up to a finite candidate endpoint T*,

```math
\int_0^{T^*}\Theta_N(t)\,dt<\infty .
```

Here `Theta_N` is the full moving same-material service carrier for the continuation-depth material record. It contains the positive moving participation-law-strain service on the original material history: pressure/RHS, coefficient/frame, transported collar, Hodge interface, viscosity, incompressibility, and velocity remain one coupled packet. It is stronger than a fixed finite collar coefficient and stronger than raw viscous stress power.

## Known installed inputs used

1. Fixed-threshold finite-collar service is L1 from energy:

```math
\Theta_N^\sharp(t)=\sum_{m=N-M}^{N+M}2^{3m}\|\Delta_m u(t)\|_2^2
\le C_{N,M}\|u(t)\|_2^2,
```

hence `Theta_N^sharp in L^1(0,T*)`.

2. Raw internal annular stress power is finite:

```math
\int_0^{T^*}\int_{A(t)}T:S\,dxdt
=2\nu\int_0^{T^*}\int_{A(t)}|S|^2\,dxdt<\infty
```

for bounded-overlap fixed material annuli.

3. The annular stress-work derivative is a same-packet material expression. After material pullback, all time derivatives are generated by the material Navier-Stokes law, the coefficient equations, and the pressure elliptic equation.

4. The Hodge/Stokes material shell correction removes the fixed-shell pressure-force overcount: pressure is the constraint-normal force on the native material divergence-free shell, viscosity is coercive, and projector motion is governed by the same material coefficient/pressure operator.

5. Top positive strain/coefficient/base-collar service is paid by the installed top-strain logarithmic storage.

## Direct proof attempt

A proof would need a same-material coercive inequality of the form

```math
\Theta_N(t)\,dt
\le
C\,dR_N(t)+d\mathfrak L_N(t),
\qquad
\int_0^{T^*}dR_N<\infty,
\qquad
\mathfrak L_N(t)\ge -C_N(u_0).
```

Integrating would give

```math
\int_0^{T^*}\Theta_N(t)\,dt
\le
C\int_0^{T^*}dR_N+\mathfrak L_N(T^*)-\mathfrak L_N(0)<\infty .
```

This is the exact missing one-way storage/coercivity inequality.

## Why the available ledgers fail to prove it

The global energy inequality pays only unweighted viscous dissipation and raw fixed-annulus stress power. It permits heat-scale samples with raw physical cost of order `r` while the material deformation clock cost remains order one.

The fixed-collar estimate pays only a selected finite strip. The full `Theta_N` carrier follows the moving active shell and includes all-active pressure-stress/coefficient/collar service.

The material record inequality has the wrong direction for closure:

```math
d[\log(1+\mathcal P_N^{mat})]_+
\le C_N\Theta_N(t)dt+dR_N^0 .
```

This says the service clock controls material record growth. It does not give an upper bound on the service clock from original data.

The Hodge/Stokes shell route pays the constraint-normal pressure overcount and pays Hodge selector/top-strain motion. The later overclaim correction leaves the full participation-law-strain service as the same remaining moving interface, rather than producing a finite total clock.

The pressure elliptic operator route identifies the service as one material operator/RHS/collar variation:

```math
L_G=-\operatorname{div}_a(G\nabla_a),
\qquad
\dot G=-2ASA^T .
```

This gives same-packet generation of the clock. It gives no bounded-below one-way inequality for the positive rectified variation of that operator/RHS interface.

## Exact surviving obstruction

The proof reduces to the following missing theorem:

```math
\boxed{
\text{There exists a bounded-below same-material Lyapunov/coercivity storage}
\ \mathfrak L_N
\text{ whose decrease pays the positive moving participation-law-strain service.}
}
```

Equivalently:

```math
\boxed{
\int_0^{T^*}\Theta_N(t)dt<\infty
\Longleftrightarrow
\text{finite positive variation of the continuation-depth material record / full participation-law-strain interface.}
}
```

The current repo surfaces install same-fluid attachment, fixed-annulus L1 tails, fixed-collar L1 service, pressure constraint correction, Hodge shell correction, and top-strain/coefficient/base-collar storage. They do not install the finite-total estimate for the remaining moving participation-law-strain interface.
