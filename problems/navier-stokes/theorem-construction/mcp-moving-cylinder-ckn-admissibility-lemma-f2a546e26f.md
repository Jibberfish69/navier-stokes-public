# Moving-Cylinder CKN Admissibility Lemma

Status: conditional local admissibility theorem.

## Statement

Let `Q_r^Phi(a_0,t_0)` be a same-fluid moving cylinder generated by the flow map `Phi`. Assume the chart satisfies the bounded distortion condition

```math
\Gamma_\sharp(Q_r^\Phi)
=
\sup_{Q_r^\Phi}\max\{|D_a\Phi|,|D_a\Phi^{-1}|\}
\le M_\sharp,
```

and the moving frame coefficients used in `SCF_base` have the corresponding scale-normalized bounds on the cylinder.

Then, after rescaling by `r` and pulling back to material/moving coordinates, there are constants `0<c_M<=C_M<infty`, depending only on `M_sharp`, `nu`, and the declared normalized moving-frame bounds, such that the moving cylinder and the normalized unit cylinder have equivalent CKN scale-critical quantities. In particular, with

```math
A_r:=r^{-2}\iint_{Q_r^\Phi}|u-u^{norm}_{r}|^3,\qquad
P_r:=r^{-2}\iint_{Q_r^\Phi}|p-p^{norm}_{r}|^{3/2},
\qquad
D_r:=r^{-1}\iint_{Q_r^\Phi}|\nabla u|^2,
```

and corresponding pulled-back unit quantities `\widetilde A_1,\widetilde P_1,\widetilde D_1`,

```math
c_M(\widetilde A_1+\widetilde P_1+\widetilde D_1)
\le
A_r+P_r+D_r
\le
C_M(\widetilde A_1+\widetilde P_1+\widetilde D_1).
```

The local energy inequality also pulls back with lower-order moving-frame terms bounded by the same normalized `SCF_base` coefficients. Thus this lemma may invoke CKN regularity only under the explicit smallness condition `C_M SCF_base(Q_r^Phi)<=epsilon_CKN`.

## Proof

Define normalized coordinates

```math
x=\Phi(a_0,t)+rA(t)y,
\qquad
s=(t-t_0)/r^2,
```

where `A(t)` is the bounded same-fluid frame from the packet. The assumptions give uniform bounds for `A`, `A^{-1}`, the Jacobian, and the metric tensor in the pulled-back cylinder. Hence spatial volumes, gradients, and parabolic cylinders are comparable with constants depending only on `M_sharp`.

For any nonnegative smooth test function `phi(y,s)` compactly supported in the normalized cylinder, push it forward to the Eulerian cylinder as

```math
\varphi(x,t)=\phi(y(x,t),s(t)).
```

The chain rule writes `partial_t varphi`, `nabla varphi`, and `Delta varphi` as finite sums of normalized derivatives of `phi` multiplied by bounded frame coefficients and the expected powers of `r`. Inserting `varphi` into the Eulerian local energy inequality gives the same inequality in the normalized cylinder, with lower-order frame terms controlled by the bounded distortion constants. These terms are exactly the moving-frame contributions included in the normalized `SCF_base` convention.

The scale-critical quantities transform as

```math
c_M\iint_{Q_1}|\tilde u-\tilde u^{norm}_{1}|^3
\le
r^{-2}\iint_{Q_r^\Phi}|u-u^{norm}_{r}|^3
\le
C_M\iint_{Q_1}|\tilde u-\tilde u^{norm}_{1}|^3,
```

```math
c_M\iint_{Q_1}|\tilde p-\tilde p^{norm}_{1}|^{3/2}
\le
r^{-2}\iint_{Q_r^\Phi}|p-p^{norm}_{r}|^{3/2}
\le
C_M\iint_{Q_1}|\tilde p-\tilde p^{norm}_{1}|^{3/2},
```

and

```math
c_M\iint_{Q_1}|\nabla_y\tilde u|^2
\le
r^{-1}\iint_{Q_r^\Phi}|\nabla u|^2
\le
C_M\iint_{Q_1}|\nabla_y\tilde u|^2,
```

again with constants depending only on `M_sharp,nu`. Thus

```math
SCF_{base}(Q_r^\Phi)\le\varepsilon_m
```

implies the standard normalized CKN smallness after choosing `epsilon_m` below the CKN threshold divided by the comparability constant.

Therefore the moving same-fluid cylinder is CKN-admissible, and the local `ATD_m^epsilon` theorem may invoke CKN epsilon regularity on the normalized cylinder.

## Output

```math
\Gamma_\sharp\le M_\sharp
+SCF_{base}(Q_r^\Phi)\le\varepsilon_m
\Longrightarrow
\text{CKN-admissible normalized cylinder}.
```

Combined with the local CKN gain, this feeds

```math
ATD_m^\varepsilon.
```
