# Original-data E_u conversion or additive carrier — obstruction

## Target

Prove the original-data theorem needed to close the E_u escape channel:

```math
E_u terminal threshold => D_u/P terminal mass
```

or build an additive E_u carrier with finite bounded-overlap upper control.

## Attempt

The moving-frame local energy inequality supplies local balances on admissible same-fluid cylinders. In affine-relative coordinates, frame terms are controlled by the SCFBase packet and frame ledgers.

A Poincare route converts local energy to gradient mass only after the E_u
packet is zero-mode-free or affine-relative with the local mean and affine modes
removed.

## Obstruction

The current original-data surfaces do not install that normalization theorem. The local energy inequality gives balance information, while a terminal E_u packet can move through labels and scales without a same-schedule D_u/P lower bound.

The additive-carrier route also remains open. Global energy gives time-slice size control, while repeated scale-critical E_u terminal packets require a bounded-overlap summability theorem. No finite additive E_u carrier with that property is installed.

## Conditional comparison

BKM or equivalent high regularity supplies terminal gradient and pressure moduli, giving a conditional closure of the E_u channel. This does not discharge the original-data branch.

## Verdict

The original-data E_u conversion/additive-carrier theorem remains open.

## Required theorem

One of the following is needed:

```math
E_u terminal threshold => D_u/P terminal mass,
```

with zero-mode/affine normalization and Poincare control, or

```math
sum_k E_u(Q_k) <= C(E_0)
```

for the separated terminal windows used in the lower recurrence argument.
