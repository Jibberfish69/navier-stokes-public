## Audit question

Does the Pack(Q) non-exclusion / `Part_{N,Q}`-Field route create an illicit fourth primitive law, delete Pack_Q, or smuggle Pack_Q back into the proof while claiming to avoid it?

## Finding 1: No fourth primitive law is introduced

The canonicality surface says that, on stable carriers `(u,p,Phi,F)`, every lawful primitive source-level factorization is generated by:

```text
Pack,
Part,
Field.
```

The Part/Field-before-Pack route introduces no fourth source-level service. It refines Part and Field for terminal test used before Packing. The services remain:

```text
`Part_{N,Q}` = participation service applied to a terminal limiting witness,
`Field_{N,r,Q}` = one-field coherence service applied to a terminal limiting witness.
```

So the route is triad-generated.

## Finding 2: Pack_Q is preserved as an ordinary service

The route does not delete Pack_Q. It records that ordinary Pack_Q has strict positive-radius / finite-comparability content and therefore has a blind spot for terminal singular witnesses.

The route then handles Pack-illegal terminal witnesses through `Part_{N,Q}` / `Field_{N,r,Q}`, and only afterward recertifies Pack_Q as the first ordinary test for remaining class testing.

So Pack_Q is preserved. Its use is staged.

## Finding 3: Primitive minimality supports the move

The primitive-minimality surface says each law carries its own irreducible theorem burden:

```text
Pack carries the no-blown / carrier burden,
Part carries the participation burden,
Field carries the no-jump / one-field burden.
```

That directly supports `Part_{N,Q}` / `Field_{N,r,Q}` certification. Part and Field have independent audit meaning. They are not merely decorative after Pack.

## Finding 4: Clay Pack-face exclusion failure supports the pivot

The Pack-face exclusion attempt already failed for the same reason the user identified. Same-fluid ancestry and incompressible volume produce entry into the terminal test, while positive terminal carrier scale and finite deformation/readout comparability remain unproved.

Therefore using Pack_Q as the first final test repeats a known blind spot. The Part/Field-before-Pack route addresses exactly that issue.

## Finding 5: CMI Pack noncollapse attempt supports the pivot

The Pack-noncollapse parent attempt says the missing piece is positive terminal carrier scale plus finite deformation/readout comparability, or a replacement readout theorem.

The Part/Field-before-Pack route treats this as a structural warning: Pack noncollapse is a strong ordinary test. Singular terminal witnesses require Part/Field testing before Pack_Q can be recertified as the first ordinary gate.

## Audit verdict

The route is compatible with current Pack/Part/Field canonicality and primitive minimality as a conditional refinement.

It is licensed only under these boundaries:

```text
1. `Part_{N,Q}` and `Field_{N,r,Q}` remain refinements of Part and Field, not a fourth law.
2. `Part_{N,Q}` and `Field_{N,r,Q}` do not assume ordinary Pack_Q, positive terminal Pack carrier radius, finite Pack cover, finite Pack deformation/readout comparability, Pack noncollapse, or ordinary Pack-dependent Part/Field tests.
3. Pack_Q remains part of the ordinary triad after terminal witnesses are handled.
4. The route still owes quantitative strength lemmas before release-level closure.
```
