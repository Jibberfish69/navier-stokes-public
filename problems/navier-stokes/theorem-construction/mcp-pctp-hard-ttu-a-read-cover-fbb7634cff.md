## Failure point

The preceding chain proves a conditional implication from a terminal positive-scale cover and scheduler package. It does not produce that package from `OriginalSmoothData`.

The missing production theorem has four linked faces.

### Pack

The deformation gradient obeys

```math
\partial_t F=(\nabla u)F,\qquad \partial_t F^{-1}=-F^{-1}\nabla u.
```

Thus pack control follows from a pack-native strain budget dominating

```math
\int_0^{T_*}\|\nabla u(t)\|_{L^\infty}\,dt.
```

Incompressibility gives `det F=1`, which controls volume only. Energy gives `\nabla u\in L^2_{t,x}`, which leaves endpoint Lipschitz growth uncontrolled. The required theorem is a direct pack-native strain estimate from original dynamics.

### Field / terminal cover

`Field_avg` is the positive-scale SCF-good terminal cover property. `Jump_avg` is its complementary terminal radius-loss face. The averaged endpoint route removes `Jump_avg` by spending `Field_avg+OFP.A_avg`; that cannot eliminate the branch where `Field_avg` fails. A valid proof needs either `CFI.A_avg` on the `Jump_avg` branch or a direct contradiction for `Jump_avg` from original dynamics.

The current charge route has the correct raw upper carrier:

```math
\sum_k \nu_{SCF}(Q_k)\le C\nu_{SCF}(\text{terminal atlas})<\infty
```

for bounded-overlap windows. The lower route needs positive scale-normalized terminal accumulation for the same charge. Raw finite mass permits shrinking windows with positive normalized density. The missing estimate is a scale-normalized Carleson or active-square upper theorem matched to the lower `Jump_avg` charge.

### Tower

The tower readout is valid once a fixed positive-scale terminal cover and local regularity inputs are supplied:

```math
READ.COVER+ATD_m^\varepsilon+DTC.Read\Longrightarrow \mathfrak A_{N,Q}\in L^\infty(0,T_*).
```

This is a downstream readout theorem. The required upstream theorem is terminal cover and local regularity production from `OriginalSmoothData`.

### Participation

Participation persistence is conditional. With positive field scale and tower control, the finite witness closes by compactness. Thus participation is a downstream consequence after field and tower production.
