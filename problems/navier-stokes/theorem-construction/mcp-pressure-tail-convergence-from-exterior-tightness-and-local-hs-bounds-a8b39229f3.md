# Pressure-Tail Convergence From Exterior Tightness And Local Hs Bounds

## Status

Pressure-tail convergence theorem under the exhaustion hypotheses.

## Inputs

Assume:

```math
\lim_{R\to\infty}\sup_n\sup_{t\le T}\int_{|x|>R}|u_n(x,t)|^2dx=0,
```

and uniform local `H^s` bounds for `s>5/2` on every fixed ball. Then, after extraction,

```math
u_n\to u
```

locally smoothly.

## Theorem `PTail.EXH`

The periodic pressure gradients converge locally to the whole-space pressure gradient:

```math
\nabla p_n\to \nabla p,
\qquad
p=\mathcal R_i\mathcal R_j(u_i u_j),
```

on every compact subset of `R^3\times[0,T]`, after choosing additive time normalizations.

## Proof

Fix `K\Subset B_R\Subset B_{2R}` and split

```math
p_n=p_{n,R}^{loc}+h_{n,R},
\qquad
-\Delta p_{n,R}^{loc}=\partial_i\partial_j(\chi_R u_{n,i}u_{n,j}).
```

Local smooth convergence gives

```math
\chi_R u_{n,i}u_{n,j}\to \chi_R u_i u_j
```

in all local `C^k` norms. Local Calderon-Zygmund/Schauder estimates give convergence of `\nabla p_{n,R}^{loc}` on `K`.

The harmonic tail is generated by the far source `(1-\chi_R)u_n\otimes u_n`. For `x\in K`, the far kernel obeys

```math
|\nabla_x^{k+1}K(x-y)|\le C_{K,k}R^{-4-k}
```

on `|y|>R`. Hence

```math
\|\nabla^{k+1}h_{n,R}\|_{L^\infty(K)}
\le C_{K,k}R^{-4-k}\int_{|y|>R}|u_n(y,t)|^2dy.
```

Exterior tightness makes this uniformly small as `R\to\infty`. The same argument controls the limiting whole-space far tail. Therefore local convergence of the full pressure gradients follows.

## Boundary

This theorem upgrades the earlier pressure-tail statement under the explicit exhaustion hypotheses: exterior energy tightness and uniform local regularity.
