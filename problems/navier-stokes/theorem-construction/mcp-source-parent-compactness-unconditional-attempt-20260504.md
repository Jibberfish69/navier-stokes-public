
```math
\mathcal C(P)\subset\mathsf{Pred}(P),
\qquad |\mathcal C(P)|\le B,
\qquad
\pi_P(\mathcal C(P))\ge c\pi_P(\mathsf{Pred}(P)).
```

```math
\pi_P={1\over M}\sum_{\alpha=1}^M\delta_{P^-_\alpha},
\qquad P^-_\alpha\in\mathsf{Pred}(P),
```

```math

\le {B\over M}\to0.
```

Thus bounded shell banding and same-fluid fidelity fail to force bounded parent concentration.
