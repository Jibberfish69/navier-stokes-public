## Consequence For The Annular Objection

A surviving annular residual on the periodic branch is no longer allowed to be
chosen as an isolated terminal object. It has to enter through `Q_atlas`.

There are two different levels here.

At the atlas-admission level, failure of terminal atlas selection, positive
carrier scale, or material comparability is still a whole-atlas Pack failure:

```text
not Pack_{Q_atlas}.
```

That is not the CM argument for an annular residual that has already been
admitted through the atlas. After annular admission, carrier and selection have
already been paid for that residual. A retained smooth branch is

```text

  and exists r>0 Field_{N_s,r,Q_atlas},
```

and it reads to `Member(Q_atlas; O_work)`. A finite nonsmooth admitted annular
residual must leave the class through one of the remaining faces:

```text

Pack_{Q_atlas} and Part_{N_s,Q_atlas}
  and for all r>0 not Field_{N_s,r,Q_atlas}.
```

So the positive-radius annular witness is not hidden by the endpoint
radius-zero statement. It is either controlled by the gold forward route or,
on the silver periodic route, admitted through the whole atlas and then decided
by Part or Field.
