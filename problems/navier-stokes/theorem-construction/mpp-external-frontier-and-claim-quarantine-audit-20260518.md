# MPP external frontier and claim-quarantine audit

Date: 2026-05-18

Status: support-surface update only. No external paper or public program is promoted into live theorem authority.

## Governing status

Clay still lists the Navier-Stokes Millennium problem as `Unsolved` and states that there is no proof for the basic existence/uniqueness questions.

Source: https://www.claymath.org/millennium/navier-stokes-equation/

## Audit rule for this lane

Every outside regularity mechanism has to pay for the terminal positive source carrier directly before it can affect the live proof state. Candidate profiles, neural searches, continuation criteria, finite packet languages, phase decorrelation stories, and geometric screening hypotheses are useful only after they are translated into an independently checked source-carrier theorem.

For this audit, external material can enter only as:

- machinery import;
- threat model;
- candidate mechanism;
- failure checklist;
- quarantine note.

It cannot enter as:

- proof authority;
- bridge license;
- discharged dependency;
- live theorem-packet closure;
- submission-readiness evidence.

## Serious active programs

### DeepMind / Buckmaster / Gomez-Serrano / Lai / Wang / Cao-Labora

Primary sources:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14185
- https://www.quantamagazine.org/using-ai-mathematicians-find-hidden-glitches-in-fluid-equations-20260109/
- https://english.elpais.com/science-tech/2025-06-24/spanish-mathematician-javier-gomez-serrano-and-google-deepmind-team-up-to-solve-the-navier-stokes-million-dollar-problem.html

Verified shape:

The serious mathematical content is AI-assisted discovery of unstable self-similar singularity candidates in model fluid equations, using tailored neural/PINN-style architectures and high-precision optimization. The arXiv paper does not claim a Clay solution; it gives unstable candidates for equations such as IPM and 3D Euler with boundary and frames them as seeds for future computer-assisted proofs.

Repo use:

Import the workflow: terminal geometry first, high-precision residual control, then rigorous certification. Do not import any claim of Navier-Stokes closure.

### Thomas Hou / Chen-style rigorous numerics

Primary source:

- https://www.ntu.edu.sg/ias/news-events/news/detail/recent-progress-on-potential-singularity-of-the-3d-navier-stokes-equation-and-related-models-by-prof-thomas-hou

Verified shape:

The program is candidate-profile search plus adaptive numerical resolution plus computer-assisted proof discipline. The useful feature for this repo is not the existence of a public classical-Navier-Stokes proof, but the proof criterion: rescaled variables, monotone/controlled quantities, interval or computer-assisted estimates, and a clear certification route.

Repo use:

Use as the benchmark for any terminal-profile audit. A candidate mechanism is not proof-grade until it has a certification envelope.

### Elgindi / Pasqualotto instability-to-singularity route

Primary source:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.19780

Verified shape:

This is a real instability-to-singularity mechanism in Boussinesq and 3D Euler settings, using instability effects that overcome transport regularization. It is not a Navier-Stokes global-regularity or blowup proof.

Repo use:

Mechanism extraction only: it is relevant to the source-residue wall because it shows how transport can fail to disperse a terminal growth mechanism.

Use it as dissipation-threshold testing. It is outside the Clay viscous equation and cannot be treated as a direct branch solution.

## Claimed-proof quarantine bucket

Primary source:

Verdict:

Quarantine. The paper claims a full classical proof through axisymmetric swirl closure and full-system reduction. The lane already has dedicated audits:

- `mpp-shahmurov-2605-09797-quarantine-audit-20260517.md`
- `mpp-shahmurov-2605-09797-basac-closed-min-audit-20260517.md`

Useful residue:

Temporal source-to-score, finite-overlap descendants, endpoint compactness, and zero-final-defect rigidity are useful candidate burden names.

Live obstruction:

No audited bridge turns a native terminal time-face source atom into an interior transfer-active burst or a final-defect channel.

### Ri, arXiv:2601.15685

Primary source:

- https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.15685

Verdict:

Quarantine. The paper claims global regularity from a sparse inverse-log supercritical frequency space and then uses Navier-Stokes regularization / vanishing viscosity to reach Euler solvability.

Useful residue:

Failure checklist for sparse supercritical norms, viscosity-independent bounds, nonlinear-product compactness, and rescaling arguments.

Promotion blocker:

The proof must independently justify the scale-critical nonlinear closure and the strong enough compactness of `u_nu tensor u_nu` on the declared global surface.

### Jia Hong Zhang, Preprints.org 202601.0992
