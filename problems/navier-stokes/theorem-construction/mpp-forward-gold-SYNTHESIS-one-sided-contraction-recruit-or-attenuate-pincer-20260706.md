# SYNTHESIS: the floor serves for free, and the enemy signs the book

## Part I — the bottom-root horn dissolves at the top endpoint

### I.1 The exact identity (Vieta)

The Gate-6 cubic is monic:

\[
  C(X) = X(I_1-X)(I_2-X) - G_0^2
       = X^3 - (I_1{+}I_2)X^2 + I_1I_2\,X - G_0^2 .
\]

Vieta on the three roots \(0 \le r_- < r_+ < r_*\):

\[
  r_- + r_+ + r_* = I_1 + I_2,
  \qquad
  r_-\,r_+\,r_* = G_0^2 .
\]

Therefore, EXACTLY,

\[
  U_+ \;=\; r_+\,(I_1+I_2-2r_+)
      \;=\; r_+\,(r_- + r_* - r_+)
      \;\ge\; r_+\,(r_* - r_+)
  \qquad(\text{since } r_-\ge0),
\]

\[
  U_- \;=\; r_-\,(r_+ + r_* - r_-),
  \qquad
  r_- \;=\; \frac{G_0^2}{r_+\,r_*} .
\]

Two consequences. First, the bottom factor \(U_-\) dies exactly on
the \(G_0^2\to0\) branch — the horn is the vanishing-Casimir branch,
as the CM audit suspected, and it kills ONLY the bottom endpoint.
Second, the TOP factor is floored by
(top root) × (separatrix gap) — and \(r_+\ge A\) (the swing) while
\(r_*-r_+\) is the root gap: BOTH already reduced to the financing
floor \(\Phi_0\) and the period cap. The top endpoint's
nondegeneracy costs nothing new.

### I.2 The conserved gap

The slow extraction flow (return-race writeup, displayed):

\[
  \dot I_1 = \dot I_2 = -\rho X
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \frac{d}{dt}\,(I_2 - I_1) = 0
  \qquad\text{EXACTLY}.
\]

The Manley-Rowe gap is an invariant of extraction: both invariants
are taxed by the same stroke, so their difference is untouchable.
On the horn (\(G_0^2\to0\)): \(r_+\to I_1\), \(r_*\to I_2\), so

\[
  U_+ \;\longrightarrow\; I_1\,(I_2-I_1) \;>\;0
\]

whenever the family enters with \(\mathrm{gap}_0=I_2-I_1>0\) —
which it then keeps forever.

\[

  U_+\,\bigl(\delta_+^{\rm fr}-d_{\rm R4}\bigr) \;>\;0 .
\]

The bottom endpoint's service is supplied by STRUCTURE:

   bottom root's total possible downward retreat is bounded by its

3. hence the interval length \(L=r_+-r_-\) contracts at the top

\[

\]

Claim: on any climb surviving \(K\) consecutive rungs

\[
  \sum_{\text{block of }K}F_j\;\ge\;\frac{e_{\rm marg}}{2}.
\]

\(E_{j+K}\le q^K E_j+\sum F \le \tfrac14 E_j+\tfrac12 e_{\rm marg}\);
survival gives \(E_j\le E_{\max}\) bounded by the package ceiling,

\(\tfrac14 e_{\rm marg}\)-scale, forcing \(E<e_{\rm marg}\) — the
climb dies. So EVERY surviving climb recruits at linear rate:
