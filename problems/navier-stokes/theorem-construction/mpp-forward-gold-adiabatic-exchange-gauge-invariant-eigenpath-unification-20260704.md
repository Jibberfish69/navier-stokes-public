# Adiabatic exchange: gauge-invariant unification of the pincer and the connection

## 1. What each prior object got right and wrong

The angle pincer (this date) is exhaustive but static: priced pointwise, it
leaves a middle band. The eigenframe connection (Codex, this date) is
dynamic — the enemy must track the expanding eigendirection while the
pressure Hessian rotates it — but has two defects. First, the eigenprojector
\(P_+(S_k)\) is singular at eigenvalue crossings, and near-degenerate strain
is generic: the tracking-cost functional blows up where the flux need not
care, so the enemy dodges through degeneracies. Second, "following costs
\(|(D_t+\dot k\,\partial_k)P_+|^2\)" is asserted, not mechanically derived:
nothing yet forces the geometric tracking cost onto the energy ledger.

## 2. The gauge-invariant object

Replace the eigenprojector path by the normalized strain path itself,

\[
\sigma(t) := \frac{S_\ell}{|S_\ell|}
\quad\text{on the sphere of trace-free symmetric tensors,}
\tag{AE.1}
\]

transported along the packet in the co-moving frame, with its evolution
split (via the strain transport equation) into a stretching part
(eigenvalue motion, radial in the flag coordinates) and a rotation part
(eigenframe motion), the latter forced by the nonlocal pressure Hessian.
The rotation part of the path length is defined through eigenvalue
crossings — no projector singularities — and is the gauge-invariant tracking
cost. Codex's connection and holonomy language survives intact on this
object; the degeneracy loophole closes.

## 3. The mechanism: adiabatic exchange (Landau-Zener for the cascade)

The missing mechanism is a regeneration race. Subscale stress is not a
passive vector that rotates with the frame: it is regenerated by the
straining itself, on the eddy turnover time. Define the local dimensionless
adiabaticity ratio on the window:

\[
\mathcal A_\ell := \frac{\text{eigenframe rotation rate (pressure-borne)}}
{\text{stress regeneration rate (turnover)}} .
\tag{AE.2}
\]

Two branches, each now mechanical rather than asserted:

**Diabatic (\(\mathcal A_\ell\) large):** the frame rotates faster than
stress regenerates; the stress lags the frame; the gate contraction
misaligns and fluctuates in sign within one residence. This is the
backscatter jaw, and it is exactly the temporal sign-decorrelation of
refinement R1 — now sourced by a named mechanism (frame slip), not by
statistics.

**Adiabatic (\(\mathcal A_\ell\) small):** the frame is slow; stress tracks;
but a slow frame means the direction fields are coherent on the window —
which is the hypothesis of the CF depletion jaw. Order is punished by
depletion, as before, but now the coherence hypothesis is *derived from*
the adiabatic condition instead of assumed.

The static middle band of the crude pincer is therefore not a static
configuration at all: survival requires holding \(\mathcal A_\ell\) at a
marginal value — fast enough to evade depletion, slow enough to keep
tracking — at every rung, for the entire ladder, in a shrinking window.

## 4. The closing theorem class: unique continuation on the marginal set

Here is the structural point that makes this endgame plausible rather than
wishful. The ratio \(\mathcal A_\ell\) is a *local* dimensionless quantity,
but its numerator is driven by the *nonlocal* pressure Hessian — determined
by the whole packet through the divergence-free constraint. Holding a local
quantity at a marginal value along an infinite ladder therefore requires the
nonlocal field to conspire, rung after rung, with vanishing tolerance. The
statement "no nontrivial NS flow holds \(\mathcal A_\ell\) marginal on a
shrinking ladder" is a quantitative unique-continuation / Carleman-class
rigidity target:

\[
\texttt{MarginalAdiabaticityUniqueContinuation.A}
\tag{AE.3}
\]

This is the correct class for two reasons. First, precedent: the only
rigidity theorems that have ever closed an NS endgame (ESS backward
uniqueness for the critical-norm theorem) are exactly Carleman/unique-
continuation results — the toolkit exists and has an NS track record.
Second, ingredient signature: unique continuation spends the local structure
of the actual operator (transport plus nonlocal pressure coupling), which is
the destroyed-by-averaging class; the dyadic and averaged systems have no
unique-continuation structure, which is consistent with their blowup.

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{bathtub gate (flux needs co-located strain)}\\
&\to \text{dyadic countermodel (energy tax insufficient)}\\
&\to \text{angle pincer (order/disorder both pay; crude gap)}\\
&\to \text{eigenframe connection (tracking cost; Codex)}\\
&\to \textbf{adiabatic exchange: gap = marginal } \mathcal A_\ell
\text{ maintenance}\\
&\to \text{closing class: quantitative unique continuation `(AE.3)'}\\
&\quad\ \ \text{plus the Landau-Zener regeneration inequality (open).}
\end{aligned}
\tag{AE.4}

Open burdens, exactly two: (i) the quantitative regeneration inequality —
stress lag under fast rotation produces a per-residence sign-decorrelation
with \(N\ge4\) effective independent fragments (diabatic branch, R1 in
mechanical form); (ii) `(AE.3)` — the marginal set is empty for nontrivial
flows (adiabatic-boundary branch, R2 in Carleman form). Silver consumes any
survivor as a terminal record either way. No closure is claimed here.
