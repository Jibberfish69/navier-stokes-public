# The bath: steady-or-tracking dichotomy, and the final recursion

## 1. BD1: no steady scaffolded production

Relative equilibrium => time-independent vorticity magnitudes =>
any stretched member is in Burgers balance: sigma * omega = viscous
spreading; amplification zero; all stretching work dissipates
immediately. To amplify is to change state; to change state is to
force every holder whose supply is tuned to you to re-tune on your
clock. Corollary: bought lifetime is never free-standing - holding
power scales with the production rate being protected. The spring,
one level up: the scaffold cannot hold ITS peak either.

## 2. BD2: the cheap inside-bath threat (recorded honestly)

Enclosing smooth strain needs no range factor: supply = bath strain.
Demand at the coherent tier ~ s*beta^(4/5); bath energy ~
s^2 beta^(8/5) d^5 ~ beta^(8/5) * pair energy; tracking power per
e-fold ~ beta^(18/5) * pair energy. At this tier the bath holds the
pair for far less than the pair is worth. If this survived all the
way up, (B) would be live. It does not obviously survive BD3.

## 3. BD3: the recursion is the decision

The bath is somebody's strain. Its pose pays at its scale. Octave
by octave, the same question with the same exponents. Define the
recursion variable: e_oct = (energy committed at octave n+1 to hold
octave n) / (energy at octave n). Fixed positive floor on the
TOTAL cost including tracking (BD1) => geometric demand meets
geometric supply-cost => starvation => (A). e_oct -> 0 admissible
=> self-financing cascade custody => (B) construction. Every 
2026-07-05 route - tolls, impedance, meter, bracing, matrix, bath -
is this question. It is now stated once, in one variable, with both
exits named.
