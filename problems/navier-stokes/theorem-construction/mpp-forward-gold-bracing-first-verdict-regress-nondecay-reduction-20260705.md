# Bracing first verdict: the summit reduces to regress non-decay

## 1. B1: bracing works (no local obstruction)

Cancelling a pair's opposite-sense skew rotations requires a
differential torque across its separation: an external second
velocity gradient ~ Gamma beta/(pi d^3). A neighboring structure at
order-one spacing supplies it. The dense crowd can hold a producer in
its producing pose. The tax is not enforced pairwise.

Follow-up clarification:
`mpp-forward-gold-dense-bracing-pairwise-tv-no-free-subcell-20260706.md`
shows that the tax is enforced in unallocated ordered-pair TV currency.
The sentence above concerns net geometric motion after bracing
cancellations, not pairwise TV before cancellations are netted.

## 2. B2: the reaction makes it a regress

The kernel is symmetric: the bracer receives the differential torque
it transmits, at the same order. A pure brace (straight pair, zero
production, zero self-eviction) accumulates skew at the brace rate
and slides into the dead sector on the brace clock. Holding it takes
another layer. Each layer is the same problem one step out. This is
the participation story exactly: no private events - to be held is
to load the holder. The scaffold is the annular collar storage,
discretized into pairs.

## 3. B3: what the scaffold buys and what it costs

Bought: lifetime linear in layer count N; integrated stretch ~ N beta.
Unbounded in N: the per-encounter cap does NOT survive dense crowds.
Cost: committed energy linear in N. Against the record ladder's
geometrically growing demand, linear supply starves - IF each layer
costs at least a fixed floor.

## 4. B4: the one lemma

REGRESS NON-DECAY: brace(n+1) >= c_r * brace(n), c_r >= 1, from the
symmetric kernel and the two eviction channels. Display owed.

If c_r >= 1: item 4b folds into item 5; the summit becomes the
composition ledger, geometric vs linear, starvation-shaped.

If c_r < 1: finite scaffold, unbounded production, the counterexample
construction opens, exit (ii). The most valuable output either way.
