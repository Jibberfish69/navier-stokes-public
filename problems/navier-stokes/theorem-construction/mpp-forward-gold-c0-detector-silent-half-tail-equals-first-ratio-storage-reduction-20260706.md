# Detector-silent reserve is the first-ratio storage wall

## 1. Same object

The object is one same-fluid incompressible viscous Navier-Stokes history. The
record edge is selected before readout, and the proof must know whether its
positive work is already owned by a parent/root/full-exchange detector or paid
as same-material service.

The pressure-active part is a finite-dimensional detector question. The
detector-silent part is not detached from the fluid-field-object; it is local
same-packet material service. The issue is that local service assignment is not
yet a finite record-unit reserve.

## 2. What is already proved on the pressure-active side

For a fixed retained amplitude space \(V\), detector pair \(D\), and record
work functional \(w\), the frozen inequality
\[
  [wz]_+\le C|Dz|
\]
is equivalent, on the sign-symmetric preselection quotient, to
\[
  \ker D\subseteq\ker w.
\]
Equivalently, \(w\) lies in the row span of the parent root and active
full-exchange detectors, with a uniform angle on the retained compact symbol
bundle.

So the pressure-active row is not the present half-tail. Once the parent-known
active quotient, exact-projector chart, legal gauges, stops, and fixed
near-silent collar are supplied, this is finite-dimensional linear algebra.

## 3. What local silent-source routing proves

A detector-silent selected pressure component returns inside the same material
strain equation:
\[
D_tS+S^2+\Omega^2+\nabla_A^2q
=
\nu\Delta_A S+\mathcal C_A^{legal}.
\]
For a selected direction \(e\), the silent Hessian contribution is routed into
material strain change, quadratic strain/rotation exchange, viscous/radius
service, collar/frame service, legal loss, stop, or exit.

Thus a detector-silent selected event is not free. It has a physical service
address in the same packet.

## 4. Why that still does not give \(c_0\)

The c_0 reserve asks for the record-weighted sum of those silent-service visits.
Let \(\sigma_L\) be the local silent-service mass at level \(L\), and let the
record meter read that level with critical weight \(2^L\). The desired reserve
is
\[
  \sum_L2^L\sigma_L<\infty .
\tag{DSF.1}
\]

Local routing gives at most the raw service sum
\[
  \sum_L\sigma_L<\infty .
\tag{DSF.2}
\]

The half-tail
\[
  \sigma_L={2^{-L}\over L+1}
\tag{DSF.3}
\]
satisfies `(DSF.2)' and violates `(DSF.1)'. In normalized tail blocks, the
record-weighted mass can be one while the raw silent-service mass tends to
zero.

This is exactly the same arithmetic as the first-ratio storage obstruction
\[
  \nu_L={2^{-L}\over L+1},
  \qquad
  \sum_L\nu_L<\infty,
  \qquad
  \sum_L2^L\nu_L=\infty .
\tag{DSF.4}
\]

The dictionary is simply
\[
  \nu_L=\sigma_L,
  \qquad
  d\Omega^{first}_L=2^L\sigma_L .
\tag{DSF.5}
\]

Therefore the detector-silent reserve is not a second theorem below Gate 2. It
is the first-ratio storage theorem restricted to the detector-silent service
coordinate.
