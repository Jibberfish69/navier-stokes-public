# Exact-projector chart license collapses to positive critical transfer

## 1. Object

The exact-projector issue is one retained future-positive signed-height record
edge before readout:

\[
e=(\text{high packet},\text{low pressure/strain/current coefficient},
\text{material window},\text{signed polarity}).
\]

The same edge must be selected, charted, detected, channel-aligned, paid, and
stored before the record reads its positive part. A pressure-Hodge/root
parametrix on a nearby packet is not enough.

## 2. What exact-projector chart license really asks

The checked chart audit reduces

\[
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeExactProjectorChartLicense.A}
\]

to two same-edge requirements:

\[
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeMetricSymbolControl.A}
+
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeProjectorEscapePayment.A}.
\tag{EPC.1}
\]

The metric-symbol audit separates the supported part from the open part.
Finite same-carrier strain clock gives metric ellipticity, but the live row is
spatial \(C^{1,1}\) / symbol-seminorm control of the same material metric
\(G=AA^\top\), or a paid route-out before the record uses the edge:

\[
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeC11MetricTowerOrExit.A}.
\tag{EPC.2}
\]

The projector-escape side is not solved by Hodge algebra. Higher pressure/RHS,
annular-stress, higher-collar, finite-depth tower, and moving-chart exact
projector residues must be paid by same-material service or route out before
readout.

## 3. Same-edge custody is the entrance condition

The pre-readout custody tuple records the required entrance:

\[
\mathfrak T_e
=
(\mathfrak R_e,\mathfrak X_e,\mathfrak D_e,\mathfrak F_e),
\tag{EPC.3}
\]

where \(\mathfrak R_e\) is the parent-announced stopped selector,
\(\mathfrak X_e\) is the retained chart/projector license, \(\mathfrak D_e\)
is the pressure-active or service-paid detector split, and \(\mathfrak F_e\) is
the parent root/endpoint/full-exchange face address.

Separate lower-face results do not imply this tuple. A selector can capture one
edge, a chart can license another, and a detector split can pay a third. The
record edge is admitted only when all of them refer to the same \(e\) before
positive readout.

The angle-lock part is finite-dimensional after a robust near-silent service
collar. The paid-rotation branch asks for same-edge payment of detector-silent
