# Exact-projector chart license: source verdict

## 1. Tested proof route

The lower entrance to parent-owned positive-record bill currency contains:

```math
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeExactProjectorChartLicense.A}.
```

The tempting proof route is:

1. use the finite-difference tower law to keep the retained packet in a coherent
   material chart;
2. use Chapter 2 closedness to pass the limiting defect pair;
3. conclude that the exact weighted root/Hodge projector used by the frozen
   detector algebra is a legal retained-packet reading.

The allowed sources do not prove this implication.

## 2. What the tower/coherence source proves

The shared participation source gives the exact tower-difference law:

```math
(\partial_t+u(x+h,t)\cdot\nabla)\delta_hU_k
+(\delta_hu)\cdot\nabla U_k
+\delta_h\nabla^{k+1}p
-\nu\Delta\delta_hU_k
=
\delta_hB_k .
```

After pairing with \(\delta_hU_k\), it gives viscous defect dissipation and the
localized scalar defect-energy estimate `TC.31/TC.31a`.

That is the right Field/coherence engine. It controls neighboring tower
differences and pressure flux in the retained window. It does not by itself
construct a material chart in which the exact weighted divergence-form projector
has uniform ellipticity, controlled symbol seminorms, and legal commutator
residues for every future-positive record-menu edge.

Physically, coherence of neighboring tower reads is not the same thing as
licensing a later exact parent-root projection as a shell-preserving same-packet
operator.

## 3. What Chapter 2 proves

Chapter 2 proves finite-readout closedness for the defect pair in a varying
cone. Its proof uses finite readout, monotone cone half-spaces, upper
semicontinuous readout envelopes, and weak-star measure passage.

That result passes a defect pair once the readout and cone structure are already
legal finite-readout objects. It does not prove that

```math
[\Delta_j,\Pi_G^{ex}]Z_e
```

is lower-order, legal, stopped, or active-paid for arbitrary record-menu edges.
It also does not build the moving material chart or symbol bounds needed for the
exact weighted projector itself.

So Chapter 2 is a closedness consumer, not the missing projector-chart producer.

## 4. What the signed-height source explicitly leaves open

The signed-height source separates the frozen detector algebra from the
operator lift. The frozen principal-symbol statement controls only the frozen
quotient:

```math
[w_{rec}z]_+\le C|Dz|
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\ker D\subseteq\ker w_{rec}.
```

To recover the full retained edge theorem, the source requires the exact
weighted projector commutator to be demoted into legal, stopped, lower-order, or
active-paid material. It names this as a strict reduction, not an installed
proof.

The same source sharpens the open operator input into two clauses:

```math
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeC11MetricTowerOrExit.A}
```

and

```math
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeHigherProjectorServiceOrExit.A}.
```

The first must supply the metric and symbol-seminorm chart control from the same
material-frame derivative tower, or route failure as a pre-readout paid exit.
The second must pay pressure/RHS, annular-stress, higher-collar, finite-depth
tower, and moving-chart exact-projector residues by finite same-material service
or route them out before record readout.

## 5. Physical meaning

The exact projector is not an innocent coordinate change. It reaches across
frequency shells and a moving material metric. A future-positive record edge can
be genuine same-fluid work while the exact parent-root projector that would
store it produces commutator or chart residues not yet in \(B\)-currency.

The proof must show those residues are already legal, stopped, lower-order, or
active-paid before the record selector counts the edge. The allowed sources do
not do that.
