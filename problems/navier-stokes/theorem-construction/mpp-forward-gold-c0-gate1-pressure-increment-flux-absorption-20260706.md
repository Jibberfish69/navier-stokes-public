# Gate 1 pressure-increment flux absorption

## 1. Correction to the pressure-collar obstruction

The pressure flux in the tower-difference identity is not a free pressure
current. It is created by pairing the pressure increment with the same tower
difference being measured.

At the base rung the checked source writes the exact identity

```math
\frac12(\partial_t+u(x+h,t)\cdot\nabla-\nu\Delta)|\delta_h u|^2
+\nu |\nabla\delta_h u|^2
+\delta_h u\cdot S(x,t)\delta_h u
+\nabla\cdot(\delta_h p\,\delta_h u)
=0.
\tag{PIFA.1}
```

So the pressure boundary term is not \(p\) alone. It is

```math
\delta_h p\,\delta_h u .
\tag{PIFA.2}
```

The higher rungs have the same structure after integrating by parts:
the flux component is a finite sum of products

```math
\delta_h U_k * \delta_h\nabla^k p .
\tag{PIFA.3}
```

This matters. A proof-model atom with nonzero pressure collar flux and
\(dE^{Field}=0\) is detached from the TC.20 algebra unless it also supplies a
nonzero tower difference in the collar or a nonlocal pressure-increment tail
generated by such a difference somewhere else.

## 2. Pressure increment is one ordinary rung times one tower difference

The pressure law is

```math
-\Delta p=\partial_i\partial_j(u_i u_j).
\tag{PIFA.4}
```

After applying \(\delta_h\nabla^k\), the differentiated pressure is a
Calderon-Zygmund image of differentiated product increments:

```math
\delta_h\nabla^k p
=
T_k\bigl(\delta_h\nabla^k(u\otimes u)\bigr).
\tag{PIFA.5}
```

Leibniz expansion gives the schematic finite sum

```math
\delta_h\nabla^k(u\otimes u)
=
\sum_{a+b\le k}
\bigl(
U_a(x+h)*\delta_h U_b
+\delta_h U_a*U_b(x)
\bigr).
\tag{PIFA.6}
```

Thus, on a retained finite-depth window with ordinary-rung envelope \(A_N\),

```math
\|\chi\,\delta_h\nabla^k p\|_2
\le
C_N A_N\sum_{m\le k}\|\chi'\,\delta_hU_m\|_2
+\mathrm{Tail}^{press}_{k,h}(\chi,\chi').
\tag{PIFA.7}
```

Here \(T_k\) is a standard order-zero singular-integral operator, \(\chi'\) is
a slightly enlarged cutoff, and the tail term is exactly the nonlocal part not
seen by the local cutoff. On the whole space, or after exact local/tail
admission, the tail term is zero, legal, absorbed, or route-out.

This is the source-native pressure mechanism that the collar-atom model did
not encode.

## 3. Flux absorption estimate

Let \(\phi\) be a same-carrier cutoff and let \(\chi\) dominate the support of
\(\phi\nabla\phi\). The pressure flux contribution satisfies, for each rung,

```math
\left|
\int \phi\nabla\phi\cdot
\bigl(\delta_h U_k * \delta_h\nabla^k p\bigr)
\,dx
\right|
\le
\|\chi\,\delta_hU_k\|_2\,
\|\chi\,\delta_h\nabla^k p\|_2 .
\tag{PIFA.8}
```

Using `(PIFA.7)' and summing over \(k\le N\),

```math
|\mathrm{Flux}^{press}_{N,h,\phi}|
\le
C_N A_N
\sum_{m\le N}\int_{\operatorname{supp}\chi'}
|\delta_hU_m|^2\,dx
+\mathrm{Tail}^{press}_{N,h,\phi}.
\tag{PIFA.9}
```

With the usual viscous \(2ab\) split, any derivative loss in the commutator
version of `(PIFA.7)' is absorbed into

```math
\eta\nu\sum_{m\le N}\int\phi^2|\nabla\delta_hU_m|^2\,dx
\tag{PIFA.10}
```

plus the same Field-energy term and the pressure tail.

Therefore the source-correct pressure closure theorem is:

```math
\texttt{RetainedPressureIncrementFluxAbsorption.A}.
\tag{PIFA.11}
```

On retained finite-depth relay windows,

```math
\int_W|\mathrm{Flux}^{press}_{N,h,\phi}|\,dt
\le
C_N\int_W A_N(t)E_{N,h,\chi'}(t)\,dt
+\eta\,dVisc_{N,h}(W)
+Legal+Stop+Tail^{press}_{N,h,\phi}(W).
\tag{PIFA.12}
```

This is the pressure analogue of the triangular cascade term in TC.31. It is
not an independent pressure-tail currency unless the final tail term survives.

## 4. What remains open

The remaining theorem is not arbitrary pressure-collar tightness. It is the
exact local/nonlocal admission of `(PIFA.7)' on the retained relay family:

```math
\texttt{RetainedPressureIncrementCZTailAdmission.A}.
\tag{PIFA.13}
```

It has two clauses.

First, the local Calderon-Zygmund and commutator part of
\(\delta_h\nabla^k p\) must be represented by one ordinary retained rung times
one retained tower difference, as in `(PIFA.7)'.

Second, every nonlocal pressure-increment tail left by the cutoff split must be
one of:

```math
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{absorbed:} & \text{bounded by the Field/viscous terms in }(PIFA.12),\\[1mm]
\text{legal/stop:} & \text{a permitted cutoff or stopped material contribution},\\[1mm]
\text{route-out:} & \text{a concrete Pack, terminal Part, or Field face failure.}
\end{array}
\tag{PIFA.14}
```

With `(PIFA.13)', pressure no longer blocks TC.31a: the pressure flux is part of
the same localized Field/viscous closure, not a standalone collar atom.

## 5. Consequence for the active c_0 gate

This note changes the Gate 1 obstruction. The old collar-atom proof model
remains useful only as a warning about detached flux bookkeeping. It is not a
licensed countermodel for the actual pressure term in TC.20 because TC.20
forces pressure flux to carry a tower difference factor.

The sharpened first Gate 1 line is now:

```math
\texttt{RetainedPressureIncrementCZTailAdmission.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{RetainedRelayFluxCutoffClosure.A}.
\tag{PIFA.15}
```

If the tail-admission theorem is proved, the localized Field compactness gate
is cleared: vanishing Field and viscous bills absorb the pressure cutoff flux.
If it fails, the precise escape is no longer an arbitrary pressure collar atom;
it is a nonlocal pressure-increment tail generated by tower differences outside
the retained cutoff that is not legal, not absorbed, and not a Pack/Part/Field
route-out.

Thus `NormalizedRelayBillCompactnessRecordPassage.A' is still not installed,
but Gate 1 has been narrowed to the exact pressure-increment tail admission
problem rather than a free pressure-collar size problem.
