# Gate 1 pressure-source law verdict

## 1. Physical question

The same-fluid read window can be transported with the packet, so advection is
not the collar problem. The viscous collar can be made small by expanding the
material window. The only surviving Gate 1 question is whether incompressibility
and the pressure equation force pressure redistribution through the collar to be
paid by the current bill.

Physically: can the pressure field, generated by the same incompressible
velocity history, re-aim a retained tower read through the boundary of the
selected window without showing up as interior Field defect, four-body payment,
or viscous production?

The allowed source answer is: not from the current data.

## 2. Source law being tested

The participation/coherence law gives the local quadratic defect identity

```math
\frac12(\partial_t+u(x+h,t)\cdot\nabla-\nu\Delta)|\delta_hU_k|^2
+\nu|\nabla\delta_hU_k|^2
=
\mathcal P^{red}_{k,h}
+\nabla\cdot\Pi^{press}_{k,h}
-\delta_hU_k:\big((\delta_hu)\cdot\nabla U_k\big)
+\delta_hU_k:\delta_hB_k .
\tag{PSV.1}
```

The checked source states the pressure role exactly: pressure is redistribution
plus flux. After localization, the source records the flux bound as theorem
data, not as a consequence of `(PSV.1)` alone:

```math
\int_I|\mathrm{Flux}_{N,h,\phi}(t)|\,dt
\le
\mathrm{Legal}_{N,h,\phi}(I).
\tag{PSV.2}
```

Thus the only available pressure mechanism before a new theorem is global
incompressible redistribution. It is not a sign-bearing dissipation term.

## 3. Critical scaling test

After the material-cutoff and viscous-collar reductions, the remaining pressure
collar is

```math
\mathcal C^{press}_{N,h,R}(W)
=
\int_W
\left|
2\sum_{k=0}^{N}\lambda_k
\int\phi_R\nabla\phi_R\cdot\Pi^{press}_{k,h}\,dx
\right|dt .
\tag{PSV.3}
```

For a large material window, \(|\nabla\phi_R|\lesssim R^{-1}\). This factor only
forces `(PSV.3)` to vanish when the pressure-flux density is tight in the
collar:

```math
\int_W
{1\over R}
\int_{\operatorname{collar}(R)}
\sum_{k=0}^{N}\lambda_k|\Pi^{press}_{k,h}|\,dx\,dt
\longrightarrow 0 .
\tag{PSV.4}
```

The critical obstruction is immediate. A same-scale annular pressure flux with
total collar size comparable to \(R\) gives

```math
{1\over R}
\int_{\operatorname{collar}(R)}
\sum_{k=0}^{N}\lambda_k|\Pi^{press}_{k,h}|\,dx
\simeq 1 .
\tag{PSV.5}
```

This is exactly the scale at which a pressure collar atom survives. The
incompressible pressure representation is order-zero at the critical scaling:
it can redistribute the source nonlocally, but it does not add a positive sign,
a compactness gain, or an extra \(R^{-1}\) decay factor.

## 4. Why this is not paid by the current bill

The current bill is

```math
B=dE^{Field}_{N,h}+dA_{4B,N}+dVisc_{N,h}.
\tag{PSV.6}
```

Each term sees a different physical channel:

1. \(dE^{Field}_{N,h}\) sees neighboring-tower decoherence inside the retained
   packet.
2. \(dA_{4B,N}\) sees retained one-way four-body positive variation.
3. \(dVisc_{N,h}\) sees created smaller scale through viscous defect
   dissipation.

The collar pressure flux in `(PSV.3)` is a boundary redistribution channel. It
can be generated by the same incompressible pressure law while remaining outside
these three currencies unless the proof supplies an ownership/tightness theorem
that brings it back into the retained packet.

So the attempted implication

```math
B(W_j)\to0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathcal C^{press}_{N,h_j,R}(W_j)\to0
\tag{PSV.7}
```

is not licensed by the allowed pressure law.

## 5. Sharpened first-gate theorem

The pressure-source form of the missing Gate 1 theorem is:

```math
\texttt{SameCarrierPressureCollarOwnershipOrTightness.A}.
\tag{PSV.8}
```

For every retained same-fluid relay sequence with \(R(W_j)=1\) and
\(B(W_j)\to0\), every nonzero limiting pressure flux through the selected
same-carrier material collar satisfies one of:

```math
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{tightness:} & \mathcal C^{press}_{N,h_j,R}(W_j)\to0
\text{ after large-window exhaustion},\\[1mm]
\text{absorption:} & \limsup_j\mathcal C^{press}_{N,h_j,R}(W_j)
\le o_R(1)+C\,B(W_j),\\[1mm]
\text{route-out:} & \text{the flux is legal/collar/Pack-Part loss rather than retained relay record.}
\end{array}
\tag{PSV.9}
```

This is the same first gate as `RetainedPressureFluxTightnessOrRouteOut.A`,
but with the pressure-source mechanism named explicitly.

## 6. Consequence for the active c_0 goal

This note does not install or refute
`NormalizedRelayBillCompactnessRecordPassage.A`. It gives a checked first-gate

```math
\text{incompressibility + pressure redistribution}
\quad\not\Rightarrow\quad

\tag{PSV.10}
```

The first exact failed gate remains Gate 1. The smallest remaining theorem at
