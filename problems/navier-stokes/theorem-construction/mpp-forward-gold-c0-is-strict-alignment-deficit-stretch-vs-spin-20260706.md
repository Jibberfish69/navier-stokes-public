# c_0 is the strict alignment deficit: stretching vs spin

A record = sustained vortex stretching. Stretching thins the tube; conserved
circulation makes it spin faster; the faster spin rotates the local frame,
including the strain field the fluid itself generates. So the act of recording
speeds the very rotation that pulls the vortex out of the stretching direction.
The record persists only while stretching keeps pace with that mis-rotation.

## 2. Physical: local spin vs non-local strain

The mis-aligning rotation is LOCAL — the vortex's own spin |omega| at the point.
The stretching strain is NON-LOCAL — a straight tube does not stretch itself;
the stretching comes from the collective vorticity elsewhere (the fluid's
incompressibility self-participation reaching in from around the point). This is
the right physical asymmetry to watch, but it is not a proof by itself. At the
strain level,

\[

does not carry a free smoothing power relative to local spin.

vortex axis). But S (symmetric) and omega (antisymmetric) are two parts of the
same grad u, both O(|grad u|). So GENERICALLY alpha ~ |omega|: the stretching
and spinning are the same size, the alignment race is a TIE. This is the
marginal 1/2 in physical form -- and it matches every prior finding
(break-even mean = 1/2; affine/local = 0; single-scale surplus = 0). Nothing
generic pulls the ratio to the depleting side.
