# c_0 is not a budget statement; it is the strict geometric-tilting excess on the thin cascade

## 1. Physical: a thin record is a high-strain record

A record is the fluid moving reserve down one scale (a cascade step). Its only
clock is the eddy-turnover set by the strain. To double the critical height H in
material time tau, the fluid must strain at rate ~ 1/tau. So thin records are
high-strain records: fast doubling costs proportionally large strain.

## 2. Physical: two costs, one budgeted and one not

Straining hard costs the fluid its own currency — bending the pair-field, i.e.
the four-body bill (~ strain^2 ~ 1/tau^2, a palinstrophy-type quantity). Against
this, the fluid has a FINITE residence budget: the energy/dissipation budget
int_0^T H^2 dt <= E_0^2/8nu.

- THICK record: long residence at high H, pays into the finite budget; only
  finitely many fit.
- THIN record: residence ~ tau (small), pays almost nothing into the budget;
  its real cost is the four-body/palinstrophy charge ~ 1/tau^2, which is NOT
  bounded a priori below the singularity.

## 3. Consequence: RUC cannot close c_0

The record-upcrossing thickness-or-charge dichotomy catches the thick horn
(finite energy budget) but the thin horn ESCAPES: it pays an unbudgeted charge.
So no budget argument forbids an infinite thin-record cascade. c_0 is therefore
NOT a budget statement. (This retires the "extend the RUC energy budget" route
as a closer.)
