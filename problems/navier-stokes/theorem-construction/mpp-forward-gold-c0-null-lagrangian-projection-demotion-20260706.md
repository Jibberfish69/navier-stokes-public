# Null-Lagrangian record passage is a projection, not the whole c_0 theorem

## 1. Same object

The corrected object is one same-fluid incompressible viscous dissipative
Navier-Stokes history. The record, pressure response, viscosity, material
transport, tower readout, selected sign, scale, and time all belong to that
same fluid-field-object.

The spatial null-Lagrangian identity is still true:
\[
  \operatorname{tr}(A^3)=3\det(\nabla u)=\operatorname{div}\Phi .
\]
It says the signed spatial record core has Jacobian structure. It does not say
that the Navier-Stokes material history can carry positive record growth through
time without a same-packet bill.

## 2. What the null-Lagrangian row really proves

The tower transcription note proves the fixed-depth spatial fact. Translations,
finite differences, and finite tower derivatives of \(u\) produce polarized
Jacobians of scalar tower components. Polarized Jacobians are finite sums of
ordinary Jacobians, hence divergences by the same Piola/div-curl structure.

So, with the required equi-integrability/no-concentration input, the signed
spatial tower core passes weakly:
\[
  J_m\rightharpoonup J
\]
for each fixed finite-depth tower Jacobian. This is a real consumer theorem.

It is not the \(c_0\) theorem.

## 3. Three missing operations

First, the live record is one-way positive variation. Weak passage of a signed
Jacobian does not pass the positive part. At the measure level, signed
oscillations can converge weakly to zero while their positive variation stays
positive; a pawl/retention theorem is exactly the missing operation that keeps
the positive leg attached to its negative/current partner before readout.

Second, spatial equi-integrability does not prevent temporal concentration. A
record density can keep its spatial profile and collapse to a material-time
Dirac. The corrected ontology puts this back on the irreversible clock: a thin
same-packet record must pay temporal thickness or same-parent charge in bill
currency.

Third, weak passage of the Jacobian core does not construct same-parent
ownership. The limit may have a spatial signed core while the positive record
edge loses the parent-known root/payment address that makes it a retained
same-fluid event.

These three operations are exactly the rows now named elsewhere as Gate 2
positive-channel/root-payment admission, theta-payer measure lift, and
zero-surplus material-time rigidity.

The older target-spec file stated that the whole \(c_0\) problem is the
\(L^2\to L^3\) equi-integrability/no-critical-concentration step for the relay
record. That statement is overstrong after the ontology correction.
