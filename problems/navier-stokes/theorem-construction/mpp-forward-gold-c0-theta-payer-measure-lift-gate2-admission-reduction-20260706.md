# Theta payer-measure lift reduces to Gate 2 admission

The object is one same-fluid incompressible viscous Navier-Stokes history in
material time. The ratio-one bad branch asks for positive normalized

same-packet payment channel remains marginal.

The closed-zero-surplus note proves an abstract fact: zero of a nonnegative
carried payer measure is closed. The remaining producer question is whether the
actual Navier-Stokes history supplies the carried payer measure for the
positive \(\Theta_N\)-record channel.

\tag{TPL.1}
\]

These are genuine certificate coordinates. When such a defect survives, it is

So the live payer-lift row is narrower. It is the positive material-record
clock itself:

\[

\tag{TPL.2}

One can always write the visible nonnegative clock
\[

\tag{TPL.3}
\]
That is not the route-b payer measure.

The bad profile is allowed to have positive \(\Theta_N\) service. The theorem
has to rule out positive service while all same-packet surplus payers are zero.
Thus the needed measure is not "how much service occurred." It is the carried
same-packet payment/admission coordinate that makes the service visible in the
bill before the record reads its positive part:

\[

C\int_W
\left(dE_{N,h}^{Field}+dA_{4B,N}+dVisc_{N,h}\right)

\tag{TPL.4}

Navier-Stokes payer.
