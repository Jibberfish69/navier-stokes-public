# Transverse restoring sign: local no-go and retained borrowing reduction

## 0. Physical target

The active \(c_0\) proof is still the full strict-bill kernel:

```math
\mathcal P=0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathcal S=0
```

on lawful same-fluid strict atoms/towers. The transverse pressure sign is a
component inside the service-persistence/payment gate:

```math
\texttt{TransverseRestoringPressureSign.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{CriticalServicePersistenceFromRestoringPayment.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{CriticalServicePersistenceFromPayment.A}.
\tag{TRB.1}
```

This note pins down what that sign theorem can and cannot be.

## 1. A local sign theorem is impossible

Let \(S\) be the retained strain frame at the record station, and let

```math
H=\nabla^2p
```

be the same-field pressure Hessian solving

```math
-\Delta p=\partial_i u_j\,\partial_j u_i.
\tag{TRB.2}
```

The exact shear-cell display proves that for every

```math
K\in {\rm Sym}_0(3)
```

there is a smooth periodic divergence-free velocity cell \(u_K\) with

```math
u_K(0)=0,\qquad
\nabla u_K(0)=0,\qquad
\nabla^2p_K(0)=K.
\tag{TRB.3}
```

Therefore, at a selected point, the local velocity and local strain can be left
unchanged while the trace-free pressure Hessian is prescribed with any
off-frame component and either sign.

Choose \(K\) anti-restoring in the \(S\)-frame. Then any proposed pointwise
inequality of the form

```math
\operatorname{Restore}(S,\omega,H_\perp^S)
\ge
c\,\mathsf S_V(S,\omega)
\tag{TRB.4}
```

fails at the jet level. The same obstruction also kills local Codazzi,
pointwise incompressibility, and finite-jet affine dynamics as sign suppliers.
The transverse sign cannot be proved locally.

This is not a Navier-Stokes counterexample. The shear cell is a pressure bank.
It shows that a bad or neutral pressure frame can be borrowed locally; it does
not show that a retained self-stretching record can borrow it through a lawful
tower at zero bill.

## 2. The exact replacement theorem

The sign row must be stated as a retained same-field borrowing theorem.

In a strict atom/tower, the pressure Hessian seen by the record is generated by
the same velocity field. Its source is either:

1. inside the retained atom/collar;
2. in the parent/complement pressure tail;
3. in a different owner, hence not child-retained service.

Thus a zero-bill retained record cannot simply choose an anti-restoring or
neutral \(H_\perp^S\). If that pressure Hessian is produced inside the retained
atom, its non-affine pressure/current work belongs to \(\mathcal P\). If it is
produced by the parent or complement, it is parent-owned pressure borrowing,
and the owner-transfer/collar/current bill must either be paid or the claimed
child service is routed out of retained ownership.

The correct smaller theorem is:

```math
\texttt{NoFreeWrongSignPressureBorrowing.A}.
\tag{TRB.5}
```

Statement. In a lawful same-fluid strict participation tower, a retained record
with positive Vieillefosse service cannot maintain a neutral or anti-restoring
transverse pressure Hessian across adjacent rungs at zero strict bill. More
precisely, the failure of the restoring sign implies at least one of:

```math
\text{retained pressure/current payment}>0,
\qquad
\text{collar or owner-transfer payment}>0,
\qquad
\text{parent/pass ownership of the service},
\qquad
\text{endpoint affine kernel}.
\tag{TRB.6}
```

Equivalently,

```math
\texttt{NoFreeWrongSignPressureBorrowing.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{TransverseRestoringPressureSign.A}
\quad
\text{in the retained zero-bill quotient}.
\tag{TRB.7}
```

The phrase "in the retained zero-bill quotient" is load-bearing: the theorem
does not assert a pointwise sign for all Navier-Stokes jets. It asserts that
the only jets with the bad sign require pressure ownership, collar attachment,
or current transport that the strict bill sees.

## 3. Adjacent-scale form

For two adjacent rungs \(Q\subset \Lambda Q\), write the transverse pressure
frame contribution at the child as

```math
H_\perp^S(Q)
=
H_{\perp,\mathrm{child}}^S
+
H_{\perp,\mathrm{parent}}^S.
\tag{TRB.8}
```

The local shear-cell no-go says \(H_{\perp,\mathrm{parent}}^S\) can have either
sign at the child station. The strict-participation theorem must prove that a
parent contribution used to cancel the child restoring sign cannot telescope
for free:

```math
\sum_Q
\mathsf P_{\mathrm{owner/collar/current}}
\bigl(H_{\perp,\mathrm{parent}}^S(Q)\bigr)
\ge
c
\sum_Q
\mathsf S_V(Q)
\tag{TRB.9}
```

unless the claimed child service is actually parent/pass owned or collapses
into the smooth affine endpoint kernel.

This is the exact place where the local pressure-bank construction becomes
useful. It supplies the adversary's only local move; the tower theorem must
charge its use as a same-fluid retained borrowing.

## 4. Effect on the direct \(c_0\) proof

The active proof chain is now:

```math
\texttt{NoFreeWrongSignPressureBorrowing.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{TransverseRestoringPressureSign.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{CriticalServicePersistenceFromPayment.A}
\tag{TRB.10}
```

together with strict atom compactness/no-loss and nonnegative ownership
accounting. Then the zero-payment kernel proof gives

```math
\mathcal S\le C\mathcal P,
\qquad
c_0>0.
\tag{TRB.11}
```

So this note does not solve \(c_0\). It removes a false local-sign target and
states the remaining analytic physics theorem in the only form compatible with
the same-field pressure equation and the exact shear-cell obstruction.

## 5. Adjacent-scale pressure-bank mismatch

There is a further scale constraint on a free borrowed bank. Under the
Navier-Stokes scaling

```math
U_r(y)=r\,u(x_0+ry),
\qquad
P_r(y)=r^2p(x_0+ry),
\tag{TRB.12}
```

the normalized pressure Hessian is

```math
H_r(y)=\nabla_y^2P_r(y)=r^4\nabla_x^2p(x_0+ry).
\tag{TRB.13}
```

For adjacent rungs \(R=2r\),

```math
H_r(y)
=
\left({r\over R}\right)^4
H_R\!\left({r\over R}y\right)
=
{1\over16}H_R(y/2).
\tag{TRB.14}
```

So if the child and parent both claim the same normalized wrong-sign pressure
bank \(K\) on the physical overlap,

```math
H_r(y)\approx K(y),
\qquad
H_R(Y)\approx K(Y),
\tag{TRB.15}
```

then the same physical field forces

```math
K(y)\approx {1\over16}K(y/2).
\tag{TRB.16}
```

A constant same-normalized pressure Hessian cannot satisfy this unless
\(K=0\). Thus a zero-bill borrowed bank cannot be a fixed local Hessian packet
reused rung after rung.

The only possible escape is a truly critical pressure cone:

```math
K(y)=|y|^{-4}\mathcal K(\theta),
\qquad
\theta={y\over |y|}.
\tag{TRB.17}
```

Then \(K(y)=16^{-1}K(y/2)\). Since \(H=\nabla^2p\) is generated by
\(\partial_i u_j\partial_j u_i\), this escape corresponds to a degree \(-1\)
velocity profile:

```math
u(x)\sim |x|^{-1}V(\theta),
\qquad
\nabla u\sim |x|^{-2},
\qquad
\nabla^2p\sim |x|^{-4}.
\tag{TRB.18}
```

Therefore the pressure-borrowing theorem has a precise terminal form:

```math
\texttt{NoFreeWrongSignPressureBorrowing.A}
```

is equivalent, after adjacent-scale mismatch, to excluding or routing out a
retained incompressible degree-\(-1\) pressure-bank cone whose transverse
pressure Hessian is neutral or anti-restoring while Vieillefosse service remains
child-retained.

This is smaller than the previous sign wall. Local shear-cell banks explain how
the adversary can choose the bad Hessian at one scale; adjacent scaling says
that using it at infinitely many zero-bill rungs forces a critical cone. The
remaining task is the cone Liouville/ownership theorem:

```math
\texttt{NoRetainedWrongSignCriticalPressureCone.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{NoFreeWrongSignPressureBorrowing.A}.
\tag{TRB.19}
```

## 6. Cone route-out by exact-profile cases

The cone row is now small enough to split exactly. Suppose a retained
zero-bill tower produces the degree \(-1\) wrong-sign pressure cone

```math
u(x)=|x|^{-1}V(\theta),
\qquad
p(x)=|x|^{-2}P(\theta),
\qquad
\nabla^2p=|x|^{-4}\mathcal K(\theta).
\tag{TRB.20}
```

There are only three same-fluid readings.

First, the cone is not an exact stationary smooth-sphere profile on
\(\mathbb R^3\setminus\{0\}\). Then the defect is one of:

```math
\partial_sU\ne0,\quad
\text{translation/rotation/selector drift},\quad
\text{annular defect},\quad
\text{source residue},\quad
U|_{S^2}\text{ not smooth}.
\tag{TRB.21}
```

Each item is precisely a strict participation bill or route-out: material-time
current, frame/owner transfer, annular collar defect, Field/source residue, or
loss of strict atom regularity. Hence this case is incompatible with retained
zero payment.

Second, the cone is an exact stationary \((-1)\)-homogeneous smooth-sphere
Navier-Stokes profile with nonzero point force \(\Phi_0\). Then the force is a
source/owner object at the cone tip:

```math
-\nu\Delta u+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p
=
\Phi_0\,\delta_0.
\tag{TRB.22}
```

That is not child-retained zero-payment service. It is parent/Field/source
ownership, hence a route-out or a paid owner-transfer.

Third, the cone is exact stationary, smooth on \(S^2\), divergence-free, and
has zero point force. The Landau/Sverak consumer recorded in
`mpp-gold-stress-flux-bridge-and-landau-liouville-test-20260617.md` applies:

```math
\begin{aligned}
&u \text{ is } (-1)\text{-homogeneous},\\
&-\nu\Delta u+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p=0
  \quad\text{on }\mathbb R^3\setminus\{0\},\\
&\nabla\cdot u=0,\qquad u|_{S^2}\text{ smooth},\qquad \Phi_0=0
\end{aligned}
\Longrightarrow
u=0.
\tag{TRB.23}
```

Then the cone carries no Vieillefosse service.

Thus, in the retained zero-bill quotient,

```math
\texttt{NoRetainedWrongSignCriticalPressureCone.A}
\tag{TRB.24}
```

is reduced to the strict-profile production/accounting assertion:
\(\mathcal P=0\) must really remove time drift, frame drift, annular defect,
source residue, and nonzero point-force ownership from the cone. Once that
accounting assertion is installed, the cone row is consumed.
