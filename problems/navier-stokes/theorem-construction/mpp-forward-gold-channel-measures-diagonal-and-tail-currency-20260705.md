# The four frame clauses

## C-I. Diagonal extraction: one subsequence, all channels

Chapter 2's eps-uniform bound is channel-stable (fixed bounded
multipliers on the same integrability budget). The channel set is
countable - that was the frame's purpose. Diagonalize: one
mollification subsequence along which EVERY channel flux converges
weak-star to its channel defect measure, each with inherited variation
control. Countably many observations of one fluid, closed by one
diagonal.

## C-II. Reconstruction on finite-readout sets

On compacts inside a finite-readout superlevel set, channels above the
readout contribute geometric locality tails only; the channel series
converges absolutely in local variation; dominated convergence of the
partial sums against test functions identifies the sum with the total
defect. Reconstruction holds exactly where the cone theorem lives; the
infinite-readout set remains the silver route's input, as designed.

## C-III. Tails in throughput currency

The flux-gate localization tails are the same increment-cubic form as
the fluxes, taken across cell boundaries. Each tail is therefore
dominated by the neighboring cells' own throughput, discounted by
geometric separation weights that sum. No foreign norm enters: the
tail budget is adjacent throughput at a discount - the currency the
taxes are already written in.

## C-IV. Row composition, cell-wise then summed

C-I gives finite-variation cell measures; Gate A (filed, both forms)
gives the oriented decomposition with channel-signed variation
control. Admission, pricing, and debt routing act within each cell;
the sum over cells is countable additivity; orientation is ray-wise
inside cells and untouched by summation. The composition clause is
discharged by the order of operations: decompose, tax, route - then
add.
