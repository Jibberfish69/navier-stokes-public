# Chapter 10 workbook: the certification program, specified

## Discipline (binding, from the spec)

Compact family at unit local Reynolds, co-moving quotient, order-lock;
mandatory gates BEFORE any value is read: (Gate R) rotation
countermodel must report full retention (else the functional is not
measuring selected recounting); (Gate D) dyadic blowup model must
FAIL the bound (else the functional is not measuring the toll). Any
value produced outside this discipline has no authority in the lane.
Stage 2: family sweep with margins. Stage 3: interval arithmetic,
Hou discipline. All results land in the first-composition manuscript
as its named symbols.

## Target C1 - the intermediate-chop prefactor (unblocks section 8)

Object: per-step hyperbolic-sector drain at chop numbers three and
up, as a function of the instability exponent (drain monotone in
exponent - filed, structural). Family: catalyst-flipped steps with
catalyst ratio at or above the appetite root a*, partners in the
mid-band hyperbolic sub-cone, elliptic modulus in the parking-capped
band. Required inequality (named form): delivered per-octave drain at
chop N at least the exact algebraic gap between the composed scalar
retention at N and one-half, for every N from three up to the
structural threshold where the completed-swing regime takes over.
Gates: R and D both apply (the drain functional must vanish on the
rotation countermodel and exceed the gap on the dyadic model).

## Target C2 - the coupling-modulus floors (unblocks section 3)

Object: the helical triple-product modulus on two fixed compact shape
families: (a) the mid-band reverse triangles at catalyst ratio at
least a*; (b) the relay/predator cross-triad ratio on the ladder's
shape family. Required inequality: positive lower floor on (a);
two-sided positive bounds on (b). Gates: not functional-type; instead
verify the degenerate boundary (collinear shapes) is approached
nowhere in the family (exact side-ratio check, part of the interval
pass domain setup).

## Target C3 - the residence and race constants (unblocks section 5)

Object: the named symbols of the closed forms - the residence constant
(elliptic half-period in the parking-capped modulus band), the return
floor at the chosen extraction fraction, the viscous tax constant.
Required inequality: each positive with stated interval enclosure;
consistency check that the single-dial floor c0 computed from the
enclosures is positive (it is a min of two enclosed positives - the
check is closure of the bookkeeping, not a new mechanism). Gates: R/D
on the race functional (parked rotation countermodel must show no
return; dyadic relay must show full return).

## Target C4 - mollifier and window constants (unblocks sections 2, 7)

Object: the mollifier-gradient norm constants of the UV bound, the
Taylor constants of the IR bound, the dyadic tail sums, the
channel-commutator constants (T2'), and the per-handoff Gronwall data
(Lipschitz-times-duration enclosure; band count threshold from the
tail-versus-exponent comparison). Required inequality: the per-handoff
tracking error enclosure below the enclosed return floor's half.
Gates: domain-setup checks only (these are calculus constants, not
functionals; the spec's gate discipline applies to C1/C3).

## Target C5 - the one-signed projector check (unblocks section 3's
Stage-1 expectation)

Object: the spec's Stage-1 comparison - the true-projection triad
retention against the shell-model value on the compact family.
Required: one-signed (depletion helps); this is a CHECK, not a floor -
if it fails anywhere on the family, the affected fractions revert to
their shell-model values and the manuscript's named symbols absorb
the change without structural rework (the ratio theorem localizes the
impact). Gates: R and D.
