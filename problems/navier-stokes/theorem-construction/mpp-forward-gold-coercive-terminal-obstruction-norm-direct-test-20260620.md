# Coercive Terminal Obstruction Norm Direct Test

Date: 2026-06-20

Status: direct norm test; not proved as an independent supplier.  The test
identifies which quantities can serve as the coercive norm in the annular
no-escape / zero-readout branch.  Raw geometry, physical energy, and bare
stress flux are not sufficient.  A usable norm must be a selected same-fluid
terminal defect norm, and every current candidate reduces to the same
unweighted critical-action reserve, same-shadow source identity, or exact
profile-production theorem already isolated elsewhere.

```math

\tag{CTN.1}
```

This note tests the first factor: what can the norm

First, it must be nonzero on the produced terminal obstruction:

```math
|\mathcal Z|_Z\ge c_0>0.
\tag{CTN.2}
```

```math
\le

|B_{\rm ann}\mathcal Z|_Y+\mathcal D_{\rm legal}

\tag{CTN.3}
```

The dangerous false move is to choose a norm that is easy to control but does
not measure the terminal defect.

## 2. Raw geometry is not a terminal obstruction norm

The annular package contains geometric objects such as

```math
(T,\mu,n,h,\omega).
\tag{CTN.4}
```

Their mass or curvature cannot by itself be \(|\mathcal Z|_Z\).  A ghost
annulus with

```math
U=0,
\qquad
P=0,
\qquad
\Phi=0,
\qquad
\mu^{src}=0
\tag{CTN.5}
```

can still carry nonzero auxiliary current mass or artificial curvature if the
surface is not generated by the selected fluid packet with quantitative
nondegeneracy.  Conversely, opposite orientations can cancel signed current
while an unsigned positive carrier remains.

Thus raw current or varifold mass is a visibility support object, not the
terminal Navier--Stokes obstruction norm.

## 3. Physical energy is too small

A heat-scale critical packet at radius \(r\) has velocity size

```math
|u|\sim{\nu\over r}
\tag{CTN.6}
```

and local kinetic energy

```math
E_r\sim\nu^2 r\to0.
\tag{CTN.7}
```

So a terminal sequence can keep order-one normalized critical strength while
ordinary physical energy tends to zero.  A norm bounded by physical local
energy cannot satisfy `(CTN.2)` on the terminal heat-scale branch.

This is the same size obstruction as the energy-bounded no-waste Lyapunov
test.

The heat-scale invariant norm is the normalized critical action:


packet gives

```math

\tag{CTN.15}
```

But using `(CTN.14)` as the norm does not prove the gold theorem.  The

```math

\quad\text{or}\quad
\lim_{N\to\infty}\sum_{j\ge N}\mathcal A(Q_j)=0.
\tag{CTN.16}
```

```math
\text{UnweightedTerminalCriticalActionReserve.A}.
\tag{CTN.17}
```

```math
\qquad

\tag{CTN.18}
```
