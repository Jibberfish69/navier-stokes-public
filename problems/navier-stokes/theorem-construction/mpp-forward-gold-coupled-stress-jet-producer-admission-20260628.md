# Coupled Stress-Jet Producer Admission

Date: 2026-06-28

## 0. Result

The producer for the square-large, current-small selected child readout is the
same-parent stress-response time jet generated by the original material
Navier-Stokes law:

```math
D_tu=\nabla\cdot(-pI+2\nu S),
\qquad
\nabla\cdot u=0.
\tag{CSJ.1}
```

Pressure and viscosity are not separate producers.  Pressure is the constraint
part of the Cauchy stress; viscosity is the strain part of the same stress; and
incompressibility is the constraint that determines the admissible stress
response.  The immediate whole-field object is therefore a compatible
velocity-time jet, not kinetic energy.  Kinetic energy and selected action are
quadratic Gram readouts of that jet.

This closes the producer/admission identity:

```math
\text{selected action}
\quad
=
\quad
\text{Gram readout of an admitted same-parent stress-response jet coordinate}.
\tag{CSJ.2}
```

It does not close the Gold route by itself.  The remaining theorem is still a
terminal-uniform \(L^1\) full-packet service storage bound for this same jet
service measure, dominating the terminal endpoint measure.

## 1. Material stress-response jet

Let \(X(a,t)\) be a stopped material chart and put

```math
v(a,t)=u(X(a,t),t),
\qquad
q(a,t)=p(X(a,t),t),
\qquad
A=(D_aX)^{-1},
\qquad
G=AA^\top .
\tag{CSJ.3}
```

The same packet satisfies the material law

```math
\partial_t v+A^\top\nabla_a q
=
\nu\,\operatorname{div}_a(G\nabla_a v),
\qquad
\operatorname{div}_a(Av)=0.
\tag{CSJ.4}
```

Equivalently,

```math
\partial_t v=\operatorname{Div}_A T_A,
\qquad
D_A\cdot v=0,
\tag{CSJ.5}
```

where \(T_A\) denotes the pulled-back Cauchy stress
\(-qI+2\nu S\) expressed on the material chart.  The exact tensor convention is
the one used by the parent material traction current; only the identity
``force = material divergence of Cauchy stress'' is used here.

For fixed depth \(N\), define the material stress-response jet

```math
Y_N(t)
:=
\big(
\partial_t^k v,\,
\partial_t^k q,\,
\partial_t^k A,\,
\partial_t^k G
\big)_{0\le k\le N}.
\tag{CSJ.6}
```

Because the prelimit solution and stopped chart are smooth, differentiating
`(CSJ.4)` finitely many times gives a closed finite-depth recursion

```math
\partial_tY_N
=
\mathcal J_N(Y_{N+2}),
\tag{CSJ.7}
```

where \(\mathcal J_N\) is generated only by `(CSJ.4)`, the coefficient identities

```math
\partial_tA=-A(\nabla_av)A,
\qquad
\partial_tG=(\partial_tA)A^\top+A(\partial_tA)^\top,
\tag{CSJ.8}
```

and the differentiated pressure/incompressibility elliptic equation.  Thus the
pressure tower is not an extra account.  It is the Lagrange-multiplier part of
the same finite material jet.

## 2. Pre-energy Gram tower

At a fixed material point or against any fixed parent-admissible linear detector
\(\ell\), write

```math
z(t)=\ell(v(t)),
\qquad
z_k=\partial_t^k z(t_0).
\tag{CSJ.9}
```

If \(v(t_0)=0\), then velocity begins with the compatible acceleration rung
\(z_1\).  The corresponding energy/action readout is downstream:

```math
{1\over2}|z(t)|^2
=
{1\over2}
\sum_{i,j\ge1}
{(t-t_0)^{i+j}\over i!\,j!}\,
z_i z_j .
\tag{CSJ.10}
```

Equivalently, for \(n\ge2\),

```math
\partial_t^n\!\left({1\over2}|z|^2\right)(t_0)
=
{1\over2}
\sum_{i+j=n}
{n!\over i!\,j!}\,
z_i z_j .
\tag{CSJ.11}
```

For the pointwise kinetic energy, replace \(z_i z_j\) by
\(\partial_t^iv(t_0)\cdot \partial_t^jv(t_0)\).  If the selected detector moves
with the stopped route, the same formula holds with the additional terms
coming from \(\partial_t^k\ell\); those terms are exactly route, frame, collar,
legal, stop, or return terms in the parent packet.  They are not a new child
producer.

Thus the first nonzero object in a build-from-rest experiment is not energy.
It is the compatible acceleration coefficient in \(Y_N\).  Energy is the Gram
trace of that coefficient and the later jerk/snap/etc. coefficients.

## 3. Parabolic zoom is coefficient transport, not new production

On a child scale \(r\) with

```math
y={x-x_0\over r},
\qquad
s={\nu(t-t_0)\over r^2},
\qquad
U(y,s)=r\,u(x_0+ry,t_0+r^2s/\nu),
\tag{CSJ.12}
```

the velocity-time jet transforms by the chain rule:

```math
\partial_s^kU(y,0)
=
r\left({r^2\over\nu}\right)^k
\partial_t^ku(x_0+ry,t_0).
\tag{CSJ.13}
```

Similarly \(P=r^2p\) gives

```math
\partial_s^kP(y,0)
=
r^2\left({r^2\over\nu}\right)^k
\partial_t^kp(x_0+ry,t_0).
\tag{CSJ.14}
```

Therefore an order-one child coefficient is a rescaled coefficient of the same
parent material stress-response jet.  The zoom can magnify a tiny far-tail
coefficient, but it does not create a second producer.  Before the child may
count a large selected square, its detector must be admitted as a bounded
original-packet coordinate of \(Y_N\).

## 4. Parent admission before squaring

Let \(H_P\) be the Hilbert packet containing the parent stress-response jet
coordinates at the stopped history.  A lawful selected child readout has the
form

```math
z_Q(t)=\langle Y_N(t),a_Q(t)\rangle_{H_P},
\qquad
\|a_Q(t)\|_{H_P}\le C_N,
\tag{CSJ.15}
```

after all pressure-Hodge, cutoff, collar, material-frame, low/high routing,
viscosity, legal, stop, and endpoint defects have been placed in the same
identity.  For high-high transfer, \(a_Q\) is the full output-local stress
detector after input colors recombine; an individual input-colored triad is not
a one-point parent-current read unless a separate two-point product-current
theory is deliberately installed.

Let \(E_{i-1}\) be the parent-predictable span of inherited directions and
earlier admitted fresh directions.  Split

```math
a_i=b_i+d_i,
\qquad
b_i=\Pi_{E_{i-1}}a_i,
\qquad
d_i=(I-\Pi_{E_{i-1}})a_i.
\tag{CSJ.16}
```

The inherited part \(b_i\) is Schur current, route/motion, return, legal, stop,
viscous service, or endpoint material.  It is not fresh child production.  The
fresh part \(d_i\) is the only new parent coordinate.  If \(d_i\ne0\), set
\(e_i=d_i/\|d_i\|_{H_P}\).  By construction the \(e_i\) are orthonormal over a
stopped Gram-Schmidt family, so Bessel gives

```math
\sum_i
|\langle Y_N,e_i\rangle_{H_P}|^2
\le
\|Y_N\|_{H_P}^2 .
\tag{CSJ.17}
```

This proves the no-free-zoom admission rule for the producer:

```math
\sum_i
|\langle Y_N,d_i\rangle_{H_P}|^2
\le
\|Y_N\|_{H_P}^2
\sup_i\|d_i\|_{H_P}^2,
\tag{CSJ.18}
```

with the harmless normalization absorbed into the selected-action weights.  A
large child square is legal only after it is one of these admitted coordinates
or after it lands in the inherited/route/return/legal/stop/viscous/endpoint
categories.

## 5. Proof of the closure claim

The proof is finite-depth algebra.

First, `(CSJ.4)` is the material form of the original Navier-Stokes law.  The
pressure equation is obtained by differentiating the incompressibility
constraint and solving the material elliptic pressure equation on the same
chart.  Hence each pressure coefficient in \(Y_N\) is determined by the same
velocity and coefficient towers; no decoupled \(\mathbb P\)-carrier is used.

Second, `(CSJ.10)` and `(CSJ.11)` are the Taylor product rule applied to
\({1\over2}|z|^2\).  They show that energy/action is a quadratic readout of the
velocity-time jet.  The instantaneous whole-field response is the first
compatible jet coefficient, not a transported energy particle.

Third, `(CSJ.13)` and `(CSJ.14)` are the chain rule under parabolic scaling.
Thus child magnitude is a rescaled parent coefficient.  A zoomed coefficient
cannot be counted as child production until its detector is admitted as a
bounded parent-packet functional.

Fourth, once the detector is admitted, `(CSJ.16)` separates inherited current
from fresh parent-subtracted novelty.  Inherited current is already one of the
parent payment categories.  Fresh novelty Bessel-packs by `(CSJ.17)`.  Therefore
positive clipping and squaring are lawful only after same-parent jet admission.
That is exactly the missing producer/admission identity.

## 6. What remains open

This theorem supplies the producer and closes the decoupled-participation
oversight.  It does not supply the final Gold storage bound.

The remaining required theorem is:

```math
d\mathcal S_N^{jet}
\le
\sigma_N(t)\,dt
dR^{legal}
dStop,
\qquad
\sup_{\tau<T_*}
\int_0^\tau \sigma_N(t)\,dt<\infty,
\tag{CSJ.19}
```

with \(\sigma_N\,dt\) dominating the terminal endpoint measure of the same
parent-known full packet.  Under `(CSJ.19)`, terminal no-jump follows by
absolute continuity.  Without `(CSJ.19)`, this note is a closed producer theorem
and an admission theorem, not a Gold smoothness closure.
