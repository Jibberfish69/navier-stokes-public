# Current active-capacity/full-exchange physical story

## 1. One physical packet

The object is one stopped parent packet in one Navier-Stokes velocity field,
before child positive clipping. Transport moves the packet. Pressure acts as a
constraint force and redistributes mass inside the same parent time slice.
Incompressibility keeps the parent volume/frame coherent. Viscosity and the
Stokes/Fisher clock give the parabolic capacity that can absorb active
potential. Legal, stop, collar, route, endpoint, and paid reselection terms are
removed into the parent error measure \(dE_P^0\).

The raw pressure-Hodge atom theorem is already a same-parent statement. After
the paid pieces are removed, the signed pressure residue has zero time
marginal, so its positive and negative lobes can be coupled at the same parent
time. The retained opposite lobe is a bounded-below reserve from the original
data.

The Gold problem begins after the critical meter \(W_P^{crit}=e^{b_P}\) prices
the two already paired lobes differently. The selected lobe can be weighted
more heavily than its retained partner. The surplus is not new physical mass;
it is the positive motion of the selected critical meter along the same parent
history.
