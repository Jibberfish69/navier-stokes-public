# Custody: the impulse ledger and the re-assembly threshold

## 1. What must be re-assembled

Production needs anti-parallel geometry at separation d (pair cell,
measured). Reconnection destroys it and redistributes the impulse onto
bridges and threads. The custody question: can the enemy re-form the
producing geometry without paying?

## 2. The transit clock (exact structure)

Impulse is conserved and localized on the debris. Re-concentration is
a transport process through the participating medium: distance ~ d at
speeds ~ Gamma/(2 pi d), i.e. one strain time 1/s. There is no faster
channel: the field's own velocities bound the transit.

## 3. The debris clock and the threshold

Thin debris (radius a_t) dies on a_t^2/nu. Lifetime over transit:

    (a_t^2/nu) * s = (a_t/d)^2 * Gamma/(2 pi nu).

Below the threshold Re_Gamma ~ (d/a_t)^2 the debris cannot survive its
own re-assembly journey. Viscosity is the tether AND the confiscator:
the penny-tax collects precisely on the enemy's logistics.

## 4. Named open routes (the gate)

(G-C-1) Thick-debris route: same-day display filed. Isolated thick
debris cannot re-concentrate fixed impulse because concentration raises
energy while unforced viscous flow dissipates. Assisted thick debris
falls under G-C-2.

(G-C-2) Payment identity: same-day display filed. Fast thick transit
through the medium is an increment-cubic event in the flux-gate
total-variation currency. Remaining: orient that payment on the same
channel-window summands as the record rows (composition, item 5).
