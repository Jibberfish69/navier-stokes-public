# Custody row lift reduces to Gate-8 allocation

## Codex gate, same date

Codex gate filed:
`mpp-forward-gold-codex-custody-row-lift-gate-20260705`.

The reduction is accepted: delivery-branch custody payment is already
scale-\(d\) increment-cubic channel-cell throughput, so custody adds no
fourth local composition row once Gate 8 supplies same-index allocation
of admission, no-recount debt, and tax. The escrow branch remains live:
stored energy must localize as prior paid residue, entrance reserve,
routed tail, or Silver mass. No custody theorem or confinement constant
decision is claimed.

## 1. Row-lift question

The local custody displays pay two branches:

1. thin debris is confiscated below the quadratic Reynolds threshold;
2. thick delivery pays increment-cubic total variation at scale \(d\).

The remaining row question is whether that payment lives on the same
channel-window summands as record admission, payer tax, and no-recount
debt.

## 2. Delivery branch

The payment identity gives, on a thick scale-\(d\) channel cell \(Q\),

\[
  \mathcal C_d(Q)
  \simeq
  \int_Q \frac{|\delta_d u|^3}{d}\,dx\,dt
  \gtrsim c\,C^{-2}U^2d^3 .
\]

This is not an extra ledger currency. It is the same increment-cubic
throughput used by the flux gate and the channel-cell row theorem.
For a channel cell \(a=(c,Q)\), write the prelimit throughput measure
as \(\mu_a=|\nu_a|\). The current orientation part is already paid by
Hahn-Jordan splitting. The abstract row-composition lemma is already
paid once admission, debt, and tax are Radon-Nikodym submeasures of
\(\mu_a\).

Therefore the delivery branch of custody does not create a fourth
composition row. It reduces to the existing Gate-8 allocation theorem:
same-packet identification, flux-gate/tail transcription, allocated
tax density, no-recount or entrance reserve, E3 tail routing, and
allocation closedness.

## 3. Escrow branch

The regated thick-debris theorem says that stored energy is not free
reserve. If thick debris already contains the scale-\(d\) energy needed
to re-form a producing pair, that energy must be typed as one of:

1. prior paid same-packet residue;
2. entrance reserve for the same channel-window economy;
3. routed tail or Silver-side mass.

If the escrow cannot be localized into one of those rows, the open
problem is escrow localization or no-side routing. It is not a new
local reassembly mechanism.

## 4. Reduction formula

\[
\text{thin confiscation}
\quad+\quad
\text{energy escrow dichotomy}
\quad+\quad
\text{fast-delivery cubic payment}
\quad+\quad
\text{Gate-8 allocation/localization}.
\]

That is the current custody state. The local escape has no unpriced
fourth channel, but the composition theorem still needs the existing
Gate-8 PDE allocation facts and the escrow localization facts.

## 5. Four-sentence result

The fast-delivery custody payment is already in channel-cell
increment-cubic throughput currency. Once Gate 8 allocates admission,
debt, and tax as submeasures of that throughput, there is no additional
row-composition problem specific to custody. The only escrow branch is
stored energy, and stored energy must be localized as prior paid
residue, entrance reserve, routed tail, or Silver mass. So item 3 has
been reduced to escrow localization plus the already-open Gate-8
allocation theorem; it has not been closed.
