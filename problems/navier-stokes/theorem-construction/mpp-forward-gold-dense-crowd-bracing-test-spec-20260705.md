# Dense crowd, first cell: the four-tube bracing test

## 1. The danger being tested

Dilute crowds are quartically quiet (monopole vanishing). The dense
danger is different in kind: not interference but AID. A neighbor at
~ d supplies order-one strain that could hold a producing pair's
geometry against its own self-eviction. If members can brace each
other cheaper than the tax, the crowd defeats the floor pairwise and
scales up.

## 2. Why bracing should fail (the story's prediction)

The reaction identity: transport and pressure redistribute, never
create; the brace is transmitted through the same coupling that
back-reacts on the bracer. Newton's third law inside the fluid. The
prediction to test: total eviction of the braced four-tube system
stays at or above the pair floor times total production.

## 3. The test, exactly

Two anti-parallel pairs, all four tubes at mutual distances ~ d,
leading filament kernel (same as the measured cell). Display items
D4b-1 through D4b-4 in the frontmatter. The optimization domain is
compact and the coefficients are exact: this is a finite theory
computation, not certification.

## 4. Stakes

Floor survives: the first dense cell closes; bracing is taxed by
back-reaction; item 4b proceeds to triads-of-pairs and lattice
configurations with a template.

Floor fails: first evasion cell found; the construction iterates
toward the broadband counterexample; the program heads to exit (ii)
with a real object in hand.
