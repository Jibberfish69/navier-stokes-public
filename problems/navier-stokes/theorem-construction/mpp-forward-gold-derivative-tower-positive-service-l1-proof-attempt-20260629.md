# Derivative tower positive-service \(L^1\) proof attempt

The target is the terminal-uniform positive service estimate for the coupled
derivative-energy tower:

\[
\sup_{\tau<T_*}
\int_0^\tau\int_P
\mathcal M_N(Y_N)_+\,da\,dt
\le
C_N(u_0).
\tag{DTL.1}
\]

Here \(Y_N\) is the root-fixed material version of the rescaled mixed derivative
tower:

\[
Y_N
=
\big(
\partial_t^k v,\,
\partial_t^k q,\,
\partial_t^k A,\,
\partial_t^k G
\big)_{0\le k\le N}.
\tag{DTL.2}
\]

The point of this attempt is to test whether `(DTL.1)` follows from the tower
energy identity itself.

## 1. Tower identity

On a stopped material packet \(P\), the material Navier--Stokes law is

\[
\partial_t v+A^\top\nabla_a q
=
\nu\,\operatorname{div}_a(G\nabla_a v),
\qquad
\operatorname{div}_a(Av)=0.
\tag{DTL.3}
\]

After \(k\) time differentiations,

\[
\partial_t^{k+1}v
+A^\top\nabla_a\partial_t^k q
-\nu\,\operatorname{div}_a(G\nabla_a\partial_t^k v)
=
\mathcal C_k(Y_k,\nabla Y_k,\nabla^2Y_{k-1}),
\tag{DTL.4}
\]

where \(\mathcal C_k\) contains only commutators generated by differentiating
\(A,G\), the material pressure operator, and the collar/frame terms.  The
pressure rung is tied to the same packet by the differentiated elliptic
constraint

\[
-\partial_{a_\ell}(G_{\ell m}\partial_{a_m}\partial_t^k q)
=
\mathcal R_k(Y_k,\nabla Y_k).
\tag{DTL.5}
\]

Thus no term in the tower is a decoupled pressure or projection account.
Everything is produced by the same pressure-viscosity-incompressibility packet.

Let

\[
\mathcal E_N(t)
=
\sum_{k\le N}\alpha_k
\|\partial_t^k v(t)\|_{L^2(P)}^2
+\mathcal E_N^{frame/collar}(A,G,q),
\tag{DTL.6}
\]

with the usual positive frame/collar terms needed to keep the material Hodge and
pressure graph on the same packet.  Testing `(DTL.4)` against
\(\alpha_k\partial_t^k v\), summing \(k\le N\), and integrating by parts gives
the exact schematic tower balance

\[
{d\over dt}\mathcal E_N(t)
+c\nu\|Y_N(t)\|_{\dot H^1(P)}^2
\le
\mathcal I_N(Y_N)(t)
+dR_P^{legal}
+dStop_P,
\tag{DTL.7}
\]

where \(\mathcal I_N\) is the same-packet pressure/coefficient/collar/nonlinear
service generated by the differentiated tower.

This proves the physical attachment of the service.  It does not yet prove
positive-service \(L^1\).

## 2. The needed accretive line

To prove `(DTL.1)` from `(DTL.7)`, one needs

\[
\mathcal M_N(Y_N)_+
\le
\theta\nu\|Y_N\|_{\dot H^1(P)}^2
+C_N(u_0)
+\partial_t\mathcal B_P
+dR_P^{legal}
+dStop_P,
\qquad
\theta<1,
\tag{DTL.8}
\]

with \(\mathcal B_P\) bounded below on the same root-fixed parent packet.
Integrating `(DTL.8)` gives `(DTL.1)`.

The direct tower identity proves the left-hand object in `(DTL.8)` is real
same-packet service.  The remaining question is whether the top-order tower
algebra gives the strict \(\theta<1\) absorption.

## 3. Direct top-order estimate

The dangerous terms in \(\mathcal I_N\) have the standard critical form

\[
\int_P
\nabla Y_{\le N}\,Y_{\le N}\,\nabla Y_N
\quad\text{or}\quad
\int_P
(\nabla A,\nabla G,\nabla q)_{\le N}\,Y_{\le N}\,\nabla Y_N ,
\tag{DTL.9}
\]

after pressure has been returned to the elliptic same-packet graph.  Sobolev,
paraproduct, and Calderon--Zygmund estimates give

\[
|\mathcal I_N(Y_N)|
\le
C_N
\|Y_N\|_{X_{\rm crit}(P)}
\|Y_N\|_{\dot H^1(P)}^2
+dR_P^{legal}
+dStop_P,
\tag{DTL.10}
\]

where \(X_{\rm crit}\) is a scale-critical tower norm.  This is the sharp
large-data estimate produced by the tower identity.

Young absorption gives

\[
|\mathcal I_N(Y_N)|
\le
\varepsilon\nu\|Y_N\|_{\dot H^1(P)}^2
+
C_{\varepsilon,N,\nu}
\|Y_N\|_{X_{\rm crit}(P)}^2
\|Y_N\|_{\dot H^1(P)}^2
+dR_P^{legal}
+dStop_P.
\tag{DTL.11}
\]

The second term on the right is still continuation-strength.  Original energy
does not bound \(\|Y_N\|_{X_{\rm crit}}\), and the desired selected-action or
service bound is exactly what would be needed to control it.  Therefore
`(DTL.10)`--`(DTL.11)` do not imply `(DTL.8)`.

## 4. Why the natural storages do not repair the sign

The first natural storage is \(-\mathcal E_N\).  From `(DTL.7)`,

\[
d(-\mathcal E_N)
+\mathcal I_N^+\,dt
\le
\nu\|Y_N\|_{\dot H^1(P)}^2\,dt
+\mathcal I_N^-\,dt
+dR_P^{legal}
+dStop_P.
\tag{DTL.12}
\]

This pays positive service only after the negative return/service variation is
already controlled.  It is the signed-to-rectified BV problem again.

The logarithmic storage gives

\[
d\log(1+\mathcal E_N)
\lesssim
\|Y_N\|_{X_{\rm crit}(P)}
{\|Y_N\|_{\dot H^1(P)}^2\over 1+\mathcal E_N}
+dR_P^{legal}
+dStop_P.
\tag{DTL.13}
\]

This is a continuation criterion after a finite service clock is known.  It
does not put \(\mathcal M_N(Y_N)_+\) on the left with a positive coefficient.

The material service-variation calculation differentiates the signed service
itself and proves the variation density is a finite expression of the same
packet:

\[
\operatorname{Var}_I(a_\psi)
\le
\int_I\mathcal V_\psi(t)\,dt .
\tag{DTL.14}
\]

But \(\mathcal V_\psi\) consists of pressure-time, pressure-second-time,
coefficient deformation, viscous higher-tower, collar, and cubic/quartic
strain-vorticity terms.  This is exactly the full tower service.  The calculation
admits the rectification cost to the same packet; it does not make that cost
finite from energy.

## 5. Scale test

The obstruction is scale-critical.  A normalized heat packet of radius \(r\)
can have physical energy or fixed-annulus cost tending to zero with \(r\), while
its normalized selected service remains order one on its own unit heat window.
The tower identity sees the packet correctly after rescaling, but summing such
unit-window positive service over a terminal Zeno chain requires a square/no-waste
or accretive storage theorem.

This scale test is not a construction of a Navier--Stokes counterexample.  It is
a proof test: energy, fixed-annulus \(L^1\), local tower identities, and
same-packet admission do not contain the missing summability mechanism.

## 6. Verdict

The derivative-energy tower proves:

\[
\text{positive service is generated by the original coupled tower.}
\tag{DTL.15}
\]

It does not prove:

\[
\sup_{\tau<T_*}
\int_0^\tau\int_P
\mathcal M_N(Y_N)_+\,da\,dt
\le
C_N(u_0).
\tag{DTL.16}
\]

The failed line is the strict accretive service inequality `(DTL.8)`.  Proving
that line would prove Gold smoothness through the already installed downstream
relay.  The tower identity alone gives `(DTL.10)`, whose coefficient is the
uncontrolled scale-critical tower size.

Partial, not Gold closed.
