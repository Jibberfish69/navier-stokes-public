# Dwell-time via twist shear

1. Alignment = resonance. M-fold focusing locks M-1 relative phases;
dwelling for time tau requires all pairwise frequency differences to stay
below epsilon/tau.

2. Twist. Triad frequencies are amplitude-linear (quadratic nonlinearity);
the coupling coefficient IS the interaction coefficient. On the marginal
line amplitudes evolve on the turnover clock, so frequencies drift at rate
~ twist x (amplitude motion) ~ 1/turnover.

3. Shear eviction. Nonzero twist implies phase differences sweep epsilon in
time ~ epsilon x turnover: dwell << the O(1) turnover per rung the relay
needs. Pressure's role: the isentropic rotation that keeps amplitude motion
feeding phase drift - rotates accessibility, evicts organization.

4. Degenerate refuge and induction. Twist can vanish only on a lower-
dimensional locus; holding the orbit there spends a fresh constraint;
finite truncation has finitely many. Either shear evicts, or constraints
exhaust: total dwell bounded either way.

5. Open burdens: (a) twist floor - certified lower bound on frequency-
amplitude coupling over the compact marginal family (Stage-2-type
computation); (b) uniformity of the constraint-exhaustion induction along
an infinite ladder (per-octave form, matching the toll law).
