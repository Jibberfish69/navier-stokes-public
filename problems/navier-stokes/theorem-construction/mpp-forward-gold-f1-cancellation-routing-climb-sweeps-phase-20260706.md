## P1. Forward cancellation is attenuation (definitional)

Financing is monomial currency at the relay slot by definition:
\(d\mathfrak e = \rho_{\rm ext}X\,dt = -d_{\rm pkg}\,2\operatorname{Re}M\,dt\),
and the financed family is defined by positive drain throughput. A
history whose forward monomial cancels has zero drain. It is not
financed; it is priced by the inactive-time charges; and it cannot
be a ladder increment, because arrival at the next band IS transfer
and transfer IS monomial mass. Such a history does not climb — under
the pincer it attenuates. Forward cancellation needs no
participation theorem. It is the pincer's second jaw.
