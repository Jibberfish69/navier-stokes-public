The W1 packet decomposition already divides band-\(\mu\) mass into
coherent packets, disjoint siblings, and DIFFUSE REMAINDER — and the
diffuse remainder is priced: per-volume amplitude down by
\(N^{-1/2}\), local Reynolds below marginal, payer 1 collects at
saturated rate.

**Definition.** The CENTERED packet is the band-\(\mu\) increment's
mass within \(\kappa_{\rm frame}\) correlation volumes of its core;
the far tail is re-classified as diffuse remainder.

Then \(s(\kappa) = 0\) for \(\kappa > \kappa_{\rm frame}\):
UniformCenteredPacketTightness holds by construction, and the
two-dial closure runs with only the \(\theta\) dial.

**The definition's price** (both at installed homes; displays owed):

2. **(x2)** the tail's trilinear forcing on the centered monomial is
   priced by the certified locality constants (\(C_{\rm loc} \le 9\),
   geometric in separation) at the Section-7(ii) home — the spatial
   analogue of the bad-cone capacity, entering the fork's ledger as
   a separate small term with the same margin bookkeeping.
