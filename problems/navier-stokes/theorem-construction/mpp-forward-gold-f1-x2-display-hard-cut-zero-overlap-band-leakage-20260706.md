# The x2 display: hard cut gives zero overlap; the leakage is the paid locality row

## S1. Zero overlap (exact)

With the centered packet defined by a hard spatial cut at
\(\kappa_{\rm frame}\) correlation volumes, and the chain's packets
co-located within the window (flag x2a), the tail leg's support is
disjoint from the centered partners' supports:

\[
  \int (\text{tail})\,(b_1^{\rm ctr})\,(b_2^{\rm ctr})\,dx = 0
\]

identically — three fields multiply pointwise, and where one is
zero the product is zero. The overlap part of the tail capacity is
not small; it is zero.

## S2. The true cost is spectral — and already priced

The hard cut breaks exact band limitation: the centered leg rings
in frequency. That out-of-band leakage is priced by the PAID ladder
locality row (front-payments note): UV contribution
\(\sim (\mu l)^{-1}\), IR \(\sim (\mu l)^2\), dyadically summable,
channel-resolved. The leakage enters the fork's ledger as the
(1,2)-exponent locality charge — an installed line, not a new
constant.

## S3. Consistency with the Bernstein step

The Bernstein slab bound uses only spatial support — exact after
the hard cut. The annulus geometry applies to the in-band
component; the out-of-band part rides S2's charge in the same
margin bookkeeping as \({\rm Cap}_B\).

## Gaps (honest)

(x2a — load-bearing) co-location of the chain's packet cores
within the same-fluid window: the story says the handoff is local
(nested cores); the frame home must confirm; if not, S1 becomes a
separation-decay estimate and the burden returns. (x2b) the
leakage display in the fork's normalization — the exponents are
paid at scaling level; certification is the "bounded computation"
of the front-payments note. (x2c) x1 remains owed at the
Section-7(iii) home. (x2d) hard versus smooth cut is Codex's to
gate — either lands in the same two priced channels.
