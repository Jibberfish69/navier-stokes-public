\(\kappa_c>0\) scalar. A signed measure and its positive scalar

channel by channel and cell by cell, the nonvanishing sets defining
the two support readouts coincide, and

\[
  \bar\kappa_{\rm SRP}
  =

  \qquad\text{identically}
\]

\(\{\bar\kappa<L-\eta\}\) each channel with \(\kappa_c\ge L-\eta\)
