# F3/P1 routing execution: paid coins into paid drawers

## 0. The remainder, read once

Per the gated record-demand collapse, the transcription remainder
(families of \(dE^{\rm trans}\)) and the record residue
\((\mathcal R^L_{\rm out})_+\) are the same remainder read twice. This
note routes it once, family by family, into the terminal channels the
formal layer already built and paid: boundary/locality, weighted
tail, Silver/finite-readout, and the licensed locality edge.

## 1. E-b: window-gate pressure (citation)

Family (b) is the gate term \(\langle p,\nabla\chi\cdot\Lambda
P_cu\rangle\). Terminal channel: the licensed doubled-cell locality
edge plus the cube-weighted routed tail, with exact moments
\((1/7,\ 8/49)\) - gated in the pressure-allocation note. Nothing to
add; the transcription inherits it verbatim.

## 2. E-c: cutoff/band commutator (executed)

The kernel of \([\chi_Q,\widetilde P_c]\) is
\(K_c(x-y)(\chi(y)-\chi(x))\) with \(K_c\) band-limited and smooth,
hence carrying ALL moments:

\[
  \int_{|z|\ge R}|K_c(z)||z|\,dz
  \le
  C_N\,(\kappa_cR)^{-N}\cdot\kappa_c^{-1}
  \qquad\text{for every }N .
\]

So the commutator's mass at distance \(2^mr\) from the gate annulus
decays like \(C_N(\kappa_c2^mr)^{-N}\). Take the \(N=4\) dominating
envelope (one power above the pressure kernel's cube): dyadic weights
\(w_m=2^{-4m}\), with

\[
  \sum_{m\ge1}2^{-4m}
  =
  \frac{1/16}{1-1/16}
  =
  \frac1{15},
  \qquad
  \sum_{m\ge1}m\,2^{-4m}
  =
  \frac{1/16}{(1-1/16)^2}
  =
  \frac{16}{225}
\]

- exact fractions. Acceptance: the on-annulus part (the
\(\kappa\)-free bill \(\le(2C_{K1}N_{\rm band}/r)\times\)cell \(L^3\)
mass, displayed in the declaration note) is boundary/locality mass on
the gate annulus - the annulus later-debt drawer; the off-annulus
reach enters the weighted first-moment routing with the displayed
moments. Both paid acceptance tests met; no dichotomy branch needed.

## 3. E-d, E-e: mollifier and pair families (executed)

Target-1 displays R-a (curl-mollifier commutator increments) and R-b
(off-diagonal channel-pair increments) as local-variation bounds with
GEOMETRIC BAND TAILS. Feed band-distance ratio \(1/2\) to the weighted
first-moment dichotomy:

\[
  \sum_{k\ge1}2^{-k}=1,
  \qquad
  \sum_{k\ge1}k\,2^{-k}=2
\]

- exact integers. Both families land on the dichotomy's PAYING
branch: first moments finite by decay alone, the failure branch never
entered. Terminal: the weighted tail channel, in the same currency
target-1 priced them.

## 4. E-f: the complement, split three ways

paid finite-readout/Silver routing (measure-shell exhaustion,
corrected chapter-2 form). Citation; unchanged.

(f2) OFF-RETAINED cells: terminal = the paid no-side-economy
reduction - unallocated mass IS tail/boundary/Silver until
explicitly allocated, and cannot back tax or become reserve.
Citation; unchanged.

(f3) REPLACEMENT tails: VOID UNDER THE DECLARATION. The projection
note lists this family as arising "if one rewrites
\(p_c^{\rm SRP}\) in increment-stress form." The declared row
performs no such rewrite - the row IS the production density in the
band's own hand. The family is empty: removed by the same
declaration that voided family (a), not estimated.
