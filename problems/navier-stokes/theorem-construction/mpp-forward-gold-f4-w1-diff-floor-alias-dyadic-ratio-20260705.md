# F4/W1: the rungs are an octave apart - DIFF^- was always kappa_min

## 1. The alias

The gated F1 corollary states, of the financed relay family: the band
ratios are EXACT POWERS OF TWO. This is frozen and gated - a property
the family carries, not an assumption made here.

For same-helicity legs \(\kappa_2<\kappa_3\) of a financed triad,
therefore \(\kappa_3\ge2\kappa_2\), and

\[
  \big|s_2\kappa_2-s_3\kappa_3\big|
  =
  \kappa_3-\kappa_2
  \ \ge\
  \kappa_2
  \ \ge\
  \kappa_{\min}.
\]

So

\[
  \boxed{\ {\rm DIFF}^-:=\kappa_{\min}^-\ }
\]

- an alias for the band floor already on the frozen sheet. No freeze
reopening. The sector citations are not spent: as the gate noted,
they clear \(k\le q\) and the zero-set exclusion, and they are not
needed for this floor.

## 2. The enclosure closes

\(\min(2\kappa_{\min}^-,{\rm DIFF}^-)=\kappa_{\min}^-\), hence

\[
  \boxed{\ \rho^+=\frac{\Sigma_{\max}^+}{2\,\kappa_{\min}^-}\ }
\]

covering both helicity branches (opposite: \(\ge2\kappa_{\min}\),
previously accepted; same: \(\ge\kappa_{\min}\), the alias). The
enclosure spends NO new symbol - the same \(\kappa_{\min}^-\) and
\(\Sigma_{\max}^+\) the angle chain already requires, so W1's symbol
count does not grow.
