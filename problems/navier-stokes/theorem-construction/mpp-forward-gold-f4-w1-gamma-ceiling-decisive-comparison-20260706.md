increasing on \([0,\pi/2]\); hence

\[
  \Gamma^-\ \le\ \Gamma_\angle(\pi/2)
  =
  \frac{\sqrt2/2}{1+\sqrt2}
  =
  \boxed{\ \frac{2-\sqrt2}{2}\ }
\]

- exact, input-independent.
