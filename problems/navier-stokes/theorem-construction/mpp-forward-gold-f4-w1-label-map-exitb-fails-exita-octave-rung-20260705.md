# F4/W1: the map says parent-and-child; the easy exit closes; the rung is the question

## 1. L1: the label map

The return-race convention: \(\kappa_1<\kappa_2<\kappa_3\); the
physical relay: catalyst \(k<\) parent \(p<\) child \(q\). The race's
returning mode is mode 1 - the one PAID BACK, which is what catalyst
means - and \(X=|a_3|^2\) is the transferred package, the child's
gain. The only convention-consistent map is order-preserving:

\[
  \Lambda_{\rm W1}(1)=k,\qquad
  \Lambda_{\rm W1}(2)=p,\qquad
  \Lambda_{\rm W1}(3)=q .
\]

## 2. L2: the check - FALSE (honest negative)

On the pinned catalyst-flipped relay branch \(k(-),p(+),q(+)\):

\[
  s_{\Lambda_{\rm W1}(2)}\,s_{\Lambda_{\rm W1}(3)}
  =
  s_p\,s_q
  =
  (+)(+)
  =
  +1
  \ \ne\ -1 .
\]

EXIT B FAILS on the financed relay branch. The pinned
opposite-helicity pair \(k(-),q(+)\) is the \((1,3)\) pair - not the
denominator pair. Recorded as a negative result: it forecloses a
false Exit-B clearance.

## 3. L3: Exit A - the surviving route, conditional on the rung

The active floor is \(q-p\ge\eta_\kappa^-\kappa_{\min}\). Triangle
geometry alone gives NO lower bound:
\((q-p)^2=k^2-2pq(1-\cos A_k)\) can vanish at isoceles triads even
with the angle floor. The floor must come from the LADDER FRAME: the
five-mode chain marches rung to rung (the child of one triad is the
parent of the next). IF the frame pins parent and child to DISTINCT
OCTAVE RUNGS - the reading the octave-toll note's own structure
suggests (its toll is computed per octave branch, presupposing the
rung assignment) - then

\[
  q\ge2p
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  q-p\ge p\ge\kappa_{\min},
  \qquad
  \eta_\kappa^-=1,
\]

and per the conditional gate's display,

\[
  \rho^+
  =
  \frac{\Sigma_{\max}^+}{2\kappa_{\min}^-\min(2,1)}
  =
  \frac{\Sigma_{\max}^+}{2\kappa_{\min}^-}.
\]

CANDIDATE HOME for the rung pinning: the octave-toll closed-form
note and the ladder concentration frame of the return-race writeup
("the ladder increment at a band").

GUARD AND PROTOCOL: if the frame does NOT pin parent/child to
