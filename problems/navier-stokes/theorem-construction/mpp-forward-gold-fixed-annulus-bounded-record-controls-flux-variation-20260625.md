# Fixed Annulus Bounded Record Controls Flux Variation

Date: 2026-06-25

## 0. Claim

Let \(u,p\) be a smooth incompressible Navier--Stokes solution on a
preterminal interval \(I\), and let \(X(a,t)\) be the material map.  Fix one
material annular cutoff \(\psi(a)\).  Put

```math
v(a,t)=u(X(a,t),t),
\qquad
q(a,t)=p(X(a,t),t),
\qquad
A=(D_aX)^{-1},
\qquad
G=AA^\top .
\tag{FAV.1}
```

The transported annular stress-work flux is

```math
F_\psi(t)
=
\int
v_i
\left(-q\delta_{ij}+2\nu S_{ij}(u)\circ X\right)
A_{\ell j}\partial_{a_\ell}\psi\,da .
\tag{FAV.2}
```

Choose \(N\) high enough to dominate the continuation-depth material record
and the finite number of derivatives falling on `(FAV.2)`.  If

```math
\sup_{t\in I}\mathcal P_N^{mat}(t)\le K
\tag{FAV.3}
```

and the same-packet service density \(\mathcal S_N^{mat}\) from the material
tower is integrable,

```math
\int_I\mathcal S_N^{mat}(t)\,dt<\infty,
\tag{FAV.4}
```

then

```math
\operatorname{Var}_I F_\psi
=
\int_I |F_\psi'(t)|\,dt
\le
C_{\psi,N}(K)
\left(
|I|+\int_I\mathcal S_N^{mat}(t)\,dt
\right).
\tag{FAV.5}
```

Thus a fixed transported annulus with bounded same-material record has finite
rectified stress-work variation.

## 1. Exact derivative

Differentiate `(FAV.2)` on the fixed material domain.  With

```math
\mathcal T=-qI+2\nu S(u)\circ X,
\tag{FAV.6}
```

one has exactly

```math
\begin{aligned}
F_\psi'(t)
= {}&
\int
(\partial_tv_i)\mathcal T_{ij}A_{\ell j}\partial_\ell\psi\,da\\
&+
\int
v_i(\partial_t\mathcal T_{ij})A_{\ell j}\partial_\ell\psi\,da\\
&+
\int
v_i\mathcal T_{ij}(\partial_tA_{\ell j})\partial_\ell\psi\,da .
\end{aligned}
\tag{FAV.7}
```

The material equations are

```math
\partial_t v+A^\top\nabla_a q
=
\nu\,\operatorname{div}_a(G\nabla_av),
\qquad
\operatorname{div}_a(Av)=0,
\tag{FAV.8}
```

and

```math
\partial_tA=-A(\nabla_av)A .
\tag{FAV.9}
```

The pressure time derivative is not a free time signal.  It is obtained by
differentiating the same material pressure equation

```math
-
\partial_{a_\ell}
\left(
G_{\ell k}\partial_{a_k}q
\right)
=
A_{\ell i}A_{kj}
(\partial_{a_\ell}v_j)(\partial_{a_k}v_i).
\tag{FAV.10}
```

After the pressure gauge is fixed, elliptic estimates express
\(\partial_tq\) through the same velocity, coefficient, pressure, and
coefficient-time records.

Substituting `(FAV.8)`--`(FAV.10)` into `(FAV.7)` gives a finite sum of
same-packet products.  No pressure-only, viscosity-only, or detached
boundary-signal term appears.

## 2. Estimate by the same material record

The material record \(\mathcal P_N^{mat}\) controls the velocity tower,
pressure tower, coefficient/deformation tower, and transported collar geometry.
For \(N\) fixed above the continuation depth, Sobolev--Moser product estimates
on the fixed material chart give

```math
|F_\psi'(t)|
\le
C_{\psi,N}\!\left(\mathcal P_N^{mat}(t)\right)
\left(1+\mathcal S_N^{mat}(t)\right).
\tag{FAV.11}
```

Here \(\mathcal S_N^{mat}\) is the same service density generated by the
differentiated material Navier--Stokes tower: viscous tower terms, pressure
elliptic service, coefficient motion, and collar/interface service.  It is not
an additional object.

Under `(FAV.3)`, `(FAV.11)` becomes

```math
|F_\psi'(t)|
\le
C_{\psi,N}(K)
\left(1+\mathcal S_N^{mat}(t)\right).
\tag{FAV.12}
```

Integrating over \(I\) proves `(FAV.5)`.

## 3. Consequence for the live clock

This proves the fixed-annulus version of the physical claim:

```math
\text{bounded same-material record}
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\text{finite rectified annular force-action variation}.
\tag{FAV.13}
```

So an infinite amount of rectified annular force-action variation cannot be a
new independent oscillation of the outer boundary signal.  It must be one of
the same material quantities in the full clock:

```math
d[\log(1+\mathcal P_N^{mat})]_+,
\qquad
\mathcal S_N^{mat}(t)\,dt,
\qquad
\text{or retained collar/interface service}.
\tag{FAV.14}
```

For a shrinking heat-scale terminal family, the remaining theorem is therefore
sharper:

```math
\boxed{
\int_0^{T_*}
\left(
d[\log(1+\mathcal P_N^{mat})]_+
+
\mathcal S_N^{mat}(t)\,dt
\right)
<\infty .
}
\tag{FAV.15}
```

That is the global same-packet full material clock.  The present lemma does
not prove `(FAV.15)` from original data.  It proves that the rectified annular
flux coordinate is controlled once the same material record and service clock
are controlled.

## 4. Meaning

The pay-it-back-later annulus is the correct physical object.  The flux through
that annulus cannot behave like an arbitrary scalar signal after the material
velocity, pressure, coefficient, collar, and viscous service records are fixed.

The remaining MPP burden is exactly positive growth of that same material
record through the terminal history, not a separate boundary-oscillation
problem.
