# Front payments: locality exponent and the D2-prime corner

## 1. Ladder-scaling locality - paid at scaling level

UV: a band mu >> 1/l contributes stress (nu mu)^2 diluted by mollification
(mu l)^-3, against resolved strain nu/l^2: contribution ~ nu^3/(l^4) x
(mu l)^-1 = total x (mu l)^-1. IR: a band mu << 1/l strains the local
stress at rate nu mu^2: contribution ~ total x (mu l)^2. Exponents (1,2),
dyadically summable: the flux at ladder scales is local, channel-resolved,
with no Besov hypothesis needed - the ladder's own scaling supplies the
decay. Certified constants: bounded computation, flagged.

## 2. D2-prime - the branch-mixing supremum is one corner

Attenuation >= c0 is per step (per residence). Consequences: fine-mixed
paths of any composition lose N c0 per octave - dead for all N >= 2.
Same-helicity coarse: multiplier 2 x 1/2 x (1 - c0) = 1 - c0 < 1 - dead.
Catalyst-flipped coarse: conservation ceiling 7/8, multiplier
2 x 7/8 x (1 - bite) < 1 requires bite >= 3/7 at that step. The crude
C_rev ~ L^2/2 x A plausibly exceeds 3/7 but is not certified. The entire
branch-mixing supremum therefore reduces to ONE certified inequality at
ONE corner of strategy space:

  C_rev(coarse flipped step) >= 3/7.

Interpolating strategies are convex combinations of dead paths and the
corner; the supremum is attained at the corner. Finite check; named.
