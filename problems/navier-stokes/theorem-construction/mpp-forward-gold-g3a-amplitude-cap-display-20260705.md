# G3-A: the amplitude cap, and why it is not circular

## 1. The non-circularity, first

Over one relay period the transferred gross is coupling-free:

  J_period = |G| x integral of |Q| over the period,
  period = 2K/lambda,  lambda proportional to the same coupling,

so the coupling cancels - a spring transfers its swing regardless
of how fast it runs. No per-period throughput floor can force
coupling. What forces coupling is the FIXED window: the financed
family's floor is stated on residence windows whose duration is set
by the residence/octave bookkeeping in the absolute clock, not by
the relay's own period. On a fixed window, a flat relay fails to
move its quota. The ledger grants clock-windows, not full periods.

## 2. The cap, two lines

On the fixed residence window I:

  |Q_relay(a)| = |a_1 a_2 a_3| <= (E/3)^(3/2)     (AM-GM, exact),

shape-free: Q carries no geometry - the shape lives entirely in the
factored-out G_relay, so the cap cannot degenerate with
collinearity (Codex's condition 4, met by construction). Hence

  integral_I |Q_relay| dt <= |I| (E/3)^(3/2),
  C_amp <= |I|_max (E_ceil/3)^(3/2),

with |I|_max the filed window-duration ceiling (TV-downstream
period-ceiling display) and E_ceil the filed conservation/package
ceiling - a closed form in two filed symbols, finite on the whole
financed family.

## 3. The same-currency conditions, per Codex's list

(1) window = the financing window of J_min, by the financed
family's own definition; (2) channel/orientation = the typed TV
identity's fixed package channel; (3) package variable = the TV
identity's Y; (4) collinearity - displayed in section 2.
