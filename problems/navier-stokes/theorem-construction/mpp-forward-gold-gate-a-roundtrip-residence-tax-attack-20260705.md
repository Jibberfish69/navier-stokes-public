# Gate A: the round-trip residence tax

## Correction header

This note is superseded by
`mpp-forward-gold-gate-a-three-residues-payment-20260705.md`. The round-trip
residence-tax mechanism remains the right marginal mechanism. The scope claim
does not stand: Leray budget does not force the blowup ladder to be marginal.
For a uniformly super-marginal ladder with Reynolds excess M, the total
dissipation cost is finite for fixed M. The corrected theorem is marginal
unweighted and general Reynolds-weighted:

  TV(Pi_l) <= C M(l) x local dissipation budget.

The weight M(l) is a tower readout and is now part of campaign theorem 2's
impedance burden.

## 1. Accounting cannot prove it

Band positivity controls net, not gross. The rotation countermodel holds
positive band energy while re-reading forever. Gate A is dynamical or it
is nothing.

## 2. The mechanism

Backscatter is a real return trip, not a ledger flip. Flux locality
(Eyink-Aluie class): transfer across scale l is dominated by bands within
a bounded ratio of l - no teleporting in scale. So a unit that crosses
down and comes back RESIDED below, and cannot re-cross faster than the
sub-band turnover. On the marginal family the sub-band turnover equals the
viscous clock: residence costs an O(1) dissipative fraction per round trip.
Away from marginality, the tax is Reynolds-weighted rather than free.
Recounting is geometrically self-extinguishing at the margin:

  TV(Pi_l) <= C ( admitted throughput + local dissipation budget )
  on marginal defect-carrying bands.

That is the lifted no-recount law in its provable form. The abstract
recounting enemy is excluded by what it lacks: a sub-band to live in.

## 3. Why this is the physical story again

Viscosity as penny-tax: never the muscle, but it taxes every
re-presentation exactly where the enemy must operate. Flux locality as
participation: energy moves through adjacent scales only - the throttle,
in scale coordinates. Gate A's content is that grossness is not free
because residence is not free.
