# Paying the three residues: two paid, one retracted and reshaped

## (a) Channel-resolved locality - paid modulo an exponent check

Helical projectors P+/- act mode-wise, commute with band-pass, and do not
increase magnitudes. Eyink-Aluie locality proofs are magnitude-level
(Bernstein plus Holder on band-passed fields), hence transcribe verbatim
to channel-resolved fluxes. Residual: on ladder scaling (delta-u ~ nu/l on
volume l^3) the standard Besov hypotheses fail (effective Holder exponent
negative); direct kernel accounting under ladder scaling gives dyadic
summability with crude exponent ~1. The exponent check is a finite
computation, flagged not certified.

## (b) Crossing-rate floor - paid

Filtered NS + Bernstein: |d/dt Pi_l| <= C (kappa delta-u) sup|Pi_l|, so
full-magnitude sign alternations of the flux are separated by >= c x
turnover(l). At marginal Reynolds, turnover = viscous clock, so each
inter-alternation interval dissipates >= c0 x band energy. Counting:

  half-cycles <= dissipation / (c0 e_band),
  gross flux  <= C x local dissipation integral.

Gross traffic is dissipation-priced on marginal bands: the no-recount law
holds there in provable form. The rotation countermodel evades this only
by having no band to reside in.

## (c) Scope - retracted as stated, reshaped honestly

The free-confinement claim is withdrawn: a uniformly super-marginal ladder
(excess M) costs M^2 nu^2 r0 in total dissipation - finite for fixed M.
Bounded local Reynolds along a blowup ladder is a Type-I-type hypothesis,
not a consequence of the budget. Replacement: at excess M the residence
tax weakens like 1/M and the crossing rate rises like M, so Gate A holds
in weighted form,

  TV(flux) <= C x M(l) x dissipation budget,

and M(l) - a tower readout - joins kappa-bar in the opening of the cone.
Campaign theorem 2 (impedance, storage currency) inherits both weights:
its burden is now explicitly to control kappa-bar and M along
defect-carrying ladders. This is where the currency-mismatch confrontation
was always scheduled; the lift has not moved it, only sharpened what must
be paid there.

## Net state

Gate A: proved-shaped (marginal), weighted (general); L1' fires on
marginal bands modulo the exponent check; the general case and both
weights pass to theorem 2. One residue paid, one paid-with-extension, one
retracted and honestly reshaped - the front narrows again, and lands where
the campaign always said the decisive fight was: the impedance estimate.
