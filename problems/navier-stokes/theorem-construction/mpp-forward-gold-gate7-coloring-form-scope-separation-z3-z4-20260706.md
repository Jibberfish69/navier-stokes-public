# Gate 7: z4 by coloring, z3 by scope separation

## Codex regate, 2026-07-06

`mpp-forward-gold-codex-gate7-coloring-scope-separation-regate-20260706.md`.

Accepted: z4 in the coloring form. Exact balls replace merged geometric
components, and \(N_{\rm col}\) is quarantined as a supply-side
multiplicity or handled by an explicit subordinate assignment
convention.

Regated: z3 is not paid by scope separation alone. W1 Section 7(i)
defines a coherent lineage packet after lineage selection; it does not
prove that every \(\lambda_a\)-rich ball contains such a packet. W1
Section 7(iii) prices spatial spread over many correlation volumes; it
does not automatically price same-volume incoherence across many
lineages or phases.

routing theorem on \(\lambda_a\)-rich balls.

## U1. z4 — the coloring form (classical)

The Besicovitch covering theorem in \(\mathbb R^3\) has the
coloring form: any Besicovitch family splits into at most
\(N_{\rm col}(\mathbb R^3)\) subfamilies, each pairwise disjoint —
a universal integer alongside \(N_{\rm Bes}\).

Adopt the colored family as the covering object:

- windows are EXACT BALLS — readout stability is trivial; no merged
  unions, no component-size clause;
- each color is a disjoint window family; taxes once per
  (channel, ball) within each color;
- cross-color multiplicity \(\le N_{\rm col}\) enters the SUPPLY
  side as the named integer — exactly where the reduced-theorem
  note already placed cross-band multiplicity.

The merger rule survives in its physical scope — co-located packets
are one packet for lineage identity — but the covering bookkeeping
never needs merged shapes. z4 is dissolved, not proved.

## U2. z3 — scope separation

Coherence was never the cover's job. The W1 ladder frame defines
the increment at band \(\mu\) as one coherent helical packet on one
correlation volume — coherence is supplied by the lineage
decomposition (the F3 first-contact chronological queue), not
asserted of greedy balls.

Composition of scopes:

1. the colored \(\lambda_a\)-cover LOCATES supra-marginal mass;
2. within each ball, the lineage frame CLASSIFIES it — one coherent
   packet (7(i)), sibling components (7(ii)), or spread mass;
3. what the lineage frame leaves unclassified is spread across
   lineages — routed by the 7(iii) \(N^{-1/2}\) dilution to
   payer 1. FLAG u1: the dilution-across-lineages reading must be
   confirmed at the W1 home; if 7(iii) is strictly spatial-spread,
   the incoherent-lump case needs its own routing line, and u1 is
   the residual z3 content.
