# Gate 7: the greedy Besicovitch window construction

## Codex regate, 2026-07-06

Codex audit filed at
`mpp-forward-gold-codex-gate7-greedy-besicovitch-regate-20260706.md`.
The covering lemma is accepted: for a positive finite channel-cell
measure, Besicovitch gives a countable bounded-overlap cover of the
maximal-superlevel set.

The Gate-7 theorem is not cleared. Bounded overlap is not a disjoint
post-merger packet family unless a separate bounded-merger/readout
stability clause is proved. Exhaustion holds only for the same positive
measure used to define the maximal readout; the active monomial/defect
ledger still needs channel-first reconstruction or domination in the
right semicontinuity direction. Coherence versus mass-greedy selection
also remains open.

## Codex follow-up, 2026-07-06

Codex filed
`mpp-forward-gold-codex-gate7-positive-control-measure-domination-20260706.md`.
The positive measure and weak-limit direction are now specified for the
channel-cell frame:
\[
  \lambda_a=\mathrm{w^*}\!-\lim|\nu_a^\varepsilon|,
  \qquad
  |\nu_a|\le\lambda_a .
\]
Thus the Besicovitch cover is taken over the positive variation envelope
of the same signed throughput measure.

This does not reconstruct the total active monomial/defect measure from
the finite-readout channel-cell sum. Tails, coherence, and
bounded-merger/readout stability remain open.

## T1. Finiteness

Per band, the defect/production measure of the actual suitable
solution is finite on compact space-time regions — the local energy
inequality, F3's home turf.

## T2. Greedy selection, countability, the named integer

Fix the band-\(\mu\) marginal threshold \(m_\mu\) (installed in
W1's diffuse pricing). Consider all correlation-volume balls
carrying window-mass \(\ge m_\mu\). Besicovitch covering in
\(\mathbb R^3\) at fixed time-slab granularity gives a countable
subfamily covering the supra-marginal set with overlap multiplicity
\(\le N_{\rm Bes}(\mathbb R^3)\) — a universal integer: the named
cross-band multiplicity. Vitali refinement plus the MERGER RULE
(overlapping selected balls merge; co-located packets are one
packet) yields a disjoint window family. Local finiteness: compact
mass \(/\,m_\mu\) bounds the count. Measurability: countably many
windows, each a finite union of balls; dominant-channel readout by
Caratheodory functional with lowest-index tie-break.

## T3. Exhaustion — no dropped mass

The un-selected remainder is sub-marginal on EVERY correlation
volume by construction of the greedy sweep — hence DIFFUSE by the
W1 definition, priced by payer 1; the infinite-readout set is
silver-routed by chapter 2. Total defect mass = windows + diffuse
+ silver. Nothing is dropped.

## T4. No double tax

Taxes are collected on the post-merger disjoint family, once per
(channel, window). Cross-band multiplicity enters the supply side
only, as \(N_{\rm Bes}\).
