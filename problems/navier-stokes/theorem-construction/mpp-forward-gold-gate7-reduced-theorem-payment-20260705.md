# Reduced gate 7: countability does the work

## Codex update, 2026-07-06

Codex follow-up filed at
`mpp-forward-gold-codex-gate7-deterministic-channel-cell-reduction-20260706.md`.
The enumeration/readout part of this theorem is reduced to the
deterministic half-open channel-cell frame from
`mpp-forward-gold-codex-channel-cell-bv-tightness-proof-20260705.md`.
For finite readout on compact cylinders, the pairs \((c,Q)\) are
countable and locally finite, and readouts are measurable on fixed
pairs.

The remaining load is not Castaing selection. It is reconstruction and
overlap: prove that the channel-cell measures reconstruct the relevant
defect/active monomial mass, that locality and mollifier tails are
charged in the eta budget, and that sibling/diffuse routing drops no
mass and double-taxes no unit.

## Codex update, 2026-07-06: positive control measure

Codex follow-up filed at
`mpp-forward-gold-codex-gate7-positive-control-measure-domination-20260706.md`.
For each finite-readout channel-cell pair \(a\),
\[
  \lambda_a=\mathrm{w^*}\!-\lim|\nu_a^\varepsilon|,
  \qquad
  |\nu_a|\le\lambda_a .
\]
This pays the positive control-measure choice and the weak-limit
domination direction. The cover is over \(\lambda_a\), the variation
envelope of the same prelimit throughput, not over the signed measure
and not over the local-energy residence budget.

At this stage the reduced theorem remained open at total reconstruction,
tails, coherence, and bounded-overlap/merger accounting.

## (a) The readout at partition grade

The intended window family is the W1 packet decomposition: per band,
per lineage, disjoint after the merger rule. The open proof obligation
is to construct this family as a countable locally finite measurable
decomposition carrying the defect mass assigned to it. T3's Castaing
machinery supplies measurable selections, but does not alone prove
exhaustive enumeration. Each window gets ONE readout:
its dominant defect-carrying channel's increment amplitude, times the
window scale, over viscosity - a Caratheodory functional of the fields
composed with the measurable selection. A measurable function on a
countable enumeration admits exact preimage partitions after that
enumeration is proved. Until then, the countability clause is the
reduced theorem, not an already-paid proof.

## (b) The overlap accounting, sin by sin

UNPRICED MASS: the proposed proof decomposes the defect by channel
first, with bounded integer multiplicity across dyadic bands. This
must be proved for the weak-limit defect measure. Per channel, windows
are intended to be disjoint by the merger rule. The complement of all
windows within a channel is intended to be the diffuse remainder - the
sub-marginal stratum, priced at viscous saturation - or the
infinite-readout set, silver-routed by chapter 2. Exhaustion by W1 is
still a theorem clause.
DOUBLE-COUNTED TAX: every tax is collected once per (channel, window)
pair. Cross-band multiplicity enters the supply side only, as the
named integer. Mollification tails are eta-budget forcing terms - they
carry no mass assignment, so they can neither hide a unit nor tax it
twice.

## (c) Edge monotonicity, at level

Payer 1: closed form, decreasing in M, saturating below. Weighted
guard: total variation bounded by weight times dissipation, increasing
in M. Chain floor: constant on the marginal band. All three are filed
closed forms; the band edges may be assigned to either side with the
pricing intact. Half-open bands fix measurability; monotonicity fixes
indifference; no referee boundary-hunt exists.
