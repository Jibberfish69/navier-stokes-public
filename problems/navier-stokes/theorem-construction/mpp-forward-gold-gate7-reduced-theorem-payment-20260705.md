# Reduced gate 7: countability does the work

## (a) The readout at partition grade

The window family IS the W1 packet decomposition: per band, per
lineage, disjoint after the merger rule. T3's Castaing machinery
enumerates it countably and measurably. Each window gets ONE readout:
its dominant defect-carrying channel's increment amplitude, times the
window scale, over viscosity - a Caratheodory functional of the fields
composed with the measurable selection. A measurable function on a
countable enumeration admits exact preimage partitions; the strata are
countable unions of measurable window-graphs. Nothing about the
continuum survives to make trouble: packets are individuals, and
individuals can be counted.

## (b) The overlap accounting, sin by sin

UNPRICED MASS: decompose the defect by channel first (exact, bounded
integer multiplicity across dyadic bands - a named structural
integer). Per channel, windows are disjoint by the merger rule. The
complement of all windows within a channel is the diffuse remainder -
the sub-marginal stratum, priced at viscous saturation - or the
infinite-readout set, silver-routed by chapter 2. Exhaustion is the
W1 trichotomy itself: every unit of defect mass has exactly one home,
by construction.
DOUBLE-COUNTED TAX: every tax is collected once per (channel, window)
pair. Cross-band multiplicity enters the supply side only, as the
named integer. Mollification tails are eta-budget forcing terms - they
carry no mass assignment, so they can neither hide a unit nor tax it
twice.

## (c) Edge monotonicity, at level

Payer 1: closed form, decreasing in M, saturating below. Weighted
guard: total variation bounded by weight times dissipation, increasing
in M. Chain floor: constant on the marginal band. All three are filed
closed forms; the band edges may be assigned to either side with the
pricing intact. Half-open bands fix measurability; monotonicity fixes
indifference; no referee boundary-hunt exists.
