# Gate 7: the strata exhaustion theorem

## 1. Classify where the pricing lives

Every tax in the campaign is collected per window. So the
decomposition theorem is stated on the measurable family of
defect-carrying windows (Castaing selection, T3), not on raw points:
point-level strata are the unions of their windows' supports, and
window overlaps are locality tails - already priced, hence never
unpriced overlap.

## 2. The classifier and the trichotomy

Each window carries one local Reynolds readout M, built by the same
machinery as the wavenumber readout (R1/T3: countable suprema of
measurable channel functionals; usc envelope). Partition the M-axis
into three half-open bands with named symbolic edges. Preimages of a
measurable function: measurable, disjoint, exhaustive - every window
lands in exactly one stratum. The infinite-readout complement is
chapter 2's silver routing, outside this theorem by design.

## 3. No dropped mass

The defect measure restricts additively over the measurable partition;
nothing is dropped by countable additivity. Overlaps between windows
are the geometric locality tails of the paid exponents: priced, so the
partition's double-cover error is inside the eta-budget, not outside
the ledger.

## 4. Pricing per stratum, attached measurably

SUB-MARGINAL: saturated viscous domination - the defect restricted to
this class is dominated by the dissipation measure times a named
symbol (payer 1 at saturation; W1's diffuse dilution puts every
diffuse window here). MARGINAL: the chain floor, through the tempo
split now written on the FCR label (fast windows: return race; slow:
predator/viscous). SUPER-MARGINAL: the Reynolds-weighted Gate A bound
with weight equal to the classifying readout itself - the guard rides
the stratum by construction; chapter 8 integrates the weight against
the dissipation budget, never dropping it.

## 5. Edge indifference

Both taxes adjacent to each band edge are monotone and continuous in
M (viscous decreasing, weighted guard increasing; the chain floor
constant between its edges). Any assignment of the edges therefore
prices correctly; half-open bands fix measurability with zero
ambiguity. No boundary hunt is available to a referee.

## 6. Register

Gate 7 paid at structural level; re-gate on the readout construction
and the overlap accounting. Remaining bridges: gate 6 core (drifting-
separatrix lemma), gate 8 (same-packet row theorem). Chapter 10
unchanged. No closure claimed.
