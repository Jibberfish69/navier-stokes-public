# Gate 8: the same-packet row theorem

## 1. One family, four roles

Let W be gate 7's measurable classified family of (helical channel,
defect-carrying window) pairs. The theorem: the record work of any
signed-height upcrossing, Gate A's admission, the payers' taxes, and
the no-recount debt are all statements about the elements of W.

## 2. F1 - the critical weight is diagonal on channels

P(t), the height's production, is the nonlinear transfer tested at
critical weight. On the helical channel decomposition the critical
weight acts as the channel wavenumber exactly - the same kinematic
identity that gave chapter 2 its line membership. Hence P(t) is an
exact channel-weighted sum of channel fluxes. No cross terms exist to
hide work outside the family.

## 3. F2 - the flux gate localizes the work onto W

The flux-gate identity writes each channel flux as increment stress
against strain, supported on the packet windows up to the paid
locality tails. Integrating over a record interval:

  record work = sum over W of (channel weight) x (admitted window
  flux) + priced tails.

An identity given T1, not an estimate. The boundary terms of the
upcrossing (height difference, dissipation) are already inside the
installed upcrossing inequality.

## 4. F3 - the three rows act on the same summands

Row 1 (parent admission): the oriented-admission decomposition of each
summand - chapter 2, per channel. Row 2 (thickness-or-charge): the
stratum pricing of each summand's window - gate 7, per window. Row 3
(sign retention): orientation routing of each summand's sign returns
as previously-admitted debt - same rays, same channel. Four roles, one
index set. The 07-04 spine's demand - "the same physical packet" - is
met by construction, because the packet family was built once and
everything else was defined on it.
