# K5 high-Re edge: custody needs a trigger

## 1. Question

The K5 high-Re edge was reduced to outside payers after the K5-only
uniform cap failed. Return-race has already been retyped as conditional on
a financed handoff. This note checks the custody word in the same payer
list.

The narrow question is:
\[
  \text{pure high-Re edge residence}
  \quad\Rightarrow\quad
  \text{custody payment?}
\]

## 2. What custody prices

The installed custody packet has a definite trigger: producing geometry
has been destroyed and must be reassembled.

Thin debris is governed by the transit-versus-viscous clock. For debris
radius \(a_t\) and pair separation \(d\), reassembly is affordable only
above the quadratic circulation-Reynolds threshold
\[
  {\operatorname{Re}}_\Gamma
  \gtrsim
  \left({d\over a_t}\right)^2 .
\]

Thick fast reassembly is relative motion. Moving the participating
impulse a distance comparable to \(d\) in a bounded number of strain times
forces a scale-\(d\) increment-cubic payment
\[
  \mathcal C_d(Q)
  \gtrsim
  c\,C^{-2}U^2d^3 .
\]

Thick stored energy is the escrow branch. The energy is not free reserve;
it must localize as prior paid same-packet residue, entrance reserve,
routed tail, or Silver-side mass.

Those are reassembly, delivery, and escrow statements. They are not a
payment law for every high-Re residence.

## 3. What the pure edge supplies

The K5 pure high-Re edge normal form supplies
\[
  \dot A
  =
  -s c_v\kappa^2\operatorname{Re}^{-1}A
  +O(sA^2),
  \qquad
  \mathcal P_{\rm edge}
  =
  s\Pi A+O(sA^2).
\]

That is an intact near-neutral producing residence. It has no reassembly
variable, no debris packet, no forced relative displacement over distance
\(d\), and no escrow/delivery branch unless another mechanism first
destroys or refreshes the producing geometry.

So the custody trigger supplied by the pure edge is
\[
  \chi_{\rm custody}=0.
\]

With no trigger, the installed custody lower bounds apply to an empty
event family:
\[
  \mathcal C_{\rm custody}(\text{pure edge})=0
\]
in the current theorem packet.

## 4. Consequence

The lawful implication is
\[
  \text{high-Re edge}
  +
  \text{reassembly/refresh/delivery trigger}
  \to
  \text{custody payment},
\]
not
\[
  \text{high-Re edge}
  \to
  \text{custody payment}.
\]

Custody remains useful for any sideband, curve-leakage, refresh, destruction,
or crowd mechanism that actually creates the reassembly episode. The exact
isolated pure edge does not create that episode or the sideband/crowd trigger
by name.

## 5. Four-sentence result

Custody is a reassembly payment, not an automatic residence payment. The
K5 pure high-Re edge is an intact near-neutral mode with production and
slow damping, so it supplies no debris transit, no fast relative
reassembly, and no stored-energy escrow event. Therefore the installed
custody packet has no positive charge on the pure edge branch. The K5
high-Re branch still needs dynamic/geometric forced sideband/leakage/crowd
contamination, route-out/exclusion, a separate theorem that first creates a
financed handoff or custody trigger, or a new unweighted high-Re mechanism.
