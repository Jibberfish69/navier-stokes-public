# The lifts: one struck, one reduced

## 1. Lift (i) struck by architecture audit

Trace the final chain: floor c0 per step (payer 1 marginal - Gate A(b);
payer 2 high-M - return race) composed with amplitude-free per-step
ceilings gives geometric critical-mass decay. The ceilings are consumed
at STEP level, inside each handoff - which is lift (ii)'s object. The
defect-cone theorem is load-bearing only where the defect bookkeeping
must be PDE-legitimate independently of step structure: the marginal
bands, where L1-prime is already proved-shaped. Off-marginal, the cone
was double bookkeeping: scaffolding, not load. Struck from the list;
recorded so no future session re-pays it.

## 2. Lift (ii) reduced: the finite-chain reduction

Triad periodicity is not a PDE fact; chain dynamics is. Project onto the
ladder's helical bands. Off-chain forcing is priced by the paid locality
exponents: far-band contributions sum geometrically, so a chain of the
nearest B bands is eta-autonomous with eta ~ 2^(-B). Gronwall over one
residence: the increment dynamics tracks the finite helical chain within
C eta. The return-race bound lives natively on chains (it was derived on
the five-mode chain); it therefore lifts with eta-corrections, provided:

  CHAIN-UNIFORM R0: the return-race constant is uniform in chain length.

The un-parking mechanism is local - adjacent-rung amplitudes and phases,
per-handoff taxation - so no dilution with length is expected; proving it
is a family computation in the triad calculus (Jacobi-elliptic tools
available), not a PDE problem. This is the entire remaining mechanism
content of lift (ii).
