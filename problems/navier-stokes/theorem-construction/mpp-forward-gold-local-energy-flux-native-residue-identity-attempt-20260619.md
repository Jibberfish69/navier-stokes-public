```math

\qquad
\mathcal D=\nu|\nabla U|^2,
```

```math

\left(\frac12|U|^2+\Pi\right)U
-\nu\nabla\frac12|U|^2 .
```

The local energy inequality is

```math
\partial_t\mathcal E+\mathcal D+\nabla\cdot\mathcal F\le0.
```

```math

```


This measure is genuinely native.  No annular current or imported stress
