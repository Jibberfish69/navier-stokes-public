# Localized Affine Global Matching Scaling Audit

The local affine material-jet audit shows that local Navier--Stokes identities
do not forbid arbitrary trace-free strain-time profiles.  The next structural
question is whether global periodic divergence-free matching and the annular
collar automatically add enough cost to force finite full same-material action.

At the critical heat scale, the answer is no by scaling alone.

This is not a Navier--Stokes solution construction.  It is a proof audit for
the proposed global-matching shortcut.

## 1. Divergence-free localized affine packet

Fix a symmetric trace-free matrix \(A_0\) with \(|A_0|\simeq1\).  At spatial
scale \(r\), set

```math
\lambda_r=r^{-2},
\qquad
A_r=\lambda_r A_0 .
\tag{LGM.1}
```

Let \(\chi_r(x)=\chi(x/r)\), with \(\chi=1\) on \(B_1\) and supported in
\(B_2\).  The raw localized affine field is

```math
w_r(x)=\chi_r(x)A_rx .
\tag{LGM.2}
```

Since \(A_r\) is trace-free,

```math
\nabla\cdot(A_rx)=0,
\tag{LGM.3}
```

and the divergence of \(w_r\) is supported only in the collar
\(B_{2r}\setminus B_r\):

```math
\nabla\cdot w_r
=
\nabla\chi_r\cdot A_rx .
\tag{LGM.4}
```

On the collar,

```math
|\nabla\chi_r|\simeq r^{-1},
\qquad
|A_rx|\simeq \lambda_r r=r^{-1},
\tag{LGM.5}
```

so

```math
|\nabla\cdot w_r|\simeq r^{-2}.
\tag{LGM.6}
```

The divergence has zero spatial mean on the periodic torus.  A Bogovskii or
periodic Hodge correction on the same collar gives a field \(b_r\) with

```math
\nabla\cdot b_r=-\nabla\cdot w_r,
\qquad
\|\nabla b_r\|_{L^2}\lesssim \|\nabla\cdot w_r\|_{L^2},
\tag{LGM.7}
```

and with the same critical scaling as the collar part of \(w_r\).  Define

```math
u_r=w_r+b_r .
\tag{LGM.8}
```

Then \(u_r\) is globally periodic and divergence-free, equals \(A_rx\) on
\(B_r\), and is supported in a fixed multiple of the collar scale.

## 2. Critical heat-scale costs

Inside \(B_r\),

```math
|u_r|\simeq \lambda_r r=r^{-1},
\qquad
|\nabla u_r|\simeq\lambda_r=r^{-2}.
\tag{LGM.9}
```

On the collar, the matching correction has the same scale:

```math
|u_r|\lesssim r^{-1},
\qquad
|\nabla u_r|\lesssim r^{-2},
\qquad
|\nabla^2u_r|\lesssim r^{-3}.
\tag{LGM.10}
```

Use the heat-time window

```math
\tau_r\simeq r^2 .
\tag{LGM.11}
```

The raw kinetic energy is

```math
\int |u_r|^2\,dx
\lesssim
r^{-2}r^3
=r .
\tag{LGM.12}
```

The raw viscous dissipation over the heat window is

```math
\int_0^{\tau_r}\int |\nabla u_r|^2\,dx\,dt
\lesssim
r^2\,r^{-4}r^3
=r .
\tag{LGM.13}
```

The collar viscous force work has the same scale:

```math
\int_0^{\tau_r}\int |u_r|\,|\nu\Delta u_r|\,dx\,dt
\lesssim
r^2\,r^{-1}r^{-3}r^3
\lesssim r .
\tag{LGM.14}
```

The convective force work has the same scale:

```math
\int_0^{\tau_r}\int |u_r|\,|(u_r\cdot\nabla)u_r|\,dx\,dt
\lesssim
r^2\,r^{-1}(r^{-1}r^{-2})r^3
\lesssim r .
\tag{LGM.15}
```

The pressure generated by the affine core has size

```math
|p_r|\simeq |A_r|^2r^2\simeq r^{-2},
\qquad
|\nabla p_r|\simeq r^{-3}.
\tag{LGM.16}
```

Thus the pressure force work is also radius-weighted:

```math
\int_0^{\tau_r}\int |u_r|\,|\nabla p_r|\,dx\,dt
\lesssim
r^2\,r^{-1}r^{-3}r^3
\lesssim r .
\tag{LGM.17}
```

These estimates are exactly the same scale as the raw energy and raw
dissipation estimates.  The divergence-free global collar matching does not
turn one critical heat-scale sample into an order-one physical energy or
physical work cost.

## 3. Normalized material action remains order one

The material strain/action in the affine core is

```math
\int_0^{\tau_r}|A_r|\,dt
\simeq
r^{-2}r^2
\simeq1 .
\tag{LGM.18}
```

Equivalently, after heat-scale normalization, the local packet has order-one
strain/frame action.  This is the same normalized quantity that the finite
full same-material action theorem must control.

Therefore a dyadic terminal sequence \(r_k=2^{-k}\) has the scaling profile

```math
\sum_k r_k<\infty,
\qquad
\sum_k1=\infty .
\tag{LGM.19}
```

The first sum is the raw energy, raw viscous dissipation, and collar matching
scale.  The second is the normalized full material action scale.

## 4. What the audit proves

Global periodic divergence-free matching is essential for the real proof
object.  It prevents a center-only or free-boundary reading of the spike.  It
also places all collar, pressure, viscous, Hodge, and annular terms in the same
fluid packet.

It does not, by scale alone, prove

```math
\int_0^{T_*}d\Omega_N^{full}<\infty .
\tag{LGM.20}
```

Here the legacy symbol \(d\Omega_N^{full}\) denotes the full
same-material action measure.

The remaining theorem must use more than global matching:

```math
\text{global matching + finite energy}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{finite normalized material action}.
\tag{LGM.21}
```

A successful proof must add a genuinely one-way same-material principle:
nonlocal Hodge pressure cancellation with a sign, a bounded-below
participation entropy, a no-free-upward-transfer theorem, or an equivalent
global action-packing law on the original material history.

## 5. Result

The global matching shortcut is ruled out as a standalone proof.

The current Gold target remains unchanged but sharper: prove finite normalized
same-material action by a non-scalar global Navier--Stokes mechanism that
controls the full pressure-viscosity-incompressibility-velocity annular
history, not merely the cost of localizing and matching an affine core.
