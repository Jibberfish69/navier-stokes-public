## F-1. Gap: exponentially decreasing

Gap(depth) = (bounded band factor) x sixteen x exp(minus twice the
depth), from the separatrix identity and the top root's analytic
dependence on the modulus with nonvanishing derivative at the
double-root limit. Chain rule, both factors named. Strictly

## F-2. Dwell correction: decreasing

Constant over depth; derivative minus constant over depth squared.
Displayed so nothing is implicit.

## F-3. The left side: uniformly positive

Left side >= bottom root x (level gap) / (level sum); bottom root
positive by the root identity with nonzero phase invariant; phase
invariant floored on the parked family by a NAMED band symbol. The
formula is displayed; the band-definition dependence is stated.

## F-4. Continuity at the split; uniqueness; coverage

Deep price: race integral fed by the contraction margin, which falls
continuously to zero at the split (the split IS the crossing).
Shallow price: race integral at unsuppressed phase, continuous in the
modulus. Both finite at the split; the half-bands are closed and
share the endpoint: no gap. Uniqueness: strictly decreasing
continuous sum (F-1 + F-2) against a positive constant (F-3) crosses
once.
