# No-post-readout thin transient retention attempt

## Claim

A positive thin transient that is selected only after the lower-scale readout is not legal retained Schur action unless it already has a parent-predictable same-material residence certificate. If no such certificate exists, the event must be charged to stop/legal, reselection, route motion, viscous service, return, or parent-orthogonal novelty.

## Proof relative to the installed selected-action currency rule

The installed selected-action currency rule says that retained selected action is source-defined as one of the following:

1. a bounded original-packet projection;
2. a bounded Riesz representative from the source graph;
3. inherited/root current;
4. paid stop/legal/reselection/selector drift;
5. paid motion or route variation;
6. parent-orthogonal fresh novelty entering a Bessel ledger;
7. viscous service or negative return.

Absolute lower-scale amplitude by itself is not retained Gold action.

Therefore a positive transient chosen only after the lower-scale readout has two possibilities.

First, it supplies a parent-predictable same-material certificate. Then it is admitted as Schur-resident production and can be tested against

\[
  v_J=G_J^{-1}m_J .
\]

Second, it lacks such a certificate. Then it is not one of the legal retained Schur-action currencies. By the selected-action classifier it must be assigned to a paid non-Schur ledger: stop/legal, reselection, route motion, viscous service, return, or parent-orthogonal novelty.

Thus the no-post-readout retention statement is formally closed relative to the selected-action currency classifier.

## What remains open

This does not yet prove the affine Schur accretive lower bound. The remaining analytic coverage issue is that every positive production term appearing in

\[
  H_w\|c_{\theta,J}^{+}\|_2^2
\]

must be shown to enter the selected-action currency classifier before it is counted as retained production in the Schur estimate.

Equivalently, the next hinge is not the selector guard itself. The next hinge is a coverage theorem:

\[

\]

Statement: every positive face contributing to the affine Schur producer is either a classifier-admitted retained Schur action face with parent-predictable residence, or else is routed to an already-paid non-Schur ledger.
