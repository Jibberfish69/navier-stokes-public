# One-holder amplitude floor: cheap bath kills the local constant

## 1. Kill condition

The one-holder amplitude note isolated a tempting local floor:
\[
  \frac{\text{minimal holder energy}}
       {\text{producer record-currency energy}}
  \ge c_0>0.
  \tag{OAF.1}
\]

If true uniformly, this would price the amplitude-supply channel at one holder:
node-sitting may ride pure amplitude growth geometrically, but the holder would
still need order-one energy to grow the cancelling field.

The kill condition for this subcell is a producing configuration with a lawful
holder whose energy ratio tends to zero while it supplies the needed coherent
strain for one tier.

## 2. Bath scaling supplies the counterexample to the local floor

The bath note already records the inside-bath holder. A smooth strain bath of
extent comparable to the producing scale supplies the coherent cancellation
without range suppression. In its notation, the bath energy is
\[
  E_{\rm bath}
  \sim
  s^2\beta^{8/5}d^5.
  \tag{OAF.2}
\]

The producer record-currency energy at that scale is
\[
  E_{\rm prod}
  \sim
  s^2d^5.
  \tag{OAF.3}
\]

Therefore the one-tier ratio is
\[
  \frac{E_{\rm bath}}{E_{\rm prod}}
  \sim
  \beta^{8/5}.
  \tag{OAF.4}
\]

As \(\beta\to0\),
\[
  \beta^{8/5}\to0.
  \tag{OAF.5}
\]

Thus (OAF.1) is false in the filed scaling model.

## 3. What this does and does not kill

This does not prove a no-eviction crowd. It kills only the local proof target:
"the amplitude-supply channel has a fixed positive one-holder energy floor."

The holder can be cheap for one tier. That is the threat the bath note recorded
honestly. The question is whether the cheap tier can repeat indefinitely.

The bath-recursion note already computes the filed recursion:
\[
  \beta_{n+1}=\beta_n^{4/5},
  \qquad
  e_n=\beta_n^{8/5}.
  \tag{OAF.6}
\]

Then
\[
  \prod_{j<N}e_j
  =
  \beta_0^{8(1-(4/5)^N)}
  \longrightarrow
  \beta_0^8>0.
  \tag{OAF.7}
\]

So in that scaling model, one-tier cheapness does not compose into a
finite-energy infinite holder chain. The local floor is false, but the infinite
cheap-bath construction is also false under the same exponent law.

## 4. Consequence for item 4b

The tracking/supply floor should not be stated as a fixed local
holder-energy ratio. That local ratio is defeated by inside-bath holding.

The live object is the octave recursion:

1. fixed positive total recursion floor after all tracking/supply costs:
   composition starvation, exit (A) after the later gates;
2. an exact matrix/node-sitting schedule whose induced \(x_n\) sequence
   satisfies the bath-recursion summability criterion: exit (B) construction;
3. finite-depth bath leverage: composition problem, not a local floor.

Thus the one-holder amplitude channel has been consumed into the same
up-scale holding recursion already named by the bath notes.

## 5. Four-sentence result

The amplitude-supply channel does not have a fixed one-holder energy floor in
the filed bath scaling. A smooth inside-bath holder has
\[
  E_{\rm bath}/E_{\rm prod}\sim\beta^{8/5}\to0.
\]
So the local ratio named in the one-holder note cannot decide the dense cell.
The remaining question is the up-scale recursion: the filed
\(\beta_{n+1}=\beta_n^{4/5}\), \(e_n=\beta_n^{8/5}\) model does not self-finance,
but an exact matrix/node-sitting schedule satisfying the summability criterion
or a finite-depth composition escape remains open.
