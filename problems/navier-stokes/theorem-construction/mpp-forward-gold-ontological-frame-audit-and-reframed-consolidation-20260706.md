# Ontological-frame audit: what the session honoured, what it reimposed, and the obligation restated in the object's terms

| commitment | honoured | violated |

The decisive error is not the translation to standard theory — that served
real verification (it caught the pincer induction error, tested the extraction
laws, checked the pressure exponents). The error is letting the shell frame
become the FRAME OF JUDGMENT. "s1 = the supercritical core = the Millennium
wall" is not a discovery about the object; it is the known failure of the
energy-only shell method, reproduced by re-imposing that method. The
supercriticality barrier LIVES in that frame. Concluding the object inherits
the barrier, because its shell image does, is a category error.

## 2. What the object's frame keeps that the shell budget discards

Two concrete, non-mystical items:

1. **The second exact invariant.** Standard supercriticality analysis uses
   energy `\int|u|^2` only — one derivative too weak. The fluid-object tracks
   energy AND helicity as the Manley-Rowe pair `(I_1, I_2)`, with the exact
   conserved gap `I_2 - I_1` (the extraction laws' equal-rate stroke). The
   helicity content is exactly what the energy-only budget throws away.
2. **Exact coherent pay-back.** The Waleffe object is periodic at every
   amplitude — it peaks and pays back; nothing tightens itself. In the shell
   frame this is invisible: one sees `\iint|u|^3` and cannot tell it is an
   exactly-periodic coherent structure returning what it borrowed. Blowup, in
   the object's frame, is not "the shell sum diverges" but whether the RELAY
   across coherent objects defeats each object's exact pay-back — adjudicated
   by the ledger's exact accounting, not a lossy sum.

The degree-mismatch strike of `D_j <= C_pc E_j` (dissipation `~eps^2`,
admitted row `~eps^3`, so `D_j/E_j ~ 1/eps` on data `eps v_0`) is itself a
shell/small-data artifact: `eps v_0 -> 0` is smooth, regular, NON-participating
data. The pricing was only ever needed on the terminal PARTICIPATING object,
where dissipation and readout are one coherent participation exactly related
through the tower — not two separately-scaling shell quantities to compare by
degree. The strike kills the over-stated "uniform for all data" inequality; it
does not bind the object the pricing must hold on.

This is why the product-side results survive the reframing intact — they are

## 4. Guardrail — this is a bet, not a proof, and it is testable

ONLY IF coherence, the participation law, and the tower are DERIVED from NS,
not posited. If the fluid-object's coherence is assumed, the barrier is hidden,
not escaped, and W-0 is the Millennium problem wearing the object's clothes.

genuinely open. The decision is TESTABLE and belongs in the object's frame:

- test the exact pricing on the terminal PARTICIPATING object (where the
  small-data strike does not apply), not on arbitrary smooth data;
- check whether the extraction laws are NS-derived under single-mode handoff

- check whether the energy+helicity pair constrains the relay enough that the
  coherent pay-back is not defeatable — the actual content the shell frame
