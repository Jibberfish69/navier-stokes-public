# Persistent WKB is the construction surface

## 1. Kill question

After the Reynolds-edge and fixed-density compensator branch collapses, the
overlap-bath front still used two names:

```text
persistent high-Re WKB / broadband construction
construction/exclusion of the near-uniform same-fluid WKB bath
```

The kill question is whether these are two different evasion branches.

They are not. Persistent high-Re WKB is the physical construction attempt for
the near-uniform same-fluid bath. It is not a separate retained supplier once
the actual-carrier, terminal-frequency, and profile-admission reductions are
kept in the object.

## 2. What persistence means

The many-cell WKB row entered after the local pressure mechanisms failed. In
the no-payer window the branch has carrier length \(\ell=\theta D\), cell count
\(N\), and producer-clock persistence
\[
  \operatorname{Re}_D\theta^2\gg1.
  \tag{PWCS.1}
\]

The parameter and support-density rows sharpen this to:
\[
  \beta^{-12/5}\lesssim N\lesssim C\beta^{12/5}\theta^{-5},
  \qquad
  \theta\lesssim C\beta^{24/25},
  \qquad
  \operatorname{Re}_D\gg\theta^{-2},
  \tag{PWCS.2}
\]
and, if a fixed retained tax density is demanded,
\[
  N\sim\theta^{-3},
  \qquad
  \theta\lesssim C\beta^{6/5},
  \qquad
  \operatorname{Re}_D\gg\theta^{-2}.
  \tag{PWCS.3}
\]

Those displays do not define an extra payer. They define the only remaining
cheap dynamic geometry for an actual near-uniform WKB bath.

## 3. Finite or atomic actual carrier

Assume first that the persistent WKB branch is represented by a finite menu of
actual same-solution phase-packet carrier families with nonzero retained
terminal pressure-Hessian moment. The actual-carrier extraction note gives one
carrier family on an infinite terminal subtail.

That carrier carries the required same-fluid clauses: same datum, fixed
viscosity, actual velocity-pressure evolution, material ancestry or licensed
original-participation record, finite/readout-compatible localization, same-law
tower, and nonvanishing terminal moment.

The terminal Field-admission reduction then consumes it:
\[
  \lambda_j\to\infty,
  \qquad
  \phi_jD_j^3|K_j|\ge c_0>0
  \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
  \text{Sobolev/Field readout after witness admission}.
  \tag{PWCS.4}
\]

So an admitted finite or atomic carrier is not retained WKB supply. It is the
Field-facing terminal witness.

## 4. Vanishing or intermittent moment

If the normalized retained moment vanishes enough to avoid the Field readout,
the terminal-frequency fork already removes the retained-bath reading. The
branch has become vanishing/intermittent high-factor source behavior.

That branch is routed by the terminal high-factor admission fork to the
selected-action source graph, no-free-upcascade, native-reserve scale births, or
the corresponding admission failure. It is not persistent retained WKB supply.

Thus the nonvanishing horn routes to Field after admission; the vanishing horn
routes to high-factor source / no-free-upcascade. Neither horn leaves an
independent persistent-WKB supplier.

## 5. Compact profile

If no fixed finite carrier survives but the WKB labels are tight enough to form
a compact microlocal or WKB profile with nonzero scalar service moment, the
profile no-supplier trichotomy applies.

The profile has three outcomes:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{legal certified profile with nonzero moment}
  &\Rightarrow \text{Field-facing admitted terminal witness},\\[1mm]
\text{first failed certificate}
  &\Rightarrow \text{Pack-side cover failure or Part-side law/ancestry/tower failure},\\[1mm]
\text{no tight stabilized profile object}
  &\Rightarrow \text{selector, carrier, label, identity, or construction/exclusion escape}.
\end{array}
\tag{PWCS.5}
\]

Again, retained WKB supply has disappeared. A legal profile is consumed; an
illegal profile is typed by its first failed certificate; absence of the profile
is an actual construction/exclusion or selected-carrier problem.

## 6. Branch merge

The persistent-WKB name survives only as the unresolved actual bath surface:
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{finite/atomic actual carrier with nonzero moment}
  &\Rightarrow \text{Field admission route},\\[1mm]
\text{vanishing/intermittent moment}
  &\Rightarrow \text{high-factor source route},\\[1mm]
\text{compact legal profile with nonzero moment}
  &\Rightarrow \text{Field admission route},\\[1mm]
\text{first failed legal certificate}
  &\Rightarrow \text{Pack/Part route},\\[1mm]
\text{no admitted carrier or profile}
  &\Rightarrow \text{actual construction/exclusion surface}.
\end{array}
\tag{PWCS.6}
\]

Therefore the overlap-bath front should no longer list persistent high-Re WKB
as a second thing beside construction/exclusion. It is the construction side of
the same thing.

The remaining dense-crowd question is exactly this: construct or exclude a
near-uniform same-fluid WKB bath that evades finite/atomic carrier admission,
microlocal-profile admission, high-factor routing, and Pack/Part certificate
failure.

## 7. Four-sentence result

Persistent high-Re WKB is not an independent retained supplier; it is the
concrete construction surface for the near-uniform same-fluid bath. If it
produces an admitted nonvanishing actual carrier or certified profile, the
Sobolev/Field readout consumes it. If the moment vanishes, is intermittent, or
loses legality, the branch routes to high-factor source or Pack/Part
certificate failure. The live evasion surface is therefore one actual bath
problem: construction or exclusion of a near-uniform same-fluid WKB bath outside
those admission and failure routes.
