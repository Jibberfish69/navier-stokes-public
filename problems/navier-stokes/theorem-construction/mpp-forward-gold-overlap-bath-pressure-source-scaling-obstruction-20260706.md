# Overlap bath: pressure-source scaling obstruction

## 1. Kill question

The local affine dynamic packet uses pressure to absorb
\[
  \dot A+A^2.
  \tag{OBPS.1}
\]

The time-dependent collar note priced the cutoff and retuning of the localized
velocity field. This note checks the next possible payer: perhaps the global
Poisson source needed to create the pressure Hessian costs an order-one fraction
of the producer.

The kill condition is an order-one lower cost for supplying the required
traceless pressure Hessian. The checked scaling result is negative, but it
changes the exponent.

## 2. Pressure Hessian from the source

For incompressible Navier-Stokes,
\[
  -\Delta p=f,
  \qquad
  f=\partial_i u_j\,\partial_j u_i.
  \tag{OBPS.2}
\]

Away from the producer point, the Hessian at the origin is
\[
  \partial_{ij}p(0)
  =
  c\int
  \frac{3y_i y_j-|y|^2\delta_{ij}}{|y|^5}\,f(y)\,dy,
  \tag{OBPS.3}
\]
up to the harmless convention-dependent constant. The kernel is trace-free.
For points \(y=D n\) on a sphere,
\[
  \frac{3y\otimes y-|y|^2 I}{|y|^5}
  =
  D^{-3}(3n\otimes n-I).
  \tag{OBPS.4}
\]

The matrices \(3n\otimes n-I\) span the five-dimensional space
\[
  \operatorname{Sym}_0(3).
  \tag{OBPS.5}
\]

Therefore any traceless pressure Hessian \(K\in\operatorname{Sym}_0(3)\) with
\[
  |K|=H
  \tag{OBPS.6}
\]
can be generated at scaling level by a mean-zero source pattern in an annulus
\(|y|\sim D\), with source magnitude \(H\) over total volume \(\sim D^3\).
The later exact shear-cell display shows why the zero-mean condition does not
change the scale: the pressure row is a quadrupole/shear-cell row, not a free
monopole row.

## 3. Realizing signed source patterns

The source
\[
  f=\partial_i u_j\,\partial_j u_i
  \tag{OBPS.7}
\]
is not an arbitrary scalar. In a periodic divergence-free field it has exact
zero mean:
\[
  \int f\,dx=0.
  \tag{OBPS.7a}
\]

So the source-service picture is not a collection of independent signed
monopoles. The exact shear-cell display supplies the corrected object: a
mean-zero shear/quadrupole pattern can create a nonzero traceless pressure
Hessian at a zero-gradient station with the same \(HD^5\) energy scale.

At the local sign level, the two signs still appear.

A symmetric trace-free strain cell with \(|S|=a\) gives
\[
  \partial_i u_j\,\partial_j u_i
  =
  \operatorname{tr}S^2
  \sim a^2>0.
  \tag{OBPS.8}
\]

A rotation-dominated cell with skew part \(\Omega\) gives
\[
  \partial_i u_j\,\partial_j u_i
  =
  \operatorname{tr}\Omega^2
  \sim -a^2<0.
  \tag{OBPS.9}
\]

Thus a source pattern of signed magnitude \(H\) is realized at scaling level by
\[
  |\nabla u|\sim H^{1/2}.
  \tag{OBPS.10}
\]

On scale \(D\),
\[
  |u|\sim H^{1/2}D,
  \qquad
  E_{\rm src}
  \sim
  \int |u|^2
  \sim
  HD^5.
  \tag{OBPS.11}
\]

The Hodge/cutoff correction is the same kind already used in the localized
affine global-matching audit and has the same scaling.

## 4. Applying it to affine retuning

For the overlap affine strain history, the pressure Hessian demanded by the
local model has size
\[
  H\sim |\dot A|+|A|^2.
  \tag{OBPS.12}
\]

Write
\[
  G=|A|.
  \tag{OBPS.13}
\]

The pressure-source energy scale is therefore
\[
  E_{\rm ph}
  \lesssim
  (|\dot A|+G^2)D^5.
  \tag{OBPS.14}
\]

For the overlap bath,
\[
  G=s\beta^{4/5},
  \tag{OBPS.15}
\]
and producer-clock retuning means
\[
  |\dot A|\sim sG=s^2\beta^{4/5}.
  \tag{OBPS.16}
\]

The producer energy at scale \(D\) is
\[
  E_{\rm prod}\sim s^2D^5.
  \tag{OBPS.17}
\]

Thus the retuning pressure-source service costs
\[
  E_{\rm ph,retune}
  \lesssim
  s^2\beta^{4/5}D^5
  =
  \beta^{4/5}E_{\rm prod}.
  \tag{OBPS.18}
\]

The quadratic affine part costs only
\[
  E_{\rm ph,quad}
  \lesssim
  G^2D^5
  =
  \beta^{8/5}E_{\rm prod}.
  \tag{OBPS.19}
\]

So pressure-Hessian service is not as cheap as the velocity collar energy, but
it is still cheap enough to evade a fixed order-one floor.

## 5. Consequence for the bath recursion

The pressure-source channel changes the live exponent for retuning service:
\[
  q_{\rm pressure}=4/5.
  \tag{OBPS.20}
\]

The earlier collar and static jet channels had
\[
  q_{\rm collar}=q_{\rm static}=8/5.
  \tag{OBPS.21}
\]

The bath-recursion summability criterion already allows arbitrary fixed
\(q>0\). This note says the exact matrix must use \(q=4/5\) when the demanded
service is the pressure Hessian for producer-clock retuning.

For a shallowing law
\[
  x_{n+1}=p x_n,\qquad 0<p<1,
  \tag{OBPS.22}
\]
the infinite chain still fails finite-energy summability for every fixed
\(q>0\). But finite-depth and growing-depth construction tests must use the
larger pressure-source cost, not the \(\beta^{8/5}\) collar cost, on any row
where \(\dot A\) is the demanded service.

## 6. What this kills

This kills the generic pressure-source floor as a route to the confinement
constant. The pressure Poisson source does not force an order-one payer for the
overlap bath; a mean-zero quadrupole/shear source at scale \(D\) can supply the needed
traceless Hessian at energy \(HD^5\).

It also kills the too-cheap reading of the dynamic bath. The pressure-Hessian
retuning service has exponent \(4/5\), not \(8/5\), on the producer clock.

## 7. What remains

The remaining exact-matrix burden is now sharper:

- multi-direction superposition and cross-term control for pressure-source
  cells;
- exact unforced evolution of the pressure-source cells and the producing bath;
- nonlocal pressure/Hodge phase constraints beyond the mean-zero source law;
- same-packet allocation of pressure service into record-ladder currency;
- beta-depth admissibility for finite-depth or growing-depth schedules;
- or an actual dynamic bath construction satisfying the summability criterion
  with the pressure row included.

This note is not such a construction. It is a source-level scaling obstruction
to an order-one pressure-Hessian floor.

## 8. Four-sentence result

The global pressure source raises the dynamic bath cost, but it does not restore
the confinement floor. A traceless pressure Hessian of size \(H\) at the
producer can be generated at scaling level by mean-zero Poisson source patterns
on scale \(D\), and those patterns cost \(HD^5\) in velocity energy because
\(|\nabla u|\sim H^{1/2}\). For producer-clock overlap retuning,
\[
  H\sim|\dot A|\sim s^2\beta^{4/5},
\]
so the pressure-source service costs \(\beta^{4/5}E_{\rm prod}\), while the
quadratic part remains \(\beta^{8/5}E_{\rm prod}\). The live matrix now carries
a \(q=4/5\) pressure row; any proof or construction has to decide that row
through exact unforced dynamics, nonlocal pressure/Hodge structure, allocation,
or admissibility.
