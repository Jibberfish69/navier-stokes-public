# Parent-announced angle-lock finite-rotation producer collapse

## 1. Same physical edge

Fix one retained future-positive signed-height edge before record readout. The
same Navier-Stokes packet carries the pressure/projector active functional
\(m_e\), the selector-announced primitive channel \(\Gamma_e\), the material
time window, the polarity, and the possible service terms.

The adaptive angle-lock obstruction showed what is not physical: the proof may
not choose \(\Gamma_e\) after seeing the winning residue \(Z_e\). The channel
has to be carried by the parent history before readout.

Once that parent-announced channel exists, there are only two physical cases for
the same edge.

First, the pressure/projector active direction is already positive in the
selector channel. That is the angle-lock case.

Second, it is not. Then the difference is not a new kind of work. It is exactly
selector/channel/order-lock/covector/collar motion of the same packet, or
detector-silent/near-silent service. Those are the paid-rotation cases already
isolated below root projection.

## 2. Collapse of the finite-rotation burden

The finite-rotation theorem asks for

```math
[m_e(Z_e)]_+
\le
C_N[\Gamma_e(Z_e)]_+
dRot_e+dPaid_e+dLegal_e+dStop_e+dExit_e,
\tag{PAC.1}
```

with \(\sum_e dRot_e<\infty\) in original-history units.

After parent-announced selector capture, the paid-rotation split supplies the
meaning of \(dRot_e\): every failure of the projector active functional to lie
inside the selector-announced cone is charged to detector-silent service,
selector/order-lock/full-exchange motion, legal, stop, chart/collar transfer,
or exit before readout.

The producer-collapse note then removes the duplicate supplier. The finite
rotation sum is finite once the same retained edge has parent-announced selector
capture and the pre-entrance selected-critical source wall is proved:

```math
\begin{aligned}
&\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeParentAnnouncedSelectorCapture.A}\\
&+\texttt{CriticalFreshSourceTentCarleson.A}

  \texttt{FreshSourceCriticalScaleMemoryBound.A}\\

\end{aligned}
\tag{PAC.2}
```

This is not an independent theorem family. It is the same physical payment row
seen through the angle between \(m_e\) and \(\Gamma_e\).

This is a strict reduction, not proof. It removes a duplicate-looking burden
from the Gold edge. Parent-announced angle-lock with finite rotation is a
consumer of:

