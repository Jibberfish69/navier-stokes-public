## 1. Same physical event

The physical event is one same Navier-Stokes velocity field trying to raise a
signed critical-height record through a retained material packet. Transport
carries the packet, pressure re-coordinates it through the Hodge/projector
frame, viscosity gives heat-scale residence, incompressibility fixes the
volume-preserving coupling, stress alignment chooses whether nonlinear work is
positive for the record, and time orders the state changes.

The parent packet state already isolated in the repo is

```math
Z_P(t)
=
\bigl(
G_P,\ \mathsf H_P,\ \Sigma_P,\ \mathsf C_P,\ \mathsf T_P,\ \mathsf m_P
\bigr)(t),
\tag{PHT.1}
```

where \(G_P\) is the Cauchy-Green/material metric record, \(\mathsf H_P\) is the
pressure-Hodge/projector frame, \(\Sigma_P\) is the stress/current coefficient,
\(\mathsf C_P\) is the collar/normal state, \(\mathsf T_P\) is
turnstile/exchange, and \(\mathsf m_P\) is the admitted parent carrier/current.

The active-state capacity theorem asks for more than coordinates on `(PHT.1)'.
It asks the original parent packet to own, before selected readout, the finite
state space in which later active child events live. In physical words: the
fluid is allowed to keep moving, rotating, aligning, and exchanging, but every
new selected active event must either delete accessible future state or pay a
real same-fluid boundary, service, legal, stop, or exit cost.

## 2. Half-tail pressure test

The physical half-tail is the test for whether a proposed proof sees the right
object. A heat-scale child may have shrinking raw residence while still being a
full selected critical event:

```math
R_{raw}(Q_r)\sim r,
\qquad
A(Q_r)\sim 1.
\tag{PHT.2}
```

Along a laminar scale sequence this is the same obstruction as

```math
\sum_\ell \nu_\ell<\infty,
\qquad
\sum_\ell 2^\ell\nu_\ell=\infty.
\tag{PHT.3}
```

The first sum is what raw physical residence can see. The second sum is the
selected critical cost the Gold route must control. Thus a candidate capacity
which only integrates raw energy, raw heat residence, collar area, raw elliptic
capacity, or unweighted geometric overlap can be finite on `(PHT.3)' while the
selected record events remain infinite.

This is not a Navier-Stokes counterexample. It is the proof-theoretic acceptance
test: a Gold source theorem must explain why the same fluid packet cannot keep
turning finite raw residence into unit selected active-state claims without
deleting a finite parent-owned state.

## 3. What the current support proves

The fixed-chart material formulas prove that, inside one chosen chart, the
primitive signed-channel measure has legal transport/pressure coordinates.
Physically, they describe how the same velocity field is read once a material
chart and active scale have already been chosen.

The pressure-Hodge/projector and collar-service surfaces prove that off-angle,
detector-silent, or collar-moving pieces can be routed as service or support.
Physically, they explain how pressure compatibility and transported normals are
measured.

The finite-band reset and active-transit overlap surfaces prove that once an
active state is already parent-known, later reset/first-exit transactions cannot
be counted with arbitrary multiplicity. Physically, they pay repeated address
changes after the state has an address.

The record native-work menu identity proves that the signed-height record work
has legal low-high, high-low, and resonant native-work coordinates. Physically,
it decomposes the nonlinear work of the same velocity field.

The TFE active/full-exchange storage rows prove how already admitted current,
critical source, finite-excess, active-transit, and full-exchange faces fit into
one coupled matrix. Physically, they say how a stored parent ledger depletes
once the record work and active faces are in the ledger.

None of those support facts proves that the parent packet builds the active
state space before the selected readout. Against `(PHT.2)'--`(PHT.3)', they can
all be true while the selected critical action is still invisible to the raw
budget.

## 4. Direct candidates that fail this test

A raw-energy or heat-residence capacity fails because it measures \(r\) in
`(PHT.2)' while the selected active event costs one.

A fixed material-chart capacity fails because the formula is local in an
already chosen chart. The missing object is the parent-known active chart,
scale, collar, and projector ownership before the future-positive event is
selected.

A pressure-Hodge or collar-service capacity fails because service can pay
detector-silent or off-chart pieces after they are classified. The missing
object is the parent state's finite accessible future, not the local service
readout.

A finite reset-overlap capacity fails because reset counting starts after a
first active state exists. It does not create that first state or show that the
first state is finite from original data.

A freestanding critical carrier fails under the signed-height restart because
the record work may disappear from that carrier. The carrier is Gold-relevant
only when the record work is admitted into it before readout.

A sequential storage proof fails because the current repo chain recycles
full-exchange storage into critical storage and critical storage back into
full-exchange storage. That is only valid after absorption through one coupled
same-parent inequality with feedback spectral radius strictly below one.
