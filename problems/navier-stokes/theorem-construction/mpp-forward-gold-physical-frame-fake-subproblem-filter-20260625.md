# Physical-Frame Fake-Subproblem Filter

Date: 2026-06-25

## 0. Governing object

The object is one original smooth incompressible Navier--Stokes material
history:

```math
\partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p=\nu\Delta u,
\qquad
\nabla\cdot u=0.
\tag{PFF.1}
```

The material map satisfies

```math
\dot X(a,t)=u(X(a,t),t),
\qquad
\det D_aX(a,t)=1.
\tag{PFF.2}
```

The spike, its transported annular layers, the pressure response,
incompressibility, viscosity, nonlinear frequency transfer, and later
stress/strain return are one material history. Heat-scale packets are samples
of that history. They are not independent events and not free annular signals.

This physical frame is now a send-veto for the proof route: no subproblem is a
primary route unless it is a coordinate of this same material history and
produces the full clock without assuming it.

## 1. What the physical frame already eliminates

### 1.1 Detached endpoint pulse

A center pulse cannot be read as a center-only endpoint atom once a transported
annular cutoff is used. For

```math
(\partial_t+u\cdot\nabla)\phi=0,
\qquad
T=-pI+2\nu S,
\tag{PFF.3}
```

the exact same-material identity is

```math
{d\over dt}\int {1\over2}|u|^2\phi\,dx
+
2\nu\int\phi |S|^2\,dx
=
-\int u\cdot T\nabla\phi\,dx .
\tag{PFF.4}
```

So center storage change is attached to the same-fluid annular stress/strain
history before any terminal limit is taken.

### 1.2 Arbitrary annular time signal

The annular flux is not a free curve. After gauge reduction,

```math
F_\phi^\sharp(t)=\int U_iT^\sharp_{ij}\partial_j\phi\,dx,
\qquad
T^\sharp=-PI+2\nu S(U),
\qquad
D_tU=\nabla\cdot T^\sharp .
\tag{PFF.5}
```

Its derivative is generated by the same pressure-viscosity-incompressibility-
velocity packet:

```math
{d\over dt}F_\phi^\sharp
=
\int(\nabla\cdot T^\sharp)_iT^\sharp_{ij}\partial_j\phi
+
\int U_i(D_tT^\sharp_{ij})\partial_j\phi
-
\int U_iT^\sharp_{ij}(\partial_jU_k)(\partial_k\phi).
\tag{PFF.6}
```

Thus oscillation of the annular return is not an independent signal problem.
It is pressure-stress, viscous-strain, cubic-collar, and transported-collar
action of the same material packet.

### 1.3 Initial infinite frequencies

Smooth data may contain infinitely many Fourier modes. What matters is finite
amplitude at every fixed continuation depth:

```math
\sum_j 2^{2Nj}\|\Delta_j u_0\|_2^2<\infty
\qquad
\text{for every fixed }N.
\tag{PFF.7}
```

The initial spectrum and the linear viscous heat tail are not the terminal
producer. Continued high-frequency annular action must be generated by the
same nonlinear pressure-viscosity-incompressibility-velocity evolution.

### 1.4 Viscosity-only and pressure-only routes

Viscous drain is not full participation. Pressure recovery is not full
participation. The affine incompressible strain normal form

```math
u(x)=Ax,
\qquad
p(x)=-{1\over2}x\cdot A^2x,
\qquad
A=A^T,\quad \operatorname{tr}A=0,
\tag{PFF.8}
```

satisfies

```math
(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p=0,
\qquad
\Delta u=0,
\qquad
\nabla\cdot u=0,
\tag{PFF.9}
```

while material lines stretch and thin. This proves that the payment is the full
annular pressure/strain transaction, not an interior viscosity-only drain and
not a pressure-only scalar.

### 1.5 Cauchy-Green-only shape storage

The Cauchy-Green identity is exact:

```math
F=D_aX,
\qquad
C=F^TF,
\qquad
\partial_tC=2F^TSF.
\tag{PFF.10}
```

It reads the spike's material shape history correctly. It does not by itself
give a bounded-below one-way storage. Any local primitive cancelling positive
stretching has the form

```math
X(t)=-c\log |D_aX(t)e|,
\tag{PFF.11}
```

and this is unbounded below along a long positive strain history. Therefore
Cauchy-Green shape data is a coordinate of the same object, not the producer of
finite positive material action.

### 1.6 Pure high-frequency velocity tail

The genuine pure velocity high-high tail is supported by the installed
gradient/cubic absorption after the strict-low and spill splits. The remaining
full annular density is stronger: it contains active pressure-time elliptic
service and cubic collar strain service. Those are not paid by the existing
fixed-threshold high-tail scheduler.

### 1.7 Raw pressure-time symbol

The raw \(D_tP\) symbol can be moved inside the packet. With

```math
Y_\phi(t)=\int P\,U\cdot\nabla\phi\,dx,
\tag{PFF.12}
```

one has

```math
-\int D_tP\,U\cdot\nabla\phi
=
-{d\over dt}Y_\phi
+
\int P(\nabla\cdot T^\sharp)\cdot\nabla\phi
-
\int P\,U_i(\partial_iU_k)(\partial_k\phi).
\tag{PFF.13}
```

This removes a fake time-oscillator. It leaves same-annulus pressure-stress and
pressure-transport action. Those terms are part of the full clock, not legal
residual.
