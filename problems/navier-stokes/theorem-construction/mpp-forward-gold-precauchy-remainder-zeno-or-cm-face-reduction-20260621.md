# MPP Forward-Gold Pre-Cauchy Remainder Zeno Or CM Face Reduction

Date: 2026-06-21

## Status

This note links the full pre-Cauchy carrier boundary to the terminal
source-curve pass-or-exit theorem.

It does not prove a forward-gold anti-atom theorem.  It proves the exact
placement of the remaining branch after the already-paid pieces have been
removed.

## 1. Starting point

The full selected positive pre-Cauchy carrier is

\[
\mu^+_{src,N}(\mathcal F_N)
=
\sum_{P\in\mathcal F_N}
\int_{Q(P)}
\left[
\mathcal N^{loc}_{preCauchy}(u;P)
\right]_+\,dxdt .
\tag{PZC.1}
\]

The main trilinear/stretching carrier has already been separated.  A terminal
atom in that main carrier is finite-rung Field-facing after same-witness CM
admission, and it has the exact combined Holder consumer

\[
\left[\left\langle \Sigma W,W\right\rangle\right]_+
\le
{2\over5}|\Sigma|^{5/2}
+
{3\over5}|W|^{10/3}.
\tag{PZC.2}
\]

The remaining object is therefore the selected pre-Cauchy remainder created by
localization, weights, lifts, projection, cutoff, terminal selection, and
positive-part extraction.

## 4. Terminal source-curve split

Let the time marginal of the selected source on the normalized Zeno packets be

\[
\mu_m(ds)=a_m(s)\,ds,
\qquad
a_m\ge0,
\qquad
s\in[-1,0],
\tag{PZC.8}
\]

with finite mass

\[
\sup_m\mu_m([-1,0])<\infty.
\tag{PZC.9}
\]

The terminal source-curve lemma gives the exact alternative.

Either the endpoint modulus holds:

\[
\lim_{\theta\downarrow0}
\limsup_m
\mu_m((-\theta,0])
=0,
\tag{PZC.10}
\]

\[
\mu_m\stackrel{*}{\rightharpoonup}\mu,
\qquad

\tag{PZC.11}
\]

The first branch is the forward-gold no-jump/source-residence branch.  It is
equivalent here to a genuine no-free terminal Zeno theorem supplied by

\[

\qquad
\tau_m\downarrow0,
\tag{PZC.12}

## 5. Same-witness CM consequence

Before same-witness CM admission, `(PZC.11)` is support/readout material.  It
does not prove the forward endpoint modulus.

After same-witness admission, the service-certification theorem applies.
Survival through `Part` or `Field` requires a uniform service modulus for the

\[

\le
\omega(\theta).
\tag{PZC.13}
\]

\[
\lim_{\theta\downarrow0}
\limsup_m
\mu_m((-\theta,0])

\tag{PZC.14}
\]

Therefore the same-witness terminal atom cannot survive the certified service
test:

\[
\boxed{

\Longrightarrow
\neg Part_{N,Q}
\ \text{or}\
\forall\rho>0\,\neg Field_{N,\rho,Q}.
}
\tag{PZC.15}
\]

This is a CM face conclusion.  It is not a new Navier-Stokes smoothing or
anti-atom estimate.
