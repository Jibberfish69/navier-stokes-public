# Predictable high-high detector net obstruction

Supersession note. This note correctly shows that a post-child raw output net is
not a free payment mechanism. Its input-coloured high-high target is not a
one-point parent-current object. The replacement is now typed by carrier:
full-output stress for the one-point route, or a two-point/product-carrier
frame for retained input-coloured triads.

## 1. Target

The previous high-high note reduced the remaining branch to a predictable
detector admission theorem. For input-coloured same-scale high-high positive
transfer, the old desired theorem was:

For each retained positive high-high triad output witness, there exists a
parent-predictable material adjoint detector \(\psi_i^P\), chosen before child
readout from a bounded-entropy parent frame, such that

\[
T_{HH,k\ell m}^+
\lesssim
|\langle J_P,\psi_i^P\rangle|^2
+\varepsilon\nu D_{k\ell m}+legal+stop,
\]

and the fresh pieces of the \(\psi_i^P\) family Bessel-pack after parent subtraction.

## 2. Raw net theorem is false

The raw theorem over the full output unit sphere is false. In an infinite-dimensional output space, no parent-predictable finite or scale-critical net can uniformly represent all possible post-child selected unit witnesses without paying entropy. A selector that sees the child product can choose a unit vector \(g\) concentrated in a direction absent from the parent net at the required accuracy.

Concretely, let \(H_N\) be an N-dimensional subspace of output witnesses not already represented in the parent frame. If the parent net has cardinality M with M << exp(cN), there exists a unit vector \(g\in H_N\) at fixed angular separation from every net point. A high-high product with output direction \(g\) then gives positive transfer against \(g\), while every parent-predictable detector under-resolves it. Passing N upward breaks any scale-critical entropy bound.

Thus the raw map

\[
\text{post-child selected output witness}
\to
\text{parent-predictable detector net}
\]

cannot be free. It must be supplied by additional structure.

## 3. What structure can save it

Only three legal structures remain consistent with the Schur/Bessel/motion route.

First, the output witness is not arbitrary: it is generated by the same material high-high stress

\[
P_m\nabla\cdot(u_k\otimes u_\ell)
\]

on one stopped parent carrier. Then the correct parent frame is not the whole output sphere; it is the material wave-packet frame transported by the parent flow and restricted to the retained high-high stress support.

Second, finite residence/viscous service must bound the number of independent high-high output directions that can be retained before dissipation, return, or stop. This supplies a finite rank / Carleson packing rule for the parent frame.

Third, any change of frame, carrier, collar, or selected output direction after parent declaration must be charged as motion/reselection through the already installed first-exit route ledger.

## 4. Replacement theorem

The next admissible theorem is therefore not a raw predictable net theorem. It is a same-carrier wave-packet frame theorem:

For each stopped parent packet P and each retained high-high scale m, construct a parent-declared material wave-packet frame \(\mathcal W_{P,m}\) such that every positive retained high-high output witness admits

\[
g_{HH}=g_{inh}+g_{nov}+g_{mot}+g_{visc}+g_{legal}
\]

with:

\[
g_{inh}\in \operatorname{span}_{parent}\mathcal W_{P,m}
\quad\text{and is Schur-visible},
\]

\[
\sum \|g_{nov}\|^2\lesssim \|J_P\|^2+E_{route},
\]

\[
\sum \|g_{mot}\|_K^2\lesssim \int S\|K\|^2+E_{route},
\]

and

\[
g_{visc}\text{ is absorbed by }\varepsilon\nu D_m.
\]

The frame must be declared from the parent material carrier, not from post-child maximization.

## 5. Minimal obstruction profile

A genuine counterexample to the Gold route must have all of the following:

1. same-scale high-high product produces a positive \(H^{1/2}\) output witness;
2. the witness is selected only after child readout;
3. it has no representative in any parent-declared material wave-packet frame with scale-critical entropy;
4. it is not parent-orthogonal Bessel novelty with finite residence;
5. it is not created by paid frame/collar/carrier/selector motion;
6. it is not absorbable by the local viscous term.

This is now the exact obstruction. Any missing fourth mechanism would have to pay this profile. No fourth payment exists in the current route.

## 6. Outcome

The raw predictable high-high detector net theorem fails. The live replacement target is:

\[
\boxed{\text{Parent-declared same-carrier wave-packet frame admission for retained high-high stress.}}
\]

That theorem is strictly sharper than generic high-high material lift. It states the exact structure needed to make high-high positive transfer compatible with Schur capacity, Bessel novelty, motion payment, and viscous absorption.
