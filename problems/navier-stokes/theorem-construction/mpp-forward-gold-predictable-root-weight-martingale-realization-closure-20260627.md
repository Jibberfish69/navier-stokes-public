## 1. Root measure and lifted space

```math

H_P(t,x)\,dt\,dx
```

where \(H_P\) is the parent-predictable active weight generated by the stopped
finite selector score system and the original scale-memory history.  The
measure is chosen before descendant selected positive readout.

Use the lifted same-packet Hilbert space

```math
\mathcal H_{\rm lift}
=
\mathcal H_{\rm vis}
\oplus
\mathcal H_{\rm sil}
\oplus
\mathcal H_{\rm ex},
```

where:

1. the visible component is the parent Schur / pressure-Hodge trace part;
2. the silent component is the source-origin normal-form part: unselected gauge
   material or selected-but-paid material;
3. the exchange component is top-strain, material-service, turnstile, covector,
   collar, and legal synchronization service.

Set

```math
\mathfrak H_P
=
L^2(d\nu_P;\mathcal H_{\rm lift}).
```

This is the common predictable root geometry required by the Bessel consumer.

## 2. Parent affine spans

Let \(\mathcal P_e\subset\mathfrak H_P\) be the closed span of all affine
quotient transition components retained before edge \(e\), together with the


## 3. Fresh innovations

The fresh terms satisfy the stopped martingale-difference condition:

```math

\tag{2}
```

Along a laminar stopped tree, siblings are disjoint up to legal overlap, and
descendants see previous innovations inside the parent span.  Therefore the
fresh family is orthogonal in \(\mathfrak H_P\), modulo legal overlap.

The Bessel consumer gives

```math

C\sum_e\int H_{e^-}\|D_e\|_{\mathcal H_{\rm lift}}^2
```

```math

C_N(u_0)\mathcal R(P)+CR_{\rm legal}(P).
\tag{4}
```

projection record/return payment, not to the fresh Bessel estimate.


