# Pressure Elliptic Energy Participation Coupling Test

Date: 2026-06-25

## 0. Object

The object is one original smooth material history:

```math
\partial_t v+A^\top\nabla_a q
=
\nu\,\operatorname{div}_a(G\nabla_a v),
\qquad
\operatorname{div}_a(Av)=0,
\tag{PEE.1}
```

with

```math
F=D_aX,\qquad A=F^{-1},\qquad G=AA^\top .
\tag{PEE.2}
```

The pressure equation is

```math
L_Gq=R,
\qquad
L_G=-\operatorname{div}_a(G\nabla_a),
\tag{PEE.3}
```

where

```math
R
=
A_{\ell i}A_{k j}
(\partial_{a_\ell}v_j)
(\partial_{a_k}v_i).
\tag{PEE.4}
```

This is the pressure/incompressibility coordinate of the same participation
law.  It is not a separate pressure-only route.

## 1. Instantaneous elliptic pressure energy

Fix the periodic zero-mean gauge, or the corresponding retained local pressure
gauge on an annular packet.  Define

```math
E_p(t)
:=
{1\over2}\langle q,L_Gq\rangle
=
{1\over2}\int G\nabla_aq\cdot\nabla_aq\,da
=
{1\over2}\langle q,R\rangle .
\tag{PEE.5}
```

At each fixed time, ellipticity gives

```math
E_p(t)\ge {1\over2}\lambda_G(t)\|\nabla_aq(t)\|_2^2 .
\tag{PEE.6}
```

So fixed-time pressure ellipticity is real: it controls the instantaneous
pressure tower once the material frame is retained.

## 2. Exact derivative of pressure energy

Differentiate `(PEE.5)` using \(L_Gq=R\):

```math
{d\over dt}E_p
=
{1\over2}\langle q,\dot L_Gq\rangle
+\langle \dot q,L_Gq\rangle .
\tag{PEE.7}
```

Since

```math
L_G\dot q=\dot R-\dot L_Gq,
\tag{PEE.8}
```

self-adjointness gives

```math
\langle \dot q,L_Gq\rangle
=
\langle q,L_G\dot q\rangle
=
\langle q,\dot R-\dot L_Gq\rangle .
\tag{PEE.9}
```

Therefore

```math
\boxed{
{d\over dt}E_p
=
\langle q,\dot R\rangle
-
{1\over2}\langle q,\dot L_Gq\rangle .
}
\tag{PEE.10}
```

The operator derivative is

```math
\dot G=-2ASA^\top,
\qquad
\dot L_G f
=
2\,\operatorname{div}_a(ASA^\top\nabla_af).
\tag{PEE.11}
```

Thus

```math
-{1\over2}\langle q,\dot L_Gq\rangle
=
\int ASA^\top\nabla_aq\cdot\nabla_aq\,da .
\tag{PEE.12}
```

The exact pressure energy derivative is

```math
\boxed{
{d\over dt}E_p
=
\langle q,\dot R\rangle
+
\int ASA^\top\nabla_aq\cdot\nabla_aq\,da .
}
\tag{PEE.13}
```

## 3. What this proves

Equation `(PEE.13)` proves attachment.  The pressure operator cannot change
without the same material strain \(S\), the same pressure RHS \(R\), and the
same material coefficient field \(A\).  The pressure elliptic law is therefore
inside the pressure-viscosity-incompressibility-velocity participation law.

It also proves that fixed ellipticity is not a one-way producer for rectified
annular action.  The term

```math
\int ASA^\top\nabla_aq\cdot\nabla_aq\,da
\tag{PEE.14}
```

has the sign of the material strain in the direction of the pressure gradient.
Incompressibility fixes volume, but it does not make \(S\) sign-definite.

The other term,

```math
\langle q,\dot R\rangle,
\tag{PEE.15}
```

contains the differentiated pressure source.  Using

```math
R=\operatorname{tr}(B^2),
\qquad
\dot B=-B^2-(\nabla_x^2p)\circ X+\nu(\Delta_xB)\circ X,
\tag{PEE.16}
```

it is generated by cubic strain/vorticity, pressure Hessian, and viscous tower
service on the same material packet.

Thus the pressure energy derivative is exactly the same signed
strain/pressure-Hessian service already identified in the material clock.

## 4. Why this does not close rectified variation

The annular proof needs positive total pressure/strain service, schematically

```math
\int_0^{T_*}[\mathcal M^{press\text{-}strain}(t)]_+\,dt<\infty .
\tag{PEE.17}
```

The pressure energy identity gives a signed derivative:

```math
\int_I {d\over dt}E_p(t)\,dt
=E_p(b)-E_p(a).
\tag{PEE.18}
```

Since \(E_p\ge0\), this controls a net storage change.  It does not control
the rectified positive variation of `(PEE.14)` and `(PEE.15)`.  Repeated
upward/downward pressure-strain service can have small net change in \(E_p\)
while still carrying large total positive annular action.

Therefore the valid conclusion is:

```math
\boxed{
\text{pressure ellipticity licenses the pressure tower and proves same-packet
attachment, but it does not by itself produce finite rectified annular action.}
}
\tag{PEE.19}
```

## 5. Live consequence

The remaining producer is unchanged but now cleaner:

```math
\boxed{
\int_0^{T_*}
\mathcal R_N^{strain\text{-}Hess}(t)\,dt<\infty ,
}
\tag{PEE.20}
```

where the density is the same material strain, pressure-Hessian, pressure-RHS,
coefficient, viscous-tower, and collar service appearing in `(PEE.13)` and in
the annular force-action variation formula.

So the pressure side stays coupled to participation.  The missing line is a
same-fluid no-recirculation / no-free-upward-transfer law for the rectified
pressure-strain pump, not a fixed-ellipticity estimate.
