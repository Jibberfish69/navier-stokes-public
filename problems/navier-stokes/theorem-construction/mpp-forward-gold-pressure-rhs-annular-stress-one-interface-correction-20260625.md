# Pressure/RHS And Annular Stress Are One Interface

Date: 2026-06-25

## 0. Correction

The live edge had drifted into a two-source wording:

```math
\text{pressure/RHS service}
\quad+\quad
\text{annular stress-work service}.
```

That is not the right object.

The pressure/RHS terms are generated by differentiating the same material
pressure equation and the same stress tensor on the same transported annulus.
They are not pressure-only, and they are not a second route beside annular
stress work.

The single object is the moving material pressure-strain interface.

## 1. Checked installed facts

The Cauchy stress-power identity gives, for incompressible Navier--Stokes,

```math
T:S
=
(-pI+2\nu S):S
=
2\nu |S|^2,
\qquad
\operatorname{tr}S=0.
\tag{PRAS.1}
```

So pressure has no internal volumetric work. Its role is boundary traction and
incompressibility service on the same material interface.

The material annular energy identity is

```math
{d\over dt}
\int_{A(t)}{1\over2}|u|^2\,dx
+
2\nu\int_{A(t)}|S|^2\,dx
=
\int_{\partial A(t)}u\cdot Tn\,dS .
\tag{PRAS.2}
```

This proves attachment: a center storage change is carried by same-fluid
boundary traction exchange plus finite raw internal strain action.

The pressure elliptic equation is part of the same participation law:

```math
-\Delta P=\partial_iU_j\,\partial_jU_i .
\tag{PRAS.3}
```

Differentiating it in the material flow produces pressure-Hessian, pressure-RHS,
strain, coefficient, and viscous tower service. Those are the same interface
coordinates already present in the material annular stress-work derivative.

## 2. What is paid already

The following parts are no longer independent live proof sources.

First, pure top-strain self-stretch is paid by the bounded-below logarithmic
top-strain storage.

Second, coefficient-frame motion is paid by the same top-strain storage because

```math
\dot G=-2ASA^\top,
\qquad
\|G^{-1/2}\dot G G^{-1/2}\|_{op}\le 2\|S\|_{op}.
\tag{PRAS.4}
```

Third, base transported collar motion is paid by the same frame/strain clock:

```math
\nabla_x\phi=A^\top\nabla_a\psi,
\qquad
|D_t\log|\nabla_x\phi||\le\|S\|_{op}.
\tag{PRAS.5}
```

Fourth, higher collar/tower time variation is demoted to same-depth service on
heat windows by the factor \(2^{-2j}\). It does not create an independent
higher-tower source.

## 3. What remains

The remaining smooth prelimit line is exactly the signed commutator/coercivity
line isolated in the June 23 audit:

```math

+
dX_{comm,m}
+
c\,d[\log(1+\mathcal P_{N,m}^{mat})]_+
\le
\varepsilon dD_{N,m}^{vis}

\tag{PRAS.6}
```

Equivalently, in the annular physical language, it is finite rectified
variation of the moving pressure-strain interface:

```math
\int_0^{T_*}\sum_j [m_j(t)]_+\,dt<\infty .
\tag{PRAS.7}
```

Here \(m_j(t)\) is the signed same-shell material pressure-strain pump from the
weighted shell reservoir identity. The signed donor law proves attachment and
payback:

```math
T_{j\leftarrow\ell}^{(k)}
+
T_{\ell\leftarrow j}^{(k)}
=0.
\tag{PRAS.8}
```

It does not by itself prove finite rectified recirculation:

```math
\int_0^{T_*}\sum_j |m_j(t)|\,dt<\infty .
\tag{PRAS.9}
```

That rectified interface variation is the same mathematical burden as the
signed smooth same-packet commutator/coercivity theorem.
