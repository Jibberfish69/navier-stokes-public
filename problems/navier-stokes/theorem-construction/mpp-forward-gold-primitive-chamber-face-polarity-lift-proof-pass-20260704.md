# Primitive chamber face/polarity lift proof pass

## 1. Physical object

Work inside one admitted stopped common-selector chamber \(C_{\mathfrak R}\). The
selector, chart/projector frame, and detector/channel readout have already been
fixed on the same primitive signed-channel packet. The face/polarity question is
whether the event has a pre-readout face owner and sign owner before selected-face
counting.

The same physical packet carries a primitive signed channel

\[
Y_{\kappa,C}\,d\sigma dt ,
\tag{FPL.1}
\]

generated by pressure, transport/frame motion, viscosity, incompressibility,
material collar motion, and paid legal/stopped errors on the chamber. A lawful
face/polarity coordinate means this signed channel can be restricted to one
pre-readout face of that packet, and loss of positive sign on that face is paid
by the negative part of the same face-local channel or by physical return,
rotation, diffusion, legal stop, or exit.

## 2. Two physical inputs

The trace input is:

\[
\texttt{PrimitiveStoppedChamberFaceTraceFiberOrPaidDetachment.A}.
\tag{FPL.2}
\]

It supplies a pre-readout face fiber

\[
\pi_F:C_{\mathfrak R}\to \mathcal F_C
\tag{FPL.3}
\]

for \(|Y_{\kappa,C}|\,d\sigma dt\)-almost every primitive event, or routes the
untraced part to face detachment, zero-thickness loss, legal stop, material
service, or exit. The fiber is parent-known chamber data, not a face chosen after
the positive lobe is read.

The sign-return input is:

\[
\texttt{PrimitiveStoppedChamberSameFaceSignedReturnBudgetOrPaidPolarity.A}.
\tag{FPL.4}
\]

On each pre-readout face \(F\in\mathcal F_C\), it gives a signed work channel

\[
dJ_{C,F}:=\mathbf 1_{\{\pi_F=F\}}Y_{\kappa,C}\,d\sigma dt
=dJ_{C,F}^{+}-dJ_{C,F}^{-}
\tag{FPL.5}
\]

with local lost-positive domination

\[
dL_{C,F}
\le
C\,dJ_{C,F}^{-}
+dPaid_{C,F}+dLegal_{C,F}+dStop_{C,F}+dExit_{C,F}
\tag{FPL.6}
\]

and original-history finite budget over disjoint chamber faces:

\[
\sum_{C,F}\int dJ_{C,F}^{-}
\le
C_N(u_0)+Paid+Legal+Stop+Exit+Err .
\tag{FPL.7}
\]

## 3. Conditional proof of face/polarity lift

Assume `(FPL.2)' and `(FPL.4)'. For every primitive event in the surviving part
of \(C_{\mathfrak R}\), define its face/polarity coordinate by

\[
\mathfrak F_C(z):=(\pi_F(z),\operatorname{sgn}Y_{\kappa,C}(z)).
\tag{FPL.8}
\]

Events where \(\pi_F\) is not defined have already been routed to the paid
detachment/legal/stop/exit side by `(FPL.2)'. Events where the positive sign is
lost without survival are controlled face-by-face by `(FPL.6)' and the finite
budget `(FPL.7)'.

Thus the same admitted primitive event has one pre-readout face owner and one
lawful sign/return account, modulo paid/legal/stop/exit pieces:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\texttt{PrimitiveStoppedChamberFaceTraceFiberOrPaidDetachment.A}\\
&+\texttt{PrimitiveStoppedChamberSameFaceSignedReturnBudgetOrPaidPolarity.A}
\end{aligned}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{PrimitiveStoppedChamberFacePolarityLiftOrPaidReturn.A}.
\tag{FPL.9}
\]

This proves the conditional lift asserted in `(NCL.13)'.

## 4. Why this is not yet the PDE proof

The proof above starts after the two physical inputs are available. Current
record-face sources show why those inputs are still open at primitive chamber
level.

First, a formal same-face localization proof already exists once the face-local
signed channel \(dJ_{C,F}\) exists. It does not construct that channel from the
pressure, transport, viscosity, incompressibility, detector, chart, and parent
address data of the original packet.

Second, primitive PLS signed-channel decomposition gives the signed channel
alphabet on retained stopped common-selector chambers. It does not by itself
assign every primitive event to a pre-readout face fiber.

Third, chamber-level negative variation can pay the wrong face:

\[
dL_A=dx,\qquad dJ_A^{-}=0,\qquad dL_B=0,\qquad dJ_B^{-}=dx .
\tag{FPL.10}
\]

The total return balances globally, but face \(A\) loses positive sign with no
same-face return. This is a type obstruction to total-return, scalar BV,
terminal anti-atom, or post-readout LP/Bony bookkeeping substitutes.

## 5. Current proof truth

The face/polarity coordinate has now been separated cleanly:

\[
\text{conditional proof of lift}
\quad+\quad
\text{open PDE construction of the two inputs}.
\tag{FPL.11}
\]

The closest noncircular Gold-facing theorem is:

\[
\texttt{PrimitiveChamberFaceLocalSignedChannelConstruction.A}
\tag{FPL.12}
\]

meaning: on one admitted stopped common-selector chamber, before selected-face
counting, construct the pre-readout face fiber of the selector-owned primitive
signed channel and prove the negative part of that same face-local channel has
finite original-data budget.
