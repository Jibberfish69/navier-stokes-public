# Primitive full-action scale-native depletion synthesis

## 1. One physical object

The object is one original smooth Navier-Stokes packet carried by the same
velocity field. Transport moves it, pressure and incompressibility re-coordinate
it, viscosity spends raw heat-scale energy, and the selected critical record
reads whether the packet makes a unit upward move at a smaller scale.

After the pressure/detector branch is made robust, the remaining bad event is no
longer detector invisibility. It is this:

```math
\text{the same material history makes infinitely many scale-native first births,}
\tag{SND.1}
```

each birth has finite raw heat/viscous/collar/legal cost, but each has unit size
in the selected critical metric. In symbols the obstruction has the same
half-tail form:

```math
\|M_{\gamma_k}\|_{\rm raw}^2\simeq r_k,
\qquad
\omega_{\gamma_k}\simeq 1,
\qquad
\sum_k r_k<\infty,
\qquad
\sum_k\omega_{\gamma_k}=\infty.
\tag{SND.2}
```

This is not a physical Navier-Stokes counterexample. It is the proof-state
enemy: local service, local endpoint seeds, and raw energy can all hold while
the selected critical birth count remains unbounded.

## 2. What is already support

The current repo already pays the following as support or consumers:

- pressure-visible record work by parent detector currency;
- exact detector-silent and detector-near-silent work by local same-packet
  service routing;
- complete-frame orientation: positive primitive pump has a compression partner;
- laminar ancestry after paid nonlaminar exits;
- selector/reselection and angle drift after a parent-known connection ledger;
- bounded-Riesz endpoint reads by parent-current reuse and Bessel packing;
- return, reset, exchange, legal, stop, and subheat branches.

These facts keep the physical story honest. They do not yet insert the missing
critical weight into a finite original-history reserve.

## 3. Why endpoint entropy is not the bottom theorem

Endpoint entropy is the right physical language only after the selected endpoint
metric is parent-known and critical. The bounded-Riesz branch is already paid:
bounded parent stress tests Bessel-pack, exact inherited reads are parent
current, and changed inherited reads are reset, angle, selector, collar, legal,
or stop motion.

The surviving branch is scale-native. A unit selected test at scale \(r\) pulls
back to original stress norm of size \(r^{-1}\). Finite raw stress energy sees
cost \(\sim r\), while the selected critical readout is \(\sim1\). Therefore no
parent metric controlled only by finite original \(L^2\) stress energy can prove
the endpoint upper edge for scale-native units.

The endpoint entropy law is thus a consumer of the same missing critical
reserve, not a separate magic storage source.

## 5. What remains open

The support-level mathematical burden is not another pressure-only estimate,
raw energy reserve, endpoint count, or local source-attachment theorem.

It is:

```math
\boxed{
\text{construct the same-parent critical depletion/no-Zeno package from the
original pressure-viscosity-incompressibility-velocity packet.}
}
\tag{SND.6}
```

In physical words: the proof must show that the same original packet cannot keep
creating selected-critical first births faster than the packet deletes,
returns, resets, exchanges, dissipates, legally exits, or occupies positive
terminal thickness in one parent-known critical carrier.

object. It becomes Gold-relevant only when a signed critical-height record

theorem may control a support carrier that does not contain the record work.
