# Radiodrome as a participation-tower relay defect

## 0. Correction of the object

The object is one VPI fluid-field read up its participation tower. In the
membership surface, `Part_Q` keeps the tower attached to the same retained
fluid object, and `Field_Q` supplies one-field finite-depth tower coherence.
The shared tower law writes, for every rung,

```math
D_t U_k = K_k+B_k,
\qquad
K_k=-\nabla^{k+1}p+\nu\Delta U_k .
\tag{RTR.1}
```

At the strain rung this is the Riccati readout

```math
D_tS+S^2+\Omega^2+\mathrm{Hess}(q)=\nu\Delta S .
\tag{RTR.2}
```

So `Hess(q)` is not the object being chased. It is one local pressure reading
inside the same field's pressure-viscosity carrier. The runner/payer split is a
coordinate on the one tower:

- runner: the nonlinear split cascade, with `S^2` at the strain rung;
- payer: the common pressure-viscosity carrier `K_k`, with
  `Hess(q)+nu Delta S` at the strain rung;
- common mode: the rotational/co-moving part, represented by `Omega^2` in
  `(RTR.2)`, which is not an external payer.

The canonical affine packet already proves that no local jet, pointwise angle,
clipping, eigenframe gap, or pressure-Hessian rigidity can supply the strict
below-`1/2` margin. The radiodrome idea survives only after being lifted to the
material participation tower.

## 1. Native relay-lag measure

The full-tower signed-total exchange attempt gives the installed material
record

```math

1+\Gamma+E_N^v+E_N^q+E_N^{A,G}+E^{collar},

```

```math

```

The pulled-back material tower also gives signed adjacent exchange currents

```math

\tag{RTR.5}
```

Net summation cancels signed exchange, while `(RTR.4)` measures total positive
variation. That mismatch is exactly where radiodrome belongs. Define the
radiodromic tower-relay defect as the minimal nonnegative measure
`dL_N^{rad}` for which

```math

\le
C\,dA_{4B,N}
```

already-enveloped legal residuals. Equivalently, `dL_N^{rad}` is the positive
material-record growth generated by the same tower that is not retained as a

This is the corrected pursuit cost. It is not the angular speed of a detached
pressure Hessian. It is the unpaid re-aiming of the pressure-viscosity carrier
against the nonlinear split cascade along the same material tower.
