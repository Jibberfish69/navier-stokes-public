# Raw zoom selector to native birth-packing live edge

The bounded original-packet projection argument is installed only for selected
units that have already been admitted as bounded reads of the original material
stress history.

On a stopped parent history \(P\), the original material stress space is

\[
\mathfrak H_P
=
L^2(\operatorname{Hist}(P);\mathrm{Sym}_0),
\qquad
G=2\nu S_A .
\]

For an admitted bounded test \(A_Q\in\mathfrak H_P\),

\[
\|A_Q\|_{\mathfrak H_P}\le 1,
\qquad
\Theta_Q=|\langle G,A_Q\rangle_{\mathfrak H_P}|^2,
\]

the fresh parent-subtracted pieces are Bessel/martingale differences, and the
original energy law gives finite square packing.

That does not prove the raw singularity selector has this form.

At scale \(r\), a unit rescaled test \(A_r\) pulls back to an original test

\[
F_r(x,t)
=
r^{-3}A_r\!\left({x-x_0\over r},{t-t_0\over r^2}\right),
\]

with

\[
\|F_r\|_{\mathfrak H_P}^2
=
r^{-1}\|A_r\|_{L^2(dy\,ds)}^2 .
\]

Thus a unit zoom-normalized read generally has original norm \(r^{-1/2}\). It is
a bounded read of the rescaled packet, not automatically a bounded read of the
original packet.

Consequently, `SelectedActionCurrencyBoundedOriginalPacketEnforcement.A`
enforces the retained admitted-selector currency. It does not by itself prove
that every singularity-forcing zoom event is captured by that currency.

The remaining smoothness-bearing theorem is the native birth-packing theorem:
after lower-band carry, entrance, legal loss, reset, reselection, and parent
leverage are removed, every retained selected high-ratio birth \(\gamma\) must
charge a bounded-multiplicity native reserve of the original coupled packet,

\[
\iota:\Gamma_N(P)\to\mathcal R_N^{nat}(u_0;P),
\]

\[
\omega_\gamma
\le
C_N\rho_{\iota(\gamma)}
+R_{\rm legal}(\gamma)+Stop(\gamma),
\qquad
\operatorname{mult}(\iota)\le C_N,
\]

    and

\[
\sum_{\rho\in\mathcal R_N^{nat}(u_0;P)}
\rho
\le
C_N(u_0)\mathcal R(P)+R_{\rm legal}(P)+Stop(P).
\]

This is equivalent, on the current Gold surfaces, to the scale-memory storage
coercivity / critical fresh source tent Carleson producer.  The sharper physical
form is the critical positive-transfer reserve:

\[
\nu\int_0^T\|\Lambda^{3/2}u(t)\|_2^2\,dt
\]

is finite once the positive nonlinear transfer in the Leray-projected
\(\dot H^{1/2}\) identity is absorbed.  The standard estimate gives that only
for small critical packet size; arbitrary data still requires the native
reserve theorem.  It is not discharged by the bounded-projection Bessel
argument. If it is proved, the existing relay gives finite selected action,
finite full same-material participation clock, bounded \(H^s\) for \(s>5/2\),
and continuation past \(T^*\). Until it is proved, the route remains
conditional.
