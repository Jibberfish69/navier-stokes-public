# Record-edge selector modulus same-packet gate reduction

## 1. Physical object

The object is one future-positive retained record edge of the same
Navier-Stokes field. The edge carries a high packet, a low pressure/strain
coefficient, a material time window, a signed polarity, a chart/collar state,
and a selector label.

The selector label is allowed to move only when the same physical edge moves:
the material packet is transported, the cotangent frame is strained by
pressure/velocity gradients, the heat-scale carrier returns to the retained
packet, a tie or label crossing is charged, or the full-packet partner that
cancels a channel-first positive part is retained and paid.

If the label changes because the future readout chose the largest later
positive channel, the parent never owned the record edge. That is the moving
argmax obstruction already isolated by the finite-menu and selector-uniformity
notes.

## 2. Exact pass case

Fix the retained edge class, depth, and packet type. Suppose the actual
record-edge selector is generated before readout by a finite-dimensional
material/cotangent chart

\[
\mathfrak s
=
(\chi,\Phi,\Gamma,\ell,\operatorname{sgn})
=
\mathcal S(\mathcal J_K^{loc},\Theta),
\tag{RSM.1}
\]

where \(\mathcal J_K^{loc}\) is the same local velocity/local-pressure finite
jet and \(\Theta\) is carried by the same material/cotangent history. Suppose
the selector chart obeys

\[
\|\partial_s\mathfrak s\|_{\mathfrak S}
\le
C\bigl(1+\|\mathcal J_K^{loc}\|_{H_y^\sigma}^d+|\dot\Theta|\bigr),
\qquad
|\dot\Theta|
\le
C\bigl(1+\|\mathcal J_K^{loc}\|_{H_y^\sigma}^d\bigr),
\tag{RSM.2}
\]

and the finite endpoint jet temporal modulus controls
\(\mathcal J_K^{loc}\) on the retained terminal window. Then the material
selector note gives

\[
\Lambda_{\rm sel}^{cont}((-\theta,0])
=
\int_{-\theta}^0\|\partial_s\mathfrak s(s)\|_{\mathfrak S}\,ds
\le
C\theta.
\tag{RSM.3}
\]

This is the continuous same-edge label modulus. It says that the selector label
cannot move through terminal time without an \(L^1\) material/cotangent cost.
At fixed absorption margin \(\eta\), compactness of the normalized selector
chart gives a parent-known finite net of scores before readout, and `(RSM.3)`
makes the score motion absolutely continuous along the same material edge.

Thus the same-packet pass case is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\texttt{RecordEdgeMaterialOrderLockedSelectorModulus.A}\\
&+\texttt{TransportedSelectorHeatScaleReturn.A}\\
&+\texttt{SelectorTieGapOrDriftCharge.A}\\
&+\texttt{SameEdgeOrderLockPartnerDeficitPayment.A}
\end{aligned}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{RecordEdgeSelectorLabelModulusOrPaidDrift.A}.
\tag{RSM.4}
\]

Here `RecordEdgeMaterialOrderLockedSelectorModulus.A` includes the actual
same-edge generation `(RSM.1)`--`(RSM.3)`. It is not a new finite-frequency
assumption on the velocity field; the finite object is the selector chart at a
fixed retained depth.

## 3. Heat-scale return is a physical gate

A material/cotangent selector can be generated by the flow and still fail to
remain on the retained heat-scale carrier. The transported metric

\[
G_{mat}=AA^T,\qquad A=F^{-1},
\tag{RSM.5}
\]

obeys

\[
\partial_sG_{mat}
=
-A(\nabla U+\nabla U^T)A^T.
\tag{RSM.6}
\]

That is real same-fluid motion. It becomes a retained record-edge selector only
when the anisotropic metric returns to the heat-scale/tower packet, or the
geometry loss is paid. Without that return, the selector is material but the
record edge has detached from the retained carrier.

This is exactly the physical content of

\[
\texttt{TransportedSelectorHeatScaleReturn.A}.
\tag{RSM.7}
\]

## 4. Tie and post-hoc selection are paid drift

An extremizing selector chosen after the density is formed can switch when two
candidate scores nearly tie:

\[
\operatorname{score}_1(s)-\operatorname{score}_2(s)\to0
\quad\text{as }s\uparrow0.
\tag{RSM.8}
\]

That switch is not a consequence of the same material finite jet. It is
selector tie/drift. The same-record modulus may use such motion only after it
is charged as selector/collar residue, legal loss, stop, donor/reselection
loss, or exit. Otherwise the future readout has selected the payer after the
positive edge appeared.

## 5. Order-lock partner loss is not label motion

For channel-first native selection, write

\[
n=\langle N,\Phi\rangle,
\qquad
b=\langle P+V+T+I+L+S,\Phi\rangle-cD^\nu,
\qquad
h=n+b.
\tag{RSM.9}
\]

The exact gap between channel-first positive selection and full-packet positive
selection is

\[
\Delta^{ol}
=
\bigl([n]_+-[h]_+\bigr)_+
=
\min\{[n]_+,[-b]_+\}.
\tag{RSM.10}
\]

Physically, this is the same retained edge selecting a positive channel before
the negative pressure/viscous/transport/incompressibility/localization/sign
partner has been retained. It is not harmless selector relabeling. It is the
lost full-packet partner.

The order-lock gap can be paid by selected-density reverse Holder/good-lambda
gain, by same-packet four-body overlap payment, or by an endpoint
Hardy/Orlicz terminal modulus. Without that payment the terminal pulse model

\[
n_m(s)=a\tau_m^{-1}\mathbf 1_{(-\tau_m,0]}(s),
\qquad
b_m(s)=-n_m(s),
\qquad
\tau_m\downarrow0
\tag{RSM.11}
\]

has \(h_m=0\) while \([n_m]_+\) remains a critical half-tail. That is the same
physical failure as a positive record selected before its full same-packet
return was admitted.

## 6. Strict reduction and current obstruction

The current noncircular dependency is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\texttt{RecordEdgeMaterialOrderLockedSelectorModulus.A}\\
&+\texttt{TransportedSelectorHeatScaleReturn.A}\\
&+\texttt{SelectorTieGapOrDriftCharge.A}\\
&+\texttt{SameEdgeOrderLockPartnerDeficitPayment.A}
\end{aligned}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{RecordEdgeSelectorLabelModulusOrPaidDrift.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{FiniteParentKnownRecordEdgeSelectorMenu.A}.
\tag{RSM.12}
\]

The checked obstruction is the union of two same-physical-story failures:

\[
\text{moving future argmax over labels}
\tag{RSM.13}
\]

and

\[
\text{channel-first positive pulse whose full-packet partner cancels it.}
\tag{RSM.14}
\]

The first failure means the parent did not own the selector label before
readout. The second means the selected positive channel was never admitted as a
full same-packet positive record. Both must be paid, stopped, made legal, or
routed to exit before the finite record-edge selector menu can be used as proof
support.

Thus the record-edge selector modulus is not an abstract compactness theorem.
It is the physical rule that every selector change of the same retained record
edge is either generated by the same material/cotangent packet and returned to
the heat-scale carrier, or else charged as the exact defect it physically is.
