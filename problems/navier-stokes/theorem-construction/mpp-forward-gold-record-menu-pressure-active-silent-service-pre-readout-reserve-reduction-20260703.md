# Record-menu pressure-active or silent-service pre-readout reserve reduction

## 1. Physical burden

The object is one retained high packet of the same velocity field, paired with
one low strain/current coefficient, before the record reads a positive edge.
The parent root and active full-exchange detectors are allowed to store only the
work they can see before that readout.

The problem is that a real strain direction of the packet can be invisible to
one pressure-Hodge/root detector. If that invisible direction still carries
positive signed-height record work, the proof must not pretend the parent
detector stored it. The work must already be paid as same-packet material
service, legal loss, stop, or exit before the record can count it.

## 2. Raw detector faithfulness is false

The selected-symbol counterboundary gives the local obstruction. At a frozen
Euclideanized material point, the pressure-Hodge scalar sees only

```math
m_{PH}(\xi)Z
\simeq
{\xi_i\xi_j Z_{ij}\over |\xi|^2}.
\tag{RPS.1}
```

For \(\xi=e_1\), a transverse trace-free tensor

```math
Z=
\begin{pmatrix}
0&0&0\\
0&1&0\\
0&0&-1
\end{pmatrix}
\tag{RPS.2}
```

satisfies

```math
m_{PH}(e_1)Z=0,
\qquad
Z\ne0.
\tag{RPS.3}
```

So a raw pressure-Hodge/root detector does not control arbitrary tensor action.
The record-menu theorem cannot be a naked detector-angle theorem on all
retained amplitudes.

## 3. What the existing physical routing gives

The complete-frame pressure-probe audit supplies the correct local physical
split. The pressure-visible part is handled by the actual parent detector. The
pressure-Hodge-null trace-free part is not free: for a retained packet frame,

```math
[p\cdot Sp]_+
\le
[-\widehat\xi\cdot S\widehat\xi]_+
+
[-n\cdot Sn]_+.
\tag{RPS.4}
```

The right-hand terms are same-packet wave-covector compression and
packet-normal/collar or annular compression service.

The silent-source integration gives the matching material equation. A selected
silent pressure component \(q=w\) reappears in the strain equation:

```math
D_tS+S^2+\Omega^2+\nabla_A^2q
=
\nu\Delta_AS+\mathcal C_A^{legal}.
\tag{RPS.5}
```

Thus a selected detector-silent component is locally routed into top-strain
exchange, complete-frame service, viscous/radius service, collar/frame service,
or legal routing of the same packet.

This is local routing support, not Gold closure. The silent-source integration
also records that local routing and complete-frame orientation payment do not
control the global weighted half-tail by themselves. A future record can still
draw positive work from detector-silent service visits unless those visits have
a finite pre-readout reserve in record units.

The primitive half-tail audit sharpens this point. The detector-silent reserve
is proved only after an independent original-history primitive full-action
reserve, such as `StoppedPrimitivePLSStrictHalfBarrierBreak.A`,
`PrimitivePLSBoundedBelowLyapunov.A`, or `WeightBeatingTailLaw.A`. Using
`RetainedRecordEdgeStoppedPrimitiveChannelAdmission.A` to supply that reserve is
circular, because that admission already assumes this pressure-active-or-silent
face.

The quotient-angle collar audit sharpens the other side of the split. The
pressure-active part has a finite-dimensional uniform angle only after a fixed
near-silent collar has been assigned to the same pre-readout service reserve.
Exact nonzero detector response is not enough: a near-null retained edge would
make the detector constant blow up, so it must be treated as service-paid rather
than pressure-active.

## 4. Strict lower split

The retained frozen theorem reduces to:

```math
\texttt{RecordMenuPressureActiveQuotientAngleGap.A}
+
\texttt{RecordMenuDetectorSilentServicePreReadoutReserve.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{RecordMenuPressureActiveOrSilentService.A}.
\tag{RPS.6}
```

The first input is the finite-dimensional pressure-active quotient statement.
After legal gauges, stopped exits, lower-order pieces, and detector-silent
service are removed, the retained active quotient must have a uniform angle
from the joint null cone of the parent root and active full-exchange detectors.
On a parent-known compact symbol bundle this is linear algebra once the
pressure-active subbundle is fixed; it is not the PDE producer.

The second input is the actual PDE producer:

```math
\texttt{RecordMenuDetectorSilentServicePreReadoutReserve.A}.
\tag{RPS.7}
```

Statement. For every arbitrary future-positive retained record-menu edge, the
component in the joint detector-silent subbundle is either unselected/gauge, or
is charged before record readout to finite same-packet material service:
strain/exchange, complete-frame compression, viscous/radius service,
collar/frame or annular service, legal loss, stop, or Pack/Part/Field exit. The
charge must be summable in the same record units used by the future-positive
menu selection.

## 5. Proof of the strict reduction

Assume `(RPS.6)' inputs. Decompose the frozen retained amplitude into its
pressure-active quotient part and its joint-detector-silent part. The
pressure-active quotient angle controls the positive record work of the first
part by parent root/full-exchange detector currency. The detector-silent reserve
charges the second part before the record can count it.

Therefore every future-positive retained record-menu edge either has the
detector angle needed by the parent storage faces, or its invisible component is
already material-service paid, legal, stopped, or exited. That is exactly
`RecordMenuPressureActiveOrSilentService.A`.

## 6. Current truth state

This is a strict reduction. The repo proves the raw detector theorem is false,
and it gives strong local same-packet routing for detector-silent work. It does
not yet prove the record-level reserve that makes all such silent service paid
before arbitrary future record readout.

The unpaid physical object is
`RecordMenuDetectorSilentServicePreReadoutReserve.A`: detector-silent work must
not be allowed to become the hidden positive payer of a future record unless the
same packet has already paid it in finite material-service currency.

After the primitive half-tail audit, the noncircular lower producer is an
original-history primitive PLS strict half-barrier / bounded-below Lyapunov /
weighted laminar reserve theorem. Local silent-source routing is support; the
half-tail is the obstruction to promoting that support into finite reserve.
After the quotient-angle collar audit, the reserve is read robustly: exact
detector-silent and fixed detector-near-silent components both belong to the
service-paid side before record readout.
