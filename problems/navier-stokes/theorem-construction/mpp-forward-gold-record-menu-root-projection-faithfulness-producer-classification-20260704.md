# Record-menu root projection faithfulness producer classification

## 1. One physical event before any theorem chain

There is one evolving Navier-Stokes velocity field. A signed-height record rises
because pressure, strain, transport, incompressibility, and viscosity move work
through the same fluid history. A future-positive record edge is therefore one
material event:

\[
e=(\text{retained high packet},\text{low pressure/strain/current coefficient},
\text{material time window},\text{signed record polarity}).
\tag{RPCf.1}
\]

The proof cannot first break that event into ledger coordinates and then pretend
the coordinates are already the event. The parent selector, the exact pressure
projector, the pressure-active/silent detector, the primitive channel, and the
critical source/reset charge all have to refer to the same \(e\) before the
record readout counts its positive work.

This is also why clipping is not the Gold object. The record edge is already a
signed physical quantity. The burden is to show where that same signed work was
visible, paid, stopped, legal, or exited before selection, not to replace it by
a positive child inventory.

## 2. What root projection faithfulness asks

`RecordMenuRootProjectionFaithfulness.A` is the entrance theorem underneath the
TFE face split. It asks that every future-positive native record edge be visible
in parent root or active full-exchange currency before the record reads it, or
else that the hidden part is already service-paid, legal, stopped, or a CM exit
face.

For one retained edge this means an inequality of the form

\[
\left[\int_{I_e\cap[a,b]}\omega_e\right]_+
\le
C_N\bigl(dA_e^{root,+}+d\Xi_e^{FE,act}+dServ_e^{silent}\bigr)
+dLegal_e+dStop_e.
\tag{RPCf.2}
\]

The left side is not an abstract positive source. It is the signed-height record
net work of the same packet. The right side is lawful only if its rows are
attached to that same packet before readout.

## 3. Checked false routes

The recent audits rule out the attractive shortcuts.

Raw detector faithfulness is false. A transverse trace-free pressure/strain
component can carry work while lying in the joint null or near-null region of
the parent root and active full-exchange detectors. Physically, the packet can
do local pressure-Hodge service without presenting itself as parent root
capacity.

Exact projector algebra is not chart license. The exact pressure-Hodge
projector is physical only when the same high packet remains inside a retained
material chart with the required metric symbol control. Otherwise boundary,
collar, cutoff, tower, and moving-chart residues can move the readout away from
the packet being selected.

Hodge/source identity is not admission. It can identify a residue as same-packet
pressure/strain service, but it does not prove that the residue enters the
selector-announced primitive signed channel before the record readout.

Separate lower-face proofs do not prove the entrance theorem. A selector can
capture one edge, a chart can license a nearby edge, and a detector split can
pay a third edge. Without same-edge custody, the positive record work can still
sit in the intersection gap.

Freestanding fresh-source or reset charge is support under the signed-height
roof. It has Gold force only after the charged source/reset is the
parent-announced carrier of the record-net work before readout.

## 4. Nonduplicate physical producer classes

After the current reductions, the same-edge entrance theorem has three
nonduplicate producer classes.

First, parent-announced selector capture:

\[
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeParentAnnouncedSelectorCapture.A}.
\tag{RPCf.3}
\]

The parent must know which material carrier, polarity, order lock, primitive
channel, and time window are being followed before the record sees the positive
edge. Selector variation estimates after capture are consumers, not this
capture theorem.

Second, retained exact-projector chart control:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\texttt{RetainedCurvatureSpikeChartPayerAdmission.A}\\
&+\texttt{TemporalRecordThicknessChargeBillCurrency.A}\\
&+\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeHigherProjectorServiceOrExit.A}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{RPCf.4}
\]

Finite same-carrier strain supports ellipticity. The \(C^{1,1}\)/symbol tower
has since been reduced: high-frequency coefficient curvature is higher-projector
service, and low-pass coefficient curvature has fixed-time spatial thickness by
Bernstein inside the retained chart. The remaining chart-side input is temporal
and admissive: the low-pass curvature superlevel must be admitted as the same
retained edge's chart payer before readout, and the resulting temporal pinch is
the existing temporal bill-currency / same-parent positive-selection storage
wall. Standard dyadic parametrix calculus is downstream of this license.

Third, pre-entrance original-history selected-critical service payment:

\[
\texttt{CriticalFreshSourceTentCarleson.A}
\quad\text{or}\quad
\texttt{FreshSourceCriticalScaleMemoryBound.A},
\tag{RPCf.5}
\]

used as an independent pre-readout reserve. This producer pays the
detector-silent or near-silent service and the projector/selector channel
rotation after the selector has captured the same retained edge, and before root
projection can count that edge. Its physical branch forms are:

\[
\texttt{RepeatedCorePositiveVariationRootBound.A}
\quad\text{and}\quad
\texttt{ScaleInvariantSelectedCapacityNoEscape.A},
\tag{RPCf.6}
\]

read in their independent original-history form. The repeated-core side is
record/return: the same material core is used again and again, so record growth
must be balanced by negative return before the root edge is admitted. The
shrinking-core side is reset/depletion: the packet is reselected at smaller
scale, so the critical weight must come from original-history scale memory
before child or record readout.

`RecordAdmittedActiveFullExchangeCoupledStorage.A` is downstream of entrance.
It stores record-admitted active/full-exchange currency after the edge is
visible. It is not evidence that the edge was visible in the first place.
The 2026-07-04 selected-critical payment noncircularity audit sharpens this
point: record-admitted depletion/reset/storage cannot be used as the
root-projection input because record admission already assumes entrance into
parent currency.

## 5. Strict reduction

The honest reduction is

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeParentAnnouncedSelectorCapture.A}\\
&+\texttt{RetainedCurvatureSpikeChartPayerAdmission.A}
 +\texttt{TemporalRecordThicknessChargeBillCurrency.A}
 +\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeHigherProjectorServiceOrExit.A}\\
&+\text{pre-entrance original-history }
  \bigl(\texttt{CriticalFreshSourceTentCarleson.A}
  /\texttt{FreshSourceCriticalScaleMemoryBound.A}\bigr)\\
&\Longrightarrow
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgePreReadoutCustodyTuple.A}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\texttt{RecordMenuRootProjectionFaithfulness.A}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{RPCf.7}
\]

Here the third line includes the repeated-core record/return branch and the
shrinking-reset/scale-native branch only in their independent pre-entrance
form. It is not a downstream storage theorem; it is the physical source payment
needed for silent service and selector/projector rotation on the same retained
record edge before root projection is proved.

Once `(RPCf.7)' lands, the existing downstream route is lawful:

\[
\begin{aligned}

&\Longrightarrow
\texttt{RecordMenuCriticalFaceAdmission.A}\\

\tag{RPCf.8}

Before `(RPCf.7)' lands, downstream storage, route menus, and ledger

## 6. Current truth state

`RecordMenuRootProjectionFaithfulness.A` is not a detector lemma, not a clipping
lemma, and not a pure exact-projector theorem. It is a same-edge physical
entrance theorem.

The work still open is to prove that the actual future-positive retained
signed-height edge has, before readout, the parent-announced selector, the
retained exact-projector chart or paid exit, and the pre-entrance
selected-critical payment needed for silent/rotation service; or else prove that
failure of one of those three producers is already legal, stopped,
material-service paid, or a CM Pack/Part/Field exit.

Any future note that cannot say how it moves one of those three producer classes
for the same edge is support-only, even when the estimate is true.
