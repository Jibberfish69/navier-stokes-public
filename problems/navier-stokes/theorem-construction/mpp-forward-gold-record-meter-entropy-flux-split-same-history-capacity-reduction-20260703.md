# Record-meter entropy-flux split / same-history capacity reduction

## 1. Same physical event

A thin signed critical-height record upcrossing is one same-fluid event:

\[
H(a)=\lambda,\qquad H(b)=2\lambda,
\qquad
\int_a^bP(t)\,dt
=H(b)-H(a)+\nu\int_a^bD(t)\,dt
\ge\lambda .
\tag{RMS.1}
\]

The selected-meter route asks where this record-net work lived before the record
was read. After parent-adjoint admission, the normalized record meter must carry
an aligned current charge:

\[
1
\le
C_N\int dQ_{P,[a,b]}^{align}
+Legal([a,b])+Stop([a,b]).
\tag{RMS.2}
\]

To close the record criterion, disjoint records need the finite budget

\[
\sum_j\int dQ_{P_j,[a_j,b_j]}^{align}
\le
C_N(u_0)+Paid+Legal+Stop .
\tag{RMS.3}
\]

This note reduces `(RMS.3)' to the non-reversible motion of the selected meter,
not to raw current representation, atom pairing, or terminal anti-atom reserve.

## 2. Aligned current before clipping

For a parent-announced selected active density \(F_P\), TFE2748B defines the

\tag{RMS.5}

Here \(J_{\Psi,P}\) is generated by the same pressure-Hodge, transported frame,
selector/collar, turnstile/exchange, and root-generator packet. Thus this is
still the same velocity field under pressure, transport, viscosity,
incompressibility, and stopped material motion.

## 3. Reversible entropy flux is support

The volume-preserving pressure/Hodge/self-advection part is not a fresh source.
In the stopped parent chart it has a generator \(B_P\) with

\[

\tag{RMS.6}

\[
-\Phi''(F_P)
\langle\nabla_{\mathfrak m}F_P,F_PB_P\rangle
=
-\operatorname{div}_{\mathfrak m}\bigl(H_\Phi(F_P)B_P\bigr).
\tag{RMS.7}
\]

So the reversible aligned-current piece is an entropy flux. After paid boundary,
legal, and stop pieces are removed, its positive part is paired with its opposite

\[

\preceq
-dM_P^{rev}
+d\Theta_P^0+dPaid_P+dStop_P .
\tag{RMS.8}
\]

This is proof/support already available from the TFE2748B split. It prevents a
wrong proof route: reversible current rearranges the selected density, but it is
not the finite record charge.

## 7. One same-parent theorem

The physical story does not license proving admission first and storage later as

the original packet has proved it had a place to live.

The cleaner noncircular theorem is:

\[
\texttt{RecordAdmittedActiveFullExchangeCoupledStorage.A}.
\tag{RMS.20}
\]
