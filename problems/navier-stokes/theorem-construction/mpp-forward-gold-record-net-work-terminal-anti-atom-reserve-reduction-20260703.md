# Record-net work terminal anti-atom reserve reduction

## 1. Same physical packet

A record climb of signed critical height gives net nonlinear work:

\[
\int_a^bP(t)\,dt
=
H(b)-H(a)+\nu\int_a^bD(t)\,dt
\ge\lambda .
\tag{RTA.1}
\]

The previous reduction says this work must be admitted as a parent-adjoint
selected meter and spent through one-sided aligned-current/Fisher domination.

The physical question is now sharper. After the record work is normalized by
\(\lambda\), can the same parent packet pay the positive aligned current by
Fisher dissipation, paid motion, and stopped material records before any child
positive readout is clipped?

## 2. What direct no-upcrossing proves and does not prove

The TFE2748B source surface rewrites the aligned current as

\[
dQ_P^{cur}=(a_P)_+\,dI_P,
\tag{RTA.2}
\]

where \(dI_P\) is the Fisher drain and \(a_P\) is the parallel coefficient of the
same-parent Hodge/current direction against the active-density gradient.

Equivalently, with

\[
U_{P,\theta}:=\phi_{\Psi,P}-\theta F_P,
\tag{RTA.3}
\]

the strict surplus is positive fibre variation of \(U_{P,\theta}\) in the
\(F_P\)-direction. A pure no-upcrossing theorem for this comparison potential
would imply the aligned-current estimate.

That pure theorem is not installed. It is also not a consequence of current
representation or zero mean alone: the flat quotient model with
\(\phi=(\theta+c)F\) has a legal same-parent Hodge representation and still has
positive surplus. Therefore a record proof cannot stop at "write the current" or
"pair signed atoms." Those are support.

## 3. Storage-compatible reduction

The storage-compatible object is not pure no-upcrossing. Positive fibre
variation may occur when its opposite lobe is retained as parent reserve
depletion or when the unpaired part is endpoint/weight-motion leakage. The
same-fibre Jordan decomposition gives

\[
d\Xi_P^{fib,+}
\le
-dM_P^{fib}
+dEnd_P^U+dJac_P^U,
\tag{RTA.4}
\]

provided the fibre reserve is root-finite and the endpoint/Jacobian terms are
paid. Thus the aligned-current theorem reduces to the tail-material record
payment theorem.

In the later TFE2748B reduction, all ordinary selector motion, selected-carrier
shape, and singular legal/collar trace are routing faces. The remaining retained
source is one terminal anti-atom reserve:

\[
\boxed{
d\mathfrak A_P^{term}
\le
C_N(d\Theta^0+dPaid+dStop+2^{-N}D_N\,dt).
}
\tag{RTA.5}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
\texttt{SameParentTerminalAntiAtomReserve.A}
\Longleftrightarrow
\texttt{TailMaterialRecordPayment.A}.
\tag{RTA.6}
\]

For the record-net line, the normalized target is:

\[
\boxed{
\texttt{RecordTerminalAntiAtomReserve.A}.
}
\tag{RTA.7}
\]

It says: every record-normalized positive terminal anti-atom generated by the
parent-adjoint record meter is paid by signed-partner saturation, endpoint jump
control, geometry/tower/material-record return, or the small \(2^{-N}D_Ndt\)
tail before the record is counted.

## 4. Strict chain

The current strict chain is:

\[
\begin{array}{c}
\texttt{SignedHeightRecordNetWorkAdmission.A}\\
+\texttt{RecordWorkParentAdjointMeterAdmission.A}\\

\end{array}
\Longrightarrow

The first line is installed only for the scalar net work `(RTA.1)'. The parent

is that the record-net problem is now tied to the deepest existing physical
source wall instead of floating as a generic native-edge search.

## 5. Noncircularity boundary

This reduction does not license the terminal anti-atom reserve as an upstream
Gold producer. The live Gold object is still the signed critical-height
sign-persistence / peak-height theorem. A terminal anti-atom is the retained
end-trace of unpaid aligned current on the same material record; it gains Gold
force only after the signed-height dynamics prove that such an end-trace pays
the record-upcrossing thickness-or-charge criterion.

Thus the forbidden use is:

\[
\texttt{RecordTerminalAntiAtomReserve.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{SignedCriticalHeightSignPersistenceOrPeakBound.A}
\tag{RTA.9}
\]

without an independent same-packet derivation of the reserve from the signed
height equation. In that use, the route is circular: the reserve is asked to
produce signed-height control while current authority says the reserve itself
still needs signed-height sign-persistence or peak-height control.

The installed boundary is recorded in
`mpp-forward-gold-terminal-anti-atom-signed-height-loop-boundary-20260703.md`.

## 6. Proof status

Proof:

\[
\int_a^bP(t)\,dt\ge\lambda .
\tag{RTA.10}
\]

Support:

\[
\text{parent current representation, same-parent atom pairing, retained-lobe
reserve depletion, fibre Jordan decomposition.}
\tag{RTA.11}
\]

Checked obstruction:

\[
\text{flat quotient current representation can have positive surplus, so
representation alone cannot prove aligned-current domination.}
\tag{RTA.12}
\]

Open theorem:

\[
\boxed{
\text{record parent-adjoint meter admission}
+
\text{record terminal anti-atom reserve / tail-material-record payment.}
}
\tag{RTA.13}
\]

This is the current clean physical story. A record climb demands net work; net
work must enter the parent meter; the parent meter's positive aligned current
can only remain unpaid as a terminal anti-atom of the same material record. The
proof still needs the original-data reserve theorem that rules out or pays that
anti-atom.
