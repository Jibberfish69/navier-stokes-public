## 3. Gate 6 clause 1: one-signedness from the loop's geometry

The far swing lies entirely on one side of the saddle in the transfer

the reduced potential is the exact three-wave cubic. Monotone
derivative, one-sided excursion: the far-swing envelope difference is
one-signed along the swing, and its loop integral has a positive floor

## 4. Gate 6 clause 2: the detuning horn pays the same ledger line

The dwell is logarithmic in the gap (filed closed form); a growing gap
therefore collapses the dwell exponentially fast, and within the same
handoff the packet's turning phase exits the parked band. The
shallow-parking horn of the un-parking identity then holds - the
return was never suppressed at sub-parked phase - and the filed race
integral collects at order-one phase before the extraction window
closes. A detuned packet cannot be used as forward financing without
paying the same closed-form return as a crossed one: two horns, one
ledger line.
