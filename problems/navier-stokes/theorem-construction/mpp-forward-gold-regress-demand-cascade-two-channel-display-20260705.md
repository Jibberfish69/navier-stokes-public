# Regress demand cascade: two channels, one transfer matrix

## 1. The correction (against my own B4)

Strength asymmetry breaks the naive reciprocity: a weak bracer
(circulation beta*Gamma*rho_s^5) suffices for a weak demand (s*beta
differential), but receives back-reaction scaled by the PRODUCER's
strength: back-demand/supplied-demand ~ 1/(beta * rho_s^5). Wide
spacing (rho_s > beta^(-1/5)) makes this ratio < 1: the differential
tower converges. B4 as a single-channel lemma is refuted. Filed
same-day against my own note.

## 2. The two channels that re-open the fight

COHERENT DETUNING: the producer's uniform far gradient (~ s/rho_s^4 =
s*beta^(4/5) at the convergent spacing) rigid-rotates the bracer;
the brace geometry detunes on a clock FASTER than the skew it
cancels. Correcting rotation is a new demand at exponent 4/5; its
correction cascades: beta^(4/5)^n -> 1. Divergent at scaling level.

INVASIVENESS: the same coherent gradient acts on the producer:
rigid rotation at s*beta^(4/5) against production at s*beta^2. The
holder's grip turns the pose faster than the pose produces. Every
cure is a new patient one exponent shallower.

## 3. The display owed (the decisive object)

The joint demand-transfer matrix T(rho_s): state = (differential,
coherent) demand at layer n, normalized by strength; entries from the
exact dipole kernel of the two-tube note. Decide growth of the
cascade T^n with the exponent bookkeeping exact. Spectral radius
above one on every admissible schedule = dense cell folds into
composition. A convergent schedule preserving pose and production =
exit (ii) opens.

## 4. Why the story predicted this shape

Participation: no structure is held for free, and the holder's grip
is itself a load on the held. The single-channel refutation honored
the first clause and forgot the second. The joint cascade is both
clauses at once.
