# Retained record-edge pre-readout custody tuple reduction

## 1. Same physical edge

A signed-height record upcrossing gives net work of the same velocity field. The
record-menu decomposition represents that work before readout as native edges:
retained high packet, low strain/current coefficient, material time window, and
parent charge coordinate.

For one future-positive retained edge \(e\), write its record work as

\[
\left[\int_{I_e\cap[a,b]}\omega_e\right]_+ .
\tag{RCT.1}
\]

The edge can enter the parent root/full-exchange currency only if the same
physical edge has all of the following before the record counts it:

1. a parent-announced stopped selector score for the carrier, polarity, order
   lock, primitive channel, and material window;
2. a retained exact-projector chart where the same high packet has the metric
   symbol control needed by the weighted pressure-Hodge/root projector, or a
   paid chart exit;
3. a pressure-active detector angle into parent root/full-exchange currency, or
   a detector-silent/near-silent service payment for the same component;
4. a face address in the positive-baseline root/log face, zero-baseline endpoint
   seed face, or active full-exchange face.

Call this simultaneous object

\[
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgePreReadoutCustodyTuple.A}.
\tag{RCT.2}
\]

The word "tuple" is literal. These are not four detachable chores. They are four
coordinates of one parent-announced retained edge.

## 2. Why separate lower faces do not prove the edge

Current repo notes isolate three useful lower faces:

\[
\begin{array}{c}
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeParentAnnouncedSelectorCapture.A},\\
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeExactProjectorChartLicense.A},\\
\texttt{RecordMenuPressureActiveOrSilentService.A}.
\end{array}
\tag{RCT.3}
\]

Each is a correct projection of the same physical burden. None is a standalone
Gold producer.

The non-aliasing obstruction is simple. A proof may show that some selector
record captures a positive edge, some retained chart licenses an exact
projector edge, and some pressure-active/silent split pays a detector edge. If
those three statements are not proved for the same edge \(e\), the record
selection can still draw its positive excess from the intersection gap:

\[
\text{captured edge}\ne\text{charted edge}\ne\text{detector-paid edge}.
\tag{RCT.4}
\]

Then no parent root/full-exchange measure has actually received the record work
in `(RCT.1)'. The separate lower faces are true support statements, but the
record edge remains unadmitted.

This is not a Navier-Stokes counterexample. It is the proof-state obstruction:
root projection faithfulness is a same-edge custody claim, not a list of
nearby face claims.

## 3. Strict reduction

The simultaneous custody tuple gives the missing entrance into the TFE face
split. For every future-positive retained record-menu edge, the tuple proves

\[
\left[\int_{I_e\cap[a,b]}\omega_e\right]_+
\le
C_N\left(
dA_e^{root,+}
+d\Xi_e^{FE,act}
+dServ_e^{silent}
\right)
+dLegal_e+dStop_e.
\tag{RCT.5}
\]

The silent-service term is already on the service-paid side before record
readout. The pressure-active part is now visible in parent root or active
full-exchange currency. The legal and stopped exits cannot be counted as hidden
record work.

Summing `(RCT.5)' over the finite-overlap record-menu family gives

\[
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgePreReadoutCustodyTuple.A}
\Longrightarrow
\texttt{RecordMenuRootProjectionFaithfulness.A}.
\tag{RCT.6}
\]

Then the existing strict chain applies:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\texttt{RecordMenuRootProjectionFaithfulness.A}
&\Longrightarrow
\texttt{RecordMenuCriticalFaceAdmission.A}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\texttt{RecordNativeWorkMenuPositiveSelectionStorage.A}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\texttt{record-upcrossing thickness-or-charge}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{RCT.7}
\]

Thus `(RCT.2)' is smaller than the signed-height theorem, but it is still
object-level Gold work: it is the pre-readout admission of the actual retained
edge that a thin signed-height record tries to use.

## 4. Physical proof pressure

The same-fluid packet must construct the tuple from the original parent history:

\[
\mathfrak T_e
=
(\mathfrak R_e,\mathfrak X_e,\mathfrak D_e,\mathfrak F_e).
\tag{RCT.8}
\]

Here \(\mathfrak R_e\) is the stopped selector record,
\(\mathfrak X_e\) is the retained material chart and exact-projector license,
\(\mathfrak D_e\) is the pressure-active/silent-service split, and
\(\mathfrak F_e\) is the root/endpoint/full-exchange face address.

The admissible proof has one of two outcomes before record readout:

\[
\text{construct }\mathfrak T_e
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
\text{route the uncaptured part to legal, stop, material service, or exit}.
\tag{RCT.9}
\]

The proof may not choose \(\mathfrak R_e\) after seeing the positive record,
borrow \(\mathfrak X_e\) from a nearby charted packet, or use a detector split
for a different component. That would be the same post-selection defect in a
lower coordinate.

Prove that every future-positive retained record-menu edge carries the same

selector-announced primitive channel must be the same signed channel that the

`RetainedRecordEdgeSelectorProjectorChannelAlignment.A` proves that same-channel
alignment or routes the mismatch before readout.

This keeps the physical story under one roof: the same high packet that raises
the signed record must be the packet that is selected, charted, detected, and
stored. Anything else is support bookkeeping around an unadmitted record edge.
