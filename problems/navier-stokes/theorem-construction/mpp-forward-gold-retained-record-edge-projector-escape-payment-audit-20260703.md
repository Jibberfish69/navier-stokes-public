# Retained record-edge projector escape payment audit

## 1. Physical object

Fix one future-positive signed-height record edge before the record reads it.
The object is the same high packet at shell \(2^j\), in the same transported
fluid history, interacting with one low strain/current coefficient. The exact
pressure-Hodge/root projector is not a detached algebraic decoration: in
material variables it is the elliptic constraint force generated by

```math
L_G=-\operatorname{div}_a(G\nabla_a),
\qquad
G=AA^\top .
\tag{REP.1}
```

Projector escape payment asks what happens when this exact projector is moved
through the retained packet chart, cutoff, collar, and shell. A generated term is
lawful only when it is one of the following before record readout:

1. the same packet's base cutoff/collar/frame leakage, already paid by a legal or
   top-strain storage surface;
2. the same packet's higher pressure/RHS, annular-stress, or finite-depth
   material service, paid by a finite same-material service clock; or
3. a real legal, stopped, Pack/Part/Field, collar, cutoff, chart-transfer, or
   material-service exit before the record is allowed to count the edge.

This is the theorem named in the chart-license split:

```math
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeProjectorEscapePayment.A}.
\tag{REP.2}
```

It is a physical same-packet payment statement, not a generic commutator label.

## 2. Narrow payments already checked

The terminal \(L^3\) Leray note proves a localized Leray/cutoff commutator
payment. Physically, when a stopped packet crosses a localized cutoff, the
collar derivative is charged to the legal localization, pressure/projection, or
Part/legal ledgers before the branch continues.

The base transported-collar note proves that the transported annular cutoff,
its first normal, and its base thickness are frame coordinates. Since
\(\dot A=-A\nabla u\), base collar motion is paid by the same top-strain
storage. That note explicitly leaves higher collar derivative/tower terms in
the pressure/RHS and finite-depth tower service.

The coefficient-frame note proves that relative metric/frame variation is a
same-packet strain coordinate and is absorbed by signed strain-log storage. It
also explicitly leaves the live producer as pressure/RHS service, transported
collar variation beyond the base coordinate, and annular stress-work.

Together these support the base escape component:

```math
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeBaseEscapePayment.A}.
\tag{REP.3}
```

This component is real, but it is narrower than `(REP.2)`.

## 3. What the moving projector reduces to

```math

\qquad
\dot G=-2ASA^\top,
\tag{REP.5}
```

and the same pressure/RHS and annular-stress interface. Its result has the
physical form

```math

\le

+
C e_j\Theta_j^{press/RHS}
```

So the exact projector does not create a new independent force. It identifies

That identification is not yet a finite payment theorem. The material-service


## 4. Legal residual boundary

The legal-residual correction prevents a false escape. On a nested terminal
heat-scale family, a scale-neutral \(L^1_{\rm loc}\) residual is not legal merely
because its density is physical. It must have positive scale gain, telescope as
bounded storage, or live on a physically bounded-overlap subfamily.

Therefore pressure, tower, strain, interface, selector, collar, or exact
projector terms at critical scaling cannot be thrown into `legal residual'
unless they have one of those three structures. Otherwise they remain inside the
same-material clock.

This is the physical reason base leakage is paid but full projector escape
remains open.

## 5. Negative overclaim checks

The retained-carrier no-defect and compactness-input attempts are warnings
against treating retained carrier language as payment. They say retained

same-ledger terminal source/commutator payment, or removal of a moving-chart
commutator/stress defect.

Thus `(REP.2)` cannot be installed from retained-chart vocabulary alone. It
needs a finite service payment or a pre-readout class-exit route.
