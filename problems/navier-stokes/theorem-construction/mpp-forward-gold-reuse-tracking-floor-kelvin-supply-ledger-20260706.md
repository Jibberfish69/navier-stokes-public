# Reuse tracking floor: the Kelvin supply ledger

## Correction header, 2026-07-06

Codex filed
`mpp-forward-gold-fresh-holder-architecture-gate-overlap-survivor-20260706.md`.
The spatial fresh-holder enumeration covers LOCALIZED support only; a
smooth/broadband bath can OVERLAP the producer region, where neither
the far-holder harmonic lemma (no vorticity-free standoff ball) nor
the bottom-tier charge (no localized bottom tier) is licensed. This
note's ledger is filament-tier and shares that scope: it prices
localized holders. The consequence claim below is corrected to a
THREE-object live space: overlap bath (exact matrix / bath-recursion
branch), reuse tracking floor (this note), Gate-8 allocation. The
overlap bath remains governed by BD1 (steady-or-tracking), BD3, and
the summability criterion - typed there, not priced here.

## 1. Kill condition

The corrected fresh-holder gate leaves overlapping smooth/broadband bath and
reuse. This note attacks the reuse branch: does holding with ALREADY-PRESENT
structure evade the production-clock price? Kill conditions, stated first: a
supply growth channel outside the five below; or the thin-core log edge proving
uncancellable in ladder currency. The first kills the ledger; the second is a
live exit-(ii) lead at high core-thinness. Both are typed in section 5.

## 2. The demand (consumed pieces)

At a cancellation node, C(q,h)=0, and homogeneity in the producer
amplitude makes the demanded cancelling strain proportional to a(t).
Held production means a e-folds at rate sigma, so

  S_h-dot / S_h = sigma          (held production)

- the supply must run on the production clock. This is F2 plus the
node-sitting merge, both filed and gated. Nothing new yet.

## 3. The ledger (the new step)

Leading filament order, Biot-Savart: the strain a holder supplies at
the producer is

  S_h = Gamma_h * G / (2 pi),

Gamma_h its circulation, G a pure geometry factor (range,
orientation, arrangement; dimension one over length squared).

KELVIN: for inviscid evolution the circulation of a material tube is
conserved: Gamma_h-dot = 0. Therefore, exactly,

  S_h-dot / S_h = G-dot / G.

Supply growth is geometry motion, or it is one of the non-Kelvin
channels (transport of other circulation into range; the holder's
own amplification; viscosity). That is the whole ledger:

T1. APPROACH: G ~ 1/r^2 in range r, so e-folds bought by closing
    range from r_0 to r_min equal 2 ln(r_0/r_min), floored at core
    contact. FINITE BUDGET per encounter, seed-independent - the
    kill-integral shape one level up. Re-arming is transit: the
    custody impulse-energy row prices the retreat-and-return.

T2. GEOMETRY-WORK: sustained G-growth at rate sigma by separation or
    re-orientation does work against the interaction energy, which
    is logarithmic in separation (exact classical identity). Power:

      P_hold = sigma * Gamma_h^2 / (2 pi)   (per unit length).

    The holder pays proportionally to its circulation squared, on
    the production clock, for as long as it holds.

T3. TRANSPORT-IN: growing supply by importing circulation is the
    assisted-transit custody row. Priced there.

T4. OWN-AMPLIFICATION: the regress - the holder needs its own
    producing geometry, strengths must match strengths (F2 route a),
    immune to node-sitting. Returns the problem one layer out; the
    fresh-holder enumeration already prices the layers.

T5. VISCOUS: diffusive or reconnective re-supply is order one only
    at unit-Re cores per the lambda-edge closure, and reconnection
    is custody-priced.

Kelvin plus Biot-Savart at filament tier admit no sixth channel: the
supplied field is determined by circulation and geometry, and
circulation cannot be grown in place.

## 4. The floor

Sustained holding past the T1 budget pays T2 (or re-enters T3-T5,
each priced). The demand side pins Gamma_h: cancelling the B1
differential demand at order-one range needs Gamma_h >= c_dem
Gamma_p with c_dem exact from the two-tube kernel; supplying from
far instead is closed by the far-holder lemma's standoff powers. So

  P_hold >= sigma * c_dem^2 * Gamma_p^2 / (2 pi)  (per unit length).

Holding is never free and never flat-rate: it is metered on the
production clock, in circulation-squared currency.

## 5. The edge, against ourselves

The producer's record currency per unit length carries the core-log:
energy ~ Gamma_p^2 log(d/a)/(4 pi). The POINTWISE ratio of holding
power to production therefore degrades like 1/log(d/a) as cores
thin. This is the same log that runs the K5 high-Re edge. Two
outcomes, neither claimed here:

1. The log cancels in LADDER currency - record demand per doubling
   is priced in the same log - and the reuse floor composes to a
   fixed per-octave constant. That comparison belongs to the
   Gate-8/high-Re-payer row and is exported there.
2. It does not cancel: thin-core reuse holding beats any fixed
   floor pointwise, and the (B) construction has a named lead.

Until that row decides, the reuse floor is a PRICED SCHEDULE, not a
constant.

## 6. Four-sentence result

A reused holder must grow its supplied strain at the production
rate, and Kelvin's theorem says its circulation cannot grow, so the
growth must come from approach, geometry-work, imported circulation,
its own amplification, or viscosity - five channels, no sixth.
Approach is a finite per-encounter budget (two log-lengths), and the
other four are each priced by an already-filed row, with
geometry-work costing power proportional to circulation squared on
the production clock. Therefore localized reuse holding is metered, and the
reuse branch's remaining questions are composition-side: same-packet allocation
and whether the thin-core log cancels in ladder currency. The overlapping
smooth/broadband bath remains live as the exact-matrix/bath-recursion branch.
That log edge is the note's recorded risk: if it does not cancel, the evasion
hunt has its next concrete target.
