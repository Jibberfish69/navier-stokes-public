# Root-payment predictable-weight causality collapse

## 1. Same physical packet

The object is one retained signed-height record event in one Navier-Stokes
evolution. The parent history carries a root geometry, an active weight
\(H_{e^-}\), a fresh innovation \(\Pi_e^{fresh}F_e\), a repeated-core
record/return face, and a genuine reset-innovation face.

The causality question is:

```text
Was the active weight chosen from the parent material history before the fresh
innovation was read?
```

That question matters because a weight chosen from the future selected tail can
make any half-tail look paid. But predictability alone does not create the
payment. It only prevents the detector from cheating.

The payment question is different:

```text
Does the same original packet have enough weighted generator/root energy to pay
the fresh innovation in that parent metric?
```

Both questions belong to the same physical story, but they are not the same
theorem.

## 2. What predictability proves

The stopped selector predictable-weight split proves the first-exit derivative
case. On a parent-announced first-exit edge, the interval \(I_e\) is known before
the fresh child increment is read, and

```math
H_{e^-}(t)
=
{|I_e|^2\over\int_{I_e}w(\sigma,t)^{-1}\,d\sigma}.
\tag{PWC.1}
```

If the fresh increment is represented by an original-history derivative
average,

```math
D_e^{fresh}(t)
=
|I_e|^{-1}\int_{I_e}G_e(\sigma,t)\,d\sigma
+Err_e^{legal}(t),
\tag{PWC.2}
```

then the harmonic-weight Cauchy step gives

```math
H_{e^-}(t)\|D_e^{fresh}(t)\|_2^2
\le
C\int_{I_e}w(\sigma,t)\|G_e(\sigma,t)\|_2^2d\sigma
+CR_e^{legal}(t).
\tag{PWC.3}
```

With stopped sparse first-exit tubes, `(PWC.3)` proves the derivative-exact
first-exit branch. This is proof-grade consumer work.

The stopped lifted Bessel note proves the other consumer: once a common
parent-predictable Hilbert geometry and martingale-difference split exist, the
fresh innovations square-pack by Pythagoras/Bessel. That consumer also does not
construct the geometry or the weight from the PDE.

## 3. What predictability does not prove

A parent-predictable envelope can still carry the critical half-tail. The direct
active-weight attack exhibits nested admissible intervals with

```math
\sum_{\ell\ge1}\|D_\ell\|^2<\infty,
\qquad
\sum_{\ell\ge1}H_{I_\ell}\|D_\ell\|^2=\infty.
\tag{PWC.4}
```

Making the weights parent-known removes future-tail cheating, but it does not
bound how often high active weights can occur on the same material history.
Thus predictability is a causality gate, not a source estimate.

Physically, the same packet may still emit summable raw fresh innovations whose
selected critical weight diverges. The proof must show that each high active
weight is announced by real original-history motion: log-scale derivative,
collar/turnstile motion, exchange, covector/frame motion, dynamic silent
commutator, or equivalent same-material reset/return structure.
