# Root-projection pre-entrance scale-memory producer collapse

## 1. Same physical edge

The selected-critical payment row under root projection is still about one
retained future-positive signed-height edge before record readout:

\[
e=(\text{retained high packet},\text{low pressure/strain/current coefficient},
\text{material time window},\text{signed polarity}).
\tag{PSM.1}
\]

The edge may have detector-silent service or selector/projector rotation that
the parent root/full-exchange detector cannot store directly. To support
`RecordMenuRootProjectionFaithfulness.A`, that hidden part must be paid from
the same original material history before the record reads the positive edge.

This is not downstream record storage. It is a pre-entrance question: did the
same packet already generate the critical scale weight from original transport,
pressure, incompressibility, strain/exchange, frame/turnstile, covector motion,
viscosity, legal loss, or stop data before selection?

\tag{PSM.6}

## 4. Checked obstruction: balance is not square coercivity

The natural scale-memory storage

\[
M=\langle w,L_Aw\rangle\simeq\int|\nabla_Aw|^2
\tag{PSM.9}
\]

has a material derivative that gives signed pairings between the generator,
\(w\), \(D_tw\), and frame/metric terms. That is a balance identity. It does not
control \(a\|G\|^2\). The generator can oscillate so that the pairing cancels
while the square energy remains large.

In fractional-energy coordinates the same obstruction is the positive transfer
boundary:

\[
{1\over2}{d\over dt}\|\Lambda^{1/2}u\|_2^2
+\nu\|\Lambda^{3/2}u\|_2^2
=
-\left\langle
\Lambda^{1/2}\mathbb P(u\cdot\nabla u),
\Lambda^{1/2}u
\right\rangle .
\tag{PSM.10}
\]

The critical reserve is finite only after the positive part of this transfer is
controlled and absorbed. For arbitrary smooth data, that is not a local energy
identity; it is the same Gold storage wall in selected-generator coordinates.
