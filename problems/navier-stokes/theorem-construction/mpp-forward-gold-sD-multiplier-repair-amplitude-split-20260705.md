# Two catches consumed

## 1. The s|D| repair: withdrawn, then rebuilt smaller and true

Curl's multiplier is s|D|; on a dyadic band the modulus varies; the
zero-remainder scalar claim was wrong and is withdrawn. Kept: the
vorticity-slot insertion with the EXACT multiplier - zero remainder as
a multiplier statement. Rebuilt: the scalar rung weight appears only
in the two-sided band inequality (band bottom and top times channel
energy bracket the channel helicity), which is ALL the cone ever
consumed - cone membership is an inequality family, and the readout
is defined at the band top, slack named and absorbed once.
Within-triad fractions are untouched: they live on single wavevectors
where the modulus is a scalar. The tower weighs its rungs to within
the rung's own width, and that was always enough.

## 2. The amplitude split: flicker dies twice

Low-amplitude intervals (below the named threshold theta): cannot
carry the enemy's gross - their rate is their own ceiling; charged
against the net floor in weighted currency. High-amplitude intervals:
sign changes require phase crossings at band-bounded phase speed -
minimum spacing a closed-form fraction of the fast period. The
demanded lemma's four clauses are met in order: amplitude treatment,
phase-speed bound, spacing, end-loss charging. The enemy may flicker
where it is weak, and may not flicker where it is strong.
