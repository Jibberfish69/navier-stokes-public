# Same-edge no-detachment producer topology synthesis

## 1. One edge, four physical jobs

The no-detachment object is one retained signed-height record edge before
readout:

\[
e=(\text{high packet},\text{low pressure/strain/current coefficient},
\text{material window},\text{signed polarity}).
\tag{NDT.1}
\]

For compactness to preserve record admission, this same edge must keep four
different physical attachments:

\[
\text{parent address}
\quad+\quad
\text{projector chart license}
\quad+\quad
\text{service/rotation payment}
\quad+\quad
\text{record face persistence}.
\tag{NDT.2}
\]

These are not four names for the same theorem.  They do different work in the
same physical story.

## 2. Parent address is not payment

The parent address theorem is

\[
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeParentAnnouncedSelectorCapture.A}.
\tag{NDT.3}
\]

It asks that the retained high packet, low coefficient, polarity, order lock,
primitive channel, and material window be available as a finite parent-known
stopped score before the record reads positive work.

The stopped-selector notes give useful consumer facts after that address exists:
selector stratum motion is variation, order-lock gaps sit under selected
density, and stopped projections are bounded reads of the original participation
law.  Those facts pay motion of an already addressed edge.  They do not choose
the edge before readout.

Thus `(NDT.3)' is an address/custody input.  It cannot be replaced by
selected-critical scale memory.  Scale memory can pay motion of a captured edge;
it does not prove which future-positive edge was parent-announced.

## 3. Chart license is not payment

The chart theorem is

\[
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeExactProjectorChartLicense.A}.
\tag{NDT.4}
\]

It asks that the same retained high packet remain in a material chart where the
exact pressure-Hodge/root projector has lawful symbol control, or that failure
of that chart already exits through legal, stop, chart-transfer, material
service, or Pack/Part/Field before readout.

The chart license has two lower faces:

\[
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeC11MetricTowerOrExit.A}
+
\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeHigherProjectorServiceOrExit.A}.
\tag{NDT.5}
\]

Finite same-carrier strain supports metric ellipticity.  It does not prove the
spatial \(C^{1,1}\) / symbol-seminorm tower needed by the moving projector.  The
higher projector service branch can later use record/return and
selected-critical payment after the residue is admitted to the same stopped
primitive channel.  The chart license itself remains a same-packet legality
input before that payment can be applied.

## 4. Payment is the selected-critical scale-memory wall

Once the edge is parent-addressed and chart-licensed, the shared payment
producer for detector-silent service, selector/projector rotation, and higher
projector service is:

\[
\texttt{SelectedGeneratorStorageCoercivity.A}
/
\texttt{PositiveCriticalTransferBound.A}
/
\texttt{NativeBirthChargePacking.A}.
\tag{NDT.6}
\]

In the current physical form, this says:

For the same original material history, first retained upward critical-scale
births must be caught by a parent-known selected generator/native-birth payment
before the future record reads the edge.

The half-tail obstruction is exactly why raw support cannot do this:

\[
\sum_\ell \nu_\ell<\infty,
\qquad
\sum_\ell 2^\ell\nu_\ell=\infty.
\tag{NDT.7}
\]

Local silent routing, selector BV, raw Bessel freshness, raw capacity, and
natural signed scale-memory balance are all compatible with `(NDT.7)'.  The
payment theorem must generate the selected-critical weight from original
history.

The extremal reduction gives the two current physical failure branches:

\[
\texttt{RepeatedCorePositiveVariationRootBound.A}
\quad\text{or}\quad
\texttt{ScaleInvariantSelectedCapacityNoEscape.A}.
\tag{NDT.8}
\]

The repeated-core branch asks for parent-predictable record geometry plus finite
negative return for one stopped material core.  The shrinking-core branch asks
for critical weighted parent-child reset charge after inherited no-reset
children are routed back to record/return.

## 5. Face persistence is not carrier compactness

After the edge has entered root/full-exchange currency, compactness still has a
separate job:

\[
\texttt{RecordAdmittedFullExchangeCriticalCompactness.A}
\tag{NDT.9}
\]

must preserve the admitted record face itself.  A freestanding critical carrier
can converge while the signed-height record face disappears or changes polarity.
Therefore face persistence requires lower semicontinuity of the admitted record
work in the same carrier, not only compactness of a nearby storage measure.

## 6. Strict topology

The no-detachment package has the nonduplicate topology:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeParentAnnouncedSelectorCapture.A}\\
&+\texttt{RetainedRecordEdgeExactProjectorChartLicense.A}\\
&+\texttt{SelectedGeneratorStorageCoercivity.A}
  /\texttt{PositiveCriticalTransferBound.A}
  /\texttt{NativeBirthChargePacking.A}\\
&+\texttt{record-admitted face lower semicontinuity}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\texttt{SameEdgeNoDetachmentModulus.A}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{NDT.10}
\]

This is a strict reduction and a checked obstruction.  It blocks two false
collapses:

\[
\text{scale-memory payment}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{parent address or chart license},
\tag{NDT.11}
\]

and

\[
\text{parent address/chart support}
\not\Rightarrow
\text{selected-critical payment of the half-tail}.
\tag{NDT.12}
\]

The physical story stays single: the packet that raises the signed record must
be the packet addressed, charted, paid, and retained in the compact face.  The
proof cannot trade one of those jobs for another without detaching the record
from its payer.
