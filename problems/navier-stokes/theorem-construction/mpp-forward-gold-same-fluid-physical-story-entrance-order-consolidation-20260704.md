# Same-fluid physical story entrance-order consolidation

## 1. The physical story before any route name

There is one smooth Navier-Stokes velocity field under test.  The live Gold
event is a signed critical-height record upcrossing:

\[
H(t)={1\over2}\|\Lambda^{1/2}u(t)\|_2^2,
\qquad
{dH\over dt}=P(t)-\nu D(t).
\tag{SFP.1}
\]

On a record interval \([a,b]\),

\[
\int_a^bP(t)\,dt
=H(b)-H(a)+\nu\int_a^bD(t)\,dt
\ge \lambda .
\tag{SFP.2}
\]

The theorem is about that same physical event.  A future-positive record edge is
not a clipped inventory item.  It is one same-fluid packet:

\[
e=(\text{retained high packet},
\text{low pressure/strain/current coefficient},
\text{material time window},
\text{signed polarity}).
\tag{SFP.3}
\]

Every proposed estimate must first answer a physical question about this same
edge: where is the edge before readout, what pressure/Hodge chart carries it,
what original-history currency pays its hidden service, and what storage row
absorbs the admitted signed work?

from that menu.

needs pre-readout material-service reserve or selected-critical payment.

parametrix and low-high commutator calculus are downstream of the same packet's
metric-symbol and projector-escape payments.

the future-positive signed-height edge entered parent root/full-exchange

The natural scale-memory identity gives a signed balance.  It does not give
selected generator square coercivity.  The cancellation that makes the identity
