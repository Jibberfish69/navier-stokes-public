# Same-parent source-payment package unification

## 1. Physical object

The object is one stopped parent material history inside one Navier-Stokes
velocity field. A future-positive signed-height record edge can only be paid by
events that already occur in that same parent history before the selected
readout: transport deformation, pressure-Hodge/projector motion,
incompressible frame and covector rotation, turnstile/exchange, viscous
scale-change, dynamic silent commutator, native birth, legal loss, or stop.

The half-tail obstruction is physical. A packet can have summable raw fresh
motion while the selected critical weighting diverges. Thus the proof cannot
only show that a fresh increment exists, that its weight is predictable, or
that a Bessel consumer will spend it once weighted. The proof must show that
high selected weight and fresh innovation are born together from the original
same-parent packet.

## 2. The one source package

The current repo names this same source payment in several coordinate dialects.
They should be read as faces of one package, not as independent walls.

`OriginalHistoryParentPredictableFreshInnovationCarleson.A` is the fresh
innovation face. After inherited parent transport is removed, the selected
endpoint/current seed must be a parent-subtracted commutator, covector, frame,
turnstile, exchange, or scale-derivative innovation, square-packed in the
parent metric from original history.

`SelectedGeneratorStorageCoercivity.A` is the accretive-storage face. The same
material generator must have a positive square bound, not only a signed balance
identity. Natural scale-memory storage gives cancellations; it does not by
itself control the selected generator square.

`PositiveCriticalTransferBound.A` is the signed-height fractional-energy face.
It asks for the positive part of the Leray critical transfer to be paid and
absorbed before arbitrary-data selected peaks can escape as thin high records.

`NativeBirthChargePacking.A` and `ForwardNativeReserveBirthPaysFullPLS.A` are
the ancestry/no-free-upcascade faces. Every retained selected high-ratio birth
must be lower-band carry, visible same-history birth, or paid/legal/stop/exit;
there is no fourth branch where a high selected birth appears invisibly and
reuses the same reserve indefinitely. `ForwardNativeReserveBirthPaysFullPLS.A`
is not renewed here as a standalone bottom producer. It is lawful only as the
forward-native coordinate that reduces to the same native-birth/source-payment
package and stays attached to the admitted signed-height carrier.

These faces are different readings of one physical demand:

\[
\text{same-parent high selected weight}
+\text{same-parent fresh innovation}
\quad\text{must be generated by original-history source motion before readout.}
\tag{SPU.1}
\]

## 3. What is support or consumer

`StoppedSelectorPredictableWeight.A` is a causality gate. It prevents the
selector from choosing the weight after seeing the fresh increment. It does not
bound the half-tail; a parent-predictable weight can still diverge against
summable raw fresh increments.

The lifted Bessel/Pythagoras argument is a consumer. It spends a common
parent-predictable Hilbert geometry and martingale split once the source
currency exists. It does not create the selected-critical weight or the
original-history source currency.

Parent-known representation and sparse witness packing are support. They say
how to read fresh increments and how to avoid duplicate counting after the
carrier and witnesses are already parent-known. They do not build the
accretive source from the Navier-Stokes packet.

`StoppedFullExchangeActionCarleson.A` has a layered role. When independently
derived from forward-native reserve, it supplies the stopped exchange/action
channel and pays chart crossings needed by fresh-innovation components. When
imported as a freestanding bottom source, it is circular: it assumes the
full-exchange action estimate that the native-birth/no-free-upcascade face is
meant to produce.

## 4. Noncircular implication order

The lawful order is physical first.

First, the original same-parent packet must produce native birth / selected
generator / positive critical transfer currency with a spent-reserve term that
prevents reuse down shrinking retained chains. Any older forward-native reserve
chain is read through this line, not beside it.

Second, that currency supplies full-exchange action and parent-subtracted fresh
innovation packing in the parent metric.

Third, predictability, Bessel, representation, sparse packing, reset, and
record/root consumers may spend the currency.

Fourth, the spent currency has Gold force only where it attaches to the
admitted signed-height record edge: the record work enters the same parent
carrier, each positive subface is locally thick or same-face paid, and sign
loss is retained or paid as same-face return.

In formal shorthand:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{same-parent native-birth / selected-generator / positive-transfer payment}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\texttt{StoppedFullExchangeActionCarleson.A}
+\texttt{OriginalHistoryParentPredictableFreshInnovationCarleson.A}\\

\text{predictable/Bessel/representation consumers}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\texttt{OriginalHistoryRecordResetRootPayment.A}
\text{ on the admitted signed-height edge.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{SPU.2}
\]

This shorthand is a custody rule, not a new closure theorem. It prevents the
same physical source from being counted six times under six names.

## 5. Checked obstruction

The repo still does not prove the first line of `(SPU.2)`.

The natural storage identities are signed and can cancel. Raw Bessel packing
misses the selected critical weight. Parent predictability blocks future-tail
cheating but still allows a predictable half-tail. Stopped full-exchange action
is downstream unless it is produced from forward-native reserve. The fractional
critical estimate gives small-packet absorption, but arbitrary-data Gold needs
a parent-known selected-generator coercivity or equivalent no-free-upcascade
source theorem.

Thus any route that starts from a consumer face and calls it source payment is
not proof. It is support being promoted above its physical license.
