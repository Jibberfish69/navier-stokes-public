# MPP Forward-Gold Scraped Coercive-Inequality Family Closeout

Date: 2026-06-20

## Status

Closeout complete.  The scraped inequality family does not supply a new
forward-gold proof from current inputs.

The useful net result is sharper than "these tools do not help."  They identify
exactly which kind of theorem would help: a same-carrier superlinear reserve on
the actual selected positive source, or a native identity that connects the
selected source to a signed stress/flux/action object already controlled by
Navier--Stokes.

## 1. Cauchy--Schwarz and Holder

Cauchy--Schwarz and Holder are paid consumers:

```math
\text{same-carrier }L^2\text{ or }L^p,\ p>1
\Longrightarrow
\text{terminal strip modulus}.
\tag{SCIC.1}
```

They do not produce the \(L^2\) or \(L^p\) input from finite \(L^1\) mass.
The terminal layer

```math
g_\tau(s)=\tau^{-1}{\bf 1}_{(-\tau,0]}(s)
\tag{SCIC.2}
```

has fixed \(L^1\) mass and divergent \(L^p\) norm for every \(p>1\).  This is
the exact source-square/source-residence wall.

## 2. Coercive functions and strict no-waste

A coercive functional would solve the route if it supplied a lower-bounded
drop:

```math
-{d\over ds}L(s)
\ge
c\,A_{\rm carrier}(s)-R_{\rm legal}(s),
\qquad
\inf L>-\infty,
\qquad
\int R_{\rm legal}<\infty.
\tag{SCIC.3}
```

The direct no-waste tests show that the available energy, pressure-corrected,
memory, and local nonlinear correctors do not produce `(SCIC.3)`.  They either
see only the radius-discounted physical ledger or expose the same unsigned
critical source/collar reserve.

Thus "coercive function" is the correct desired shape, not an installed
supplier.

## 3. Brascamp--Lieb and Loomis--Whitney

Projection inequalities give geometry:

```math
\text{small transverse projections}
\Longrightarrow
\text{small volume}.
\tag{SCIC.4}
```

The selected source carrier is not volume.  To turn `(SCIC.4)` into source
evacuation one needs:

```math
\mu_m^+(A)
\le
C|A|^{1-1/p}\|\rho_m^+\|_{L^p},
\qquad p>1,
\tag{SCIC.5}
```

or an equivalent same-carrier Orlicz/source-square bound.  Without `(SCIC.5)`,
positive source can concentrate on a small nodal collar or in a last time
strip.

So BL/LW are consumers for nodal-collar cleanup after source-square/UI, not a
source-square producer.

## 4. Clarkson and Minkowski

Clarkson gives uniform convexity after an \(L^p\), \(p>1\), norm exists.
Minkowski gives finite summability and norm exchange after the relevant family
norms are already controlled.

Their usable roles are:

```math
\text{produced }L^p\text{ reserve}
\Longrightarrow
\text{profile alignment / finite lobe summability}.
\tag{SCIC.6}
```

They do not recover positive-part source from signed cancellation, and they do
not turn \(L^1\) participation into \(L^p\) residence.

## 5. Mahler

Mahler's geometric-mean inequality needs positive entries in every shell/time
slot to give a useful lower output.  A terminal Zeno schedule is sparse:

```math
x_1=1,\qquad x_k=\varepsilon\quad(2\le k\le n),
\qquad \varepsilon\downarrow0.
\tag{SCIC.7}
```

Then total linear mass stays positive while the geometric mean tends to zero:

```math
\left(\prod_{k=1}^nx_k\right)^{1/n}
=
\varepsilon^{(n-1)/n}
\longrightarrow0.
\tag{SCIC.8}
```

Mahler becomes useful only after a source-residence floor is already installed.

## 6. Kantorovich

Kantorovich compares a moving metric and its inverse only after a uniform
condition number is known:

```math
\langle G\xi,\xi\rangle
\langle G^{-1}\xi,\xi\rangle
\le
{(\kappa+1)^2\over4\kappa}|\xi|^4.
\tag{SCIC.9}
```

For a material metric \(G=F^TF\), uniform \(\kappa\) is exactly a terminal
strain-tail/deformation-action input.  Heat-scale trace-free strain pulses can
have order-one normalized deformation action and summable physical cost.  So
Kantorovich is a metric-conversion consumer after the strain/action reserve is
paid.
