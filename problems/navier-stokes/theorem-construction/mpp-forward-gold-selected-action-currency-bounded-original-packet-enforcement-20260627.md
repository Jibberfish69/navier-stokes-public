# Selected action currency bounded original-packet enforcement

The retained selected unit is not an arbitrary normalized child read. It is a
bounded projection of the one original material participation packet.

This note installs that sentence as the source-level currency rule for the
bounded-test channel.

The scale-native correction is now part of the rule: a unit test in rescaled
coordinates is not automatically bounded in the original material stress
history.  If its pullback has norm \(r^{-1/2}\), the bounded-Riesz channel does
not pay that selected unit.  Such a pure zoom event must be paid by the native
reserve / scale-memory channel, or it remains an open Gold obstruction.

## 1. Admissible retained selected unit

On a stopped parent history \(P\), use the original material stress Hilbert
space

\[
\mathfrak H_P
=
L^2(\operatorname{Hist}(P);\mathrm{Sym}_0),
\qquad
G=2\nu S .
\tag{1}
\]

The original material energy law gives

\[
\|G\|_{\mathfrak H_P}^2
\lesssim_\nu
\|u_0\|_{L^2}^2 .
\tag{2}
\]

A retained selected child unit is admissible only when there is a test

\[
A_Q\in\mathfrak H_P,
\qquad
\|A_Q\|_{\mathfrak H_P}\le 1,
\tag{3}
\]

and the selected unit is read as

\[
\Theta_Q
=
|\langle G,A_Q\rangle_{\mathfrak H_P}|^2 .
\tag{4}
\]

Equation (4) is the source-level definition of retained selected action in this
Gold branch.

Equivalently, before positive selected readout the stress part of the selected
functional must be continuous in \(\mathfrak H_P\):

\[
|\Lambda_Q(H)|\le C_N\|H\|_{\mathfrak H_P}.
\tag{4a}
\]

Riesz then gives the representative \(A_Q\) in `(3)`. A selected measurement
without this uniformly bounded representative is not a retained source-level
unit; it is an unbounded scale-renormalized read.

## 2. Parent-predictable split

Let \(E_{Q^-}\) be the parent-predictable stress record. Split

\[
A_Q
=
\Pi_{E_{Q^-}}A_Q
+
(I-\Pi_{E_{Q^-}})A_Q
=
B_Q+F_Q .
\tag{5}
\]

The inherited part \(B_Q\) is parent current. Exact same-direction reuse is not
a new child unit.

The fresh part \(F_Q\) lives in martingale-difference spaces in the same stopped
material history. Hence

\[
\sum_Q
|\langle G,F_Q\rangle_{\mathfrak H_P}|^2
\le
C\|G\|_{\mathfrak H_P}^2
\le
C(u_0).
\tag{6}
\]

The inherited/root part telescopes through the parent reserve:

\[
\Theta_Q^{reuse}
\le
C\log\!\left(
1+
{\mu_G(\widehat Q)\over s_{Q^-}}
\right)
+
R_Q+Stop_Q .
\tag{7}
\]

Therefore, on each retained stopped ancestry made only of bounded-Riesz
admitted units plus inherited/root and paid defect terms,

\[
\sum_Q\Theta_Q
\le
C(u_0)+R+Stop .
\tag{8}
\]

This is the finite selected participation statement for the bounded-Riesz
admitted channel.  It is not a proof that all scale-native singularity-forcing
zoom reads have been captured.

## 3. Source-level exclusion rule

Any proposed selected read that requires

\[
\|A_Q\|_{\mathfrak H_P}>1
\tag{9}
\]

after normalization is not a retained selected unit. It is one of the paid exits:
stop, legal, donor loss, reselection, selector drift, collar/frame drift, or a
failed admission of the measurement rule.

Such a read may appear in failed attempts as a diagnostic. It has no proof force
as retained selected action.

## 4. Currency audit rule

Every source-level use of selected action in the live Gold route must now be one
of these four forms:

1. fresh bounded original-packet projection, as in (3)--(6);
2. inherited/root current, as in (7);
3. stop/legal/reselection/selector-drift defect paid before scalar readout;
4. notation alias for one of the first three.

An absolute child selected mass is not an admissible fifth form.

The older determinant, leverage, stopped-score, and entropy-compensator notes
remain support only. Their valid content is read through this currency rule:
they may describe parent reuse, first-exit drift, diagnostic failure, or
bounded-projection consequences, but they do not define a separate source of
selected action.

## 5. Consequence

For the bounded-Riesz admitted channel, the source graph can use the relay

\[
\sum_Q\Theta_Q<\infty
\Longrightarrow
\text{finite same-material full action}
\Longrightarrow
\sup_{t<T^*}\|u(t)\|_{H^s}<\infty .
\tag{10}
\]

This closes only the bounded original-packet selected-unit channel.

It does not certify the full MPP package.  The remaining mathematical producer
is the scale-native active-participation reserve:

