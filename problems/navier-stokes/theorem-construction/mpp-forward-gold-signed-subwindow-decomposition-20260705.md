# Target 4: the signed subwindow decomposition

## 1. The derivation and the decomposition

The package's extraction term is the second triad's coupling against
the conjugate pair; the instantaneous rate rho(t) carries the sign of
the instantaneous transfer. Decompose the window by that sign:
forward subwindows and backward subwindows - the sign set of a
continuous function, measurable, countable union of intervals.

## 2. Forward subwindows: the contraction runs

On each forward subwindow all three drains (both levels, the squared
phase invariant) are one-signed by construction. The
swing-contraction algebra runs verbatim there - it never needed the
whole window, only intervals of genuinely forward extraction. Horn
(b)'s net-transfer floor guarantees the forward subwindows carry
gross extraction at least the net amount, so the accumulated
contraction meets the f-scaled requirement of the un-parking
argument.

## 3. Backward subwindows: that IS the tax

Negative rho means the second triad returns energy to the package:
the swap-back - D2's return channel, by its filed definition. A
backward moment is not a hole in the proof; it is the race integral
collecting. Book it on the return line directly.

## 4. No third kind of moment

Every instant of the enemy's window either narrows the spring
(forward: contraction accrues) or pays the return (backward: tax
collected). The sign decomposition does not split the proof into a
good case and a bad case; it splits the window into two ways of
losing.
