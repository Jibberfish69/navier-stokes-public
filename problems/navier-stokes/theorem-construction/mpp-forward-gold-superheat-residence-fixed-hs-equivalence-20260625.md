# Superheat Residence / Fixed Hs Equivalence

Date: 2026-06-25

## 0. Object

The object is the original smooth solution and its transported material
annular history.  The active shell variables are read in the same material
chart:

```math
v(a,t)=u(X(a,t),t),
\qquad
q(a,t)=p(X(a,t),t),
\qquad
A=F^{-1},
\qquad
G=AA^\top .
\tag{SHC.1}
```

For a fixed continuation depth \(N\), let

```math
E_N^{mat}(t)
```

be the weighted material record energy, let \(D_N^{mat}\) be the corresponding
weighted viscous/tower loss, and let

```math
\mathcal R_{sh,N}(t)
:=
\sum_j
\Theta_j^{mat}(t)e_j(t)
\mathbf 1_{\{\Theta_j^{mat}(t)>\delta2^{2j}\}}
\tag{SHC.2}
```

be the super-heat-rate material residence isolated in the moving-pump
dichotomy.

Here \(\Theta_j^{mat}\) is the full same-packet material rate:

```math
\Theta_j^{mat}
=
\Theta_j^{strain}
+\Theta_j^{press}
+\Theta_j^{coef}
+\Theta_j^{collar}.
\tag{SHC.3}
```

No term in `(SHC.3)` is a detached pressure, viscosity, frequency, or endpoint
object.

## 1. Finite superheat residence gives the material record bound

The weighted material pump identity is

```math
{d\over dt}E_N^{mat}(t)+D_N^{mat}(t)
=
\mathcal M_N^{mat}(t)+R_N^{legal}(t),
\tag{SHC.4}
```

where \(\mathcal M_N^{mat}\) is the same pressure/incompressibility/coefficient
interface plus viscous coefficient commutator of the transported packet.

The sub-heat/super-heat dichotomy gives, after summing over the active shells
and absorbing the sub-heat part into viscosity,

```math
[\mathcal M_N^{mat}(t)]_+
\le
{1\over2}D_N^{mat}(t)
+C\mathcal R_{sh,N}(t)
+R_N^{legal}(t).
\tag{SHC.5}
```

Since

```math
\mathcal M_N^{mat}(t)\le[\mathcal M_N^{mat}(t)]_+,
\tag{SHC.6}
```

equations `(SHC.4)` and `(SHC.5)` imply the one-sided material record
inequality

```math
{d\over dt}E_N^{mat}(t)
+{1\over2}D_N^{mat}(t)
\le
C\mathcal R_{sh,N}(t)
+C R_N^{legal}(t).
\tag{SHC.7}
```

Integrating from \(0\) to \(t<T_*\) gives

```math
E_N^{mat}(t)
+{1\over2}\int_0^tD_N^{mat}(s)\,ds
\le
E_N^{mat}(0)
+C\int_0^t\mathcal R_{sh,N}(s)\,ds
+C\int_0^tR_N^{legal}(s)\,ds .
\tag{SHC.8}
```

Smooth initial data gives \(E_N^{mat}(0)<\infty\).  The legal residual is
restricted by the installed normal form to be summable.  Therefore

```math
\int_0^{T_*}\mathcal R_{sh,N}(t)\,dt<\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sup_{t<T_*}E_N^{mat}(t)<\infty
\quad\text{and}\quad
\int_0^{T_*}D_N^{mat}(t)\,dt<\infty .
\tag{SHC.9}
```

By the installed material-record readout, for every fixed \(s>5/2\) one
chooses finite \(N=N_s\) so that

```math
\sup_{t<T_*}E_{N_s}^{mat}(t)<\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\sup_{t<T_*}\|u(t)\|_{H^s}<\infty .
\tag{SHC.10}
```

The classical continuation criterion then relaunches the solution past \(T_*\).

Thus finite superheat residence is not a diagnostic support estimate; it is
already a fixed-\(H^s\) continuation-strength theorem.

## 2. Bounded material record gives finite superheat residence

Conversely, assume the continuation-depth material record is bounded at the
finite depth required to control the annular density:

```math
\sup_{t<T_*}\mathcal P_{N+K}^{mat}(t)<\infty .
\tag{SHC.11}
```

The bounded-record theorem controls the full transported annular density
generated by the same velocity, pressure, incompressibility, viscosity,
coefficients, and collar.  The superheat residence `(SHC.2)` is one component
of that same full density after the sub-heat part has been absorbed by
viscosity.  Hence

```math
\sup_{t<T_*}\mathcal P_{N+K}^{mat}(t)<\infty
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\int_0^{T_*}\mathcal R_{sh,N}(t)\,dt<\infty .
\tag{SHC.12}
```

This direction is a consumer direction.  It does not produce regularity from
energy; it confirms that the remaining superheat residence is exactly the part
that disappears once the material history is known to continue.

## 3. Exact equivalence at the live edge

Combining `(SHC.9)`--`(SHC.12)` gives the route-level equivalence:

```math
\boxed{
\text{finite super-heat-rate material residence}
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\text{bounded continuation-depth material record}
}
\tag{SHC.13}
```

where the reverse implication may require the fixed finite depth \(N+K\) needed
by the annular density formula.

Therefore the remaining MPP-facing estimate is exactly

```math
\boxed{
\int_0^{T_*}
\sum_j
\Theta_j^{mat}(t)e_j(t)
\mathbf 1_{\{\Theta_j^{mat}(t)>\delta2^{2j}\}}
\,dt
<\infty
}
\tag{SHC.14}
```

from original smooth data, on the same transported material history.
