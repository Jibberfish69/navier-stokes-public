# Target 1: the exact channel-flux identity

## 1. Energy side: exact by construction

Define the channel flux by inserting the channel projection into the
single LINEAR increment slot of the Duchon-Robert cubic, leaving the
quadratic factor whole. Linearity of the slot plus convergence of the
Littlewood-Paley resolution in the local topology that dominates the
cubic gives, at every fixed mollification scale,

  sum over channels of the channel fluxes = the total mollified flux,

an identity of integrals with zero remainder. The partition demanded
is delivered in its strongest sense. The multiplier domain: smooth
band projections with helicity splitting, L1-normalized kernels,
bounded on the local cubed-integrability budget - the exact object
E1's display now attaches to.

## 2. Pressure: a phantom burden removed

The Duchon-Robert defect cubic is pressure-free; pressure lives in
divergence terms of the balance that vanish against test functions in
the limit. No pressure multiplier domain exists in the flux identity.

## 3. Helicity side: definition plus two listed remainder families

Channel helicity flux := signed channel wavenumber times channel
energy flux (kinematic identity, per channel, definitional). Summing
to the mollified helicity balance flux holds up to:

  (R-a) curl-mollifier commutator increments: local variation bounded
  by the mollifier gradient moment x local cubed budget x band tail;
  (R-b) off-diagonal channel-pair increments: bounded by
  band-separation geometric tails x the same budget.

Both displayed in defect-measure currency, geometrically summable,
joining the eta-budget as priced terms - the same currency as the E2
tails. The T1 transcription expands both at manuscript length.
