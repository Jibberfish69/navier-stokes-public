## 2. The R-dial monotonicity

With S = k^2 + q^2 and R = 2kq/S: c(beta)^2 = S(1 - R cos(beta)),
R in (0, 1], R = 1 exactly at k = q. Then

  F2^2 = (1 - R cos(phi)) / (1 - R cos(phi + w)),

and the R-derivative of the quotient has the sign of
cos(phi+w) - cos(phi) < 0 on the arc: F2 DECREASES as the legs
equalize; its worst case is R = 1, where c(beta) = 2q sin(beta/2)
and

  F2 >= sin(phi/2) / sin((phi + w)/2)   pointwise, every (k, q).

Identically G = c(phi-w)/c(phi) decreases in R:

  G >= sin((phi - w)/2) / sin(phi/2),

and F1 >= G by the filed slack inequality (q >= k, cited). F3 is
R-free. When the helper grows to the payer's size, every chord
fence flattens to its angle-only height.

## 3. The telescope

  gamma_home = F1 F2 F3

       [sin(phi-w)/sin(phi)]
    =  [sin((phi-w)/2)/sin((phi+w)/2)] [sin(phi-w)/sin(phi)].
