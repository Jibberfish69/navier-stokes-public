# TFE2748 original-data source theorem attack

The physical object is one coupled packet transaction.  Pressure/Hodge
re-coordinates compatibility in the stopped parent frame, viscosity/Stokes gives
heat-time thickness before a velocity/strain readout is counted, self-advection
and material frame motion move the carrier, and endpoint/selector motion changes
the readout geometry.  The Gold \(L^1\) theorem can only close if all of that
same transaction is entered into one parent-known full-exchange ledger before the
child positive part is clipped.

The July 1 audit made the source burden exact.  `(TFE.2747)' is the right
conditional source-line target, but it is not a completed original-data theorem.
The missing source theorem is `(TFE.2748)'.

## 1. Proof role

This is a direct Gold source theorem.  It is not a downstream consumer and not a
CM exit witness.  The proof method is an analytic estimate with a circularity
check:

\[
\begin{array}{c}
\text{construct the parent detector and full-exchange ledger from original data;}\\
\text{admit endpoint and zero-baseline material into that ledger before clipping;}\\
\text{prove a strict same-parent no-self-feeding storage inequality for the ledger.}
\end{array}
\tag{TFE2748A.1}
\]

The nearest wrong proof is to prove endpoint packing by using the finiteness of
`(TFE.2426)' and then use endpoint packing to prove `(TFE.2426)'.  This note
removes that loop by changing the endpoint branch from "finite by storage" to
"admitted into the storage ledger."

## 2. The theorem statement

Fix a stopped parent packet \(P\) generated by a smooth Navier--Stokes solution
before the alleged terminal time.  All objects below are parent-announced:
they are determined by the original stopped material history and the selected
covector/packet geometry before the selected child readout is clipped to its
positive part.

Let \(G_P^{root}\) be the coupled root generator and let \(d\Omega_P^{gen}\) be
the generator clock from `(TFE.2411)'.  Replace the older full-exchange ledger
by the endpoint-admitted ledger

\[
\begin{aligned}
d\Xi_P^{FE,\ast}
:={}&
|J_P^S|^2\,d\mathcal R_P
+dA_P^{collar/Hodge/frame}
+dA_P^{reselec/reset}
+dA_P^{crit,birth} \\
&+d\Omega_P^{EP,tr}
+d\rho_{crit,P}^{ac}
+d\rho_{crit,P}^{entry}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{TFE2748A.2}
\]

The last three terms are not new sources.  They are the positive-baseline and
zero-baseline first-entry material that Sections 224 and 236--237 had previously
routed through endpoint trace capacity.  The correction is their location in
the proof order: they enter \(d\Xi_P^{FE,\ast}\) before strict storage is used.

The source theorem has three clauses.

First, the stopped coupled Duhamel-Hodge pullback is a parent operator:

\[
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{(A1)}&
(\partial_t+\mathcal L_P)Y_P=G_P^{root}+R_P^{route},
\qquad \mathcal C_PY_P=0,\\[1mm]
\text{(A2)}&
\|\mathcal U_P(t,s)f\|_{\mathcal H_{root}}^2
\le
\|e^{-\nu(t-s)\mathcal A_P}f\|_{\mathcal H_{root}}^2
+d\Theta_P^0+dPaid_P,\\[1mm]
\text{(A3)}&
\displaystyle
\sum_{Q\preceq P}
\|\mathfrak d_Q^P P_Q^{A_P}(I-\Pi_{\mathcal K_P^0})h_Q\|^2
\le
C_{\Pi,N}^2\sum_{Q\preceq P}\|h_Q\|_{\mathcal H_{root}}^2
+d\Theta_P^0+dPaid_P .
\end{array}
\tag{TFE2748A.3}
\]

These are the audited estimate targets `(TFE.2735)', `(TFE.2741)', and
`(TFE.2742)'.  Section 8 derives them as support clauses from the smooth
preterminal stopped coupled packet.  They make the retained detector lawful;
they do not by themselves prove the strict positive \(L^1\) source theorem.

Second, the zero-baseline endpoint branch is admitted without using finite
full-exchange storage:

\[
\int_{\mathcal Q_P^{ret}}d\rho_{crit,P}^{entry}
\le
C_N\int_{\operatorname{Hist}(P)}d\Omega_P^{EP,tr}
+C_N\int_{\operatorname{Hist}(P)}d\Theta_P^0
+dPaid_P
\le
C_N\int_{\operatorname{Hist}(P)}d\Xi_P^{FE,\ast}
+dPaid_P .
\tag{TFE2748A.4}
\]

This is a ledger-admission line, not an integrability line.  It says the first
visible zero-baseline endpoint square belongs to the original packet's
full-exchange ledger.  It does not say that ledger is finite.

Third, the endpoint-admitted full-exchange ledger must satisfy the strict
no-self-feeding storage inequality

\[
\begin{aligned}
d\Omega_P^{gen}
&\le
C_Nd\Xi_P^{FE,\ast}
+dR_{\rm legal,P}
+dStop_P,\\
d\Xi_P^{FE,\ast}
&\le
-dM_P
+d\Theta_P^0

\tag{TFE2748A.5}
\]

This is `(TFE.2426)' with the endpoint/first-entry material placed on the

\[
\int_{\operatorname{Hist}(P)}d\Omega_P^{gen}

C_N(u_0)\mathcal R(P)+Paid(P).
\tag{TFE2748A.6}

If `(TFE2748A.5)' is proved from the original stopped coupled packet, then the
conditional chain `(TFE.2747)' supplies terminal-uniform selected \(L^1\), and
the downstream no-jump/full-clock/\(H^s\) chain may consume it.

## 8. The stopped Duhamel-Hodge and detector clauses are support

The first three clauses of `(TFE2748A.3)' are now support facts, not source
burdens.  They follow from the smooth stopped original packet before terminal
time once all metric, collar, Hodge, selector, legal, and stop defects are
charged to the already listed ledgers.

First, the full material jet

\[
Y_P=(v,\nabla_a v,A,G,q,\nabla_aq,\ldots)_P

to the material chart, projecting to the stopped incompressible/Hodge

\[
(\partial_t+\mathcal L_P)Y_P
=

\qquad
\mathcal C_PY_P=0,
\tag{TFE2748A.25}
\]

with \(\mathcal L_P=\nu\mathcal A_P+\mathcal B_P+\mathcal H_P\).  Duhamel

\[

=

+R_Q^{route}.
\tag{TFE2748A.26}
\]

This proves `(A1)' from the original smooth packet.

Second, in the stopped root inner product, \(\mathcal B_P+\mathcal H_P\) is

\[

+2\nu\|\nabla_A\mathcal U_P(t,s)f\|_{\mathcal H_{root}}^2
\le
d\Theta_P^0+dPaid_P,

and comparison with the material heat semigroup gives

\|\mathcal U_P(t,s)f\|_{\mathcal H_{root}}^2
\le

\tag{TFE2748A.28}
\]

This is `(A2)'.  Pressure is included here as the Hodge/constraint part of the
same reversible re-coordination; it is not a separate positive source.

Third, after endpoint heat normalization and removal of

The inverse norm of a zero-baseline endpoint detector is canceled by the

from arbitrary smooth preterminal original data.  The support package proves

## 9. Pre-clipping signed atoms are constructed from the original packet

This section records the raw signed-measure construction in the shorter
TFE2748 source surface.  It is the same theorem installed in the strict-storage
note at `(TFE2748B.933)'--`(TFE2748B.943)'.

Fix a stopped preterminal parent packet \(P\) and a preterminal interval
\([0,\tau]\).  Let \(dJ_P\) be the pre-clipping pressure-Hodge/material residue
after route, collar, Hodge-commutator, metric, detector-motion, legal, stop, and
endpoint leakage have been separated.  Smoothness of the original solution on
the stopped interval gives finite Radon measures, and the parent Hodge
constraint gives

\[
dJ_P=dJ_P^0+dR_P,
\qquad
dJ_P^0(X_P)=0,
\qquad
|dR_P|\le d\Theta_P^0+dPaid_P+dStop_P .
\tag{TFE2748A.31}
\]

The zero-mass clause is the before-clipping pressure constraint in parent
coordinates: after paid exits are removed, the retained pressure-Hodge part
redistributes the same parent packet and has no standalone positive mass.

Take the Jordan split of the retained zero-mass part,

\[
dJ_P^0=dJ_{P,+}^0-dJ_{P,-}^0,
\qquad
dJ_{P,+}^0(X_P)=dJ_{P,-}^0(X_P)=:m_P^J .
\tag{TFE2748A.32}
\]

Choose the same-parent coupling

\[
d\pi_P(x,y)
:=
\begin{cases}
\displaystyle {dJ_{P,+}^0(x)dJ_{P,-}^0(y)\over m_P^J},
&m_P^J>0,\\[2mm]
0,&m_P^J=0.
\end{cases}
\tag{TFE2748A.33}
\]

Then every parent-measurable test \(\varphi\) satisfies

\[
\int_{X_P}\varphi\,dJ_P^0
=
\iint_{X_P\times X_P}
\bigl(\varphi(x)-\varphi(y)\bigr)\,d\pi_P(x,y),
\qquad
\pi_P(X_P\times X_P)={1\over2}|dJ_P^0|(X_P).
\tag{TFE2748A.34}
\]

Equivalently,

\[
dJ_P^0
=
\int_{X_P\times X_P}(\delta_x-\delta_y)\,d\pi_P(x,y).
\tag{TFE2748A.35}
\]

This is the exact atomization.  Countable atom ledgers are simple-measure
approximants to the transport measure \(d\pi_P\); the parent object is the
transport integral itself.

The coefficient mass and leakage are controlled by the original energy drain.
In the local Hardy form this is

\[
\sum_\alpha |c_\alpha(t)|+|(dR_P)_+|(t)
\le
C_N\|\nabla u(t)\|_2^2
dPaid_P^{collar}(t)+dLegal_P(t)+dStop_P(t),
\tag{TFE2748A.36}
\]

and the Leray energy inequality gives

\[
\int_0^\tau \pi_P(t)(X_P\times X_P)\,dt
+\int_0^\tau |(dR_P)_+|(t)
\le
B_P^{atom}(\tau),
\tag{TFE2748A.37}
\]

where

\[
B_P^{atom}(\tau)
:=
C_N{\|u_0\|_2^2\over\nu}
+Paid_P([0,\tau])
+Legal_P([0,\tau])
+Stop_P([0,\tau]).
\tag{TFE2748A.38}
\]

For any parent-announced selector \(0\le\omega_P\le1\) fixed before the child
positive part is clipped, define the retained opposite lobe by the second
projection of the same selected atom measure:

\[
dJ_{P,\omega}^{ret,-}
:=
(\mathrm{pr}_2)_\#\bigl(\omega_P(x)\,d\pi_P(x,y)\bigr).
\tag{TFE2748A.39}
\]

The selected positive lobe and its opposite lobe have the same atom mass:

\[
\int_{X_P}\omega_P\,dJ_{P,+}^0
=dJ_{P,\omega}^{ret,-}(X_P),
\qquad
\omega_P(dJ_P)_+
\preceq_{atom}
dJ_{P,\omega}^{ret,-}+\omega_P(dR_P)_+ .
\tag{TFE2748A.40}
\]

Thus the retained opposite lobe is a bounded-below parent reserve depletion.
For \(0\le\tau'\le\tau\), set

\[
M_{P,\omega}^{atom}(\tau')
:=
B_P^{atom}(\tau)
-dJ_{P,\omega}^{ret,-}\bigl([0,\tau']\times X_P\bigr).
\tag{TFE2748A.41}
\]

M_{P,\omega}^{atom}(\tau')\ge0,

-dM_{P,\omega}^{atom}

\quad\text{on retained same-parent atoms.}
\tag{TFE2748A.42}
\]

So arbitrary original smooth data constructs the pre-clipping same-parent

The selected-critical storage theorem begins only after the critical meter is
allowed to vary across a same-parent atom:

\[
W_P^{crit}(x)d\pi_P(x,y)
\le
W_P^{crit}(y)d\pi_P(x,y)
+
W_P^{crit}(y)\bigl(e^{[b_P(x)-b_P(y)]_+}-1\bigr)d\pi_P(x,y).
\tag{TFE2748A.43}
\]

The first term is the weighted retained-lobe reserve.  The second term is the
positive log-meter variation.  Therefore this section closes the raw
pre-clipping atom attachment and leaves `(TFE2748A.5)' / `(TFE2748A.21)' as the
strict selected-critical storage line.
