# Theorem 2: the two-payer impedance structure

## 1. The obstruction being confronted

The record-cost accounting priced octaves in high-currency window
dissipation, while the only unconditional budget is low-currency (the
missing half derivative - the classical wall). Theorem 2 must pay the
per-octave toll without drawing on the unbudgeted norm.

## 2. Payer 1 - viscous residence tax (low local Reynolds)

Proved-shaped at Gate A residue (b): on marginal bands, turnover equals
the viscous clock; sign alternations are separated by turnovers; gross and
net flux are dissipation-priced. Per-octave toll O(1), paid in budgeted
dissipation. No high currency invoked.

## 3. Payer 2 - the return-race tax (high local Reynolds)

Exact input: the inviscid triad is periodic. Energy transferred from
parent to daughters returns - fully, on the swap period. This is the
vortex-spring at triad level: without viscosity, everything is paid back.
Sustained descent therefore requires HANDOFF BEFORE RETURN: the next triad
must extract the daughters' energy within a fraction of the return period.
That is an alignment-persistence demand on a window comparable to the
turnover - exactly what the twist-shear/dwell machinery prices, and its
price is amplitude-independent (the relevant clock ratios cancel
amplitude). At high M viscosity fades like 1/M but the return-race tax
does not fade: the spring gets stiffer exactly as the leak gets smaller.

Supporting amplitude-independence facts, already derived: Manley-Rowe
ceilings are dynamics-free (centered split exactly 1/2 at any amplitude -
high-M ladders are at best neutrally financed before the race tax); the
mixed-branch C_rev cancellation is a coupling-ratio fact, amplitude-free.

## 4. The claim and its consequence

CLAIM (T2, open): per-octave attenuation floor c0 > 0 uniform in local
Reynolds - viscous at low M, return-race at high M, positive across the
crossover.

CONSEQUENCE: retention <= (1 - c0)/2 per octave; critical mass along any
ladder decays geometrically; the signed height has a uniform peak bound;
smoothness follows via the installed critical-embedding import. The
kappa-bar and M weights stop being separate burdens: a uniform floor makes
the cone bookkeeping scaffolding rather than load-bearing.

## 5. Why the currency mismatch dissolves in shape

Neither payer draws on the missing currency: the low-M toll is paid in
budgeted dissipation (marginality means the viscous clock is already
there); the high-M toll is paid in phase geometry (periodicity plus
alignment impossibility), which has no norm currency at all. The wall that
stopped ninety years of estimates was the assumption that the toll must be
paid in dissipation currency at all Reynolds. It must not.

## 6. Open burdens, exactly

(i) Return-race lower bound: dwell/twist floor on the handoff window -
the previously named certification constants, now load-bearing here.
(ii) Crossover uniformity: no M-window where both payers vanish.
(iii) PDE lift of handoff-before-return at increment level (same
discipline as L1: kinematic identity, oriented admission, closedness).
Inherited: ladder-scaling locality exponent; L1-prime beyond marginal
bands. The campaign's decisive fight is now (i)-(ii): one uniform floor,
two payers, no free window.
