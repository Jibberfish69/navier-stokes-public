"threshold set equal to the spacing floor" as the amplitude
threshold. D-III's threshold is the WINDOW-LENGTH dial (how brief
is too brief - set equal to the spacing floor so no short
high-amplitude interval exists). The amplitude dial (how weak is
too weak) is catch 2's "named fraction of the net-floor rate." My
own T4 flagged the units mismatch; the answer is separation:
