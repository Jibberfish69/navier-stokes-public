# The top bracket in exact elliptic form; the bottom pays once

## C1. The root identity (exact)

At any root \(r_\sigma\) of the Gate-6 cubic,
\(r_\sigma(I_1-r_\sigma)(I_2-r_\sigma)=G_0^2\). Hence

\[
  h(r_\sigma)
  =
  \frac{(I_1-r_\sigma)(I_2-r_\sigma)}{I_1+I_2-2r_\sigma}
  =
  \frac{G_0^2}{U_\sigma},
  \qquad
  M_\sigma^{\rm fr}
  =
  U_\sigma\,\langle X\rangle_{\rm fr}
  -
  G_0^2 .
\]

The gated one-sided supplier is therefore EXACTLY

\[
  \Delta_{\rm G6a}^{\rm top}>0
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \inf_{\mathcal K}
  \Bigl[\,U_+\bigl(\langle X\rangle_{\rm fr}-d_{\rm R4}\bigr)-G_0^2\,\Bigr]
  \;>\;0 .
\]

## C2. The exact average (classical)

On the oscillation \(X(t)=r_-+A\,{\rm sn}^2(\lambda t;m)\),

\[
  \langle X\rangle_{\rm fr}
  =
  r_- + A\,\sigma(m),
  \qquad
  \sigma(m)=\frac{1-E(m)/K(m)}{m},
\]

with \(\sigma\) increasing on \([0,1)\), \(\sigma(0^+)=\tfrac12\),
\(\sigma(m\to1)\to1\). Hence ALWAYS

\[
  \langle X\rangle_{\rm fr}\;\ge\;r_-+\frac{A}{2}:
\]

the spring's average height is at least half the swing above the
floor.

## C3. The sufficient displayed row

With the gate's improved floor \(U_+\ge A(r_*-r_+)\) and
\(G_0^2=r_-r_+r_*\) (Vieta), the per-cell top margin obeys

\[
  U_+\bigl(\langle X\rangle_{\rm fr}-d_{\rm R4}\bigr)-G_0^2
  \;\ge\;
  A(r_*-r_+)\Bigl(r_-+\frac{A}{2}-d_{\rm R4}\Bigr)-r_-r_+r_* ,
\]

so the SUFFICIENT ROW, exact in root coordinates, is

\[
  \boxed{\;
  A\,(r_*-r_+)\Bigl(\frac{A}{2}-d_{\rm R4}\Bigr)
  \;\ge\;
  r_-\Bigl[\,r_+r_* - A(r_*-r_+)\,\Bigr]_+
  \;}
\]

On the horn (\(r_-\to0\)) it holds whenever \(A/2>d_{\rm R4}\) —
the swing beating twice the drift. Off the horn, the right side is
the displayed \(r_-\) correction.

## C4. The bottom pays once (telescoping)

The gate's per-episode offset \(r_-(t_0)\) telescopes across
consecutive routed episodes of one same-packet history:

\[
  \sum_k\bigl[r_-(t_0^k)-r_-(t_1^k)\bigr]
  =
  r_-(\text{initial})-r_-(\text{final})
  \;\le\;
  r_-(\text{initial}).
\]

The bottom's total lifetime offset is ONE prepaid coin — the
\(r_-\) the history entered with — not a per-episode charge:

\[
  B_{\rm bot}^{\rm end}=r_-(\text{initial})\le r_+^{\rm cap},
\]

one bounded term per history in the endpoint/length row. No bulk
dichotomy, no bottom margin anywhere: the one-sided architecture
covers all \(r_-\).
