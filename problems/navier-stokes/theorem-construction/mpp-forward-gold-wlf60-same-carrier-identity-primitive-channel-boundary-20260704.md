# WLF.60 same-carrier identity primitive-channel boundary

## 1. Physical story

WLF.60 is not a request for another label on a child packet.  It is asking for a
finite selected-critical service bill on one parent history \(P\).  The parent
velocity packet is transported, pressure and incompressibility choose the
projected stress, viscosity dissipates part of the packet, and the remaining
signed pressure/strain work appears later as a heat-lag first-admission pulse.
The parent weight is the scale price of making that delayed selected pulse
readable.

The physical question is therefore:

\[
\text{does the same fluid carrier that creates the lower fresh source also carry
the later selected positive queue that asks for weighted service?}
\tag{WSC.1}
\]

If the answer is yes, the bill can be attacked on one carrier.  If the answer is
not yet proved, the proof is only comparing two legitimate readouts of the same
Navier-Stokes evolution after selection.

## 2. What WLF.60 already reduced to

The current WLF.60 pressure test says that same-parent detection, first-admission
updates, no-recount pushforward, raw Bessel admission, and local native atoms are
installed support.  They do not imply the weighted selected bill:

\[
\sum_{I\subset P}\rho_I^{fresh}
\le
C_N(u_0)\mathcal R(P)+R_{\rm legal}(P)+Stop(P).
\tag{WSC.2}
\]

The direct critical estimate gives only the small \(\dot H^{1/2}\) packet case.
For arbitrary original data the smaller live forms are:

\[
\int_0^{T_*}
H_w(t)\|\mathcal P_{avg}^{fresh}(t)\|_{L_x^2}^2\,dt
\le
C_N(u_0)\mathcal R(P)+R_{\rm legal}(P)+Stop(P),
\tag{WSC.3}
\]

or

\[
\widetilde Z^{fresh}
=
(\partial_\sigma-1)\partial_\sigma G^{fresh}
+R^{fresh},
\tag{WSC.4}
\]

with the residual paid by original-history legal/stop service or by selected
overrun payment on the actual positive native carrier.

In the older source-square language this is the ordered pair:

\[
\texttt{FreshSameCarrierIdentity.A}
+
\texttt{FreshSelectedCoefficientOverrunPayment.A}.
\tag{WSC.5}
\]

The identity comes first.  Without it the overrun layer is not yet known to be
the same selected native carrier as the lower fresh source.

## 4. Boundary: ownership is not identity

The primitive chain does not yet prove `FreshSameCarrierIdentity.A`.

Primitive ownership says:

\[
\text{this signed event has a lawful pre-readout carrier and owner.}
\tag{WSC.11}
\]

Fresh same-carrier identity says:

\[
\text{the lower fresh source and the upper selected positive queue are the same
selected positive native trilinear carrier.}
\tag{WSC.12}
\]

Those are different physical claims.  A same-packet pressure or strain event may
be admitted before readout, while the later positive overrun queue is still a
chosen positive face of the carrier.  Negative return events, detector-silent
events, cancellation pieces, and unretained primitive events can all be lawful
same-packet events without being the selected positive overrun carrier that pays
WLF.60.

Thus the primitive channel package can serve as a producer for the identity only
after it is combined with a lower/upper selected-carrier identification:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{primitive pre-readout event ownership}\\
&+\text{lower fresh source = upper selected positive queue on that owner}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\texttt{FreshSameCarrierIdentity.A}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{WSC.13}
\]

The second line is the live extra theorem.  It is not installed by fixed-chart
formulas, raw same-packet Hodge identities, retained-edge selector capture, or
common-selector ownership alone.

## 5. Checked obstruction to shortcut closure

The invalid shortcut is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{same-material primitive event support}\\
&+\text{stopped carrier/common-selector admission}\\
&+\text{raw Bessel/no-recount first-admission support}\\
&\not\Rightarrow
\text{WLF.60}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{WSC.14}
\]

The physical reason is selected linear service.  Raw square/Bessel mass can be
finite while a coherent half-tail keeps asking for unit selected-critical service
on thinner and thinner heat-lag scales.  The primitive event can be a lawful
same-packet event at every scale, and the no-recount rule can prevent duplicate
counting, while the selected positive overrun still has no finite parent-weighted
root bill.

So the primitive-channel work blocks one class of fake proof: it prevents
post-readout event ownership.  It does not block the WLF.60 half-tail unless the
same selected carrier also carries the lower fresh source and the upper positive
overrun payment.

## 6. Current clean target

The physical target under WLF.60 is now:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{pre-readout primitive ownership}\\
&+\text{fresh lower/upper same-selected-carrier identity}\\
&+\text{selected overrun payment on that carrier}\\
&\Longrightarrow
\text{finite parent-weighted first-admission bill}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{WSC.15}
\]

Equivalently, prove the stopped potential-average estimate `(WSC.3)' or the
positive second-order scale exactness formula `(WSC.4)' on the same selected
carrier.  The primitive-channel story is the physical roof underneath the word
"same"; it is not the selected-carrier identity or the overrun payment by
itself.
