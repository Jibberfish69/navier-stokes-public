# GOAL: decide the confinement constant

## The claim to be decided

Either there exists c > 0 such that every producing configuration of
the true field pays geometric eviction at rate at least c times its
production - or there exists a producing class that evades it. One is
true. The goal is reached on the day we know which.

## Why decide, not close

Every route in the repo and the literature ends at the same
tied-scaling inequality; the constant is its entire content. If the
constant holds AND composes onto the record ladder, the gold route
closes - two further gates, taken in order, never assumed. If it
fails, the physical story receives its honest boundary as a theorem.
Both endings are deliverables. The MPP finishes only through the
first, and no schedule may presume it.
