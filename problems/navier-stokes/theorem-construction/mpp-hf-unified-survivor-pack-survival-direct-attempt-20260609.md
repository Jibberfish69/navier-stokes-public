## Direct Attempt

on the canonical same-solution record.




## Why This Does Not Close

The sharpened h/F reduction is not yet a contradiction.

Preterminal smoothness gives the identities defining `h`, `F`, and `D_{h/F}`.
It does not give uniform terminal vanishing of `D_{h/F}/|r|` on every licensed
scale window. That missing vanishing is the same mathematical content as a
terminal strain-integrability or Pack-restoration theorem.

The enhanced survivor packet repairs an earlier scalar defect: signed readouts
can factor through `J_*`, and positive quadratic readouts can factor through
`M_*`. But the audit keeps the real promotion atoms open:

```text
USCP.A2-Pack,
USCP.A2-Field,
USCP.B^sharp,
(B1)^sharp + (B2)^sharp + (B3)^sharp.
```

The two-family comparison is also not closed. The current terminal comparison
has:

```text
Family A: affine quotient / lower-prefix / shell barrier readouts,
Family B: gap-kernel / upper-tail readouts.
```

The h/F packet proposes a common source object for those families. It does not
prove that the historical terminal survivors already factor through that object
with theorem-grade bridge edges.

The pair-defect PDE packet gives a legitimate parabolic home for observability:

```text
W_J solves a coarse two-point parabolic equation
with explicit high-frequency divergence forcing.
```

That packet still depends on a route-local observability or backward-uniqueness
input and on source estimates strong enough to make the h/F survivor mass
vanish. Those inputs are not installed.
