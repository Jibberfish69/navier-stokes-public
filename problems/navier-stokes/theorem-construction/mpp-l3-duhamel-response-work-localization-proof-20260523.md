legal/cutoff/collar/projection loss has already carried the terminal lower
bound. Let `Q_m` be the selected terminal cylinder of radius `ell_m` on the

```math
\ell_m^{-2}\int_{Q_m}|w_m|^3\,dxdt\ge c_0>0 .
```

Choose a smooth cutoff `eta_m` equal to one on the selected inner terminal
cylinder and supported in the selected ledger cylinder. By losing only a fixed
factor, the lower bound remains on the inner cylinder. Define the critical dual
test

```math

{|\eta_m w_m|\eta_m w_m\over \|\eta_m w_m\|_{L^3(Q_m)}^2}.
```

```math

```

Let `Phi_m` be the backward heat-Leray response generated by this spacetime

```math

e^{(t-s)\nu\Delta}P\psi_m(t)\,dt .
```

smooth carrier. Fubini's theorem applied to the Duhamel formula gives the exact

```math

P\nabla\cdot(u_m\otimes u_m)(s)\cdot\Phi_m(s)\,ds
```

```math

```

backward response remains on the selected carrier and inside the same
