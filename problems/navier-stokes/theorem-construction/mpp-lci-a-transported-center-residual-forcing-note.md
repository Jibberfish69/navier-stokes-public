## Exact Residual Center-Forcing Surface After `LCI.B2g4`

Define the total pressure-center ledger

```math
\mathfrak K_{N,m,\rho,\psi}^{press,ctr}(t)
:=
\sum_{q=0}^{m}\mathfrak K_{q,\rho,\psi}^{press,ctr}(t),
\tag{RCF.5}
```

the top-viscous center ledger

```math
\mathfrak K_{N,m,\rho,\psi}^{visc,top,ctr}(t)
:=

\tag{RCF.6}
```

and the residual center-forcing ledger

```math
\mathfrak F_{N,m,\rho,\psi}^{ctr,res}(t)
:=
\mathfrak K_{N,m,\rho,\psi}^{press,ctr}(t)
+
\mathfrak K_{N,m,\rho,\psi}^{visc,top,ctr}(t).
\tag{RCF.7}
```

### Proposition `RCF.2a` (After `LCI.B2g4`, only `\mathfrak F^{ctr,res}` remains externally unreduced)

For every lower viscous rung `0\le q\le m-2`,

```math
\mathfrak K_{q,\rho,\psi}^{ctr}(t)
\le
\mathfrak K_{q,\rho,\psi}^{press,ctr}(t)
+
C_q\,\nu\,\mathfrak A_{q+2,\rho,\psi}^{ctr}(t).
\tag{RCF.8}
```

So every lower-rung viscous contribution is already recycled through the
transported-center carrier system itself. What remains externally unreduced
after `LCI.B2g4` is exactly the residual center-forcing ledger
`\mathfrak F_{N,m,\rho,\psi}^{ctr,res}` from `(RCF.7)`.

### Proof

Proposition `LCI.B2g4` gives
`\mathfrak K_{q,\rho,\psi}^{ctr}\le \mathfrak K_{q,\rho,\psi}^{press,ctr}
+ \mathfrak K_{q,\rho,\psi}^{visc,ctr}` and, for `0\le q\le m-2`,
`\mathfrak K_{q,\rho,\psi}^{visc,ctr}\le C_q\nu\mathfrak A_{q+2,\rho,\psi}^{ctr}`.
Combining those two inequalities yields `(RCF.8)`. The only viscous ledgers not
already recycled this way are therefore the top two, and the pressure-center
readout remains external at every rung. That is exactly `(RCF.7)`. ∎
