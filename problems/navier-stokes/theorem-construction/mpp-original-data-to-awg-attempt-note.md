
## Local-Energy No-Hopping Attempt

Pull a bad window back to a normalized moving CKN cylinder. The local energy
inequality gives

```math
A(\tau_2)+D(\tau_1,\tau_2)
\le
A(\tau_1)
+F_{cut}+F_{conv}+F_{press}+F_{frame}.
\tag{LE}
```

The pressure and frame terms are exactly the selected `\nu_{SCF}` and geometry
ledgers. On `(AWG.bad)` they are small or already charged.

The convective term is the problem. After affine normalization and interpolation
it has the same active factor as the velocity channel:

```math
F_{conv}
\le
C_{LE}
(A^{aff}D)^{3/4}
```

where `C_{LE}` includes the retained same-fluid chart constants on the selected
normalized cylinder. Thus `(LE)` proves only:

```math
\text{high affine amplitude at }\tau_2
\Longrightarrow
\text{high affine amplitude at }\tau_1
```

unless pressure/dissipation/active-square charge is spent.

This is a backward persistence statement, not a contradiction. It says that an
uncharged active amplitude packet must hop to an earlier comparable packet.

## Required No-Hopping Theorem

To convert the previous implication into `AWG.A`, the route needs:

```math
NOHOP.A:
\quad
\text{an infinite terminal chain of uncharged affine-amplitude hops is
impossible unless it creates active-window charge.}
```

More explicitly, for every terminal chain

```math
Q_{R_{\ell+1}}^\Phi \leadsto Q_{R_\ell}^\Phi
```

generated by the local-energy persistence alternative, one must prove either

```math
\sum_\ell SCF_{norm}^{\nu}(Q_{R_\ell}^\Phi)\ge c\sum_\ell 1,
```

or

```math
\sum_\ell \mathcal R_\ell\ge c\sum_\ell 1
```

for a reserve already known to be summable, or else show that the chain reaches
a fixed positive scale in finite backward steps. No installed surface proves
this.

Finite energy alone does not supply `NOHOP.A`: the quantity being counted is
scale-normalized and can move through shrinking terminal windows while raw
energy remains finite.

## Dyadic Source Attempt

The equivalent dyadic shell model is

```math
E_j'(t)+c\nu 2^{2j}E_j(t)
\le
F_j(t).
```

`OriginalSmoothData` gives finite raw energy and first-moment dissipation:

```math
\sum_j\int D_j(t)\,dt<\infty.
```

But `AWG.A` needs active-square control such as

```math
\int 2^{-j}D_j(t)^2\,dt<\infty
```

on terminal active shells, or the pointwise gain

```math
2^{-j}D_j(t)^2
\le
\varepsilon\nu D_j(t)+r_j(t)
```

with summable reserve. The installed source closures are `L^1` source closures;
they do not give square-source control of `F_j` on moving active shells.

Thus the dyadic route fails at the same place as the local-energy route:

```math
\boxed{
\text{source-balanced active pulses are not excluded by the current inputs.}
}
```
