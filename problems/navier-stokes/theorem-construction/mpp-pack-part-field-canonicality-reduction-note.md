## Stable Carried Structures

For `TRI.E1`, the carried structures are taken in the narrowed stable sense

```math
(u,p,\Phi,F),
\qquad
F:=\nabla_a\Phi.
\tag{TCR.2}
```

The remaining readouts used by the installed route are derived from those same
carriers:

1. the finite-separation field

```math
h(a,r,t):=\Phi(a+r,t)-\Phi(a,t)
\tag{TCR.2a}
```

is determined by `\Phi`;
2. the tower carrier family

```math
U_k:=\nabla^k u,
\qquad
K_k:=-\nabla^{k+1}p+\nu\Delta U_k
\tag{TCR.2b}
```

is determined by `(u,p)`;
3. the neighboring-difference readouts `\delta_hU_k`, `\delta_hK_k` are then
   determined by `(u,p,\Phi,F)` through the same field and the transported
   separation geometry.

So a candidate primitive factorization may speak through those derived readouts,
but it may not count them as additional primitive carriers distinct from
`(u,p,\Phi,F)`.

## Fixed Semantic Boundaries

Three semantic boundaries are already installed.

### Boundary 1. No extra transported substance

Transported regions are only reparametrizations of the same field:

```math
\boxed{
\text{no lawful primitive may introduce a new sub-fluid, surface-friction law, or interface mechanics on transported parcels.}
}
\tag{TCR.3}
```

So any primitive law speaking through transported geometry must still be a law
of the same field carried by `(\Phi,F)` and its derived finite-separation
readout `h`.

### Boundary 2. Pressure is not a separate primitive body

Pressure is tied to incompressibility by

```math
-\Delta p=\partial_i\partial_j(u_i u_j),
\tag{TCR.4}
```

and in the class-membership reading it acts inside the common participation law
rather than as a separate free primitive.

So no fourth primitive law may be introduced by declaring "pressure law" as an
independent primitive source surface.

### Boundary 3. Derived readouts and downstream branches are consumers, not new
primitives

Finite separation, tower ledgers, neighboring-difference ledgers, selector
packets, exact-potential projectors, Hodge/normal-covector readouts, YM-heat
carriers, and Euclidean export conditions are derived readouts, downstream
consumers, or auxiliary suppliers once `(u,p,\Phi,F)` is fixed.

Therefore:

```math
\boxed{
\text{no derived readout or downstream branch may serve as a new primitive source-level law family.}
}
\tag{TCR.5}
```

## The Three Source-Level Service Families

Inside those boundaries, the carried law bundle already exposes exactly three
source-level service families.

### 1. Material carrier / deformation service

The flow-map, deformation, finite-separation, and volume-preservation laws

```math

\qquad

\det F=1,
```

## Definition `TCR.A0` (Route-lawful primitive factorization on stable carriers)

Fix a still-live window `Q`, a finite depth `N`, and a scale `r>0`.

A candidate primitive local-law factorization

```math
\mathscr P=\{L_j\}_{j\in J}
\tag{TCR.11a}
```

is called **route-lawful on the stable carriers `(u,p,\Phi,F)`** if:

1. every clause `L_j` is written only through `(u,p,\Phi,F)` and the derived
   readouts from `(TCR.2a)`--`(TCR.5)`;
2. the conjunction `\bigwedge_{j\in J}L_j` is asserted to characterize lawful
   local one-fluid membership on `Q`;
3. no clause violates the semantic boundaries `(TCR.3)`--`(TCR.5)`.

For such a clause, define its **triad support**

```math
\operatorname{supp}_{\triangle}(L_j)\subset
\{\mathrm{Pack},\mathrm{Part},\mathrm{Field}\}
\tag{TCR.11b}
```

to be the smallest subset of witnesss whose readouts occur in `L_j`.

## Theorem `TCR.A` (Stable-carrier canonicality reduction to triad-generated form)

Let `\mathscr P` be any candidate primitive local-law factorization of lawful
one-fluid Navier--Stokes behavior that is route-lawful in the sense of
`(TCR.11a)`--`(TCR.11b)`.

Then every primitive clause of `\mathscr P` has nonempty triad support

```math
\varnothing\neq \operatorname{supp}_{\triangle}(L_j)\subset
\{\mathrm{Pack},\mathrm{Part},\mathrm{Field}\},
\tag{TCR.12a}
```

and no clause carries primitive source content outside those three services.

More exactly:

1. if `\operatorname{supp}_{\triangle}(L_j)=\{\mathrm{Pack}\}`, then `L_j` is a
   lawful rebadging or refinement of the packing predicate
   `\mathrm{Pack}_Q(u,\Phi)`;
2. if `\operatorname{supp}_{\triangle}(L_j)=\{\mathrm{Part}\}`, then `L_j` is a
   lawful rebadging or refinement of the participation predicate
   `\mathrm{Part}_{N,Q}(u,p)`;
3. if `\operatorname{supp}_{\triangle}(L_j)=\{\mathrm{Field}\}`, then `L_j` is a
   lawful rebadging or refinement of the one-field predicate
   `\mathrm{Field}_{N,r,Q}(u,p)`;
4. if `|\operatorname{supp}_{\triangle}(L_j)|\ge 2`, then `L_j` is a mixed
   clause generated by the conjunction of those supporting triad services.

Consequently, `\mathscr P` is triad-generated.

Equivalently,

```math
\boxed{
\text{inside the installed stable-carrier route, every lawful primitive factorization built from }(u,p,\Phi,F)\text{ collapses to the packing / participation / one-field envelope up to lawful rebadging, refinement, and regrouping.}
}
\tag{TCR.12}
```

### Proof

Take one primitive clause of `\mathscr P`.

If it speaks only through transported material geometry, deformation,
invertibility, volume preservation, or finite parcel separation, then by
`(TCR.2)`--`(TCR.3)` and `(TCR.6)` it is a law of the same carried geometry
`(\Phi,F,h)`, so its support is `\{\mathrm{Pack}\}` and it is a refinement or
rebadging of the packing side `(TCR.7)`.

If it speaks only through local dynamic carriage of tower rungs,
pressure-viscosity actuation, or the common differentiated Navier--Stokes
hierarchy, then by `(TCR.2)`--`(TCR.4)` and `(TCR.8)` it is carried by the same
pressure-viscosity law on `(u,p)`, so its support is `\{\mathrm{Part}\}` and it
is a refinement or rebadging of the participation side `(TCR.9)`.

If it speaks only through compatibility of neighboring local towers,
neighboring response, finite-difference propagation, or "one common field
rather than local patchwork," then by `(TCR.2)`--`(TCR.5)` and `(TCR.10)` it is
carried by the same neighboring-response law on one common field, so its
support is `\{\mathrm{Field}\}` and it is a refinement or rebadging of the
one-field side `(TCR.11)`.

If it mixes those readouts, then because the only lawful source services on the
same stable carriers are `(TCR.7)`, `(TCR.9)`, `(TCR.11)`, its primitive content
is generated by the conjunction of the corresponding supporting services.

No fourth primitive case remains. A candidate clause outside those support
categories would have to do one of the following:

1. add a new transported substance or interface primitive, forbidden by
   `(TCR.3)`;
2. split pressure into an independent primitive body, forbidden by `(TCR.4)`;
3. promote a derived or downstream selector/projector/normal/export/carrier
   readout to primitive status, forbidden by `(TCR.5)`.

Therefore every lawful primitive clause of `\mathscr P` is triad-generated, and
the whole factorization carries no primitive source content outside the triadic
service envelope. `\square`
