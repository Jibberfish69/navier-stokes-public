# MPP Periodic Terminal-Promotion Bridge

## Status

Theorem-facing bridge note for the periodic Navier-Stokes branch on
`\mathbb T^3`.

This note repairs the earlier bridge wording.  The installed Theorem 2.1
class-membership closure warrant on retained same-fluid approach windows is not
by itself a proof that the maximal classical periodic solution cannot have a
finite endpoint.  The endpoint exclusion becomes a continuation theorem only
after the same branch supplies a uniform terminal-tail continuation readout.

## Theorem

Let \(u_0\in C^\infty(\mathbb T^3;\mathbb R^3)\) be divergence-free and
mean-zero, and let \((u,p)\) be the corresponding maximal classical solution of
the unforced incompressible Navier-Stokes equation on \([0,T_*)\).

Assume the installed Theorem 2.1 class-membership closure warrant on every
retained same-fluid approach window generated by this maximal classical
solution.  Assume also that the terminal package gives the same-branch
continuation readout

\[
  \sup_{t<T_*}\|u(t)\|_{H^s(\mathbb T^3)}<\infty
  \qquad\text{for some }s>5/2.
\]

Then \(T_*<\infty\) is impossible.

## Proof

Assume for contradiction that \(T_*<\infty\).  Let

\[
  M_s:=\sup_{t<T_*}\|u(t)\|_{H^s(\mathbb T^3)}<\infty.
\]

Classical periodic Navier-Stokes local theory at this fixed \(H^s\) level gives
a restart lifespan \(\tau=\tau(\nu,s,M_s)>0\).  Choose
\(t_j\uparrow T_*\) with \(T_*-t_j<\tau/2\).  Restarting the original
classical solution at \(t_j\) extends it past \(T_*\), and uniqueness identifies
the restarted branch with \((u,p)\) on the overlap.  This contradicts
maximality.

The class-membership and endpoint-matrix inputs remain relevant only as the
route that is supposed to produce the displayed terminal-tail readout.  They do
not replace that readout.

## Consequence

On the periodic branch, the exact bridge is

\[
  \text{same-branch terminal-tail }H^s\text{ readout}
  \Longrightarrow
  T_*=\infty.
\]

The remaining terminal-promotion work is to prove that readout from the original
periodic dynamics, equivalently `PCTP.hard` / `TTU.A` with the endpoint package
and readout bridge on the same maximal solution.
