This note works the final loop item: the Row 5 lifted high-side non-low packet. It is separate from the Row 6 positive source-wall square reserve. Its target is to make the lifted high-side commutator a legal part of `R_N` in the gradient-tail inequality.

```math

[\Delta_j,(S_{j-4}-S_{N+M})u(t)\cdot\nabla]\Delta_j u(t),
```


This would place the lifted high-side remainder into legal `R_N` and complete the non-low commutator packet once the other Row 5 slots are held fixed.

## Failed shortcuts

### Shellwise carrier shortcut

The lifted band has geometry

```math
N+M<k<j-4.
```

The coefficient shell may lie far below the output shell `j`, so finite near-diagonal high-high support does not apply. The genuine high-high cubic-tail absorption lemma does not control this term.

### Pointwise tail-smallness shortcut

A bound of the form `|\mathcal J_N^{lift}(t)|\le C\|u_{\ge N+M}(t)\|_2(\cdots)`
would require pointwise high-tail smallness. The available scale barrier is
integrated in time, not pointwise. Thus this shortcut cannot produce

```math
\varepsilon\nu D_N(t)+C_*2^{-2\delta N}
```

at almost every time.

### Integrated shortcut using `\sup E_N`

An integrated estimate of the form

```math
\int \|u_{\ge N+M}\|_2 E_N^{1/2}D_N^{1/2}dt
```

would become circular if it uses the desired conclusion `\sup_t E_N<\infty` as an input. Row 5 must supply the inequality used to prove that bound.

## Signed-descent route

The correct order is the signed route. At continuous scale define the signed lifted commutator density

```math
\mathfrak c_\ell(x,t)
:=
\ell^{-2}\,
u_\ell(x,t)
[Q_\ell,b_\ell^{meso}(t)\cdot\nabla]u(x,t).
```

The signed scale-descent target is

```math
\mathfrak c_\ell
=
(\ell\partial_\ell)\Psi_\ell
+\nabla_x\cdot J_\ell
+\partial_t R_\ell
+\mathcal S_\ell^{spill}
+\mathcal E_\ell.
```

If this identity is global on the terminal lifted-band regime and if the boundary, dual derivative, spill, and error terms satisfy barrier-scale control, then the descent-coboundary theorem gives `L-Flux`.

Thus the exact conditional closure is

```math
L\text{-}Descent + L\text{-}Dual
\Longrightarrow
L\text{-}Flux
\Longrightarrow
LiftedHighSide.A.
```

Here `L-Descent` is global signed exactness of the lifted commutator channel, and `L-Dual` is the boundary/dual-pairing estimate generated by integrating the exactness identity against the `H^1` band stress.

## Verdict

The Row 5 lifted-band packet is not closed unconditionally from current inputs. It is reduced to the clean conditional theorem

```math
\boxed{
```
