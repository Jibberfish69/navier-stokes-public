The failed unconditional certificate is the last one:

```math

```

as a no-diffuse-ancestry statement.

The first-pulse construction rules out earlier threshold-crossing source-pulse
tiles on finite backward slices.  It does not, by itself, rule out diffuse legal
source-parent measures in the terminal ancient limit.

The obstruction is the legal diffuse parent model:

```math
\pi_P
=
{1\over M}
\sum_{\alpha=1}^{M}\delta_{P^-_\alpha},
\qquad
M\to\infty.
```

Every bounded parent selector captures only

```math

\le
{B\over M}
\to0,
```

while the total legal parent mass may remain bounded below by a fixed `c_0>0`.
Therefore first-pulse selection plus bounded parent ancestry does not imply
source-parent compactness.
