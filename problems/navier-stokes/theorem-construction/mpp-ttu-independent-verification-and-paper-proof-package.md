## 2. Strict-margin topology proof

Define the SCF-good radius

```math
r_g(a,t)=\sup\{r>0:SCF_{base}(Q_r^\Phi(a,t))\le\varepsilon_m\}.
```

### Lemma StrictMargin.Jump

For a terminal tail T,

```math
\inf_{(a,t)\in T}r_g(a,t)=0\Longrightarrow Jump_{avg}(T).
```

### Proof

If r_g is bounded below by R_*>0 on a compact terminal piece, the SCF-good cylinders of radius at least R_*/2 form an open cover in same-fluid coordinates. Compactness gives a finite subcover with positive minimum radius. If the infimum of available radii along the terminal tail equals zero, there is a sequence (a_n,t_n) in the terminal tail with r_g(a_n,t_n) tending to zero. This is loss of every positive same-fluid SCF-good coherence scale on the tail, exactly Jump_avg.

```math
\sum_{i,p}(1+A_{i,p}+V_i+P_i+F_{i,p})\in L^1(0,T_*),
```

```math
B=\sum_{i,p}(1+A_{i,p}+V_i+P_i),
```
