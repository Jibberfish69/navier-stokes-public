# MPP ZenoAncestryQuantization.A direct attempt

Date: 2026-05-17

Status: failed direct quantization. The idea is the correct production shape, and the installed quantities supply no quantized monotone resource yet.

## Target

`ZenoAncestryQuantization.A` asks for a positive terminal same-fluid source ancestry to spend a quantized resource before reaching the terminal face.

A useful statement would be:

```math
\text{each legal same-fluid refill edge carrying terminal source mass }\ge c_0
\text{ spends at least }\eta>0
\text{ of a monotone resource }\mathcal Q.
```

Then an infinite terminal Zeno ancestry forces infinite resource spend, contradicting a finite original-data budget.

## Test 1: scale rank

A Zeno ancestry has shrinking heat scales

```math
r_k\downarrow0.
```

Scale strictly changes, yet the decrement is continuous. The sequence can have arbitrarily small ratio drops or rapidly decreasing scales without any uniform integer rank decrement licensed by current hypotheses.

Thus scale rank supplies ordering, while it supplies no quantized spend.

## Test 2: heat-time depth

The Zeno condition allows

```math
\sum_k r_k^2<\infty.
```

Heat-time depth is exactly the wrong resource: the total time budget can be finite along an infinite ancestry.

## Test 3: raw energy or dissipation spend

A positive terminal source residue can be carried by increasingly small packets. Raw energy and local dissipation can split over infinitely many descendants. Current local energy bounds give finite total budget and finite-tree telescoping, while they supply no uniform lower spend per terminal refill edge.

A uniform spend theorem here would be an active reserve or PRD-type theorem, already audited as conditional.

## Test 4: ASAC defect spend

Zero ASAC defect is part of `B_ASAC^closed`. The ASAC-paid angular leakage channel has already vanished in the tangent class. It therefore cannot serve as the monotone resource for the surviving branch.

## Test 5: same-fluid label entropy

Same-fluid ancestry preserves carrier identity. It gives a legal lineage, not a decreasing finite label set. Parent choices can diffuse across many legal packets while preserving same-fluid coherence.

Thus label entropy needs an additional parent-concentration or branch-entropy theorem before it becomes quantized.

## Test 6: finite donor balance

Finite donor balance telescopes every finite truncation. In the infinite limit, the unpaid source moves to the terminal leaves. Turning this into a quantized contradiction is exactly the missing Zeno production theorem.

ZenoAncestryQuantization.A
