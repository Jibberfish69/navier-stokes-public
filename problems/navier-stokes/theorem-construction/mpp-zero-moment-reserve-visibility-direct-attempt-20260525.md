Normalize the first retained reserve threshold by a fixed constant

## Case Split

### 1. No retained negative partner

If the positive packet has no same-ledger negative partner on the retained
terminal family, then the positive part itself is visible.  This gives the
active height-flux level set required by `ActiveHeightFluxTrichotomy.A`.

This case is discharged by the visible positive-packet alternative above; it
does not require a separate zero-moment reserve.

### 2. Partner is not on the same carrier

If the cancelling partner lives on a different carrier, separated scale, or
incompatible packet family, the cancellation is not a legal same-fluid retained
exchange.  The defect lands in the witness tree:

```text
carrier/packing mismatch     -> Pack_Q failure,
participation mismatch       -> Part_{N,Q} failure,
unlicensed source transfer   -> Field_{N,r,Q} charge.
```

This is exactly the CM contrapositive landing.  No positive deletion theorem is
needed in this case.

### 3. Partner collapses terminally

If the cancelling partner exists only through shorter and shorter terminal
subwindows or zero-radius ancestry, the obstruction is a terminal Zeno source
residue.  Under the installed Pack correction, terminal zero-radius donor
ancestry is not `Pack_Q`.

This case is also a witness-face exit, not an in-class continuation.

### 4. Same-ledger positive-scale partner survives

The remaining hard case is:

```text
J_+ and J_- both survive at positive scale,
both stay on the same retained terminal ledger,
their first moments cancel,
and their square reserve stays bounded below by `c_0>0`.
```

This is the exact hidden branch.

To close it, one must prove that such a same-ledger signed pair cannot be a
free terminal cancellation.  It must either:

1. create active height flux on one side of the pair;
2. pay by ASAC / positive pair-weight charge;
3. force `TerminalWeightedNoFreeSink.A`;
4. force `TwoTowerDonorDepletion.A`;
5. collapse into the Zeno source-residue class.

The current installed surfaces do not prove that quantitative no-free-sink
step for every same-ledger positive-scale signed pair.

## Failed Closure Attempt

The tempting argument is:

```text
square reserve bounded below by `c_0>0` + zero signed moment
=> two visible opposite packets
=> one packet has positive active height flux.
```

That implication is too fast.  It ignores the possibility that the positive and
negative packets exchange through the same retained terminal ledger while the
observable first moment cancels on every heat subwindow.  The square reserve
sees the pair, but the active-height readout can still miss it unless the
signed-pair exchange is charged.

Thus the missing theorem is not another layer-cake selection lemma.  It is a
same-ledger signed-pair no-free-sink theorem.
