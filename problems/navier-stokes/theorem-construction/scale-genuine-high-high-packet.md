# Scale Genuine High-High Packet

## Object

`scale_genuine_high_high_packet`

## Definition

Let `\{u^{(n)},p^{(n)}\}` be the fixed classical approximation family and `N` a
dyadic cutoff. After the strict low-mode reduction and the threshold-local
spill collar have both been isolated, define the genuine scale-side high-high
packet by

```math
\mathfrak{H}^{scale,HH}_{N}[u^{(n)}](t)
:=
\sum_{j\ge N}
\left|
\left\langle
\mathcal{T}^{HH}_{j,n},
\Delta_j u^{(n)}
\right\rangle
\right|,
```

with integrated form

```math
\mathfrak{P}^{scale,HH}_{N}[u^{(n)}]
:=
\int_0^T \mathfrak{H}^{scale,HH}_{N}[u^{(n)}](t)\,dt.
```

## Exact Bound Needed

The remaining scale-side theorem burden is discharged once one proves

```math
\sup_n \mathfrak{P}^{scale,HH}_{N}[u^{(n)}]
\le
\eta\nu\int_0^T D_N^{(n)}(t)\,dt + C_0 2^{-2\delta N}
```

for all large `N`, with constants uniform on the same classical approximation surface.

This bound is now supplied by the coefficient-margin estimate in `scale-cubic-tail-absorption-lemma.md`.

## Reduction Interface

- `scale-paraproduct-reduction-lemma.md` removes the strict low-mode low-high and high-low pieces.
- `scale-spill-reduction-lemma.md` isolates the threshold-local spill collar generated by the frozen low-mode cutoff.
- `scale-high-high-near-diagonal-reduction-lemma.md` reduces the genuine high-high packet itself to a near-diagonal cubic dyadic tail.
- the lifted high-side remainder is tracked separately in `scale-high-side-remainder-packet.md`.
- What remains is the genuine high-high packet defined above, equivalently the cubic tail packet from that reduction.

## Theorem Interface

Once the displayed bound holds, the scale-barrier transport defect closes on the classical equation and Proposition S.1 of the scale package follows after the viscous margin is retained. That tail estimate then feeds the compactness and gradient packages on the same theorem surface.

## Equivalent Cubic Tail Form

By `scale-high-high-near-diagonal-reduction-lemma.md`, it is enough to prove

```math
\int_0^T \sum_{j\ge N-M} 2^j \|\Delta_j u^{(n)}(t)\|_{L^2_x}^3\,dt
\le
\eta\nu\int_0^T D_N^{(n)}(t)\,dt + C_0 2^{-2\delta N}.
```

This cubic-tail form is the intended input for the coefficient-margin estimate in
`scale-cubic-tail-absorption-lemma.md`.
