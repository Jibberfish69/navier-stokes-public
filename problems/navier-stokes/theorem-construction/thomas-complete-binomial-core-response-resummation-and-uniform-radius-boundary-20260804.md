# Thomas's complete binomial core response: exact resummation and uniform-radius boundary

Status: proved exact all-orders resummation and checked no-gain boundary inside
Thomas Birnie's one-fluid, pressure-explicit binomial/VPI
reversible-intersection method.

The physical object is one smooth fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes velocity field
generated by one datum.  A triad is only one simultaneous reading of that
field.  Its receiving leg, its two physical parents, every other parent
incident at the same output, the instantaneous pressure completion, and the
whole-field enstrophy selector all evolve together.  Following the response
to arbitrary temporal depth cannot delete an exterior parent, reset a leg, or
turn entry into new fluid.  The all-orders calculation below keeps that one
history intact.

The result is exact.  The original binomial tower resums to the continuous
actual-minus-heat response, and the complete Carleman insertion tree retains
the correct heat rate at every newly inserted parent.  The reciprocal
parent-age kernels converge pointwise, but not uniformly over unbounded parent
rates without a new tail estimate.  Where their complete resummation is
legitimate, their multiplier is exactly the critical
\(\nu^{-1}\Lambda^{-1}\) source multiplier, every nonlinear insertion
survives, and the moving selector reconstructs its signed entry/exit flux.
Thus the all-depth limit returns the joined source/insertion/crossing action in
`(DCT.47)`; it does not give a datum-finite boundary for `(TIR.37a)`.

## 1. The continuous field is derived from the original binomial tower

Put

\[
 J_{N,\alpha}=\partial_t^N\partial_x^\alpha u,
 \qquad
 \Pi_{N,\alpha}=\partial_t^N\partial_x^\alpha p.
 \tag{BCR.1}
\]

The primitive pressure-explicit recurrence is

\[
\begin{aligned}
J_{N+1,\alpha}
&+\sum_{a=0}^{N}\sum_{\beta\leq\alpha}
 \binom Na\binom\alpha\beta
 (J_{a,\beta}\cdot\nabla)
 J_{N-a,\alpha-\beta}
 +\nabla\Pi_{N,\alpha}
 =-\nu A J_{N,\alpha},\\
&\hspace{50mm}\nabla\cdot J_{N,\alpha}=0,
\end{aligned}
\tag{BCR.2}
\]

where \(A=\Lambda^2=-\Delta\), and pressure is fixed at the same rung by

\[
 -\Delta\Pi_{N,\alpha}
 =\partial_i\partial_j\partial_t^N\partial_x^\alpha(u_i u_j).
 \tag{BCR.3}
\]

For the pure-time slice \(V_N=\partial_t^Nu\),
\(P_N=\partial_t^Np\), `(BCR.2)` reads

\[
 V_{N+1}
 +\sum_{a=0}^{N}\binom Na(V_a\cdot\nabla)V_{N-a}
 +\nabla P_N
 =-\nu A V_N.
 \tag{BCR.4}
\]

Fix a terminal time \(T<T_*\) and an age \(0<\delta<T\).  Before introducing
any heat comparison, resum `(BCR.4)` itself:

\[
 \mathcal U_{T,\delta}(\theta)
 :=\sum_{N\geq0}{(-\delta\theta)^N\over N!}V_N(T),
 \qquad
 \mathcal \Pi_{T,\delta}(\theta)
 :=\sum_{N\geq0}{(-\delta\theta)^N\over N!}P_N(T).
 \tag{BCR.5}
\]

The Leibniz binomial coefficients are exactly the coefficients of the product
of the two exponential generating fields.  Hence, coefficientwise, and as an
identity wherever the series and products converge,

\[
\boxed{
 -{1\over\delta}\partial_\theta\mathcal U
 +(\mathcal U\cdot\nabla)\mathcal U
 +\nabla\mathcal \Pi
 =-\nu A\mathcal U,
 \qquad \nabla\cdot\mathcal U=0,}
 \tag{BCR.6}
\]

with

\[
 -\Delta\mathcal \Pi
 =\partial_i\partial_j(\mathcal U_i\mathcal U_j).
 \tag{BCR.7}
\]

Only now define the equivalent pressure-complete source

\[
 \mathcal B(v,w):=\mathbb P((v\cdot\nabla)w),
 \qquad
 Q(v):=-\mathcal B(v,v),
 \tag{BCR.8}
\]

so that `(BCR.6)` is

\[
 -{1\over\delta}\partial_\theta\mathcal U+\nu A\mathcal U
 =Q(\mathcal U).
 \tag{BCR.9}
\]

The source field has the exact expansion

\[
\boxed{
 \mathcal Q_{T,\delta}(\theta)
 :=Q(\mathcal U_{T,\delta}(\theta))
 =\sum_{N\geq0}{(-\delta\theta)^N\over N!}
 \left[-\sum_{a=0}^{N}\binom Na
 \mathcal B(V_a,V_{N-a})\right]_{t=T}.}
 \tag{BCR.10}
\]

Every incident parent and its pressure completion is present in `(BCR.10)`.
On a finite true-Leray Fourier prefix these are algebraic identities.  In the
PDE, if `(BCR.5)` converges in a class in which `(BCR.6)` is unique on
\(0\leq\theta\leq1\), then

\[
 \mathcal U_{T,\delta}(\theta)=u(T-\delta\theta),
 \qquad
 \mathcal Q_{T,\delta}(\theta)=Q(u(T-\delta\theta)).
 \tag{BCR.11}
\]

This is a consequence of the resummed recurrence, not its replacement by a
detached semigroup.

The heat comparison is introduced after `(BCR.10)`.  Put

\[
 \mathcal C_{T,s}(\theta)
 :=\Lambda^s e^{-\nu\delta\theta A}\mathcal U_{T,\delta}(\theta).
 \tag{BCR.12}
\]

Equation `(BCR.9)` gives

\[
 \partial_\theta\mathcal C_{T,s}
 =-\delta\Lambda^s e^{-\nu\delta\theta A}
 Q(\mathcal U_{T,\delta}(\theta)),
 \tag{BCR.13}
\]

and therefore

\[
\boxed{
 \Lambda^su(T)
 -\Lambda^s e^{-\nu\delta\theta A}u(T-\delta\theta)
 =\delta\int_0^\theta
 \Lambda^s e^{-\nu\delta rA}Q(u(T-\delta r))\,dr.}
 \tag{BCR.14}
\]

This is the continuous actual-minus-heat defect derived from the binomial
tower.  Its height-level counterpart is `(TIR.34)`: at \(t=T-\delta\),
\(z=\delta\),

\[
\boxed{
 \sum_{n\geq1}{\delta^n\over n!}\mathfrak R_{s,n}(T-\delta)
 =E_s(T)
 -\frac12\left\|\Lambda^s e^{-\nu\delta A}u(T-\delta)\right\|_2^2.}
 \tag{BCR.15}
\]

Neither `(BCR.14)` nor `(BCR.15)` compares two independent fluids.  The
heat term is the descendant of the earlier state of the same fluid, and the
defect is exactly the signed pressure-complete nonlinear refill accumulated
by its actual continuation.

## 2. Complete time-oriented generating law for the dynamic core

Use the real helical triads and both input orders of `(DCT.4)`--`(DCT.13)`.
For each triad \(\tau\), including its conjugate, let

\[
 c_\tau
 =\sum_{\ell\in\tau}r_\ell
 \operatorname{Re}\langle\widehat u(\ell),
 Q_{\tau,\ell}(u)\rangle,
 \qquad
 d_\tau=\nu S_\tau
 =\nu\sum_{\ell\in\tau}r_\ell^2.
 \tag{BCR.16}
\]

The full output remains

\[
 Q_\ell=\sum_{\sigma\ni\ell}Q_{\sigma,\ell};
 \tag{BCR.17}
\]

it is not replaced by the selected contribution \(Q_{\tau,\ell}\).  The exact
first-binomial calculation `(DCT.11)`--`(DCT.14)` is

\[
\boxed{c_\tau'+d_\tau c_\tau=a_\tau,}
 \tag{BCR.18}
\]

where

\[
\begin{aligned}
a_\tau
=\sum_{\ell\in\tau}r_\ell\bigl[
&\operatorname{Re}\langle Q_\ell,Q_{\tau,\ell}\rangle\\
&+\operatorname{Re}\langle\widehat u(\ell),
 DQ_{\tau,\ell}(u)[Q(u)]\rangle\bigr].
\end{aligned}
\tag{BCR.19}
\]

Thus `(BCR.19)` differentiates the receiving leg and both physical parents
through the complete source `(BCR.17)`.  Its exponential generating fields
are

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathfrak c_{\tau,T}(\theta)
 &:=\sum_{n\geq0}{(-\delta\theta)^n\over n!}
 c_\tau^{(n)}(T)
 =c_\tau(\mathcal U_{T,\delta}(\theta)),\\
 \mathfrak a_{\tau,T}(\theta)
 &:=\sum_{n\geq0}{(-\delta\theta)^n\over n!}
 a_\tau^{(n)}(T)
 =a_\tau(\mathcal U_{T,\delta}(\theta)).
\end{aligned}
\tag{BCR.20}
\]

The last equality is coefficientwise, or analytic where `(BCR.5)` converges.
The expression on the right of the second line is exactly `(BCR.19)` with
\(u,Q,Q_{\tau,\ell}\) replaced by
\(\mathcal U,\mathcal Q,\mathcal Q_{\tau,\ell}\).  It therefore retains every
incident parent at every depth.  Resumming the differentiated versions of
`(BCR.18)` gives

\[
 -{1\over\delta}\partial_\theta\mathfrak c_{\tau,T}
 +d_\tau\mathfrak c_{\tau,T}
 =\mathfrak a_{\tau,T}.
 \tag{BCR.21}
\]

Now keep the moving whole-field selector.  With `(DCT.19)`--`(DCT.23)`, put

\[
 \mathcal Z_T(\theta)
 :=\|\Lambda\mathcal U_{T,\delta}(\theta)\|_2^2,
 \qquad
 \Omega_{\tau,T}(\theta)
 :=\eta\!\left({\mathcal Z_T(\theta)\over Z_\tau^\dagger}\right).
 \tag{BCR.22}
\]

Its first coefficient is the complete enstrophy row:

\[
 -{1\over\delta}\partial_\theta\Omega_{\tau,T}
 ={\eta'(\mathcal Z_T/Z_\tau^\dagger)\over Z_\tau^\dagger}
 \left[2(Q(\mathcal U),A\mathcal U)_2
 -2\nu\|A\mathcal U\|_2^2\right].
 \tag{BCR.23}
\]

Define the observed current and its joined refill by

\[
 \mathfrak g_\tau:=\Omega_\tau\mathfrak c_\tau,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak f_\tau
 :=\Omega_\tau\mathfrak a_\tau
 -{1\over\delta}(\partial_\theta\Omega_\tau)\mathfrak c_\tau.
 \tag{BCR.24}
\]

Then

\[
\boxed{
 -{1\over\delta}\partial_\theta\mathfrak g_\tau
 +d_\tau\mathfrak g_\tau
 =\mathfrak f_\tau.}
 \tag{BCR.25}
\]

Writing \(g_\tau=\omega_\tau c_\tau\) and
\(f_\tau=\omega_\tau a_\tau+\omega_\tau'c_\tau\) in physical time, every
coefficient is explicit:

\[
\boxed{
 f_\tau^{(n)}
 =\sum_{j=0}^n\binom nj
 \left(
 \omega_\tau^{(j)}a_\tau^{(n-j)}
 +\omega_\tau^{(j+1)}c_\tau^{(n-j)}
 \right).}
 \tag{BCR.26}
\]

Thus higher selector derivatives are not omitted or declared small.  They are
the binomial tower of the same signed enstrophy-threshold crossing.

Multiplying `(BCR.25)` by the three-leg heat factor gives the exact continuous
core defect

\[
\boxed{
 \mathfrak g_\tau(0)
 -e^{-\delta d_\tau\theta}\mathfrak g_\tau(\theta)
 =\delta\int_0^\theta e^{-\delta d_\tau r}
 \mathfrak f_\tau(r)\,dr.}
 \tag{BCR.27}
\]

The left side is the actual terminal current minus the heat-descended earlier
current of the same triad.  The right side is its complete incident refill
plus its moving-core entry/exit contribution.  Equation `(BCR.27)` is the
triad-current counterpart of `(BCR.14)` and preserves the *difference*

## 3. Every inserted parent keeps its own heat rate

The all-orders content of `(BCR.20)` can be made explicit without pretending
that the heat and insertion operations commute.  Work first on a finite
true-Leray Fourier prefix.  For a Fourier monomial observable \(M_\gamma\)
whose current leaf list is \((k_1,\ldots,k_m)\), define

\[
 \mathscr I M_\gamma(u):=DM_\gamma(u)[Q(u)],
 \qquad
 \mathscr H M_\gamma(u):=\nu DM_\gamma(u)[Au].
 \tag{BCR.28}
\]

The pressure-complete source \(Q\) in \(\mathscr I\) replaces each physical
leaf, one at a time, by every incident Leray parent pair.  Hence

\[
 \mathscr I M_\gamma
 =\sum_{\gamma'\succ\gamma}I_{\gamma\gamma'}M_{\gamma'},
 \qquad
 \mathscr H M_\gamma=d_\gamma M_\gamma,
 \qquad
 d_\gamma:=\nu\sum_{j=1}^m|k_j|^2.
 \tag{BCR.29}
\]

Along the one Navier--Stokes history,

\[
\boxed{
 {d\over dt}M_\gamma+d_\gamma M_\gamma
 =\sum_{\gamma'\succ\gamma}I_{\gamma\gamma'}M_{\gamma'}.}
 \tag{BCR.30}
\]

Every child \(\gamma'\) has its own rate \(d_{\gamma'}\).  Consequently

\[
 \partial_t^nM_\gamma(u(t))
 =(\mathscr I-\mathscr H)^nM_\gamma(u(t)),
 \tag{BCR.31}
\]

where the exact expansion is the ordered-word sum

\[
\boxed{
 (\mathscr I-\mathscr H)^n
 =\sum_{\varepsilon_1,\ldots,\varepsilon_n
 \in\{\mathscr I,\mathscr H\}}
 (-1)^{\#\{j:\varepsilon_j=\mathscr H\}}
 \varepsilon_n\cdots\varepsilon_1.}
 \tag{BCR.32}
\]

There is no scalar binomial collapse of `(BCR.32)`: applying
\(\mathscr H\) before and after an insertion reads different leaf lists and
therefore different heat rates.  The exact exponential generating form is

\[
\boxed{
 M_\gamma(\mathcal U_{T,\delta}(\theta))
 =\sum_{n\geq0}{(-\delta\theta)^n\over n!}
 (\mathscr I-\mathscr H)^nM_\gamma(u(T)).}
 \tag{BCR.33}
\]

Equations `(BCR.30)`--`(BCR.33)` are the complete Carleman insertion tree of
the original binomial recurrence, not a new model.

For completeness, its finite-depth causal expansion can be written without
an infinite series.  Let \(t_0<t\).  Iterating `(BCR.30)` \(N\) times gives
endpoint terms over every insertion path
\(\gamma_0\to\gamma_1\to\cdots\to\gamma_r\), \(0\leq r<N\):

\[
\begin{aligned}
M_{\gamma_0}(t)
={}&\sum_{r=0}^{N-1}
\sum_{\gamma_0\to\cdots\to\gamma_r}
\int_{t_0<s_r<\cdots<s_1<t}
 e^{-d_{\gamma_0}(t-s_1)}I_{01}
 e^{-d_{\gamma_1}(s_1-s_2)}I_{12}\cdots\\
&\qquad\qquad\cdots I_{r-1,r}
 e^{-d_{\gamma_r}(s_r-t_0)}M_{\gamma_r}(t_0)
 \,ds_r\cdots ds_1
 +\mathcal R_N(t;t_0).
\end{aligned}
\tag{BCR.34}
\]

For \(r=0\), the displayed integral means
\(e^{-d_{\gamma_0}(t-t_0)}M_{\gamma_0}(t_0)\).  The exact remainder is

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathcal R_N(t;t_0)
={}&\sum_{\gamma_0\to\cdots\to\gamma_N}
\int_{t_0<s_N<\cdots<s_1<t}
 e^{-d_{\gamma_0}(t-s_1)}I_{01}\cdots\\
&\qquad\qquad\cdots
 e^{-d_{\gamma_{N-1}}(s_{N-1}-s_N)}I_{N-1,N}
 M_{\gamma_N}(s_N)\,ds_N\cdots ds_1.
\end{aligned}
\tag{BCR.35}
\]

No heat rate or incident child is missing in `(BCR.34)`--`(BCR.35)`.  If
\(N\to\infty\) is justified and \(\mathcal R_N\to0\), the sum is simply the
exact observable \(M_{\gamma_0}(u(t))\).  Thus convergence reconstructs the
same nonlinear history; it is not yet an estimate of that history.

## 4. The reciprocal depths sum to the critical source multiplier

The reciprocal parent-age hierarchy `(PRM.3)`--`(PRM.8)` is the resolvent
coordinate of the first edge in `(BCR.34)`.  At output \(k\), let

\[
 \gamma_k:=\nu|k|^2,
 \qquad
 \delta_a:=\nu(|p|^2+|q|^2),
 \qquad k=p+q.
 \tag{BCR.36}
\]

Then the exact three-leg heat denominator is

\[
 d_{k,a}=\gamma_k+\delta_a
 =\nu(|k|^2+|p|^2+|q|^2),
 \tag{BCR.37}
\]

which is `(DCT.1)`.  Define

\[
 f_a:={1\over\gamma_k+\delta_a},
 \qquad
 r_a:={\delta_a\over\gamma_k+\delta_a},
 \qquad
 w_m(\delta_a):=f_ar_a^m.
 \tag{BCR.38}
\]

For every finite depth \(N\),

\[
\boxed{
 \sum_{m=0}^{N-1}w_m(\delta_a)
 ={1-r_a^N\over\gamma_k},
 \qquad
 {1\over\gamma_k}-\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}w_m(\delta_a)
 ={r_a^N\over\gamma_k}.}
 \tag{BCR.39}
\]

The first DCT multiplier is \(|k|f_a\).  Joining every reciprocal depth gives

\[
\boxed{
 |k|\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}w_m(\delta_a)
 ={|k|\over\gamma_k}
 ={1\over\nu|k|}.}
 \tag{BCR.40}
\]

This is exactly the raw critical source-square multiplier, not a smaller one.
The equality is invariant under a common Navier--Stokes dilation.  No finite
or infinite reciprocal depth creates a subcritical factor.

The moving selector passes through the same exact sum.  Parentwise,

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \sum_{m=0}^{N-1}\omega_a w_m(\delta_a)
 &=\omega_a{1-r_a^N\over\gamma_k},\\
 \sum_{m=0}^{N-1}\dot\omega_a w_m(\delta_a)
 &=\dot\omega_a{1-r_a^N\over\gamma_k}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{BCR.41}
\]

Hence all depths preserve both the selected core--exterior source port and
the enstrophy-threshold crossing.  When \(\omega_a\equiv1\), recombination
returns the positive complete source square.  When \(\omega_a\) depends on
the parent triad, the first factor remains the complete output source
\(Q_k=\int\Theta_{k,a}\,da\), while the second is selected; the indefinite
cross port `(DCT.30)`--`(DCT.31)` remains.

The parent-pair storage reaches the same boundary.  With

\[
 n_m(x,y):={\gamma_k c\over2}
 {w_m(x)+w_m(y)\over x+y},
 \qquad
 a_N(x):=1-\left({x\over\gamma_k+x}\right)^N,
 \tag{BCR.42}
\]

the exact finite joined kernel is

\[
\boxed{
 \sum_{m=0}^{N-1}n_m(x,y)
 ={c\over2}{a_N(x)+a_N(y)\over x+y}\succeq0.}
 \tag{BCR.43}
\]

The individual kernels at \(m\geq2\) remain indefinite by `(PRM.26)`--
`(PRM.32)`; positivity belongs to the joined prefix, not to each later
response.  In the all-depth limit,

\[
\boxed{
 \sum_{m\geq0}n_m(x,y)={c\over x+y},
 \qquad
 C_\infty
 =c\int_0^\infty
 \left\|\int e^{-sx}\Theta_x\,dx\right\|^2ds\geq0.}
 \tag{BCR.44}
\]

If

\[
 G_s:=\int e^{-sx}\Theta_x\,dx,
 \qquad
 \mathcal N_s:=\int e^{-sx}N_x\,dx,
 \tag{BCR.45}
\]

then the exact derivative is

\[
\boxed{
 \dot C_\infty
 =-c\|G_0\|^2
 +2c\operatorname{Re}\int_0^\infty
 \langle\mathcal N_s,G_s\rangle\,ds.}
 \tag{BCR.46}
\]

Thus the positive all-depth storage returns the coherently collapsed source
square and the complete next binomial insertion.  In particular,

\[
 \sum_{m\geq0}\mathcal N_m
 ={1\over\gamma_k}\int N_a\,da;
 \tag{BCR.47}
\]

the insertion is redistributed, not changed into an endpoint.

## 5. Exact convergence and uniformity boundary

There are three distinct limiting operations.  None may be inferred from the
existence of every finite row.

### 5.1 Temporal exponential generating radius

At a fixed preterminal time, `(BCR.5)`, `(BCR.15)`, `(BCR.20)`, and
`(BCR.33)` hold coefficientwise.  Evaluating them at nonzero age requires a
common time-analytic radius in a product-stable spatial class.  The
coordinatewise all-orders intersection says that every coefficient exists; it
does not give

\[
 \inf_{t\uparrow T_*}\rho_m(t)>0
 \tag{BCR.48}
\]

for a time-Taylor radius \(\rho_m(t)\), and `(BCR.48)` cannot be imported while
the intersection is still being derived.  Even when the series converges at
one time, `(BCR.15)` is an exact actual-minus-heat identity; convergence does
not bound its signed defect.

### 5.2 Reciprocal normal-form convergence is stronger than analyticity

For any scalar component of the observed core law

\[
 g'+dg=f,
 \tag{BCR.49}
\]

finite iteration gives, for every \(N\geq1\),

\[
\boxed{
 g(t)=\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}{(-1)^j\over d^{j+1}}f^{(j)}(t)
 +{(-1)^N\over d^N}g^{(N)}(t).}
 \tag{BCR.50}
\]

Therefore the infinite alternating reciprocal normal form is legitimate only
if its series converges and the last term in `(BCR.50)` tends to zero.  A
sufficient local condition is a derivative bound

\[
 |f^{(j)}(t)|\leq M\rho^j\quad\hbox{with }\rho<d,
 \qquad
 d^{-N}|g^{(N)}(t)|\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{BCR.51}
\]

Ordinary analyticity gives instead a Cauchy bound of the form
\(M j!R^{-j}\), which does not imply `(BCR.51)`.  The analytic function
\(f(t)=(1-t/R)^{-1}\) has \(f^{(j)}(0)=j!R^{-j}\), so the terms of the
reciprocal series fail even to tend to zero for every finite \(dR\).  The
homogeneous heat component \(g(t)=e^{-dt}\), \(f=0\), has

\[
 {(-1)^N\over d^N}g^{(N)}(t)=g(t)
 \tag{BCR.52}
\]

at every depth.  It remains the earlier-state heat endpoint; it cannot be
discarded by taking \(N\to\infty\).

Whenever `(BCR.50)` does converge with its remainder vanishing, it is only the
particular resolvent identity

\[
 g=(d+\partial_t)^{-1}f,
 \tag{BCR.53}
\]

with the homogeneous endpoint fixed separately.  Its causal finite-history
form is `(BCR.27)`.  Thus the convergent alternating series reconstructs the
same refill integral and endpoint.

### 5.3 Parent-rate convergence has a sharp unsuppressed tail

For each fixed parent, \(0<r_a<1\) and the remainder in `(BCR.39)` tends to
zero.  The convergence is not uniform over the complete parent field:

\[
 \sup_{\delta_a>0}r_a^N=1.
 \tag{BCR.54}
\]

More sharply, if \(\delta_a\geq N\gamma_k\), then

\[
\boxed{
 r_a^N
 \geq\left({N\over N+1}\right)^N
 \geq e^{-1}.}
 \tag{BCR.55}
\]

Thus depth \(N\) leaves essentially the whole parent field at rates
\(\delta_a\gtrsim N\gamma_k\).  The exact unsummed source residual is

\[
 \mathcal T_{N,k}(t)
 :=\int r_{k,a}^N\Theta_{k,a}(t)\,da,
 \tag{BCR.56}
\]

with \(\omega_{k,a}r_{k,a}^N\Theta_{k,a}\) and
\(\dot\omega_{k,a}r_{k,a}^N\Theta_{k,a}\) on the moving core.  A uniform
passage through the full parent continuum therefore requires a critical
parent-tail domination strong enough to make `(BCR.56)` vanish after output,
time, and selector recombination.  The first-moment version is exactly the
condition already exposed in `(PRM.32j)`--`(PRM.32m)`.

On a scale-local sector the ratio \(\delta_a/\gamma_k\) is bounded, so
`(BCR.56)` decays geometrically.  But then the limit is exactly `(BCR.40)`,
the critical source multiplier.  Hence the reciprocal tower has an exhaustive
dichotomy:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{array}{ll}
\text{unbounded parent-rate ratio:}
 &\text{the uniform tail `(BCR.56)` is unpaid};\\[1mm]
\text{uniformly local ratio:}
 &\text{the sum converges to }(\nu|k|)^{-1}
   \text{ and returns the open source action}.
\end{array}}
\tag{BCR.57}
\]

The moving \(Z\)-selector does not create a third branch: `(BCR.41)` shows
that it retains the same tail and, in the limit, retains the signed crossing
flux.

## 6. The all-depth limit is exactly the existing open action

Restore the full output and parent recombination.  With no selector, the
limit `(BCR.40)` is

\[
 {1\over\nu}\|\Lambda^{-1/2}Q(u)\|_2^2.
 \tag{BCR.58}
\]

With the dynamic core selector, it is the signed complete-source/selected-
parent port of `(DCT.29)`--`(DCT.32)`, including every exterior cross term.
Equation `(BCR.47)` retains the full pressure-complete next insertion, and
`(BCR.41)` retains the entry/exit term.  The complete unselected weighted law
`(PRM.46b)`, at \(W=\nu^{-1}\Lambda^{-1}\), says exactly that the time
integral of `(BCR.58)` equals a datum heat boundary plus the signed triangular
binomial insertion.  It is a no-recount identity, not a bound for either
side.

Consequently, after the already proved exterior/FIM absorptions are restored,
the all-depth dynamic-core normal form lands on

\[
\boxed{
 \sup_{T<T_*}\left[
 \sum_{\tau,j}
 \bigl(b_\tau(\alpha_j)-b_\tau(\beta_j)\bigr)
 +\int_0^T\mathfrak R_{\rm core}(t)\,dt
 \right],}
 \tag{BCR.59}
\]

which is exactly `(DCT.47)`.  The first term is the sharp moving-selector
entry/exit telescope.  The second is the critically weighted complete
source/insertion action.  The complete pressure-explicit Carleman tree has
neither deleted nor bounded either term.

The verdict is therefore precise:

1. `(BCR.5)`--`(BCR.15)` prove the continuous actual-minus-heat response
   directly from Thomas's original binomial recurrence.
2. `(BCR.20)`--`(BCR.35)` retain every incident pressure-complete parent,
   every successive heat rate, and every moving-selector derivative.
3. Every finite reciprocal depth is exact.  Its only new residual is the
   explicit parent tail `(BCR.56)`.
4. If that tail is not controlled uniformly, the all-depth passage is
   unavailable.  If it is controlled, the limit is `(BCR.58)`--`(BCR.59)`,
   the already open critical action.
5. Therefore this resummation does not prove `(TIR.37a)` and does not close
   the MPP.  A closing continuation inside Thomas's method must prove a
   datum-finite signed cancellation for `(BCR.59)` along the one actual fluid
   history; convergence or positivity of the resummed kernel alone cannot be
   counted as that payment.
