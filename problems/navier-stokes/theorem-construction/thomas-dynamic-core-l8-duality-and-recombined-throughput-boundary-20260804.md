# Thomas's dynamic-core (L_t^8) duality and recombined-throughput boundary

Status: proved a sharp norm-exponent boundary and an exact modal/first-row
obstruction inside Thomas Birnie's one-fluid, pressure-explicit binomial/VPI
reversible-intersection method.  The datum-paid coefficient in `(FIM.51)` is
exactly an eighth root of the kinetic-energy enstrophy clock.  After all
triads incident to a mode have been recombined, its only legal direct Hölder
use leaves an (L_t^{8/7}) norm of the normalized **signed** core current.
The energy row and the pressure-complete
(L_t^1\dot H_x^{-1/2}) source charge do not pay that norm.  An
incidence-native donor magnitude is worse: on the scale-local core its sharp
(L_t^{8/7}) density is the closure-equivalent enstrophy time-square.

This note does not prove `(TIR.37a)` and does not close the MPP.  It rules out
turning \(\Xi\in L_t^8\) into a prefix payment by an unproved throughput norm,
by modal energy or helicity bookkeeping, or by differentiating the moving
core while omitting its enstrophy-threshold crossing.  It also states the
smallest norm-form theorem that would make the paid eighth power useful.

The one object throughout is the smooth fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes field
generated by one smooth finite-energy datum \(u_0\).  The dynamic core,
helical modes, triads, and temporal rows are simultaneous readouts of that
field.  No triad is assigned an independently owned positive input, and no
modal sideband is treated as lost when it can re-enter another incidence.

## 1. The paid eighth power is exactly the enstrophy clock

Put

\[
 U:=\|u_0\|_2>0,
 \qquad
 Z(t):=\|\Lambda u(t)\|_2^2,
 \qquad
 D(t):=\|\Lambda^{3/2}u(t)\|_2^2.
 \tag{DCL.1}
\]

The zero-datum case is the zero solution.  On the nonzero branch, `(FIM.51)`
defines, up to the fixed Littlewood--Paley constants already absorbed there,

\[
 \Xi(t):={U^{1/4}Z(t)^{1/8}\over\nu^{1/2}}.
 \tag{DCL.2}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \boxed{\Xi(t)^8={U^2\over\nu^4}Z(t).}
 \tag{DCL.3}
\]

The kinetic-energy identity gives

\[
 \int_0^{T_*}Z(t)\,dt\le {U^2\over2\nu},
 \qquad
 \boxed{
 \int_0^{T_*}\Xi(t)^8\,dt
 \le {U^4\over2\nu^5}.}
 \tag{DCL.4}
\]

Thus `(DCL.4)` is a useful geometric reformulation of a paid clock, but it is
not an independent time regularity statement.  In particular it supplies no
pointwise bound, no bounded variation, and no higher time power of \(\Xi\).

Let \(\mathcal P_c:=\mathcal P_{1/2}^{\rm core}\) denote the fully signed,
pressure-complete current on `(FIM.49)`.  All six assignments of the three
legs and every incident triad at a mode are recombined before
\(\mathcal P_c\) is read.

## 2. The instantaneous energy/source estimate lands on the time-square

For maximum shell \(h\simeq\lambda_j\), `(FIM.44)` gives the complete
pressure-coupled estimate

\[
 |\mathcal P_c(t)|
 \le C Z(t)^{1/2}
 \sum_{j\in\mathcal J_c(t)}
 \lambda_j^{5/2}
 \bigl(a_j^2+a_j\widetilde a_j\bigr),
 \tag{DCL.5}
\]

where \(a_j=\|\Delta_j u\|_2\), finite overlap is understood, and
\(\mathcal J_c(t)\) contains only shells on which the dynamic core is
nonempty.  Define, with the same fixed cutoff collars,

\[
 Z_c:=\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_c}\lambda_j^2
       (a_j^2+\widetilde a_j^2),
 \qquad
 D_c:=\sum_{j\in\mathcal J_c}\lambda_j^3
       (a_j^2+\widetilde a_j^2).
 \tag{DCL.5a}
\]

Cauchy--Schwarz and finite overlap give

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_j\lambda_j^{5/2}a_j^2
 &\le
 \left(\sum_j\lambda_j^2a_j^2\right)^{1/2}
 \left(\sum_j\lambda_j^3a_j^2\right)^{1/2},\\
 \sum_j\lambda_j^{5/2}a_j\widetilde a_j
 &\le C
 \left(\sum_j\lambda_j^2a_j^2\right)^{1/2}
 \left(\sum_j\lambda_j^3a_j^2\right)^{1/2}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{DCL.6}
\]

Hence

\[
 \boxed{
 |\mathcal P_c(t)|
 \le C Z(t)^{1/2}Z_c(t)^{1/2}D_c(t)^{1/2}
 \le C Z(t)D_c(t)^{1/2}
 \le C Z(t)D(t)^{1/2}.}
 \tag{DCL.7}
\]

This is the same exponent obtained from the pressure-complete source charge

\[
 \|Q(u)\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}\le C Z,
 \qquad
 |(Q,\Lambda u)_2|
 \le \|Q\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}D^{1/2}.
 \tag{DCL.8}
\]

Equation `(DCL.7)` is derived for the core from the fully recombined dyadic
current; it does not pretend that a triad-selected source has the global norm
in `(DCL.8)`.

The upper roof in `(FIM.49)` says every shell in \(\mathcal J_c(t)\) obeys

\[
 \lambda_j\le C{Z(t)\over\nu^2}.
 \tag{DCL.9}
\]

Consequently its restricted viscous density satisfies

\[
 D_c(t)
 \le C{Z(t)\over\nu^2}
       \sum_{j\in\mathcal J_c(t)}\lambda_j^2
       (a_j^2+\widetilde a_j^2)
 \le C{Z(t)^2\over\nu^2},
 \tag{DCL.10}
\]

and the magnitude estimate reduces to

\[
 \boxed{|\mathcal P_c(t)|\le {C\over\nu}Z(t)^2.}
 \tag{DCL.11}
\]

The datum pays \(Z\in L_t^1\), not \(Z\in L_t^2\).  Moreover the finite
classical-horizon equivalence `(RPT.25)` is

\[
 \sup_{T<T_*}\int_0^TZ^2\,dt<\infty
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \sup_{t<T_*}E_{1/2}(t)
 +\nu\int_0^{T_*}D(t)\,dt<\infty.
 \tag{DCL.12}
\]

The right side is precisely the stock/action consequence of `(TIR.37a)`.
Thus viscosity does remove the exterior of `(FIM.49)`, but a magnitude bound
on its remaining core lands on the already known closure-equivalent
time-square.  In terms of `(DCL.3)`, `(DCL.11)` asks for
\(\Xi^{16}\in L_t^1\), while the datum supplies only
\(\Xi^8\in L_t^1\).

## 3. The only legal direct (L^8) factorization after recombination

On \(\{Z>0\}\), define the normalized **signed** core current

\[
 \mathcal J_c(t):={\mathcal P_c(t)\over\Xi(t)}.
 \tag{DCL.13}
\]

Set \(\mathcal J_c=0\) on \(\{Z=0\}\); there the finite-energy
divergence-free field and its nonlinear current vanish.  This definition is
made only after complete modal incidence recombination; it is not a donor colouring.
In particular, it does not take an absolute value of an input leg.

Since \(\Xi\ge0\), for every \(T<T_*\),

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \int_0^T\mathcal P_c(t)\,dt
 &=\int_0^T\Xi(t)\mathcal J_c(t)\,dt\\
 &\le \|\Xi\|_{L^8(0,T_*)}
       \|[\mathcal J_c]_+\|_{L^{8/7}(0,T_*)}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{DCL.14}
\]

The positive part in `(DCL.14)` is taken only after the entire
pressure-complete current has been recombined.  It is therefore a legal
one-sided norm estimate, although it deliberately discards the signed time
cancellation which Thomas's method ultimately seeks.

The exponent \(8/7\) is sharp for an argument which knows only
\(\Xi\in L^8\):

\[
 {1\over8}+{1\over{8/7}}=1.
 \tag{DCL.15}
\]

For every \(1\le q<8/7\), one can choose nonnegative scalar functions
\(f\in L^8(0,1)\) and \(g\in L^q(0,1)\) with \(fg\notin L^1(0,1)\).
This is only the sharp Hölder-duality countertest; it makes no claim that an
arbitrary pair \((f,g)\) is a Navier--Stokes history.

Accordingly the smallest direct norm-form theorem exposed by the paid
eighth power is

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_0^{T_*}
 \left[{\mathcal P_{1/2}^{\rm core}(t)\over\Xi(t)}\right]_+^{8/7}
 dt
 \le C(u_0,\nu,T_*)<\infty.}
 \tag{DCL.16}
\]

Equation `(DCL.16)` would imply the core prefix bound by `(DCL.4)` and
`(DCL.14)`, and then `(TIR.37a)` after the already proved FIM absorptions are
restored.  It is not proved here.

## 4. Exact failure of the energy and source charges at the dual exponent

Dividing `(DCL.7)` by `(DCL.2)` and raising to the dual power gives

\[
 \boxed{
 [\mathcal J_c(t)]_+^{8/7}
 \le C\nu^{4/7}U^{-2/7}
 Z(t)D(t)^{4/7}.}
 \tag{DCL.17}
\]

Every exponent in `(DCL.17)` is forced:

\[
 \left(ZD^{1/2}\right)^{8/7}\Xi^{-8/7}
 =\nu^{4/7}U^{-2/7}ZD^{4/7}.
 \tag{DCL.18}
\]

Neither the energy charge \(\int Z\) nor the source charge
\(\int\|Q\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}\) controls the mixed moment

\[
 \int_0^{T_*}ZD^{4/7}\,dt.
 \tag{DCL.19}
\]

Using only the core roof `(DCL.10)` makes the sufficient remainder worse:

\[
 [\mathcal J_c]_+^{8/7}
 \le C\nu^{-4/7}U^{-2/7}Z^{15/7},
 \tag{DCL.20}
\]

and \(Z^{15/7}\) is not paid by \(Z\in L^1\).

The exponent failure is already sharp on a scale-local, pressure-complete
packet.  Choose one real divergence-free Schwartz field \(v\) whose Fourier
support lies in fixed small neighborhoods of a nondegenerate heterochiral
triad and its conjugate, and choose its phases so that
\(\mathcal P_{1/2}(v)>0\).  Such a field is obtained by thickening a
nonzero true-Leray helical triad interaction.  For \(A>0\), \(K\gg1\), put

\[
 u_{A,K}(x):=AK^{3/2}v(Kx).
 \tag{DCL.21}
\]

This is an exact smooth divergence-free field, not a shell model.  Leray
projection and the pressure completion commute with the dilation.  Direct
scaling gives

\[
 \begin{array}{c|c}
 \text{quantity}&\text{scale in }(A,K)\\ \hline
 \|u_{A,K}\|_2&A\\
 Z(u_{A,K})&A^2K^2\\
 D(u_{A,K})&A^2K^3\\
 \|Q(u_{A,K})\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}&A^2K^2\\
 \mathcal P_{1/2}(u_{A,K})&A^3K^{7/2}\\
 \Xi(u_{A,K})&\nu^{-1/2}A^{1/2}K^{1/4}\\
 \mathcal J_c(u_{A,K})&\nu^{1/2}A^{5/2}K^{13/4}.
 \end{array}
 \tag{DCL.22}
\]

The omitted factors in `(DCL.22)` are fixed positive constants determined by
\(v\).  All interacting frequency ratios are fixed.  For sufficiently large
\(K\), the static lower cutoffs grow only like \(K^{4/5}\), the dynamic
lower cutoff grows like \(K^{2/3}\), and the upper roof is of order
\(K^2\).  Hence the whole nonzero current in `(DCL.22)` lies strictly inside
the dynamic core.

At fixed \(A\), `(DCL.22)` yields

\[
 \boxed{
 [\mathcal J_c(u_{A,K})]_+^{8/7}
 \asymp C(A,\nu,v)\,Z(u_{A,K})^{13/7}.}
 \tag{DCL.23}
\]

Thus no estimate based only on the paid \(L_t^1\) mass of \(Z\), the
pressure-complete \(L_t^1\dot H_x^{-1/2}\) source charge, and instantaneous
dynamic-core geometry can prove `(DCL.16)`.  For example, on disjoint time
sets assign packet scales with \(Z_n\to\infty\) and lengths
\(|I_n|=Z_n^{-3/2}\).  Then

\[
 \sum_n|I_n|Z_n<\infty,
 \qquad
 \sum_n|I_n|Z_n^{13/7}=\infty.
 \tag{DCL.24}
\]

Equation `(DCL.24)` is an instantaneous norm-insufficiency schedule assembled
from lawful true-Leray snapshots.  It is not one Navier--Stokes history
and is not a counterexample to `(TIR.37a)`.  Its exact conclusion is that the
first binomial same-history law must contribute a new time correlation; the
paid scalar norms cannot do so by themselves.

## 5. An incidence-native donor magnitude is already time-square critical

To audit the word *throughput* without colouring inputs, work first in a
finite pressure-complete Galerkin truncation.  Let
\(T_{\sigma,k}^{c}\) be the net kinetic-energy source at helical mode
\((\sigma,k)\) after **all** dynamically admitted incident triads have been
summed at that mode.  Define its recombined critical variation

\[
 \mathfrak T_c^{\rm rec}(t)
 :=\sum_{\sigma,k}|k|\,|T_{\sigma,k}^{c}(t)|.
 \tag{DCL.25}
\]

For a Schwartz field the corresponding Fourier integral is finite and is
used in place of the sum.

The absolute value in `(DCL.25)` is taken only after reconvergence at the
mode.  It is not a positive donor assignment to individual triads.  It gives

\[
 |\mathcal P_c(t)|
 =\left|\sum_{\sigma,k}|k|T_{\sigma,k}^{c}(t)\right|
 \le\mathfrak T_c^{\rm rec}(t).
 \tag{DCL.26}
\]

The Hilbert-space source charge `(DCL.8)` does not uniformly control the
Fourier total variation `(DCL.25)`; modal multiplicity and cancelling
reconvergence are deliberately absent from that charge.  More decisively,
the local packet `(DCL.21)` has only a fixed interaction geometry and obeys

\[
 \mathfrak T_c^{\rm rec}(u_{A,K})
 \gtrsim \mathcal P_c(u_{A,K})
 \asymp A^3K^{7/2}.
 \tag{DCL.27}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 \bigl(\mathfrak T_c^{\rm rec}(u_{A,K})\bigr)^{8/7}
 \gtrsim C(A,v)\,Z(u_{A,K})^2.}
 \tag{DCL.28}
\]

This is the sharp exponent failure for an actual recombined donor magnitude.
On the scale-local sector the geometric ratio \(h/a\) is order one, so the
upper bound \(h/a\le\Xi\) supplies no small factor.  Asking
\(\mathfrak T_c^{\rm rec}\in L_t^{8/7}\) from the energy row therefore asks,
already on this simplest lawful packet family, for the time-square in
`(DCL.12)`.  Defining a per-triad positive donor ledger can only increase the
throughput and is forbidden in any event by full incidence/reconvergence.

## 6. Exact modal moments do not control the recombined variation

For a complete heterochiral triad with ordered radii \(a\le b\le c\), let
\(C_\tau\) be its signed critical current and \(S_\tau\) its signed
enstrophy current.  If the unique-helicity leg has radius \(a\), \(b\), or
\(c\), respectively, energy and signed-helicity conservation give the exact
relations

\[
 \boxed{
 S_\tau=\kappa_\tau C_\tau,\qquad
 \begin{aligned}
 \kappa_a&={(a+b)(a+c)\over2a},\\
 \kappa_b&={(a+b)(b+c)\over2b},\\
 \kappa_c&={(a+c)(b+c)\over2c}.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{DCL.29}
\]

Each \(\kappa_\tau\) is positive, so one isolated triad has the same sign in
its critical and enstrophy currents.  That fact does not survive as a
one-sided comparison after all triads are recombined, because
\(\kappa_\tau\) varies with the full three-leg geometry.  Two complete
triad blocks with \(\kappa_2>\kappa_1\), currents

\[
 C_1=L,
 \qquad
 C_2=-{\kappa_1\over\kappa_2}L
 \tag{DCL.30}
\]

have

\[
 S_1+S_2=0,
 \qquad
 C_1+C_2=L\left(1-{\kappa_1\over\kappa_2}\right)>0.
 \tag{DCL.31}
\]

The blocks can be chosen as two nonresonant real true-Leray helical triads;
their phases and amplitudes set the two signed transfer scalars, while cross
outputs initially have zero modal energy.  This is a lawful instantaneous
Fourier realization of `(DCL.31)`, not an invariant finite-mode model or a
continuing singular history.

For the actual solution the complete modal rows are

\[
 e_{\sigma,k}'+2\nu|k|^2e_{\sigma,k}
 =T_{\sigma,k}^{c}+T_{\sigma,k}^{\rm ext}.
 \tag{DCL.32}
\]

Summing `(DCL.32)` with weights \(1\) and \(\sigma|k|\) gives the exact
kinetic-energy and signed-helicity rows.  They control signed net moments,
not the total variation `(DCL.25)`.  Summing with \(|k|\) and integrating
gives the critical endpoint plus viscous action, with the already paid
exterior current restored.  That is `(TIR.42)` and hence the original prefix
interface.  Taking absolute values in `(DCL.32)` instead requires the total
variation of every modal energy and exterior incidence; neither endpoints nor
viscosity bound repeated transfer and reconvergence.

Thus energy, helicity, the enstrophy row, and viscosity do not supply the
missing recombined throughput norm.

## 7. What the first binomial row adds, including the moving boundary

The estimates in Sections 2--5 are instantaneous.  They use the dynamic core
at each time but do **not** differentiate its selector, so no crossing term is
missing from those estimates.

A time-oriented integration by parts is different.  Let
\(\mathfrak C_Z(u)\) be a smooth fully symmetrized three-leg functional whose
selector is a smoothed version of `(FIM.49)`, so that

\[
 \mathcal P_c(t)=\mathfrak C_{Z(t)}(u(t)).
 \tag{DCL.33}
\]

With \(Q=-\mathbb P((u\cdot\nabla)u)\), the original equation and the
enstrophy row are

\[
 u_t=Q-\nu\Lambda^2u,
 \qquad
 Z'=2\mathcal P_1-2\nu\|\Lambda^2u\|_2^2.
 \tag{DCL.34}
\]

The exact derivative is therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 {d\over dt}\mathfrak C_{Z(t)}(u(t))
 =D_u\mathfrak C_Z(u)[Q-\nu\Lambda^2u]
 +2\bigl(\mathcal P_1-\nu\|\Lambda^2u\|_2^2\bigr)
   \partial_Z\mathfrak C_Z(u).}
 \tag{DCL.35}
\]

The first term contains all three temporal insertions of the cubic current.
Equivalently it is obtained from the pressure-complete first source row

\[
 Q_t+\nu\Lambda^2Q
 =DQ(u)[Q]+2\nu\sum_{j=1}^3
   \mathcal B(\partial_ju,\partial_ju).
 \tag{DCL.36}
\]

The second term in `(DCL.35)` is the signed entry/exit flux of the
\(Z\)-dependent core.  For a sharp selector it becomes a Stieltjes crossing
measure on the thresholds `(DCT.19)`--`(DCT.28)`.  It may not be dropped or
assigned a sign.

The same issue appears if `(DCL.13)` is differentiated.  On \(\{Z>0\}\),

\[
 {\Xi'\over\Xi}
 ={1\over8}{Z'\over Z}
 ={\mathcal P_1-\nu\|\Lambda^2u\|_2^2\over4Z},
 \tag{DCL.37}
\]

and hence

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal J_c'
 ={}&\Xi^{-1}D_u\mathfrak C_Z(u)[Q-\nu\Lambda^2u]\\
 &+2\Xi^{-1}
   \bigl(\mathcal P_1-\nu\|\Lambda^2u\|_2^2\bigr)
   \partial_Z\mathfrak C_Z(u)\\
 &-{\mathcal P_1-\nu\|\Lambda^2u\|_2^2\over4Z}
   \mathcal J_c.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{DCL.38}
\]

Thus the first temporal row does orient the remaining current, but it does
not make `(DCL.16)` datum-finite: it contributes the complete quartic
source/insertion port and imports the signed first spatial current through
the moving selector and the normalization.  The more resolved triadwise
identity is `(DCT.14)`--`(DCT.47)`.  That identity keeps every incident
source and proves that the scale-local heat-normalized refill remains at the
critical \((\nu|k|)^{-1}\) weight.

For example, integrating `(DCL.35)` once gives the exact signed identity

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \int_0^T\mathcal P_c(t)\,dt
 ={}&T\mathcal P_c(0)\\
 &+\int_0^T(T-t)
 \left[
 D_u\mathfrak C_Z(u)[Q-\nu\Lambda^2u]
 +2(\mathcal P_1-\nu\|\Lambda^2u\|_2^2)
   \partial_Z\mathfrak C_Z(u)
 \right]dt.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{DCL.39}
\]

The bracket in `(DCL.39)` has no proved datum-finite signed primitive.  A
bound for it is exactly the core prefix in first-binomial coordinates, not a
second payment.

## 8. Exact verdict and the remaining theorem

The datum-paid statement \(\Xi\in L_t^8\) cannot close the dynamic-core
prefix through any currently paid norm:

1. direct viscosity/source magnitude control lands on \(\int Z^2\), which is
   closure-equivalent;
2. the legal post-recombination Hölder factorization requires the sharp
   normalized-current norm `(DCL.16)`, while energy leaves the unpaid mixed
   moment `(DCL.19)` and scale-local packets force the exponent
   \(Z^{13/7}\);
3. an incidence-native recombined donor variation has the sharper failure
   `(DCL.28)`, namely the time-square itself;
4. exact modal energy, helicity, and enstrophy moments permit the cancellation
   `(DCL.31)` and do not control modal variation; and
5. using the first binomial row in time adds the complete pressure-coupled
   insertion and the signed selector-crossing term `(DCL.35)`, not a monotone
   charge.

Consequently the smallest **norm-form** continuation furnished by the paid
eighth power is `(DCL.16)`.  The only weaker lawful alternative is not another
magnitude estimate: it is a direct one-history signed cancellation in
`(DCL.39)` or the equivalent triadwise entry/exit-plus-refill identity
`(DCT.47)`.  Neither statement is proved here.  No intersection row, critical
stock/action bound, or endpoint regularity has been assumed in reaching this
boundary.
