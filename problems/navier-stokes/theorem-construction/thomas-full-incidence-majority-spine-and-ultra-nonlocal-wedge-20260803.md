# Thomas's full-incidence majority spine and ultra-nonlocal wedge

Status: proved exact recombined-incidence obstruction and a strict
datum-paid interaction-support reduction inside Thomas Birnie's one-fluid,
pressure-explicit binomial/VPI reversible-intersection method.

The sharpened heterochiral fraction law does not become a contraction when
all incident triads are recombined mode by mode.  Along a serial
same-helicity majority spine, helicity conservation makes each
unique-helicity deposit add to the critical strength of the continuing
carrier.  The exact recursion is

\[
 X_{n+1}=X_n+Y_n,
 \tag{FIM.1}
\]

not \(X_{n+1}\leq(1-\varepsilon)X_n\).  This remains true after every
intermediate modal gain is cancelled against that mode's next donor loss, so
it is not an input-colouring or duplicate-donor artifact.

There is nevertheless one new noncircular payment.  If \(h\) is the largest
frequency in a pressure-complete triad, every interaction whose smallest
frequency obeys

\[
 a\leq c_0
 \left({\nu\over\|u_0\|_2}\right)^{2/5}h^{4/5}
 \tag{FIM.2}
\]

is absorbable into a fixed fraction of the critical viscous row using the
kinetic-energy bound only.  No factor of
\(\|\Lambda^{1/2}u\|_2\) occurs.  Thus the extreme high--high--low branch is
paid.  The exact remaining interaction wedge has

\[
 a\gtrsim
 \left({\nu\over\|u_0\|_2}\right)^{2/5}h^{4/5}.
 \tag{FIM.3}
\]

A uniform signed prefix for the full recombined current restricted to this
wedge would prove `(TIR.37a)`.  That wedge prefix is not proved here.  Its
critical high--low valuation can still grow like \(h^{1/5}\), and `(FIM.1)`
shows why the sub-half kinetic fraction does not pay it.

The one object throughout is the smooth fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes field
generated by \(u_0\).  Helical legs, dyadic blocks, and incidence paths below
are simultaneous readouts of that field.  The path calculation is used only
to audit what survives full modal recombination; it is not treated as a
second fluid, a packet colouring, or a freely prescribed Navier--Stokes
history.

## 1. One heterochiral split in critical coordinates

Take a positive-current heterochiral triad.  Let the donor have helicity
\(\sigma\), radius \(r\), and kinetic-energy loss rate \(L>0\).  Let the
same-helicity receiver have radius \(b\) and gain \((1-\theta)L\), and let
the unique-helicity receiver have helicity \(-\sigma\), radius \(c\), and
gain \(\theta L\).  The exact child-fraction calculation in
`thomas-heterochiral-child-fraction-local-nonlocal-boundary-20260803.md`
gives

\[
 0<\theta<\frac12.
 \tag{FIM.4}
\]

Energy conservation is already built into this notation.  Signed-helicity
conservation is

\[
 -\sigma rL
 +\sigma b(1-\theta)L
 -\sigma c\theta L=0.
 \tag{FIM.5}
\]

After division by \(\sigma L\), `(FIM.5)` is the exact critical relation

\[
 \boxed{b(1-\theta)=r+c\theta.}
 \tag{FIM.6}
\]

Put

\[
 X:=rL,
 \qquad
 Y:=c\theta L.
 \tag{FIM.7}
\]

Then the critical strength carried into the same-helicity receiver is

\[
 b(1-\theta)L=X+Y.
 \tag{FIM.8}
\]

The unique child received less than one half of the donor's *kinetic* loss,
but its critical valuation is not subtracted from the continuing carrier.  It
is added to it.  Relative to the donor critical valuation,

\[
 {Y\over X}={c\theta\over r}=:\vartheta,
 \qquad
 {b(1-\theta)L\over rL}=1+\vartheta.
 \tag{FIM.9}
\]

Here \(\vartheta\) is exactly the critically valued ratio `(HCF.5a)`.  In a
high--high--low triad it can diverge even though \(\theta\uparrow1/2\).

## 2. Full modewise recombination of a serial majority spine

Now join \(N\) such triads through one sequence of same-helicity modes

\[
 d_0,d_1,\ldots,d_N,
 \qquad |d_n|=r_n,
 \tag{FIM.10}
\]

with unique-helicity receivers \(o_n\), \(|o_n|=c_n\).  Let triad \(n\)
take kinetic loss \(L_n\) from \(d_n\), put \(\theta_nL_n\) into \(o_n\),
and put \((1-\theta_n)L_n\) into \(d_{n+1}\).  Match the next donor rate to
the preceding same-helicity gain:

\[
 L_{n+1}:=(1-\theta_n)L_n.
 \tag{FIM.11}
\]

Thus the total nonlinear modal source at every intermediate mode \(d_n\),
\(1\leq n<N\), is zero.  No copy of that mode or its loss remains after
recombination.

Define

\[
 X_n:=r_nL_n,
 \qquad
 Y_n:=c_n\theta_nL_n.
 \tag{FIM.12}
\]

Applying `(FIM.6)` at stage \(n\) and then `(FIM.11)` gives

\[
 \boxed{X_{n+1}=X_n+Y_n.}
 \tag{FIM.13}
\]

Therefore

\[
 X_N-X_0=\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}Y_n.
 \tag{FIM.14}
\]

After full modewise recombination the net kinetic source measure of this
finite incidence chain is

\[
 -L_0\delta_{d_0}
 +L_N\delta_{d_N}
 +\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\theta_nL_n\delta_{o_n}.
 \tag{FIM.15}
\]

It conserves kinetic energy because

\[
 L_N+\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}\theta_nL_n=L_0.
 \tag{FIM.16}
\]

It conserves signed helicity because `(FIM.14)` is precisely the helical
first-moment balance of `(FIM.15)`.  Its fully recombined signed critical
current, with the unsigned radial multiplier on every modal source, is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal P_{1/2}^{\rm chain}
 &=-X_0+X_N+\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}Y_n\\
 &=\boxed{2\sum_{n=0}^{N-1}Y_n}
 =\boxed{2(X_N-X_0)}.
\end{aligned}
 \tag{FIM.17}
\]

Equation `(FIM.17)` is the serial form of the unique-helicity identity
`(SFT.18)`.  It takes no positive part before the intermediate incidences
are summed.  It has no incidence cycle and no repeated copy of a donor stock.
The failure of contraction is therefore structural:

\[
 {X_N\over X_0}
 =\prod_{n=0}^{N-1}(1+\vartheta_n),
 \qquad
 \vartheta_n={Y_n\over X_n}\geq0.
 \tag{FIM.18}
\]

The HCF kinetic inequality \(\theta_n<1/2\) implies only
\(L_{n+1}>L_n/2\).  It does not put a factor below one in `(FIM.18)`.

## 4. Kinetic energy absorbs the ultra-nonlocal region

The full-field estimate improves once the interaction *rate* is retained.
Let \(\Delta_j\) be a smooth finite-overlap Littlewood--Paley decomposition,
\(\lambda_j=2^j\), and

\[
 u_j:=\Delta_ju,
 \qquad
 a_j:=\|u_j\|_2,
 \qquad
 U_0:=\|u_0\|_2>0.
 \tag{FIM.25}
\]

The zero-datum case is the zero solution.  The kinetic-energy identity gives

\[
 \sum_j a_j(t)^2\leq C_{\rm LP}U_0^2
 \quad(0<t<T_*).
 \tag{FIM.26}
\]

Put

\[
 \kappa_\nu:=\left({\nu\over U_0}\right)^2
 \tag{FIM.27}
\]

and choose \(J_\nu\) so that \(\lambda_j\geq\kappa_\nu\) for
\(j\geq J_\nu\), up to the fixed dyadic overlap constant.  For a constant
\(c_0>0\) to be fixed below, define

\[
 A_j:=c_0
 \left({\nu\over U_0}\right)^{2/5}\lambda_j^{4/5},
 \qquad
 m(j):=\max\{m:\lambda_m\leq A_j\}.
 \tag{FIM.28}
\]

For \(j\geq J_\nu\), \(A_j/\lambda_j\leq c_0\).  Taking \(c_0\) below the
fixed paraproduct separation constant makes \(m(j)\leq j-C_0\).

Let \(\mathcal P_{1/2}^{\rm ultra}\) be the fully signed three-leg sum of all
pressure-complete triads whose largest dyadic frequency is \(\lambda_j\),
\(j\geq J_\nu\), and whose smallest frequency is at most \(A_j\).  Every
three-leg contribution is recombined before the absolute value below is
taken.  The low--high transport commutator and the high--high-to-low output
term in `(HCF.14)`, with the fixed cutoff replaced pointwise by \(m(j)\), give

\[
\begin{aligned}
 |\mathcal P_{1/2}^{\rm ultra}|
 \leq C_{\rm LP}\sum_{j\geq J_\nu}
 \Bigg[&
 \lambda_j a_j^2
 \sum_{\ell\leq m(j)}\lambda_\ell^{5/2}a_\ell\\
 &+a_j\widetilde a_j
 \sum_{\ell\leq m(j)}\lambda_\ell^{7/2}a_\ell
 \Bigg],
\end{aligned}
 \tag{FIM.29}
\]

where
\(\widetilde a_j^2:=\sum_{|k-j|\leq C_0}a_k^2\).  The first line of
`(FIM.29)` is the low--high transport contribution after incompressibility
removes the undifferentiated low transport.  The second is the
high--high-to-low contribution after the derivative and critical multiplier
are moved onto the low output.  They are not independent physical channels;
they are the two paraproduct faces of the same pressure-complete triad sum.
The ultra subset is defined symmetrically by the minimum and maximum of all
three leg frequencies.  All six assignments of low input, high input, and
output, together with the fixed cutoff collars and neighboring high shells,
are included in `(FIM.29)`; their finite multiplicities are absorbed into
\(C_{\rm LP}\) and \(C_0\).

Kinetic-energy Cauchy gives the two datum-only bounds

\[
 \sum_{\ell\leq m}\lambda_\ell^{5/2}a_\ell
 \leq C_{\rm LP}U_0\lambda_m^{5/2},
 \tag{FIM.30}
\]

and

\[
 \sum_{\ell\leq m}\lambda_\ell^{7/2}a_\ell
 \leq C_{\rm LP}U_0\lambda_m^{7/2}.
 \tag{FIM.31}
\]

For the low--high term, `(FIM.28)` gives the exact exponent balance

\[
 U_0A_j^{5/2}
 =c_0^{5/2}\nu\lambda_j^2.
 \tag{FIM.32}
\]

Consequently its summand is bounded by
\(C_{\rm LP}c_0^{5/2}\nu\lambda_j^3a_j^2\).

For the high--high-to-low term,

\[
 {U_0A_j^{7/2}\over\nu\lambda_j^3}
 =c_0^{7/2}
 \left({\nu\over U_0}\right)^{2/5}\lambda_j^{-1/5}
 \leq c_0^{7/2},
 \tag{FIM.33}
\]

where the last inequality is exactly \(\lambda_j\geq\kappa_\nu\).
Finite overlap and \(a_j\widetilde a_j\leq
(a_j^2+\widetilde a_j^2)/2\) therefore give

\[
 \boxed{
 |\mathcal P_{1/2}^{\rm ultra}(t)|
 \leq {\nu\over4}
 \|\Lambda^{3/2}P_{\gtrsim\kappa_\nu}u(t)\|_2^2,}
 \tag{FIM.34}
\]

after choosing \(c_0=c_0(C_{\rm LP},C_0)>0\) sufficiently small.
Equation `(FIM.34)` contains no critical-height coefficient.  It is uniform
on the whole preterminal interval and is paid by the viscous row already
present in the critical energy balance.

## 5. The exact residual wedge

The finitely bounded maximum-frequency region
\(h\lesssim\kappa_\nu\) is also datum-paid.  Indeed, with
\(u_{\rm lo}=P_{\lesssim\kappa_\nu}u\), Bernstein gives

\[
 |(Q(u_{\rm lo}),\Lambda u_{\rm lo})_2|
 \leq C\kappa_\nu^{7/2}U_0^3,
 \tag{FIM.35}
\]

so its absolute time integral is at most
\(C T_*\kappa_\nu^{7/2}U_0^3\).  Smooth cutoff collars change only the fixed
constant.

Let \(\mathcal P_{1/2}^{\rm wedge}\) be the remaining fully signed triad
sum: its largest frequency satisfies \(h\gtrsim\kappa_\nu\), and its smallest
frequency satisfies

\[
 a>A(h):=c_0
 \left({\nu\over U_0}\right)^{2/5}h^{4/5}.
 \tag{FIM.36}
\]

The exact critical row and `(FIM.34)`--`(FIM.35)` imply, for every
\(T<T_*\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
 {1\over2}\|\Lambda^{1/2}u(T)\|_2^2
 +{3\nu\over4}\int_0^T\|\Lambda^{3/2}u(t)\|_2^2\,dt
 \leq{}&
 {1\over2}\|\Lambda^{1/2}u_0\|_2^2\\
 &+C T_*\kappa_\nu^{7/2}U_0^3
 +\int_0^T\mathcal P_{1/2}^{\rm wedge}(t)\,dt.
\end{aligned}
 \tag{FIM.37}
\]

Thus the strictly smaller interaction-support target

\[
 \boxed{
 \sup_{T<T_*}
 \int_0^T\mathcal P_{1/2}^{\rm wedge}(t)\,dt
 <\infty}
 \tag{FIM.38}
\]

would prove `(TIR.37a)`.  Conversely, the completed critical stock/action
bound controls the removed ultra-nonlocal term by `(FIM.34)` and the bounded
region by `(FIM.35)`, so `(FIM.38)` is closure-equivalent modulo these proved
datum-paid pieces.  It is a strict reduction in interaction support, not a
claim that the remaining signed estimate has already been paid.

Inside the wedge,

\[
 {h\over a}
 \leq Cc_0^{-1}
 \left({U_0\over\nu}\right)^{2/5}h^{1/5}.
 \tag{FIM.39}
\]

The HCF critical receiver/donor ratio therefore has at worst the sharpened
growth

\[
 \vartheta
 \leq {h\over2a}
 \lesssim
 \left({U_0\over\nu}\right)^{2/5}h^{1/5},
 \tag{FIM.40}
\]

but `(FIM.40)` is still unbounded.  More importantly, even a uniform bound
\(\vartheta<1/2\) would enter the serial law as
\(X_{n+1}=(1+\vartheta_n)X_n\), not as a contraction.  The surviving theorem
must therefore control the signed, pressure-complete incidence history in
`(FIM.38)`—through phase cancellation, a datum-finite history charge, or an
equivalent full-field mechanism—not through the kinetic child fraction.

### 5.1 The datum-paid enstrophy clock narrows the wedge again

The kinetic-energy cutoff is uniform in time.  The instantaneous enstrophy

\[
 Z(t):=\|\Lambda u(t)\|_2^2
 \tag{FIM.41}
\]

provides a second, time-dependent removal without using the critical height.
The energy identity pays its full time mass:

\[
 \int_0^{T_*}Z(t)\,dt\leq {U_0^2\over2\nu}.
 \tag{FIM.42}
\]

Dyadic Cauchy now gives

\[
 \sum_{\ell\leq m}\lambda_\ell^{5/2}a_\ell
 \leq C_{\rm LP}Z^{1/2}\lambda_m^{3/2},
 \qquad
 \sum_{\ell\leq m}\lambda_\ell^{7/2}a_\ell
 \leq C_{\rm LP}Z^{1/2}\lambda_m^{5/2}.
 \tag{FIM.43}
\]

First take \(m=j+C_0\), so every permutation of every triad with maximum
frequency \(h\simeq\lambda_j\) is included.  Equations `(FIM.29)` and
`(FIM.43)` then give

\[
 |\mathcal P_{1/2}^{\max\simeq h}|
 \leq C_{\rm LP}Z^{1/2}h^{5/2}
       (a_j^2+a_j\widetilde a_j).
 \tag{FIM.44}
\]

Consequently, for a sufficiently large fixed \(C_Z\), every interaction with

\[
 h\geq K_Z(t):=C_Z{Z(t)\over\nu^2}
 \tag{FIM.45}
\]

is absorbable into an arbitrarily prescribed fixed fraction of \(\nu D(t)\).
This is the full signed triad sum at those maximum frequencies, not only its
nonlocal part.

For \(h<K_Z(t)\), define the dynamic low-leg cutoff

\[
 A_Z(h,t):=c_Z\nu^{2/3}Z(t)^{-1/3}h^{4/3}
 \tag{FIM.46}
\]

on \(\{Z>0\}\); when \(Z=0\), the velocity is zero in the homogeneous
energy class.  Choose \(c_Z\) small enough that \(A_Z/h\) lies below the
fixed paraproduct separation constant whenever \(h<K_Z\).  The first
quantity in `(FIM.43)` then satisfies the exact balance

\[
 Z^{1/2}A_Z^{3/2}
 =c_Z^{3/2}\nu h^2.
 \tag{FIM.47}
\]

For the high--high-to-low face,

\[
 {Z^{1/2}A_Z^{5/2}\over\nu h^3}
 =c_Z^{5/2}\nu^{2/3}Z^{-1/3}h^{1/3}
 =c_Z^{3/2}{A_Z\over h},
 \tag{FIM.48}
\]

which is small by the same separation choice.  Thus the complete signed
subregion with smallest frequency \(a\leq A_Z(h,t)\) is also viscously paid.
All low--high, high--low, and high--high-to-low permutations are already
included through `(FIM.29)`.

Shrinking \(c_0,c_Z\) and enlarging \(C_Z\) if necessary makes the sum of the
static removal `(FIM.34)`, the dynamic removal `(FIM.46)`--`(FIM.48)`, and
the all-interaction high-frequency removal `(FIM.45)` at most
\(\nu D/2\).  Hence `(FIM.38)` can be reduced further to the signed current
on the dynamic core

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{gathered}
 \kappa_\nu\lesssim h\lesssim {Z(t)\over\nu^2},\\
 a\gtrsim
 \max\left\{
 \left({\nu\over U_0}\right)^{2/5}h^{4/5},
 \ \nu^{2/3}Z(t)^{-1/3}h^{4/3}
 \right\}.
 \end{gathered}}
 \tag{FIM.49}
\]

Call that complete recombined current
\(\mathcal P_{1/2}^{\rm core}\).  A uniform upper bound for its signed prefix
implies `(TIR.37a)` after the proved absorptions and the bounded-frequency
term are restored.

The geometry of `(FIM.49)` gives a useful exact exponent improvement.  On

\[
 {h\over a}
 \lesssim
 \min\left\{

 \tag{FIM.50}

Using
\(\min(Ah^{1/5},Bh^{-1/3})\leq A^{5/8}B^{3/8}\) gives

\[
 \boxed{

 \tag{FIM.51}
\]

Moreover, `(FIM.42)` pays the eighth power of this geometric coefficient:

\[
 \boxed{

 \tag{FIM.52}
\]

This does not close the prefix.  The coefficient in `(FIM.51)` multiplies
the critical donor *throughput* in a serial incidence decomposition.  Neither
kinetic energy nor `(FIM.42)` bounds the gross, pressure-complete,

itself.

## 6. Exact verdict

Full incident recombination removes the input-colouring defect but does not
turn the HCF share into payment.  The true critical recurrence on a
mode-consistent majority spine is additive, `(FIM.13)`, and a two-stage real
true-Leray field realizes the cancellation exactly.  Kinetic energy and
viscosity do pay the extreme ultra-nonlocal region `(FIM.2)`, leaving only the
parabolic interaction wedge `(FIM.36)` and, after the enstrophy refinement,
only the dynamic core `(FIM.49)`.  No datum-finite prefix for that core, no
proof of `(TIR.37a)`, and no global-regularity conclusion are established
here.
