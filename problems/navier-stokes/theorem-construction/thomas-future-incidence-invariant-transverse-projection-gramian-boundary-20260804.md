## 5. At every finite depth the tangent tower is exactly zero

\[

\[
 \boxed{

 \bigl(Q^{[j]},D_-^{m-j}\chi\bigr)_2.}
 \tag{FIP.32}
\]

The fields \(Q^{[j]}=(\partial_r+\nu A)^jQ\) are exactly the complete

\[
 \boxed{

 ={}&\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}{\tau_n^{j+1}\over(j+1)!}

This is `(SAP.38)` itself.  The \(\tau_n\)-edge, the \(\delta_n\)-edge,
