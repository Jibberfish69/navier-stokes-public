The physical object remains the one smooth fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes

## 1. The first inherited storage is transported into one signed prefix

Retain the first pressure-explicit binomial row

\[
 Q_t+\nu\Lambda^2Q=\mathcal N_Q,
 \qquad
 \mathcal N_Q=DQ(u)[Q]+2\nu\mathcal C(u),
 \tag{ISR.1}
\]

where $Q=-(u\cdot\nabla)u-\nabla p$ contains the simultaneous pressure
completion and $DQ(u)[Q]$ contains both ordered velocity-parent insertions.
Put

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 b(t)&:=\|\Lambda^{-1/2}Q(t)\|_2,\\
 R_Q(t)&:={1\over2}\|\Lambda^{-3/2}Q(t)\|_2^2,\\
 J_Q(t)&:=(\mathcal N_Q(t),\Lambda^{-3}Q(t))_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{ISR.2}
\]

The exact row `(LSS.35)` is

\[
 \boxed{R_Q'+\nu b^2=J_Q.}
 \tag{ISR.3}
\]

On a canonical last-half record $I_n=(s_n,t_n)$, the source-square lower
bound `(LSS.14)` gives

\[
 \int_{I_n}b^2\,dt
 \ge {\nu A_n^2\over16(A_n+H_0)}.
 \tag{ISR.4}
\]

Integrating `(ISR.3)` only on $I_n$ gives the joined identity

\[
 \boxed{
 R_Q(s_n)+\int_{s_n}^{t_n}J_Q\,dt
 =R_Q(t_n)+\nu\int_{s_n}^{t_n}b^2\,dt.}
 \tag{ISR.5}
\]

Thus the local alternative does not require $[J_Q]_+$: either the signed
work on the window is large or the source storage was already present at its
left endpoint.

Now measure that left endpoint from the actual datum.  Equation `(ISR.3)`
also gives

\[
 R_Q(s_n)
 =R_Q(0)+\int_0^{s_n}J_Q\,dt
   -\nu\int_0^{s_n}b^2\,dt
 \le R_Q(0)+\int_0^{s_n}J_Q\,dt.
 \tag{ISR.6}
\]

More directly, integration to $t_n$ absorbs both alternatives at once:

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \int_0^{t_n}J_Q\,dt
 &=R_Q(t_n)-R_Q(0)+\nu\int_0^{t_n}b^2\,dt\\
 &\ge {\nu^2A_n^2\over16(A_n+H_0)}-R_Q(0).
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{ISR.7}
\]

Since $A_n\to\infty$, every failure of `(TIR.37a)` therefore forces

\[
 \boxed{
 \sup_{0<t<T_*}\int_0^tJ_Q(s)\,ds=\infty.}
 \tag{ISR.8}
\]

This is a complete answer to the *custody* of $R_Q(s_n)$: the datum storage
$R_Q(0)$ is finite, while every later inherited part is the earlier portion
of the same signed $J_Q$ prefix.  It is not an additional unpaid object.
The unpaid theorem is an upper bound for that signed prefix.

## 2. The pressure-explicit continuation at every temporal depth

To test whether `(ISR.8)` descends to a genuinely cheaper next row, define
the heat-compensated pressure-explicit source sequence

\[
 Q^{[0]}:=Q,
 \qquad
 Q^{[j+1]}:=(\partial_t+\nu\Lambda^2)Q^{[j]}
 \quad(j\ge0).
 \tag{ISR.9}
\]

Thus $Q^{[1]}=\mathcal N_Q$ is exactly `(ISR.1)`.  Moreover,

\[
 Q^{[j]}
 =\sum_{a=0}^{j}\binom ja
   \nu^{j-a}\Lambda^{2(j-a)}\partial_t^aQ,
 \tag{ISR.9a}
\]

so this is a binomial recombination of the original temporal tower, not a
replacement for it.  At higher depth,
`(ISR.9)` is obtained by differentiating the complete preceding row; all
ordered parent insertions and their simultaneous pressure completions remain
inside $Q^{[j+1]}$.

The increasingly negative homogeneous storages can have an irrelevant
low-frequency divergence because each pressure-complete source is guaranteed
only one divergence factor.  Fix $\kappa>0$ and let $P_{\ge\kappa}$ be the
self-adjoint orthogonal Fourier projection onto $|\xi|\ge\kappa$.  It
commutes with $\partial_t$ and $\Lambda$;
applying it to the complete equation therefore retains the projected full
source.  Put

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R_{j,\kappa}
 &:={1\over2}
 \|\Lambda^{-(2j+3/2)}P_{\ge\kappa}Q^{[j]}\|_2^2,\\
 b_{j,\kappa}
 &:=\|\Lambda^{-(2j+1/2)}P_{\ge\kappa}Q^{[j]}\|_2,\\
 J_{j,\kappa}
 &:=(P_{\ge\kappa}Q^{[j+1]},
       \Lambda^{-(4j+3)}P_{\ge\kappa}Q^{[j]})_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{ISR.10}
\]

Pairing the $j$-th row with its displayed storage multiplier gives, for
every $j\ge0$,

\[
 \boxed{R_{j,\kappa}'+\nu b_{j,\kappa}^2=J_{j,\kappa}.}
 \tag{ISR.11}
\]

The signed work factors across two adjacent rows:

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 J_{j,\kappa}
 ={}&(\Lambda^{-(2j+5/2)}P_{\ge\kappa}Q^{[j+1]},
       \Lambda^{-(2j+1/2)}P_{\ge\kappa}Q^{[j]})_2,\\
 |J_{j,\kappa}|
 \le{}& b_{j+1,\kappa}b_{j,\kappa}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{ISR.12}
\]

Young's inequality, used only after the complete signed row is written,
yields

\[
 R_{j,\kappa}'+{\nu\over2}b_{j,\kappa}^2
 \le {1\over2\nu}b_{j+1,\kappa}^2.
 \tag{ISR.13}
\]

Consequently

\[
 \int_0^{T_*}b_{j,\kappa}^2\,dt=\infty
 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad
 \int_0^{T_*}b_{j+1,\kappa}^2\,dt=\infty,
 \tag{ISR.14}
\]

and `(ISR.11)` then gives

\[
 \sup_{T<T_*}\int_0^T J_{j,\kappa}(t)\,dt=\infty.
 \tag{ISR.15}
\]

The first source-square divergence really does reach every fixed high-pass.
Indeed

\[
 |\widehat Q(\xi,t)|
 \le C|\xi|\|u(t)\otimes u(t)\|_1
 \le C|\xi|\|u_0\|_2^2,
 \tag{ISR.16}
\]

so for each fixed $\kappa$,

\[
 \|P_{<\kappa}Q(t)\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}^2
 \le C\kappa^4\|u_0\|_2^4.
 \tag{ISR.17}
\]

Because $T_*<\infty$, the low-frequency square has finite time integral.
Equations `(ISR.4)`, `(ISR.14)`, and `(ISR.17)` therefore prove the exact
all-row consequence

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{failure of `(TIR.37a)`}
 \Longrightarrow
 \forall j\ge0,\ \forall\kappa>0:\quad
 \begin{cases}
 \displaystyle\int_0^{T_*}b_{j,\kappa}^2\,dt=\infty,\\[1mm]

 \tag{ISR.18}

Each unbounded continuous signed prefix in `(ISR.18)` has its own canonical

## 3. Why the all-row record is not a well-founded payment descent

Apply the exact fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes scaling

\[
 u_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda u(\lambda x,\lambda^2t),
 \qquad
 p_\lambda(x,t)=\lambda^2p(\lambda x,\lambda^2t).
 \tag{ISR.19}
\]

The complete source rows scale as

\[
 Q^{[j]}_\lambda(x,t)
 =\lambda^{2j+3}Q^{[j]}(\lambda x,\lambda^2t).
 \tag{ISR.20}
\]

With the cutoff scaled covariantly from $\kappa$ to $\lambda\kappa$,

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 R_{j,\lambda\kappa}[u_\lambda](t)
 &=R_{j,\kappa}[u](\lambda^2t),\\
 b_{j,\lambda\kappa}[u_\lambda](t)
 &=\lambda b_{j,\kappa}[u](\lambda^2t),\\
 J_{j,\lambda\kappa}[u_\lambda](t)
 &=\lambda^2J_{j,\kappa}[u](\lambda^2t).
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{ISR.21}
\]

Hence every storage $R_{j,\kappa}$, every square action
$\int b_{j,\kappa}^2dt$, and every signed prefix
$\int J_{j,\kappa}dt$ has the same scale-critical size at every depth.
The datum-paid kinetic energy, enstrophy action, and first-source mass instead
scale as

\[
 \|u_{\lambda,0}\|_2^2=\lambda^{-1}\|u_0\|_2^2,
 \quad
 \int\|\nabla u_\lambda\|_2^2dt
 =\lambda^{-1}\int\|\nabla u\|_2^2dt,
 \quad
 \int b_\lambda\,dt=\lambda^{-1}\int b\,dt.
 \tag{ISR.22}
\]

This is an exact true-Navier--Stokes estimate stress test: scaling a smooth
solution preserves the pressure Poisson equation and every binomial row while
leaving the row actions unchanged and shrinking the three already paid
masses.  It is not a one-datum singular history and therefore is not a
counterexample to a possible cumulative same-history theorem.  It proves
that row depth and the paid scalar masses alone do not create a smaller
scaling class.

Thus `(ISR.18)` is a genuine exact consequence of failure but not a
well-founded descent.  Excluding the next record requires a uniform
all-depth signed cancellation, parent-tail domination, or common tower-radius
law.  Coordinatewise preterminal existence of every binomial row supplies
none of those uniform statements.

## 4. The `(BCR.58)` source action restores the same endpoint

The complete resummation `(BCR.58)` lands on the instantaneous action

\[
 {1\over\nu}\|\Lambda^{-1/2}Q\|_2^2
 ={1\over\nu}b^2.
 \tag{ISR.23}
\]

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_s^t{1\over\nu}b(r)^2\,dr
 ={R_Q(s)-R_Q(t)\over\nu^2}
  +{1\over\nu^2}\int_s^tJ_Q(r)\,dr.}
 \tag{ISR.24}
\]

The storage term also has the linear output-heat resolvent representation

\[
 {R_Q(t)\over\nu^2}
 ={1\over\nu}\int_0^\infty
 \|\Lambda^{-1/2}e^{-\nu\sigma\Lambda^2}Q(t)\|_2^2\,d\sigma.
 \tag{ISR.25}
\]

Equation `(ISR.25)` is only the spectral heat-age representation of the
complete output $Q(t)$; it does not replace the nonlinear heat-aged source
field in `(PRM.46a)`--`(PRM.46b)`.  Its role is to show exactly where the
endpoint sits.

Therefore $R_Q(s_n)$ may be *included algebraically* in the all-depth
heat-age/binomial storage, but it cannot be absorbed as a datum payment.
On the selected interval, `(ISR.24)` restores $R_Q(s_n)$ as the entering
endpoint.  On the full interval from the datum,

\[
 \int_0^T{1\over\nu}b^2\,dt

 \tag{ISR.26}
\]

so only $R_Q(0)$ is datum storage; the later inherited portion is precisely
the earlier signed insertion prefix.  With a moving selector, `(BCR.59)`
adds its exact entry/exit telescope and complete insertion/crossing current.
It does not delete the entering endpoint.  Treating that telescope as an
independent bound would count the same storage twice.

This is the endpoint-alias verdict:

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{all-depth heat-age storage}
 =\text{datum endpoint}+\text{signed insertion history}
 -\text{terminal endpoint},}

not a new finite resource for `(TIR.37a)`.
