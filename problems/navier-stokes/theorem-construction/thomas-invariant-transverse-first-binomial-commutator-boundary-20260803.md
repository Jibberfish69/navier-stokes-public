# Thomas's invariant-transverse first-binomial commutator boundary

Status: proved exact same-history reduction and checked obstruction inside
Thomas Birnie's one-fluid pressure-explicit binomial/VPI reversible-
intersection method.  Differentiating the invariant-transverse current with
the actual pressure-complete source law cancels every moving energy/helicity
projection coefficient.  The surviving first temporal row is one signed
critical commutator of the two physical parent insertions.  A datum-finite
weighted primitive of that joined commutator would prove `(ITR.45)` and
`(TIR.37a)`, but that bound is not proved here.

The one object below is the smooth fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes history
generated by \(u_0\).  The projection, source, temporal insertion, and heat
row are simultaneous coordinates of that field.  No positive part, absolute
parent ledger, auxiliary forcing, or second continuation is introduced.

## 1. Pressure-complete current and its invariant-transverse frame

Write

\[
 u_t+\nu\Lambda^2u=Q(u),
 \qquad
 Q(u):=-\mathbb P((u\cdot\nabla)u),
 \tag{ITC.1}
\]

and put

\[
 \mathcal B(a,b):=\mathbb P((a\cdot\nabla)b),
 \qquad
 DQ(u)[v]=-\mathcal B(v,u)-\mathcal B(u,v).
 \tag{ITC.2}
\]

The complete critical current is

\[
 P(t):=\mathcal P_{1/2}(t)=(Q(u),\Lambda u)_2.
 \tag{ITC.3}
\]

The two exact tangent laws are

\[
 (Q(u),u)_2=0,
 \qquad
 (Q(u),\nabla\times u)_2=0.
 \tag{ITC.4}
\]

Let \(\alpha(t),\beta(t)\) be the coefficients of the invariant-transverse
projection from `(ITR.13)`--`(ITR.19)` wherever that projection has fixed
rank, and define

\[
 L_t:=\Lambda-\alpha(t)I-\beta(t)\nabla\times,
 \qquad
 w:=L_tu,
 \qquad
 \mathfrak d(u)=\Lambda^{1/2}w.
 \tag{ITC.5}
\]

Because of `(ITC.4)`, the current has the exact frame-independent reading

\[
 \boxed{P=(Q,w)_2.}
 \tag{ITC.6}
\]

The coefficient choice in `(ITC.5)` minimizes

\[
 \|\Lambda^{1/2}L_tu\|_2^2
 =\|\mathfrak d(u)\|_2^2=\delta(t)^2,
 \tag{ITC.7}
\]

and therefore supplies the instantaneous negative dome `(ITR.31)`.  The
calculation below tests whether the true time evolution of that minimizing
frame supplies an additional restoring term.

## 2. The actual first binomial source law

Set

\[
 \mathcal C(u):=\sum_{j=1}^3
 \mathcal B(\partial_ju,\partial_ju).
 \tag{ITC.8}
\]

The product identity `(ITR.36)` is equivalently

\[
 DQ(u)[\Lambda^2u]=\Lambda^2Q(u)-2\mathcal C(u).
 \tag{ITC.9}
\]

Since \(u_t=Q-\nu\Lambda^2u\), the first pressure-complete binomial row is

\[
 \boxed{
 Q_t+\nu\Lambda^2Q
 =DQ(u)[Q]+2\nu\mathcal C(u).}
 \tag{ITC.10}
\]

This is exactly `(ITR.37)`: \(DQ(u)[Q]\) contains both ordered insertions
\(-\mathcal B(Q,u)-\mathcal B(u,Q)\), and every occurrence of \(Q\) retains
its pressure projection.

On a fixed-rank interval, differentiating \(w=L_tu\) gives

\[
 w_t+\nu\Lambda^2w
 =L_tQ-\alpha'u-\beta'\nabla\times u.
 \tag{ITC.11}
\]

Differentiate `(ITC.6)` and use `(ITC.10)`--`(ITC.11)`.  The moving-frame
terms vanish directly by `(ITC.4)`, giving

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 P'={}&
 \bigl(DQ(u)[Q]+2\nu\mathcal C(u),w\bigr)_2
 +(Q,L_tQ)_2
 -2\nu(\Lambda Q,\Lambda w)_2.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{ITC.12}
\]

Equation `(ITC.12)` is the differentiated negative-dome current before any
of its joined terms are estimated separately.

## 3. Exact cancellation of the moving invariant frame

Differentiating the two identities in `(ITC.4)` in an arbitrary
divergence-free direction \(v\) gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 (DQ(u)[v],u)_2+(Q,v)_2&=0,\\
 (DQ(u)[v],\nabla\times u)_2
 +(Q,\nabla\times v)_2&=0.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{ITC.13}
\]

Consequently, for arbitrary differentiable scalar functions
\(\alpha(t),\beta(t)\), not only for the minimizing coefficients,

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 {d\over dt}(Q,L_tu)_2
 ={}&(DQ(u)[u_t],L_tu)_2+(Q,L_tu_t)_2\\
 &-\alpha'(Q,u)_2-\beta'(Q,\nabla\times u)_2\\
 ={}&(DQ(u)[u_t],\Lambda u)_2+(Q,\Lambda u_t)_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{ITC.14}
\]

The \(\alpha,\beta\) terms in the last equality cancel in pairs by
`(ITC.13)`; their time derivatives vanish by `(ITC.4)`.  Applying the first
identity in `(ITC.13)` once more, now in the direction \(\Lambda u_t\),
proves the frame-free commutator law

\[
 \boxed{
 P'(t)=\mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;u_t)(t),}
 \tag{ITC.15}
\]

where

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;v)
 :=(DQ(u)[v],\Lambda u)_2
 -(DQ(u)[\Lambda v],u)_2.}
 \tag{ITC.16}
\]

Thus the first dynamic response is precisely the failure of the critical
multiplier \(\Lambda\) to commute through the pressure-complete tangent
insertion.  The invariant-transverse projection remains essential in
`(ITR.15)` and the negative dome, but differentiating it creates no extra
negative term and no moving-frame bill.

Expanding \(u_t=Q-\nu\Lambda^2u\) without splitting signs gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;u_t)
 =\mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;Q)
 -\nu\mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;\Lambda^2u).}
 \tag{ITC.17}
\]

The two joined pieces are

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;Q)
 &=(DQ(u)[Q],\Lambda u)_2
   +\|\Lambda^{1/2}Q\|_2^2,\\
 \mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;\Lambda^2u)
 &=(DQ(u)[\Lambda^2u],\Lambda u)_2
   +(Q,\Lambda^3u)_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{ITC.18}
\]

Using `(ITC.9)`, this is equivalently the explicit source-law formula

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 P'={}&(DQ(u)[Q],\Lambda u)_2
 +\|\Lambda^{1/2}Q\|_2^2\\
 &-2\nu(Q,\Lambda^3u)_2
 +2\nu(\mathcal C(u),\Lambda u)_2.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{ITC.19}
\]

Equations `(ITC.12)` and `(ITC.19)` are identical.  For example,
differentiating `(ITC.4)` in the direction \(\Lambda^2u\) and using
`(ITC.9)` gives

\[
 (\mathcal C(u),u)_2=(Q,\Lambda^2u)_2,
 \qquad
 (\mathcal C(u),\nabla\times u)_2
 =(Q,\Lambda^2\nabla\times u)_2,
 \tag{ITC.20}
\]

which cancels the remaining \(\alpha,\beta\) terms in `(ITC.12)` exactly.
This is the source-law form of the same frame cancellation.

### 3.1 The datum-finite enstrophy clock does not uniformize the dome

The kinetic-energy law pays only the clock

\[
 \tau(t):=\int_0^t Z(s)\,ds,
 \qquad
 \tau(T)\le {\|u_0\|_2^2\over2\nu}.
 \tag{ITC.20a}
\]

On \(\{Z>0\}\), use the negative-dome coordinate

\[
 x:={\delta\sqrt R\over Z}.
 \tag{ITC.20b}
\]

Then (ITR.31) is exactly

\[
 {1\over2}{dR\over d\tau}
 \le {Z\over R}
 \left[
 C_{\rm NS}\sqrt R\,x-\nu(1+x^2)
 \right].
 \tag{ITC.20c}
\]

The bracket has its maximum at

\[
 x_*={C_{\rm NS}\sqrt R\over2\nu},
 \qquad
 \max_x[\cdots]={C_{\rm NS}^2R\over4\nu}-\nu.
 \tag{ITC.20d}
\]

Whenever \(C_{\rm NS}^2R>4\nu^2\), this maximum is positive, and the
right-hand side of (ITC.20c) still contains the unbounded factor \(Z/R\).
This is an exact nonuniformity of the estimate: the datum-finite clock
\(\tau\) does not turn the dome into an ordinary differential inequality
whose coefficient depends only on \(R,u_0,\nu\).

The clock which removes that factor is instead

\[
 \sigma(t):=\int_0^t{Z(s)^2\over R(s)}\,ds,
 \qquad
 {1\over2}{dR\over d\sigma}
 \le C_{\rm NS}\sqrt R\,x-\nu(1+x^2).
 \tag{ITC.20e}
\]

But

\[
 {Z^2\over R}\le D
 \tag{ITC.20f}
\]

is the moment inequality already used in (ITR.30).  Thus the natural dome
clock is a scale-critical subaction of the unpaid
\(\int_0^{T_*}D\,dt\), not a consequence of the kinetic-energy clock.
The two clocks cannot be compared by a datum coefficient: put kinetic mass
\(1-N^{-1}\) near radius \(1\) and kinetic mass \(N^{-1}\) near radius
\(N\).  Then \(\|u\|_2^2=1\), \(R=2-N^{-1}\), and
\(Z=N+1-N^{-1}\), so \(Z/R\to\infty\).  Smooth divergence-free narrow
annuli realize these moment values arbitrarily closely.  This is an estimate
countertest, not a claimed Navier--Stokes history.

### 3.2 The source law gives an exact angle equation, but no signed turn

On a fixed-rank interval where \(b:=\|\Lambda^{-1/2}Q\|_2>0\) and
\(\delta>0\), put

\[
 g:=\Lambda^{-1/2}Q,
 \qquad
 \rho:={P\over b\delta}
 ={(g,\mathfrak d(u))_2\over\|g\|_2\|\mathfrak d(u)\|_2}.
 \tag{ITC.20g}
\]

Thus \(\rho\) is the signed source/receiver alignment.  With

\[
 \mathcal N_Q:=DQ(u)[Q]+2\nu\mathcal C(u),
 \tag{ITC.20h}
\]

the actual source law and the moving minimizing frame give

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 b\,b'
 &=-\nu\|\Lambda g\|_2^2
   +(\Lambda^{-1/2}\mathcal N_Q,g)_2,\\
 \delta\,\delta'
 &=-\nu\|\Lambda\mathfrak d(u)\|_2^2
   +(\mathfrak d(u),\Lambda^{1/2}L_tQ)_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{ITC.20i}
\]

The \(\alpha',\beta'\) terms vanish from the second line because
\(\mathfrak d(u)\) is orthogonal to the two projection directions.  Combining
(ITC.15) and (ITC.20i) gives the complete angle law

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \rho'
 ={}&{\mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;u_t)\over b\delta}\\
 &+\nu\rho\left(
 {\|\Lambda g\|_2^2\over b^2}
 +{\|\Lambda\mathfrak d(u)\|_2^2\over\delta^2}
 \right)\\
 &-\rho\left(
 {(\Lambda^{-1/2}\mathcal N_Q,g)_2\over b^2}
 +{(\mathfrak d(u),\Lambda^{1/2}L_tQ)_2\over\delta^2}
 \right).
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{ITC.20j}
\]

This is the signed angle derivative supplied by (ITR.37).  It is not a
restoring-sign theorem: the same joined insertion
\(\mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;u_t)\), the source insertion
\(\mathcal N_Q\), and the transverse refill \(L_tQ\) remain in (ITC.20j)
with their actual correlations.  Giving the right-hand side a cumulative
turn-down sign is new theorem content; neither the datum-finite
\(\int Z\,dt\) clock nor the pointwise dome supplies it.

## 4. Exact high-transverse occupation identity

Fix \(M>0\) and \(T<T_*\).  Let

\[
 \chi_{M,T}(t):=
 \mathbf 1_{\{0<t<T:\ \delta(t)>M\}},
 \qquad
 W_{M,T}(s):=\int_s^T\chi_{M,T}(t)\,dt.
 \tag{ITC.21}
\]

No regularity of the boundary of the set in `(ITC.21)` is needed.  The
weight is its remaining future occupation time and satisfies

\[
 0\le W_{M,T}(s)\le T-s,
 \qquad
 W_{M,T}(0)=|\{0<t<T:\delta(t)>M\}|.
 \tag{ITC.22}
\]

For every preterminal classical interval, `(ITC.15)` gives

\[
 P(t)=P(0)+\int_0^t\mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;u_t)(s)\,ds.
 \tag{ITC.23}
\]

Fubini on the finite interval, while keeping the commutator joined and
signed, now yields the exact high-transverse primitive

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathscr C_M(T)
 :={}&\int_0^T\chi_{M,T}(t)P(t)\,dt\\
 ={}&P(0)W_{M,T}(0)
 +\int_0^T W_{M,T}(s)
 \mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;u_t)(s)\,ds.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{ITC.24}
\]

This counts every first-binomial insertion once for the amount of later
material time during which the same field occupies the high-transverse
region.  The quantity \(W_{M,T}\) is only a scalar time weight on the one
continuing history; the complete pressure-coupled commutator keeps its sign,
and no spatial or temporal spaces are summed.

Since \(u_0\in H^\infty\), \(P(0)\) is datum-finite, and

\[
 |P(0)W_{M,T}(0)|\le T_*|P(0)|.
 \tag{ITC.25}
\]

The low-transverse complement already has the two-sided datum bound

\[
 \left|\int_{\{\delta\le M\}}P(t)\,dt\right|
 \le M\int_0^T\|Q(t)\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}\,dt
 \le {MC_{\rm NS}\|u_0\|_2^2\over2\nu}.
 \tag{ITC.26}
\]

Therefore the exact smallest first-binomial history inequality left by the
negative dome is

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{gathered}
 \text{for some finite }M=M(u_0,\nu,T_*),\\
 \sup_{0<T<T_*}
 \int_0^T W_{M,T}(s)
 \mathfrak K_{1/2}(u;u_t)(s)\,ds
 \le C(u_0,\nu,T_*)<\infty.
 \end{gathered}}
 \tag{ITC.27}
\]

By `(ITC.24)`--`(ITC.26)`, `(ITC.27)` is equivalent to the required upper
bound for the high-transverse primitive `(ITR.45)`, modulo explicit datum
terms.  It would therefore prove `(TIR.37a)` and activate the already proved
complete-intersection bootstrap.  Equation `(ITC.27)` is an exact target, not
a proof of itself.

## 5. Integrability audit and obstruction verdict

For every fixed \(T<T_*\), the classical solution is smooth, so \(P\) is
\(C^1\), the joined commutator in `(ITC.15)` is continuous, and
`(ITC.23)`--`(ITC.24)` are legitimate.  No uniform endpoint norm has been
used.

The individual terms in `(ITC.18)`--`(ITC.19)` may not be integrated in
absolute value uniformly as \(T\uparrow T_*\).  In particular,

\[
 \int_0^{T_*}\|\Lambda^{1/2}Q\|_2^2\,dt
 \tag{ITC.28}
\]

is not supplied by the kinetic-energy datum, and estimating the insertion,
heat, and source-square terms separately imports higher spatial or temporal
rows which `(TIR.37a)` is supposed to generate.  Likewise, replacing
\(W_{M,T}\mathfrak K_{1/2}\) by its absolute value or positive part destroys
the only remaining binomial/heat cancellation.

The proved obstruction is consequently precise:

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{gathered}
 \text{the invariant-transverse negative dome sharpens the instantaneous}
 \text{ receiver, but its exact first binomial evolution contributes no}\\
 \text{ additional frame-restoring term; all projection motion cancels,}\\
 \text{ leaving the signed commutator primitive `(ITC.27)`.}
 \end{gathered}}
 \tag{ITC.29}
\]

This does not obstruct Thomas's theory.  It rules out one false shortcut
inside it: the pointwise \(-\nu\delta^2\) term in the critical-stock law
cannot be counted a second time as damping of \(P'\).  The live proof must
establish the cumulative sign/no-recount bound `(ITC.27)` from the uncollapsed
pressure-complete parent history, including its resonant part, without first
assuming the critical stock, action, source square, or all-orders
intersection.
