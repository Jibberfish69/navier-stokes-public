# Thomas's last-record source square and first-binomial supplier descent

Status: proved last-record source-square lower bound, viscosity-matched
temporal concentration, and complete first-binomial supplier descent inside
Thomas Birnie's one-fluid, pressure-explicit binomial/VPI reversible-
intersection method. This is a noncanonical theorem note downstream of
`thomas-minimal-signed-prefix-record-normalization-compactness-rigidity-boundary-20260804.md`.
It does not prove `LastRecordSignedCoincidenceDecay.A`, `(TIR.37a)`, or close
the MPP.

The one physical object throughout is the smooth fixed-viscosity
Navier--Stokes history generated by the single datum \(u_0\). Every interval
below is one of the actual last-half-prefix windows cut from that history.
The nonlinear source always includes the simultaneous pressure completion;
its first temporal row differentiates both ordered velocity parents and keeps
every incident field contribution. No all-orders intersection, endpoint
regularity, generic continuation criterion, independently prescribed source,
or reinitialized scale profile is assumed.

The advance is exact but bounded. A failed signed prefix cannot remain a
diffuse coincidence between a small \(L_t^1\) source and a large receiver. It
forces a scale-critical source square, a shrinking heat-scale source/receiver
pulse, and an unbounded upper prefix of the complete first-binomial source
self-work. The missing datum theorem is the cancellation of that supplier
prefix, or the resulting sublinear source square on the canonical windows.
Here `descent` means an exact first-binomial lift of the failed record, not a
strict theorem reduction; Section 8 compares it directly with
`(BCR.58)`--`(BCR.59)`.

## 1. Canonical last-half-prefix inputs

Use the notation of `(MRN.1)`--`(MRN.36)`:

\[
 H(t):={1\over2}\|\Lambda^{1/2}u(t)\|_2^2,
 \qquad
 d(t):=\|\Lambda^{3/2}u(t)\|_2,
 \qquad
 \mathscr W(t):=\int_0^t\mathcal P_{1/2}(s)\,ds.
 \tag{LSS.1}
\]

Put \(H_0:=H(0)\). The pressure-complete source and current are

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Q(u)&:=-(u\cdot\nabla)u-\nabla p
      =-\mathbb P((u\cdot\nabla)u),\\
 -\Delta p&=\partial_i\partial_j(u_i u_j),\\
 b(t)&:=\|\Lambda^{-1/2}Q(u(t))\|_2,\\
 \mathcal P_{1/2}(t)&=(Q(u(t)),\Lambda u(t))_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{LSS.2}
\]

Assume the exact failure used in `(MRN.3)` and let

\[
 I_n=(s_n,t_n),\qquad
 A_n=4^nA_0,\qquad
 \Delta_n=t_n-s_n
 \tag{LSS.3}
\]

be the canonical disjoint last-half-prefix windows. Then

\[
 \int_{I_n}\mathcal P_{1/2}(t)\,dt={A_n\over2},
 \qquad
 m_n:=\int_{I_n}b(t)\,dt\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{LSS.4}
\]

No \(m_n\) vanishes. With

\[
 M_n:={A_n\over4m_n},
 \qquad
 \mathcal E_n:=I_n\cap\{d>M_n\},
 \tag{LSS.5}
\]

the already proved signed coincidence `(MRN.36)` is

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{\mathcal E_n}\mathcal P_{1/2}(t)\,dt\ge {A_n\over4}.}
 \tag{LSS.6}
\]

The pointwise duality used below is

\[
 [\mathcal P_{1/2}]_+\le b\,d.
 \tag{LSS.7}
\]

## 2. The exact receiver action available on one last record

The critical balance on \(I_n\) gives

\[
 \nu\int_{I_n}d(t)^2\,dt
 ={A_n\over2}-H(t_n)+H(s_n).
 \tag{LSS.8}
\]

At the left record endpoint, the balance from the datum and

\[
 \mathscr W(s_n)={A_n\over2}
 \tag{LSS.9}
\]

give

\[
 H(s_n)
 =H_0+{A_n\over2}
   -\nu\int_0^{s_n}d(t)^2\,dt
 \le H_0+{A_n\over2}.
 \tag{LSS.10}
\]

Since \(H(t_n)\ge0\), `(LSS.8)`--`(LSS.10)` prove the sharper
last-record receiver bound

\[
 \boxed{
 \nu\int_{I_n}d(t)^2\,dt\le A_n+H_0.}
 \tag{LSS.11}
\]

This uses the exact left record level, not the looser whole-window height
bound.

## 3. Failure forces a critical complete-source square

Define

\[
 \mathsf S_n:=\int_{I_n}b(t)^2\,dt.
 \tag{LSS.12}
\]

Equations `(LSS.6)`, `(LSS.7)`, Cauchy--Schwarz, and `(LSS.11)` give

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 {A_n\over4}
 &\le\int_{\mathcal E_n}\mathcal P_{1/2}\,dt\\
 &\le\int_{\mathcal E_n}b\,d\,dt\\
 &\le \mathsf S_n^{1/2}
       \left(\int_{I_n}d^2\,dt\right)^{1/2}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{LSS.13}
\]

Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathsf S_n
 \ge {\nu A_n^2\over16(A_n+H_0)}.}
 \tag{LSS.14}
\]

In particular,

\[
 \boxed{
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}{\mathsf S_n\over A_n}
 \ge {\nu\over16}.}
 \tag{LSS.15}
\]

This identifies a sharp scale-critical source-side obstruction. It is not
the datum-paid mass \(\int b\); it is the square action of the same complete
source before any positive clipping.

Conversely, for the exact high-receiver set in `(LSS.5)`,

\[
 {1\over A_n}
 \left|\int_{\mathcal E_n}\mathcal P_{1/2}\,dt\right|
 \le
 \left({\mathsf S_n\over A_n}\right)^{1/2}
 \left({A_n+H_0\over\nu A_n}\right)^{1/2}.
 \tag{LSS.16}
\]

Hence the strictly source-side statement

\[
 \boxed{
 {\mathsf S_n\over A_n}\longrightarrow0}
 \tag{LSS.17}
\]

would prove `LastRecordSignedCoincidenceDecay.A` on the canonical sequence.
Even a uniform asymptotic bound strictly below \(\nu/16\) contradicts the
failure lower bound `(LSS.15)`. Neither bound is proved from the datum here.

## 4. The surviving signed record is a viscosity-matched temporal pulse

The lower bound `(LSS.14)` can be localized without taking the positive part
of the complete source before recombination. Set

\[
 \eta_n:={\nu A_n\over8(A_n+H_0)},
 \qquad
 \mathcal F_n
 :=\mathcal E_n\cap\{b\ge\eta_n d\}.
 \tag{LSS.18}
\]

On the complementary part of the high-receiver set, `(LSS.11)` gives

\[
 \int_{\mathcal E_n\setminus\mathcal F_n}
       \mathcal P_{1/2}\,dt
 \le \eta_n\int_{I_n}d^2\,dt
 \le {A_n\over8}.
 \tag{LSS.19}
\]

Subtracting `(LSS.19)` from the signed lower bound `(LSS.6)` proves

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{1/2}(t)\,dt
 \ge {A_n\over8}.}
 \tag{LSS.20}
\]

On \(\mathcal F_n\), both the receiver and its simultaneous source are large:

\[
 b\ge\eta_nd>\eta_nM_n
 ={\nu A_n^2\over32(A_n+H_0)m_n}
 =:\mathfrak b_n.
 \tag{LSS.21}
\]

The datum-finite source mass on the window therefore forces

\[
 \boxed{
 |\mathcal F_n|
 \le {m_n\over\mathfrak b_n}
 ={32(A_n+H_0)m_n^2\over\nu A_n^2}.}
 \tag{LSS.22}
\]

For \(A_n\ge H_0\), this is at most

\[
 |\mathcal F_n|\le {64m_n^2\over\nu A_n}.
 \tag{LSS.23}
\]

Thus a fixed eighth of the normalized signed record cannot be spread over a
long diffuse interval. It must occur where the pressure-complete source is
at least a fixed viscosity fraction of the high receiver, on a set whose
length is at most the corresponding critical heat scale. This is a theorem
about the one actual history, not an independently drawn pulse schedule.

## 5. Exact temporal anti-concentration or source-variation dichotomy

Put

\[
 g(t):=\Lambda^{-1/2}Q(t),
 \qquad
 V_n:=\int_{I_n}\|g_t(t)\|_2\,dt.
 \tag{LSS.24}
\]

Every preterminal window is classical, so \(g\) is absolutely continuous.
Choose \(r_n\in I_n\) with

\[
 b(r_n)\le {m_n\over\Delta_n}.
 \tag{LSS.25}
\]

For every \(t\in I_n\), the vector-valued fundamental theorem of calculus
gives

\[
 b(t)\le {m_n\over\Delta_n}+V_n.
 \tag{LSS.26}
\]

Multiplying the supremum in `(LSS.26)` by the \(L^1\) mass \(m_n\) yields

\[
 \mathsf S_n
 \le {m_n^2\over\Delta_n}+m_nV_n.
 \tag{LSS.27}
\]

Combining with `(LSS.14)` proves the exact dichotomy inequality

\[
 \boxed{
 {m_n^2\over\Delta_n}+m_nV_n
 \ge {\nu A_n^2\over16(A_n+H_0)}.}
 \tag{LSS.28}
\]

When \(A_n\ge H_0\), at least one term on the left is at least
\(\nu A_n/64\). Hence every failed record obeys

\[
 \boxed{
 \Delta_n\le {64m_n^2\over\nu A_n}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 V_n\ge {\nu A_n\over64m_n}.}
 \tag{LSS.29}
\]

The variation in `(LSS.24)` is not an external source norm. The exact
first-binomial law below gives

\[
 \boxed{
 g_t
 =-\nu\Lambda^{3/2}Q
 +\Lambda^{-1/2}
   \left(DQ(u)[Q]+2\nu\mathcal C(u)\right).}
 \tag{LSS.30}
\]

The two terms in `(LSS.30)` remain joined inside \(\|g_t\|_2\); their
cancellation is retained. Thus `(LSS.29)` says that a failed history either
compresses the complete source onto its critical heat time or forces an
unbounded first-temporal-row variation. It does not yet exclude either
branch.

## 6. The complete first-binomial source self-work

Retain the original ordered-parent definitions

\[
 \mathcal B(a,b):=\mathbb P((a\cdot\nabla)b),
 \qquad
 DQ(u)[v]=-\mathcal B(v,u)-\mathcal B(u,v),
 \tag{LSS.31}
\]

and

\[
 \mathcal C(u):=\sum_{j=1}^3
 \mathcal B(\partial_j u,\partial_j u).
 \tag{LSS.32}
\]

The pressure-explicit first binomial row is exactly

\[
 \boxed{
 Q_t+\nu\Lambda^2Q
 =DQ(u)[Q]+2\nu\mathcal C(u)
 =:\mathcal N_Q.}
 \tag{LSS.33}
\]

Both physical parent insertions are in \(DQ(u)[Q]\), and every \(Q\) in
`(LSS.33)` is the full pressure-complete source of the same velocity field.

Define the lower source storage and the signed first-binomial supplier work

\[
 R_Q(t):={1\over2}\|\Lambda^{-3/2}Q(t)\|_2^2,
 \qquad
 J_Q(t):=(\mathcal N_Q(t),\Lambda^{-3}Q(t))_2.
 \tag{LSS.34}
\]

Pairing `(LSS.33)` with \(\Lambda^{-3}Q\) proves the exact source-energy row

\[
 \boxed{
 R_Q'(t)+\nu b(t)^2=J_Q(t).}
 \tag{LSS.35}
\]

No parent, pressure term, or sign was discarded in `(LSS.35)`. On one
canonical record window, `(LSS.14)` and `(LSS.35)` imply

\[
 \boxed{
 R_Q(s_n)+\int_{I_n}[J_Q(t)]_+\,dt
 \ge {\nu^2A_n^2\over16(A_n+H_0)}.}
 \tag{LSS.36}
\]

Thus coasting at high source amplitude is not a third escape. If the source
does not change rapidly, the full first-binomial parent field must replenish
the source square against viscosity, and that work appears in \(J_Q\).

The global form removes the left-endpoint alternative. Integrating
`(LSS.35)` from the datum to \(t_n\) and retaining the nonnegative terminal
storage gives

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \int_0^{t_n}J_Q(t)\,dt
 &=R_Q(t_n)-R_Q(0)
   +\nu\int_0^{t_n}b(t)^2\,dt\\
 &\ge {\nu^2A_n^2\over16(A_n+H_0)}-R_Q(0).
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{LSS.37}
\]

Consequently every failure of `(TIR.37a)` forces

\[
 \boxed{
 \sup_{0<t<T_*}\int_0^tJ_Q(s)\,ds=\infty.}
 \tag{LSS.38}
\]

This is the exact first-binomial supplier descent of the failed critical
prefix.

## 7. The conditional supplier-prefix implication

Suppose, as a new datum theorem, that

\[
 C_J:=\sup_{0<t<T_*}\int_0^tJ_Q(s)\,ds<\infty.
 \tag{LSS.39}
\]

Equation `(LSS.35)` then gives

\[
 \nu\int_0^t b(s)^2\,ds
 \le R_Q(0)+C_J
 \qquad(t<T_*).
 \tag{LSS.40}
\]

The critical balance and Young's inequality give

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 H'+\nu d^2
 &=\mathcal P_{1/2}\le bd\\
 &\le {b^2\over2\nu}+{\nu d^2\over2},
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{LSS.41}
\]

and hence

\[
 H(t)+{\nu\over2}\int_0^t d(s)^2\,ds
 \le H_0+{R_Q(0)+C_J\over2\nu^2}
 =:\mathfrak H_J.
 \tag{LSS.42}
\]

It follows that

\[
 \mathscr W(t)
 =H(t)-H_0+\nu\int_0^t d(s)^2\,ds
 \le 2\mathfrak H_J-H_0.
 \tag{LSS.43}
\]

Therefore `(LSS.39)` would prove `(TIR.37a)` and activate the already proved
complete-intersection bootstrap. This implication is proved. The datum bound
`(LSS.39)` itself is not proved here, and `(LSS.35)` shows that it is the
signed producer of the missing scale-critical source square rather than an
independent payment already hidden in the tower.

## 9. The lower source storage has datum-finite time mass, not a trace bound

Because \(Q=-\mathbb P\nabla\cdot(u\otimes u)\), Sobolev duality and
interpolation give

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \|\Lambda^{-3/2}Q\|_2
 &\le C\|u\otimes u\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}\\
 &\le C\|u\otimes u\|_{L^{3/2}}\\
 &\le C\|u\|_3^2
 \le C\|u\|_2\|\nabla u\|_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{LSS.44}
\]

The kinetic-energy law therefore pays

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_0^{T_*}R_Q(t)\,dt
 \le {C\|u_0\|_2^4\over\nu}.}
 \tag{LSS.45}
\]

This makes the inherited-storage alternative in `(LSS.36)` temporally thin,
but it does not bound \(R_Q(s_n)\) at the selected record endpoints. A
continuous \(L_t^1\) quantity may still have shrinking point spikes. The
creation of those spikes is again measured by the signed source law
`(LSS.35)`; no endpoint trace is silently inferred from `(LSS.45)`.

## 10. Scale-sharp true-Navier--Stokes stress test

The limitation is not an artifact of freely prescribing \(b\) and \(d\).
Take a smooth finite-energy divergence-free Navier--Stokes solution \(U\) on
a short interval on which its complete critical current is positive. Such
data can be obtained from a smooth nondegenerate three-wave Fourier packet;
replacing the datum by its negative selects the positive sign. Choose
\(0<s<t\) inside that interval so that

\[
 \mathscr W_U(s)={A\over2},
 \qquad
 \mathscr W_U(t)=A,
 \tag{LSS.46}
\]

with \(\mathscr W_U\) increasing between them. This is one exact
last-half-prefix window for the smooth history \(U\).

For \(\lambda>1\), apply the exact fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes scaling

\[
 u_\lambda(x,\tau)
 =\lambda U(\lambda x,\lambda^2\tau),
 \qquad
 p_\lambda(x,\tau)
 =\lambda^2p_U(\lambda x,\lambda^2\tau).
 \tag{LSS.47}
\]

Then

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Q_\lambda(x,\tau)&=\lambda^3Q_U(\lambda x,\lambda^2\tau),\\
 b_\lambda(\tau)&=\lambda b_U(\lambda^2\tau),\\
 d_\lambda(\tau)&=\lambda d_U(\lambda^2\tau),\\
 \mathcal P_{1/2,\lambda}(\tau)
 &=\lambda^2\mathcal P_{1/2,U}(\lambda^2\tau),\\
 R_{Q,\lambda}(\tau)&=R_{Q,U}(\lambda^2\tau),\\
 J_{Q,\lambda}(\tau)&=\lambda^2J_{Q,U}(\lambda^2\tau).
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{LSS.48}
\]

On the scaled window

\[
 I_\lambda=(\lambda^{-2}s,\lambda^{-2}t),
 \tag{LSS.49}
\]

one has

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 A_\lambda&=A,\\
 \Delta_\lambda&=\lambda^{-2}\Delta,\\
 m_\lambda&=\lambda^{-1}m\longrightarrow0,\\
 M_\lambda&=\lambda M,\\
 \int_{I_\lambda}b_\lambda^2\,d\tau
 &=\int_I b_U^2\,dt,\\
 \int_{I_\lambda}J_{Q,\lambda}\,d\tau
 &=\int_I J_{Q,U}\,dt.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{LSS.50}
\]

The high-receiver set scales exactly, so

\[
 {1\over A}
 \int_{I_\lambda\cap\{d_\lambda>M_\lambda\}}
 \mathcal P_{1/2,\lambda}\,d\tau
 ={1\over A}
 \int_{I\cap\{d_U>M\}}
 \mathcal P_{1/2,U}\,dt.
 \tag{LSS.51}
\]

Every scaled solution satisfies the complete pressure Poisson equation and
the first-binomial law `(LSS.33)`. Thus the \(L_t^1\) source mass can tend to
zero while the normalized signed coincidence, source-square action, and
first-binomial supplier work remain unchanged.

This family uses different scaled data and is not a failed one-datum history.
It is therefore an estimate stress test, not a Navier--Stokes counterexample
and not an obstruction to a same-history theorem. It proves only that
`(BRC.26)` plus formal covariance of the first-binomial equation cannot by
itself yield `(LSS.17)`: the missing information must act cumulatively along
the one fixed history.

## 11. Exact verdict

The canonical last-record failure has now been narrowed as follows:

1. the receiver action on \(I_n\) is bounded by `(LSS.11)`;
2. the signed high-receiver record forces the scale-critical complete-source
   square `(LSS.14)`;
3. at least \(A_n/8\) of signed current occurs on the viscosity-matched,
   heat-scale temporal pulse `(LSS.20)`--`(LSS.23)`;
4. the pulse either occupies an ultra-short whole record window or forces the
   first-temporal-row variation `(LSS.29)`;
5. the complete first-binomial source-energy identity converts every failed
   record into the unbounded signed supplier prefix `(LSS.38)`; and
6. a datum upper bound for that prefix would give the source square, prove
   `(TIR.37a)`, and activate the established intersection, endpoint, and
   restart implications.

The proved advance is the source-square/concentration localization and the
exact first-binomial lift of failure. By `(LSS.43a)`--`(LSS.43c)`, the
supplier-prefix condition `(LSS.39)` is an endpoint-augmented alias of the
open action `(BCR.58)`--`(BCR.59)`, not a strict reduction. The unproved
payment remains a one-history upper-prefix cancellation in `(LSS.39)`, or a
direct canonical-window source-square bound contradicting `(LSS.15)`.
This note does not claim that payment, `LastRecordSignedCoincidenceDecay.A`,
`(TIR.37a)`, or the MPP is closed.
