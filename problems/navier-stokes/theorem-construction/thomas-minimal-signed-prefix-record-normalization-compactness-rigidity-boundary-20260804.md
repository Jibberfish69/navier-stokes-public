# Thomas's minimal signed-prefix records: exact normalization, compactness, and rigidity boundary

Status: proved contrapositive normalization and weak compactness reduction
inside Thomas Birnie's one-fluid, pressure-explicit all-orders binomial/VPI
method.  The exact current-retention and same-history rigidity statements are
isolated below.  They are not consequences of the coordinatewise existence of
the mixed tower, and this note does not prove `(TIR.37a)` or close the MPP.

The physical object remains the one smooth fixed-viscosity fluid history
generated by \(u_0\).  The windows below are cut from that one history in their
actual order.  Rescaling a window changes its observer coordinates and its
displayed viscosity, but it does not reinitialize the fluid, prescribe a new
source, or replace the original history by an Euler solution.  Pressure is
retained through its Poisson equation at every normalized rung.

The construction covers the exact failure of `(TIR.37a)`, including the case
where the critical stock stays below the accumulated critical action.  It does
not assume in advance that critical-height records diverge.

## 1. Canonical last-half-prefix windows from one signed history

Put

\[
 H(t):=\frac12\|\Lambda^{1/2}u(t)\|_2^2,
 \qquad
 D(t):=\|\Lambda^{3/2}u(t)\|_2^2,
 \qquad
 \mathscr W(t):=\int_0^t\mathcal P_{1/2}(s)\,ds.
 \tag{MRN.1}
\]

The exact critical balance is

\[
\boxed{
 \mathscr W(t)=H(t)-H(0)+\nu\int_0^tD(s)\,ds.}
 \tag{MRN.2}
\]

Assume the exact negation of `(TIR.37a)`:

\[
 \sup_{t<T_*}\mathscr W(t)=\infty.
 \tag{MRN.3}
\]

Choose \(A_0>0\) and \(A_n:=4^nA_0\).  By continuity, define

\[
 t_n:=\inf\{t<T_*:\mathscr W(t)=A_n\},
 \qquad
 s_n:=\sup\{t<t_n:\mathscr W(t)=A_n/2\}.
 \tag{MRN.4}
\]

Then

\[
 \mathscr W(t)\in[A_n/2,A_n]
 \quad(s_n\leq t\leq t_n),
 \qquad
 \int_{s_n}^{t_n}\mathcal P_{1/2}(t)\,dt=A_n/2.
 \tag{MRN.5}
\]

The intervals \(I_n:=(s_n,t_n)\) are ordered and disjoint.  Indeed, the first
time at which \(\mathscr W=A_{n-1}=A_n/4\) precedes the last time at which
\(\mathscr W=A_n/2\), so

\[
 t_{n-1}<s_n<t_n.
 \tag{MRN.6}
\]

Thus no event is pasted at a new scale and no cost is counted twice.  These
are the actual last-half-record windows of one signed prefix.

Equation `(MRN.2)` and the upper record property give, throughout \(I_n\),

\[
 0\leq H(t)\leq H(0)+\mathscr W(t)\leq H(0)+A_n.
 \tag{MRN.7}
\]

This is the base critical bound available before any compactness argument.

## 2. One exact unit-window normalization

Write

\[
 \Delta_n:=t_n-s_n,
 \qquad
 \ell_n^2:=\Delta_n\sqrt{A_n},
 \qquad
 \varepsilon_n:={\nu\over\sqrt{A_n}}\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{MRN.8}
\]

For arbitrary spatial centers \(c_n\), define on \(-1\leq\tau\leq0\)

\[
\begin{aligned}
 v_n(y,\tau)
 &:={\ell_n\over\sqrt{A_n}}
 u\!\left(c_n+\ell_ny,
 s_n+\Delta_n(\tau+1)\right),\\
 \pi_n(y,\tau)
 &:={\ell_n^2\over A_n}
 p\!\left(c_n+\ell_ny,
 s_n+\Delta_n(\tau+1)\right).
\end{aligned}
\tag{MRN.9}
\]

The identity \(\Delta_n=(\ell_n/\sqrt{A_n})\ell_n\) makes the transport
coefficient exactly one.  Direct substitution, with no term separated from
the fluid, gives

\[
\boxed{
 \partial_\tau v_n+(v_n\cdot\nabla)v_n+\nabla\pi_n
 =\varepsilon_n\Delta v_n,
 \qquad \nabla\cdot v_n=0,}
 \tag{MRN.10}
\]

and pressure remains fixed by

\[
 -\Delta\pi_n=\partial_i\partial_j((v_n)_i(v_n)_j).
 \tag{MRN.11}
\]

Let \(H_n,D_n^{\rm c},\mathcal P_n\) be the critical height, critical
viscous receiver, and complete signed current of \(v_n\).  The scaling is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 H_n(\tau)&={1\over A_n}H(t),\\
 D_n^{\rm c}(\tau)&={\Delta_n\over\sqrt{A_n}}D(t),\\
 \mathcal P_n(\tau)&={\Delta_n\over A_n}\mathcal P_{1/2}(t),
 \qquad t=s_n+\Delta_n(\tau+1).
\end{aligned}
\tag{MRN.12}
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
 \sup_{-1\leq\tau\leq0}H_n(\tau)
 \leq1+{H(0)\over A_n},
 \qquad
 \int_{-1}^0\mathcal P_n(\tau)\,d\tau={1\over2}.}
 \tag{MRN.13}
\]

More strongly, the last-record property itself is retained:

\[
 0\leq\int_{-1}^{\tau}\mathcal P_n(r)\,dr\leq{1\over2}
 \qquad(-1\leq\tau\leq0).
 \tag{MRN.14}
\]

The normalized critical balance reads

\[
 {1\over2}
 =H_n(0)-H_n(-1)
 +\varepsilon_n\int_{-1}^0D_n^{\rm c}(\tau)\,d\tau.
 \tag{MRN.15}
\]

Therefore the critical viscous defect measures

\[
 d\mathfrak d_n(\tau)
 :=\varepsilon_nD_n^{\rm c}(\tau)\,d\tau
 \tag{MRN.16}
\]

have the uniform mass bound

\[
 0\leq\mathfrak d_n([-1,0])
 \leq{3\over2}+{H(0)\over A_n}.
 \tag{MRN.17}
\]

This normalization covers both exact failure faces:

- if a positive fraction of \(A_n\) appears in \(H\), a critical-stock
  profile can survive;
- if \(H/A_n\to0\) on the window, `(MRN.15)` forces the unit record into a
  nonzero limiting critical viscous defect.

Neither face may be discarded.

## 3. The complete pressure-explicit tower survives the change of coordinates

For the original mixed jets

\[
 J_{N,\alpha}=\partial_t^N\partial_x^\alpha u,
 \qquad
 \Pi_{N,\alpha}=\partial_t^N\partial_x^\alpha p,
 \tag{MRN.18}
\]

the normalized jets are exactly

\[
\begin{aligned}
 J^{(n)}_{N,\alpha}
 &= {\ell_n\over\sqrt{A_n}}
    \Delta_n^N\ell_n^{|\alpha|}
    J_{N,\alpha},\\
 \Pi^{(n)}_{N,\alpha}
 &= {\ell_n^2\over A_n}
    \Delta_n^N\ell_n^{|\alpha|}
    \Pi_{N,\alpha}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{MRN.19}
\]

Substitution into the primitive recurrence gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
J^{(n)}_{N+1,\alpha}
&+\sum_{a=0}^{N}\sum_{\beta\leq\alpha}
 \binom Na\binom\alpha\beta
 (J^{(n)}_{a,\beta}\cdot\nabla)
 J^{(n)}_{N-a,\alpha-\beta}
 +\nabla\Pi^{(n)}_{N,\alpha}\\
&=\varepsilon_n\Delta J^{(n)}_{N,\alpha},
\qquad \nabla\cdot J^{(n)}_{N,\alpha}=0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{MRN.20}
\]

Every nonlinear parent and the simultaneous pressure completion remains in
`(MRN.20)`.  The coordinatewise existence of all these smooth jets for each
fixed \(n\) does not give a bound uniform in \(n\), derivative order, or
record level.  In particular it supplies neither a common time-Taylor radius
on \([-1,0]\) nor compactness of the full mixed tower as
\(n\to\infty\).  Importing either would assume the endpoint-spanning
intersection that `(TIR.37a)` is meant to generate.

## 4. What compactness is already paid

The first inequality in `(MRN.13)` gives

\[
 \sup_n\|v_n\|_{L_\tau^\infty\dot H_y^{1/2}(-1,0)}<\infty,
 \qquad
 \sup_n\|v_n\|_{L_\tau^\infty L_y^3(-1,0)}<\infty.
 \tag{MRN.21}
\]

With

\[
 Q(v):=-(v\cdot\nabla)v-\nabla\pi
 =-\mathbb P((v\cdot\nabla)v),
 \tag{MRN.22}
\]

duality against \(\dot H^{3/2}\), using
\(\dot H^{1/2}\hookrightarrow L^3\), gives

\[
\boxed{
 \|Q(v_n)\|_{\dot H^{-3/2}}
 \leq C\|v_n\|_{\dot H^{1/2}}^2.}
 \tag{MRN.23}
\]

Also

\[
 \|\varepsilon_n\Delta v_n\|_{\dot H^{-3/2}}
 =\varepsilon_n\|v_n\|_{\dot H^{1/2}}.
 \tag{MRN.24}
\]

Thus `(MRN.10)` yields

\[
 \sup_n\|\partial_\tau v_n\|_{L_\tau^\infty\dot H_y^{-3/2}}
 <\infty.
 \tag{MRN.25}
\]

Local Rellich compactness and time interpolation now prove, after a
subsequence,

\[
\begin{aligned}
 v_n&\stackrel{*}{\rightharpoonup}v
 &&\text{in }L^\infty(-1,0;\dot H^{1/2}),\\
 v_n&\longrightarrow v
 &&\text{in }C([-1,0];H^{-1}_{\rm loc})
   \cap L^2((-1,0)\times K)
   \quad\text{for every compact }K,\\
 \pi_n&\rightharpoonup\pi
 &&\text{locally in }L^{3/2},\\
 d\mathfrak d_n&\stackrel{*}{\rightharpoonup}d\mathfrak d
 &&\text{as finite positive measures on }[-1,0].
\end{aligned}
\tag{MRN.26}

The pressure bound follows from `(MRN.11)` and the Riesz-transform estimate
\(\|\pi_n\|_{3/2}\leq C\|v_n\|_3^2\).  Since
\(\varepsilon_n\Delta v_n\to0\) in \(L^\infty\dot H^{-3/2}\), the limit
satisfies

\[
 \partial_\tau v+(v\cdot\nabla)v+\nabla\pi=0,
 \qquad \nabla\cdot v=0
 \tag{MRN.27}
\]

in distributions.  Equation `(MRN.27)` is only the weak local shadow of the
one fixed-\(\nu\) history.  It is not authority to switch the proof to an
Euler program.

## 5. What the paid compactness does not retain

The topology `(MRN.26)` is insufficient at exactly the critical record.

1. **Spatial/frequency nonvanishing.** A bounded \(\dot H^{1/2}\) sequence
   can split among more and more separated pressure-coupled pieces or escape
   in scale.  Translations \(c_n\) alone need not produce a nonzero local
   limit.  Even the two normalizations
   \(H_n(0)=D_n^{\rm c}(0)=1\) used in `(RAM.7)` do not exclude a many-packet
   vanishing sequence.

2. **Critical-current continuity.** The complete current pairs a
   pressure-complete source with the \(\dot H^{3/2}\) receiver.  The bounds
   `(MRN.21)`--`(MRN.25)` control the source only in
   \(\dot H^{-3/2}\), not in the topology needed to pass
   \(\mathcal P_n\).  Therefore

   \[
    \int_{-1}^0\mathcal P_n\,d\tau={1\over2}
   \tag{MRN.28}
   \]

   need not pass to \(v\).  It may survive as spatial escape, frequency
   escape, multiplicity, temporal concentration, or the positive measure
   \(d\mathfrak d\).

3. **Temporal trace of the signed primitive.** Equation `(MRN.14)` bounds the
   primitive in \(L^\infty\), but it does not bound its variation.  The
   all-orders tower gives every derivative preterminally, not a uniform
   derivative bound on these normalized windows.  Oscillation can therefore
   lose the endpoint value under weak-star convergence.

4. **Material ancestry.** Every \(v_n\) comes from the same smooth material
   flow, but `(MRN.21)` gives no uniform deformation-gradient or label-map
   compactness.  The weak limit does not identify whether its visible core is
   carried by one fixed positive label cohort or recruited from changing
   labels.

5. **Pressure/current no-loss.** Pressure itself has the weak limit in
   `(MRN.26)`, but the nonlocal critical pressure-current pairing is not weakly
   continuous.  A compact local velocity limit does not prove that the
   far-field pressure parents or their reconvergence have disappeared.

These are compactness defects, not hypothetical Navier--Stokes failure
histories.  The construction above proves only that the currently paid bounds
do not remove them.

## 6. A sharper same-history reduction from the datum-finite source mass

The pressure-complete source has the independently datum-paid mass from
`(BRC.26)`.  Put

\[
 b(t):=\|(u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}},
 \qquad
 d(t):=\|u(t)\|_{\dot H^{3/2}}.
 \tag{MRN.29}
\]

Then

\[
 [\mathcal P_{1/2}(t)]_+\leq b(t)d(t),
 \qquad
 \int_0^{T_*}b(t)\,dt
 \leq{C_{\rm NS}\|u_0\|_2^2\over2\nu}.
 \tag{MRN.30}
\]

Because the record windows are disjoint, their source masses

\[
 m_n:=\int_{I_n}b(t)\,dt
 \tag{MRN.31}
\]

satisfy

\[
 \sum_nm_n\leq{C_{\rm NS}\|u_0\|_2^2\over2\nu},
 \qquad m_n\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{MRN.32}
\]

No \(m_n\) can vanish, because `(MRN.5)` and `(MRN.30)` give

\[
 {A_n\over2}
 \leq\int_{I_n}b(t)d(t)\,dt.
 \tag{MRN.33}
\]

Define the intrinsic last-record receiver threshold

\[
 M_n:={A_n\over4m_n}\longrightarrow\infty.
 \tag{MRN.34}
\]

The low-receiver part is already paid:

\[
 \int_{I_n\cap\{d\leq M_n\}}\mathcal P_{1/2}(t)\,dt
 \leq M_nm_n={A_n\over4}.
 \tag{MRN.35}
\]

Subtracting `(MRN.35)` from the exact signed record `(MRN.5)` proves the
pressure-complete signed coincidence lower bound

\[
\boxed{
 {1\over A_n}
 \int_{I_n\cap\{d>M_n\}}
 \mathcal P_{1/2}(t)\,dt
 \geq{1\over4},
 \qquad M_n\to\infty.}
 \tag{MRN.36}
\]

This is stronger than saying that a positive majorant is large.  The complete
signed current itself remains positive after the low receiver is removed.
Failure of `(TIR.37a)` therefore forces infinitely many disjoint, same-history
last-record windows whose datum-finite pressure-complete source mass tends to
zero while a nonzero normalized signed current remains coincident with an
unbounded critical receiver.

## 7. Exact compactness and rigidity statements needed

The compactness route would close only if it proves all of the following for
the canonical sequence `(MRN.4)`--`(MRN.14)`.

### 7.1 Current-and-ancestry compactness

\[
\begin{aligned}
\texttt{MinimalRecordCurrentAncestryCompactness.A}:\quad
&\text{after centers, a subsequence, and any further lawful critical}\
&\text{profile extraction, the normalized complete pressure current,}\
&\text{the viscous defect, and the actual material-label ancestry are}\
&\text{retained with no spatial, frequency, temporal, pressure-tail,}\
&\text{or multiplicity loss, and the retained total record is }1/2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{MRN.37}
\]

The weak compactness `(MRN.26)` proves the velocity/pressure distributional
part of `(MRN.37)`.  It does not prove nonvanishing, current continuity, or
material-label retention.

### 7.2 Same-history rigidity

After `(MRN.37)`, the physical alternatives must be exhaustive:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\texttt{MinimalRecordSameHistoryRigidity.A}:\quad
&\text{every retained unit record either}\
&\quad\text{(P) belongs through the record sequence to one fixed}\
&\qquad\text{positive-volume material cohort, or}\
&\quad\text{(R) is recruited through the complete pressure-explicit source;}\
&\text{(P) produces the contraction/escape alternative of `(BRC.23)`,}\
&\text{while (R) obeys the signed coincidence decay `(MRN.39)` below.}
\end{aligned}
\tag{MRN.38}
\]

The persistent conclusion is already contradictory once its localization is
proved: `(BRC.14)` and `(BRC.18)` exclude infinite finite-time contraction or
escape of a fixed positive-volume cohort.  The recruitment conclusion reduces
to one narrower signed statement on the canonical record windows:

\[
\boxed{
 \texttt{LastRecordSignedCoincidenceDecay.A}:\qquad
 \lim_{n\to\infty}{1\over A_n}
 \int_{I_n\cap\{d>A_n/(4m_n)\}}
 \mathcal P_{1/2}(t)\,dt=0.}
 \tag{MRN.39}
\]

Equation `(MRN.39)` is not a generic regularity criterion, a positive
all-orders norm, or the desired prefix rewritten verbatim.  Its low-receiver
complement and its source mass are already paid by `(MRN.30)`--`(MRN.35)`;
it concerns only the pressure-complete signed source/receiver coincidence on
the disjoint last-record windows canonically generated by a failure.  Yet
`(MRN.36)` shows that `(MRN.39)` contradicts every failure of `(TIR.37a)`.

## 8. Exact verdict

The contrapositive/minimal-record route produces three proved advances:

1. every divergent signed prefix produces disjoint last-half-record windows
   and the unit normalization `(MRN.8)`--`(MRN.15)`, covering both stock and
   action divergence;
2. the pressure-explicit all-orders recurrence survives exactly as
   `(MRN.20)`, while the base critical row supplies the weak compactness
   `(MRN.21)`--`(MRN.27)`;
3. the datum-finite source mass discharges the entire low-receiver part and
   forces the sharp signed high-receiver coincidence `(MRN.36)`.

The all-orders intersection/binomial structure currently does **not** prove
`(MRN.37)`: its rows are finite separately before \(T_*\), but no normalized
uniform row bounds retain the current, common Taylor radius, pressure tail, or
material ancestry.  Weak compactness can yield a zero Euler shadow while the
unit record survives in the critical viscous/current defect.  Euler rigidity
therefore cannot be asserted from `(MRN.27)`.

The genuinely narrowed in-method theorem is `(MRN.39)`, joined with the
persistent/recruited ancestry alternative in `(MRN.38)`.  Proving that signed
coincidence decay from the complete pressure-explicit parent tree would
contradict `(MRN.36)` and close `(TIR.37a)`.  It remains unproved here; no
authority, compactness theorem, or row estimate currently inspected supplies
it.
