# Thomas's one-fluid high-pass prefix and first-moment reduction

Status: exact theorem-construction reduction inside Thomas's one-fluid
reversible-intersection method. This note proves the high-pass identities,
the datum-paid initial-tail bounds, and the causal Duhamel factorization below.
It does **not** prove the datum-finite first moment, the higher-response theorem
`(TIR.36)`, the endpoint-spanning intersection, or global regularity.

The governing source is
`thomas-one-fluid-reversible-intersection-proof-spine-20260803.md`, especially
`(TIR.41)`--`(TIR.43)`. All fields below are readouts of the one
fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes history generated by \(u_0\). The heat descendant
and the Duhamel-created field are an exact decomposition of that history, not
independent fluid participants.

## 1. Conventions and the complete high-pass current

Use the unitary Fourier transform on \(\mathbb R^3\), put
\(\Lambda=(-\Delta)^{1/2}\), and let \(P_{>K}\) be the sharp radial Fourier
projection to \(|\xi|>K\). Define

\[
E_s(t):=\frac12\|\Lambda^su(t)\|_2^2,
\qquad
E_{s,>K}(t):=\frac12\|\Lambda^sP_{>K}u(t)\|_2^2.
\tag{HPF.1}
\]

Retain the full nonlinear and simultaneous pressure response

\[
Q:=-(u\cdot\nabla)u-\nabla p
=-\mathbb P((u\cdot\nabla)u),
\qquad
u_t+\nu\Lambda^2u=Q.
\tag{HPF.2}
\]

The second expression in `(HPF.2)` is the equivalent Leray readout of the
first; it does not remove pressure from the governing history. Set

\[
\Phi_K(t):=(P_{>K}u(t),P_{>K}Q(t))_2,
\qquad
F_K(T):=\int_0^T\Phi_K(t)\,dt.
\tag{HPF.3}
\]

Since \(P_{>K}\), \(\Lambda\), and the Leray projection commute, direct
pairing of `(HPF.2)` with \(P_{>K}u\) gives

\[
\boxed{
\frac d{dt}E_{0,>K}(t)+2\nu E_{1,>K}(t)=\Phi_K(t).
}
\tag{HPF.4}
\]

The factor \(2\nu\) in `(HPF.4)` follows from
\(E_{1,>K}=\frac12\|\Lambda P_{>K}u\|_2^2\).

## 2. The exact positive history measure and the datum-paid negative tail

Define the positive Fourier energy measure

\[
d\varepsilon_t(\xi):=\frac12|\widehat u(t,\xi)|^2\,d\xi
\tag{HPF.5}
\]

and, for each terminal prefix \(T<T_*\), the positive terminal-plus-dissipated
history measure

\[
d\Gamma_T(\xi)
:=d\varepsilon_T(\xi)
+2\nu\int_0^T|\xi|^2\,d\varepsilon_t(\xi)\,dt.
\tag{HPF.6}
\]

Integrating `(HPF.4)` gives the exact tail identity

\[
\boxed{
F_K(T)
=\Gamma_T\{\,|\xi|>K\,\}
-\varepsilon_0\{\,|\xi|>K\,\}.
}
\tag{HPF.7}
\]

Incompressibility and the complete nonlinear-pressure cancellation in the
kinetic-energy pairing give

\[
\Gamma_T(\mathbb R^3)
=E_0(T)+2\nu\int_0^TE_1(t)\,dt
=E_0(0).
\tag{HPF.8}
\]

Consequently

\[
-E_{0,>K}(0)
\leq F_K(T)
\leq E_0(0)-E_{0,>K}(0).
\tag{HPF.9}
\]

The negative cutoff tail is therefore paid entirely by the datum:

\[
F_K(T)^-\leq E_{0,>K}(0),
\qquad
\boxed{
\int_0^\infty F_K(T)^-\,dK\leq E_{1/2}(0).
}
\tag{HPF.10}
\]

More generally, for every \(\alpha>0\),

\[
\alpha\int_0^\infty K^{\alpha-1}F_K(T)^-\,dK
\leq E_{\alpha/2}(0).
\tag{HPF.11}
\]

Thus divergence of the signed first cutoff moment cannot be hidden by an
unpaid negative span of thresholds.

## 3. Weighted layer cake reconstructs every spatial row

For every \(\alpha>0\), Tonelli's theorem applied to the positive measures in
`(HPF.5)`--`(HPF.6)` gives

\[
\boxed{
\alpha\int_0^\infty K^{\alpha-1}F_K(T)\,dK
=E_{\alpha/2}(T)-E_{\alpha/2}(0)
+2\nu\int_0^TE_{1+\alpha/2}(t)\,dt.
}
\tag{HPF.12}
\]

At \(\alpha=1\), `(HPF.12)` is `(TIR.43)`:

\[
\int_0^\infty F_K(T)\,dK
=E_{1/2}(T)-E_{1/2}(0)
+2\nu\int_0^TE_{3/2}(t)\,dt.
\tag{HPF.13}
\]

Put

\[
G_K(T):=F_K(T)+E_{0,>K}(0)
=\Gamma_T\{\,|\xi|>K\,\}\geq0.
\tag{HPF.14}
\]

Then the positive weighted identity is

\[
\alpha\int_0^\infty K^{\alpha-1}G_K(T)\,dK
=E_{\alpha/2}(T)
+2\nu\int_0^TE_{1+\alpha/2}(t)\,dt.
\tag{HPF.15}
\]

For \(\alpha>1\), it follows that

\[
\int_R^\infty G_K(T)\,dK
\leq
R^{1-\alpha}
\left(
E_{\alpha/2}(T)
+2\nu\int_0^TE_{1+\alpha/2}(t)\,dt
\right).
\tag{HPF.16}
\]

Equation `(HPF.16)` is a tail formula, not a datum-finite tail theorem. A
uniform bound for its parenthesis is exactly an evolved higher Sobolev stock
and action. Importing that bound would assume part of the all-orders
intersection that Thomas's argument is constructing.

The same point appears under integration by parts in the cutoff. If \(\gamma_T\)
is the radial pushforward of \(\Gamma_T\), and \(\gamma_0\) is the radial
pushforward of \(\varepsilon_0=\Gamma_0\), then for finite \(R\)

\[
\int_0^R F_K(T)\,dK
=\int_0^\infty\min(r,R)\,d(\gamma_T-\gamma_0)(r),
\tag{HPF.17}
\]

and, whenever the displayed first moments are finite,

\[
\int_R^\infty F_K(T)\,dK
=\int_0^\infty(r-R)_+\,d(\gamma_T-\gamma_0)(r).
\tag{HPF.18}
\]

These are exact rearrangements of the same first moment; they create no new
datum resource.

## 4. What the heat lifetime pays

By `(HPF.8)` and `(HPF.14)`,

\[
0\leq G_K(T)\leq E_0(0).
\tag{HPF.19}
\]

Since \(E_{1,>K}\geq K^2E_{0,>K}\), `(HPF.6)` also gives

\[
\boxed{
\int_0^TE_{0,>K}(t)\,dt
\leq\frac{E_0(0)}{2\nu K^2}.
}
\tag{HPF.20}
\]

Thus the heat lifetime pays time occupancy of a fixed high-pass field. It does
not make \(G_K(T)\) decay in \(K\) at the terminal prefix. A narrow-in-time
high-frequency response is exactly the branch on which the joined nonlinear
parent response must be used.

For every fixed \(T<T_*\) and every integer \(m\geq1\), preterminal
smoothness gives

\[
0\leq G_K(T)
\leq K^{-2m}
\left(
E_m(T)+2\nu\int_0^TE_{m+1}(t)\,dt
\right).
\tag{HPF.21}
\]

This proves rapid decay for each fixed preterminal time. Uniformity as
\(T\uparrow T_*\) requires uniform control of the bracket in `(HPF.21)`, which
is again the endpoint-spanning spatial column.

## 5. The exact one-history Duhamel action

Let

\[
S(t):=e^{-\nu t\Lambda^2},
\qquad
v(t):=S(t)u_0,
\qquad
w(t):=u(t)-v(t)=\int_0^tS(t-s)Q(s)\,ds.
\tag{HPF.22}
\]

The field \(w\) is the part of the same velocity history created after time
zero by its complete nonlinear-pressure response. For

\[
M_K:=(\Lambda-K)_+,
\qquad
\mathscr R_K(T):=\int_K^\infty F_L(T)\,dL,
\tag{HPF.23}
\]

layer cake gives

\[
\mathscr R_K(T)
=\int_0^T(M_Ku(t),Q(t))_2\,dt.
\tag{HPF.24}
\]

Splitting \(u=v+w\) and using
\(Q=w_t+\nu\Lambda^2w\) gives

\[
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\mathscr R_K(T)
={}&I_{K,0}(T)+\mathcal K_{K,T},
\\
I_{K,0}(T)
:={}&\int_0^T(M_KS(t)u_0,Q(t))_2\,dt,
\\
\mathcal K_{K,T}
:={}&\frac12\|M_K^{1/2}w(T)\|_2^2
+\nu\int_0^T
\|\Lambda M_K^{1/2}w(t)\|_2^2\,dt
\geq0.
\end{aligned}
}
\tag{HPF.25}
\]

The positive action in `(HPF.25)` is obtained from the exact signed pairing

\[
(M_Kw,Q)
=(M_Kw,w_t+\nu\Lambda^2w),
\tag{HPF.26}
\]

not by replacing the complete current with its absolute value.

### Old-data assumption and bound

Assume \(u_0\in L^2\) and \(\widehat u_0\in L^1\). The latter follows, for
example, from \(u_0\in H^\sigma(\mathbb R^3)\) for any \(\sigma>3/2\), and
therefore holds for the smooth finite-energy datum in the governing problem.
Because \(M_KS(t)u_0\) is divergence-free,

\[
|(M_KS(t)u_0,Q(t))|
\leq\|u(t)\|_2^2
\|\nabla M_KS(t)u_0\|_\infty.
\tag{HPF.27}
\]

The Fourier multiplier gives, uniformly in \(K\geq0\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^\infty
\|\nabla M_KS(t)u_0\|_\infty\,dt
&\leq C\int_{\mathbb R^3}
|\widehat u_0(\xi)|
|\xi|(|\xi|-K)_+
\int_0^\infty e^{-\nu t|\xi|^2}\,dt\,d\xi
\\
&\leq\frac C\nu\|\widehat u_0\|_1.
\end{aligned}
\tag{HPF.28}
\]

The kinetic-energy identity then yields

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{K\geq0,\,T<T_*}|I_{K,0}(T)|
\leq
\frac C\nu\|u_0\|_2^2\|\widehat u_0\|_1.
}
\tag{HPF.29}
\]

All possible divergence of the signed high-pass prefix is therefore confined
to the same-history action \(\mathcal K_{K,T}\).

## 6. Heat-time coherence pays mass, not the first moment

Put \(g:=\Lambda^{-1}Q\), defining its zero mode as zero, extend it by zero
outside \((0,T)\), and use
\(\widehat h(\omega)=\int_{\mathbb R}e^{-it\omega}h(t)\,dt\). Define

\[
d\mu_T(\xi,\omega)
:=\frac1{2\pi}
\frac{\nu|\xi|^4}{\nu^2|\xi|^4+\omega^2}
\left|
\widehat{\mathbf1_{(0,T)}g}(\omega,\xi)
\right|^2
\,d\xi\,d\omega.
\tag{HPF.30}
\]

The causal heat kernel is positive definite in time, and exact Fourier
factorization gives

\[
\boxed{
\mathcal K_{K,T}
=\iint(|\xi|-K)_+\,d\mu_T.
}
\tag{HPF.31}
\]

One spatial moment lower,

\[
\iint d\mu_T
=\frac12\|w(T)\|_2^2
+\nu\int_0^T\|\Lambda w(t)\|_2^2\,dt
\leq2\|u_0\|_2^2.
\tag{HPF.32}
\]

Thus energy pays the mass of \(d\mu_T\) and every fixed bounded-frequency
piece of its first moment. It does not pay

\[
\mathcal K_{0,T}=\iint|\xi|\,d\mu_T.
\tag{HPF.33}
\]

The heat-time multiplier in `(HPF.30)` suppresses temporal frequencies
\(|\omega|\gg\nu|\xi|^2\). On the coherent region
\(|\omega|\lesssim\nu|\xi|^2\), it is comparable to \(1/\nu\) and supplies no
spatial decay. For the genuine source,

\[
Q=(\partial_t+\nu\Lambda^2)w,
\tag{HPF.34}
\]

so the interior factor
\(|i\omega+\nu|\xi|^2|^2\) cancels the denominator in `(HPF.30)`; the terminal
boundary supplies the stock in `(HPF.25)`. Heat-time coherence therefore
identifies the exact same-history action but does not contribute the missing
half spatial derivative.

## 7. All temporal responses do not supply uniform cutoff decay by themselves

At a fixed nonzero Fourier frequency \(r=|\xi|>0\), put \(a=\nu r^2\). Repeated
integration by parts in the exact Duhamel formula gives, for every integer
\(N\geq1\),

\[
\begin{aligned}
\widehat w(T,\xi)
={}&
\sum_{j=0}^{N-1}
\frac{(-1)^j}{a^{j+1}}
\left[
\partial_t^j\widehat Q(T,\xi)
-e^{-aT}\partial_t^j\widehat Q(0,\xi)
\right]
\\
&+
\frac{(-1)^N}{a^N}
\int_0^Te^{-a(T-t)}
\partial_t^N\widehat Q(t,\xi)\,dt.
\end{aligned}
\tag{HPF.35}
\]

The initial boundary jets in `(HPF.35)` are generated from \(u_0\) by the
pressure-explicit binomial recurrence and are datum-finite. Every attempted
additional heat factor also introduces a terminal \(Q\)-jet and a remaining
differentiated full-source history. Uniformly bounding those terms is the
mixed temporal-spatial intersection in another coordinate. The formal
existence of every temporal response at each \(T<T_*\) therefore gives
preterminal cutoff decay, not uniform decay through \(T_*\).

## 8. The smallest signed tail estimate is closure-equivalent

Fix any \(K_0>0\). The smallest all-high signed estimate at this interface is

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{T<T_*}
\mathscr R_{K_0}(T)
=
\sup_{T<T_*}
\int_0^T\int_{K_0}^\infty
\Phi_K(t)\,dK\,dt
<\infty.
}
\tag{HPF.36}
\]

The low cutoff span is already bounded above by \(K_0E_0(0)\) and below by
\(-E_{1/2}(0)\), using `(HPF.9)`--`(HPF.10)`. Hence `(HPF.36)` is equivalent
to a uniform upper bound for the complete signed prefix in `(HPF.13)`. By
`(HPF.25)` and `(HPF.29)`, it is also equivalent to

\[
\sup_{T<T_*}\mathcal K_{K_0,T}<\infty.
\tag{HPF.37}
\]

Restoring the energy-paid low frequencies and the smooth heat descendant,
`(HPF.37)` is equivalent to the critical stock and action bound

\[
\sup_{T<T_*}E_{1/2}(T)
+2\nu\int_0^{T_*}E_{3/2}(t)\,dt
<\infty.
\tag{HPF.38}
\]

Indeed, `(HPF.13)` gives both directions directly. Therefore `(HPF.36)` is an
exact closure estimate, not a smaller theorem already proved from the datum.
The identities above isolate where a one-history pressure-complete argument
must act; they do not close `(TIR.36)`.

## 9. Structurally local sufficient target: pressure-complete shell contraction

Fix \(q>1\), write \(\lambda_j=q^j\), and form shell masses from the exact
measure `(HPF.30)`:

\[
\alpha_j(T)
:=\mu_T\{\lambda_j\leq|\xi|<\lambda_{j+1}\},
\qquad
A_j(T):=\sum_{p\geq j}\alpha_p(T),
\qquad
b_j(T):=\lambda_jA_j(T).
\tag{HPF.39}
\]

Finite Abel summation gives

\[
\sum_{p=J}^N\lambda_p\alpha_p
=\lambda_JA_J
+(1-q^{-1})\sum_{p=J+1}^N\lambda_pA_p
-\lambda_NA_{N+1}.
\tag{HPF.40}
\]

Thus the first moment is paid if the Dini tail
\(\sum_{j\geq J}b_j(T)\) is bounded uniformly in \(T\). A structurally local
sufficient target is to derive, from the joined signed full-pressure parent
interactions, constants \(\varepsilon\in(0,1)\) and residuals \(r_j(T)\geq0\)
such that

\[
\boxed{
b_{j+1}(T)
\leq(1-\varepsilon)b_j(T)+r_j(T),
\qquad
\sup_{T<T_*}\sum_{j\geq J}r_j(T)
\leq C(u_0,\nu).
}
\tag{HPF.41}
\]

Summing `(HPF.41)` gives

\[
\varepsilon\sum_{j=J+1}^{N}b_j(T)+b_{N+1}(T)
\leq(1-\varepsilon)b_J(T)+\sum_{j=J}^{N}r_j(T),
\tag{HPF.42}
\]

and `(HPF.32)` pays \(b_J\leq2\lambda_J\|u_0\|_2^2\). Equations
`(HPF.40)`--`(HPF.42)` would then prove `(HPF.37)`.

For `(HPF.41)` to be noncircular, \(r_j\) must be derived from an independently
datum-finite signed action of the complete pressure-coupled parent field. It
may not be defined from the desired Dini tail, from an absolute flux sum, or
from an already assumed mixed Sobolev row. Establishing that independently
paid contraction is the next theorem target. It is not proved in this note.
