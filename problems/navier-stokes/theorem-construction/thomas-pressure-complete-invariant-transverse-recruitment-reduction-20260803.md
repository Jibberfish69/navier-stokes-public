\[

\[

\[
\boxed{

\tag{ITR.3}

\[
\widehat Q(k)
=-\frac{ic_{\mathcal F}}2\mathbb P_k

\left[
(\widehat u(p)\cdot q)\widehat u(q)

\tag{ITR.4}

The first complete critical response is

## 3. Exact Gram formula and the null configurations

Write

\[

&Z&:=\|\Lambda u\|_2^2,
&D&:=\|\Lambda^{3/2}u\|_2^2,\\

\end{aligned}
\tag{ITR.16}
\]

\[

\begin{pmatrix}R&J_2\\J_2&D\end{pmatrix},
\qquad

\Delta_u:=RD-J_2^2\ge0.

\[

For \(\Delta_u=0\), the quotient in (ITR.18) is not used. The all-rank

\[

\right\|_2^2 .}

Let \(u=u_++u_-\), where \(\mathsf Su_\sigma=\sigma u_\sigma\). Equality
\(\delta(u)=0\) means that for some \(\alpha,\beta\),

\[
\bigl((1-\beta\sigma)|\xi|-\alpha\bigr)

Consequently:
