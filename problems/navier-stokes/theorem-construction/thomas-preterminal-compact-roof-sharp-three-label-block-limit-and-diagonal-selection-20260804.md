# Thomas's preterminal compact-roof sharp three-label block limit and diagonal selection

**Date:** 2026-08-04

**Status:** scoped exact preterminal block-limit theorem and compact-cutoff
diagonal-selection theorem inside Thomas Birnie's one-fluid,
pressure-explicit binomial/VPI reversible-intersection method. This note does
not alter the direct theorem authority and proves no datum estimate.

This note answers only the sharp-limit question left in (TPR.15a). The
physical object is still the one smooth fixed-viscosity fluid generated by one
datum. The current leg, the two recent legs, the six receiver assignments,
the contracting-line projector, and the material selector are simultaneous
readouts of that same history.

There is a decisive distinction between the untruncated critical VPI kernel
and the canonical failed-record receiver. On each fixed preterminal record,
the dynamic FIM roof bounds every retained frequency from above, while the
minimum-leg and high-output floors exclude zero. The order-two receiver
multiplier is therefore bounded on a compact frequency set. Multiplication
by a sharp selector creates arbitrarily high frequencies in an individual
leg, but the target multiplier projects them back onto that compact set.
Consequently every one of the eight reality-completed blocks has a lawful
Fourier-multiplier limit, belongs to \(L^1\) on that record, and still
recombines exactly. No BV hypothesis is needed for this undifferentiated
record statement.

BV is still required to pass the separated derivative responses of an
individual block. The value theorem below does not bound the variation of a
sharp membership selector, the crossing variation of the moving Fourier
gate, or the selected-block terms containing \(D_tW\). Those are different
claims and remain separate.

## 1. The fixed-record multiplier has a compact roof

Fix one canonical record

\[
 I_n=(s_n,t_n),\qquad \overline I_n\subset[0,T_*),
 \tag{PSL.1}
\]

and retain the complete double-recent receiver (DRF.25)--(DRF.26). Every
retained triad belongs to (FIM.49), so, up to the fixed smooth-cutoff
collars,

\[
 0<\underline r_n
 \le \min\{|k|,|p|,|q|\}
 \le \max\{|k|,|p|,|q|\}
 \le C_h{Z(t)\over\nu^2},
 \qquad Z(t)=\|\Lambda u(t)\|_2^2.
 \tag{PSL.2}
\]

For each fixed \(n\), preterminal smoothness gives

\[
 H_n:=1+C_{\rm col}{\sup_{t\in I_n}Z(t)\over\nu^2}<\infty.
 \tag{PSL.3}
\]

The harmless \(1\) absorbs the periodic lattice scale and fixed cutoff
collars. Thus the support of every one of the six assignment multipliers is
contained in

\[
 \Omega_n:=\{(k,p,q):k+p+q=0,\quad
             \underline r_n\le |k|,|p|,|q|\le H_n\}.
 \tag{PSL.4}
\]

The critical pairing in (DRF.26) has total multiplier order two: the
one-derivative pressure-complete source, the factors
\(\Lambda^{-1/2}\) and \(\Lambda^{3/2}\), and the receiver pairing combine
to two powers of frequency. Leray projection and the helical tensor factors
are bounded away from zero frequency, every gate has modulus at most one,
and there are only six assignments. Therefore the complete composite symbol
satisfies

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{t\in I_n}
 \|\mathfrak M_n^{\rm rr}(t)\|_{L^\infty(\Omega_n)}
 \le C_{\rm ass}H_n^2<\infty.}
 \tag{PSL.5}
\]

This is the point at which the record receiver differs from the full
untruncated singular VPI kernel. A rough pointwise multiplication does not
preserve Fourier support, but (PSL.4) is support of the operator symbol, so
only the restrictions of the multiplied legs to \(|\xi|\le H_n\) enter.

## 2. The compact-frequency trilinear bound

For one tensor component and one assignment, write

\[
 \mathcal T_m(f,g,h)
 ={1\over(2\pi)^6}\iint
 m(-p-q,p,q)\widehat f(-p-q)\widehat g(p)\widehat h(q)
 \,dp\,dq.
 \tag{PSL.6}
\]

On \(\mathbb R^3\), if \(m\) is supported where all three frequencies lie
in \(B_H\), then Young and Cauchy--Schwarz give

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 |\mathcal T_m(f,g,h)|
 &\le C\|m\|_\infty
 \|\widehat f\mathbf1_{B_H}\|_1
 \|\widehat g\mathbf1_{B_H}
       *\widehat h\mathbf1_{B_H}\|_\infty\\
 &\le C\|m\|_\infty |B_H|^{1/2}
       \|f\|_2\|g\|_2\|h\|_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{PSL.7}
\]

Thus the record form obeys

\[
 \boxed{
 |\mathcal T_m(f,g,h)|
 \le C\|m\|_\infty H^{3/2}
       \|f\|_2\|g\|_2\|h\|_2.}
 \tag{PSL.8}
\]

On \(\mathbb T^3\), the same proof uses the number
\(N(H)=\#(\mathbb Z^3\cap B_H)\):

\[
 |\mathcal T_m(f,g,h)|
 \le C\|m\|_\infty N(H)^{1/2}
       \|f\|_2\|g\|_2\|h\|_2
 \le C\|m\|_\infty(1+H)^{3/2}
       \prod_{\ell=1}^3\|f_\ell\|_2.
 \tag{PSL.9}
\]

In particular, on the torus the roof leaves only finitely many triads. On
the whole space it leaves a finite frequency volume. Summing the finite
tensor components, six assignments, and reality partners changes only the
constant.

## 3. Sharp selectors give \(L^2\) legs

Let

\[
 W=\chi P_-,\qquad \overline W=I-W,
 \qquad 0\le\chi\le1,
 \tag{PSL.10}
\]

with \(P_-\) the orientation-free orthogonal projector onto the unique
contracting line on the active adverse set. The positive-threshold active
set has a spectral gap; at exceptional points the product \(\chi P_-\) is
defined by its measurable active-set representative and is zero off that
set. In every case needed for the block value,

\[
 0\le W\le I,\qquad
 \|W\|_{\rm op}\le1,\qquad
 \|I-W\|_{\rm op}\le1.
 \tag{PSL.11}
\]

Hence \(W^\sigma F\in L^2\) for every current or recent leg \(F\) and every
\(\sigma\in\{s,c\}\). Extend \(W\) beyond the time endpoints with values in
the same operator interval \(0\le W\le I\), for example by zero, and choose a
standard nonnegative spacetime mollifier \(\rho_j\). Then

\[
 W_j:=\rho_j*W,\qquad
 0\le W_j\le I,\qquad
 W_j\to W\quad\hbox{a.e. on }I_n\times\mathbb M^3,
 \tag{PSL.12}
\]

where \(\mathbb M^3\) is either \(\mathbb R^3\) or \(\mathbb T^3\), and the
convergence holds at spacetime Lebesgue points. Positivity of the mollifier
preserves the operator interval \(0\le W\le I\), and the algebraic complement
is always \(I-W_j\). Dominated convergence and Fubini give, for almost every
\(t\in I_n\),

\[
 W_jF\to WF,
 \qquad (I-W_j)F\to(I-W)F
 \quad\hbox{strongly in }L^2_x.
 \tag{PSL.13}
\]

No spatial derivative of \(W\) appears in (PSL.13). In particular, the
fact that a sharp interface may create an unbounded Fourier tail in \(WF\)
does not matter: (PSL.7)--(PSL.9) use only the compact-frequency projection
imposed by the target multiplier.

## 4. Every sharp block converges and is integrable on the record

Extend the bounded symbol by zero outside a fixed collar of \(\Omega_n\),
mollify it in time and in the two independent frequency variables, and average
over the finite assignment and reality symmetries. This constructs smooth
compact-cutoff symbols \(m_j(t)\) which converge almost everywhere to \(m(t)\)
on \(I_n\times\Omega_n^{\rm col}\), are supported in one common collar
\(\Omega_n^{\rm col}\subset B_{H_n}^3\), and obey (PSL.5), after one harmless
fixed enlargement of \(C_{\rm ass}\). Thus the approximation preserves the
complete six-assignment reality symmetry and smooths only the existing sharp
gates inside the fixed FIM roof; it is not an expanding ultraviolet truncation.

For fixed limiting legs, dominated convergence in the compact
\((p,q)\)-domain gives

\[
 \mathcal T_{m_j}(f,g,h)\longrightarrow\mathcal T_m(f,g,h).
 \tag{PSL.14}
\]

For varying selected/complementary legs, split the difference into one
multiplier difference and three input differences. Equations
(PSL.8) and (PSL.13) then give, for every
\(\sigma=(\sigma_a,\sigma_b,\sigma_c)\),

\[
 \boxed{
 J_{n,\sigma}^{(j)}(t)\longrightarrow
 J_{n,\sigma}^{\rm rr}(t)
 :=\mathcal T_{m(t)}
   (W^{\sigma_a}F^{\rm cur/rec},
    W^{\sigma_b}G^{\rm cur/rec},
    W^{\sigma_c}H^{\rm cur/rec})}
 \tag{PSL.15}
\]

after the six assignments and their reality partners are summed.

For a fixed preterminal \(I_n\), the current field and both recent Duhamel
fields are bounded in \(L^2_x\). Indeed, \(u\) and its pressure-complete
source are smooth on the compact interval containing \(I_n\) and the last
\(\tau_n\) units of its history; the heat-window integral defining each
recent leg is therefore continuous and bounded in \(L^2\). Put

\[
 U_n:=\sup_{I_n}\|u(t)\|_2,\qquad
 V_n:=\sup_{I_n}\|v_{n,\tau_n}^{\rm rec}(t)\|_2<\infty.
 \tag{PSL.16}
\]

These are fixed-record consequences of ordinary preterminal classical
smoothness. No endpoint-spanning intersection membership, datum-uniform bound
for \(H_n\) or \(V_n\), or information at \(T_*\) is used here.

Equations (PSL.5) and (PSL.8) give the cutoff-independent domination

\[
 \boxed{
 |J_{n,\sigma}^{(j)}(t)|+|J_{n,\sigma}^{\rm rr}(t)|
 \le C_n H_n^{7/2}U_nV_n^2,
 \qquad t\in I_n.}
 \tag{PSL.17}
\]

The right side is finite and constant in time for this record. Therefore
dominated convergence in \(t\) yields

\[
 \boxed{
 J_{n,\sigma}^{(j)}\to J_{n,\sigma}^{\rm rr}
 \quad\hbox{in }L^1(I_n),
 \qquad
 \int_{I_n}|J_{n,\sigma}^{\rm rr}(t)|\,dt<\infty.}
 \tag{PSL.18}
\]

This defines the physical three-label expression as the limit of the exact
smooth material pullbacks. The physical-space kernel need not be absolutely
integrable: (PSL.6) is the lawful distributional pairing, and
(PSL.14)--(PSL.18) show that every approximating family with the stated common
support, uniform bound, and almost-everywhere convergence has the same limit
\(\mathcal T_m(W^{\sigma_a}F,W^{\sigma_b}G,W^{\sigma_c}H)\).

## 5. Recombination and the sharp \(1/128\) record block

Because the limiting trilinear form is continuous on the three \(L^2\)
legs, multilinearity and

\[
 W+(I-W)=I
 \tag{PSL.19}
\]

give directly

\[
 \boxed{
 J_n^{\rm rr}(t)
 =\sum_{\sigma\in\{s,c\}^3}J_{n,\sigma}^{\rm rr}(t)
 \quad\hbox{for a.e. }t\in I_n.}
 \tag{PSL.20}
\]

The reality completion makes every integrated block real. Integrating
(PSL.20) and using (DRF.25),

\[
 \int_{I_n}J_n^{\rm rr}(t)\,dt\ge {A_n\over16},
 \tag{PSL.21}
\]

shows that some fixed component pattern \(\sigma_n\), chosen once for the
whole record, satisfies

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{I_n}J_{n,\sigma_n}^{\rm rr}(t)\,dt
 \ge {A_n\over128}.}
 \tag{PSL.22}
\]

Since there are only eight patterns, one pattern \(\sigma_*\) occurs on an
infinite record subsequence \(\mathcal N_*\), and

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{I_n}J_{n,\sigma_*}^{\rm rr}(t)\,dt
 \ge {A_n\over128},
 \qquad n\in\mathcal N_*.}
 \tag{PSL.23}
\]

Thus the additional blockwise value-limit hypothesis stated before
(TPR.15a) is discharged by the existing preterminal record regularity and
the FIM compact roof. This does not select a positive physical subspace or
exclude the all-complement pattern.

## 6. A weaker diagonal route which needs no individual sharp limit

There is also a fallback which uses only convergence of the recombined
compact-cutoff record. Suppose

\[
 R_n^{(j)}:=\int_{I_n}J_n^{(j)}(t)\,dt
 \longrightarrow R_n:=\int_{I_n}J_n^{\rm rr}(t)\,dt,
 \qquad R_n\ge {A_n\over16}.
 \tag{PSL.24}
\]

Choose one cutoff \(j_n\) per record so that

\[
 |R_n^{(j_n)}-R_n|\le {A_n\over32}.
 \tag{PSL.25}
\]

Then \(R_n^{(j_n)}\ge A_n/32\). Exact eight-block recombination at that
smooth cutoff gives one pattern \(\widetilde\sigma_n\) with

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{I_n}J_{n,\widetilde\sigma_n}^{(j_n)}(t)\,dt
 \ge {A_n\over256}.}
 \tag{PSL.26}
\]

Infinite pigeonhole again fixes one pattern on an infinite subsequence. More
generally, cutoff error at most \(\theta A_n\), \(0<\theta<1/16\), gives the
exact block constant

\[
 {1\over8}\left({1\over16}-\theta\right)A_n.
 \tag{PSL.27}
\]

This diagonal route produces smooth record blocks, but the cutoff \(j_n\)
may grow with the record and it does not itself provide any cutoff-uniform
response estimate.

## 7. What remains genuinely unproved for differentiated blocks

The preceding theorem concerns \(J_{n,\sigma}\), not the separated terms in
\(D_tJ_{n,\sigma}\). Passing (TPR.9) term by term would additionally require
uniform control of quantities of the following form:

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 &\sup_j\int_{I_n}
  |\mathcal T_{m_j}((D_tW_j)F,G,H)|\,dt<\infty,\\
 &\sup_j\int_{I_n}
  |\mathcal T_{\partial_tm_j}(WF,WG,WH)|\,dt<\infty,
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{PSL.28}
\]

together with the corresponding two separation transports, all three
pressure-complete leg responses, recent-window endpoints, and one-sided
trace products.

Preterminal smoothness makes every fixed smooth approximation classical, but
it does not supply the uniform limits in (PSL.28) for the canonical sharp
passage:

1. a sharp episode or boundary-trace selector can have unproved or infinite
   material BV variation, so \(|D_tW_j|\) can concentrate;
2. the moving dynamic gate contains the enstrophy crossing row (DCT.23),
   whose cumulative variation is not controlled by the datum;
3. derivatives of the smoothing layers can grow as their widths shrink even
   though the undifferentiated symbols stay bounded;
4. the selected/complementary \(D_tW_j\) responses cancel only after all
   eight blocks are recombined, not on the record-carrying block alone;
5. \(H_n\), \(U_n\), and the high preterminal norms in fixed-record bounds
   are finite for each \(n\) but are not uniform datum bounds as
   \(t_n\uparrow T_*\).

For a fixed positive jointly regular and transverse threshold pair,
(SLC.4) does give a finite Radon membership derivative on a fixed compact
preterminal record, and the active-set spectral gap makes \(P_-\) smooth
there. That special per-record fact does not supply the uniform
record-sequence estimates above, does not cover exceptional or zero-threshold
traces, and does not create the receiver-angle coercivity missing after
(TPR.16b).

Thus the exact irreducible defect is not existence of the eight
undifferentiated sharp record blocks. It is cutoff-uniform control and signed
coercive use of the separated response terms of the record-carrying block.

## 8. Exact verdict

On every fixed canonical preterminal failed record,

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{gathered}
 \text{FIM compact roof + zero-frequency floors + bounded }W
 \Longrightarrow
 J_{n,\sigma}^{\rm rr}\in L^1(I_n)
 \quad\text{for all eight patterns},\\
 J_n^{\rm rr}=\sum_\sigma J_{n,\sigma}^{\rm rr},
 \qquad
 \exists\,\sigma_n:\
 \int_{I_n}J_{n,\sigma_n}^{\rm rr}\ge A_n/128,\\
 \exists\,\sigma_*,\ \mathcal N_*\text{ infinite satisfying (PSL.23)}.
 \end{gathered}}
 \tag{PSL.29}
\]

The weaker diagonal compact-cutoff route gives \(A_n/256\) without using
individual sharp limits. Neither result controls the derivative/refund
terms of the chosen block, proves a datum-finite receiver-angle estimate,
establishes (TIR.37a), or closes the MPP.
