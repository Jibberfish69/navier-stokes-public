pressure-explicit binomial/VPI reversible-intersection method. Starting from the
recent high-frequency Duhamel parent in `(RHL.22)--(RHL.31)`, the exact

The conclusion is outcome (iii), with a conditional outcome-(ii) branch at
the first row: every failed pulse forces either an inherited high-\(Q\)
endpoint synchronization or a recent \(\mathcal N_Q\) response, but the
response is recent only on the already known lag \(\tau_n\). No new disjoint
datum-paid charge and no strictly shorter lag are forced. The normalized
remainder at every finite row has the same critical heat singularity and the
same scale-invariant receiver synchronization. This is a proved failure
localization, not a strict reduction, `(TIR.37a)`, or MPP closure.
It does not prove a new datum payment or exclude a failed record.

The physical object remains the one fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes history
generated by \(u_0\). Every \(Q^{[j]}\) below is a simultaneous differentiated
source row of that history, with both ordered velocity-parent insertions and
their pressure completion still joined.

## 2. Exact first-binomial characteristic split

The complete first-binomial equation is

\[
 \boxed{
 Q_t+\nu A Q=\mathcal N_Q,
 \qquad
 \mathcal N_Q=DQ(u)[Q]+2\nu\mathcal C(u).}
 \tag{FBC.7}
\]

For fixed \(t\), differentiation along the backward heat characteristic gives

\[
 {d\over dr}\left(
 e^{-\nu(t-r)A}P_{\ge R_n}Q(r)
 \right)
 =e^{-\nu(t-r)A}P_{\ge R_n}\mathcal N_Q(r).
 \tag{FBC.8}
\]

Integrating `(FBC.8)` first from \(a_n(t)\) to \(r\), and then in \(r\) from
\(a_n(t)\) to \(t\), proves

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 V_n(t)
 ={}&\ell_n e^{-\nu\ell_nA}P_{\ge R_n}Q(a_n)\\
 &+\int_{a_n}^t(t-\sigma)e^{-\nu(t-\sigma)A}
 P_{\ge R_n}\mathcal N_Q(\sigma)\,d\sigma\\
 =:{}&E_n^{[0]}(t)+G_n^{[1]}(t).
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{FBC.9}
\]

This is not an integration of a free source. The first term is the actual
high-frequency source already present at the left end of the RHL heat window;
the second contains the complete first-binomial response of the same fluid
during that window.

By linearity of \(\mathscr T_n\), `(FBC.5)` and the triangle inequality imply

\[
 \boxed{

 \int_{\mathcal F_n}
 \|\Lambda^{-1/2}\mathscr T_n[X](t)\|_2^2dt
 \ge {c_{\rm ov}\over32}\nu A_n.}
 \tag{FBC.10}
\]

Thus the RHL pulse does not force \(\mathcal N_Q\) alone. It forces the exact

\[
 \boxed{
 \text{inherited high-\(Q\) endpoint synchronization}
 \quad\text{or}\quad
 \text{same-lag first-binomial response synchronization}.}
 \tag{FBC.11}
\]

## 4. Exact finite-depth characteristic expansion

The preceding equality iterates without changing the heat window. For every
integer \(m\ge1\), repeated use of

\[
 {d\over dr}\left(e^{-\nu(t-r)A}Q^{[j]}(r)\right)
 =e^{-\nu(t-r)A}Q^{[j+1]}(r)
 \tag{FBC.17}
\]

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 V_n(t)
 ={}&\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}
 {\ell_n^{j+1}\over(j+1)!}
 e^{-\nu\ell_nA}P_{\ge R_n}Q^{[j]}(a_n)\\
 &+\int_{a_n}^t
 {(t-\sigma)^m\over m!}e^{-\nu(t-\sigma)A}
 P_{\ge R_n}Q^{[m]}(\sigma)\,d\sigma.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{FBC.18}
\]

This is the pressure-explicit characteristic Taylor formula with integral
remainder. It is a finite identity and assumes no infinite Taylor radius.

For \(j\ge0\), put

\[
 \gamma_{j,R}(s):={s^j\over j!}
 \sup_{\rho\ge R}\rho^{2j+3/2}e^{-\nu s\rho^2}.
 \tag{FBC.19}
\]

Every row has the uniform-in-lag form

\[
 \boxed{

 \tag{FBC.20}

Consequently each endpoint in `(FBC.18)` obeys

\[

\[

There are \(m+1\) joined output terms after `(FBC.18)` is inserted into

## 6. Exact three-outcome verdict

Following the RHL recent source through the exact first-binomial equation gives:

1. **No new disjoint datum-paid charge.** Disjointness survives on the interior
   branch, but the forced quantity is a synchronized square or higher-row
   convolution, not the paid \(L_t^1\dot H^{-1/2}\) source mass.
2. **No forced even-shorter-lag \(\mathcal N_Q\) pulse.** The first-row response
   is one branch of `(FBC.11)` and remains supported on the same lag
   \(0<s\le\tau_n\). The inherited endpoint branch is not excluded.
3. **The survivor is the same critical all-row synchronization boundary.**
   This does not force every higher row separately. At every finite depth,
   `(FBC.18)--(FBC.23)` leave an inherited endpoint jet or a same-lag
   remainder with the same \(s^{-3/4}\) heat singularity and scale-critical
   receiver product.

This is a genuine exact localization of the RHL recent parent, but it is not a
smaller payment. Excluding the inherited endpoints, forcing a strictly shorter
lag, or summing the finite-depth formula uniformly would require a new
same-history theorem not contained in the first-binomial equation itself.
