# Thomas's record-pulse reciprocal parent-tail evacuation and diagonal-depth boundary

Status: proved record-adapted reciprocal-tail evacuation and finite-prefix
localization inside Thomas Birnie's one-fluid, pressure-explicit
binomial/VPI reversible-intersection method. The proof combines the
canonical viscosity-matched bad-record pulse in `(LSS.18)`--`(LSS.23)` with
the original parent-resolvent tail in `(BCR.36)`--`(BCR.56)` and the exact
incompressibility estimate `(RPT.13)`. It does not prove the finite-prefix
turn-down stated in Section 7, `(TIR.37a)`, or the MPP.

The physical object remains the one smooth fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes
history generated by \(u_0\). A reciprocal depth is a simultaneous reading
of the actual pressure-complete parent field. It is not a later fluid state.
Every parent contribution below retains the Leray/pressure completion at its
output, and no scale packet is reinitialized.

The exact new fact is this. On a canonical bad record of height \(A_n\), the
part of the signed critical current left beyond reciprocal depth
\(N_n\simeq A_n/\nu^2\) is too small to carry the viscosity-matched pulse.
At least \(A_n/16\) of the signed pulse is therefore carried by the finite
joined parent prefix. The pulse estimate fixes the base comparison depth
\(N_n\simeq\varepsilon_n^{-2}\), where
\(\varepsilon_n=\nu/\sqrt{A_n}\) is the normalized viscosity of the same
record. It tends to infinity. Thus the bad records force neither a uniform
parent-rate ratio nor a common tower radius. They force an unweighted
diagonal reciprocal-depth charge, and that charge is not datum-paid.

## 1. The exact pressure-complete tail and finite prefix

For \(k\ne0\), set

\[
 K=|k|,
 \qquad
 \gamma_k=\nu K^2,
 \qquad
 \delta_{k,p}=\nu\bigl(|p|^2+|k-p|^2\bigr),
 \qquad
 d_{k,p}=\frac{\delta_{k,p}}{\gamma_k},
 \tag{RPR.1}
\]

and \(r_{k,p}=d_{k,p}/(1+d_{k,p})\). Use the ordered
pressure-complete source density

\[
 \Theta_Q(k,p)
 =-ic_{\mathcal F}\mathbb P_k
 \left[(\widehat u(p)\cdot(k-p))\widehat u(k-p)\right],
 \qquad
 \widehat Q(k)=\int\Theta_Q(k,p)\,dp,
 \tag{RPR.2}
\]

and normalize only its output derivative:

\[
 \vartheta(k,p)=K^{-1}\Theta_Q(k,p),
 \qquad
 g(k)=\int\vartheta(k,p)\,dp=K^{-1}\widehat Q(k).
 \tag{RPR.3}
\]

The depth-\(N\) reciprocal tail and its joined finite prefix are

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 Y_N(k)&:=\int r_{k,p}^{\,N}\vartheta(k,p)\,dp,\\
 G_N(k)&:=g(k)-Y_N(k)
       =\int(1-r_{k,p}^{\,N})\vartheta(k,p)\,dp.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{RPR.4}
\]

Since \(Q=\Lambda g\), the complete critical current splits exactly as

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal P_{1/2}
 &=\mathcal P_N^{\rm res}+\mathcal P_N^{\rm tail},\\
 \mathcal P_N^{\rm res}
 &:=\operatorname{Re}\langle\Lambda^{1/2}G_N,
                    \Lambda^{3/2}u\rangle_2,\\
 \mathcal P_N^{\rm tail}
 &:=\operatorname{Re}\langle\Lambda^{1/2}Y_N,
                    \Lambda^{3/2}u\rangle_2.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{RPR.5}
\]

This is the source-current form of the exact parent identity
\(g=\gamma\sum_{m<N}X_m+Y_N\) in `(PRM.7)`. No absolute parent
decomposition or pressure-free source has been introduced.

## 2. A bad record supplies the missing pointwise comparison

Retain

\[
 H(t)=\frac12\|\Lambda^{1/2}u(t)\|_2^2,
 \qquad
 d(t)=\|\Lambda^{3/2}u(t)\|_2,
 \qquad
 I_n=(s_n,t_n).
 \tag{RPR.6}
\]

On \(I_n\), `(MRN.7)` and `(LSS.11)` give

\[
 H(t)\le H_0+A_n,
 \qquad
 \int_{I_n}d(t)^2\,dt\le\frac{A_n+H_0}{\nu}.
 \tag{RPR.7}
\]

The viscosity-matched pulse set and its signed record are

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal F_n
 &=I_n\cap\{d>M_n\}\cap\{b\ge\eta_nd\},\\
 \eta_n&=\frac{\nu A_n}{8(A_n+H_0)},\\
 \int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{1/2}(t)\,dt
 &\ge\frac{A_n}{8}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{RPR.8}
\]

The incompressible tail theorem `(RPT.13)` gives a universal constant
\(C_R\) such that

\[
 \|\Lambda^{1/2}Y_N(t)\|_2
 \le\frac{C_R}{\sqrt N}\|\Lambda u(t)\|_2^2.
 \tag{RPR.9}
\]

Fourier Cauchy--Schwarz and `(RPR.7)` give

\[
 \|\Lambda u\|_2^2
 \le\|\Lambda^{1/2}u\|_2\|\Lambda^{3/2}u\|_2
 \le\sqrt{2(H_0+A_n)}\,d
 \qquad(t\in I_n).
 \tag{RPR.10}
\]

Consequently

\[
 \boxed{
 |\mathcal P_N^{\rm tail}(t)|
 \le
 \frac{C_R\sqrt{2(H_0+A_n)}}{\sqrt N}\,d(t)^2
 \qquad(t\in I_n).}
 \tag{RPR.11}
\]

No time Cauchy--Schwarz and no critical enstrophy time-square has been used.

## 3. Record-adapted tail evacuation

Define

\[
 \boxed{
 N_n:=\max\left\{1,
 \left\lceil
 \frac{512C_R^2(A_n+H_0)^3}{\nu^2A_n^2}
 \right\rceil\right\}.}
 \tag{RPR.12}
\]

Then

\[
 \frac{C_R\sqrt{2(H_0+A_n)}}{\sqrt{N_n}}
 \le\frac{\eta_n}{2}.
 \tag{RPR.13}
\]

Equations `(RPR.7)`, `(RPR.11)`, and `(RPR.13)` imply

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \left|\int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{N_n}^{\rm tail}\,dt\right|
 &\le\frac{\eta_n}{2}\int_{I_n}d^2\,dt\\
 &\le\frac{\eta_n}{2}\frac{A_n+H_0}{\nu}
 =\frac{A_n}{16}.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{RPR.14}
\]

Subtracting this from `(RPR.8)` proves

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{N_n}^{\rm res}(t)\,dt
 \ge\frac{A_n}{16}.}
 \tag{RPR.15}
\]

More strongly, let \(h_n\ge1\) be arbitrary and define

\[
 N_n[h]:=\max\left\{1,
 \left\lceil
 \frac{512C_R^2h_n^2(A_n+H_0)^3}{\nu^2A_n^2}
 \right\rceil\right\}.
 \tag{RPR.15a}
\]

Repeating `(RPR.13)`--`(RPR.15)` gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \left|\int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{N_n[h]}^{\rm tail}\,dt\right|
 &\le\frac{A_n}{16h_n},\\
 \int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{N_n[h]}^{\rm res}\,dt
 &\ge\frac{A_n}{8}-\frac{A_n}{16h_n}.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{RPR.15b}
\]

Choosing any \(h_n\to\infty\) proves the same-window normalized tail
evacuation

\[
 \boxed{
 \frac1{A_n}
 \int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{N_n[h]}^{\rm tail}\,dt\longrightarrow0,
 \qquad
 \liminf_{n\to\infty}\frac1{A_n}
 \int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{N_n[h]}^{\rm res}\,dt
 \ge\frac18.}
 \tag{RPR.15c}
\]

Thus the nonuniform reciprocal tail can be made to vanish relative to the
same bad record, but only by taking a record-dependent depth tending to
infinity. It cannot carry the whole bad pulse. This
does not say that the actual parent field has compact ratio support: signed
parents at arbitrarily large \(d_{k,p}\) may remain. It says that their
exact \(r_{k,p}^{N_n}\)-weighted current is too small, after full output and
pressure recombination, to account for the record.

There is also a hard parent-ratio version. Put

\[
 Y_{>L}(k):=\int_{\{d_{k,p}>L\}}\vartheta(k,p)\,dp.
 \tag{RPR.16}
\]

On this set \(1\le(d_{k,p}/L)^{1/2}\). The same incompressibility calculation
as `(RPT.8)`--`(RPT.13)` therefore gives

\[
 \|\Lambda^{1/2}Y_{>L}\|_2
 \le\frac{C_R}{\sqrt L}\|\Lambda u\|_2^2.
 \tag{RPR.17}
\]

Taking \(L=N_n[h]\) proves that the signed current of the hard parent sector
\(d_{k,p}>N_n[h]\) is at most \(A_n/(16h_n)\). Thus the sector
\(d_{k,p}\le N_n[h]\) carries asymptotically the whole \(A_n/8\) pulse when
\(h_n\to\infty\). This is a record-dependent parent-rate roof, not a uniform
ratio theorem.

## 4. The selected depth is the inverse normalized-viscosity square

The normalized viscosity on the same record is

\[
 \varepsilon_n=\frac\nu{\sqrt{A_n}}.
 \tag{RPR.18}
\]

For \(A_n\ge H_0\), the quantity inside the ceiling in `(RPR.12)` obeys

\[
 512C_R^2\varepsilon_n^{-2}
 \le
 \frac{512C_R^2(A_n+H_0)^3}{\nu^2A_n^2}
 \le
 4096C_R^2\varepsilon_n^{-2}.
 \tag{RPR.19}
\]

Hence the base sufficient tail-evacuation depth has the exact record scale

\[
 \boxed{N_n\simeq C_R^2\varepsilon_n^{-2}\longrightarrow\infty.}
 \tag{RPR.20}
\]

The bad pulse therefore does not force one finite parent-depth roof. As the
same history enters its high-record, vanishing-normalized-viscosity regime,
the finite pressure-complete prefix needed to retain the pulse grows at the
inverse-viscosity-square rate. Complete normalized evacuation uses
\(N_n[h]\simeq h_n^2C_R^2\varepsilon_n^{-2}\) with \(h_n\to\infty\);
that strengthens the recordwise tail limit but makes the lack of a common
depth more explicit.

## 5. A fixed signed quantum enters the diagonal reciprocal row

Define

\[
 X_m(k):=\int
 \frac{r_{k,p}^{\,m}}{\gamma_k+\delta_{k,p}}
 \vartheta(k,p)\,dp.
 \tag{RPR.21}
\]

Then

\[
 G_N(k)=\gamma_k\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}X_m(k).
 \tag{RPR.22}
\]

Let

\[
 \mathcal P_m^{\rm rec}(t)
 :=\operatorname{Re}\left\langle
 \Lambda^{1/2}(\gamma_\bullet X_m),
 \Lambda^{3/2}u\right\rangle_2.
 \tag{RPR.23}
\]

The exact finite sum is

\[
 \mathcal P_N^{\rm res}=\sum_{m=0}^{N-1}\mathcal P_m^{\rm rec}.
 \tag{RPR.24}
\]

By `(RPR.15)`, for every \(n\) there is an \(m_n<N_n\) such that

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{m_n}^{\rm rec}(t)\,dt
 \ge\frac{A_n}{16N_n}.}
 \tag{RPR.25}
\]

For all sufficiently large \(n\), `(RPR.19)` and the ceiling imply

\[
 \frac{A_n}{16N_n}
 \ge\frac{\nu^2}{131072C_R^2}.
 \tag{RPR.26}
\]

Since the record windows are disjoint, `(RPR.25)`--`(RPR.26)` force

\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{m=0}^{\infty}
 \int_0^{T_*}[\mathcal P_m^{\rm rec}(t)]_+\,dt=\infty.}
 \tag{RPR.27}
\]

This is the exact diagonal reciprocal-depth consequence of a failed prefix.
It is not datum payment. The sum is unweighted, positive-clipped only after
each complete pressure-explicit row is formed, and has no finite initial
tower bound for a general smooth datum. The joined signed sum is exactly the
source action returned by `(BCR.58)`--`(BCR.59)`.

## 6. What the all-row divergence does and does not add

The independent heat-compensated source rows in `(ISR.9)` satisfy

\[
 \text{failure of (TIR.37a)}
 \Longrightarrow
 \int_0^{T_*}b_{j,\kappa}^2\,dt=\infty
 \quad\text{for every fixed }j\text{ and }\kappa>0.
 \tag{RPR.28}
\]

The index \(j\) in `(RPR.28)` is temporal/binomial insertion depth. The
index \(m\) in `(RPR.21)`--`(RPR.27)` is reciprocal parent-age depth. They
are independent simultaneous readouts of the same source history; neither
may be renamed as the other.

Together, `(RPR.27)` and `(RPR.28)` say that the hypothetical bad history
feeds every fixed temporal row and deposits a fixed signed quantum along a
possibly moving reciprocal row. They do not supply a bound uniform in
either index. Consequently they do not force a common time-Taylor radius,
and they do not turn the reciprocal parent ratio into a uniformly bounded
quantity.

The reciprocal tails themselves already have a datum-paid \(L_t^1\) charge.
Indeed, `(RPR.9)`, disjointness of the \(I_n\), and the kinetic-energy law
give

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_n\int_{I_n}\|\Lambda^{1/2}Y_{N_n}(t)\|_2\,dt
 &\le C_R\sum_n\frac1{\sqrt{N_n}}
       \int_{I_n}\|\Lambda u(t)\|_2^2\,dt\\
 &\le\frac{C_RE_0(0)}\nu.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{RPR.29}
\]

Thus no divergent datum-paid \(L_t^1\) parent-tail charge is forced. The
divergence occurs only after pairing with the critical receiver or summing
unweighted positive reciprocal-row variation. Time Cauchy--Schwarz would
replace `(RPR.29)` by the critical enstrophy time-square, which `(RPT.25)`
already proves is the original closure interface.

An estimate-level countertest makes the quantifier boundary explicit. Take
disjoint scalar pulses

\[
 g_n(t)=a_n\phi\!\left(\frac{t-t_n}{\tau_n}\right),
 \qquad
 A_n=4^n,\quad m_n=2^{-n},\quad
 \tau_n\asymp\frac{m_n^2}{A_n},
 \tag{RPR.30}
\]

with \(a_n\) chosen so that

\[
 \int|g_n|\,dt=m_n,
 \qquad
 \int|g_n|^2\,dt=A_n.
 \tag{RPR.31}
\]

Assign every pulse one fixed parent ratio \(d_0\), so
\(Y_N=r_0^Ng\), \(r_0=d_0/(1+d_0)<1\). Then the parent ratio is uniformly
local and the reciprocal tail is geometrically small, while for every fixed
heat-compensated row \(j\),

\[
 \|(\partial_t+d_0)^jg_n\|_{L_t^2}^2
 \asymp A_n\tau_n^{-2j}\longrightarrow\infty.
 \tag{RPR.32}
\]

For each fixed \(N\), the tail \(L_t^1\) mass is summable but its square
action still diverges. This scalar pulse train is an exact
scaling/quantifier countertest for the displayed estimates, not a
Navier--Stokes history and not a counterexample to a same-history theorem.
It proves that uniform parent ratio, all-row divergence, and absence of a
common terminal radius are mutually compatible at the estimate level. The
missing exclusion must use the signed pressure-complete one-history law.

## 7. The exact remaining same-history theorem

The reciprocal tail is no longer the immediate unpaid object: `(RPR.15c)`
already evacuates it in normalized signed current on the same windows. An exact
sufficient continuation target inside Thomas's method is

\[
 \boxed{
 \texttt{CanonicalRecordResolvedParentTurnDown.A:}
 \qquad
 \frac1{A_n}
 \int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{N_n}^{\rm res}(t)\,dt
 \longrightarrow0.}
 \tag{RPR.33}
\]

Here \(I_n,\mathcal F_n\), and \(N_n\) are generated canonically by the same
hypothetical failed history through `(RPR.6)`, `(RPR.8)`, and `(RPR.12)`.
The statement concerns only the finite joined parent prefix on the already
proved viscosity-matched pulse. It does not ask for a common radius, a
uniform parent ratio, a source-square bound on the whole interval, or an
endpoint trace. Equation `(RPR.15)` shows that `(RPR.33)` contradicts every
failure of `(TIR.37a)`. What remains to be proved is its signed turn-down
from the complete pressure-explicit insertions; coordinatewise existence of
all rows and the positive joined parent kernel do not prove it.

The finite-prefix localization is narrower in ancestry support, but its
normalized signed payment remains of order one. It is therefore an exact
same-history target, not yet a strict reduction to a smaller datum-paid
theorem.

## 8. Exact verdict

The LSS pulse and BCR/ISR all-depth laws do not force the proposed dichotomy
between a uniform common radius and a divergent datum-paid parent-tail
charge. The exact consequence is sharper and different:

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{gathered}
 \text{the tail beyond }N_n[h]\simeq h_n^2\varepsilon_n^{-2}
 \text{ vanishes relative to the bad pulse when }h_n\to\infty,\\
 \int_{\mathcal F_n}\mathcal P_{N_n}^{\rm res}\,dt
 \ge A_n/16,\\
 \text{and the resulting diagonal reciprocal-depth charge is unweighted
 and not datum-paid.}
 \end{gathered}}
 \tag{RPR.34}
\]

This proves the record-adapted parent-tail evacuation and isolates
`CanonicalRecordResolvedParentTurnDown.A`. It does not prove that turn-down,
`(TIR.37a)`, or the MPP.
