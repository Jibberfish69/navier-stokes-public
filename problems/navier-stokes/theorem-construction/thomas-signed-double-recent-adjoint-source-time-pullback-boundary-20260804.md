# Thomas's signed double-recent adjoint source-time pullback boundary

**Date:** 2026-08-04

**Status:** proved noncanonical signed localization inside Thomas Birnie's
one-fluid, pressure-explicit binomial/VPI reversible-intersection method.

The physical object is still one continuing fixed-viscosity fluid history
generated by one smooth datum.  The pulse or spring winding, the imbalanced
stretched vortex, and the perfectly balanced rip-cord remain the physical
readings of that same history.  The calculation below starts from the signed
double-recent current `(DRF.25)--(DRF.27)` and uses the Lorentz heat-age law
`(LSP.1)--(LSP.7)` before replacing the current by a scalar source square.

The exact advance is this.  A fixed signed fraction of the double-recent
current lies on a shrinking receiver pulse.  An arbitrarily young source-age
slice can then be removed from both parents while retaining a fixed signed
fraction.  Both remaining parents are generated strictly earlier by the
complete pressure source.  The resulting current has an exact two-source
Duhamel representation and two exact one-slot adjoint pullbacks, one for each
ordered parent position.  On the interior branch these source incidences lie
inside the same canonical record; on the edge branch they may begin before the
record.

This produces a positive signed *future-incidence* floor, not a new canonical
height record.  The unweighted high-source mass still tends to zero.  The
adjoint receiver weight must therefore diverge, and the pressure-explicit
first-binomial integration by parts reproduces two moving heat-age edges plus
a same-window higher-row remainder.  No datum-controlled endpoint, strict
reduction, `(TIR.37a)`, or MPP closure is obtained.

## 1. Retain the sign on a shrinking receiver pulse

Use the branch selected by the signed DRF current, not the independently
selected nonnegative-square branch from CDR or LSP:

\[
 \Gamma_n:=I_n^{\sigma_n},
 \qquad \sigma_n\in\{\mathrm{edge},\mathrm{int}\},
 \qquad
 \int_{\Gamma_n}J_n^{\rm rr}(t)\,dt
 \ge {A_n\over32}.
 \tag{SAP.1}
\]

Put

\[
 d_n(t):=\|\Lambda^{3/2}u(t)\|_2,
 \qquad
 D_n^\star:=\left({2A_n\over\nu}\right)^{1/2}.
 \tag{SAP.2}
\]

The record balance and the pressure-complete sector estimate give

\[
 \left(\int_{\Gamma_n}d_n(t)^2dt\right)^{1/2}
 \le D_n^\star,
 \qquad
 |J_n^{\rm rr}(t)|
 \le C_{\rm sec}\mathfrak A_n(t)^2d_n(t).
 \tag{SAP.3}
\]

Here \(\mathfrak A_n\) is the actual recent high-source ancestry from
`(LSP.1)`.  With

\[
 M_n:=\int_0^{T_*}
 \|P_{\ge r_n}\Lambda^{-1/2}Q(u(t))\|_2dt,
 \qquad
 W_n:=C_W\nu^{-3/4}M_n,
 \tag{SAP.4}
\]

the exact weak heat-kernel law is

\[
 \|\mathfrak A_n\|_{L^{4/3,\infty}(0,T_*)}
 \le W_n,
 \qquad M_n\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{SAP.5}
\]

For every \(\lambda>0\), the distribution bound in `(SAP.5)` gives

\[
 \int_{\{\mathfrak A_n\le\lambda\}}
 \mathfrak A_n(t)^4dt
 \le {3\over2}W_n^{4/3}\lambda^{8/3}.
 \tag{SAP.6}
\]

Define the signed-current threshold

\[
 \boxed{
 \lambda_n^{\rm sgn}
 :=\left(
 {\sqrt{\nu A_n}
  \over128\sqrt3\,C_{\rm sec}W_n^{2/3}}
 \right)^{3/4}.}
 \tag{SAP.7}
\]

Equations `(SAP.2)--(SAP.7)` imply

\[
 \int_{\Gamma_n\cap\{\mathfrak A_n\le\lambda_n^{\rm sgn}\}}
 |J_n^{\rm rr}(t)|dt
 \le {A_n\over128}.
 \tag{SAP.8}
\]

Consequently the receiver set

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn}
 :=\Gamma_n\cap
 \{t:\mathfrak A_n(t)>\lambda_n^{\rm sgn}\}}
 \tag{SAP.9}
\]

retains sign and a fixed record fraction:

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{\mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn}}J_n^{\rm rr}(t)dt
 \ge {3A_n\over128}.}
 \tag{SAP.10}
\]

This set is nonempty and shrinks.  Indeed,

\[
 \boxed{
 |\mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn}|
 \le {128\sqrt3\,C_{\rm sec}W_n^2
        \over\sqrt{\nu A_n}}
 =C_{\rm sgn}
 {M_n^2\over\nu^2A_n^{1/2}}
 \longrightarrow0.}
 \tag{SAP.11}
\]

The time cut in `(SAP.9)` is made only after the complete current has been
formed.  It does not clip a triad, choose a favorable output, separate the two
ordered input positions, or assign pressure an independent sign.  The full
frozen symmetric mask and all six output/input assignments remain inside
`(SAP.10)`.

## 2. Remove the zero-age slice from both physical parents

For sufficiently large \(n\), \(s_n>\tau_n\).  On \(I_n\), write the actual
double-recent parent as

\[
 V_n(t)
 =\int_{t-\tau_n}^{t}
 e^{-\nu(t-r)A}P_{\ge r_n}Q(u(r))\,dr.
 \tag{SAP.12}
\]

For \(0<\delta<\tau_n\), remove only the youngest source ages:

\[
 \boxed{
 V_{n,\delta}(t)
 :=\int_{t-\tau_n}^{t-\delta}
 e^{-\nu(t-r)A}P_{\ge r_n}Q(u(r))\,dr.}
 \tag{SAP.13}
\]

The full source \(Q(u(r))\) in `(SAP.13)` already contains the simultaneous
Leray/pressure completion.  It is not a freely prescribed source profile.

For each fixed \(n\), the history is smooth on the compact preterminal source
interval involved in `(SAP.12)`.  Hence

\[
 V_{n,\delta}\longrightarrow V_n
 \quad\hbox{in the required }H^1\hbox{ topology as }\delta\downarrow0,
 \tag{SAP.14}
\]

uniformly on the fixed receiver record.  The frozen pressure-complete
bilinear map and the actual receiver are continuous under this convergence.
Therefore one may choose

\[
 0<\delta_n<\tau_n
 \tag{SAP.15}
\]

so that, with

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 J_{n,\delta_n}^{\rm rr}(t)
 :={}&\operatorname{Re}\Bigl(
 \Lambda^{-1/2}\mathcal Q_n^{\rm ch}
 [V_{n,\delta_n},V_{n,\delta_n}](t),
 \Lambda^{3/2}u(t)\Bigr)_2,
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{SAP.16}
\]

one has

\[
 \int_{\mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn}}
 |J_n^{\rm rr}(t)-J_{n,\delta_n}^{\rm rr}(t)|dt
 \le {A_n\over128}.
 \tag{SAP.17}
\]

Combining `(SAP.10)` and `(SAP.17)` proves

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathfrak J_{n,\delta_n}^{\rm sgn}
 :=\int_{\mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn}}
 J_{n,\delta_n}^{\rm rr}(t)dt
 \ge {A_n\over64}.}
 \tag{SAP.18}
\]

Both physical input parents in `(SAP.18)` are now generated at least
\(\delta_n\) before their receiver time and at most \(\tau_n\) before it.
The number \(\delta_n\) is positive for every record, but the argument gives
no datum-controlled lower bound for it.

Let \(S_n^{\rm sgn}\) be the smallest time interval containing every source
time \(r\) with

\[
 t\in\mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn},
 \qquad \delta_n\le t-r\le\tau_n.
 \tag{SAP.19}
\]

Since the records are disjoint in the finite interval \((0,T_*)\),
\(\Delta_n\to0\).  Thus

\[
 |S_n^{\rm sgn}|
 \le\Delta_n+\tau_n\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{SAP.20}
\]

The original edge/interior fork remains exact:

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{array}{ll}
 \sigma_n=\mathrm{int}:&S_n^{\rm sgn}\subset I_n,\\[1mm]
 \sigma_n=\mathrm{edge}:&S_n^{\rm sgn}
 \text{ may meet }(s_n-\tau_n,s_n).
 \end{array}}
 \tag{SAP.21}
\]

## 3. Exact two-source Duhamel representation

For brevity set

\[
 \mathscr B_{n,t}[a,b]
 :={1\over2}\left(
 \mathcal Q_n^{\rm ch}[a,b](t)
 +\mathcal Q_n^{\rm ch}[b,a](t)\right),
 \qquad R_n(t):=\Lambda u(t).
 \tag{SAP.22}
\]

This symmetric polarization changes nothing on the diagonal:

\[
 \mathscr B_{n,t}[V,V]
 =\mathcal Q_n^{\rm ch}[V,V](t).
 \tag{SAP.22a}
\]

The operator \(\mathscr B_{n,t}\) is therefore the same frozen complete
dynamic-core, high-output source from DRF, now written so that both ordered
input positions remain explicit after their source times separate.  It retains
the symmetric three-leg mask, all three output choices, both orders of the two
physical parents, and the output Leray/pressure completion.

Define the signed two-source incidence density

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathcal K_n(t;r,s)
 :={}&{\bf1}_{\mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn}}(t)
 {\bf1}_{\{\delta_n\le t-r\le\tau_n\}}
 {\bf1}_{\{\delta_n\le t-s\le\tau_n\}}\\
 &\times\operatorname{Re}\Bigl(
 \mathscr B_{n,t}\bigl[
 e^{-\nu(t-r)A}P_{\ge r_n}Q(u(r)),
 e^{-\nu(t-s)A}P_{\ge r_n}Q(u(s))
 \bigr],R_n(t)\Bigr)_2.
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{SAP.23}
\]

Smoothness on each preterminal record makes `(SAP.23)` integrable.  Bilinearity
and Fubini give the exact identity

\[
 \boxed{
 \iiint\mathcal K_n(t;r,s)\,dr\,ds\,dt
 =\mathfrak J_{n,\delta_n}^{\rm sgn}
 \ge {A_n\over64}.}
 \tag{SAP.24}
\]

Thus a fixed signed fraction has been pulled to two strictly earlier,
pressure-complete source times before either source is replaced by its norm.
In particular,

\[
 \iiint[\mathcal K_n(t;r,s)]_+\,dr\,ds\,dt
 \ge {A_n\over64}.
 \tag{SAP.25}
\]

Equation `(SAP.25)` is a positive signed incidence floor for the joined
two-parent event.  It is not a positive sign for any one triad assignment and
does not produce two disjoint source intervals: the same earlier source field
may feed both ordered parent positions.

## 4. Each ordered parent has an exact adjoint source-time pullback

Freeze the other actual parent only after the complete operator in `(SAP.22)`
has been formed.  Define

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathscr L_{n,t}^{(1)}h
 &:=\mathscr B_{n,t}[h,V_{n,\delta_n}(t)],\\
 \mathscr L_{n,t}^{(2)}h
 &:=\mathscr B_{n,t}[V_{n,\delta_n}(t),h],
 \end{aligned}
 \tag{SAP.26}
\]

and the two actual future receiver fields

\[
 \boxed{
 \Theta_n^{(a)}(t)
 :={\bf1}_{\mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn}}(t)
 (\mathscr L_{n,t}^{(a)})^*R_n(t),
 \qquad a\in\{1,2\}.}
 \tag{SAP.27}
\]

Neither \(\Theta_n^{(a)}\) is an independent test profile.  It is determined
by the same future receiver, the other actual recent parent, and the complete
six-assignment pressure operator.

For each ordered slot put

\[
 \boxed{
 \Phi_n^{(a)}(r)
 :=P_{\ge r_n}
 \int_{r+\delta_n}^{r+\tau_n}
 e^{-\nu(t-r)A}\Theta_n^{(a)}(t)\,dt.}
 \tag{SAP.28}
\]

Extend \(\Theta_n^{(a)}\) by zero outside the receiver pulse.  Adjointness,
self-adjointness of the heat semigroup, and Fubini prove, separately for both
ordered slots,

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{S_n^{\rm sgn}}
 \operatorname{Re}
 \langle Q(u(r)),\Phi_n^{(a)}(r)\rangle\,dr
 =\mathfrak J_{n,\delta_n}^{\rm sgn}
 \ge {A_n\over64},
 \quad a=1,2.}
 \tag{SAP.29}
\]

No factor is split between the two slots: expanding either copy of the same
parent reproduces the full bilinear current while the other copy remains in
the adjoint receiver.  Consequently each slot also satisfies

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{S_n^{\rm sgn}}
 \bigl[
 \operatorname{Re}\langle Q(u(r)),\Phi_n^{(a)}(r)\rangle
 \bigr]_+dr
 \ge {A_n\over64}.}
 \tag{SAP.30}
\]

On the interior branch `(SAP.30)` is a signed nested source-incidence record
inside \(I_n\).  It is not a canonical critical-height record: its receiver
\(\Phi_n^{(a)}\) depends on the later fluid and on the other recent parent.

## 5. The source mass vanishes; the adjoint incidence must diverge

Set

\[
 b_n(r):=\|P_{\ge r_n}\Lambda^{-1/2}Q(u(r))\|_2,
 \qquad
 \mathcal W_n^{(a)}(r)
 :=\|\Lambda^{1/2}\Phi_n^{(a)}(r)\|_2.
 \tag{SAP.31}
\]

Equation `(SAP.29)` and Hilbert-space Cauchy give

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_{S_n^{\rm sgn}}
 b_n(r)\mathcal W_n^{(a)}(r)\,dr
 \ge {A_n\over64},
 \qquad a=1,2.}
 \tag{SAP.32}
\]

But the unweighted mass on the shrinking source interval obeys

\[
 \int_{S_n^{\rm sgn}}b_n(r)dr
 \le M_n\longrightarrow0.
 \tag{SAP.33}
\]

Therefore the exact price of retaining sign is future-incidence amplification:

\[
 \boxed{
 \operatorname*{ess\,sup}_{r\in S_n^{\rm sgn}}
 \mathcal W_n^{(a)}(r)
 \ge {A_n\over64M_n}
 \longrightarrow\infty,
 \qquad a=1,2.}
 \tag{SAP.34}
\]

This is the exact answer to the source-mass fork.  The calculation produces a
fixed positive *adjoint-weighted signed incidence* floor `(SAP.29)--(SAP.32)`.
It cannot produce a fixed unweighted source-mass floor, because `(SAP.33)`
proves that the actual high-source mass vanishes.  The signed current survives
through an increasingly concentrated future receiver, not through a new stock
of independently positive source.

## 6. Pressure-explicit first-binomial rows give two moving edges

The positive age gap in `(SAP.15)` lets one test the complete primitive rows
without touching the datum endpoint.  For \(m\ge0\), define

\[
 \boxed{
 \Phi_n^{(a),[m]}(r)
 :=P_{\ge r_n}
 \int_{r+\delta_n}^{r+\tau_n}
 {(t-r)^m\over m!}
 e^{-\nu(t-r)A}\Theta_n^{(a)}(t)\,dt.}
 \tag{SAP.35}
\]

For \(m\ge1\), direct differentiation gives the exact two-edge law

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 (\partial_r-\nu A)\Phi_n^{(a),[m]}(r)
 ={}&{\tau_n^m\over m!}
 e^{-\nu\tau_nA}P_{\ge r_n}\Theta_n^{(a)}(r+\tau_n)\\
 &-{\delta_n^m\over m!}
 e^{-\nu\delta_nA}P_{\ge r_n}\Theta_n^{(a)}(r+\delta_n)
 -\Phi_n^{(a),[m-1]}(r).
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{SAP.36}
\]

Use the complete pressure-explicit rows

\[
 Q^{[0]}:=Q,
 \qquad Q^{[j+1]}:=(\partial_r+\nu A)Q^{[j]}.
 \tag{SAP.37}
\]

At row one, `(SAP.37)` contains both ordered nonlinear source insertions, the
viscous product commutator, and their simultaneous pressure completion.  The
higher rows are the same primitive binomial/VPI recursion; no Leray-free or
source-only substitute is introduced.

The ordinary integration endpoints vanish because the incidence fields are
supported strictly inside the preterminal history.  The same viscous
cancellation as in AFI and `(SAP.36)` yield, for every finite \(m\ge1\),

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathfrak J_{n,\delta_n}^{\rm sgn}
 ={}&\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}{\tau_n^{j+1}\over(j+1)!}
 \int_{\mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn}}
 \operatorname{Re}\left\langle
 e^{-\nu\tau_nA}P_{\ge r_n}Q^{[j]}(t-\tau_n),
 \Theta_n^{(a)}(t)\right\rangle dt\\
 &-\sum_{j=0}^{m-1}{\delta_n^{j+1}\over(j+1)!}
 \int_{\mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn}}
 \operatorname{Re}\left\langle
 e^{-\nu\delta_nA}P_{\ge r_n}Q^{[j]}(t-\delta_n),
 \Theta_n^{(a)}(t)\right\rangle dt\\
 &+\int_{S_n^{\rm sgn}}
 \operatorname{Re}\langle
 Q^{[m]}(r),\Phi_n^{(a),[m]}(r)\rangle dr,
 \qquad a=1,2.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{SAP.38}
\]

No time regularity of the superlevel-set indicator is being assumed here.
One may first replace it by smooth receiver-time cutoffs and pass by dominated
convergence.  Equivalently, apply the finite-depth characteristic identity
directly to the source integral `(SAP.13)` at each receiver time and only then
integrate over \(\mathcal E_n^{\rm sgn}\).  Both derivations give `(SAP.38)`
without differentiating the receiver selector.

At every finite depth, one of the \(2m+1\) signed terms on the right of
`(SAP.38)` is at least

\[
 {A_n\over64(2m+1)}.
 \tag{SAP.39}
\]

This is an exact all-row signed alternative.  It is not a datum payment.  The
\(\tau_n\)-edge is inherited from the oldest admitted source age, the
\(\delta_n\)-edge is inherited from the newly removed youngest age, and the
last term stays inside the same source-age window at the next complete
binomial row.  On the interior branch all these times remain in \(I_n\); on
the edge branch the \(\tau_n\)-edge may precede \(s_n\).

## 7. Exact verdict

The signed pullback proves

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \text{failed record}
 &\Longrightarrow
 \text{shrinking signed double-recent receiver pulse}\\
 &\Longrightarrow
 \text{two strictly earlier pressure-complete parents}\\
 &\Longrightarrow
 \text{two-source signed incidence and each-slot adjoint floor}
 \ge {A_n\over64}.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{SAP.40}
\]

On the interior branch the source interval is nested inside the same record.
On the edge branch it may begin before that record.  Both ordered parent
positions, all six assignments, the same receiver, and pressure completion are
present throughout.

The new floor is future-dependent.  The actual unweighted high-source mass on
the same interval tends to zero, forcing the adjoint weight in `(SAP.34)` to
diverge.  The first-binomial recursion then yields the two moving-edge/same-row
alternative `(SAP.38)`, not a datum endpoint.  Thus this is a stronger signed
same-history localization and an exact boundary: it is not an iterable
canonical record, a positive unweighted source-mass charge, a strict reduction,
`(TIR.37a)`, or MPP closure.
