## 1. Primitive pressure-explicit law

Let

\[
 \partial_tu+(u\cdot\nabla)u=-\nabla p+\nu\Delta u,
 \qquad \nabla\cdot u=0,
 \tag{SSR.1}
\]

and put

\[
 M=\nabla u=S+\Omega,
 \qquad S={M+M^T\over2},
 \qquad \Omega={M-M^T\over2}.
 \tag{SSR.2}
\]

Writing \(H=\nabla^2p\), the exact gradient and strain equations are

\[
 D_tM=-M^2-H+\nu\Delta M,
 \qquad
 D_tS=-S^2-\Omega^2-H+\nu\Delta S.
 \tag{SSR.3}
\]

If \(\omega=\nabla\times u\), then

\[
 -\Delta p=g:=\operatorname{tr}(M^2)
 =\operatorname{tr}(S^2)-{1\over2}|\omega|^2,
 \qquad
 H=-{g\over3}I+H^\circ,
 \tag{SSR.4}
\]

where \(H^\circ\) is the trace-free pressure Hessian determined by the same
whole field.

## 2. Exact strain-shape and frame response

At a simple-spectrum point order the strain eigenvalues as in (BWS.26):

\[

\[

 \tag{SSR.7}

The off-diagonal entries retain the exact frame motion:

\[
 \boxed{

 \tag{SSR.8}

Thus the trace-free pressure response changes both strain shape and frame.
The off-diagonal frame terms cancel from a spectral invariant, but they have
not been discarded: they remain in every orientation-dependent selector and
in the complete finite-chord tangent below. At an eigenvalue collision the
named frame is not intrinsic; the invariant formula in the next section
