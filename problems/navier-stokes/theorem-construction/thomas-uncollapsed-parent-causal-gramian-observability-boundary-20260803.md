# Thomas's uncollapsed-parent causal Gramian and observability boundary

Status: exact auxiliary theorem and checked storage-family obstruction inside
Thomas Birnie's one-fluid pressure-explicit binomial/VPI method.  Keeping the
complete ordered velocity-parent tensor before output collapse turns the
Navier--Stokes source-response history into one block-linear evolution along
the actual solution.  Its backward observability Gramian is positive and
cancels every parent insertion without separating outputs or signs.  The
corresponding forward, causal Lyapunov storage also cancels the insertion
exactly, but its required endpoint lower bound is algebraically equivalent to
the open critical response action.  The lawful coherent-parent examples show
that no cutoff-uniform product-tensor bound repairs this equivalence.  Thus
this note does not prove `(TIR.37a)` and does not call the surviving
coercivity condition a smaller theorem.

`thomas-one-fluid-high-pass-prefix-first-moment-reduction-20260803.md`.
The pressure-complete parent-age identities are in
`thomas-parent-age-reciprocal-moment-telescope-positivity-boundary-20260803.md`
and

## 1. The complete parent tensor evolves by the original binomial insertion

Work first on a finite real divergence-free Galerkin prefix.  This removes
domain questions while retaining every convolution whose output belongs to
the prefix.  Let

\[
 A:=\Lambda^2,
 \qquad
 \mathcal B(a,b):=\mathbb P((a\cdot\nabla)b),
 \qquad
 \mathcal A_u v:=-\mathcal B(u,v).
 \tag{UPG.1}
\]

For divergence-free (u), integration by parts and self-adjointness of the
Leray projection give

\[
 (\mathcal A_uv_1,v_2)_2=-(v_1,\mathcal A_uv_2)_2.
 \tag{UPG.2}
\]

Thus \(\mathcal A_u\) is skew-adjoint and the pressure-complete source is

\[
 Q(u)=\mathcal A_u u=-\mathcal B(u,u).
 \tag{UPG.3}
\]

Do not collapse the two physical parents.  Put

\[
 F:=u\otimes u,
 \qquad
 \mathscr D:=A\otimes I+I\otimes A,
 \qquad
 \mathscr A_u:=\mathcal A_u\otimes I+I\otimes\mathcal A_u.
 \tag{UPG.4}
\]

If \(\mathscr C(a\otimes b):=-\mathcal B(a,b)\), then
\(\mathscr CF=Q(u)\).  Direct differentiation of the same two velocity
parents gives

\[
 \boxed{
 F_t=(\mathscr A_u-\nu\mathscr D)F,
 \qquad
 \mathscr A_u^*=-\mathscr A_u.}
 \tag{UPG.5}
\]

The two terms in \(\mathscr A_uF\) are exactly the two ordered nonlinear
parent insertions.  Equation `(UPG.5)` is the tensor form of `(PRM.2)` and
the first row of the pressure-explicit binomial recurrence; it is not a
freely prescribed source system.

## 2. One homogeneous block system carries the causal response

Let

\[
 v(t):=e^{-\nu tA}u_0,
 \qquad
 w(t):=u(t)-v(t),
 \qquad
 w(0)=0.
 \tag{UPG.6}
\]

Then \(w_t+\nu Aw=Q(u)=\mathscr CF\).  Hence the uncollapsed response-parent
state

\[
 X:=\binom{w}{F}
 \tag{UPG.7}
\]

obeys the single homogeneous, history-dependent linear equation

\[
 \boxed{
 X_t=\mathscr G_u(t)X,
 \qquad
 \mathscr G_u(t)=
 \begin{pmatrix}
 -\nu A&\mathscr C\\
 0&-\nu\mathscr D+\mathscr A_u(t)
 \end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 X(0)=\binom0{u_0\otimes u_0}.}
 \tag{UPG.8}
\]

Although `(UPG.8)` is linear in \(X\) once the one velocity history is
fixed, its coefficient \(\mathscr A_u(t)\) is generated by that same history.
No second fluid, outputwise source port, or independently chosen parent path
has been introduced.

For a nonnegative Fourier multiplier \(B\) commuting with \(A\), define

\[
 \mathsf M_B:=
 \begin{pmatrix}B&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix},
 \qquad
 \mathsf R_B:=
 2\nu\begin{pmatrix}AB&0\\0&0\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{UPG.9}
\]

Taking \(B=M_K=(\Lambda-K)_+\), the exact Duhamel action `(HPF.25)` is

\[
 \boxed{
 \mathcal K_{K,T}
 ={1\over2}(X(T),\mathsf M_{M_K}X(T))
 +{1\over2}\int_0^T(X(t),\mathsf R_{M_K}X(t))\,dt.}
 \tag{UPG.10}
\]

At \(K=0\), \(B=\Lambda\), so `(UPG.10)` is the terminal
\(\dot H^{1/2}\) response stock plus its \(\dot H^{3/2}\) viscous action.
By `(HPF.25)`--`(HPF.38)`, a uniform datum bound for this action is equivalent,
modulo the already paid heat-descendant term, to `(TIR.37a)`.

## 3. The positive cancelling Gramian is necessarily anti-causal

Let \(\Phi(t,s)\) be the evolution operator of `(UPG.8)`.  For fixed
\(T<T_*\), define the full backward observability Gramian

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathsf O_{B,T}(t)
 :={}&\Phi(T,t)^*\mathsf M_B\Phi(T,t)\\
 &+\int_t^T
 \Phi(r,t)^*\mathsf R_B\Phi(r,t)\,dr .
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{UPG.11}
\]

Every parent output and every interference cross term is inside \(\Phi\)
before the quadratic form is taken.  Therefore

\[
 \mathsf O_{B,T}(t)\succeq0
 \tag{UPG.12}
\]

and direct differentiation gives the backward Lyapunov equation

\[
 \boxed{
 -\partial_t\mathsf O_{B,T}
 =\mathscr G_u^*\mathsf O_{B,T}
  +\mathsf O_{B,T}\mathscr G_u+\mathsf R_B,
 \qquad
 \mathsf O_{B,T}(T)=\mathsf M_B.}
 \tag{UPG.13}
\]

Along the actual state, `(UPG.13)` yields

\[
 \boxed{
 2\mathcal K_{K,T}
 =(X(0),\mathsf O_{M_K,T}(0)X(0)).}
 \tag{UPG.14}
\]

Thus a positive non-scalar storage which cancels the complete insertion does
exist.  But it is computed backward from the terminal time and its value at
the datum is exactly the open action.  Positivity has been obtained by
putting the desired future response into the Gramian; `(UPG.14)` is not a
datum estimate.

## 4. The forward causal correction and its exact coercivity alias

Now choose any datum-computable self-adjoint initial metric \(\mathsf P_0\)
and solve the same Lyapunov law forward:

\[
 \boxed{
 \partial_t\mathsf P
 +\mathscr G_u^*\mathsf P
 +\mathsf P\mathscr G_u
 =-\mathsf R_B,
 \qquad
 \mathsf P(0)=\mathsf P_0.}
 \tag{UPG.15}
\]

This is the exact causal history correction.  It retains the full insertion
operator \(\mathscr A_u\) rather than estimating either parent leg.  Its
storage satisfies

\[
 {d\over dt}{1\over2}(X,\mathsf PX)
 =-{1\over2}(X,\mathsf R_BX).
 \tag{UPG.16}
\]

At finite Galerkin level \(\Phi(T,0)\) is invertible.  Subtracting the
backward solution `(UPG.13)` from `(UPG.15)`, or integrating `(UPG.16)`,
gives the exact endpoint identity

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 &(X(T),(\mathsf P(T)-\mathsf M_B)X(T))\\
 &\qquad=(X(0),\mathsf P_0X(0))-2\mathcal K_{K,T}\\
 &\qquad=(X(0),(\mathsf P_0-\mathsf O_{B,T}(0))X(0)).
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{UPG.17}
\]

Consequently, for any datum quantity \(C_0\ge0\),

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 &(X(T),(\mathsf P(T)-\mathsf M_B)X(T))\ge-C_0
 \quad\text{for every }T<T_*\\
 &\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 \mathcal K_{K,T}
 \le{1\over2}(X(0),\mathsf P_0X(0))+{C_0\over2}
 \quad\text{for every }T<T_*.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{UPG.18}
\]

In particular, demanding that the causal corrected metric dominate the
critical terminal metric on the actual state,

\[
 (X(T),\mathsf P(T)X(T))
 \ge (X(T),\mathsf M_BX(T))-C_0,
 \tag{UPG.19}
\]

is exactly a bounded-real/observability inequality for the action, not a
new payment.  An operator inequality \(\mathsf P(T)\succeq\mathsf M_B\) on
the whole state space is stronger; restricted to the actual rank-one parent
orbit, its quadratic-form content is already `(UPG.18)`.

This also classifies the proposed correction of the positive parent-age
storage.  If \(\mathsf P_{\rm heat}\) is the fixed positive Stokes-age metric
behind `(SH.253)`--`(SH.258)`, the full insertion contributes the metric
commutator.  On the response-parent block put

\[
 \widehat{\mathscr A}_u
 :=\begin{pmatrix}0&0\\0&\mathscr A_u\end{pmatrix}.
 \tag{UPG.20a}
\]

Then the commutator is

\[
 \mathsf S_u
 :=\widehat{\mathscr A}_u^*\mathsf P_{\rm heat}
   +\mathsf P_{\rm heat}\widehat{\mathscr A}_u.
 \tag{UPG.20}
\]

A history correction \(\mathsf H\) satisfying

\[
 \mathsf H'
 +\mathscr G_u^*\mathsf H+\mathsf H\mathscr G_u
 =-\mathsf S_u
 \tag{UPG.21}
\]

cancels the joined binomial insertion exactly.  But
\(\mathsf P=\mathsf P_{\rm heat}+\mathsf H\) is an instance of `(UPG.15)`.
Its required lower bound is therefore the same endpoint condition
`(UPG.18)`, after the explicit datum terms are restored.  Passing from a
scalar completed action to a non-scalar causal metric does not remove the
coercivity payment.

## 5. Parent-fiber collapse forbids an ambient tensor bound

The preceding equivalence cannot be repaired by comparing the positive
Gramian with the uncollapsed product-tensor norm.  In Fourier variables the
observation \(\mathscr C\) integrates a parent density over the fiber
\(p+q=k\).  The map

\[
 J_k:f(k,\cdot)\longmapsto\int f(k,p)\,dp
 \tag{UPG.22}
\]

is not bounded from the parent-frequency \(L^2\) fiber to the collapsed
output.  More importantly, `(SH.224)`--`(SH.228)` realize the sharp loss on
lawful smooth divergence-free helical data:

\[
 \boxed{
 {\|\Lambda^{-1/2}Q[v_N,v_N]\|_2^2
  \over
  \displaystyle\iint {|\Theta_N(k,p)|^2\over|k|}\,dp\,dk}
 \gtrsim N^3.}
 \tag{UPG.23}
\]

The same \(N^3\) ratio persists through one heat lifetime.  Hence no
cutoff-uniform inequality of the form

\[
 \mathsf O_{\Lambda,T}(0)
 \preceq C\,\mathsf P_{\rm paid}
 \tag{UPG.24}
\]

can follow from an energy-paid ambient product-parent metric with a universal
constant.  The skewness in `(UPG.5)` preserves full parent interference but
does not bound the collapse observation.  Any surviving inequality must be
restricted to the cumulative actual Navier--Stokes range and must pay the
rebuilding of coherent parent incidence through the same history.

## 6. Exact verdict for the non-scalar/history-dependent storage attack

The complete storage search therefore has the following exact outcome:

\[
 \boxed{
 \begin{aligned}
 &\text{uncollapsed parent tensor plus causal response}
   &&\Longrightarrow\text{the exact block law `(UPG.8)`};\\
 &\text{backward full-history Gramian}
   &&\Longrightarrow\text{positive cancellation, but value = open action};\\
 &\text{forward causal Lyapunov correction}
   &&\Longrightarrow\text{exact insertion cancellation, but}\
 &&&\hspace{12mm}\text{lower bound }\Longleftrightarrow
       \text{ bounded critical response action};\\
 &\text{ambient product-parent comparison}
   &&\Longrightarrow\text{failure by the lawful }N^3\text{ collapse}.
 \end{aligned}}
 \tag{UPG.25}
\]

Accordingly, no bounded-below causal storage and no smaller datum theorem is
proved here.  The only legitimate continuation inside this family is an
actual-range, cumulative parent-incidence inequality strong enough to prove
the shell contraction `(HPF.41)` with an independently datum-finite residual.
Without that independent residual, the causal coercivity condition
`(UPG.18)` is exactly `(TIR.37a)` in non-scalar Gramian form and must not be
reported as progress toward its proof.
