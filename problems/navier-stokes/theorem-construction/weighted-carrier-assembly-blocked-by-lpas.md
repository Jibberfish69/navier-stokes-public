# Weighted Carrier Assembly Blocked By LPAS

## Target

The weighted carrier closure is

```math
\int_0^T(\Lambda_N+\Theta_N^\sharp)(t)E_N(t)dt
\le
\varepsilon\nu\int_0^TD_N(t)dt+C2^{-2\delta N}.
```

## Current product status

The collar product

```math
\int_0^T\Theta_N^\sharp E_Ndt
```

closes after the tail-square reserve is promoted.

The low-mode product

```math
\int_0^T\Lambda_NE_Ndt
```

closes after the tail-generated part uses the same tail-square reserve and the finite-mode coefficient measure is applied on terminal windows.

Both product closures currently require the active-square far-corona theorem

```math
\int_0^TLPAS_N(t)dt
\le
\varepsilon\nu\int_0^TD_N(t)dt+C2^{-2\delta N}.
```

## Result

Weighted carrier assembly remains blocked by LPAS/FC. Once LPAS/FC is installed, the collar product and low-mode product promote together, and the displayed weighted carrier estimate follows by addition.
