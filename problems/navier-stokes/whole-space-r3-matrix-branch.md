# Whole-Space R3 Silver Branch

## Status

Silver supplies the same VPI participation-complement classification on the
whole-space branch. It does not prove that the original history retains full
participation at a candidate terminal time, so it is not a global-regularity
proof. The old positive tail-export dichotomy and its no-third-branch
presentation remain historical support.

## Fixed object

Fix one smooth divergence-free whole-space datum, one positive viscosity, and
the one no-reset original Navier--Stokes history issuing from that datum. Full
VPI participation means the same history retains transport, the simultaneous
nonlocal pressure response, viscosity, incompressibility, material ancestry,
and a coherent field realization. Merely satisfying Navier--Stokes in a weak or
distributional sense is not enough.

```math
\neg\operatorname{Smooth}(Q)
```


the candidate is no longer the original full VPI participant.

Pack stays outside CM. An exterior packet or positive-radius window may locate

branch.

## Other PDE boundary

The analysis starts with the original fixed-viscosity Navier--Stokes history.
Euler appears only if the candidate departure actually removes the viscous
term and leaves no stress, forcing, pressure, incompressibility, or ancestry
defect. Any surviving residual selects the corresponding defect, forced, or
Reynolds law. These equations classify how original participation was lost;
they do not start a replacement proof.
