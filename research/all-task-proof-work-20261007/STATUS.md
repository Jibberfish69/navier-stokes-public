This package preserves the task’s worked mathematical derivations, original-source executions and bounded reviews. It includes positive constructions, conditional implications, exact cancellations and findings at their stated hypotheses. It does not turn a later sufficient criterion into a prerequisite of the original proof.

The original proposed order is: one datum and prescribed force → complete nonlinear/pressure mixed graph and common local history → claimed datum-generated whole-terminal finite rectangles → literal restrictions and both intersections → endpoint jet and same-absolute-time force restart.
