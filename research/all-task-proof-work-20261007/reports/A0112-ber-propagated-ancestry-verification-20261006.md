## 2. Original pressure-complete band edge and the six assignments

Write \(A=\Lambda^2\), \(K=\|u_0\|_2>0\), and
\[
u_t+\nu Au+N(u,u)=0,
\qquad N(f,g)=\mathbb P((f\cdot\nabla)g).
\tag{BA1}
\]
The pressure in every ordered \(N\) is retained: \(N(f,g)=(f\cdot\nabla)g+\nabla\pi(f,g)\), \(\nabla\pi=-(I-\mathbb P)((f\cdot\nabla)g)\). For the complete velocity this is the usual slaved \(p=R_iR_j(u_i u_j)\). Every readout is of this same fluid.

Fix \(r\) above the bounded FIM collar and
\[
a=c_{\rm st}(\nu/K)^{2/5}r^{4/5},\qquad 2a<r,
\quad U=P_{<a}u,\ H=P_{\ge a}u,\ V=Q_ru,\ B=(I-Q_r)H.
\tag{BA2}
\]
With \(L_a z=N(U,z)+N(z,U)\), BER.5/PLF.17 gives
\[
C=Q_r[L_a,Q_r]H=-Q_rL_aB.
\tag{BA3}
\]
The exact sharp support is
\[
|p|<a,\quad r-a<|q|<r,\quad r\le|p+q|<r+a.
\tag{BA4}
\]
Thus the band input in BA3 may be replaced by \(B_e=P_{r-a<|\xi|<r}u\), and the source output lies in \([r,r+a)\). Both \(N(U,B_e)\) and \(N(B_e,U)\), with their output Leray tensors, are present.

For the source's triad set \(\Gamma\), put \(T(f,g;z)=-\operatorname{Re}(N_\Gamma(f,g),\Lambda z)\). The original signed relation is
\[
\begin{aligned}
J^{\rm be}&=T(U,B;V)+T(B,U;V)=\operatorname{Re}(C,\Lambda V),\\
J^{\rm ret}&=T(U,V;B)+T(V,U;B)+T(B,V;U)+T(V,B;U),\\
J^{(6)}&=J^{\rm be}+J^{\rm ret},\qquad J^{\rm be}=J^{(6)}-J^{\rm ret}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{BA5}
\]
The first transport pair satisfies, by integration by parts and incompressibility,
\[
T(U,B;V)+T(U,V;B)=-\operatorname{Re}([\Lambda,U\cdot\nabla]V,B).
\tag{BA6}
\]
The other low–high terms differentiate \(U\); the high–high-to-low assignments differentiate the low output including its critical multiplier. This directly recovers the two derivative placements in HCF.14 and FIM.29. For largest frequency \(\lambda_j\) and low admission \(a_j=c_0(\nu/K)^{2/5}\lambda_j^{4/5}\), kinetic Cauchy pays the low sums by \(CKa_j^{5/2}\) and \(CKa_j^{7/2}\). Their viscous ratios are respectively \(Cc_0^{5/2}\) and
\[
C c_0^{7/2}(\nu/K)^{2/5}\lambda_j^{-1/5}\le Cc_0^{7/2}
\quad(\lambda_j\ge(\nu/K)^2).
\tag{BA7}
\]
The complete signed submask includes all six assignments and reality partners. Shrinking the free static constant therefore gives the original BER.13 bound
\[
|J^{(6)}|\le\theta\nu\|\Lambda^{3/2}P_{\sim r}u\|_2^2.
\tag{BA8}
\]
BA8 is a same-time complete-current payment. Its proof does not identify \(J^{\rm be}\) alone with that complete current. The later positive payment in §6 is a separate derived consequence of the actual source equation, not an assertion that BA8 already bounded an arbitrary transported receiver.

## 3. Exact projected propagation and receiver custody

On the solenoidal range of \(Q_r\), the original propagator solves
\[
z_t+\nu Az+Q_rL_a(t)z=0.
\tag{BA9}
\]
Since transport is skew against this solenoidal receiver,
\[
\tfrac12(\|z\|_2^2)'+\nu\|\nabla z\|_2^2
=-\langle z,(z\cdot\nabla)U\rangle.
\tag{BA10}
\]
With \(\ell_a=\sup_t\|\nabla U(t)\|_{\infty,F}\le C_BKa^{5/2}\le\varepsilon\nu r^2\), \(0<\varepsilon<1\), PLF.14 follows:
\[
\|\mathscr U_{r,a}(t,s)\|_{2\to2}\le e^{-(1-\varepsilon)\nu r^2(t-s)}.
\tag{BA11}
\]
The projection remains in the generator. The actual \(V=Q_ru\) solves
\[
V_t+\nu AV+Q_rL_aV=F^{\rm hh}+C,
\qquad F^{\rm hh}=-Q_rN(H,H),
\tag{BA12}
\]
because \(2a<r\) removes the low–low output. Hence
\[
V(t)=\mathscr U(t,s)V(s)+\int_s^t\mathscr U(t,\tau)(F^{\rm hh}+C)(\tau)d\tau.
\tag{BA13}
\]
Pressure is present in the evolution even though its inner product with a solenoidal full receiver vanishes in BA10.

The exact adjoint is \(L_a^*w=\mathbb P[-(U\cdot\nabla)w+(\nabla U)^Tw]\). Let \(g(\tau)=\Lambda V(\tau)\), \(\Psi_t(\tau)=\mathscr U(t,\tau)^*\Lambda V(t)\). Then
\[
\Psi_\tau=(\nu A+Q_rL_a^*Q_r)\Psi,
\qquad\Psi_t(t)=\Lambda V(t).
\tag{BA14}
\]
BER.22 is exactly
\[
\|\Lambda^{1/2}V(t)\|_2^2
=\operatorname{Re}(V(s),\Psi_t(s))
+\int_s^t\operatorname{Re}(F^{\rm hh},\Psi_t)
+\int_s^t\operatorname{Re}(C,\Psi_t).
\tag{BA15}
\]
With \(D_t(\tau)=\Psi_t(\tau)-g(\tau)\), the last integral is
\[
\int_s^t\operatorname{Re}(C,\Psi_t)
=\int_s^tJ^{(6)}-\int_s^tJ^{\rm ret}+\int_s^t\operatorname{Re}(C,D_t).
\tag{BA16}
\]
This retains both original BER.26 terms with their signs. As an additional direct execution, the defect obeys
\[
\begin{aligned}
\partial_\tau D_t-(\nu A+Q_rL_a^*Q_r)D_t
={}&2\nu A\Lambda V

\tag{BA17}

The sign of its backward integral is minus: \(D_t(\tau)=-\int_\tau^t\mathscr U(s,\tau)^*(\text{right side of BA17})(s)ds\). No source, pressure or commutator is discarded.

## 5. The original recent heat parent and all five ancestry terms

Fix the original lag \(\tau\) and \(\sigma(t)=(t-\tau)_+\), \(\ell(t)=\min(t,\tau)\). Denote \(x=Q_ru\). The original recent nonlinear parent is
\[
V_\tau(t)=x(t)-S_{\ell(t)}x(\sigma(t)),\qquad S_s=e^{-\nu sA}.
\tag{BA21}
\]
For \(t>\tau\), its exact low-flow row is
\[
\begin{aligned}
(\partial_t+\nu A+Q_rL_t)V_\tau
&=F_t-S_\tau F_{t-\tau}+K_{\rm lag},\quad F=F^{\rm hh}+C,\\
K_{\rm lag}
&=Q_r[S_\tau L_{t-\tau}-L_tS_\tau]x(t-\tau)\\
&=Q_rS_\tau(L_{t-\tau}-L_t)x(t-\tau)
+Q_r[S_\tau,L_t]x(t-\tau).
\end{aligned}
\tag{BA22}
\]
For \(t\le\tau\), its source is \(F_t-Q_rL_tS_tx(0)\). Both delayed endpoints, the change in the actual low coefficient and the heat/low-flow commutator remain. Replacing \(S_\tau\) by \(\mathscr U(t,t-\tau)\) would change BA21's original parent.

The exact compatible pointwise decomposition is instead
\[
\begin{aligned}
V_\tau(t)&=E_{\rm lag}(t)+z^{\rm hh}_{t,\sigma(t)}+z^{\rm be}_{t,\sigma(t)},\\
E_{\rm lag}(t)&=(\mathscr U(t,\sigma(t))-S_{\ell(t)})x(\sigma(t)),\\
z^{\rm hh}_{t,\sigma}&=\int_\sigma^t\mathscr U(t,s)F^{\rm hh}(s)ds,\qquad
z^{\rm be}_{t,\sigma}=\int_\sigma^t\mathscr U(t,s)C(s)ds.
\end{aligned}
\tag{BA23}
\]
For the instantaneous BER parent, replace \(E_{\rm lag}\) by the actual inherited \(\mathscr U(t,\sigma)x(\sigma)\). For either version write \(z=z^{\rm be}\), \(W=W^0+z^{\rm hh}=V_{\rm actual}-z\). The complete band-edge-containing source is exactly

Only after retaining the five terms is this compressed into three bilinear terms. Its actual receiver is

J_I[F_{\rm be}]=\int_I\operatorname{Re}(\Lambda^{-1/2}F_{\rm be},\Lambda^{3/2}u)dt,

exactly BER.31–32 and DRF.4. Source histories extend over their clipped interval hull \(((\inf I-\tau)_+,\sup I)\); present/delayed insertions alone do not describe all those histories.

## 6. New derived fixed-cut positive payment

This section is a derived estimate from BA3 and BA9, rather than an equation printed as a theorem in BER. It keeps the actual source and complete receiver. The original free \(c_{\rm st}\) may be fixed smaller once, so that \(\varepsilon\le1/8\). BER.7, .13 and PLF.9 all permit this same fixed shrinking. There is no restriction on the amplitude \(K\) of the datum. Without this extra choice of the free static constant, finite-cut Gronwall still gives a finite price; the following choice gives a sharper polynomial price.

For the Fourier convention in STK.13a, sharp Bernstein constants may be taken as \(c_b=1/(\sqrt6\pi)\), \(c_g=1/(\sqrt{10}\pi)\):
\[
\|U\|_\infty\le c_bKa^{3/2},\qquad
\|\nabla U\|_{\infty,F}\le c_gKa^{5/2}.
\tag{BA26}
\]
Here \(F\) specifies the Frobenius matrix norm. Choose \(c_gc_{\rm st}^{5/2}\le1/8\); finite smooth-collar constants can instead be included in these constants. Since BA4 restricts the band input to \(B_e\),
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|C(t)\|_2
&\le\|U\|_\infty\|\nabla B_e\|_2
+\|\nabla U\|_{\infty,F}\|B_e\|_2\\
&\le C_eKa^{3/2}r\|B_e\|_2,
\qquad C_e=c_b+c_g/2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{BA27}
\]
The exact projectors are \(L^2\) contractions, and \(a<r/2\). Put \(D_e=\int_0^T\|\nabla B_e\|_2^2dt\). Then the entire terminal source square is paid:
\[
C_2:=\int_0^T\|C(t)\|_2^2dt
\le4C_e^2K^2a^3D_e
\le\frac{2C_e^2K^4a^3}{\nu}.
\tag{BA28}
\]
This uses only the kinetic \(\int\|\nabla u\|_2^2\le K^2/(2\nu)\), not a critical-height entry.

For a fixed source start \(\sigma\), \(z(t;\sigma)=z^{\rm be}_{t,\sigma}\) solves
\[
z_t+\nu Az+Q_rL_az=C,\qquad z(\sigma;\sigma)=0,\quad z\in\operatorname{Ran}Q_r.
\tag{BA29}
\]
Test against \(Az\). Pressure and \(Q_r\) commute with \(A\) and may be removed from this solenoidal inner product, while remaining in BA29. A single integration by parts in the transport term gives
\[
|\langle(U\cdot\nabla)z,Az\rangle|
\le\ell_a\|\nabla z\|_2^2,
\qquad
|\langle(z\cdot\nabla)U,Az\rangle|
\le\ell_a\|z\|_2\|Az\|_2.
\tag{BA30}
\]
No \(\nabla^2U\) estimate or derivative of \(C\) is required. Since \(\|\nabla z\|_2^2\le r^{-2}\|Az\|_2^2\), \(\|z\|_2\le r^{-2}\|Az\|_2\), the two costs are at most \(2\varepsilon\nu\|Az\|_2^2\). Young gives \(\langle C,Az\rangle\le(\nu/4)\|Az\|_2^2+\nu^{-1}\|C\|_2^2\). Thus, for \(Y_z=\|\nabla z\|_2^2\),
\[
Y_z'+\nu\|Az\|_2^2\le\frac2\nu\|C\|_2^2,
\qquad
Y_z'+\nu r^2Y_z\le\frac2\nu\|C\|_2^2.
\tag{BA31}
\]
Consequently, uniformly over all actual source-window starts,
\[
Y_z(t;\sigma)\le\frac2\nu\int_\sigma^t e^{-\nu r^2(t-s)}\|C(s)\|_2^2ds,
\qquad M:=\sup_{0\le\sigma\le t<T}Y_z(t;\sigma)\le\frac{2C_2}{\nu}.
\tag{BA32}
\]
For any measurable choice \(\sigma(t)\in[0,t]\), including the original fixed-lag start, domination by the nonnegative kernel with lower start zero and Fubini yield
\[
D_z:=\int_0^T Y_z(t;\sigma(t))dt\le\frac{2C_2}{\nu^2r^2}.
\tag{BA33}
\]
BA33 does not differentiate \(\sigma(t)\) and does not assert the \(\int\|Az\|^2\) estimate for a varying-start family. Its gradient mass is exactly what the following source estimate uses. For a fixed start only, BA31 additionally pays \(\int_\sigma^T\|Az\|^2\le2C_2/\nu^2\) and \(\int\|\Lambda^{3/2}z\|^2\le2C_2/(\nu^2r)\).

The selected CDR source is its bounded symmetric mask times the full tensor \(-i\mathbb P_k[(\widehat f(p)\cdot q)\widehat g(q)]\). The full finite index/assignment/reality sum is included in a single constant \(C_Q\). Uniformly in its actual frozen mask and cuts,
\[
\|\mathcal Q^{\rm ch}[f,g]\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}
\le C_Q\|\nabla f\|_2\|\nabla g\|_2.
\tag{BA34}
\]
This is the original CDR.27/RHL.10–10b modulus-convolution operation. A complete dual justification that avoids an unjustified rough-mask \(L^p\) multiplier theorem is to bound the dual Fourier integral by the nonnegative convolution of \(|\widehat f(p)|\), \(|q||\widehat g(q)|\), and \(|\widehat\phi(-p-q)|\). With the normalization \((2\pi)^{-6}\) in STK.13b, Fourier inversion identifies this convolution with the integral of the three scalar Fourier majorants. They need not be pointwise positive. Hölder uses \(L^6,L^2,L^3\), and Sobolev gives \(\|f^\#\|_6\le S_6\|\nabla f\|_2\), \(\|\phi^\#\|_3\le S_3\|\Lambda^{1/2}\phi\|_2\). Plancherel preserves their homogeneous norms. Finite Fourier truncation followed by monotone convergence of the nonnegative convolution justifies the general bound. The fixed finite tensor multiplicity, including Leray, is in \(C_Q\); it is not only one scalar component supremum. No cutoff is imposed on the inner children of either actual parent.

For the complete BA24 and \(W=V_{\rm actual}-z\), let \(Y_V=\|\nabla V_{\rm actual}\|_2^2\). BA34 gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|F_{\rm be}\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}^2
&\le C_Q^2(2\sqrt{Y_WY_z}+Y_z)^2\\
&\le C_Q^2(8Y_WY_z+2Y_z^2)
\le C_Q^2(16Y_VY_z+18Y_z^2).
\end{aligned}
\tag{BA35}
\]
The exact five-term sum is bounded together. No separate \(W^0\) or \(z^{\rm hh}\) membership has been assumed; \(Y_W\le2Y_V+2Y_z\) is an algebraic consequence of the actual parent.

Let \(D_V=\int_0^T Y_Vdt\). For the instantaneous parent \(D_V\le K^2/(2\nu)\). For the original recent heat parent, its exact mild difference BA21 gives the uniform kinetic bound \(D_V\le3K^2/\nu\), as derived in [RR.4](A0563-response-remainder-storage-continuations-20261006.md). BA32–35 now give

\begin{aligned}
E_{\rm be}:=\|F_{\rm be}\|_{L^2(0,T;\dot H^{-1/2})}^2
&\le C_Q^2M(16D_V+18D_z)\\
&\le\frac{32C_Q^2 C_2D_V}{\nu}
+\frac{72C_Q^2 C_2^2}{\nu^3r^2}.
\end{aligned}
\tag{BA36}
\]
For any actual receiver interval \(I\subset(0,T)\) and any measurable weight \(|\omega|\le1\), the full signed pairing has the absorption price
\[
\begin{aligned}
\left|\int_I\omega\operatorname{Re}(\Lambda^{-1/2}F_{\rm be},\Lambda^{3/2}u)dt\right|
\le{}&\delta\nu\int_I\|\Lambda^{3/2}u\|_2^2dt
+\frac{E_{\rm be}}{4\delta\nu}\\
\le{}&\delta\nu\int_I\|\Lambda^{3/2}u\|_2^2dt
+\frac{96C_e^2C_Q^2K^4a^3D_e}{\delta\nu^3}

\end{aligned}
\tag{BA37}
\]
This is a finite prescribed-data fixed-cut price for the complete original band-edge ancestry. It is stronger than leaving that ancestry as an unidentified signed survivor. It is not a cutoff-uniform estimate as \(r\to\infty\) by itself: its displayed positive envelope has \(a^3\sim r^{12/5}\) and \(a^6/r^2\sim r^{14/5}\). This observation does not lower-bound the actual band-edge fields or their signed current. The original lawful record choice in the next section resolves the price on that selected sequence.
