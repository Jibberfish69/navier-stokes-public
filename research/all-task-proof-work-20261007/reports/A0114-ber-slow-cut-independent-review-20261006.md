## 3. Full pressure-complete H1 payment

Sharp Bernstein in STK's convention gives \(c_b=1/(\sqrt6\pi)\), \(c_g=1/(\sqrt{10}\pi)\):
\[
\|U\|_\infty\le c_bKa^{3/2},\quad
\|\nabla U\|_{\infty,F}\le c_gKa^{5/2}
                    =c_gc_{\rm st}^{5/2}\nu\rho^2.
\]
The norm on the velocity-gradient matrix is Frobenius. FIM.28–34 and PLF.8–9 allow fixing the original free constant once so that \(\varepsilon=c_gc_{\rm st}^{5/2}\le1/8\), also below the admitted static exterior constant. This changes no cut in physical time.

With \(B_e=P_{\rho-a<|\xi|<\rho}u\), \(C_e=c_b+c_g/2\),
\[
\begin{split}
\|C\|_2&\le C_eKa^{3/2}\rho\|B_e\|_2,\\
C_2:=\int_0^{T_*}\|C\|_2^2
 &\le4C_e^2K^2a^3\int_0^{T_*}\|\nabla B_e\|_2^2
 \le 2C_e^2K^4a^3/\nu.
\end{split}
\tag{BR.5}
\]
The factor four uses \(a<\rho/2\). The band gradient mass is at most the actual kinetic mass \(K^2/(2\nu)\); no critical-height norm enters.

For each fixed source start, \(z_t+\nu Az+Q_\rho L_a z=C\), \(z(\sigma)=0\). Testing with \(Az\) keeps the complete projected pressure in the equation. The self-adjoint Leray and sharp high projections may be removed from this particular pairing because \(Az\) is solenoidal and in their range. Transport integration by parts gives at most \(\|\nabla U\|_{\infty,F}\|\nabla z\|_2^2\). Stretching costs at most \(\|\nabla U\|_{\infty,F}\|z\|_2\|Az\|_2\). Each is bounded by \(\varepsilon\nu\|Az\|_2^2\), using the exact high spectral gap.

Young's inequality with \(\nu/4\) therefore proves
\[
Y'+\nu\|Az\|_2^2\le(2/\nu)\|C\|_2^2,\quad
Y'+\nu\rho^2Y\le(2/\nu)\|C\|_2^2,\quad Y=\|\nabla z\|_2^2.
\tag{BR.6}
\]
No second derivative of \(U\) or derivative of \(C\) is used. Consequently
\[
Y(t;\sigma)\le\frac2\nu\int_\sigma^t
 e^{-\nu\rho^2(t-s)}\|C(s)\|_2^2ds,\quad
M\le2C_2/\nu,\quad
D_z\le2C_2/(\nu^2\rho^2).
\tag{BR.7}
\]
Here \(M\) is the supremum over every actual \(0\le\sigma\le t<T_*\). For any measurable \(\sigma(t)\), the last bound follows by enlarging the nonnegative convolution to start zero and then Fubini. It does not differentiate a changing start or claim the fixed-start \(Az\) time estimate for a varying-start family.

## 4. Bounded full mask and the five-term source price
