## Symmetrize the actual triad with pressure retained

Use the unitary convention \(c_{\mathcal F}=(2\pi)^{-3/2}\). For \(p+q=k\), \(a_p=\widehat u(p)\), \(b_q=\widehat u(q)\), incompressibility gives

\[
p\cdot a_p=0,\quad q\cdot b_q=0,\quad
a_p\cdot q=a_p\cdot k,\quad b_q\cdot p=b_q\cdot k.
\]

Changing \(p\leftrightarrow q\) in the complete convolution gives the exact symmetrized source

\[
\widehat Q(k)=\int\mathsf M(k;p,q)[a_p,b_q]dp,
\]
\[
\boxed{
\mathsf M(k;p,q)[a,b]
=-\frac{ic_{\mathcal F}}2\mathbb P_k[(a\cdot k)b+(b\cdot k)a].}
\tag{CF.3}
\]

The tensor symbol before imposing the transverse input conditions is

\[
\mathsf M_{iab}(k;p,q)
=-\frac{ic_{\mathcal F}}2
\left[k_m(\mathbb P_p)_{ma}(\mathbb P_k\mathbb P_q)_{ib}
+k_n(\mathbb P_q)_{nb}(\mathbb P_k\mathbb P_p)_{ia}\right].
\tag{CF.4}
\]

Every input and output projector is explicit. The factor one half is an exact averaging over the two original ordered parents. It does not identify \(\mathcal B(a,b)\) with \(\mathcal B(b,a)\). At \(k=0\) the divergence source is zero.

For noncollinear \(p,q\), let \(P=|p|\), \(Q=|q|\), \(K=|k|\), \(\theta=\angle(p,q)\),

\[
n=\frac{p\times q}{|p\times q|},\qquad e_j=n\times\widehat j,
\quad a=a_\parallel e_p+a_\perp n,\quad b=b_\parallel e_q+b_\perp n.
\]

The exact numerator after the outer Leray projection is

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb P_k[(a\cdot q)b+(b\cdot p)a]
=\sin\theta\left[
\frac{Q^2-P^2}{K}a_\parallel b_\parallel e_k
+(Qa_\parallel b_\perp-Pa_\perp b_\parallel)n\right].}
\tag{CF.5}
\]

To verify it, the unprojected in-plane vector is
\(\sin\theta\,a_\parallel b_\parallel(Qe_q-Pe_p)\). Its component along \(e_k\) is \((Q^2-P^2)/K\); the component along \(k\) is the pressure-gradient part and is removed. The out-of-plane component remains unchanged. The formula holds for complex Fourier coefficients and keeps their phases.

Consequences of the actual symbol are precise:

- Collinear parents have zero source, since both transport contractions vanish.
- High–high-to-low, \(P\sim Q\sim N\gg K\), has the genuine output derivative \(K\), rather than an artificial parent derivative \(N\). Equal parent radii cancel the in-plane term of (CF.5) exactly. Mixed polarizations retain the out-of-plane term, so this cancellation supplies no universal additional power of \(K/N\).
- High–low, \(P\sim N\gg Q\sim M\), has \(K\sim N\). The \(a\)-advector leg is of order \(M\), while the \(b\)-advector/high-receiver leg is of order \(N\). More explicitly,
  \[
  \mathsf M(k;p,q)[a,b]
  =-\frac{ic_{\mathcal F}}2(b\cdot p)a
  +O(c_{\mathcal F}M|a||b|).
  \]
  Here \(\mathbb P_k a=a+O(M/N)|a|\) follows from \(p\cdot a=0\). The strong ordered leg remains after pressure completion and symmetrization.

There is a sharper pressure cancellation than the elementary two-leg triangle bound. Let \(e=k/K\), \(a=\alpha e+a_\perp\), \(b=\beta e+b_\perp\). Then

\[
\mathbb P_k[(a\cdot k)b+(b\cdot k)a]
=K(\alpha b_\perp+\beta a_\perp),
\]
\[
\boxed{|S_k|\le K(|\alpha||b_\perp|+|\beta||a_\perp|)
\le K|a||b|.}
\tag{CF.5a}
\]

The complex parallel/perpendicular decomposition is Hermitian-orthogonal because \(e\) is real. This bound removes the longitudinal quadratic contribution and improves the symmetric density by a factor two. The angular bound \(|S_k|\le(P+Q)|\sin\theta||a||b|\) also holds. No selected triad sign test is used.

\[

\lesssim K\min(K,N,M)^{3/2}\|u_N\|_2\|u_M\|_2.
\tag{CF.5b}
\]

Thus the per-pair critical squares are bounded by \(\nu^{-1}K^4\|u_N\|_2^2\|u_M\|_2^2\) for \(K\ll N\sim M\), and by \(\nu^{-1}NM^3\|u_N\|_2^2\|u_M\|_2^2\) for \(M\ll N\sim K\), up to fixed band constants.
