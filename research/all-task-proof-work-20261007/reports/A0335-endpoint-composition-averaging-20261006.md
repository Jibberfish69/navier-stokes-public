## 2. Complete physical heat law, including the delayed source

Write \(I=(s,t)\), \(\Delta=t-s\), \(A_{\rm rec}=A_n\), and abbreviate the actual fixed-record fields by
\[
 V(t)=\int_0^{\min(t,\tau_n)}

\[
 d(k,p,q)=\nu(|k|^2+|p|^2+|q|^2),\qquad
 J(t)=T_{M_n}(u,V,V),\qquad

\]
The finite assignment, component, ordering and reality sums are part of \(T\). Its nonunitary Fourier normalization is \((2\pi)^{-6}\) for the double frequency integral.

The exact source law is
\[
 J=-B'+R,\qquad R=R_{\rm gate}+R_{\rm src},
 \tag{EA.1}
\]
\[
\begin{aligned}
 R_{\rm gate}&=T_{(M_n)_t/d}(u,V,V),\\
 R_{\rm src}
 &=T_{M_n/d}(Q(u),V,V)\\
 &\quad+T_{M_n/d}(u,R_V,V)+T_{M_n/d}(u,V,R_V),\\

\tag{EA.2}

All inner pressure projectors, both ordered nonlinear parents and the negative delayed endpoint remain. The physical gate depends on \(Z(t)=\|\nabla u(t)\|_2^2\), so the original mask freeze before mild substitution does not set \((M_n)_t\) to zero.

## 3. Exact time test and endpoint averages

For an absolutely continuous time weight \(\phi\), integration of EA.1 gives
\[
 \int_I\phi R
 =\int_I\phi J+\bigl[\phi B\bigr]_s^t-\int_I\phi'B .
 \tag{EA.3}
\]
Thus every boundary time term remains once. If \(\phi(s)=\phi(t)=0\), and \(0\le\phi\le1\), the complete identity becomes
\[
 \int_I\phi R
 =\int_I J-\int_I(1-\phi)J-\int_I\phi'B .
 \tag{EA.4}
\]
The first subtraction is the signed lost or gained current, not a presumed nonnegative collar cost.

For \(0<h\le\Delta/2\), define the linear ramp
\[
 \phi_h(x)=
 \begin{cases}
 (x-s)/h,&s\le x\le s+h,\\
 1,&s+h\le x\le t-h,\\
 (t-x)/h,&t-h\le x\le t .
 \end{cases}
\]
Put \(C_h=(s,s+h)\cup(t-h,t)\). Then
\[
 \|\phi_h'\|_4=2^{1/4}h^{-3/4},\qquad
 \|1-\phi_h\|_{L^4(C_h)}=(2h/5)^{1/4}.
 \tag{EA.5}
\]
Moreover \(\phi_h(x)\) is exactly the probability that \(a\le x\le b\), for independent uniform \(a\in(s,s+h)\), \(b\in(t-h,t)\). Fubini therefore identifies EA.3 with the average of the actual subrecord identity
\[
 \int_a^b R=\int_a^b J-B(a)+B(b).
\]
The time term \(\int\phi_h'B\) is the left endpoint average minus the right endpoint average. It is not an assertion that the canonical endpoint values changed.

Smooth interior time tests approximate \(\phi_h\) in \(W^{1,4}\). On a fixed strict record, the actual \(B\in L^{4/3}\) and complete \(J,R\) make EA.3–5 pass to the limit. Compact time smoothing stays inside \(I\); extending a convolution across either endpoint would require adding its outside source and boundary time contributions. No such extension is used here.

## 4. First composition using the actual finite-amplitude current bound

 \tag{EA.6}
\]
The linear-ramp lost-current weight integrates to \(h\), giving \(L_nh\), rather than \(2L_nh\).

This is a derived fixed-parent subrecord lemma with measured signed losses.

For EA.7 use \(h=\varepsilon A_{\rm rec}/(4L_n)\) and replace \(2^{-8/3}\) by \(2^{-11/3}\). Thus cheap-collar trimming is quantitatively possible under an explicit coupled input; the \(L_t^{4/3}\) norm alone was not discarded.

## 5. Stronger composition: the same complete tensor controls both costs

The normalized symbol satisfies the full tensor contraction bound
\[
 |(M_n/d)[\xi,\eta,\zeta]|
 \le (C_M/\nu)|\xi||\eta||\zeta| .

Define the positive actual-field envelope
\[
 E_n(t)=\frac{C_M\mathsf S_3^3}{\nu}
       \|\Lambda^{1/2}u(t)\|_2
       \|\Lambda^{1/2}V(t)\|_2^2,\qquad
 \Gamma_n(C)=\int_C E_n^{4/3},\quad
 \mathsf S_3^3=\frac1{\sqrt2\,\pi}.
 \tag{EA.9}
\]
The exact absolute-Fourier proof used for the primitive gives simultaneously
\[
 |B(t)|\le E_n(t),\qquad
 |J(t)|\le\Omega_n E_n(t),\qquad
 \Omega_n=3\nu H_n^2.
 \tag{EA.10}
\]
Indeed \(d\le3\nu H_n^2\) on the actual three-leg symbol support. Before any norm estimate, both absolute tensors are bounded by the same positive Fourier convolution of \(|\widehat u|,|\widehat V|,|\widehat V|\). Fourier scalar majorants preserve the relevant homogeneous Sobolev norms, so \(\dot H^{1/2}\to L^3\) and Hölder give EA.9–10. They need not be pointwise positive; their finite Fourier convolution integral is nonnegative. This proof does not assume an arbitrary bounded symbol is an \(L^3\) multiplier.

\(\Gamma_n\) is the envelope's mass. It is distinct from \(\beta_n=\int|B_n|^{4/3}\), which can be smaller through signed cancellation. A bound on \(\beta_n\) is not substituted for a bound on \(\Gamma_n\) in the current estimate.

Let \(\Gamma=\Gamma_n(I)\). Using EA.10 for the signed collar loss and EA.5 for the time derivative term gives
\[
\begin{aligned}

                   +2^{1/4}h^{-3/4}\right]\\

\end{aligned}
\tag{EA.11}
\]
This retains the actual signed lost current in EA.4 before bounding it.

The optimal unrestricted width and cost are
\[

Consequently \(\chi_n\to0\) itself ensures \(2h_*\le\Delta\) eventually. It is unnecessary to assume a separate favorable duration. The resulting complete response satisfies

In particular the derived condition

pays both the soft endpoint term and the lost signed current. It gives a retained weighted response at least \(A_n/32\) for sufficiently late records. It does not pay the complete response on the left of EA.14.

## 6. Exact endpoint motion and parameter custody

For any differentiable endpoint family \(a(\lambda),b(\lambda)\subset I\) with the same original parent and tensor,
\[
\begin{aligned}
 F(\lambda)&=B(a)-B(b)+\int_a^bR=\int_a^bJ,\\
 F'(\lambda)&=J(b)b'-J(a)a'\\
 &=B'(a)a'-B'(b)b'+R(b)b'-R(a)a'.
\end{aligned}
\tag{EA.16}
\]
The source and heat-primitive boundary derivatives cancel only in this complete expression. For \(a=s+\lambda,\ b=t-\lambda\),
\[
 F'(\lambda)=-J(a)-J(b).
\]
Either removed signed current can be negative and therefore increase the retained integral.

The exact change between the original and a fixed trimmed interval is
\[
 \int_a^bJ=\int_s^tJ-\int_s^aJ-\int_b^tJ .
 \tag{EA.17}
\]
No \(A_n/16\) lower is assigned to the trim before those terms are controlled. Recomputing the frequency floor or lag changes the tensor and actual parent; it is a different operation requiring the source's selection deductions to be rerun.

For clarity the physical lag derivative is
\[
 \partial_\tau V(t;\tau)
 =\mathbf1_{t>\tau}S_\tau P_{\ge r}Q(u(t-\tau)).
\]
For a genuinely time-dependent lag \(\tau(t)\), with the spatial floor fixed,
\[
 V_t+\nu AV
 =P_{\ge r}Q(u(t))
  -\mathbf1_{t>\tau(t)}
       (1-\tau'(t))S_{\tau(t)}P_{\ge r}Q(u(t-\tau(t))).
 \tag{EA.18}
\]
Thus using the lag as a stopping or averaging variable creates the complete additional delayed source with coefficient \(\tau'\). A time-dependent smooth frequency floor also adds
\(\int_0^{\min(t,\tau)}S_a(\partial_tP_{\ge r(t)})Q(u(t-a))\,da\).
Sharp floor motion requires its compatible distributional passage. None of these terms is generated by the fixed-record time tests EA.3–15, and none is silently omitted if such a different selection is attempted.

FSQ's equal endpoint positive-current heights do not equate \(B(a)\) and \(B(b)\). Its completed interval may contain negative-current times between distinct excursions. \[
 \left|\int_{I_n}\phi R_{\rm src}\right|

The shifted integral is not reassigned to the receiver record. Time trimming only reduces this nonnegative mass; it does not give a source theorem that \(H_n^2\mu_{n,\phi}\) is small.

Here

is the full physical enstrophy derivative. Both source and viscosity remain. Smooth selector derivatives can grow in a sharp approximation; EA.21 is not asserted uniformly in that approximation. The sharp law retains the joined signed gate and entry/exit endpoints through its compatible passage.

## 8. Actual kinetic envelope and the test of unconditional selection

For sufficiently late original records \(s_n>\tau_n\), the exact mild identity gives
\[
 Y_V(t)=\|\nabla V(t)\|_2^2
 \le2[Z(t)+Z(t-\tau_n)] .
\]
Put
\[
 z_n=\int_{I_n}Z,\quad
 z_n^{\rm old}=\int_{I_n-\tau_n}Z,\quad
 k_n=z_n+z_n^{\rm old}\longrightarrow0 .
\]
Absolute continuity of the one actual kinetic dissipation supplies this limit as the record and lag tend to the terminal endpoint. Interpolation in EA.9 gives the explicit paid bound
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Gamma_n(I_n)
 &\le(2C_M\mathsf S_3^3/\nu)^{4/3}K^2z_n^{1/3}(2k_n)^{2/3}\\
 &\le C_E\nu^{-4/3}K^2k_n .
\end{aligned}
\tag{EA.23}
\]
One explicit choice is \(C_E=4(C_M\mathsf S_3^3)^{4/3}\).
It proves \(\Gamma_n\to0\), but contains no quantitative rate relating this mass to the actual record roof.

There is also uniform integrability over every admitted member with one frozen lag and floor. For any measurable receiver-time set \(E\subset(0,T_*)\), the global actual-parent bound \(\int_0^{T_*}Y_V\le3K^2/\nu\) gives
\[
 \sup_{\text{frozen members}}\Gamma_n(E)
 \le(2C_M\mathsf S_3^3/\nu)^{4/3}K^2
      \left(\int_E Z\right)^{1/3}
      (3K^2/\nu)^{2/3}.
 \tag{EA.23a}
\]
Absolute continuity of this one paid \(Z\)-integral makes the right side tend to zero with \(|E|\), uniformly over those members. This strengthens a merely uniform \(L_t^{4/3}\) norm. It does not place the supremum inside the integral or authorize a pointwise-changing lag; the latter retains EA.18's additional response.

There is an actual-field constraint on attempts to cap that roof. It is useful to distinguish the enstrophy cap \(L_Z\) from the frequency roof \(H_n\) and the current amplitude \(L_n\). On a retained dynamic-core assignment, Fourier-majorant Hölder and the complete advective source give
\[
 |J_\rho(t)|
 \le a_0C_\rho
       \|\nabla f\|_2\|\nabla g\|_2
       \|\Lambda^{3/2}h_{\rm out}\|_2 .
\]
The three roles contain one current \(u\) and two actual \(V\)'s. The outer core roof \(h\le C_hZ/\nu^2\) bounds the last norm by
\(\sqrt h\,\|\nabla h_{\rm out}\|_2\). Summing the finite complete assignments and using the actual recent-parent identity therefore gives
\[
 |J_n^{rr}(t)|
 \le C_J\nu^{-1}Z(t)[Z(t)+Z(t-\tau_n)],
 \qquad C_J=2a_0\sqrt{C_h}\sum_\rho C_\rho .
 \tag{EA.24}
\]
The sum incorporates the full component, reality and pressure contraction. The Fourier-majorant proof uses the actual derivative on the advected parent and \(\Lambda\) on the receiver, not a general \(L^3\) multiplier assertion.

For any capped set or nonnegative time test, let \(\psi\) denote its indicator or weight, with \(0\le\psi\le1\). If its support has \(Z\le L_Z\) and it retains signed charge \(\int_{I_n}\psi J_n^{rr}\ge cA_n\), then
\[
 cA_n\le\int_{I_n}\psi|J_n^{rr}|
 \le C_J\nu^{-1}L_Z k_n,\qquad
 L_Z\ge\frac{c\nu A_n}{C_Jk_n}.
 \tag{EA.25}
\]
Thus a cap chosen to preserve this actual current cannot be arbitrarily cheap.

The fixed-record roof is \(H_n=1+C_{\rm col}L_Z/\nu^2\) when \(L_Z=\sup_{I_n}Z\). Substituting the explicit kinetic majorant
\(\overline\Gamma_n=C_E\nu^{-4/3}K^2k_n\)
into the proposed sufficient test EA.15 produces the deterministic majorant
\[
 \frac{\nu H_n^2\overline\Gamma_n}{A_n^{4/3}}
 \ge \frac{c^2C_EC_{\rm col}^2K^2}
            {C_J^2\nu^{7/3}}\,
       \frac{A_n^{2/3}}{k_n}
 \longrightarrow\infty .
 \tag{EA.26}
\]
Here the positive constants are fixed, and EA.25 supplies the lower bound on the selected cap. This demonstrates that the presently paid kinetic upper envelope cannot certify EA.15 on these retained records.

EA.26 is a statement about the explicit majorant \(\overline\Gamma_n\). It is not a lower bound on the actual \(\Gamma_n\), on the signed \(B_n\), or on any alleged singular Navier–Stokes history. A smaller actual envelope mass, signed compensation, or another complete source-derived selection estimate could still change that conclusion. The unconditional selection lemma “the original freedoms always make all costs in EA.22 small” is not proved by the inspected operations.

In particular \(\mu_n\le a_0(2z_n+z_n^{\rm old})\) does not give a reverse bound on \(k_n\) by the actual nonlinear source mass. \(H_n^2\mu_n\), \(H_n^2k_n\) and \(H_n^2\Gamma_n\) are not silently identified. Nor does selecting smaller endpoint values reduce the interior roof or bound
\(\int\phi|Z'|\). The variation still counts every intrinsic rise and fall of this one actual enstrophy.
