### 2.1 Canonical and branch endpoints

Write \(H=\|\Lambda^{1/2}u\|_2^2/2\), \(D=\|\Lambda^{3/2}u\|_2^2\), \(P=(Q(u),\Lambda u)\), and \(W(t)=\int_0^tP\). MRN.2–6 gives
\[
W=H-H(0)+\nu\int_0^tD,\quad A_n=4^nA_0,\quad
t_n=\inf\{W=A_n\},\quad s_n=\sup\{t<t_n:W=A_n/2\}.
\tag{RV1}
\]
Thus \(W\in[A_n/2,A_n]\) on the actual \(I_n=(s_n,t_n)\), and \(\int_{I_n}P=A_n/2\). These endpoints are fixed by the signed critical primitive. They are not stopping times of \(Z=\|\Lambda u\|_2^2\). The arbitrary spatial centers in MRN.9 provide no time-endpoint freedom.

DRF.6,14,19–23 fixes, using this original interval,
\[
K_n^4=\frac{\nu A_n}{512C_LU_0^4\Delta_n},\quad
r_n=c_{\rm st}(\nu/U_0)^{2/5}K_n^{4/5},\quad
\tau_n=\frac{1+\log(1+F_n/(\eta\nu A_n))}{2\nu r_n^2},\quad \eta=1/512.
\tag{RV2}
\]
Here \(F_n=C_{\rm old}(D_ne_n+D_n^2\Delta_n)\), \(D_n=4U_0^2r_n^2\), and \(e_n=\int_{I_n}\|\Lambda P_{\ge r_n}w\|_2^2\). The datum heat, old-containing parents and low output are refunded before DRF.25's signed floor \(\int_{I_n}J_n^{rr}\ge A_n/16\).

\tag{RV3}
\]
The edge is \((s_n,\min(s_n+\tau_n,t_n))\); the interior is \([s_n+\tau_n,t_n)\). All parameters in (RV2) remain those of \(I_n\). ## 3. Exact signed enstrophy calculation and stopped-cap variation

Put \(Y=\|Au\|_2^2\), \(A=-\Delta\). The whole-field DCT.22/JEM.51 receiver is
\[
Z'=2(Q,Au)-2\nu Y,\qquad Q=-\mathbb P(u\cdot\nabla u).
\tag{RV4}
\]
Only here, after full-field recombination, does the pressure pairing vanish because \(Au\) is solenoidal. Hölder, interpolation, Kato and the original sharp Sobolev constant give
\[
|(Q,Au)|\le \|u\|_6\|\nabla u\|_3\|Au\|_2
\le S_6^{3/2}Z^{3/4}Y^{3/4},\quad
S_6=\frac{2^{2/3}}{\sqrt3\,\pi^{2/3}}.
\]
The exact Young supremum gives \(2S_6^{3/2}Z^{3/4}Y^{3/4}\le\nu Y+\pi^{-4}\nu^{-3}Z^3\), so
\[
Z'\le \pi^{-4}\nu^{-3}Z^3-\nu Y.
\tag{RV5}
\]
For an actual interval on which \(Z\le L\), retain the signed endpoint difference:
\[
\operatorname{Var}_{[a,b]}Z=Z(a)-Z(b)+2\int_a^b(Z')_+
\le Z(a)-Z(b)+2\pi^{-4}\nu^{-3}L^2\int_a^b Z.
\tag{RV6}
\]
In particular the right side is at most \(L+2\pi^{-4}\nu^{-3}L^2\int Z\). This is a derived local NS bound, not an assumption that the original failed record stays under a prescribed cap.

For the **whole disconnected capped set**, use \(Z_L=\min(Z,L)\), not one entering endpoint per component. The Lipschitz chain rule gives
\[
\int_{B\cap\{Z<L\}}|Z'|=\operatorname{Var}_{B}Z_L
\le Z_L(a)-Z_L(b)+2\pi^{-4}\nu^{-3}L^2\int_B Z
\le L+2\pi^{-4}\nu^{-3}L^2\int_B Z.
\tag{RV7}
\]
Level plateaus have \(Z'=0\) almost everywhere, so changing the strict inequality does not change this integral. This joined truncation identity resolves the disconnected-component endpoint issue without discarding any crossing flux in the current equation.

The actual first upcrossing from \(Z(a)>0\) to \(L>Z(a)\), if it occurs, has
\[
b-a\ge\frac{\pi^4\nu^3}{2}\bigl(Z(a)^{-2}-L^{-2}\bigr)
\]
by integrating (RV5) for \(Z^{-2}\). Stopping at that crossing gives a valid cap but gives no lower bound for the critical/current mass accrued before it. MRN bounds \(W\), not the relative locations of these two stopping events.

## 4. A stronger actual recent-parent bound with both delayed fields

For a single ordered masked critical term with fields \(f,g,h\), bounded Fourier masks and pressure/helical projectors can be dominated **after** taking the absolute value of the complete term. Apply the original \(H^{1/2}\to L^3\) duality to scalar Fourier-modulus representatives, and \(H^1\to L^6\) to the first field. This gives
\[
|\mathcal T(f,g;h)|\le a_0\|\Lambda f\|_2\|\Lambda g\|_2
\|\Lambda^{3/2}h\|_2,\qquad a_0=S_3S_6=\sqrt{2/3}/\pi.
\tag{RV8}
\]
Explicitly, the dominated scalar product is that of the product with Fourier factors \(|\widehat f(p)|\), \(|q||\widehat g(q)|\), against \(|k||\widehat h(k)|\); its last \(H^{1/2}\) norm is \(\|\Lambda^{3/2}h\|_2\). This preserves the ordered physical derivative and uses the norm bound on the actual Leray projector. Each original mask is bounded before domination, and the finite six-role/LP normalization is included in \(C_{\rm sec}\). No same-field DCL estimate is transferred to a different receiver.

Every outer current/recent/recent leg in DRF.26 has the **current-time** FIM roof \(H(t)\le C_h Z(t)/\nu^2\). Restricting all three fields to that roof gives \(\|\Lambda^{3/2}h\|_2^2\le H(t)\|\Lambda h\|_2^2\). Sum all assignments to obtain
\[
|J_n^{rr}(t)|\le C_{\rm sec}a_0\sqrt{H(t)}\,\sqrt{Z(t)}\,Z_V(t).
\tag{RV9}
\]
For \(t>\tau_n\), the actual unforced mild identity is
\[
V=P_{\ge r_n}[u(t)-E_{\tau_n}u(t-\tau_n)],\qquad
Z_V\le2[Z(t)+Z(t-\tau_n)].
\]
For \(t\le\tau_n\), retain \(E_tu_0\) instead. Since \(\tau_n\to0\) and \(s_n\to T_*>0\), sufficiently large actual records satisfy \(s_n>\tau_n\). Then
\[
|J_n^{rr}(t)|\le C_J\nu^{-1}Z(t)[Z(t)+Z(t-\tau_n)],
\quad C_J=2C_{\rm sec}a_0\sqrt{C_h}.
\tag{RV10}
\]
The delayed velocity is essential. The current roof does not impose a cap on \(Z(t-\tau_n)\). For any measurable \(E\subset B_n\cap\{Z\le L\}\),
\[
\int_E|J_n^{rr}|\le \frac{C_JL}{\nu}\,
\mathfrak m_n,\qquad
\mathfrak m_n:=\int_{B_n}Z+\int_{(B_n-\tau_n)\cap[0,T_*)}Z.
\tag{RV11}
\]
Both terminal-tail intervals have vanishing paid \(Z\)-mass. In particular, an independently retained \(\int_EJ_n^{rr}\ge cA_n\) forces \(L\ge c\nu A_n/(C_J\mathfrak m_n)\). This is an actual conditional cap lower bound, proved using both physical recent parents.

## 5. Joint current retention, cap level and crossing control

\int_{B\cap\{Z>L_0\}}[J]_+\le cA_n/2.
\tag{RV12}
\]
Such a level exists because this original \(J\in L^1(B)\) and \(Z\) is finite on the compact preterminal interval. This is a current-weighted quantile; it is not assigned from an average of \(Z\). Every \(\ell\ge L_0\) then satisfies

Let \(N(\ell)\) count the actual interior crossings \(Z(t)=\ell\) in \(B\). One-dimensional change of variables on the intervals where \(Z'\ne0\) gives
\[
\int_{L_0}^{2L_0}N(\ell)d\ell

The image of the remaining critical set has zero measure: cover it by intervals on which \(|Z'|\le\epsilon\), then let \(\epsilon\downarrow0\). Therefore a regular level \(\ell\in[L_0,2L_0]\), different from the two endpoint values, can be chosen with

\tag{RV14}
\]
At a regular level its crossings are finite on this compact interval. This is an actual joint selection: (RV13), the cap \(\ell\), (RV7) and the count (RV14) hold together. It uses no simulated scalar profile.

### 5.1 Exact new cap flux, with original parameters frozen

For the same fixed smooth receiver with the retained floor, denote its cubic primitive by \(\mathcal B\), to distinguish it from the branch \(B\). The supporting full equation is \(J=-\mathcal B'+R_{\rm gate}+R_{rr}\). An additional smooth cap \(\zeta(Z)\) has
\[
\zeta J=-(\zeta\mathcal B)'+\zeta(R_{\rm gate}+R_{rr})+\zeta'(Z)Z'\mathcal B.
\tag{RV15}
\]
In the sharp cap limit at the chosen regular level \(\ell\), the last term is the actual finite crossing flux
\[
\mathcal X_\ell=-\sum_{Z(t_j)=\ell}\operatorname{sgn}Z'(t_j)\,\mathcal B(t_j).
\]
The clipped original branch endpoints remain separately in \(\zeta(a)\mathcal B(a)-\zeta(b)\mathcal B(b)\). On the capped set use \(H_\ell=C_h\ell/\nu^2\). The actual normalized primitive bound and both recent \(2U_0\) norms give
\[
|\mathcal X_\ell|\le4C\nu^{-1}H_\ell^{3/2}U_0^3N(\ell),
\qquad |\zeta(a)\mathcal B(a)|+|\zeta(b)\mathcal B(b)|
\le8C\nu^{-1}H_\ell^{3/2}U_0^3.
\tag{RV16}
\]
No uniform smooth-cap derivative is assumed; the limit is identified by its finite regular crossings and actual preterminal traces. Sharp original core gates still require their own complete distributional/trace passage; this argument does not invent derivatives for the original rough subblocks.

The full response upper on this same selection is
\[
\begin{aligned}
\int_{E_\ell}|R_{rr}|&\le4C\nu^{-1}H_\ell^2U_0^2\mu_{E_\ell},\\
\int_{E_\ell}|R_{\rm gate}|&\le
4CC_h\|\eta'\|_\infty\nu^{-3}H_\ell^{3/2}U_0^3K_n^{-1}
\left(\ell+2\pi^{-4}\nu^{-3}\ell^2\int_BZ\right),
\end{aligned}
\tag{RV17}

where
\[
\mu_{E_\ell}=2\int_{E_\ell}\|\Lambda^{-1/2}Q\|_2+

It is at most the whole-branch present/shifted source mass and tends to zero. Both delayed insertions are retained in this coefficient. The original \(\eta'\) belongs to the fixed smooth core gate, with its fixed per-triangle threshold; no uniform derivative estimate for its sharp approximants is inserted.

Combining the exact cap identity with these estimates gives a concrete joint finite bound:
\[
\begin{aligned}

Accordingly the particular positive roof-based primitive majorant in (RV16), divided by \(A_n\), has scale at least a fixed multiple of \(\sqrt{A_n}/\mathfrak m_n^{3/2}\). ## 7. Force terms and scope

For \(F=\mathbb Pf\), the exact whole-field row is \(Z'=2(Q+F,Au)-2\nu Y\). Allocating \(\nu Y/2\) to the nonlinear term and to the force term gives the independently checked finite bound
\[
Z'\le8\pi^{-4}\nu^{-3}Z^3-\nu Y+2\nu^{-1}\|F\|_2^2.
\]
Thus the forced capped variation is at most
\[
L+16\pi^{-4}\nu^{-3}L^2\int_BZ+4\nu^{-1}\int_B\|F\|_2^2.
\tag{RV20}
\]
Alternatively, integrating the force receiver by parts retains \(2(\nabla F,\nabla u)\), giving the original \(\pi^{-4}\) nonlinear coefficient and an additional \(4\sqrt L\int_B\|\nabla F\|_2\) in (RV7). Use whichever displayed known-force norm is finite on the given horizon.

For intrinsic recent parents, \(V=P[u(t)-E_\tau u(t-\tau)-\int_{t-\tau}^tE_{t-r}F(r)dr]\), so
\[
Z_V\le3\left[Z(t)+Z(t-\tau)+q_F(t)^2\right],\quad
q_F(t)=\int_{t-\tau}^t\|\nabla F(r)\|_2dr.
\]
The analogue of (RV10) retains this entire known-force lift. Its time mass obeys
\(\int_Bq_F^2\le\tau\min(\tau,|B|)\int_{(a-\tau,b)}\|\nabla F\|_2^2\).
For full-source recent parents the lift is absent because it is already included in \(V=P[u-E_\tau u_{\rm delayed}]\). The primitive uses both recent kinetic norms \(2K_T\), the full response adds the actual current force slot and gate term \(2L(F,Au)\), and full-source parents add both delayed force slots. Arbitrary amplitude changes these known quantities, without changing the algebra.
