The fully recombined **whole-space \(\mathbb R^3\)** sharp current \(J\) and its heat-oriented cubic primitive \(B\) have continuous representatives at every strict preterminal time on one fixed compact record. This follows from the actual source definitions, strong \(L^2\) continuity of the current and both recent legs, the compact operator roof, and the zero six-dimensional measure of each fixed positive geometric threshold level. The result concerns values and traces. It supplies no time BV estimate, separated differentiated-gate passage, bound uniform over the record sequence, or terminal limit. It is not asserted for a sharp discrete-frequency \(\mathbb T^3\) current.

## 2. The complete sharp multiplier and the compact bounds

For \(k+p+q=0\), put
\[
 h=\max\{|k|,|p|,|q|\},\quad
 a=\min\{|k|,|p|,|q|\},\quad
 z(k,p,q)=\nu^2\max\!\left\{C_h^{-1}h,\ c_Z^3h^4/a^3\right\},
 \qquad d(k,p,q)=\nu(|k|^2+|p|^2+|q|^2).
 \tag{SC4}
\]
This is exactly DCT.19, including **both** dynamic admission conditions. Static minimum-leg and output floors stay fixed. They need not be homogeneous.

In the STK Fourier convention, write the complete role tensor as
\[
 M(t;k,p,q)=M^0(k,p,q)\,\mathbf1_{\{Z(t)>z(k,p,q)\}}.
 \tag{SC5}
\]
The finite assignment sum, all reality partners, all static masks and all pressure projectors are included in \(M^0\). For example, one ordered pressure-complete critical assignment has tensor proportional to
\[
 -i|k|q_j(\mathbb P_k)_{i\ell},\qquad
 (\mathbb P_k)_{i\ell}=\delta_{i\ell}-k_i k_\ell/|k|^2,
 \tag{SC6}
\]
contracted with the actual current leg and two actual recent legs in their prescribed roles. All complementary receiver choices and the other parent order are retained, with the original normalization.

Define the fully summed scalar forms
\[
 J(t)=\mathcal T_{M(t)}(u(t),V(t),V(t)),\qquad
 B(t)=\mathcal T_{M(t)/d}(u(t),V(t),V(t)).
 \tag{SC7}
\]
This notation includes the role permutations; it does not turn the source into a positive assignment or an autonomous recent-velocity nonlinearity.

For any fixed compact preterminal neighborhood of \(t_0\), PSL.2–4 provides one finite, fixed envelope \(H\), with every operator leg in \(B_H\) and every retained leg bounded away from zero. The envelope is a proof domain; it is not a moving cutoff. Since the source and critical receiver together have order two,
\[
 \|M(t)\|_\infty\le C H^2,\qquad
 \|M(t)/d\|_\infty\le C/\nu .
 \tag{SC8}
\]
The second bound follows directly from
\(|k||q|\le (|k|^2+|q|^2)/2\); finite assignment/projector multiplicities change only \(C\).
Consequently PSL.7–8 yields
\[
 |\mathcal T_{M(t)}(f,g,h_1)|\le C H^{7/2}\|f\|_2\|g\|_2\|h_1\|_2,

       \|f\|_2\|g\|_2\|h_1\|_2.
 \tag{SC9}
\]
These are uniform in \(t\) only on this fixed neighborhood and for these value forms.

## 3. Independent proof of continuity

**Threshold levels.** Regard \(z\) as a function of the two independent frequencies \((p,q)\in\mathbb R^6\), with \(k=-p-q\). Off the measure-zero set where a leg vanishes,
\[
 z(\lambda p,\lambda q)=\lambda z(p,q),\qquad\lambda>0,
 \tag{SC10}
\]
and \(0<z(p,q)<\infty\). Write \((p,q)=r\theta\) in six-dimensional polar coordinates. For every nonsingular direction \(\theta\), \(z(r\theta)=r z(\theta)\). For any fixed \(c>0\), the equation \(z(r\theta)=c\) has exactly one radial solution. Radial Fubini therefore gives
\[
 \big|\{(p,q):z(-p-q,p,q)=c\}\big|_{\mathbb R^6}=0.
 \tag{SC11}
\]
At \(c=0\) there is no nonsingular solution. Intersecting these null sets with static admission, finite collars or the compact roof preserves their null measure. No differentiability of the piecewise min/max threshold is required.

**Leg continuity.** On the actual strict preterminal history, \(u(t)\) is strongly continuous in \(L^2\), and \(Z(t)=\|\Lambda u(t)\|_2^2\) is continuous. The full pressure-complete \(Q(u(t))\) is locally \(L^2\)-continuous. The moving-window heat integral (SC3), or the explicit assertion at PSL.16, gives strong \(L^2\) continuity of \(V\), including the window-onset time \(t=\tau\). No source endpoint is replaced by \(Q(V)\).

**Field differences.** For either tensor \(m(t)=M(t)\) or \(M(t)/d\), subtract the forms at \(t\) and \(t_0\) by inserting the three fixed legs at \(t_0\). The three resulting input differences tend to zero by (SC9), strong \(L^2\) continuity and local boundedness of the other legs. It remains to control
\[
 \mathcal T_{m(t)-m(t_0)}
       (u(t_0),V(t_0),V(t_0)).
 \tag{SC12}
\]

**Mask difference.** Continuity of \(Z\) and (SC11) imply
\[
 \mathbf1_{\{Z(t)>z(p,q)\}}
 \longrightarrow\mathbf1_{\{Z(t_0)>z(p,q)\}}
 \quad\text{for almost every }(p,q).
 \tag{SC13}
\]
For the fixed three \(L^2\) legs at \(t_0\), the absolute Fourier product on the compact operator roof is integrable. Explicitly, writing \(F,G,H_1\) for the absolute values of their Fourier transforms restricted to \(B_H\),
\[
 \iint F(-p-q)G(p)H_1(q)\,dp\,dq
 \le \|F\|_1\|G*H_1\|_\infty
 \le |B_H|^{1/2}\|F\|_2\|G\|_2\|H_1\|_2<\infty .
 \tag{SC14}
\]
The uniformly bounded fixed coefficient \(M^0\), or \(M^0/d\), times this product dominates the integrand in (SC12). Dominated convergence proves (SC12) tends to zero. Finite summation over every pressure-complete assignment, tensor component and reality partner preserves convergence.

Together these arguments prove
\[
 \boxed{J,\ B\in C(I;\mathbb R)}
 \tag{SC15}
\]
for the fully recombined sharp whole-space forms on every fixed strict compact record \(I\). They define the continuous representatives of the corresponding almost-everywhere value forms. The same proof extends to one-sided endpoints contained in a strict compact preterminal neighborhood.

## 4. Exact scope and use of traces

At a finite regular crossing of an **additional scalar cap** \(Z(t)=\ell\), (SC15) supplies the actual value \(B(t_j)\). Thus the capped primitive has well-defined boundary traces for the finite-crossing product formula when its complete original distributional response identity is separately available. Continuity alone does not establish that original identity after a sharp geometric-gate limit. It neither pays nor deletes any original gate/source response.

No claim is made that \(B\) or \(J\) has finite time variation. An \(L^2\) operator bound and moving-mask value convergence do not pass the terms containing \(\partial_tM\), and all differentiated pressure/current/delayed source terms remain in the full response. The existing PSL.28 distinction is preserved.

The argument is explicitly restricted to \(\mathbb R^3\). On \(\mathbb T^3\), frequency measure is discrete; a fixed positive threshold can contain finitely many nonzero triads with positive counting measure, and a sharp gate can jump at a crossing. The radial null-level argument is not a torus proof. The original fixed smooth selector has no such obstruction.

There is no bound uniform over \(n\), since the record roof \(H\) and local high norms can depend on the record. No assertion at \(T_*\), no terminal limit, and no independent upper payment for the surviving signed response follow from (SC15).

The separate coordinated check of the draft common-cap equations RV12–19 found no algebraic error: quantile retention, clipped variation, coarea crossing count and the sign of the additional-cap trace flux are consistent. That check did not remove the original sharp geometric-gate response requirement.
