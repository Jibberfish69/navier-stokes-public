## Both complete rows, including pressures

Write \(A=-\Delta\) for the positive spatial operator, \(M_{ij}=\partial_j u_i\) for the velocity-gradient matrix, \(D=(M+M^T)/2\), \(V=u_t\), \(N(a,b)=(a\cdot\nabla)b\), \(g=\mathbb Pf\), and
\[
 L_u a=\mathbb P[N(u,a)+N(a,u)].
 \tag{GT1}
\]
The original \(n=0,\alpha=e_j\) and \(n=1,\alpha=0\) rows are
\[
 \begin{split}
 M_t+\nu AM+\nabla^2p&=\nabla f-(u\cdot\nabla)M-M^2,\\
 V_t+\nu AV+\nabla p_t&=f_t-(u\cdot\nabla)V-MV,\\
 M_t&=\nabla V.
 \end{split}
 \tag{GT2}
\]
In particular \(\partial_jN(u,u)=(u\cdot\nabla)\partial_j u+((\partial_j u)\cdot\nabla)u\), whose gradient matrix is \((u\cdot\nabla)M+M^2\). Both temporal parents are also present in the second row.

Index the actual vector-field bundle by
\[
 H_0=V,\quad H_j=\partial_j u\ (1\le j\le3),\quad
 F_0=f_t,\quad F_j=\partial_jf,\quad
 \pi_0=p_t,\quad \pi_j=\partial_jp.
\]
Each field is solenoidal. The shared row is exactly
\[
 (H_\alpha)_t+\nu AH_\alpha+N(u,H_\alpha)+N(H_\alpha,u)+\nabla\pi_\alpha=F_\alpha,
 \qquad (\partial_t+\nu A+L_u)H_\alpha=\mathbb PF_\alpha.
 \tag{GT3}
\]
The field pressures are canonical:
\[
 \pi_\alpha=R_iR_j(u_i(H_\alpha)_j+(H_\alpha)_i u_j)
             -A^{-1}\operatorname{div}F_\alpha,
 \qquad \nabla\pi_\alpha=(I-\mathbb P)\mathcal R_\alpha,
 \quad \mathcal R_\alpha=F_\alpha-N(u,H_\alpha)-N(H_\alpha,u).
 \tag{GT4}
\]
Thus pressure includes the actual temporal or spatial force potential. Projection cannot replace the full raw source square while leaving this pressure square unchanged.

The two diagonal complete squares are
\[
 \begin{split}
 \|\nabla V\|_2^2+\nu^2\|\nabla Au\|_2^2+\|\nabla^2p\|_2^2
       +\nu(\|Au\|_2^2)'&=\|\nabla f-(u\cdot\nabla)M-M^2\|_2^2,\\
 \|V_t\|_2^2+\nu^2\|AV\|_2^2+\|\nabla p_t\|_2^2
       +\nu(\|\nabla V\|_2^2)'&=\|f_t-(u\cdot\nabla)V-MV\|_2^2.
 \end{split}
 \tag{GT5}
\]
The first is the sum of the column squares of the gradient row, equal to original \(n=0,s=1\); the second is original \(n=1,s=0\). They are higher complete-equation contractions and have their own stocks \(\nu\|Au\|_2^2\) and \(\nu\|\nabla V\|_2^2\).

## Full positive block and original nine-pairing corner

For a tuple \(K\), define \(\operatorname{Gr}(K)_{\alpha\beta}=\langle K_\alpha,K_\beta\rangle\), and put \(C_\nabla=\operatorname{Gr}(\nabla H)\). Squaring both factors of (GT3), using orthogonality of each pressure gradient to each solenoidal temporal/viscous field, gives
\[
 \boxed{\operatorname{Gr}(H_t)+\nu^2\operatorname{Gr}(AH)
       +\operatorname{Gr}(\nabla\pi)+\nu C_\nabla'
       =\operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal R).}
 \tag{GT6}
\]
Every matrix entry is included. For every constant real coefficient vector \(c\), this is the complete square for \(H_c=\sum_\alpha c_\alpha H_\alpha\), with pressure \(\pi_c=\sum_\alpha c_\alpha\pi_\alpha\) and full raw source \(\mathcal R_c\). For every constant positive semidefinite weight matrix the trace contraction of (GT6) gives a positive weighted complete square.

Integrating retains both initial and strict-terminal stocks:
\[
 \nu C_\nabla(B)
 +\int_0^B[\operatorname{Gr}(H_t)+\nu^2\operatorname{Gr}(AH)+\operatorname{Gr}(\nabla\pi)]
 =\nu C_\nabla(0)+\int_0^B\operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal R).
 \tag{GT7}
\]
This is a matrix equality. Its right side is the full nonlinear/forcing source Gram, not the Gram of the prescribed force alone. Positivity gives Cauchy control of cross entries by the actual diagonal actions; it has not made those actions datum-paid.

The source's J13d provides the same full corner without first taking a diagonal. For arbitrary selected original fields \(J_a,J_b\), put \(T_a=F_a-\mathcal B_a-\nabla\Pi_a=\mathbb P(F_a-\mathcal B_a)\). Its \(k=\ell=1\) specialization is
\[
 \langle(J_a)_t,(J_b)_t\rangle
 =\nu^2\langle\Delta J_a,\Delta J_b\rangle
 +\nu\langle T_a,\Delta J_b\rangle
 +\nu\langle\Delta J_a,T_b\rangle+\langle T_a,T_b\rangle.
 \tag{GT8}
\]
The last entry is originally nine force/nonlinear/pressure pairings. Writing them out gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \langle T_a,T_b\rangle={}&\langle F_a,F_b\rangle-\langle F_a,\mathcal B_b\rangle-\langle\mathcal B_a,F_b\rangle+\langle\mathcal B_a,\mathcal B_b\rangle\\
 &-\langle F_a,\nabla\Pi_b\rangle+\langle\mathcal B_a,\nabla\Pi_b\rangle
 -\langle\nabla\Pi_a,F_b\rangle+\langle\nabla\Pi_a,\mathcal B_b\rangle
 +\langle\nabla\Pi_a,\nabla\Pi_b\rangle\\
 ={}&\langle F_a-\mathcal B_a,F_b-\mathcal B_b\rangle
       -\langle\nabla\Pi_a,\nabla\Pi_b\rangle.
 \end{split}
 \tag{GT9}
\]
The two gradient cross groups supply minus twice the pressure Gram and its own final term supplies plus once. This is their exact orthogonal recombination, rather than omission of the pressure pairings.
In particular \(\operatorname{Gr}(\nabla\pi)\preceq\operatorname{Gr}(\mathcal R)\), with difference \(\operatorname{Gr}(\mathbb P\mathcal R)\) positive semidefinite. This genuine pressure control retains the complete nonlinear source on its right.

For \(J_a=\partial_j u=M_j\), \(J_b=V\), (GT8)–(GT9) become the particular cross square
\[
 \begin{split}
 &\langle\partial_jV,V_t\rangle+\nu^2\langle A M_j,AV\rangle
 +\langle\partial_j\nabla p,\nabla p_t\rangle
 +\nu\frac d{dt}\langle\nabla M_j,\nabla V\rangle\\
 &\qquad=\langle\partial_jf-N(u,M_j)-N(M_j,u),\ f_t-N(u,V)-N(V,u)\rangle.
 \end{split}
 \tag{GT10}
\]
This cross entry is additional information beyond the two separate diagonal equalities. Its pressure cross pairing is not automatically zero: differentiating the identity \(\langle\nabla p,\partial_j\nabla p\rangle=0\) cancels two opposite terms; it does not establish that either \(\langle\nabla p_t,\partial_j\nabla p\rangle\) vanishes. The same distinction applies to the temporal/spatial action pairing.

## Shared linearization, Gram current and complete mixed compatibility

The original J10 evolution for this bundle simplifies only the transported cross terms:
\[
 C_{\alpha\beta}'+2\nu\langle\nabla H_\alpha,\nabla H_\beta\rangle
 =-2\int H_\alpha^TDH_\beta
 +\langle F_\alpha,H_\beta\rangle+\langle H_\alpha,F_\beta\rangle.
 \tag{GT11}
\]
Indeed \(L_u+L_u^*=2\mathbb P D\mathbb P\) on solenoidal fields. The intrinsic stretching remains on every weighted Gram row. Full-space pressure pairing vanishes only in this solenoidal test; (GT4) and (GT6) retain the pressure fields and their squares.

For a fixed spatial direction \(a\), the cross current \(C_a=\langle V,(a\cdot\nabla)u\rangle\) has the native value
\[
 C_a=-\langle N(u,u),(a\cdot\nabla)u\rangle
          +\langle g,(a\cdot\nabla)u\rangle.
 \tag{GT12}
\]
Viscosity vanishes in this pairing by skew-adjointness of the constant spatial derivative, and the pressure vanishes by solenoidality. This current is a genuinely additional directional coordinate. It is not determined by differentiating the identically zero scalar \(\langle u,(a\cdot\nabla)u\rangle\); that derivative cancels its two opposite terms. Its evolution is exactly the corresponding off-diagonal (GT11), with stretching and both source slots.

For a fixed \(a\), \(V+(a\cdot\nabla)u=(\partial_t+a\cdot\nabla)u\), and its complete raw row is \((\partial_t+a\cdot\nabla)\) applied to the original full momentum residual. This explains the relationship of its square to (GT6), without discarding its extra directional information or treating a derivative coordinate as a later physical time.

The common linearization's actual spatial and temporal commutators are
\[
 [\partial_j,L_u]z=\mathbb P[N(M_j,z)+N(z,M_j)],\qquad
 [\partial_t,L_u]z=\mathbb P[N(V,z)+N(z,V)].
 \tag{GT13}
\]
The Leray projection commutes with these fixed derivatives. Differentiating the spatial row in time and the temporal row in space yields, respectively,
\[
 (\partial_t+\nu A+L_u)\partial_jV
 =\partial_jg_t-L_VM_j
 =\partial_jg_t-L_{M_j}V,
 \tag{GT14}
\]
where \(L_a z=\mathbb P[N(a,z)+N(z,a)]\). The equality is symmetry of the full two insertions; they do not vanish. The complete unprojected mixed corner is
\[
 \begin{split}
 (\partial_jV)_t+\nu A\partial_jV
 &+N(u,\partial_jV)+N(\partial_jV,u)+\nabla\partial_jp_t\\
 &=\partial_jf_t-N(M_j,V)-N(V,M_j).
 \end{split}
 \tag{GT15}
\]
This is original J2–J3 at \((n,\alpha)=(1,e_j)\), containing all four nonlinear allocations and its exact force/pressure derivative. Thus the covariant time/spatial corner carries an explicit inhomogeneous quadratic insertion.

For a nonconstant smooth spatial/time selector \(\zeta\), the directly read original J13a gives
\[
 \begin{split}
 \frac d{dt}\int\zeta H_\alpha\cdot H_\beta
 ={}&-2\nu\int\zeta\sum_i\partial_iH_\alpha\cdot\partial_iH_\beta
      -2\int\zeta H_\alpha^TDH_\beta\\
 &+\int\zeta(F_\alpha\cdot H_\beta+H_\alpha\cdot F_\beta)\\
 &+\int(\zeta_t+u\cdot\nabla\zeta+\nu\Delta\zeta)H_\alpha\cdot H_\beta
      +\int\nabla\zeta\cdot(\pi_\alpha H_\beta+\pi_\beta H_\alpha).
 \end{split}
 \tag{GT16}
\]
This executes the source's local pressure flux. A spatially weighted Gram contraction retains pressure and derivative-of-weight terms; the whole-space constant-weight cancellation cannot be carried over without these terms.

## Completed-height anti-diagonal and actual joint storage comparison

For the selected original height operation put \(Y=\|\nabla u\|_2^2\), \(Z=\|Au\|_2^2\), and
\(\mathcal G_{0,s}=\langle\Lambda^su,\Lambda^s(f-N(u,u)-\nabla p)\rangle\).
The original J11, or equation (7) with \(r=0,s=1,k=2\), says
\[
 2\nu^2\|\nabla Au\|_2^2
 =\tfrac12Y''+2\nu\mathcal G_{0,2}-\mathcal G_{0,1}'.
 \tag{GT17}
\]
The complete differentiated work in this case is explicitly
\[
 \mathcal G_{0,1}'=\langle AV,f-N(u,u)-\nabla p\rangle
 +\langle Au,f_t-N(u,V)-N(V,u)-\nabla p_t\rangle.
\]
The full equation gives \(f-N(u,u)-\nabla p=V+\nu Au\), and its full temporal derivative is \(V_t+\nu AV\). Therefore, before any estimate,
\[
 \mathcal G_{0,1}=\tfrac12Y'+\nu Z,\qquad
 \mathcal G_{0,2}=\tfrac12Z'+\nu\|\nabla Au\|_2^2,\qquad
 \mathcal G_{0,1}'=\tfrac12Y''+\nu Z'.
 \tag{GT18}
\]
Substitution into (GT17) cancels both derivative terms and gives exactly \(2\nu^2\|\nabla Au\|_2^2\). All nonlinear, pressure and forcing work is included in this substitution. The positive temporal square in \(\tfrac12Y''=\|\nabla V\|_2^2+\langle Au,V_t\rangle\) is retained with its cross pairing; it has not been separated into an upper bound. Higher J11/J12 anti-diagonals likewise contain their complete work derivatives. Their finite-order algebra supplies no order/time-uniform payment merely from coordinate compatibility.

To compare the combined row directly with the actual \(I_\delta\), enlarge the common-operator bundle only by the original zeroth field and known force field. With \(B_u=\nu A+L_u\), \(q=-\mathbb PN(u,u)\),
\[
 (\partial_t+B_u)V=g_t,\quad
 (\partial_t+B_u)u=g-q,\quad
 (\partial_t+B_u)g=g_t+\nu Ag+L_ug.
\]
For the actual time-dependent combination \(w=V-g+\lambda u\), where
\(\lambda=\nu Y/(e+\delta)\), this gives
\[
 (\partial_t+B_u)w=j_g+\lambda(g-q)+\lambda' u,
 \qquad j_g=-\nu Ag-L_ug.
 \tag{GT19}
\]
Thus a row transformation retains both the intrinsic zeroth-row source \(-q\) and its scalar derivative port. It does not turn \(w\) into a homogeneous known-source row.

Write \(h=V-g\), \(b=e+\delta\), \(d=e+3\delta\), \(F=\langle u,g\rangle\). Direct pairing, using \(\langle w,u\rangle=-\delta\lambda\), produces the exact stock
\[
 \Phi_\delta=\tfrac12\|h\|_2^2-\frac{\nu^2Y^2}{4b}
            =\tfrac12\|w\|_2^2+\tfrac d4\lambda^2,
 \quad
 \Phi_\delta'+\nu\|\nabla w\|_2^2+\lambda\|w\|_2^2+\tfrac d2\lambda^3
 =-S(u,w,w)+\langle w,j_g\rangle+\lambda\langle q,g\rangle+\tfrac12\lambda^2F.
 \tag{GT20}
\]
The joint \(u,V,g\) pairing therefore returns this stock, with its initial and strict-terminal values retained. The gradient/temporal positive block gives additional higher complete squares (GT5)–(GT10); it has not estimated their full nonlinear source Gram or supplied an independent upper bound on the signed input in (GT20).
