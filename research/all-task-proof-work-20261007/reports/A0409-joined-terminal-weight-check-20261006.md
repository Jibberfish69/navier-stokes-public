## 1. The actual response and retained forced insertions

On the actual proposed finite maximal interval $I=(0,T)$, use

\[
 Q(u)=-\mathbb P((u\cdot\nabla)u),\quad g=\mathbb Pf,\quad
 u_t+\nu\Lambda^2u=Q(u)+g,
\qquad p=R_iR_j(u_i u_j)-(-\Delta)^{-1}\nabla\cdot f .
\]

The response is the total forced response, not an unforced comparison:

\[
 w=u-S_tu_0,\quad S_t=e^{-\nu t\Lambda^2},\quad
 w_t+\nu\Lambda^2w=Q(u)+g,\quad w(0)=0,
\]
\[
 E=\tfrac12\|\Lambda^{1/2}w\|_2^2,\quad
 I=\nu\|\Lambda^{3/2}w\|_2^2,\quad
 E'=F_0+F_f-I,
\quad F_0=\operatorname{Re}\langle\Lambda w,Q(u)\rangle,\quad
 F_f=\operatorname{Re}\langle\Lambda w,g\rangle .
\]

At every present time put $v_s=S_su$, $G_s=Q(v_s)$. The complete forced insertion is

\[
 \mathfrak N_s=DQ(v_s)[S_s(Q(u)+g)]
 =-\mathcal B(S_s(Q(u)+g),v_s)-\mathcal B(v_s,S_s(Q(u)+g)),
\]

where $\mathcal B(a,b)=\mathbb P((a\cdot\nabla)b)$. Both ordered parent legs retain their distinct inner and outer Leray projectors. FIH.11–14 then give

\[
 C_\infty=\nu^{-1}\int_0^\infty\|\Lambda^{-1/2}G_s\|_2^2\,ds,\qquad
 C_\infty'=-A+J_{\rm NL}+J_f,
\]
\[
 A=\nu^{-1}\|\Lambda^{-1/2}Q(u)\|_2^2,\qquad
 J_{\rm NL}=\frac2\nu\int_0^\infty
 \operatorname{Re}\langle\Lambda^{-1}DQ(v_s)[S_sQ(u)],G_s\rangle\,ds,
\]
\[
 J_f=\frac2\nu\int_0^\infty
 \operatorname{Re}\langle\Lambda^{-1}DQ(v_s)[S_sg],G_s\rangle\,ds .
\]

These identities hold classically on each strict $[0,S]$, $S<T$, and locally as distributions. The heat age is applied to the actual present field, not to an independent parent history.

## 2. Additional unconditional response and force payments

Let $K=\|u_0\|_2+\|g\|_{L^1(I;L^2)}$ and $D_0=K^2/(2\nu)$. The actual kinetic budget gives

\[
 \sup_I\|w\|_2\le2K,\qquad D_w:=\int_I\|\nabla w\|_2^2\le2K^2/\nu .
\]

Fourier interpolation yields the stronger stock payment

\[
 E\le K\|\nabla w\|_2,\qquad
 \boxed{\int_I E^2\le K^2D_w\le2K^4/\nu.}
\]

Thus the joined limit stock is datum-paid in $L^2_t$, in addition to the earlier $L^1_t$ result. This does not assign it a terminal value.

Write $a_0=\mathsf S_3\mathsf S_6=\pi^{-1}\sqrt{2/3}$ and $C_b=a_0^2=2/(3\pi^2)$. The complete homogeneous product estimate is

\[
 \|\Lambda^{-1/2}\mathcal B(a,b)\|_2
 \le a_0\|\nabla a\|_2\|\nabla b\|_2 .
\]

Both force-parent legs therefore satisfy

\[
 \|\Lambda^{-1/2}DQ(v_s)[S_sg]\|_2
 \le2a_0\|\nabla S_sg\|_2\|\nabla v_s\|_2 .
\]

For $F(s)=\|\nabla S_su\|_2^2$, use $F(s)\le F(0)$ and $\int_0^\infty F(s)\,ds=\|u\|_2^2/(2\nu)$. This gives the explicit arbitrary-amplitude force payment

\[
 |J_f(t)|\le\frac{2C_bK^2}{\nu^2}\|\nabla g(t)\|_2\|\nabla u(t)\|_2,
\qquad
 \|J_f\|_{L^1(I)}
 \le B_f:=\frac{2C_bK^2}{\nu^2}\|\nabla g\|_{L^2(I;L^2)}\sqrt{D_0}.
\]

The same $B_f$ bounds the absolute insertion before integration over heat age:

\[
 \frac2\nu\int_I\int_0^\infty
 \left|\operatorname{Re}\langle\Lambda^{-1}DQ(v_s)[S_sg],G_s\rangle\right|\,ds\,dt
 \le B_f.
\]

Indeed the absolute integrand is bounded by $2C_b\|\nabla S_sg\|_2F(s)^{3/2}$, and $\int F(s)^{3/2}ds\le\|\nabla u\|_2\|u\|_2^2/(2\nu)$. This retains payment on restricted heat-age triangles, without using cancellation in $J_f$.

The direct response work is also paid:

\[
 \|F_f\|_{L^1(I)}
 \le2K\|\Lambda g\|_{L^1(I;L^2)} .
\]

The remaining insertion in the full storage law is precisely $J_{\rm NL}$ above, retaining both nonlinear parent legs. FIH.29–30 restricts its pairing to the transverse component; it does not provide an upper primitive for that component.

## 3. What the whole-terminal joined convergence allows at the boundary

The checked joined theorem supplies

\[
 0\le\overline C_N\le2C_\infty,\quad
 \overline C_N\to C_\infty\text{ in }L^1(I),\quad
 \widetilde K_N=NE+\overline B_N+\overline C_N\ge0,
\]
\[
 |\overline B_N|\le2\sqrt{NE\,\overline C_N},\qquad
 \widetilde K_N/N\to E\text{ in }L^1(I).
\]

Consequently, at every strict slice and uniformly in $N\ge1$,

\[
 0\le\widetilde K_N/N\le2E+4C_\infty .
\]

Since $E+C_\infty\in L^1(I)$, there is one actual slice sequence $S_j\uparrow T$ with

\[
 (T-S_j)(E+C_\infty)(S_j)\longrightarrow0 .
\]

Otherwise a positive lower bound would force a divergent $\int (T-t)^{-1}dt$. The same sequence gives vanishing terminal-linear weighted boundaries for every $\overline C_N$ and every $\widetilde K_N/N$, uniformly in $N$. This is a simultaneous reciprocal-depth boundary conclusion; it is not an all-slice terminal trace.

The $L^2$ stock payment further supplies a slice sequence with $(T-S_j)E(S_j)^2\to0$. For response weights $(T-t)^\alpha$, $\alpha>1/2$, their derivative pairs integrably with $E$:

\[
 \int_I\alpha(T-t)^{\alpha-1}E
 \le\frac{\alpha T^{\alpha-1/2}}{\sqrt{2\alpha-1}}\|E\|_{L^2(I)} .
\]

Along the corresponding vanishing-boundary sequence the complete weighted response net current has the actual finite limit

\[
 \int_0^{S_j}(T-t)^\alpha(F_0+F_f-I)\,dt
 \longrightarrow
 \alpha\int_I(T-t)^{\alpha-1}E(t)\,dt .
\]

The positive action and the signed production have not been separated in this conclusion.

## 4. Terminal weights preserve the exact unsigned action and signed insertion

For a nonnegative $C^1$ weight $\phi$ and every strict $S<T$, the exact storage identity is

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \int_0^S\phi A={}&
 \phi(0)C_\infty(0)-\phi(S)C_\infty(S)
 +\int_0^S\phi'C_\infty\\
 &+\int_0^S\phi J_{\rm NL}+\int_0^S\phi J_f .
\end{aligned}
\]

The interior term is paid when $\phi'$ is bounded; $\phi(T)=0$ alone does not establish that the endpoint product has an all-slice limit. Terminal-linear weights do give its zero limit along the sequence in Section 3. The complete net identity passes there; individual signed terms cannot be passed separately without their own integrability.

If $\phi'\le0$, positivity permits a useful one-sided inequality without replacing the identity:

\[
 \int_0^S\phi A
 \le\phi(0)C_\infty(0)+\int_0^S\phi J_{\rm NL}
                              +\|\phi\|_\infty\|J_f\|_{L^1(I)} .
\]

In the response identity, $F_0\le(I+A)/2$ and $E(0)=0$, so the same nonincreasing weight gives

\[
 \boxed{
 \int_0^S\phi I
 \le\phi(0)C_\infty(0)+\int_0^S\phi J_{\rm NL}
 +\|\phi\|_\infty\left(\|J_f\|_{L^1}+2\|F_f\|_{L^1}\right).}
\]

This composes the original source-square and actual response operations into weighted critical dissipation. Its exact remaining unbounded signed term is $\int\phi J_{\rm NL}$; positivity supplies no upper bound for it. The same identity supplies a datum lower bound for that signed term, not the upper bound needed on its right side.

For $\phi(t)=T-t$, the weight vanishes at the proposed terminal time. Even a bound for its positive weighted action would only imply

\[
 \int_0^S I\le(T-S)^{-1}\int_0^S(T-t)I,\qquad S<T,
\]

whose factor degenerates as $S\uparrow T$. The actual time domain is preserved.

## 5. Execute the source's triangular arrangement

FIH.15–20 gives the exact no-recount triangle with $q_0(s)=\nu^{-1}\|\Lambda^{-1/2}Q(S_su_0)\|_2^2$:

\[
 \int_0^S A(t)\,dt
 =\int_0^S q_0(s)\,ds
 +\frac2\nu\int_{t+s<S}
 \operatorname{Re}\langle\Lambda^{-1}\mathfrak N_s(t),G_s(t)\rangle\,ds\,dt .
\]

Integrating this identity once more over strict horizons, with all terms finite before $T$, gives the terminal-linear triangle:

\[
\begin{aligned}
 \int_0^S(S-t)A(t)\,dt
 ={}&\int_0^S(S-s)q_0(s)\,ds\\
 &+\frac2\nu\int_{t+s<S}(S-t-s)
 \operatorname{Re}\langle\Lambda^{-1}\mathfrak N_s(t),G_s(t)\rangle\,ds\,dt .
\end{aligned}
\]

The initial term is at most $S C_\infty(u_0)$; the force-insertion contribution is at most $S B_f$ in absolute value. The complete nonlinear triangular insertion with weight $S-t-s$ remains signed. This exact rearrangement has not paid it by the positive storage.

For the response itself,

\[
 \int_0^S(S-t)(F_0+F_f)\,dt
 =\int_0^S E(t)\,dt+\int_0^S(S-t)I(t)\,dt .
\]

It gives a datum lower bound for the nonlinear triangular production after subtracting the paid force work. It does not give its upper bound.

## 6. Abel and terminal averages retain their normalization

Whole-interval $L^1$ convergence permits bounded averages. For $H=E+C_\infty$,

\[
 \int_{T-\eta}^T H\to0,\qquad
 \eta^{-1}\int_{T-\eta}^T(T-t)H(t)\,dt\to0 .
\]

The latter follows because it is bounded by the first tail integral. These vanish uniformly for the joined storages dominated by $H$.

Likewise the unnormalized Abel tail $\int_I e^{-(T-t)/\eta}H(t)\,dt$ tends to zero. Its normalized version has an additional $\eta^{-1}$ factor that the $L^1$ storage theorem does not pay uniformly. The $L^2$ response stock gives the more precise scaled statement

\[
 \sqrt\eta\left[\eta^{-1}\int_I e^{-(T-t)/\eta}E(t)\,dt\right]\to0 .
\]

This follows by localizing near $T$, applying $L^2$ Cauchy–Schwarz there, and exponentially suppressing the earlier interval. It is a scaled terminal average, not an endpoint value. For the genuine cutoff traces constructed below, ordinary normalized terminal/Abel averages do converge to those actual cutoff endpoints.

## 7. Genuine positive-heat-age endpoint

The previously verified datum construction gives $u\in C([0,T];H^{-1/4})$ and a weak $L^2$ endpoint $u_*$ with $\|u_*\|_2\le K$. For each fixed $s>0$,

\[
 S_su(t)\longrightarrow S_su_*
 \quad\text{in every finite }H^m .
\]

The full quadratic source therefore converges in $\dot H^{-1/2}$. For every $\epsilon>0$ define

\[
 C_{\infty,\epsilon}(t)=
 \nu^{-1}\int_\epsilon^\infty\|\Lambda^{-1/2}Q(S_su(t))\|_2^2\,ds
 =C_\infty(S_\epsilon u(t)).
\]

The Fourier maximum gives $F(s)\le K^2/(2e\nu s)$. With the exact source constant $C_b=2/(3\pi^2)$,

\[
 \boxed{
 C_{\infty,\epsilon}(t)\le
 \frac{K^4}{6e^2\pi^2\nu^3\epsilon},\qquad
 C_{\infty,\epsilon}(t)\to C_{\infty,\epsilon}(u_*) .}
\]

Dominated convergence over the positive heat ages proves an actual continuous terminal trace. The bound degenerates at $\epsilon=0$.

## 8. Genuine endpoint for every bounded-output joined storage

There is a stronger conclusion which retains every parent, including arbitrarily high parent frequencies, at each bounded output range. Original FIH.3–5 and incompressibility give

\[
 |\widehat{Q(S_su)}(k)|
 \le c_{\mathcal F}|k|
 \int e^{-\nu s(|p|^2+|k-p|^2)}
             |\widehat u(p)||\widehat u(k-p)|\,dp
 \le c_{\mathcal F}|k|e^{-\nu|k|^2s/2}K^2 .
\]

The outer pressure projector remains norm at most one, and the two actual velocity parents remain in the integral. Let $P_{\le R}$ be the output Fourier cutoff, not a replacement of the original parents by a truncated fluid. Then

\[
 C_\infty^{\le R}(t)=
 \nu^{-1}\int_0^\infty
 \|\Lambda^{-1/2}P_{\le R}Q(S_su(t))\|_2^2\,ds ,
\]
\[
 \boxed{
 C_\infty^{\le R}(t)\le
 \frac{2\pi c_{\mathcal F}^2K^4R^2}{\nu^2}.}
\]

Indeed $\int_{|k|\le R}|k|^{-1}d^3k=2\pi R^2$. In the unitary convention $c_{\mathcal F}=(2\pi)^{-3/2}$, the bound is $K^4R^2/(4\pi^2\nu^2)$. The zero output is harmless under this integrable bound.

The near-zero heat-age portion also has the explicit uniform bound

\[
 \nu^{-1}\int_0^\epsilon
 \|\Lambda^{-1/2}P_{\le R}Q(S_su)\|_2^2\,ds
 \le\frac{\epsilon\pi c_{\mathcal F}^2K^4R^4}{\nu},
\]

because $\int_{|k|\le R}|k|d^3k=\pi R^4$. The positive-age convergence above and either this small-age bound or the integrable full $(k,s)$ majorant establish

\[
 \boxed{
 C_\infty^{\le R}(t)\to C_\infty^{\le R}(u_*)
 \quad\text{for every fixed finite }R.}
\]

This is a genuine endpoint conclusion beyond the kinetic row. It is not an instantaneous claim that $Q(u(t))$ has its defect-free pressure trace.

### Reciprocal prefixes and response completions at the same bounded output

In the original parent heat metric the difference of two physical parent densities is the heat-age norm of $Q(S_su)-Q(S_su_*)$. The preceding dominated argument, with its squared difference majorant multiplied by four, shows that the actual bounded-output parent path is continuous through $T$ in this metric. It is compact as the image of $[0,T]$.

The PRM.32h reciprocal multiplier $R_{\rm age}$ is a contraction in that metric, and $R_{\rm age}^N\Theta\to0$ for each physical parent density as verified in the joined audit. Strong convergence of contractions is uniform on this compact parent path. Therefore

\[
 \boxed{
 \overline C_N^{\le R}\to C_\infty^{\le R}
 \quad\text{uniformly on }[0,T].}
\]

Every finite joined prefix has an actual terminal trace at fixed bounded output. The reciprocal-depth and terminal limits commute there. No first-parent-rate moment is imposed to pass this complete kernel.

The actual cutoff response has $E^{\le R}\in C([0,T])$ and

\[
 \sup E^{\le R}\le2RK^2,\qquad
 \int_I I^{\le R}\le\nu R D_w\le2RK^2 .
\]

The same completion inequality gives

\[
 \left|\frac{\widetilde K_N^{\le R}}N-E^{\le R}\right|
 \le\frac2{\sqrt N}\sqrt{E^{\le R}\overline C_N^{\le R}}
          +\frac{\overline C_N^{\le R}}N .
\]

Its right side tends to zero uniformly on $[0,T]$, using the explicit bounded-output stock bounds. Thus the positive completed sum also has compatible cutoff endpoints and a uniform reciprocal limit.

## 9. Exact remaining scope

The original joined construction now has genuine terminal traces at every fixed positive heat age and every bounded output, including the complete finite reciprocal prefixes and their normalized response completion. It additionally has a datum-paid $L^2$ time norm of the full response stock and explicit payment of both force insertions.

The full-output limit is still different: $C_\infty-C_\infty^{\le R}\to0$ in $L^1(I)$ by the joined storage theorem, but the bound above grows like $R^2$ and supplies no uniform terminal output tail. Positive-age removal is also only time-integrated under the available full-storage theorem. The weighted identities retain the exact full nonlinear insertion $J_{\rm NL}$ or its triangular counterpart. Their positivity gives one-sided lower information and the conditional weighted-action inequality displayed in Section 4, not a datum upper primitive for that signed insertion.

All terminal, depth, output, and heat-age limits in this report retain their displayed quantifiers. No positive terminal Sobolev norm, order-uniform jet norm, individual-current limit, or generic substitute criterion has been assumed.
