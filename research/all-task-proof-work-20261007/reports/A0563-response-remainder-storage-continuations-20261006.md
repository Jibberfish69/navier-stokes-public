## 2. The actual two high recent parents admit a sharper kinetic bound

Let \(I=(0,T)\), \(T<\infty\), first on strict smooth prefixes, and use

Q(u)=-\mathbb P[(u\cdot\nabla)u],\qquad S_a=e^{-\nu a\Lambda^2}.

\[
V_n(t)=\int_0^{\ell_n(t)}S_aP_{\ge r_n}Q(u(t-a))\,da,\qquad
\ell_n(t)=\min(t,\tau_n).
\tag{RR.1}
\]
The mild equation of this same full fluid, rather than a new equation for its high part, gives
\[
\boxed{V_n(t)=P_{\ge r_n}\{u(t)-S_{\ell_n(t)}u(t-\ell_n(t))\}.}
\tag{RR.2}
\]
In particular, if \(K=\|u_0\|_2\), then
\[
\boxed{\sup_{t<T}\|V_n(t)\|_2\le2K.}
\tag{RR.3}
\]
No pressure or low-parent term of the equation is removed in RR.2; only the final orthogonal readout is high passed.

Put \(Y(t)=\|\nabla u(t)\|_2^2\), \(Y_{V_n}(t)=\|\nabla V_n(t)\|_2^2\). Heat contraction and RR.2 give
\[

2Y(t)+2
\begin{cases}
\|\nabla S_tu_0\|_2^2,&t\le\tau_n,\\
Y(t-\tau_n),&t>\tau_n.
\end{cases}
\]
Consequently
\[
\boxed{\int_IY_{V_n}\le4\int_IY+\frac{K^2}{\nu}

\tag{RR.4}

## 3. A complete Fourier-majorant proof without an \(L^3\) multiplier assumption

\tag{RR.5}
\]

For a vector field \(F\), define a scalar Fourier-majorant readout
\[

\[

\]
Approximation in \(\dot H^{1/2}\) proves convergence of the trilinear majorant and extends it to the actual fields. Complex coefficients, conjugate partners and finite component sums are already covered by RR.5; no positivity of an individual physical triad is used.

Thus, writing \(C=C_M\mathsf S_3^3\),

\le\frac C\nu\|\Lambda^{1/2}u(t)\|_2

\tag{RR.6}
\]
This proves the required estimate by absolute Fourier majorization. It does not assert that an arbitrary bounded symbol defines a bounded multilinear \(L^3\) operator.
