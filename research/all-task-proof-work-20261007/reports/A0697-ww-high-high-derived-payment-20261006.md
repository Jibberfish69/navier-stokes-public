## 2. Derived inherited bounds and actual \(P\) budgets

The original free static constant is fixed once so that
\[
\ell_\infty:=\sup_t\|\nabla U(t)\|_{\infty,F}
\le c_gKa^{5/2}\le\varepsilon\nu\rho^2,\quad
\varepsilon\le1/8,\quad c_g=(\sqrt{10}\pi)^{-1}.
\tag{WH4}
\]
This shrinks the permitted PLF.9 constant, not the datum amplitude. Let \(c_b=(\sqrt6\pi)^{-1}\). The already derived band-edge budgets and actual heat-parent budgets are
\[
C_2\le2C_e^2K^4a^3/\nu,\quad
M_C:=\sup_{\sigma,t}\|\nabla z_C\|^2\le2C_2/\nu,\quad
D_C:=\int_0^T\|\nabla z_C\|^2\le2C_2/(\nu^2\rho^2),
\quad
\sup\|V\|_2\le2K,\quad D_V\le3K^2/\nu.
\tag{WH5}
\]
These bounds do not assert \(W\le2K\).

Homogeneous \(H^1\) decay and the \(L^2\) energy imply
\[
\|\nabla\mathscr U(s+h,s)y\|_2
\le\frac{\|y\|_2}{\sqrt{(1-\varepsilon)\nu h}}e^{-\nu\rho^2h/4}.
\]
Use the exact Duhamel difference
\[
(\mathscr U(t,\sigma)-S_{t-\sigma})y
=-\int_\sigma^tS_{t-q}\Pi\mathcal L_q\mathscr U(q,\sigma)y\,dq.
\tag{WH6}
\]
The high heat kernel satisfies \(\|\nabla S_h\Pi\|_{2\to2}\le(e\nu h)^{-1/2}e^{-\nu\rho^2h/2}\). The transport beta integral is \(\pi\), while the stretching convolution is at most \(2/(e\nu\rho)\). Thus, uniformly over actual starts without an initial \(H^1\) assumption,
\[
M_E:=\sup_t\|\nabla E(t)\|^2
\le K^2\left[\frac{\pi M_0}{\nu\sqrt{e(1-\varepsilon)}}+
\frac{2\ell_\infty}{e\nu\rho}\right]^2
=O(\rho^{12/5}+\rho^2),\quad
M_0=\sup\|U\|_\infty\le c_bKa^{3/2}.
\tag{WH7}
\]
For \(t>\tau\), homogeneous \(H^1\) and heat contractions give \(Y_E(t)\le4\|\nabla\Pi u(t-\tau)\|^2\). Onset \(t\le\tau\) uses the two separate \(L^2\) energy identities. Consequently
\[
D_E:=\int_0^T Y_Edt
\le4\int_0^T\|\nabla\Pi u\|^2dt+
\nu^{-1}[1+(1-\varepsilon)^{-1}]\|\Pi u_0\|^2
\longrightarrow0\quad(\rho\to\infty).
\tag{WH8}
\]
This is uniform over fixed lags and does not differentiate adaptive time choices.

The actual fields \(W=V-z_C=E+P\) now satisfy, simply by their exact difference,
\[
D_W\le2D_V+2D_C,\qquad
D_P\le3(D_V+D_C+D_E)=O(1+\rho^{2/5}),\qquad
\sup\|P\|_2^2\le32K^2+2M_C/\rho^2.
\tag{WH9}
\]
The last estimate uses \(\|E\|_2\le2K\) and \(z_C\)'s actual high support. No balanced-gradient or datum-only \(L^2\) assumption on \(P\) is introduced.

Let \(C_{Q,\nabla}\) be an admissible full finite mask/tensor constant in CDR.27/RHL.10,
\[
\|\mathcal Q^{ch}[f,g]\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}
\le C_{Q,\nabla}\|\nabla f\|_2\|\nabla g\|_2.
\tag{WH10}
\]
It includes assignments, reality and Leray. Its absolute-Fourier modulus proof is uniform for the actual frozen mask and does not assume a rough-mask \(L^p\) multiplier theorem. For a component \(z\) of an actual parent \(Y\), complete recombination gives
\[
\|\mathcal Q^{ch}[Y,Y]-\mathcal Q^{ch}[Y-z,Y-z]\|_{L^2_t\dot H^{-1/2}}^2
\le C_{Q,\nabla}^2M_z(16D_Y+18D_z).
\tag{WH11}
\]
The coefficients follow from \(2\sqrt{Y_{Y-z}Y_z}+Y_z\) and \(Y_{Y-z}\le2Y_Y+2Y_z\), after both mixed orders and the square term are joined.

In particular the complete inherited-containing outer source square is
\[
C_{Q,\nabla}^2M_E(16D_W+18D_E)
=O(\rho^{14/5}+\rho^{12/5})=o(A_n).
\tag{WH12}
\]
Even the cruder \(D_E\le TM_E\) would give \(O(\rho^{24/5})=o(A_n)\). Pairing with the actual receiver and record action \(\nu\int_{I_n}\|\Lambda^{3/2}u\|^2\le2A_n\) costs \(2\delta A_n+o(A_n)\). Its net response/cap/primitive-ramp block has exactly that payment, not separate bounds on every derivative piece.

## 3. New payment for every birth with one bounded physical parent

Partition the actual physical velocity before its full ordered convolution:
\[
H_\beta=P_{a\le|\xi|\le\beta}u,\quad H_>=P_{|\xi|>\beta}u,\quad H=H_\beta+H_>.
\tag{WH13}
\]
The exact split is
\[
\begin{aligned}
F_\ell&=-\Pi\{N(H_\beta,H)+N(H_>,H_\beta)\}\\
&=-\Pi\{N(H_\beta,H_\beta)+N(H_\beta,H_>)+N(H_>,H_\beta)\},\\
F_h&=-\Pi N(H_>,H_>),\qquad F^{hh}=F_\ell+F_h.
\end{aligned}
\tag{WH14}
\]
Both mixed input orders are included. This is an explicit reversible decomposition, not a silent band cutoff on inner children.

The pressure-complete physical \(L^2\) bounds are
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|N(H_\beta,H)\|_2&\le c_bK\beta^{3/2}\|\nabla H\|_2,\\
\|N(H_>,H_\beta)\|_2&\le c_gK\beta^{5/2}\|H_>\|_2
\le c_gK\beta^{3/2}\|\nabla H_>\|_2.
\end{aligned}
\tag{WH15}
\]
The second bound uses the actual support \(>\beta\); \(\mathbb P,\Pi\) contract \(L^2\). No \(L^\infty\) or higher derivative bound on the high parent is needed. Therefore
\[
S_\ell:=\int_0^T\|F_\ell\|_2^2dt
\le(c_b+c_g)^2K^2\beta^3\int_0^T\|\nabla H\|^2dt
\le(c_b+c_g)^2K^4\beta^3/(2\nu).
\tag{WH16}
\]
This is a prescribed whole-terminal source square for each fixed decomposition.

On the same source windows define
\[
L(t)=\int_{\sigma(t)}^t\mathscr U(t,q)F_\ell(q)dq,\quad
T_h(t)=\int_{\sigma(t)}^t\mathscr U(t,q)F_h(q)dq,\quad P=L+T_h.
\tag{WH17}
\]
The original projected \(H^1\) row tested against \(Az\) yields \(Y_z'+\nu\|Az\|^2\le2\|F_\ell\|^2/\nu\): transport and stretching each cost at most \(\varepsilon\nu\|Az\|^2\), Young costs \(\nu\|Az\|^2/4\). Its nonnegative decay kernel proves
\[
M_L\le2S_\ell/\nu,\qquad D_L\le2S_\ell/(\nu^2\rho^2).
\tag{WH18}
\]
These estimates do not differentiate a varying lower start. Its exact time row does retain the delayed endpoint:
\[
L_t+\nu AL+\Pi\mathcal L_tL
=F_\ell(t)-\mathbf1_{t>\tau}\mathscr U(t,t-\tau)F_\ell(t-\tau).
\tag{WH19}
\]
The joined outer component is exactly
\[
\begin{aligned}
F_{\rm out,\ell}
&=\mathcal Q^{ch}[P,P]-\mathcal Q^{ch}[T_h,T_h]\\
&=\mathcal Q^{ch}[T_h,L]+\mathcal Q^{ch}[L,T_h]+\mathcal Q^{ch}[L,L].
\end{aligned}
\tag{WH20}
\]
Only after this full sum use WH11:
\[
E_{\rm out,\ell}
\le C_{Q,\nabla}^2M_L(16D_P+18D_L)
=O(\beta^3(1+\rho^{2/5})+\beta^6/\rho^2).
\tag{WH21}
\]
At the distinct actual scales WH1,
\[
\boxed{E_{\rm out,\ell}
=O(A_n^{1/2}+A_n^{27/50}+A_n^{4/5})=o(A_n).}
\tag{WH22}
\]
For every actual interval/cap/ramp weight \(|\omega|\le1\),
\[
\left|\int_{I_n}\omega\operatorname{Re}
(\Lambda^{-1/2}F_{\rm out,\ell},\Lambda^{3/2}u)dt\right|
\le2\delta A_n+o(A_n).
\tag{WH23}
\]
This pays every source birth with at least one physical parent below \(\beta_n\), including descendants and the exact net response block. It does not say each differentiated term is separately small.

For explicit constants start from the supporting \(W/W\) floor \(3A_n/64\), or \(A_n/64\) on the original signed branch. Fix \(\delta=1/1024\) for the inherited and low-source payments. Eventually each costs at most \(A_n/256\). The remaining actual \(T_h/T_h\) floor is at least \(5A_n/128\) on the full record, hence \(A_n/32\), and at least \(A_n/128\) on the signed branch. A cap/ramp must have an actually retained starting floor.

## 4. Exact surviving energy and fractional projected flow

The remaining row is
\[
(T_h)_t+\nu AT_h+\Pi\mathcal L_tT_h
=F_h(t)-\mathbf1_{t>\tau}\mathscr U(t,t-\tau)F_h(t-\tau),
\quad F_h=-\Pi N(H_>,H_>).
\tag{WH24}
\]
At onset the delayed term is absent. Both physical source parents exceed \(\beta\); their descendants are not confined below \(\beta\). The current receiver remains full \(u\).

Before modulus, the exact \(L^2\) and critical rows are
\[
\begin{aligned}
\tfrac12(\|T_h\|^2)'+\nu\|\nabla T_h\|^2
&=\langle F_h-\mathbf1\mathscr U F_h^-,T_h\rangle
-\langle(T_h\cdot\nabla)U,T_h\rangle,\\
\tfrac12(\|\Lambda^{1/2}T_h\|^2)'+\nu\|\Lambda^{3/2}T_h\|^2
&=\langle F_h-\mathbf1\mathscr U F_h^-,\Lambda T_h\rangle
-\langle\Pi\mathcal L_tT_h,\Lambda T_h\rangle.
\end{aligned}
\tag{WH25}
\]
Transport is skew only in the first; its critical commutator remains in the second. The fixed-window positive Gram is
\[
\|\Lambda^{1/2}T_h(t)\|^2
=\int_{\sigma(t)}^t\int_{\sigma(t)}^t
\langle F_h(q),\mathscr U(t,q)^*\Lambda\mathscr U(t,v)F_h(v)\rangle\,dq\,dv.
\tag{WH26}
\]
It retains source interference and pressure. Its positivity alone gives no source-mass upper payment.

A new direct fractional estimate uses the actual Fourier coefficient norm
\[
\ell_F=(2\pi)^{-3}\int|p|\,|\widehat U(p)|dp
\le c_gKa^{5/2}\le\varepsilon\nu\rho^2.
\tag{WH27}
\]
This is not an interchange with physical \(\|\nabla U\|_\infty\). For \(s=\pm1/2\), put \(R_s=\|\Lambda^sy\|^2\) and test the homogeneous row with \(\Lambda^{2s}y\). Transport skewness gives half the commutator. On \(k=p+q\), \(|p|<a\), \(|k|,|q|\ge\rho\), its normalized factor is bounded by \(|p|\sqrt{|q|/|k|}\), using respectively \(\big||k|-|q|\big|\le|p|\) or \(\big||k|^{-1}-|q|^{-1}\big|\le|p|/(|k||q|)\). Stretching has factor \(|p|(|k|/|q|)^s\). Both ratios are at most \(\sqrt{1+a/\rho}\). Cauchy in the high frequency and the low \(L^1\) Fourier convolution yield
\[
|\langle\Lambda^{2s}y,\Pi\mathcal L_ty\rangle|
\le\tfrac32\sqrt{1+a/\rho}\,\ell_FR_s
<(\nu\rho^2/4)R_s.
\tag{WH28}
\]
The half coefficient is transport, the full coefficient stretching. The final inequality uses \((3/2)\sqrt{3/2}/8<1/4\). Pressure vanishes only after testing the complete solenoidal receiver and remains in the generator.

Since \(\|\Lambda^{s+1}y\|^2\ge\rho^2R_s\), this proves on the actual projected solenoidal homogeneous spaces
\[
\boxed{\|\mathscr U(t,q)\|_{\dot H^{1/2}\to\dot H^{1/2}},
\ \|\mathscr U(t,q)\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}\to\dot H^{-1/2}}
\le e^{-3\nu\rho^2(t-q)/4}.}
\tag{WH29}
\]
Finite Fourier approximation on a strict prefix followed by these uniform energy estimates gives the claimed domains. No unprojected spectral gap is assumed.

## 5. Quantified criterion on the actual remaining source hull

For receiver \(I=(s,t)\subset I_n\), its source hull is \(J=((s-\tau)_+,t)\). Define
\[
B_h(J):=\int_J\|F_h(q)\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}^2dq.
\tag{WH30}
\]
It is finite on a strict smooth prefix. No uniform whole-terminal entry is assumed. The actual entering window \(T_h(s)=\int_{(s-\tau)_+}^s\mathscr U(s,q)F_h(q)dq\) has critical stock at most \(2B_h(J)/\nu\), by the forced critical row with zero start at that actual window edge. The full current/deferred source \(h=F_h-\mathbf1\mathscr U F_h^-\) satisfies
\[
\int_I\|h\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}^2\le4B_h(J)
\tag{WH31}
\]
by WH29 and fixed-lag translation; no source before \(J\) is omitted. WH25, WH28 and Young give \(R_{1/2}'+\nu\|\Lambda^{3/2}T_h\|^2\le2\|h\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}^2/\nu\), hence
\[
\sup_I\|\Lambda^{1/2}T_h\|^2\le10B_h(J)/\nu,\quad
\int_I\|\Lambda^{3/2}T_h\|^2\le10B_h(J)/\nu^2.
\tag{WH32}
\]
The actual entering source window is included in 10, not replaced by an assumed endpoint bound.

Define a separate admissible constant \(C_{Q,\mathrm{frac}}\) for
\[
\|\mathcal Q^{ch}[f,f]\|_{\dot H^{-1/2}}
\le C_{Q,\mathrm{frac}}\|\Lambda^{1/2}f\|\|\Lambda^{3/2}f\|.
\tag{WH33}
\]
A valid choice follows explicitly from WH10 and Fourier Cauchy \(\|\nabla f\|^2\le\|\Lambda^{1/2}f\|\|\Lambda^{3/2}f\|\). This does not equate sharp constants. Alternatively the absolute-Fourier dual argument uses three \(L^3\) Sobolev factors and a finite tensor constant times \(S_3^3\). \(C_{Q,\mathrm{frac}},C_{Q,\nabla}\) and the unmasked \(C_{\rm src}\) below stay distinct.

The exact remainder consequently has the conditional estimates
\[
\begin{aligned}
\|\mathcal Q^{ch}[T_h,T_h]\|_{L^2(I;\dot H^{-1/2})}^2
&\le100C_{Q,\mathrm{frac}}^2B_h(J)^2/\nu^3,\\
|J_{I,\omega}^{hh}|&\le10\sqrt2\,C_{Q,\mathrm{frac}}B_h(J)\sqrt{A_n}/\nu^2
\quad(|\omega|\le1).
\end{aligned}
\tag{WH34}
\]
Thus \(B_h(J_n)=o(\sqrt{A_n})\) would pay this current by \(o(A_n)\). Young equivalently gives \(2\delta A_n+25C_{Q,\mathrm{frac}}^2B_h(J_n)^2/(\delta\nu^4)\).

Conversely the actual retained full-record \(A_n/32\) floor forces
\[
\boxed{B_h(((s_n-\tau_n)_+,t_n))
\ge\nu^2\sqrt{A_n}/(320\sqrt2\,C_{Q,\mathrm{frac}}).}
\tag{WH35}
\]
This is a lower bound on the actual source action from the actual current, not a replacement of it by a majorant. Branches/caps use their own retained floor and hull.

The available source majorant is
\[
B_h(J)\le C_{\rm src}^2\int_J\|\nabla H_>(q)\|^4dq.
\tag{WH36}
\]
Kinetic energy pays its unsquared gradient mass and high-frequency tail; this derivation has not paid the time square. WH36 is an upper bound, not an identity or a lower bound on \(B_h\). WH35–36 explicitly imply a lower bound on that time-square majorant along retained records, but do not supply an upper payment.

Keep the same full tensor primitive \(B_h^{out}=\mathcal T_{M_n/d}(u,T_h,T_h)\), \(d=\nu(|k|^2+|p|^2+|q|^2)\). Its exact response is
\[
\begin{aligned}

R_h^{out}&=\mathcal T_{M_{n,t}/d}(u,T_h,T_h)
+\mathcal T_{M_n/d}(Q(u),T_h,T_h)\\

\end{aligned}
\tag{WH37}
\]
Both low-flow orders, delayed source, full current \(Q(u)\), pressure and full-field gate remain. For the same cap and zero-end ramp,
\[
\boxed{\int\phi\zeta R_h^{out}+X_h+\int\phi'\zeta B_h^{out}
=\int\phi\zeta J_h^{out},\quad
X_h=-\sum\phi(t_j)\operatorname{sgn}Z'(t_j)B_h^{out}(t_j).}
\tag{WH38}
\]
The identical formula for \(P/P-T_h/T_h\) carries WH23 into its entire NET source/gate/cap/average block. Neither it nor the conditional WH34 upper input pays separated terms. An unconditional continuation needs an upper payment of this exact retained block below its floor, or a source-derived estimate implying it. WH30's \(o(\sqrt A)\) bound is one sufficient input, not asserted as a universal prerequisite.

## 6. The actual source-linear finite-chord receiver

FRS.30–31 uses the full actual \(Q(u)\), not \(Q(S_su)\):
\[
\langle h,G_{\rm fc}(u)\rangle
=\int_0^\infty D\mathcal E_\zeta(w_s)[S_sh]ds,\quad w_s=S_su,\quad
R_{\rm fc}=\langle Q(u),G_{\rm fc}(u)\rangle.
\tag{WH39}
\]
FRS.8–10 gives \(\mathcal E_\zeta=\mathcal P-\mathcal S_\zeta\),
\(\mathcal P(w)=-\langle(w\cdot\nabla)w,\Lambda w\rangle\),
\(\mathcal S_\zeta=-(32\ell_0/(45\pi))\int\operatorname{tr}S_w^3\).
Differentiating these actual functionals proves
\[
\begin{aligned}
G_B&=\int_0^\infty S_s\mathbb P\{-(\nabla w_s)^T\Lambda w_s
+(w_s\cdot\nabla)\Lambda w_s-\Lambda((w_s\cdot\nabla)w_s)\}ds,\\
G_A&=\frac{32\ell_0}{15\pi}\int_0^\infty S_s\mathbb P\operatorname{div}(S_{w_s}^2)ds,
\qquad G_{\rm fc}=G_B-G_A.
\end{aligned}
\tag{WH40}
\]
The complete signed commutator is retained before modulus. For \(k=p+q\), the unsymmetrized numerator of \(G_B\) is
\[
i\mathbb P_k\big[(|q|-|k|)(q\cdot\widehat u(p))\widehat u(q)
-p|q|(\widehat u(p)\cdot\widehat u(q))\big].
\]
Its two terms have absolute bound \(2|p||q|\,|\widehat u(p)||\widehat u(q)|\), using the actual signed \(|q|-|k|\) before estimating. The numerator of \(G_A\) has bound \(|k||p||q|\,|\widehat u(p)||\widehat u(q)|\), since the Fourier strain has Frobenius norm at most \(|p||\widehat u(p)|\). Integration in age divides by \(\nu(|k|^2+|p|^2+|q|^2)\). The inequalities \(2|p||q|\le|p|^2+|q|^2\) and convolution Cauchy with \(\int|\widehat u|^2=(2\pi)^3\|u\|^2\) cancel the actual STK product factor \((2\pi)^{-3}\), and yield
\[
|\widehat G_B(k)|\le K^2/\nu,\qquad
|\widehat G_A(k)|\le16\ell_0|k|K^2/(15\pi\nu).
\tag{WH41}
\]
Output Leray remains. Pressure vanishes in the complete global functional pairing, not by omitting its source. Plancherel on the actual output ball proves
\[
\|P_{\le2\beta}G_{\rm fc}\|_{\dot H^{1/2}}
\le\frac{K^2}{\nu\pi}\left[\sqrt2\,\beta^2+
\frac{64\ell_0}{15\sqrt3\pi}\beta^3\right].
\tag{WH42}
\]
Only the compact source \(F_{\beta\beta}=-\Pi N(H_\beta,H_\beta)\) has output at most \(2\beta\). Its actual source estimate gives the independent payment
\[
\int_0^T|\langle F_{\beta\beta},G_{\rm fc}(u)\rangle|dt
\le\frac{C_{\rm src}K^4}{2\nu^2\pi}
\left[\sqrt2\,\beta^2+\frac{64\ell_0}{15\sqrt3\pi}\beta^3\right]
=o(A_n)\quad(\beta_n\asymp A_n^{1/6}).
