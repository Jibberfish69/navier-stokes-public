## 4. Two exact transport–heat comparison orders

\tag{WH8}
\]

\tag{WH9}

because $\kappa\ge1/2$ and $\sup_{h>0}\sqrt h e^{-\nu\rho^2h/2}=1/(\sqrt{e\nu}\rho)$. Hence
