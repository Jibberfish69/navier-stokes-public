## 2. Full cubic terminal identity with a positive mixed-row completion

\[

\[

\[
 S_{a,\eta}=c_{a,\eta}Y^3+(1-a)\nu^2Z
 +(1+\eta^{-1})\|f\|_2^2-\|f-B\|_2^2\ge0.

This is the actual derivative Y'=2<u_t,Lu>, not an independent assignment of temporal and spatial rows. Combining ZS4, ZS6 and ZS7 gives the full signed terminal balance

At f=0, eta=0 is allowed and its force term vanishes. With arbitrary smooth force, eta is kept positive through the terminal passage. Then z->0 makes its force contribution o(T-t) for each fixed eta. The remainder retains full canonical pressure and the actual alignment u_t-nu Lu/3. Along any actual time sequence on which z²/(T-t) tends to L_*, its mean terminal cubic alignment/pressure remainder tends to zero. No such sequence or profile is presumed.
