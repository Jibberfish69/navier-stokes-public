# Mathematical coverage

These reports examine one bounded hierarchy of quadratic operations derived from the full pressure-complete Navier–Stokes history: finite temporal jets, positive projective forms, actual time shifts, source-compensated covariance and adaptive clocks. The constructions are identified as derived analysis, rather than new printed manuscript lemmas.

The exact coefficient programs verify finite algebra. They neither simulate a fluid history nor verify analytic convergence or an unconditional terminal bound. All complete nonlinear parents, physical pressure/force terms, strict endpoints and stated limit hypotheses remain in the reports.

The receiving target is the original physical-time first rectangle: velocity in whole-terminal L²H² and its genuine temporal derivative in whole-terminal L²L². Paid positive stocks, signed joined-current primitives, fixed-shift integrability and conditional quotient/variance/tracking consumers retain their exact scope. The missing physical action or convergence hypotheses are not silently assumed.

This package makes no claim about every possible construction, every other research branch or an unconditional certification of the manuscript.
