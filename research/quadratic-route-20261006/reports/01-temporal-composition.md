# Arbitrary temporal depth: exact cancellation, positive weights and legitimate limits

6 October 2026. This is a bounded mathematical execution of the actual pressure-complete temporal rows and Gram operation. It extends the previously verified positive three-row contraction to every finite temporal depth, classifies its matched-coefficient cancellation weights, and checks what a projective limit would need to retain the original first rectangle. It supplies exact identities and conditional implications, without assuming the terminal estimate under examination. These contractions are derived analysis; they are not presented as new printed definitions or lemmas of the manuscript.

The finite cancellation is substantive: every matched interior ordered triple cancels with its incompressible partner. Its full remainder is an unmatched derivative-cutoff boundary, including all highest-row strain and proper curvature parents. Within this particular factorial-Hankel coefficient family, PSD and cancellation of every boundary coefficient leave only kinetic energy. An infinite positive form can retain uniform low-row coercivity, but a legitimate limit additionally needs the actual stocks, sources and signed boundary to converge. Arbitrary smooth forcing supplies each finite order, rather than the required order-uniform receiver bounds. An explicit admitted, globally smooth forced solution below verifies that distinction for the proposed fixed projective receiver class.

## Exact finite coefficient class and boundary

For real symmetric \(Q\) on indices \(0,\ldots,N\), let
\(T(r,a,b)=\langle V_r,N(V_a,V_b)\rangle\). Full-space incompressibility gives exactly
\(T(r,a,b)=-T(b,a,r)\). Extend \(Q\) by zero outside the selected square. The complete nonlinear contraction is
\[
 \mathcal P_N(Q)=\frac12\sum_{r,a,b=0}^N
 \left[\binom{a+b}{a}\widehat Q_{r,a+b}
       -\binom{a+r}{a}\widehat Q_{b,a+r}\right]T(r,a,b).
 \tag{AD3}
\]
Every coefficient with both \(a+b\le N\) and \(a+r\le N\) vanishes if and only if
\[
 \boxed{Q_{rs}=\frac{m_{r+s}}{r!s!},\quad 0\le r,s\le N.}
 \tag{AD4}
\]
Indeed \(H_{rs}=r!s!Q_{rs}\) turns the difference in AD3 into
\((\widehat H_{r,a+b}-\widehat H_{b,a+r})/(r!a!b!)\). For each antidiagonal total \(d\), take \(b=\max(0,d-N)\), \(a=d-r-b\). The matched equalities join every admissible \(H_{r,d-r}\) to \(H_{b,d-b}\), so one number \(m_d\) represents that entire antidiagonal. Conversely equal totals immediately cancel. This proves necessity and sufficiency at arbitrary finite \(N\), with dimension \(2N+1\). It classifies these paired coefficients, rather than all possible identities of special fluid histories or mixed spatial operations.

Under AD4 the complete remainder is
\[
 \boxed{\mathcal B_N(m,V)=
 \sum_{\substack{0\le r,a,b\le N\\a+b\le N<a+r}}
 \frac{m_{r+a+b}}{r!a!b!}\,T(r,a,b).}
 \tag{AD5}
\]
Its totals are \(N+1,\ldots,2N\). At degree \(N+j\) it has exactly \(j(N-j+1)\) displayed ordered terms, and \(\binom{N+2}{3}\) in all. The missing partner is the source-column index \(a+r>N\); all velocity factors in AD5 already belong to the original selected rows. Thus this formula neither adds a row nor substitutes an unknown boundary for a force.

Writing \(S(u,x,x)=\langle x,N(x,u)\rangle\), the highest total is
\[
 \mathcal B_N^{[2N]}=Q_{NN}
 \left[S(u,V_N,V_N)+\sum_{a=1}^{N-1}\binom Na
              \langle V_N,N(V_a,V_{N-a})\rangle\right].
 \tag{AD6}
\]
The omitted transport term \(a=0\) is exactly zero. Every proper curvature parent remains. The complete boundary is not always only the literal receiving row \(N\): at \(N=3\) the degree-four layer is
\(m_4\langle V_1,(\operatorname{sym}\nabla u)V_3\rangle/3
 +m_4S(u,V_2,V_2)/4\), before its degree-five and degree-six layers. At \(N=2\),
\[
 \mathcal B_2=m_3\langle V_1,(\operatorname{sym}\nabla u)V_2\rangle
 +\frac{m_4}{4}\{S(u,V_2,V_2)+2\langle V_2,N(V_1,V_1)\rangle\}.
 \tag{AD7}
\]
This reproduces the genuine lower strain cancellation \(Q_{11}=2Q_{02}\), with its full higher current. The complete proof and every \(N=3\) layer appear in [the independently reviewed finite derivation](02-temporal-coefficients.md).

## Positive weights and exact balance

AD4 is PSD precisely when the finite Hankel matrix \(H_N=(m_{r+s})\) is PSD, by factorial diagonal congruence. A positive measure with moments through \(2N\) supplies examples; \(N+1\) distinct real support points with positive masses make it positive definite. Finite PSD alone does not supply a representing measure: \((m_0,\ldots,m_4)=(1,1,1,1,2)\) gives the PSD form \((z_0+z_1+z_2)^2+z_2^2\), whereas a represented measure would have \(\int(x-1)^2=0\) and therefore \(m_4=1\).

If \(Q_{NN}=0\), its PSD zero-row principle first forces \(m_N,\ldots,m_{2N}=0\). Applying the same principle successively to rows \(N-1,\ldots,1\) forces every \(m_j=0\), \(j\ge1\). Consequently, within AD4, PSD and cancellation of every formal boundary coefficient leave \(Q=m_0e_0e_0^T\): pure kinetic energy. This does not supply a sign for AD5 or prove that another analytic construction cannot pay it.

For \(C^1\) moments, or locally absolutely continuous moments in the integrated law, define
\(E_N=\frac12\sum Q_{ij}\langle V_i,V_j\rangle\),
\(D_N=\sum Q_{ij}\langle\nabla V_i,\nabla V_j\rangle\). Then
\[
 E_N'+\nu D_N=-\mathcal B_N(m,V)
       +\sum Q_{ij}\langle V_i,F_j\rangle
       +\frac12\sum Q'_{ij}\langle V_i,V_j\rangle.
 \tag{AD8}
\]
Its strict-prefix integral retains \(E_N(B)\), \(E_N(0)\), all diffusion, boundary, force and coefficient work. The initial \(V_0(0),\ldots,V_N(0)\) are generated from the datum and \(F_0(0),\ldots,F_{N-1}(0)\) by the full projected recurrence; the top equation also retains \(F_N\). Actual fields through \(V_{N+1}\) enter the derivative of the top selected row. There is no depth truncation of NS. PSD pays neither the signed boundary nor coefficient derivatives.

The full-square companion from the same original rows is
\[
 \sum Q_{ij}\langle V_{i+1},V_{j+1}\rangle
 +\nu^2\sum Q_{ij}\langle LV_i,LV_j\rangle
 +\sum Q_{ij}\langle\nabla\Pi_i,\nabla\Pi_j\rangle
 +\nu D_N'-\nu\sum Q'_{ij}\langle\nabla V_i,\nabla V_j\rangle
 =\sum Q_{ij}\langle F_i-B_i,F_j-B_j\rangle.
 \tag{AD9}
\]
All four force/nonlinear pair classes on the right remain, and pressure remains in its positive Gram square. Locally AD8 keeps the full flux
\(u e_N-\nu\nabla e_N+\sum Q_{ij}\Pi_jV_i\), with \(e_N=\frac12\sum Q_{ij}V_i\cdot V_j\); only the common \(N(u,V_j)\) transport was placed in that flux. Localization retains its selector derivatives and the remaining nonlinear terms. The paired cancellation additionally moves paired nonlinear terms into their exact divergence flux. No full-space orthogonality is applied to erase a selected-region pressure face.

## Full shifted forcing, coefficient derivatives and positive response

Let \(g_j=\mathbb PF_j\), \(\phi_f=-L^{-1}\operatorname{div}f\), \(p_0=p-\phi_f=R_iR_j(u_iu_j)\). Define \(H_0=u\), \(G_0=0\) and \(H_n=V_n-g_{n-1}\), \(G_n=g_{n-1}\) for \(n\ge1\). Then \(H_n'=H_{n+1}\) for \(n\ge1\), while \(u'=H_1+g_0\). With \(b_n=\sum_a\binom naN(H_a,H_{n-a})\), the exact raw rows are
\[
 H_n'+\nu LH_n+b_n+\nabla p_0^{(n)}=R_n,
 \quad R_0=g_0,
 \tag{AD10}
\]
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 R_n={}&-\nu Lg_{n-1}-N(u,g_{n-1})-N(g_{n-1},u)\\
 &-\sum_{a=1}^{n-1}\binom na
 \{N(H_a,g_{n-a-1})+N(g_{a-1},H_{n-a})+N(g_{a-1},g_{n-a-1})\},\quad n\ge1.
 \end{aligned}\tag{AD11}
\]
The raw pressure is
\(p_0^{(n)}=R_iR_j\sum_a\binom na(H_a+G_a)_i(H_{n-a}+G_{n-a})_j\);
the original pressure is exactly \(p^{(n)}=p_0^{(n)}+\phi_f^{(n)}\). This keeps the force–force allocations, raw source, scalar potential and pressure correction together. Prolongation at \(n\ge1\) gives
\[
 \partial_t^qR_n=R_{n+q}+\sum_{a=0}^{q-1}\binom q{a+1}
 \{N(g_a,H_{n+q-1-a})+N(H_{n+q-1-a},g_a)\}.
 \tag{AD12}
\]
It includes the base-force correction; \(R_n'=R_{n+1}\) would lose it. Each derivative of coefficient-weighted force work has the full three-factor multinomial allocation across coefficient, source and receiving field. Pressure prolongation retains its exterior divergence and all original force-potential derivatives.

For \(C_{ij}=\langle H_i,H_j\rangle\), \(K_{ij}=\langle\nabla H_i,\nabla H_j\rangle\), the same factorial coefficient cancellation applies directly to the full \(b_n\), giving
\[
 \mathcal E_N'+\nu\operatorname{tr}(QK)
 =-\mathcal B_N(m,H)+\sum Q_{ij}\langle H_i,R_j\rangle
                          +\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(Q'C).
 \tag{AD13}
\]
On each finite force horizon, kinetic control pays \(\|u\|_2\le K_T=\|u_0\|_2+\int_0^T\|g_0\|_2\). For \(n\ge1\), the complete source bound is
\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \|R_n\|_{\dot H^{-1}}\le{}&A_n+
 2\sum_{\ell=1}^{n-1}\binom n\ell\|g_{n-\ell-1}\|_\infty\|H_\ell\|_2,\\
 A_n={}&\nu\|\nabla g_{n-1}\|_2+2K_T\|g_{n-1}\|_\infty
 +\sum_{a=1}^{n-1}\binom na\|g_{a-1}\|_\infty\|g_{n-a-1}\|_2.
 \end{aligned}\tag{AD14}
\]
Thus for every fixed positive-definite finite \(Q\), the full force work is bounded by
\(\varepsilon\nu\operatorname{tr}(QK)+C_{N,g,Q,T,\nu,\varepsilon}(1+\operatorname{tr}(QC))\).
The separate base source is \(R_0=g_0\), with its known finite \(\dot H^{-1}\) norm supplied by the prescribed force decay. This retains actual lower stocks, and its constant is finite-order. A singular \(Q\) leaves uncontrolled coefficient directions visible. No common force constant in \(N\) follows from smooth forcing.

For the regularized normalization used in the preceding joined calculation, \(\delta>0\),
\(\lambda=\nu Y/(e+\delta)\), \(Y=\|\nabla u\|_2^2\), \(e=\|u\|_2^2\),
\(w=H_1+\lambda u\). The exact relation \(\langle u,w\rangle=-\delta\lambda\) means the derivative of the rank-one weight \(r r^T\), \(r=(\lambda,1,0,\ldots)\), contributes \(-\delta\lambda\lambda'\). Its complete response stock satisfies
\[
 \Psi_w'+\nu\|\nabla w\|_2^2+S(u,H_1,H_1)
       =\langle R_1,w\rangle+\lambda\langle g_0,w\rangle,
 \qquad \Psi_w=\tfrac12(\|w\|_2^2+\delta\lambda^2).
 \tag{AD15}
\]
Every \(\lambda^{(q)}\) is generated by differentiating
\((e+\delta)\lambda=\nu Y\), including full derivatives of \(e,Y\) and all \(V_j=H_j+G_j\) force parents. It is not a prescribed-force coefficient.

Adding this rank-one matrix to an already Hankel matrix breaks the lower cancellation; the total matrix must satisfy AD4. A constructive finite positive embedding is available at every \(N\ge2\): choose a fixed positive-definite factorial-Hankel \(Q_*\), set
\(M=1+r^TQ_*^{-1}r\), \(Q=M Q_*\), \(Q_s=Q-r r^T\succeq0\).
Then \(\mathcal E_Q=\|w\|_2^2/2+\operatorname{tr}(Q_sC)/2\), and the stock augmented by \(\delta\lambda^2/2\) has exactly the coefficient work
\[
 \tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(Q'C)+\delta\lambda\lambda'
           =\tfrac12\operatorname{tr}(Q_s'C).
 \tag{AD16}
\]
The total nonlinear contraction still has AD5, with full higher currents and every \(M'\) or \(Q_s'\) port. This embedding proves positivity and preserves the lower cancellation; it does not estimate those ports.

For the previously successful \(N=2\) choice
\((m_0,m_1,m_2,m_3,m_4)=(\lambda^2+a,\lambda,1,0,4c)\), \(a,c>0\), \(ac>1/4\), the \(N=3\) principal minor on indices \(0,1,3\) necessarily satisfies
\[
 a(m_6-m_4^2)-\lambda^2m_4^2\ge0.
 \tag{AD17}
\]
More generally that determinant is \(a(m_6-m_4^2)-(m_3-\lambda m_4)^2\) with the same \(m_0,m_1,m_2\). Fixed higher moments cannot extend this particular block over unbounded \(\lambda\); moving them introduces their actual derivatives in AD13. The positive embedding AD16 is an alternative construction retaining those derivatives, so AD17 is a diagnostic of this fixed extension, rather than a no-go for every adaptive weight. ## Projective positivity, feasible coercivity and order cost

Let a single moment sequence give PSD \(H_N\) at every depth. Quotient the polynomial form \(\langle x^i,x^j\rangle_m=m_{i+j}\) by its nullspace and complete to \(\mathcal H_m\), with moment vectors \(e_n\). This requires no measure theorem. Its actual finite receivers are
\(Z_N=\sum_{n=0}^N X_n\otimes e_n/n!\), where \(X=V\) or the explicitly compensated \(H\). Positivity gives \(\|Z_N\|^2=2E_N\). For \(m_0,m_2>0\), the even-moment minors give
\[
 m_{2n}^2\le m_{2n-2}m_{2n+2},\qquad
 m_{2n}\ge m_0\rho^{2n},\quad \rho=\sqrt{m_2/m_0}>0.
 \tag{AD18}
\]
For a specified Hilbert field norm \(\mathcal X\), if every prefix obeys \(\|Z_N\|_{\mathcal X\otimes\mathcal H_m}\le C\), adjacent-prefix subtraction, with no orthogonality assumption, gives
\[
 \frac{\sqrt{m_{2n}}}{n!}\|X_n\|_{\mathcal X}=\|Z_n-Z_{n-1}\|
 \le2C,\qquad
 \|X_n\|_{\mathcal X}\le\frac{2C}{\sqrt{m_0}}n!\rho^{-n}.
 \tag{AD19}
\]
Here the Hilbert field norm may be \(L^2_x\) at a time or \(L^2_tL^2_x\). A uniform time stock means applying the pointwise Hilbert inequality with the same bound \(C\) at every time, rather than treating \(L^\infty_tL^2_x\) as a Hilbert tensor factor. A bounded prefix sequence need not be Cauchy; Cauchy implies AD19 and its adjacent increments tend to zero. This argument never replaces the correlated receiver by a diagonal sum. A factorial bound at one time alone need not identify a smooth function with its Taylor series. A bound uniform on a neighborhood supplies the corresponding analytic radius through Taylor's integral remainder; the source's \(C^\infty\) condition supplies neither uniformity.

The exact test for first-coefficient coercivity is
\(\kappa_{1,N}=\inf\{z^TH_Nz:z_1=1\}\downarrow\kappa_{1,\infty}\ge0\).
It remains valid at rank loss; for a positive-definite block it equals \(1/(H_N^{-1})_{11}\). There are positive sequences with \(\kappa_{1,\infty}>0\). For example, take \(q>1\),
\[
 m_{2n}=q^{n^2},\qquad m_{2n+1}=0.
 \tag{AD20}
\]
The explicit law \(\theta=\pm e^G\), \(G\) centered normal with variance \(\log q/2\), verifies this particular positivity. Odd and even blocks are orthogonal. The odd block factors as \(qD_jq^{2jk}D_k\), \(D_j=q^{j^2+2j}\). Lagrange interpolation at the Vandermonde nodes \(1,q^2,\ldots,q^{2K}\) gives
\[
 \kappa_{1,N}=q\prod_{k=1}^{\lfloor(N-1)/2\rfloor}(1-q^{-2k})\to qP>0,
 \quad \kappa_{0,N}\to P=\prod_{k=1}^\infty(1-q^{-2k})>0.
 \tag{AD21}
\]
For \(N\ge1\), this yields \(\|Z_N\|^2\ge P\|X_0\|^2+qP\|X_1\|^2\). Thus losing low-row coercivity is not an inevitable algebraic failure of an infinite completion. The same positive sequence requires \(\|X_n\|\le2Cn!q^{-n^2/2}\) if its actual prefixes are bounded: a much stronger order condition than a fixed analytic radius.

The degenerate all-depth case \(m_2=0\) forces all \(m_j=0\), \(j\ge1\), by the PSD zero row at index one. Independent very small diagonal weights fail AD4; letting \(m_2\to0\) loses temporal weight, while letting \(m_0\to\infty\) changes the coefficient of the zeroth datum stock (which can itself be zero for a zero datum). Nonprojective depth-varying matrices do not share this single Hilbert receiver and need their own change-of-weight and coercivity analysis. No statement here excludes every such construction.

## Legitimate infinite cancellation and all source ports

In addition to the relevant endpoint stocks and gradient/derivative actions, an infinite use of AD8 or AD13 needs convergence of force work, coefficient work, and the explicit signed \(\mathcal B_N\). Sufficient conditions for the first include convergence of both actual receiver and source receiver in their paired Hilbert time norms. For fixed moments, sufficient conditions for the nonlinear cancellation include the actual integrated absolute triple condition
\[
 \sum_{r,a,b\ge0}\frac{|m_{r+a+b}|}{r!a!b!}
 \int_0^B\int_{\mathbb R^3}|X_r|\,|X_a|\,|\nabla X_b|\,dx\,dt<\infty.
 \tag{AD22}
\]
It permits Fubini and exchange of the receiver and final slot; the complete infinite contraction then cancels. For moving moments the corresponding sufficient condition places \(|m_{r+a+b}(t)|\) inside the time integral and separately retains convergence of the full \(Q'\) work. Alternatively, directly proving the signed boundary tends to zero in the required integrated or distributional sense can suffice. Ordered Hilbert convergence alone supplies neither nonlinear rearrangement nor convergence of an unbounded temporal/spatial shift. The all-source pressure square AD9 has additional shifted, Laplacian and pressure norms and retains all source cross terms.

For \(\varphi\in C_c^1(0,B)\), the exact finite weak balance reads
\(-\int\varphi'E_N+\nu\int\varphi D_N
=-\int\varphi\mathcal B_N+\int\varphi\mathcal F_N+\frac12\int\varphi\operatorname{tr}(Q'C)\).
Test functions reaching the endpoint keep \([\varphi E_N]_0^B\). Taking \(N\to\infty\), then \(B\uparrow T_*\), requires the respective actual convergences at each stage. Pointwise compatible finite rows and PSD alone supply neither passage. The source finite-rectangle argument does not require a common bound over all orders; it acquires this additional obligation only if this particular infinite weighted contraction is used to produce its first rectangle.

## An admitted complete forced history testing the proposed bound

Choose a nonzero real solenoidal Schwartz field \(U\), for example \(\nabla\times(0,0,e^{-|x|^2})\). Choose \(c>\pi\) and smooth compactly supported \(\chi\), supported away from zero and equal to one on \(I=[c-\pi,c+\pi]\). Prescribe
\[
 a(t)=\chi(t)\sum_{j\ge1}e^{-\sqrt j}\cos(j(t-c)),\quad
 u=aU,\quad p=0,\quad
 f=a'U+\nu aLU+a^2N(U,U).
 \tag{AD23}
\]
Every finite derivative series converges uniformly since \(\sum j^ke^{-\sqrt j}<\infty\). This is an actual globally smooth solution, with \(u_0=0\), of the complete forced NS equation in the admitted Schwartz force class. Its canonical pressure is exactly zero because
\(R_iR_j(a^2U_iU_j)-L^{-1}\operatorname{div}f=0\).
The kinetic law retains \(\langle u,f\rangle\), and every finite spatial/temporal rectangle is finite. All initial jets and force jets vanish because the cutoff is supported away from zero; the failure of the later weighted budget is not an initial-stock failure. This example concerns the proposed additional all-depth receiver budget, rather than a counterexample to regularity.

At \(c\), all even derivative terms have the same sign. For \(n=2k\) choose the integer Fourier mode \(j=(4k)^2\):
\[
 |a^{(2k)}(c)|\ge e^{-4k}(4k)^{4k},\qquad
 |a^{(n)}(c)|/n!\ge(4n/e^2)^n.
 \tag{AD24}
\]
This exceeds every fixed geometric bound. There is also an integrated check on the exact flat window: Fourier orthogonality gives
\(\|a^{(n)}\|_{L^2(I)}^2=\pi\sum_{j\ge1}e^{-2\sqrt j}j^{2n}\), hence the same growth multiplied by \(\sqrt\pi\). Thus this actual raw jet violates AD19 for every nontrivial fixed/projective positive form with finite \(m_0,m_2>0\), both at \(c\) and for a uniformly bounded sequence of receivers in \(L^2(I;L^2_x\otimes\mathcal H_m)\).

Full force compensation preserves the distinction:
\[
 h=u_t-\mathbb Pf=-\nu aLU-a^2\mathbb PN(U,U),\qquad
 \langle U,H_{n+1}(t)\rangle=-\nu\|\nabla U\|_2^2a^{(n)}(t).
 \tag{AD25}
\]
The quadratic parent pairs to zero with \(U\) at every derivative order. Therefore the compensated jets have the same superfactorial growth, including in the integrated window. A time-adaptive projective form with finite nonzero \(m_0(c),m_2(c)\) still faces the pointwise AD19 at \(c\). Making \(m_2(c)=0\) removes first-row coercivity there. This directly disproves a universal bounded-prefix claim for this proposed nontrivial projective coefficient family under arbitrary smooth forcing; it leaves unforced derivative behavior and other finite/nonprojective producers outside this test. The complete source and pressure verification appears in [the projective execution](04-temporal-projective-forms.md).

## Conditional return to the actual first rectangle

The source's actual norm is
\(\mathfrak R_{N,M}=\sum_{n=0}^N\|V_n\|_{L^2H^{M+1}}^2
+\sum_{n=0}^N\|V_{n+1}\|_{L^2H^{M-1}}^2\).
Consequently \(\mathfrak R_{0,1}\) requires \(u\in L^2H^2\), \(u_t\in L^2L^2\). A low temporal stock can pay it through the original momentum if that stock has actually been bounded; it need not have an explicit spatial \(Lu\) term in its norm.

In detail suppose \(\sup_{t<\min(T,T_*)}\|H_1(t)\|_2\le H\) has been independently established. The original coupled equation and kinetic bound give
\[
 Y=-\langle u,H_1\rangle/\nu\le K_TH/\nu,
 \quad \nu\|Lu\|_2\le H+C_SY^{3/4}\|Lu\|_2^{1/2},
 \quad \|Lu\|_2\le 2H/\nu+C_S^2Y^{3/2}/\nu^2.
 \tag{AD26}
\]
Here \(\|N(u,u)\|_2\le C_SY^{3/4}\|Lu\|_2^{1/2}\) follows from the actual \(L^6\) Sobolev and \(\nabla u\) interpolation; it estimates the complete convection. On a finite horizon these bounds pay the original \(L^2H^2\) face, and \(u_t=H_1+g_0\) pays its companion. The pressure is preserved exactly:
\(H_1+N(u,u)=-\nu Lu-\nabla p_0\) gives
\(\|H_1+N(u,u)\|_2^2=\nu^2\|Lu\|_2^2+\|\nabla p_0\|_2^2\),
with the physical \(\phi_f\) restored separately. This is a conditional conversion after an actually paid stock, rather than a replacement of the manuscript method by an assumed a priori closure.

The exact remaining implication for this attempted operation is therefore: produce a terminal-uniform budget for a coefficient form coercive on the first response, retaining AD5, AD11–16, all current/initial stocks and the appropriate limit topology. The finite cancellation proves none of that upper payment by itself. The infinite family cannot supply a universal nontrivial bounded-prefix budget for the full arbitrary smooth forcing class, as AD23–25 verify. These are conclusions about the executed family and its actual quantifiers. They are not a general impossibility claim or a certificate that no other part of the paper can supply its membership producer.
