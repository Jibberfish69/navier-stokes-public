Kinetic energy does not see the width of a sharpening velocity field.  For the
maximal classical solution \(u\) generated by divergence-free Schwartz data at
viscosity \(\nu>0\), the first estimate capable of crossing a proposed finite
endpoint \(T_*\) is exactly the finiteness of the critical dissipation:

\[
  D_*:=\int_0^{T_*}\norm{\Lambda^{3/2}u(t)}_2^2\,dt<\infty
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \begin{cases}
    u\in L^2(0,T_*;H^2),\\
    u_t\in L^2(0,T_*;L^2).
  \end{cases}
\]
The critical-height balance reads the same condition as boundedness of the
signed critical-production prefix.  The enstrophy equation also shows where
the direct route stops: with
\(Z=\norm{\Lambda u}_2^2\) and \(Y=\norm{\Lambda^2u}_2^2\),

\[
  Z'+\nu Y\leq C\nu^{-3}Z^3,
\]

while the kinetic-energy law controls only \(\int_0^{T_*}Z(t)\,dt\).  If
\(D_*<\infty\), the adjacent trace gives
\(u\in C([0,T_*];H^1)\subset L^\infty(0,T_*;L^3)\), contradicting the
Escauriaza--Seregin--\v{S}ver\'ak endpoint criterion.  Thus the first rectangle
alone would rule out \(T_*<\infty\).

If the corresponding whole-terminal bound holds for every finite rectangle,
their compatibility carries the differentiated flow to one \(H^\infty\)
terminal state, and local uniqueness continues the same history beyond
\(T_*\).  That smooth endpoint places a finite roof over the critical height:
the Zeno ladder ends, fixed material labels cannot collapse, and the local
scale-invariant singularity content vanishes.  No estimate proved here controls
the cubic enstrophy term from the kinetic-energy bound, so the global conclusion
remains conditional on the critical dissipation above.
