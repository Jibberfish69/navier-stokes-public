We prove that for every fixed viscosity \(\nu>0\) and every divergence-free initial datum \(u_0\in\mathscr S(\R^3;\R^3)\), the maximal classical solution of the unforced three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes equations is global, with velocity and pressure smooth on \(\R^3\times[0,\infty)\).

Write \([0,T_*)\) for the maximal classical lifespan and suppose that \(T_*<\infty\).
The central datum-generated estimate is uniform up to \(T_*\): for every finite \(N,M\in\Nzero\),
\[
  \sum_{n=0}^{N}
  \left(
    \|\partial_t^n u\|_{L^2(0,T_*;H^{M+1})}^{2}
    +
    \|\partial_t^{n+1}u\|_{L^2(0,T_*;H^{M-1})}^{2}
  \right)
  <\infty.
\]
At \((N,M)=(0,1)\), the Hilbert-space trace theorem gives \(u\in C([0,T_*];H^1(\R^3))\).
Sobolev embedding then gives \(u\in L^\infty(0,T_*;L^3(\R^3))\).
The Escauriaza--Seregin--\v{S}ver\'ak endpoint criterion makes this bound incompatible with a finite maximal time.

Under the same finite-time hypothesis, the finite-order velocity and normalized-pressure families agree on overlaps, and their compatible intersection gives the stronger endpoint statement: \(u(t)\to u_*\) in every \(H^m(\R^3)\) as \(t\uparrow T_*\), for one divergence-free \(u_*\in\bigcap_{m<\infty}H^m(\R^3)\).
At \(T_*\), the differentiated equations recover every velocity derivative and the corresponding pressure derivatives under the fixed decaying normalization.
Local well-posedness and uniqueness then restart the same solution from \(u_*\).
The \(L^3\) contradiction and the smooth restart both rule out a finite \(T_*\), so \(T_*=\infty\).
The global kinetic-energy identity gives \(\|u(t)\|_2\leq\|u_0\|_2\) for every \(t\geq0\).
