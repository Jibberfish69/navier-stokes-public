We prove global existence and smoothness for the unforced three-dimensional incompressible Navier--Stokes equations on \(\R^3\), for every viscosity \(\nu>0\) and every divergence-free Schwartz initial velocity.
Fix such a datum \(u_0\), and let \((u,p)\) be its pressure-normalized maximal classical solution on \([0,T_*)\).
If \(T_*<\infty\), the central estimate controls every prescribed finite adjacent rectangle of temporal and spatial derivatives of this same solution on the whole interval \((0,T_*)\); the pressure recurrence supplies the corresponding normalized pressure rows.
Larger rectangles restrict to identical coordinates in smaller ones, so the finite blocks assemble into one datum-specific compatible intersection.
That intersection gives a common terminal velocity in \(H^\infty\) and the complete pressure-normalized endpoint jet.
Local well-posedness and uniqueness then continue the same history through \(T_*\), contradicting maximality.
Thus \(T_*=\infty\); velocity and normalized pressure remain smooth for all time, and the energy identity keeps kinetic energy bounded by its initial value.
