\subsection{More spatial regularity, not less}\label{sub:ThePressureCompleteMixedDerivativeTower}

Now put the two directions beside each other.
The heat clock has already shown that one time derivative costs two powers of scale.
At critical height, moving one full Sobolev step upward in the quadratic spatial energy costs the same two powers.
Set
\[
  E_s(t):=\frac12\norm{\Lambda^su(t)}_2^2,
  \qquad
  E_{s,\lambda}(t):=\frac12\norm{\Lambda^su_\lambda(t)}_2^2,
  \qquad
  H=E_{1/2}.
\]
The scaling calculation from the stretching vortex gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodySpatialEnergyScaling}
  E_{s,\lambda}(t)
  =\lambda^{2s-1}E_s(\lambda^2t).
\end{equation}
At \(s=\tfrac12\),
\[
  H_\lambda(t):=E_{1/2,\lambda}(t)=H(\lambda^2t).
\]
Differentiating \(n\) times in \(t\) gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCriticalHeightTemporalScaling}
  H_\lambda^{(n)}(t)
  =\lambda^{2n}H^{(n)}(\lambda^2t).
\end{equation}
At the spatial height \(s=n+\tfrac12\), equation \eqref{eq:BodySpatialEnergyScaling} gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodySpatialHeightScalingDiagonal}
  E_{n+1/2,\lambda}(t)
  =\lambda^{2n}E_{n+1/2}(\lambda^2t).
\end{equation}
The \(n\)-th temporal response of critical height and the spatial energy at height \(n+\tfrac12\) have the same scale weight.
This is the time--space match hidden in the heat clock.

Scaling tells us which spatial height belongs beside each temporal order.
The differentiated equation tells us why that height is present.
Since
\[
  H(t)=\frac12\pair{\Lambda^{1/2}V_0(t)}{\Lambda^{1/2}V_0(t)}_{L^2},
\]
Leibniz's rule gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyHeightDerivativeTemporalContraction}
  H^{(n)}(t)
  =\frac12\sum_{a=0}^{n}\binom{n}{a}
  \operatorname{Re}
  \pair{\Lambda^{1/2}V_a(t)}
       {\Lambda^{1/2}V_{n-a}(t)}_{L^2}.
\end{equation}
Thus \(H^{(n)}\) contracts together the temporal rows \(V_0,\ldots,V_n\).
It is a scalar response built from those rows, distinct from every individual \(V_n\).

Now apply \(\Lambda^s\) to the momentum equation and pair with \(\Lambda^su\).
The time derivative gives \(E_s'\), while viscosity gives
\[
  \nu\pair{\Lambda^su}{\Lambda^s\Delta u}_{L^2}
  =-\nu\norm{\Lambda^{s+1}u}_2^2
  =-2\nu E_{s+1}.
\]
Incompressibility cancels the global pressure pairing after integration by parts.
Keep the complete transport--pressure transfer in
\[
  \mathcal P_s
  :=-
  \operatorname{Re}
  \pair{\Lambda^su}
  {\Lambda^s\bigl((u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p\bigr)}_{L^2}.
\]
The exact energy row is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCriticalEnergyBalance}
  E_s'=\mathcal P_s-2\nu E_{s+1}.
\end{equation}

Start at critical height.
One time derivative brings in the next spatial energy:
\[
  H'=\mathcal P_{1/2}-2\nu E_{3/2}.
\]
Differentiate once more and use \eqref{eq:BodyCriticalEnergyBalance} at \(s=\tfrac32\):
\begin{align*}
  H''
  &=\partial_t\mathcal P_{1/2}-2\nu E_{3/2}'
  \\
  &=\partial_t\mathcal P_{1/2}
  -2\nu\mathcal P_{3/2}
  +(2\nu)^2E_{5/2}.
\end{align*}
The next derivative reaches the row at \(s=\tfrac52\):
\[
  H'''
  =\partial_t^2\mathcal P_{1/2}
  -2\nu\partial_t\mathcal P_{3/2}
  +(2\nu)^2\mathcal P_{5/2}
  -(2\nu)^3E_{7/2}.
\]
Every derivative retains the earlier transport--pressure transfers and adds one higher viscous energy.

The two directions now oppose one another.
The Zeno singularity picture asks for worse behavior in time; the energy rows point upward in space.
Once the critical response and its transfer terms are controlled, the same rung controls a higher Sobolev energy.
More spatial regularity, not less.

The first rungs also show the complete pattern.
Induction gives, for \(n\geq1\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCriticalHeightSpatialAscent}
  H^{(n)}
  =(-2\nu)^nE_{n+1/2}
  +\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
  (-2\nu)^j
  \partial_t^{\,n-1-j}\mathcal P_{j+1/2}.
\end{equation}
Because \(\nu>0\), the coefficient of the top spatial energy is nonzero at every order.
Solving for that height gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCompletedCriticalRow}
  E_{n+1/2}
  =(-2\nu)^{-n}
  \left(
    H^{(n)}
    -\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
      (-2\nu)^j
      \partial_t^{\,n-1-j}\mathcal P_{j+1/2}
  \right).
\end{equation}
At this stage, \emph{reaches} has an exact meaning.
The identity exposes the higher spatial coordinate and names the response and transfer terms whose control would determine it.
The estimate that supplies that control comes later.

Now return to the mixed jet.
The calculation above used \(V_0=u\), but the differentiated Navier--Stokes equation has the same viscous structure in every temporal row.
Fix \(V_r\) and define
\begin{align*}
  E_{r,s}
  &:=\frac12\norm{\Lambda^sV_r}_2^2,
  \\
  \mathcal P_{r,s}
  &:=-\operatorname{Re}
  \pair{\Lambda^sV_r}
  {\Lambda^s\!\left(
    \sum_{a=0}^{r}\binom{r}{a}(V_a\cdot\nabla)V_{r-a}
    +\nabla Q_r
  \right)}_{L^2}.
\end{align*}
Pairing the \(r\)-th equation in \eqref{eq:BodyTemporalVelocityRecurrence} with \(\Lambda^{2s}V_r\) gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyEveryTemporalRowSpatialEnergy}
  E_{r,s}'=\mathcal P_{r,s}-2\nu E_{r,s+1}.
\end{equation}
For a fixed row \(r\), the same calculation repeats to every finite ascent depth \(k\geq1\):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyEveryTemporalRowCompletedHeight}
  \partial_t^kE_{r,1/2}
  =(-2\nu)^kE_{r,k+1/2}
  +\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}
  (-2\nu)^j
  \partial_t^{\,k-1-j}\mathcal P_{r,j+1/2}.
\end{equation}
The left side is a scalar quadratic readout involving \(V_r,\ldots,V_{r+k}\), distinct from any single response row.
There is one spatial ascent inside \(V_0\), another inside \(V_1\), and another inside every finite \(V_r\).
Temporal depth and spatial height are independent choices.

The diagonal has now opened into a grid:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
 & H^0_x & H^1_x & H^2_x & H^3_x & \cdots\\
\hline
V_0=u      & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
V_1=u_t    & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
V_2=u_{tt} & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
V_3=u_{ttt}& \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
\vdots     & \vdots  & \vdots  & \vdots  & \vdots  & \ddots
\end{array}
\]
Moving down selects a later temporal response.
Moving across selects a higher Sobolev height inside that response.

This is where the intersection comes from.
No single diagonal coordinate gives smoothness, and no single row contains every order by itself.
On a time interval \(I\), the full target is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyDesiredMixedIntersection}
  u\in
  \bigcap_{r=0}^{\infty}
  \bigcap_{m=0}^{\infty}
  H^r(I;H^m(\R^3)).
\end{equation}
Every finite temporal response at every finite spatial height has to meet in that intersection.

An estimate can pay for finitely many coordinates at a time.
Choose a finite temporal depth \(N\) and a finite spatial height \(M\); that choice cuts a rectangle out of the mixed grid.
Increasing either cutoff enlarges the rectangle while keeping its earlier coordinates.
Section~\ref{sec:TheIntersectionInsideTheTower} turns this geometric rectangle into the exact adjacent trace block and applies the datum-generated estimate that fills it.
