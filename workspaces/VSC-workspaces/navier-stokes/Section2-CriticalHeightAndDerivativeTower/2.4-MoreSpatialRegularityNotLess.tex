\subsection{More spatial regularity, not less}\label{sub:ThePressureCompleteMixedDerivativeTower}

Up to this point, every new rung looked worse.
A nontrivial change squeezed into a shorter interval produces a larger response, so the temporal Tower seems to run straight toward singularity.
But viscosity has been present in every row, and every Laplacian reaches farther into space.
At critical height, those two counts line up.

For
\[
  E_s(t):=\frac12\norm{\Lambda^su(t)}_2^2,
  \qquad
  E_{s,\lambda}(t):=\frac12\norm{\Lambda^su_\lambda(t)}_2^2,
\]
the calculation from critical height gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodySpatialEnergyScaling}
  E_{s,\lambda}(t)
  =\lambda^{2s-1}E_s(\lambda^2t).
\end{equation}
Since \(H=E_{1/2}\),
\[
  H_\lambda(t)=H(\lambda^2t).
\]
After \(n\) time derivatives,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCriticalHeightTemporalScaling}
  H_\lambda^{(n)}(t)
  =\lambda^{2n}H^{(n)}(\lambda^2t).
\end{equation}
The spatial energy at height \(n+\tfrac12\) has the same scale factor:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodySpatialHeightScalingDiagonal}
  E_{n+1/2,\lambda}(t)
  =\lambda^{2n}E_{n+1/2}(\lambda^2t).
\end{equation}
The equal scale weight does not make \(H^{(n)}\) and \(E_{n+1/2}\) the same object.
It identifies the spatial height that can appear in the \(n\)-th completed row.

Applying \(\Lambda^s\) to Navier--Stokes and pairing the result with \(\Lambda^su\) gives the viscous contribution
\[
  \nu\pair{\Lambda^su}{\Lambda^s\Delta u}_{L^2}
  =-\nu\norm{\Lambda^{s+1}u}_2^2
  =-2\nu E_{s+1}.
\]
The complete transport--pressure transfer is
\[
  \mathcal P_s
  :=-
  \operatorname{Re}
  \pair{\Lambda^su}
  {\Lambda^s\bigl((u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p\bigr)}_{L^2}.
\]
The exact spatial-energy row is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCriticalEnergyBalance}
  E_s'=\mathcal P_s-2\nu E_{s+1}.
\end{equation}

At critical height, the first derivative already contains the next spatial energy:
\[
  H'=\mathcal P_{1/2}-2\nu E_{3/2}.
\]
Differentiate again and use the same row at \(s=\tfrac32\):
\[
\begin{aligned}
  H''
  &=\partial_t\mathcal P_{1/2}-2\nu E_{3/2}'
  \\
  &=\partial_t\mathcal P_{1/2}
  -2\nu\mathcal P_{3/2}
  +(2\nu)^2E_{5/2}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The third derivative reaches one height farther:
\[
  H'''
  =\partial_t^2\mathcal P_{1/2}
  -2\nu\partial_t\mathcal P_{3/2}
  +(2\nu)^2\mathcal P_{5/2}
  -(2\nu)^3E_{7/2}.
\]
Every differentiation keeps the transfer terms already produced and adds one higher viscous energy.
For \(n\geq1\), induction gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCriticalHeightSpatialAscent}
  H^{(n)}
  =(-2\nu)^nE_{n+1/2}
  +\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
  (-2\nu)^j
  \partial_t^{\,n-1-j}\mathcal P_{j+1/2}.
\end{equation}
Because \(\nu>0\), the top spatial energy can be isolated:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCompletedCriticalRow}
  E_{n+1/2}
  =(-2\nu)^{-n}
  \left(
    H^{(n)}
    -\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
      (-2\nu)^j
      \partial_t^{\,n-1-j}\mathcal P_{j+1/2}
  \right).
\end{equation}
The identity exposes the higher Sobolev energy.
It does not control that energy until \(H^{(n)}\) and every transfer term beside it have also been controlled.

The temporal Tower enters through \(H^{(n)}\) itself.
Since
\[
  H(t)
  =\frac12
  \pair{\Lambda^{1/2}V_0(t)}
       {\Lambda^{1/2}V_0(t)}_{L^2},
\]
Leibniz's rule gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyHeightDerivativeTemporalContraction}
  H^{(n)}(t)
  =\frac12\sum_{a=0}^{n}\binom{n}{a}
  \operatorname{Re}
  \pair{\Lambda^{1/2}V_a(t)}
       {\Lambda^{1/2}V_{n-a}(t)}_{L^2}.
\end{equation}
The three entries on one rung are distinct but connected: \(V_n\) is the Eulerian response, \(H^{(n)}\) contracts the temporal rows at critical height, and \(E_{n+1/2}\) is the spatial energy exposed through viscosity.

Whenever the Zeno route keeps a nontrivial change alive, the temporal column asks for a larger response.
The matched spatial column moves the other way through the Sobolev scale, through
\(E_{1/2},E_{3/2},E_{5/2},E_{7/2},\ldots\): more spatial regularity, not less.

That is not regularity yet.
The transfer terms still have to be controlled, and one diagonal inside \(u\) does not fill the whole time--space Tower.

Every temporal response needs its own spatial ladder.
For \(r\in\Nzero\), let \(E_{r,s}:=\tfrac12\norm{\Lambda^sV_r}_2^2\), and let \(\mathcal P_{r,s}\) denote the complete transport--pressure transfer in the \(r\)-th recurrence.
Pairing the \(r\)-th temporal equation with \(\Lambda^{2s}V_r\) gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyEveryTemporalRowSpatialEnergy}
  E_{r,s}'=\mathcal P_{r,s}-2\nu E_{r,s+1}.
\end{equation}
After any finite ascent depth \(k\geq1\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyEveryTemporalRowCompletedHeight}
  \partial_t^kE_{r,1/2}
  =(-2\nu)^kE_{r,k+1/2}
  +\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}
  (-2\nu)^j
  \partial_t^{\,k-1-j}\mathcal P_{r,j+1/2}.
\end{equation}
The temporal index \(r\) chooses the response, and the spatial index \(k\) chooses the height inside that response.
They are independent coordinates of the mixed jet.

Together they form the grid
{\small
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
 & H^0_x & H^1_x & H^2_x & H^3_x & \cdots\\
\hline
V_0=u      & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
V_1=u_t    & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
V_2=u_{tt} & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
V_3=u_{ttt}& \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
\vdots     & \vdots  & \vdots  & \vdots  & \vdots  & \ddots
\end{array}
\]
}
Moving down selects a later temporal response, while moving across selects a higher Sobolev height inside that response.
Controlling every finite coordinate would place the solution in the mixed intersection
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyDesiredMixedIntersection}
  u\in
  \bigcap_{r=0}^{\infty}
  \bigcap_{m=0}^{\infty}
  H^r(I;H^m(\R^3)).
\end{equation}

A proof can only pay for a finite part of that grid at a time.
For fixed \(N\) and \(M\), the first \(N\) temporal rows and first \(M\) spatial columns form one finite rectangle, and every finite coordinate belongs to one.
The next section has to control every such rectangle.
