\subsection{More spatial regularity, not less}\label{sub:ThePressureCompleteMixedDerivativeTower}

At first, the temporal Tower seems to confirm the singularity.
Each collapsing passage asks for a larger response, and every response that still has to change passes that demand to the derivative above it.
But \eqref{eq:BodyTemporalVelocityRecurrence} has carried another fact from the beginning.
The right side is \(\nu\Delta V_n\) at every temporal order.
Every temporal row reaches two derivatives farther into space through the same viscous term.
The higher we climb in time, the deeper the equation reaches in space.

Critical scaling fixes the diagonal on which those two directions meet.
For
\[
  E_s(t):=\frac12\norm{\Lambda^su(t)}_2^2,
  \qquad
  E_{s,\lambda}(t):=\frac12\norm{\Lambda^su_\lambda(t)}_2^2,
\]
the calculation that produced critical height gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodySpatialEnergyScaling}
  E_{s,\lambda}(t)
  =\lambda^{2s-1}E_s(\lambda^2t).
\end{equation}
Since \(H=E_{1/2}\),
\[
  H_\lambda(t)=H(\lambda^2t).
\]
Differentiating \(n\) times in time gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCriticalHeightTemporalScaling}
  H_\lambda^{(n)}(t)
  =\lambda^{2n}H^{(n)}(\lambda^2t).
\end{equation}
The spatial energy at height \(n+\tfrac12\) carries exactly the same factor:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodySpatialHeightScalingDiagonal}
  E_{n+1/2,\lambda}(t)
  =\lambda^{2n}E_{n+1/2}(\lambda^2t).
\end{equation}
The scalar response \(H^{(n)}\) and the spatial energy \(E_{n+1/2}\) occupy the same scale rung.
That equality of scale does not identify them.
It tells us where the equation has to be differentiated to find their connection.

Apply \(\Lambda^s\) to Navier--Stokes and pair with \(\Lambda^su\).
The viscous term is
\[
  \nu\pair{\Lambda^su}{\Lambda^s\Delta u}_{L^2}
  =-\nu\norm{\Lambda^{s+1}u}_2^2
  =-2\nu E_{s+1}.
\]
Keep the complete transport--pressure transfer in
\[
  \mathcal P_s
  :=-
  \operatorname{Re}
  \pair{\Lambda^su}
  {\Lambda^s\bigl((u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p\bigr)}_{L^2}.
\]
The spatial energy therefore satisfies the exact row
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCriticalEnergyBalance}
  E_s'=\mathcal P_s-2\nu E_{s+1}.
\end{equation}
At critical height,
\[
  H'=\mathcal P_{1/2}-2\nu E_{3/2}.
\]
One time derivative has exposed one higher Sobolev energy.
Differentiate again and use the same row at \(s=\tfrac32\):
\[
\begin{aligned}
  H''
  &=\partial_t\mathcal P_{1/2}-2\nu E_{3/2}'
  \\
  &=\partial_t\mathcal P_{1/2}
  -2\nu\mathcal P_{3/2}
  +(2\nu)^2E_{5/2}.
\end{aligned}
\]
The third derivative reaches one height farther:
\[
  H'''
  =\partial_t^2\mathcal P_{1/2}
  -2\nu\partial_t\mathcal P_{3/2}
  +(2\nu)^2\mathcal P_{5/2}
  -(2\nu)^3E_{7/2}.
\]
Nothing already produced is discarded.
Each derivative keeps the earlier transfer terms and adds the next viscous energy.
For every \(n\geq1\), induction gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCriticalHeightSpatialAscent}
  H^{(n)}
  =(-2\nu)^nE_{n+1/2}
  +\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
  (-2\nu)^j
  \partial_t^{\,n-1-j}\mathcal P_{j+1/2}.
\end{equation}
Since \(\nu>0\), the top spatial energy can be isolated:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCompletedCriticalRow}
  E_{n+1/2}
  =(-2\nu)^{-n}
  \left(
    H^{(n)}
    -\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
      (-2\nu)^j
      \partial_t^{\,n-1-j}\mathcal P_{j+1/2}
  \right).
\end{equation}
The identity exposes the higher height; an estimate must still control \(H^{(n)}\) and every transfer term retained beside it.

The temporal responses enter through \(H^{(n)}\) itself.
Because
\[
  H(t)
  =\frac12
  \pair{\Lambda^{1/2}V_0(t)}
       {\Lambda^{1/2}V_0(t)}_{L^2},
\]
Leibniz's rule gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyHeightDerivativeTemporalContraction}
  H^{(n)}(t)
  =\frac12\sum_{a=0}^{n}\binom{n}{a}
  \operatorname{Re}
  \pair{\Lambda^{1/2}V_a(t)}
       {\Lambda^{1/2}V_{n-a}(t)}_{L^2}.
\end{equation}
Thus \(V_n\), \(H^{(n)}\), and \(E_{n+1/2}\) are three different objects on the same rung: an Eulerian response, its scalar critical-height contraction, and the spatial energy exposed by viscosity.

Now the singularity reads differently.
The Zeno route asks the later temporal responses to become more violent whenever they keep carrying nontrivial changes across the shrinking intervals.
Navier--Stokes answers those same orders by exposing higher Sobolev energies.
A singularity suggested less regularity as the Tower rises.
The viscous side of the Tower reaches toward more spatial regularity, not less.

This is the reversal, but it is not yet the estimate.
The critical row has shown one diagonal through the mixed jet.
To reach smoothness, the same spatial ascent has to occur inside every temporal response.

Fix \(r\in\Nzero\), and set
\begin{align*}
  E_{r,s}
  &:=\frac12\norm{\Lambda^sV_r}_2^2,
  \\
  \mathcal P_{r,s}
  &:=-\operatorname{Re}
  \pair{\Lambda^sV_r}
  {\Lambda^s\!\left(
    \sum_{a=0}^{r}\binom{r}{a}(V_a\cdot\nabla)V_{r-a}
    +\nabla Q_r
  \right)}_{L^2}.
\end{align*}
Pairing the \(r\)-th temporal equation with \(\Lambda^{2s}V_r\) gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyEveryTemporalRowSpatialEnergy}
  E_{r,s}'=\mathcal P_{r,s}-2\nu E_{r,s+1}.
\end{equation}
After any finite ascent depth \(k\geq1\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyEveryTemporalRowCompletedHeight}
  \partial_t^kE_{r,1/2}
  =(-2\nu)^kE_{r,k+1/2}
  +\sum_{j=0}^{k-1}
  (-2\nu)^j
  \partial_t^{\,k-1-j}\mathcal P_{r,j+1/2}.
\end{equation}
Every temporal row has its own spatial ladder.
Temporal order chooses the response; spatial order chooses how finely that response is controlled.
The two indices are independent.

The diagonal has opened into a grid:
\[
\begin{array}{c|ccccc}
 & H^0_x & H^1_x & H^2_x & H^3_x & \cdots\\
\hline
V_0=u      & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
V_1=u_t    & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
V_2=u_{tt} & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
V_3=u_{ttt}& \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \bullet & \cdots\\
\vdots     & \vdots  & \vdots  & \vdots  & \vdots  & \ddots
\end{array}
\]
Moving down selects a later temporal response.
Moving across selects a higher Sobolev height inside that response.
Smoothness is not waiting at the end of either direction by itself.
It belongs to their intersection.
On a time interval \(I\), the mixed target is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyDesiredMixedIntersection}
  u\in
  \bigcap_{r=0}^{\infty}
  \bigcap_{m=0}^{\infty}
  H^r(I;H^m(\R^3)).
\end{equation}

No finite estimate can take hold of the whole intersection at once.
Fixing a temporal depth \(N\) and a spatial height \(M\) cuts out a finite block of rows and columns.
That block is a rectangle, and every coordinate in the infinite intersection lies inside some rectangle.

The vortex began as a way the fluid might concentrate beyond the reach of the energy law.
Following that motion through critical scaling, the heat clock, the response Tower, and the viscous ascent has converted the singularity into a family of finite mixed rectangles.
That is the gain.
The exact burden is now to control every chosen rectangle.
