\section{Critical Height and the Derivative Tower}\label{sec:CriticalHeightAndDerivativeTower}

Assume the opposite. Let the maximal classical history have a finite last time and write
\[
  T_*<\infty,
  \qquad
  I_*:=(0,T_*).
\]
Nothing breaks at a time strictly below \(T_*\); the solution is smooth on every closed subinterval of \(I_*\).
Every possible loss of smoothness is confined to the approach \(t\uparrow T_*\).
Begin with a vortex that remains in the geometry most favourable to stretching.
