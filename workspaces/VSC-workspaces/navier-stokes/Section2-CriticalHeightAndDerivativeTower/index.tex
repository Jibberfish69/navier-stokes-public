\section{Critical Height and the Derivative Tower}\label{sec:CriticalHeightAndDerivativeTower}

Assume, for contradiction, that the maximal time is finite, and write
\[
  T_*<\infty,
  \qquad
  I_*:=(0,T_*).
\]
The solution is classical on every closed subinterval of \(I_*\). Any
obstruction is confined to the approach \(t\uparrow T_*\), where at least one
continuation-controlling bound becomes unbounded.
