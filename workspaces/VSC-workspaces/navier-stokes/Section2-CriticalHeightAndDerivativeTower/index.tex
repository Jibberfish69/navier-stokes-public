\section{Critical Height and the Derivative Tower}\label{sec:CriticalHeightAndDerivativeTower}

Section~\ref{sec:TheRegularityProblem} tells us what a global solution has to
satisfy.  It does not yet tell us what the fluid would have to do in order to
lose that regularity.  We begin with the three-dimensional mechanism capable
of sharpening rotation while viscosity is diffusing it: vortex stretching.
We follow that mechanism through its scale, the time available at that scale,
and the responses the equation must produce if the sharpening is to continue.
The derivative Tower is not imposed on this picture in advance.  It appears
when the proposed singularity is asked to account for its own motion.
