Assume the opposite.
Let the maximal classical history have a finite last time and write
\[
  T_*<\infty,
  \qquad
  I_*:=(0,T_*).
\]
The history remains smooth on every closed subinterval of \(I_*\); any breakdown has
been pushed entirely into the approach \(t\uparrow T_*\).  Now take a vortex that
remains in the geometry most favourable to stretching.
