\section{What a Finite Endpoint Would Demand}\label{sec:CriticalHeightAndDerivativeTower}

Finite energy can survive while a smooth history loses control of its
derivatives.  In three dimensions the equation itself supplies the mechanism
that could exploit that gap: the same strain that stretches rotation contracts
the structure across it, raising gradients as the width falls, while viscosity
diffuses those gradients.  We follow that one competition until it selects its
scale-invariant height.  If the height still escapes before a finite last time,
the remaining time then forces the successive responses of the same velocity
field.
