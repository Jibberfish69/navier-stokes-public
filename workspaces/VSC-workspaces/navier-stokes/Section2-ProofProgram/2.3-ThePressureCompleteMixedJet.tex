\subsection{The Pressure-Complete Mixed Jet}\label{sub:ThePressureCompleteMixedJet}

The temporal recurrences \eqref{eq:BodyTemporalVelocityRecurrence} and \eqref{eq:BodyTemporalPressureRecurrence} couple \(V_{n+1}\) to \(\nu\Delta V_n\), the binomial transport sum, and \(\nabla Q_n\), while the normalized pressure Poisson row recovers \(Q_n\) from those same velocity rows.
The fixed-space escalation above explains why adjacent temporal rows matter near a finite endpoint.
What the temporal notation still hides is how spatial derivatives distribute through each nonlinear product.
Differentiate one transport factor in the coordinate direction \(x_\ell\):
\[
  \partial_\ell\bigl((V_a\cdot\nabla)V_{n-a}\bigr)
  =
  ((\partial_\ell V_a)\cdot\nabla)V_{n-a}
  +(V_a\cdot\nabla)\partial_\ell V_{n-a}.
\]
At higher spatial order, every derivative must choose between the two factors.
For \(\alpha\in\Nzero^3\), retain the velocity coordinate from the tower and attach the pressure coordinate at the same depth:
\[
  J_{n,\alpha}
  :=
  \partial_t^n\partial_x^\alpha u,
  \qquad
  \Pi_{n,\alpha}
  :=
  \partial_t^n\partial_x^\alpha p,
\]
and for \(\beta\le\alpha\), set
\[
  \binom{\alpha}{\beta}
  :=
  \prod_{k=1}^{3}\binom{\alpha_k}{\beta_k}.
\]
The simultaneous temporal and spatial product rules give
\[
  \partial_t^n\partial_x^\alpha\bigl((u\cdot\nabla)u\bigr)
  =
  \sum_{a=0}^{n}
  \sum_{\beta\le\alpha}
  \binom{n}{a}
  \binom{\alpha}{\beta}
  (J_{a,\beta}\cdot\nabla)
  J_{n-a,\alpha-\beta}.
\]
Substitution into the equation exposes the full mixed recurrence:
\begin{align*}
  J_{n+1,\alpha}
  &+
  \sum_{a=0}^{n}
  \sum_{\beta\le\alpha}
  \binom{n}{a}
  \binom{\alpha}{\beta}
  (J_{a,\beta}\cdot\nabla)
  J_{n-a,\alpha-\beta}
  \\
  &+\nabla\Pi_{n,\alpha}
  =
  \nu\Delta J_{n,\alpha},
  \qquad
  \nabla\cdot J_{n,\alpha}=0.
\end{align*}
Its pressure coordinates satisfy
\[
  -\Delta\Pi_{n,\alpha}
  =
  \partial_i\partial_j
  \sum_{a=0}^{n}
  \sum_{\beta\le\alpha}
  \binom{n}{a}
  \binom{\alpha}{\beta}
  (J_{a,\beta})_i
  (J_{n-a,\alpha-\beta})_j.
\]

This is the mixed jet of the original pressure-complete solution.
The temporal rows, spatial columns, nonlinear splits, divergence relations, and pressure rows are coordinates of one object generated by the original equations.
Nothing later generates a second jet or selects derivatives from outside this one.
A finite rectangle will only cut out finitely many of these coordinates after the equation has produced them.

The escalation now has a precise geometry: climbing one temporal row repeatedly opens the spatial column already coupled to it.
Critical height is the first scalar at which that column can be read without losing the scale singled out by the endpoint threat.
