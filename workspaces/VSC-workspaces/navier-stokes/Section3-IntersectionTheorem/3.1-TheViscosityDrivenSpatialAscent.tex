\subsection{The other direction inside critical height}\label{sub:TheViscosityDrivenSpatialAscent}

Now differentiate the critical height itself.  This is where the temporal Tower turns.
The singularity argument used successive time derivatives to ask how rapidly the same
velocity history would have to respond.  Viscosity makes that same count expose a
second direction: every differentiation of the height reaches one full Sobolev step
higher in space.

For \(s\geq0\), write
\[
  E_s(t):=\frac12\norm{\Lambda^su(t)}_2^2
\]
and keep the entire nonlinear and pressure transfer in
\[
  \mathcal P_s(t)
  :=-
  \operatorname{Re}
  \pair{\Lambda^su}
  {\Lambda^s\bigl((u\cdot\nabla)u+\nabla p\bigr)}_{L^2}.
\]
The global pressure pairing vanishes because \(\Lambda^su\) is divergence-free, but
we retain it here so that the scalar row still records the full equation from which it
came.  Pairing Navier--Stokes with \(\Lambda^{2s}u\) gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyFullSpatialEnergyBalance}
  E_s'=\mathcal P_s-2\nu E_{s+1}.
\end{equation}

At \(s=\tfrac12\), this says
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyFirstCriticalRow}
  H'=\mathcal P_{1/2}-2\nu E_{3/2}.
\end{equation}
Across one Zeno first-passage interval,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:EachZenoPassageCost}
  \int_{t_k}^{t_{k+1}}\mathcal P_{1/2}(t)\,dt
  =L_k+2\nu\int_{t_k}^{t_{k+1}}E_{3/2}(t)\,dt.
\end{equation}
The identity records two simultaneous contributions on every first-passage interval.
The net rise is \(L_k\), while viscosity removes an additional
\(2\nu\int_{t_k}^{t_{k+1}}E_{3/2}(t)\,dt\).  Shortening the crossing interval changes
neither term; it concentrates the required transfer into less time.

Differentiate once more and use \eqref{eq:BodyFullSpatialEnergyBalance} at height
\(s=\tfrac32\):
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodySecondCriticalRow}
  H''
  =\partial_t\mathcal P_{1/2}
  -2\nu\mathcal P_{3/2}
  +(2\nu)^2E_{5/2}.
\end{equation}
The next derivative gives
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyThirdCriticalRow}
  H'''
  =\partial_t^2\mathcal P_{1/2}
  -2\nu\partial_t\mathcal P_{3/2}
  +(2\nu)^2\mathcal P_{5/2}
  -(2\nu)^3E_{7/2}.
\end{equation}
Nothing below the new top term disappears.  Each derivative keeps every earlier
transfer and adds one higher viscous energy.  Induction gives, for \(n\geq1\),
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCriticalHeightSpatialAscent}
  H^{(n)}
  =(-2\nu)^nE_{n+1/2}
  +\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
  (-2\nu)^j
  \partial_t^{\,n-1-j}\mathcal P_{j+1/2}.
\end{equation}
Because \(\nu>0\) is fixed, the highest spatial energy can be isolated:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyCompletedCriticalRow}
  \boxed{
  E_{n+1/2}
  =
  \frac{
    H^{(n)}
    -\displaystyle\sum_{j=0}^{n-1}
      (-2\nu)^j
      \partial_t^{\,n-1-j}\mathcal P_{j+1/2}
  }{(-2\nu)^n}}
  \qquad(n\geq1).
\end{equation}
Since \(\nu>0\), the coefficient \((-2\nu)^n\) is nonzero, and the row can be
solved for its top spatial energy.  The numerator still contains the derivative of
\(H\) and the complete chain of transfer terms.
Equation \eqref{eq:BodyCompletedCriticalRow} isolates the higher Sobolev height; it
does not bound it for free.

The two middle columns cannot be identified.  Their exact connection is the quadratic
contraction
\begin{equation}\label{eq:BodyHeightDerivativeVelocityContraction}
  H^{(n)}
  =\frac12\sum_{a=0}^{n}\binom{n}{a}
  \operatorname{Re}
  \pair{\Lambda^{1/2}V_a}{\Lambda^{1/2}V_{n-a}}_{L^2}.
\end{equation}
But now the reversal is visible.  A finite singular time asks every fixed response
\(V_n\) to lose \(L^3\) control.  The \(n\)-th completed scalar row points at the same
time toward \(E_{n+1/2}\), hence toward \(n+\tfrac12\) spatial derivatives of the
velocity.  If those heights can be controlled, then for any prescribed \(k\), choosing
\(n>k+1\) first gives
\[
  \norm{u(t)}_{H^{n+1/2}}
  \lesssim
  \norm{u(t)}_2+\norm{\Lambda^{n+1/2}u(t)}_2.
\]
The energy identity controls the low-frequency term, while \(E_{n+1/2}\) controls the
homogeneous term.  We may then use
\[
  H^{n+1/2}(\R^3)\hookrightarrow C_b^k(\R^3).
\]
The singularity is asking the history to become rougher while the viscous calculation
is opening stronger spatial regularity.

That is the shape of the contradiction, not yet the contradiction itself.  The
transfer terms still have to be controlled, and the same spatial ascent has to be
recovered inside every temporal response \(V_r\), not only inside \(u=V_0\).  We turn
to that row-by-row calculation now.
