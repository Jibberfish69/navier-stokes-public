\section{The Intersection Inside the Tower}\label{sec:TheIntersectionInsideTheTower}

The Tower was built because a finite-time singularity would have to force the
same velocity through faster and faster responses.  Yet the equation that
generates the next response contains \(\nu\Delta V_n\): two spatial
derivatives of the response already in hand.  The attempted temporal escape
cannot be read only in time.  We have to ask whether climbing the Tower spends
spatial regularity or whether viscosity forces a higher spatial energy into
the same calculation.

Put the two directions beside each other.  Time runs down through
\(V_0,V_1,V_2,\ldots\); space runs across the Sobolev heights of each response.
A dot in the table names a mixed derivative of the same velocity.  It does not
yet say that the derivative stays bounded near a final time.  We begin at the
critical height and let the equation show exactly what viscosity exposes.
