Navier–Stokes Scaling
At a time t0 < T∗ , call the effective radius ℓ := reff (t0 ). For λ > 1, the exact Navier–Stokes
comparison at the finer width λ−1 ℓ is

                     uλ (x, t) := λu(λx, λ2 t),               pλ (x, t) := λ2 p(λx, λ2 t).

At time λ−2 t0 , the corresponding segment in uλ has effective radius λ−1 ℓ. This is another
solution at another scale, not yet a later state of the original segment. Each momentum term
transforms with it:
                                 ∂t uλ (x, t) = λ3 (∂t u)(λx, λ2 t),
                                                                     
                           (uλ · ∇)uλ (x, t) = λ3 (u · ∇)u (λx, λ2 t),
                                  ∇pλ (x, t) = λ3 (∇p)(λx, λ2 t),
                                 ν∆uλ (x, t) = λ3 ν(∆u)(λx, λ2 t).
The finer scale strengthens viscosity and nonlinear transport by the same λ3 factor. Pressure
and local acceleration scale with them. Scale alone does not make viscosity dominate.
