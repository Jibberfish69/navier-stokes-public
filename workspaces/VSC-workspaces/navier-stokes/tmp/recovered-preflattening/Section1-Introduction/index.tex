\section{Introduction}\label{sec:Introduction}

The section begins with the exact regularity question and the sequence of estimates that has narrowed its possible failure.
Gold then states the direct proof contract, while Silver turns the same statement around and exposes a class-membership boundary.
The remaining subsections give that boundary physical and mathematical content through participation, Euler slip, the derivative tower, concrete failure pictures, and the one-fluid VPI law.
